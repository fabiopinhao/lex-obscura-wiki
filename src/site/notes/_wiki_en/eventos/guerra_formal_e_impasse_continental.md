# Formal War and Continental Stalemate

The Formal War was the explicit declaration of war in the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Reinkardic Wars]], issued by [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] and not by [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. It marked the first continental war in the history of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]: the entire continent allied against the shadowy plateau. The conflict, however, settled into a prolonged stalemate that, with each broken truce, tipped a little further toward Morvgrad.

### The Declaration and the Alliance

The Central [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] could no longer watch the absorption of neighboring kingdoms without reacting. More than half the human population of the northern kingdoms had already fled to the central lands, and Myrgarde was suffocating under the weight of the refugees. It then gathered its allies: the cosmopolitanism forged by the catastrophe of the [[_wiki_en/eventos/sopro_do_eclipse\|Breath of the Eclipse]] had proven its worth, and peoples who had previously fought one another began to cooperate out of necessity. [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]] from [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], militias from border kingdoms, companies of gnome alchemists, and what remained of the [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarar]] paladins united under the banner of the Central Empire. The dwarves did not fight in the ranks, but their forges supplied the armies with the finest blades and armor on the continent.

Historians disagree on where to place the true beginning of the conflict. Some date it to decades before the declaration, to the [[_wiki_en/eventos/submissao_da_ventrista\|extortion of Ventrista]] and the first campaigns to conquer the smaller kingdoms; for them, the first wagon of tribute to descend from the plateau was already an act of war. Others treat the formal declaration as the definitive milestone.

### Myrgarde’s Dilemma

The formal war exposed the structural weakness of the Central Empire. It had allies, superior weapons, and Álvarar leadership, but smaller armies than it would have had an era earlier: the Breath of the Eclipse had left a demographic deficit that centuries of reconstruction had not reversed. Each campaign against the plateau consumed men whom Myrgarde could not replace as quickly as it lost them. The plateau was a natural fortress, and the orc forts that dotted its edges turned every assault into slaughter. Morvgrad absorbed the attacks and seemed impenetrable.

### The Surrounding World

The stalemate in the heart of Reinkard did not render the rest of the world inert. To the east, beyond the swamps, the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]] of [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] monitored everything with precision, but did not intervene: [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] physiology did not tolerate direct sunlight or the humid climate, and the lakes and swamps between the continents made military conquest impracticable. Their neutrality cost them their reputation among the peoples of the plains, who did not forgive the inaction of those who could see and chose not to act. Not a word was heard from the [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]] during the war. Frenims and undines, however, grew closer during this period, and the Neranthian language began to circulate more widely among the races of the east.

To the west, in the mountain ranges of [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]] also kept apart, and even if they had offered help, the peoples of the plains would not have accepted it: beings of demonic blood were not conceivable allies. The war nevertheless stirred the mountains on its own. Armed conflicts broke out between Varnakians and [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] in the valleys and between rival Draconid tribes in the mountains. The clans tied to [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]] emerged mostly victorious, while [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/cla_myr_vethan\|clan Myr’vethan]], her rival, took advantage of the instability to absorb defeated tribes. The conflict in [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]] only ended when Varna decided to end it: the female dragon burned part of the valley and the mountains and forced the rival clan to retreat, resolving through the brute force of a single being a war that dozens of tribes could not.

### The Logic of Attrition

Back on the main continent, the war followed a logic of calculated attrition. The two powers signed agreements of mutual non-destruction whose ink was barely dry before the next violation: Morvgrad needed productive plains to plunder, and Myrgarde needed time to reorganize armies that returned smaller with each campaign. The stalemate tipped toward one side, however, because Morvgrad could replace its losses and Myrgarde could not. With each broken truce, the Cult advanced a little further and the plains retreated by the same amount.
