# Founding of Morvgrad and Creation of the Lex Obscura

The Founding of Morvgrad is the event of the Second Era in which the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], protected from the external threat on the plateau, carried out an unprecedented reorganization. [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]], the oldest and most cunning of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templar]] leaders, drafted the first document of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] and imposed a unification that the diabolical temples had never tolerated.

### The Unification of the Cults

Until then, the cults had been divided into rival orders bound to different [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|black gods]]. Xarn absorbed them into a single structure. [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] was born of this unification: not as a spontaneous city, but as the planned seat of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], built with the discipline of those who plan a religion and a state at the same time.

### The Alliance with the orcs

The relationship with the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] evolved in stages. The Shadars began by offering free passage south, to human territories that the orcs could pillage without the Shadars having to expend soldiers. Logistical support and provisions followed. Revenge against humans and elves became the cement of an alliance that, over time, evolved from opportunism into mutual dependence. The orcs began to occupy the edges of the plateau as a defensive barrier, without formality or a command structure: there was expediency, not hierarchy. Military integration would come later, when the Reinkardic Wars demanded more than uncoordinated brutality. Together, the three races of the plateau also learned to tame goblinoids from the frozen lands, expanding their contingent of domesticated creatures.
