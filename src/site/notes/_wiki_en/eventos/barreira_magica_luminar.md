# Luminar Magical Barrier

The Luminar Magical Barrier was the definitive marker of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]]’ victory over the reptiles in [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]]. Raised in the mountain ranges surrounding the plateau and the central plains, it sealed the mountains with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] of such intensity that dragons and [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] could not cross them toward the lowlands.

### Origin

The conflict between Luminars and [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]] defined the geopolitics of the Forgotten Age as much as racial fragmentation did. Dragons and Draconids inhabited the western mountain ranges and the volcanic peaks, forming tribes far more cohesive than those of today. The Luminars recognized a demonic tendency in them, for they had served the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|red gods]] since time immemorial, and treated both species as a scourge to be contained.

To fight them, Luminar artificers forged artifacts of war imbued with ancient magi. The most powerful were deliberately forged to reject hands corrupted by diabolical pacts or demonic bonds, so that neither [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] nor [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] could wield them against their creators. None was more feared than [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], an enchanted dagger capable of wounding any draconic creature, which answered only to hands free of pacts.

### The Barrier

The conflict culminated in the construction of the great arcane barriers. The Luminars infused the very rock of the mountain ranges with magi, and dragons ceased crossing the mountains. Without the dragons’ support, the Draconids likewise did not dare descend, for the Luminar artifacts were too lethal for them to face alone. The barrier became the sign of Luminar supremacy over the reptiles.

The dwarves, who had inhabited the mountains’ interior since times as ancient as the Luminars themselves, remained a race apart throughout the conflict. Isolationist and devoted to stone and the forge, they defended their citadels with an ingenuity no other race equaled: false tunnels, stone trapdoors, and passages that collapsed onto invaders. The fortresses that withstood the conflict became impenetrable, and the humans who would later try to enter them would discover what the dragons already knew.

### Legacy

The barrier’s durability proved as remarkable as its power. Even after the fragmentation of the Luminars and the fall of the parent race, the residual magi etched into the rock sustained it for eras. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], it served an unforeseen dual function: it contained the dragons directly and, indirectly, reduced the flow of orcs to the plateau, since crossing the mountain ranges was deadly beneath the reptiles’ shadow. The pagan humans of the north owed their survival to magic that had never been raised to protect them. The barrier would only be consumed in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], when the dark corruption accumulated on the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau eroded the residual magi that sustained it.
