# Reinkardic Wars (Beginning)

The Reinkardic Wars were the first continental war in the history of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] and marked the transition from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]] to the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]]. They began when the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], having exhausted the possibilities on the plateau, descended to the continent. The conflict would last about fifty years and consume all of Reinkard.

### The Exhaustion of the Plateau

In the last century of the Second Era, the Shadow Cult had garrisoned its outlying fortresses, unified the temples under the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]], and consolidated the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]]. [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] operated as the religious and military capital of a power that had nowhere left to expand without descending. The descent began not with armies, but with hunger and extortion.

### Command and Strategy

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]] led the armies in the early phases of expansion. Each [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] temple recruited its own troops, but the chain of command extended above the loyalties of each priesthood: prominent figures on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] coordinated the campaigns so that one temple’s ambition would not undermine the overall strategy. The tradition persists in another form, and many of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]’s generals came from the Templar ranks before receiving their commissions.

The Cult was content, at first, to dominate the smaller kingdoms, which lacked the population or walls to withstand a prolonged siege. The strategy was one of progressive strangulation: subjugate the periphery before confronting the center. [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]] was the first piece to fall, and each conquest fed the next.

### Myrgarde’s Apprehension

[[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], the largest empire of the plains, watched the expansion with growing apprehension and tried to protect its neighbors, even those with whom it maintained hostile relations: by its logic, a poorly governed human kingdom was still preferable to territory dominated by [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]]. But that protection had limits. Myrgarde could not be everywhere, and the armies it sent north returned smaller with each campaign. The formal declaration of war would come only decades after hostilities had actually begun, and the cycle of expansion that then began would continue for a long time.
