# Fragmentations of the Luminars

The Fragmentations of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] were the series of schisms, occurring during the Forgotten Age, that gave rise to the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], and the [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]]. The Luminars were not a homogeneous race: over countless ages, internal factions diverged in their beliefs, ambitions, and alignments. The majority remained faithful to the light and the cycles of the surface, but minorities broke with that tradition, and each schism produced a lineage that would never return. The event predates the [[_wiki_en/eventos/queda_dos_luminares\|Fall of the Luminars]].

### The Descent to Vor-Ghol

The first to separate were those who descended to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], repelled by the majority and drawn to the depths. They took with them fragments of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] language and culture, which they corrupted in the caverns until they forged something entirely new. Underground, they established dominion over older races, enslaving them, and expanded their influence through the subterranean galleries. They became the proto-Druls, and it was with them, not with humans, that the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] established the first channels of communication. There was friction between factions in Vor-Ghol: those who had made pacts with devils remained in the depths and definitively gave rise to the Druls, while those bonded to [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], chaotic and destructive in nature, proved incompatible with underground discipline.

### The Migration to the Surface

Those who were incompatible abandoned Vor-Ghol and migrated to the tundras, glaciers, and mountains of the surface. Isolation and the brutality of the environment shaped their bodies and their culture over millennia, and from them arose the orcs. A third faction took the opposite path: it migrated to the eastern regions and began inhabiting the coasts and wetlands, isolating itself from the more populated regions. This group placed little importance on material life and sought transcendence, meditating and contemplating the celestial cycles in search of communion with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereal]] gods. Over time, their lives became more ephemeral and less bound to the earth; they mastered the seas and coastal regions and gave rise to the undines.

### The Legacy of the Divine Channels

The importance of the fragmentations transcends racial genealogy. Each schism established the divine channels that would define the balance of power in [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] for ages: the Druls bound themselves to the black gods, the orcs to the red demons, the undines to the blue sidereals. The Luminars of the surface remained divided between the influence of the green [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] and the white [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]]. This distribution was not accidental: each faction engraved in blood and faith the alignment that separated it from the others. When the unified Luminars finally fell, the renegade lineages had already been firmly established for millennia, and none experienced the loss of the mother race as a tragedy of its own.
