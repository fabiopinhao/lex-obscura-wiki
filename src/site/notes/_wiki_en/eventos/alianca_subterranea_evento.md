# The Underground Alliance

The Underground Alliance was the formal pact between [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]] that united [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] in the Second Era of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. In an unprecedented development in the region’s history, the Druls began living alongside the Shadars on the surface, participating in the religious and political structure. It was [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]] who made the symbiosis possible and engineered it, consolidating the political and military pacts that formalized it.

### The Pact

The Alliance was not a gesture of fraternity, but a calculation. The Druls brought with them millennia of subterranean arcane tradition, pact rituals refined by the experience of eras, and a network of influence in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] that the Shadars could never have attained alone. The Shadars offered military discipline, administrative capacity, and an expansionist ambition that the Druls, isolationist by temperament, had never cultivated. Each side obtained what it could not generate on its own, and the cultural exchange transformed both races.

### Consequences

The Druls needed reach on the surface to protect their interests in Vor-Ghol; the Shadars needed arcane depth to compete with the Sancta system that sustained [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]. The formalization of the pact locked a lasting mechanism into place: the Druls supplied the magic, the Shadars supplied the sword, and together they dominated both the surface and the subterranean reaches of the northern plateau, a structure that would endure until the Reinkardic Wars and beyond.
