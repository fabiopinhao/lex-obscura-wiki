# Siege of Khordun

In the Siege of Khordun, prisoners were forced to carry barrels of boiling pitch under a rain of arrows.

### Legacy in Language

The episode gave rise to the expression “carry a barrel,” an insult among [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] denoting degrading labor. Directing it at a captain is equivalent to calling him a slave. The orc [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]] used it when refusing this kind of service, asserting that he was a warrior and not a beast of burden.
