# Conversion of Dravhok

The Conversion of Dravhok was the [[_wiki_en/magia/rito_de_ossarium\|Rite of Ossárium]] through which a human became a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] and came to be called Dravhok, about eighty years ago, during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]]. In the decades that followed, the convert would rise to positions that no other Shadar, by birth or otherwise, had attained in the history of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], and would ultimately take power as [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]].

### Context

The conversion occurred during the phase when the war was still being decided by the weight of the legions and the raw endurance of the armies. The imbalance created by the orc campaigns produced generals, and the second half of the war demanded enough audacity from them to turn tactical advantage into permanent territorial conquest. Several tried, and most achieved partial victories, advances measured in leagues, and retreats measured in corpses. One of them advanced farther than any other and did not return until there was no more land to take.

### The Convert

Dravhok was not a Shadar by birth. Converted through the Rite, he bore a previous name that no record preserved. The ritual erased his humanity and transformed him into a Shadar, and the name Dravhok replaced the previous one. Many humans were converted through the Rite, and no one in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] had known him before his conversion; Dravhok never said that he had been human, and no one had the courage to ask him. What the records preserved was the result: a commander who thought differently from the others.
