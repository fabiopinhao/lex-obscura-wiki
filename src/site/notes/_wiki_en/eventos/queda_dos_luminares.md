# Fall of the Luminars

The Fall of the Luminars was the definitive fragmentation of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] that ushered in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]]. The last enchanted citadels were reduced to ashes or swallowed by the earth, and the light elves ceased to exist as a unified people. The world, until then shaped by elven longevity and natural cycles, came to be governed by the restless rhythm of humanity.

### Diaspora

The fall was less a destruction and more a diaspora. The Luminars who survived split into two irreconcilable lineages. Those who sought refuge in the deep forests adapted to green, abandoned the traditions of pure light, and turned to the cycles of nature, giving rise to the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]]. Those who settled in hidden magical citadels preserved what they could of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] heritage, giving rise to the [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]]; the most famous of their refuges would become [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]]. The two lineages would never reconcile, and the division between green and white, between forest and citadel, between [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] and [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]], would define elven politics for eras.

The [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] and the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], whose separation from the Luminars predated the fall, already inhabited the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] and the wild regions of the north, respectively, and the destruction of the parent race did little to alter their destinies.

### The Relics

The relics left by the Luminars became as powerful as they were misunderstood: artifacts from a time before the First Era, whose effects and forging methods were never reproduced. [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], forged against dragons, ended up buried in the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], citadel of the dwarves who guarded it without fully understanding its purpose. Other artifacts were fought over, preserved, or concealed by secret orders, ambitious kingdoms, and silent temples.
