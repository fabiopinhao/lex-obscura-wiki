# First Migration of the Dragons

The First Migration of the Dragons is the event of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] in which the dragons, pressured by the dark corruption that was spreading across the plateau, abandoned the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] and migrated west, toward the lands that would come to be known as [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]]. The elders departed first. The [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]], once home to the most prominent among them, was abandoned by all but one.

### Varna on the Peak of Nethys

[[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]], too young to accompany the elders and too stubborn to flee, took possession of the deserted nest on the Peak of Nethys. There she remained for most of the First Era, hunting on the slopes of the Dorsal. Prey was scarce, but the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] that tried to cross the mountain range made up for the shortage. Varna hunted them as naturally as she would bring down any large animal, and the orcs learned to fear her with a dread that was passed down through generations. Orc hatred of reptiles arose neither from rivalry nor from theology, but from predation: because of Varna and dragons like her, the orcs never worshiped draconic deities, and the draconic gods never responded to the Orcanic language.

### An Unintentional Barrier

Varna’s presence in the Dorsal had an unforeseen consequence: while she hunted, the orcs did not threaten the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] of the plateau. A young, hungry, territorial dragon did more for the security of the northern border than any fortress. The irony is structural, since the Shadars, whose corruption had driven the dragons away, benefited from the only female dragon that remained. Varna did not protect the Shadars by choice or through an alliance: she protected her territory. But the effect was the same, and would last until the corruption made the Peak of Nethys untenable for her as well.
