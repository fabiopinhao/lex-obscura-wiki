# Breath of the Eclipse

The Breath of the Eclipse was the plague that marked the transition between eras. Its origin remains disputed to this day, but its effect is not: the disease wiped out a significant portion of the continental population and struck humans with disproportionate ferocity, as they were the most numerous and most exposed race. According to popular belief, it is said to have emerged on a night of an eclipse and was transmitted through the air or through contact with the infected.

### Blame and Suspicion

The accusation fell on the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|black gods]] and, with them, on the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] and the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]]. This blame was instinctive and convenient: the Shadars lived in isolation on the northern plateau and the Druls in the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], far from the central plains. They say the Breath did not reach the plateau. Some attribute this to geographic isolation; others maintain that Shadars and Druls manufactured the plague to exterminate the races they considered inferior. The truth, if it was ever recorded, was buried with the dead.

### A Surrounded Continent

[[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] closed itself off. The dwarves sealed the interiors of their mountains and cut off all contact with the plains, and the trade routes of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] fell silent overnight. The [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]], on the other side of the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]], took in the humans who managed to reach them, and paid dearly: much of their population died from infection by the refugees they were trying to save. The swamps to the east became a trap, and the crossing toward [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] became suicide. The [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]], on their continent, escaped unscathed. Those who took to the sea were met by giant, deadly waves. There were also those who sought refuge underground: gnomes descended in unprecedented numbers, but Vor-Ghol proved more dangerous than the plague-ridden surface, for its creatures did not distinguish refugees from invaders. To the north, Shadars and [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] received migrants with steel.

### The Campaigns of Vengeance

Animosity toward Shadars and Druls grew into fury. As early as the first centuries of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], campaigns of vengeance marched on the plateau. Humans and [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]], weakened by the plague and driven more by hatred than by strategy, tried to avenge the blood of the dead. They failed: their forces were scattered and poorly coordinated, and the Shadars held out despite their smaller population. The plateau confirmed its reputation as a natural fortress, and the Shadars began surrounding its entire perimeter with small fortresses, sealing off access for good.
