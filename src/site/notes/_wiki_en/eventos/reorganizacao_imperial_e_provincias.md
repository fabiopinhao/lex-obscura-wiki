# Imperial Reorganization and Provinces

The Imperial Reorganization and Provinces describes the territorial consolidation of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] after the Reinkardic Wars. With the continent of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] under its control, the Empire began to govern through a system of provincial lords according to a governing maxim: force conquers, but only order sustains.

### The Monarch’s Government

[[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] abandoned the [[_wiki_en/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]] and began to govern through intermediaries and the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]]. Virtually no one dared challenge him, even covertly. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] operated as an instrument of the monarch’s will, not as a partner in government.

### The System of Provinces

The system of provincial lords predated Arothistar’s monarchy. Conquered territories were allocated to local lords, responsible for administration and control of the populations, and those that became established became formal provinces. Not all survived the battles: some were destroyed and decimated during the wars, others were preserved and rebuilt. Border regions became provincial orcish forts, permanent garrisons for the legions.

[[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]], the center of imperial agriculture, was divided into three provinces and became the granary that fed [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and its legions. [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], the former human capital, was rebuilt under occupation: the [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]] population lived under constant surveillance, the mark of the condemned was burned into those who dared resist, and the luminous faith was driven underground, with the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/igreja_de_sao_lucian\|Church of Saint Lucian]] and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ordem_de_azazel\|Order of Azazel]] surviving in the shadows.

[[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], the Crystal Fortress, was preserved for its strategic value. Its [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/nexus\|arcane nexuses]], the continent’s greatest raw sources of magical reagents, had no equivalent. Gnomes were relocated there in large numbers, assisting [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] studies of the use and concentration of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] in crystals. The distribution of this energy, almost a nascent industrialization, underpinned a technological revolution in the Empire.

### The Boundaries of Dominion

The Empire was not advancing onto the eastern continent. [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] remained out of reach for purely practical reasons: the humid climate and the geography of swamps, lakes, and rivers made occupation unfeasible for Shadar troops. The [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]], [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereal]] humans of Neranthis, chose to keep their distance and retreated as the shadows advanced.

To the west, [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|the Dorsal]] separated Reinkard from [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], and [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]] was the reason Arothistar did not cross the mountain range. The monarch could subjugate the valleys of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]], but Varna’s [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] allies would not leave such a province in peace, and Morvgrad feared losing battles above almost everything else.
