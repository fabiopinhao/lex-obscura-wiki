# Second Migration of the Dragons

The Second Migration of the Dragons was a migratory wave of the great dragon tribes from the mountain ranges of [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], in the Second Era, driven by a twofold pressure that fragmented the dragons’ presence in the region.

To the west, in the mountain ranges of Veinkard, the dragons faced new pressure. The [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]], whose pacts bound them to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|red gods]], had already occupied central Veinkard for centuries, and tension with the dragon tribes was growing. The great dragon tribes, which in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] had migrated to Veinkard fleeing [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] corruption, fragmented under the twofold pressure: to the east, the advancing shadows; in the center, the Varnakian demons.

With the instability in [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]], there was another migratory wave. The great dragon tribes departed even farther west, beyond the interior mountain ranges, toward distant lands about which little is known. [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]] remained. Her refusal to join the exodus, combined with the absence of other dragons in the region, concentrated on her the worship of the smaller [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] tribes, which lacked the strength to follow the elders. Over the generations, the veneration grew.

The second migration established Varna as a singular figure in Veinkard: the only significant dragon in a territory that had once harbored entire tribes.
