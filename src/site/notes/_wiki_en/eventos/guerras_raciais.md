# Racial Wars

The Racial Wars, also called the War of the Gods or the First Carnage, are the oldest stratum of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]’s history: a dispute between gods waged with flesh instead of words, predating treaties, borders, and any organized strategy. From them arose the races of hybrid humanoids, and their biological legacy extends through all subsequent eras. The event has no direct documentation and is reconstructed from fragments of oral tradition, archaeological traces, and theological inference; each race preserves its own version, and none is complete.

### Nature of the Event

The term “war” is imprecise. The gods intervened directly in the material plane, crossbreeding primitive humans and wild animals to produce soldiers aligned with their [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/cor_arcana\|arcane colors]]. The result was the hybrid humanoids: races with bestial strength, combat instinct, and innate divine alignment. The interference was neither subtle nor gradual, but direct manipulation of blood and form, fusing human and animal in a single generation.

### The Colors and Their Races

Each arcane color produced its races:

- **Black (Diabolical):** [[_wiki_en/racas/vulfano\|Vulfans]], of canine lineage
- **Red (Demonic):** [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]], reptiles
- **Green ([[_wiki_en/linguas/titanico\|Titan-tongue]]):** [[_wiki_en/racas/lynxiano\|Lynxians]], felines
- **White (Angelic):** [[_wiki_en/racas/aviano\|Avians]], birds
- **Blue (Sidereal):** [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidans]], amphibians

Pure humans are not racially aligned with any color; hybrids are. This distinction is the most visible mark of divine interference: the human was the raw material, and the gods shaped from it variations aligned with themselves.

### Scale and End

The hybrid races remained pre-civilizational, operating on instinct and divine alignment, without cities, writing, or government. Stronger in direct combat than pure humans, they never converted brute strength into political structure. The conflicts were scattered and had no lasting objective: packs against bands, color against color. The Wars ended without an armistice and without a victor; the gods simply withdrew. The reasons remain disputed among theologians and arcanists: loss of interest, equilibrium achieved, or the impossibility of definitive victory in the material plane. The biological legacy, however, remained.

### Long-Term Consequences

The hybrid races are, in practice, living fossils of a conflict between gods: they carry their creators’ arcane color in their blood, which gives them innate magical access and instinctive alignment, but also dooms them, since they were shaped for a carnage that has ended.

Over the following eras, the hybrid and gnomish races have been giving way to humans and elven humanoids, a phenomenon called **debestialization**: the gradual retreat of direct divine interference, with the material plane favoring the forms the gods left behind. Each race has its specific reason for declining, but the outcome converges.

No race has a direct record of the event. It survives in the fragmentary oral tradition of the hybrid races, in archaeological traces such as fields of mixed skeletal remains, in the theological inference of arcanists, and in a kind of instinctive memory: [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] that prostrate themselves before [[_wiki_en/panteao/abbhal\|Abbhal]] without knowing why, [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worgs]] that howl at [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] on nights of the full moon without understanding the origin of the habit.

### Place in History

The rise of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]], the elven fragmentation, and the three known eras were all built upon the legacy of the Racial Wars. The Luminars did not participate in the event, which predates their rise. The races that remain still carry the colors of the gods who abandoned them.
