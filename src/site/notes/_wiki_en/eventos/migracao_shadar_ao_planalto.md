# Shadar Migration to the Plateau and Contact with Druls

The Shadar Migration to the Plateau is the event of the First Era in which the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], facing growing repression, migrated to the far north, a cold, arid region neglected by the alabastrine kingdoms. It was in the isolation of this [[_wiki_en/locais/dhar-morveth\|plateau]] that Shadar culture took shape and their first alliances with the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] were born.

### The Northern Refuge

The Shadars were not alone. The northern plateau already sheltered scattered human communities, largely made up of those who had rejected religious conversion. Under the influence of the [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]], the kingdoms of the plains offered little room for dissent: those who refused the celestial faith lost access to power, protection, and trade. Pagans and transgressors sought refuge beyond the reach of alabastrine banners.

The options were few. To the south lay the very kingdoms that rejected them. The mountain ranges to the west were the territory of territorial [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]], and the few safe places belonged to the dwarves, who did not allow permanent human settlement. The wetlands to the east sheltered only the most determined, who eventually joined the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]], humans who had sought the knowledge of the [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]] and, rejected, devoted themselves to studying the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] until they learned to move between the two planes. Only the north remained.

### The Barrier and Its Dangers

The north had its own risks: the tundras beyond the plateau were the domain of the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], who plundered any settlement within reach. The plateau, however, offered protection for an unexpected reason, the magical barrier of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]]. The dragons were confined to the outer slopes of the mountains, and the plateau, though arid and freezing, was one of the few northern territories where humans could live without fearing draconic attacks. The orcs had to cross mountains patrolled by dragons to reach the plateau, and few succeeded. The barrier thus served a dual function: it contained the dragons and, indirectly, reduced the flow of orcs.

The Shadars found the plateau’s pagan communities and subjugated them without mercy, some forcibly converted, others sacrificed in consecration rituals. It was in this brutal isolation that Shadar culture was forged, in exclusion, discipline, devotion to dark power, and vengeance.

### Contact with the Druls

On the plateau, the Shadars crossed paths with the Druls. The first contact brought tension and distrust. The Druls, proud and ancient, had guarded the secrets of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] for millennia; the newly established Shadars represented ambition and expansion. Since the Druls did not wish to colonize the surface and the Shadars had no interest in Vor-Ghol, their routes remained separate for a long time.

Over the years, both recognized what they had in common: the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|black gods]] and contempt for the races of light. Caution evolved into pragmatic collaboration. The gods of the Druls were incorporated into Shadar rituals, and the matriarchs of the Depths came to respect the strategic effectiveness of their surface allies. From this rapprochement arose various political and military pacts, long before the emergence of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] or the formal structure of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]]. Together, the two races launched coordinated campaigns of enslavement against deep gnomes, [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]], and other subterranean creatures, expanding their influence over Vor-Ghol and the plateau. Far from the eyes of the world, the Shadars slowly began expanding their influence, preparing the return of the shadow to the heart of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]].
