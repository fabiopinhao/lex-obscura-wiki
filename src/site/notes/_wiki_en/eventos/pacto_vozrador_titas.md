# Vozrador-Titans Pact for Thal’Serin

The Vozrador-Titans Pact is an agreement from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] that spared the sacred forest of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] from destruction and made it the source of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]]’s power. It is one of the most decisive mechanisms in the history of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] and the paradigm for the world’s divine politics: [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] do not conquer through brute force, but negotiate, bind, and collect.

### The Ambition for Green

Vozrador, the most complex of the black gods, turned his eyes toward Thal’Serin. Unlike other devils, who coveted the territories of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|white gods]] or demanded expansion, he pursued supremacy within his own domains of interest: control of the soul mattered more to him than possession of the world. His ambition turned toward green, not to conquer it by force, but to extract from it a power that no other devil would dare challenge.

### The Agreement

[[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]] and [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]], the primordial [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] who guarded Thal’Serin, entered the pact not out of fear, but because their interests converged: they wanted the forest intact, and that was exactly what Vozrador offered. From the agreement, unthinkable to the rest of the pantheon, arose a symbiosis between green and black.

He promised that the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] would never burn Thal’Serin, and that the forest would remain inviolate for as long as he had influence over his followers. In exchange, the titans allowed him to extract power from Thal’Serin itself. The sacred forest became the source of his strength, a prerogative no other devil ever had: draining the vigor of a titan sanctuary by consent, instead of fighting over it, made him the most feared devil in Reinkard for as long as he kept his word.

### Consequences

The pact explains why Thal’Serin survived the Reinkardic Wars, when so many other unprotected forests were burned. More than that, it established the pattern that would define Reinkard’s divine politics: devils’ power is built through agreement and binding, not mere conquest, and every agreement exacts its price.
