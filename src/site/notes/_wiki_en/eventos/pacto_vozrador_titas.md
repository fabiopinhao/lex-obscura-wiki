# Vozrador-Titans Pact for Thal’Serin

The Vozrador-Titans Pact is an agreement from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] that spared the sacred forest of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] from destruction and made it the source of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]]’s power. It is one of the most decisive mechanisms in the history of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]].

### The Ambition for Green

Vozrador, the most complex of the black gods, turned his eyes toward Thal’Serin. Unlike other [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], who coveted the territories of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|white gods]] or demanded expansion, he pursued supremacy within his own domains of interest: control of the soul mattered more to him than possession of the world. His ambition turned toward green, from which he intended to extract a power that no other devil would dare challenge.

### The Agreement

[[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]] and [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]], the primordial [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] who guarded Thal’Serin, entered the pact because their interests converged, with fear playing no part in the calculation: they wanted the forest intact, and that was exactly what Vozrador offered. From the agreement, unthinkable to the rest of the pantheon, arose a symbiosis between green and black.

He promised that the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] would never burn Thal’Serin, and that the forest would remain inviolate for as long as he had influence over his followers. In exchange, the titans allowed him to extract power from Thal’Serin itself, a prerogative no other devil ever had, and draining the vigor of a titan sanctuary by consent made him the most feared devil in Reinkard for as long as he kept his word.

### Consequences

The pact explains why Thal’Serin survived the Reinkardic Wars, when all other unprotected forests were burned. It also established the paradigm for Reinkard’s divine politics: devils build power through agreement and binding rather than through brute force, and they exact the price of every agreement.
