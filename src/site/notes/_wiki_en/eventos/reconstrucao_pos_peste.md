# Reconstruction and Rise of Myrgarde

The Reconstruction and Rise of Myrgarde is the period of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]] in which the continent recovered after the [[_wiki_en/eventos/sopro_do_eclipse\|Breath of the Eclipse]] and in which [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] established itself as the dominant power of the plains. It is not a single event, but the sociopolitical context that extends from the catastrophe to the founding of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]].

### A Cosmopolitan Continent

With the end of human incursions against the plateau, the continent began to rebuild. The dwarves opened the mountains and resumed trade with the races of the surface, and the shared catastrophe dissolved rivalries that centuries of diplomacy had failed to shake. Cities that had once closed themselves off into ethnic and religious factions reopened their gates with fewer restrictions, because rebuilding required hands and no one asked where those wielding the hammers came from. The cities of the plains became more cosmopolitan, with gnomes, humans, half-elves, and [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]] living together in mixed neighborhoods. The racial hierarchy persisted and prejudices did not die with the plague, but the need to survive reshaped the social fabric of the plains in a way that ideology had never achieved.

### The Rise of Myrgarde

It was on this weakened and cooperative continent that Myrgarde rose as the dominant power. The kingdom expanded until it became a great [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|empire]], annexing almost all the human city-states, some through peaceful means, others by force. The human race, divided before the plague into dozens of competing factions and kingdoms, no longer had the capacity to resist a centralized power that offered protection and stability. Myrgarde’s secret was twofold, and both components came from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]]: the influence of the [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]], settled in the region since before the kingdom’s founding, and the enduring alliance with [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]]. The Elarins supplied superior magical equipment, artifacts from [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]], and arcane knowledge that no other kingdom possessed. Myrgarde armed itself with the past to dominate the present.

### An Unstable Balance

The Second Era ended without the population of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] recovering to the numbers of the First. The Breath of the Eclipse had left a demographic deficit that centuries of rebuilding did not reverse, and the plains remained less populated, the cities less full, the armies less numerous. The imbalance between a weakened continent and a power on the rise made the coming conflict inevitable.
