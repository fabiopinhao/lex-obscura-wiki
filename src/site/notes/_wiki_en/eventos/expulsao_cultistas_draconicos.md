# Expulsion of the Draconic Cultists (Varnakian Origin)

The Expulsion of the Draconic Cultists was the event of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] in which the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] expelled from their territory the human tribes of the plateau that had developed dragon cults and bonded themselves to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|red gods]]. Forced to cross the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] westward, these tribes settled in the valleys of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]] and gave rise to the [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]] people.

### The Draconic Cults

Not all the pagans of the plateau were absorbed by the Shadars. Over generations living in the shadow of the mountain ranges, some of these humans developed practices of their own. They observed dragons with fascination and dread, and the fascination, over time, turned into veneration. Draconic cults arose among the tribes, and through them some drew closer to the red gods, some of whom were deities of fire and blood whose dominion extended over dragons and [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]]. It was among these tribes that the first humans with demonic physical traits appeared: vestigial horns, coppery skin, eyes with vertical pupils.

### The Expulsion

When the Shadars consolidated their rule, they did not tolerate chaotic cultists in their territory. Shadar logic was diabolical: contract, discipline, hierarchy. The draconic cults, bonded to demons, represented the opposite: chaos, instinct, destruction. Coexistence was impossible. The tribes bonded to red entities were expelled westward, forced to cross the Dorsal toward the lands that would come to be known as [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]]. Those that had not sealed pacts with crimson gods met a different fate: they were subjugated, converted, or sacrificed, as suited the Shadars.

### Two Frontiers

The expulsion defined two lasting frontiers: the ideological, between the diabolical and the demonic, black and red; and the geographical, between the plateau and Veinkard. Settled in the valleys around Varnak, these peoples lent the region’s name to their own race. The Varnakians carried with them their veneration of dragons and affinity with fire, and developed a culture of their own over eras.
