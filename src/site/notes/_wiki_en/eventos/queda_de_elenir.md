# Fall of Elenir

The Fall of Elenir, also called the Capture of the Crystal Fortress, was the conquest of the most celebrated of the [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]]’ magical citadels, which took place about thirty-three years ago, during the decade of final campaigns. An ally of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] since the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]] fell as the other elven citadels fell, one by one.

### Consequences

Its [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/nexus\|arcane nexuses]], the continent’s largest raw sources of magical reagents, were partially incorporated into the imperial infrastructure. The number of elven slaves soared, benefiting the slave masters and the capital’s economy. [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] still remained, and [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] did not intend to conquer it by force: he planned to turn it into a vassal state through diplomacy, without sending armies to lands his soldiers would be unable to bear living in.

### The Fendris

The dwarves of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], subjugated decades earlier during the western campaign, were already producing for the Cult in their own forges. It was there that the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]] lay, a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] relic that the dwarves had kept since the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] without ever understanding its true nature. After the conquest, a shadow lord took the dagger to his private gallery in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], treating it as a trophy.
