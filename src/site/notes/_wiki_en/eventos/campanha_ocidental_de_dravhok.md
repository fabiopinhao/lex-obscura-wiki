# Dravhok’s Western Campaign

Dravhok’s Western Campaign was the first major offensive led by the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] general Dravhok during the Reinkardic Wars, directed against the dwarven fortresses of the western mountain range. Instead of attacking [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] head-on, as so many generals had tried without success, Dravhok went around the Central [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] through the mountains, targeting the forges that armed the rival armies. The campaign swept the mountain range from north to south and culminated at [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], the largest forge on the continent.

### Dravhok’s Doctrine

What distinguished Dravhok began with the composition of his troops. Most Shadar generals maintained conservative ratios, one Shadar or [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] for every [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]], for fear of losing control of the legions. Dravhok operated with ratios his peers considered suicidal: his platoons included far more orcs than doctrine prescribed, and he delegated captain positions to veteran orcs, a role Shadar military culture reserved for its own race. Where others saw a risk of mutiny, Dravhok saw soldiers who fought more fiercely when they felt the hierarchy recognized their worth.

### The Capture of the Forges

The tactic required trust between races that rarely had it. Dravhok advanced across the surface with his legions, destroying the stone gates of the fortresses the dwarves had built to withstand centuries of siege. At the same time, Drul contingents commanded by matriarchs ascended through the underground, infiltrating through passages the dwarves themselves had opened unintentionally: in some mountains, mines dug too deep breached the boundaries of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], connecting to the underground world the Druls knew like the back of their hands. Fortress after fortress, the pattern repeated: the dwarves’ attention focused on the gates crumbling under the battering rams while death rose silently through the lower galleries.

The fortresses fell one after another, and with them the supply of weapons and armor that sustained the resistance on the plains. Each mountain taken was a forge lost to Myrgarde and gained by [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. The campaign culminated at Thandruk Varn.

### The Return as a Legend

Dravhok returned to the plateau as a legend. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] recognized what the soldiers already knew: no commander in the history of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] had conquered so much territory. He received the title of lead strategist of all the generals, a position without precedent in Morvgrad’s military hierarchy, and was elevated to councilor. [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]], the founder of the Shadow Cult, granted him his personal blessing, a distinction no other living Shadar had received.

Morvgrad’s admiration was no accident. The Shadars had long memories: they remembered their expulsion from the central regions of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] and their forced exile on the frozen plateau of the north. Dravhok was the general who was finally collecting the debt, and each conquered city sounded like vengeance and historical redress. He was also the first councilor to serve simultaneously as a general in the field: while the others governed from Morvgrad, administering faith, commerce, and logistics, he commanded from the plains, with steel in his hand and orcs at his back. In him, the Shadar people placed their hope of overthrowing the [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]] and taking Myrgarde, the capital of the rival empire.
