# Founding of Myrgarde

The Founding of Myrgarde is the act that established, in the First Era, the city that would become the largest kingdom on the continent and the symbolic heart of the alabastrine world. It arose from the union of five human citadels after the definitive expulsion of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] from the central lands, as a seal of victory for the celestial faith.

### Context

The victory over the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] brought a new imbalance. With them out of the way, an interplanar distortion occurred, and the alliance between ambitious humans and [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|black gods]], born at the end of [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]], proliferated in human cities. The Shadars, products of the first profane pacts, began to appear in growing numbers, and humanity became the preferred channel for the dark ambitions that had previously depended on the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]].

Throughout the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], the Shadars became feared and unwanted figures among humans themselves. Their origin, associated with diabolical alliances and heretical fanaticism, led to their rejection by the great kingdoms of the continent. Considered traitors to the faith and a threat to stability, they formed secret cults in the shadows of cities, in abandoned ruins, and in forgotten catacombs, while the dominant church, devoted to the celestial gods, persecuted them with purges and armed sanctifications.

The ideological clash between [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]] and Shadars became one of the forces that shaped the era. The Álvarars were consolidating their position as defenders of the status quo, while the Shadars, fueled by dark pacts and a culture of conquest and discipline, deepened their doctrine of absolute self-overcoming and began to see themselves as a new species. The elves, though more open to humans, never forgot the ancestral genocide and regarded the Shadars with silent bitterness.

### The Founding

With the definitive expulsion of the dark race from the central lands, the five human citadels that bordered one another united and founded [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]. The city was born as proof that the light had prevailed and that the central plains belonged to the celestial faith. Over centuries, Myrgarde would grow to become the largest kingdom on the continent and the symbolic center of the alabastrine world.
