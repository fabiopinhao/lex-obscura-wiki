# Eastern Campaign and Fall of Dernessal

The Eastern Campaign, or Campaign of the East, was the second major offensive by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] during the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Reinkardic Wars]], led by [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] General Dravhok against the human cities that formed the eastern belt under the protection of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]. Unlike the previous campaign, fought in the mountains, this one advanced across the eastern plains in a war of direct attrition, and culminated in the fall of Dernessal and an unprecedented retreat on the coast of [[_wiki_en/locais/valdris\|Valdris]].

### The Offensive on the Plains

The campaign was conceived as a joint effort: several generals, equal in rank, each at the head of their own legion, descended along the east of the continent. The battles on the plains were fought through direct attrition, without the shelter of mountain ambushes: besieged walls, open fields, cavalry charges against orc formations. All sides were bleeding.

As the Shadar generals fell in battle, their legions, far from the plateau and without a clear chain of command, joined the nearest general strong enough to protect them. Successive incorporations swelled Dravhok’s troops, and Dravhok reached Dernessal at the head of the largest legion the Cult had assembled under a single commander.

### Fall of Dernessal

Dernessal was the largest independent kingdom southeast of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] of Myrgarde, and for generations had resisted both incorporation by the Central Empire and shadow incursions. It fell. The area east of Myrgarde was subjugated, and the campaign continued to the Austral sea, sweeping through the coastal cities without encountering coordinated resistance.

### The Retreat at Valdris

At Valdris, the sea answered. Waves of a magnitude no natural storm could explain swept over the city and the legion camped nearby, without warning and without apparent cause, carrying away soldiers, equipment, and any ambition of coastal conquest. The order was to retreat, and the long journey back to the plateau crossed a continent that, for the first time in decades, saw a shadow army retreat.

The retreat did not undo the achievement. The campaigns had irreversibly altered the map of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]: the dwarven mountains of the west belonged to the Cult, the eastern belt of Myrgarde was dismantled, and the southern coastal cities had proved that no corner of the continent was beyond the reach of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]].
