# Silenced Nexuses at the Crystal Fortress

The Silenced Nexuses at the Crystal Fortress, also known as the Nexus Crisis, are an arcane anomaly in [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]] in which the crystal nexuses, although they remain active, were silenced from the inside out. The interference is deliberate, but its nature remains unknown.

### The Anomaly

Since the conquest of Elenir, the city’s arcane nexuses have been partially incorporated into the infrastructure of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] and have formed the foundation of a technological revolution. Gnomes relocated to the city assist in studies of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] contained in the crystals. These continue to pulse with magi, but the energy does not propagate: it is as though something had interposed a barrier between the source and the distribution channels. The crystals are active and, at the same time, mute.

### A Distinct Form of Sabotage

The crisis in Elenir differs from the others in its subtlety. In [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], the sabotage is visible: the forges stop, production falls, the numbers plummet. In Elenir, everything seems to work until one tries to extract what the crystals should provide. The difference suggests different agents, different methods, or a degree of sophistication that dwarven sabotage lacks.
