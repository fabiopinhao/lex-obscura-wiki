# Sabotage at the Forges of Thandruk Varn

The Sabotage at the Forges of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], also referred to as the Production Decline in Thandruk Varn, is a series of deliberate acts that reduces the output of the dwarven forges and alarms the imperial administration. Its nature and those responsible remain unknown, and its coincidence with the [[_wiki_en/eventos/desaparecimento_de_draven-thol\|disappearance of Draven-Thol]] deepens the mystery.

### Context

Thandruk Varn, subjugated by the Empire decades earlier, had become the only original dwarven fortress still operational on the continent. The production decline reveals that the subjugation of the clans was not as complete as [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] presumed.

### Logistical Risk

The reduction threatens a supply chain that feeds both the surface and the underground, through the tunnel that connects Thandruk Varn to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. If the sabotage persists, its consequences will spread far beyond the mountains.
