# Destruction of Lendalas

The Destruction of Lendalas, also called the Fall of Lendalas, was the destruction of one of the largest sacred forests of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]] during the final campaigns of the Reinkardic Wars. Consumed by alchemical fire, the forest gave way to the Waste of Lendalas, a gray expanse that remains barren.

### The Final Campaigns

Beginning around thirty-nine years ago, with [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] restructured and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] securing the new order in the capital, [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] returned to the battlefield with new generals. The orc troops, better trained than at any previous point in the Cult’s history, formed the bulk of the legions. Ten years of devastating conquests followed, and it was during this period that the densest forests of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] burned.

### The Waste of Lendalas

[[_wiki_en/locais/lendalas\|Lendalas]] was consumed by the alchemical fire unleashed by [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]]’s legions. What remained became the Waste of Lendalas: a gray expanse where the earth refuses to sprout and where, on stormy nights, voices seem to echo beneath the cauterized ground.

### Thal’Serin

Only the titanic forests, protected by ancient pacts with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], withstood the flames. [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] remained inviolate. The Cult’s strategy remained the same as that established in the first offensives: to wait for the dark corruption to accomplish what the armies could not.
