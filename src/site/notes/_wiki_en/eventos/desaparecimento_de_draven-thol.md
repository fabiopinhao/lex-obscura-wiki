# Disappearance of Draven-Thol

[[_wiki_en/personagens/draven-thol\|Draven-Thol]], a [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|justiciar]] assigned to the dwarven citadel of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], disappeared without a trace. The disappearance was inexplicable for a figure of his stature: Justiciars do not disappear in silence; they die in combat or are found. The absence of a body, witnesses, or any evidence makes the case unique in the institution’s history.

### The Case

The crisis coincided with sabotage at the dwarven forges of Thandruk Varn, and the overlap of events in the citadel suggests a coordinated disruption that goes beyond chance. The exact circumstances remain obscure.

The first sign that the disappearance was not routine did not come from a report. [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]], who still assumed he was on duty, recommended Draven-Thol to [[_wiki_en/personagens/gorvam_threnk\|Gorvam Threnk]], the Master of the Forges, for the forges’ diplomatic front, and the councilor faltered midway through the name, coughed twice, averted his gaze, and hastened to end the connection through the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]], without explaining his reaction.
