# Breaking of the Luminar Magical Barrier

The Breaking of the Luminar Magical Barrier was the depletion, during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], of the ancient barrier erected by the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] over the mountain ranges of the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]]. Corroded over decades by the dark corruption accumulated on the plateau, the barrier lost its entire arcane charge and opened [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]’s northern border.

### The Erosion of the Barrier

The dark corruption that had been accumulating on the plateau for generations consumed what remained of the Luminars’ magical barrier. The residual [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] that had sustained the enchantments in the mountain ranges for eras was depleted in the face of the growing diabolical presence. It took decades of silent erosion for the rock to lose its entire arcane charge.

### Consequences

In the immediate aftermath, the breaking had a limited effect: the dragons that the barrier had been erected to contain no longer inhabited the region, as the first migration had taken them to [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]] back in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]]. In the long term, however, the effect was devastating. Without the barrier, the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] began crossing the Dorsal freely, and the northern border, previously protected by the combination of [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] magic and draconic predation, lay open. The fortresses that the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] had built against human revenge campaigns became, by strategic accident, the only real containment.
