# Failed Attack on Thal’Serin

The Failed Attack on Thal’Serin, also called the First Invasion of Thal’Serin or the Titanic Defense of Thal’Serin, was the first major clash between the Cult and the forces of nature during the Reinkardic Wars. Fought in [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], the sacred forest closest to the plateau, it ended in defeat for the dark armies and defined the Cult’s strategy for all elven forests.

### The Attack

The sacred forest closest to the plateau was a logical target, and the strategy called for its destruction before any advance onto the plains. The dark armies entered through the northern edge with alchemical fire and steel. They killed many [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]] in the first days, burned entire clearings, and desecrated ancestral sanctuaries, seemingly on the verge of repeating what they had already done to smaller groves along the mountain ranges.

### The Defense

The forest responded. The earth trembled beneath the invaders’ feet, century-old roots burst from the soil like living walls, and shock waves hurled entire ranks of soldiers against the trunks. Fissures opened in the ground and swallowed platoons before they could retreat. At first, the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] did not recognize the hand of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] in the earthquakes or understand that the earth itself was declaring war on them. They withdrew with heavy losses.

### Consequences

The councilors of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] reassessed the situation. Thal’Serin did not control trade routes, did not guard mineral resources, and did not threaten any ongoing campaign, so they decided to wait, expecting the surrounding dark corruption to weaken the forest over time. The lesson defined the Cult’s strategy for all elven forests: every forest protected by avatars of titans was too great a risk for the investment, while the unprotected ones burned under alchemical fire, reduced to fields of ash and blackened trunks. The war in the forests produced confrontations on a divine scale: the titans responded when their domains were invaded, and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] of the dark armies manifested to face them, battles that devastated entire regions and confirmed that the Reinkardic Wars were also an extension of divine rivalries that predated any kingdom.
