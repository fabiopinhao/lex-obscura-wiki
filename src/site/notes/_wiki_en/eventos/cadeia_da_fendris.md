# Chain of Fendris

The Chain of [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]] designates the sequence of events that began with the theft of a dagger from the shadow lord’s private gallery in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Fendris, a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] relic that the dwarves of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]] had kept since the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] without understanding its nature, had been brought to the city as a trophy of conquest and displayed in the private museum of Councilor [[_wiki_en/personagens/dessian_broel\|Dessian Broel]], the Administrator of Resources.

### The Theft

The gallery robbery was carried out without leaving a trace, and the official investigation never solved it. The blade’s immediate destination remained the subject of contradictory rumors among the few circles that learned of it.

### The Chain of Custody

After the robbery, the blade passed through the network of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]] until it was confiscated by [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]], who was unaware of the dagger’s origin and original purpose: a weapon capable of killing Drakhonins, such as the female dragon [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]]. The chain of custody, which began with dwarven custody of a Luminar work, passed through aristocratic museums and criminal networks before ending up in hands no one had planned for.
