# Fall of Myrgarde and End of the Reinkardic Wars

The Fall of Myrgarde was the conquest of the last great city to resist the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] and marked the end of the Reinkardic Wars. After a three-year siege, the gates of the ancient human capital gave way, and shadow rule over [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] became absolute.

### The Last Wall

When the gates finally gave way, [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] appeared in person. It was the last time the monarch descended to the battlefield before his permanent isolation in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]]. On that occasion, he decreed in a speech the definitive abolition of the [[_wiki_en/magia/rito_de_ossarium\|Rite of Ossárium]], which became a capital heresy. The heart of the former Central Empire, seat of the church that had once persecuted the first [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], bowed beneath the shadow.

### The Fate of the Defenders

[[_wiki_en/personagens/auren\|Auren]], a winged guard of the Temple and aspiring paladin, had already been a prisoner of the empire for years, captured before the siege; with the fall, he was transferred to the Empire’s dungeons, a fate also shared by [[_wiki_en/personagens/iluvrae\|Ilúvrae]], an Elarin healer captured in [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]].

Gnomes who had prospered under the protection of the walls were enslaved in the deep forges. Few members of the clergy survived; among them, the old cleric [[_wiki_en/personagens/corvina\|Mother Corvina]], bent under the weight of decades. Humans who resisted or who knew too much were imprisoned in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], some sustained by necromancy so that oblivion would not free them.

### The Monarch’s Seclusion

With resistance reduced to isolated pockets and [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] as the last significant refuge, Arothistar reached the maximum extent of shadow rule over Reinkard. There were no more walls to bring down or armies to defeat. The monarch withdrew permanently to Mor’Severith and never descended from the tower again. Then began the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/protocolo_iconografico_da_coroa\|Iconographic Protocol of the Crown]]: effigies of Arothistar began to leave the tower from time to time and to be placed at significant locations in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], replacing the sovereign’s physical presence with a symbolic omnipresence.
