# Tensions and Threats of the Third Era

The principal sources of instability during the imperial period are grouped under the title Tensions and Threats of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]]. Although the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] has operated in apparent stability since the fall of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], geopolitical, demographic, and cultural pressures are accumulating along simultaneous vectors.

### Underground Alliance and Drul Distrust

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]] between [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]] remains formally intact, sustained by the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] that founded it centuries earlier. The [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] matriarchs, however, feel increasingly threatened by Morvgrad’s rapid development, growth in which they are not always included. Distrust ferments in the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]].

### Orc Migration

Orc migration, a longstanding problem, intensifies in the Third Era. Since the breaking of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars’]] magical barrier in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], orc tribes have been crossing [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|the Dorsal]] in increasing numbers. Without the wars that slaughtered [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] in droves, and with the species’ natural reproduction, the population buildup has become an administrative problem. The Council debates solutions, and the castration of female orcs practiced in some provinces is no longer enough to stem the flow. The far [[_wiki_en/locais/terras_geladas_do_norte\|frozen north]] from which they come is sparsely patrolled by the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], who avoid the cold as much as almost all races do, with the exception of the orcs themselves. Rumors about the causes multiply: news of plenty in the empire spreading among the northern tribes, [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna’s]] presence prowling the region and pushing tribes south, or the tribal wars that displace the defeated tribes.

### Attrition of the Justiciars

In the forty-four years since the founding, twenty-seven [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] have been ordained, and only seven survive at present. The mortality stems from the work itself: the Justiciars operate in Morvgrad and in all the provinces, in hostile territories and on missions that seldom forgive mistakes. [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], the first and the oldest, serves as dean at ninety-nine, the living memory of an institution that consumes its own members.

### Purple Serpent

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]] is older than the monarchy itself. The criminal organization operates in Morvgrad and Vor-Ghol, led by Shadars and Druls, with members of various races. Its lieutenants bear a horizontal scar at the base of the throat. The central base changes location periodically, sometimes in the depths of Ghilanna, sometimes on the plateau of Morvgrad, and no one knows for certain where the headquarters is at any given moment, an uncertainty that is deliberate.

### Titanic Forests and Shadow-Taint

All the titanic forests of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] are a refuge for rebel [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Gerut]] peoples, and fugitive humans. [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], the closest to the plateau and on the eastern edge of [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]], is merely the best known. The Shadars do not invade these forests directly, for the ancient pacts with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] protect them. They are patient, and they cast black curses over the zones of resistance. The phenomenon the druids call [[_wiki_en/magia/macula-sombria\|shadow-taint]], the rot that precedes imperial attacks, seeps through the roots before the Shadars appear at the borders: magic ages like a tree without water, drying from the inside out. Since shadow rule over Reinkard became firmly established, the process is accelerating. The Shadars hope that the accumulated corruption will, at some point, make the titans give up on these sanctuaries and leave the forests unprotected, ready for the fire.
