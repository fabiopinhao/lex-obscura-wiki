# Military Formalization of the Orcs

The Military Formalization of the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] was the turning point of the Reinkardic Wars, the process by which the Cult transformed tribal orc fury into battlefield discipline. It is also known as the Domestication of the Goblinoids. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], orcs already served the Cult as a defensive barrier and occasional brute force, pillaging the plains with [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] connivance, but pillage is not war, and disorganized bands do not win campaigns. The process began with the fortresses along the perimeter of the [[_wiki_en/locais/dhar-morveth\|Shadar Plateau]].

### The Failed Attempt

The first attempt used [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]], small, obedient creatures, for the logistical and administrative tasks that orcs did not perform. In practice, the kobolds lasted days. The orcs killed them for amusement or used them as throwing targets, not out of calculated cruelty, but out of instinctive aversion: they carried in their racial memory the terror of [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]] and the dragons that had hunted them in the mountain ranges for millennia, and any creature with a reptilian appearance provoked a hostility that no order from above could suppress.

### The Goblinoid Solution

The [[_wiki_en/racas/goblin\|goblins]], domesticated by the Shadars back in the Second Era, proved to be the missing piece. They did what the kobolds could not do in the presence of orcs: detailed administration, supply logistics, fortress maintenance, and the organization of guard shifts. These were tasks that the orcs despised and that the Shadars themselves refused to perform, as they did not consider dealing directly with orcs worthy of their time. The stewardry of the peripheral fortresses came to be handled almost exclusively by goblins, and with it came the structure that allowed the orcs to cease being bands and become regiments. Increased goblinoid reproduction depended on a specific resource: goblinoids multiplied in direct proportion to what they ate, and the food came from the tribute paid by [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]]. The logic formed a closed cycle, as the tribute from the first conquered region fed the creatures that organized the armies that would conquer the following regions.

### The New Chain of Command

With the peripheral forts organized and goblins managing the rear, the Shadars redesigned the chain of command. The orcs came to be led by Shadar and [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] officers, within a hierarchy that respected tribal culture, in which the strongest commanded the platoons, but subordinated the tribal chiefs to the Cult’s strategic command. Orc forts multiplied around the plateau, and what had once been surveillance outposts became a network of headquarters. The transformation changed the nature of the war: in the second half of the conflict, the orcs became the spearhead of the devastation of the plains, and Shadars and Druls withdrew to command positions. Losses became primarily orc losses, and the Cult absorbed them with a coldness that horrified the generals of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], as orcs reproduced at a rate that no human race could match and the goblins ensured that each new regiment was equipped before marching.
