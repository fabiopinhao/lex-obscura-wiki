# Move into Mor’Severith

The Move into Mor’Severith was the seizure of the Council elite’s former library and residence by [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], about forty years ago. The monarch emptied the building and converted it into his private tower. The remaining members of the Council who still inhabited the upper floors were relocated to smaller quarters in the city, and no one protested.

### The Monarch’s Tower

[[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]] became the absolute center of power in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]: the residence, office, and fortress of a monarch who, over time, would no longer go beyond its walls. The same building had witnessed, fifteen years earlier, the purge of the councilors that had consolidated Arothistar’s rule.

### Consequences

In the year following the move, Arothistar began the second phase of the monarchy: the final campaigns that would consume the last forests, the last citadels, and the last wall on the continent.
