# Founding of the Justiciars

The Founding of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] was the creation, about forty-four years ago, of the judicial arm of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], instituted by [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] with power of life and death over subjects and dissidents. The move reshaped the judicial structure of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and ensured the enforcement of both the old and the new law established by the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]].

### Context

Part of the shadow lord elite still resisted the changes imposed by the Verbum Arkhon. The Justiciars arose in response to this internal resistance: a body charged with enforcing the monarch’s law without distinguishing between external enemies and dissent within the Empire itself.

### Activities

In the early years, several shadow lords considered dissidents against the new order were executed, sending an unequivocal message that the monarch’s law also reached internal resistance. At the same time, spies from [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] were discovered in Morvgrad: almost all were executed, some sent to the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]].

[[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], a former Templar of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], was the first Justiciar appointed and would serve forty-four years in the office.
