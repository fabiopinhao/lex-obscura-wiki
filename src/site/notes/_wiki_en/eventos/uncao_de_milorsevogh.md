# Anointing of Milorsevogh

The Anointing of Milorsevogh was the ceremony in which [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] was anointed Justiciar at the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], at the beginning of the present. The rite was conducted by the sorcerer [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]], and during it Milorsevogh received the three instruments of office: the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/spina_lexis\|Spina Lexis]], the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]], and the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]].

### The Gesture of the Sword

In the ceremony’s central gesture, Jaakurs laid the consecrated sword across Milorsevogh’s open palms, with its edge facing the anointed man himself. The law reaches the Justiciar’s hands as a weight he bears, without his grasping it by the hilt. Then the officiant took back the sword, and at the end of the rite two initiates collected it, along with the tome and the sphere.

### The Seventh Justiciar

Milorsevogh was the seventh living Justiciar, out of twenty-seven ordained over four and a half decades, since the founding of the order. The twenty who preceded him in death did not fall to old age: they fell in the field, in the provinces, on the missions the institution required of each member.

### The Dean

[[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], the first and oldest of the Justiciars, watched the ceremony from the gallery of the Temple, beside [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]], his pupil. At ninety-nine years of age, he had served for forty-four, and his faith in [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], the god to whom he had dedicated his entire life, remained unshakable.

### Afterward

Once the anointing was complete, Milorsevogh departed for [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]] on his first official mission.
