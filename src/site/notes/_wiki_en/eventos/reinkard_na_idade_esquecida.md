# Reinkard in the Forgotten Age

Before the counting of the eras, [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] belonged to the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]]. Light elves and wielders of the continent’s oldest magic, they did not rule as conquerors: their supremacy was arcane and cultural, not territorial. They lived in harmony with the cycles of the earth and with the races that respected their borders, and for eras nothing disturbed the balance they maintained.

### The Luminar Citadels

The Luminars did not confine themselves to a single type of terrain. They adapted their preferred regions to their own tastes, whether ponds, hills, plains, or woods, and the soil became imbued with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]]. A citadel could rise on the shore of a glacial lake or in the heart of a clearing, and the surrounding land absorbed centuries of arcane presence. For this reason, even today, some Luminar ruins retain magi strong enough for the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] to erect artificial nexuses upon their foundations.

### The Abundance of Magi

In [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]], white, green, and blue entities exerted influence over the material plane with far greater intensity than in the present day. Magi was abundant, the earth responded generously to prayers and rituals, and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] walked among the forests with a frequency that would seem inconceivable today.

### The Decline

This abundance began to recede after the extinction of the Luminars, diminishing from era to era. Those who lived through that time, such as the elder dragons, perceive the difference: magi is thinner, the divine response more distant. On the other hand, [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] and [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], whose influences are more direct and targeted than those of the gods of light, encounter less and less resistance to exercising their powers on the material plane.
