# Qadesh

Qadesh is a city-state nestled in the eastern desert, in the transition zone between [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]]. A vassal of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], it owes its continental reputation to curved blades forged from lunar steel and to the martial tradition of its warriors.

### Geography and Position

Its border position defines the nature of Qadesh: a dry port between two worlds, a trading hub where imperial caravans cross paths with [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]] merchants and trade flows despite border tensions. Its distance from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and its commercial usefulness guarantee the city a functional autonomy that other provinces do not enjoy. Qadesh pays tribute and accepts the purple seal; in exchange, the Empire does not ask what passes through its gates or for whom its blades are forged.

### Population

The population is predominantly human. [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] hold administrative and military positions tied to the imperial presence, while Frenims from Neranthis maintain trading enclaves and arcane craft workshops. The harmony of their coexistence is a matter of calculation: everyone profits when trade flows.

### Lunar Steel and Martial Tradition

The technique for producing lunar steel remains a secret of the local smiths. The material is not a magical artifact; its edge, which other steels cannot match, comes from a tempering process tied to lunar cycles and ores found exclusively in the desert mines. The curved blade is both a tool and a cultural symbol. The assassins who wield it form the deadliest echelon of a tradition rooted in society, outside any clandestine guild: in Qadesh, carrying a blade is a birthright, and mastering it is the measure of a man.

### Trade

Qadesh sells to whoever pays. Its blades reach markets as different as the stalls of [[_wiki_en/locais/ezrath\|Ezrath]] and the personal arsenals of informants such as [[_wiki_en/personagens/tibaut\|Tibaut]]. The city does not ask whom the steel serves, and this mercantile indifference is its greatest protection and its greatest infamy. Qadesh also exports glass, fragile and expensive, which is transported with the care reserved for anything that cannot withstand a single jolt.
