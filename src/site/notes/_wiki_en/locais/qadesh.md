# Qadesh

Qadesh is a city-state nestled in the eastern desert, in the transition zone between [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]]. A vassal of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], it owes its continental reputation to curved blades forged from lunar steel and to the martial tradition of its warriors.

### Geography and Position

Its border position defines the nature of Qadesh: a dry port between two worlds, a trading hub where imperial caravans cross paths with [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]] merchants and trade ignores the tensions that maps draw. Its distance from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and its commercial usefulness guarantee the city a functional autonomy that other provinces do not enjoy. Qadesh pays tribute and accepts the purple seal; in exchange, the Empire does not ask what passes through its gates or for whom its blades are forged.

### Population

The population is predominantly human, but Qadesh is not a land of just one race. [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] hold administrative and military positions tied to the imperial presence, while Frenims from Neranthis maintain trading enclaves and arcane craft workshops. Their coexistence is harmonious through calculation rather than principle: everyone profits when trade flows.

### Lunar Steel and Martial Tradition

Qadesh’s fame comes from curved blades forged from lunar steel, a material whose production technique remains a secret of the local smiths. Lunar steel is not a magical artifact, but its tempering process, tied to lunar cycles and ores found exclusively in the desert mines, produces an edge that other steels cannot match. The curved blade is both a tool and a cultural symbol. The assassins who wield it do not form a clandestine guild, but the deadliest echelon of a tradition rooted in society: there, carrying a blade is a birthright, and mastering it is the measure of a man.

### Trade

Qadesh sells to whoever pays. Its blades reach markets as different as the stalls of [[_wiki_en/locais/ezrath\|Ezrath]] and the personal arsenals of informants such as [[_wiki_en/personagens/tibaut\|Tibaut]]. The city does not ask whom the steel serves, and this mercantile indifference is both its greatest protection and its greatest infamy. Steel is not the only commodity that leaves its gates: Qadesh also exports glass, fragile and expensive, which is transported with the care reserved for anything that cannot withstand a single jolt.
