# Council Hall

The Council Hall, also called the Obsidian Circle, is a monumental vault of living stone in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], within [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], where the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] meets. It accommodates about fifty people and serves as a political barometer of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]: what is debated there defines the health of [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] dominion.

### The Chamber

At the center, a circular table surrounds a pit of pitch that reflects the torches with an oily sheen, and old pitch seals the floor slabs. The rough granite seats, scored with coarse runes, are reserved for [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] and generals, distinct from the silk and gold seats intended for the Archons. A larger, swiveling chair dominates the head of the table. On the ceiling, twelve obsidian statues of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] watch over the debates; the twelfth, dedicated to [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], stands out for its wear, in contrast to the others. During one of the sessions, [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] rests his gaze on the worn figure of Vozrador and feels the ancient faith come undone along with the sculpture.

### The Agenda Items

The Council’s discussions reveal the Empire’s simultaneous crises: disputes in [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], magical instability in [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], uncontrolled [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] migration, and tension within [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|the Underground Alliance]].
