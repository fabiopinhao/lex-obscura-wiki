# Khargoth

Khargoth is the arcane tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], in the sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], dedicated to teaching the shadow schools of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. The name combines Khar, tower, and Goth, magic. Built of obsidian, it houses about five hundred members organized into hierarchical circles of access to knowledge, a system that mirrors the structure of the Cult: advancement through merit, but merit defined by loyalty.

### Function

The tower operates as both a university and an arcane tribunal. It teaches, watches, and punishes. On the twentieth floor, a corridor-library holds grimoires of curses, treatises on the seven schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], and, in the third circle, a poison laboratory. Black ergot, a hallucinogenic fungus from [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], circulates among the students. The laboratories of the arcane district also produce crunchy candies, flavored with spices and volatile salts.

### Discipline and Prohibitions

The spells of [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]] are forbidden within the tower because of past incidents that brought chaos: uncontrolled curses whose exact nature remains deliberately obscure. The price of study is the central doctrine of the house. The master necromancer Zarukh Veymar coined the maxim that sums it up: whoever delves into curses ends up marked, just as pyromancers burn themselves, cryomancers freeze, and electromancers fall dead. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]], who studied at Khargoth before his anointing, bears a dark mark on his wrist that extends onto his forearm, living proof of Veymar’s maxim, and which he himself attributes to delving deeper into Anatho. The mark lies dormant for now, and Milor asked [[_wiki_en/personagens/ravhor\|Ravhor]] that, if it awoke, he remember [[_wiki_en/locais/valdris\|Valdris]] and do what had to be done. Later, Milor violated the prohibition by invoking Malagoth within the tower, and his wrist throbbed as a reminder of the price.

### Setting

The tower’s basalt courtyard serves the Cult’s ceremonies. It was there that [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] gathered his lieutenants to announce his promotion to general and introduced [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]], still a child, to the platoon. [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] questioned the presence of [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] captains on grounds devoted to arcane knowledge, suggesting that the ceremony would be better suited to [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_morthar\|Khar Morthar]]. The tower also holds what is known about the imprisonment of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], a subject of investigation within its walls.
