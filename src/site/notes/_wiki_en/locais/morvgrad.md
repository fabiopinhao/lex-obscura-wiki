# Morvgrad

Morvgrad is the capital of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], built on the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau of [[_wiki_en/locais/dhar-morveth\|Dhar-Morveth]]. It was founded by [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]] in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]] with the unification of the diabolical temples, and is the political, religious, and military center of the Shadar dominion. The name means “City of the Tomb” in the ancient Shadar language; seen from afar, it resembles an obsidian crown embedded in the skull of the world.

### The City

Built of obsidian and basalt, it occupies three levels of the black plateau, connected by monumental stairways, arcane elevators, and secret passages. Its verticality reflects the hierarchy of the cult: the higher the elevation, the greater the status; descending into the depths is the fate of criminals and merchants of illicit goods. In the Lower Ring, an alley whose ground never dries brings together open ditches, clandestine tanneries, and pawn taverns, amid beggars, informants who sell secrets for a bowl of soup, and semiferal dogs. A vertical elevator spanning a thousand fathoms connects it to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. During significant events, phenomena of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/noctiluz\|noctiluz]] rend the sky. More than half a million souls inhabit the capital, in the largest urban concentration in the Shadar dominion.

### Order and Economy

Crimes are rare not because of virtue, but because of the certainty of punishment: executions are public, creative, and sometimes prolonged for days. Trade treaties are sealed with binding curses that automatically punish anyone who breaches a contract, and certain [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]] lineages fulfill hereditary soul contracts.

### Locations

The capital occupies three levels of the plateau and encompasses dozens of structures, including at least twelve diabolical temples, some served by seven or eight [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]]. The middle ring, [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], is the administrative and religious core, where the [[_wiki_en/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]], the registry tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_tabreth\|Khar’Tabreth]], and [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_morthar\|Khar Morthar]], the Tower of Death, are located. Within it, the sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]] houses the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], where the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] are anointed, the arcane tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], where the shadow schools are taught, and [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], the isolated tower from which [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] rules. Completing the city are the dungeons of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]]; the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_vozrador\|temple of Vozrador]], cursed and off-limits, and the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_velmorain\|temple of Velmorain]]; the elite neighborhood of [[_wiki_en/locais/ezrath\|Ezrath]]; the [[_wiki_en/locais/fossa_dos_suplicantes\|Pit of the Supplicants]]; and the [[_wiki_en/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]], where the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]] operates.
