# Ghol’Mournak

Ghol’Mournak is the underground level of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], connected to the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. Beneath the surface city, it houses dungeons, markets, forges, and chambers that descend toward the depths beneath the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]].

### Structures

The dungeons, called [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], extend to the depths of Vor-Ghol and include the [[_wiki_en/locais/coracao_oco\|Hollow Heart]]. In the [[_wiki_en/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]], the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/mercado_negro\|black market]] operates on a semiofficial basis, under the control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]], led by [[_wiki_en/personagens/dribbel_olgund\|Dribbel]]. The [[_wiki_en/locais/poco_etereo\|Ethereal Well]] serves as the central communications nexus of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]]: all transmissions converge there before being redirected. Deep forges produce artifacts and armaments.

Farther below lie the lower galleries, where vast cauldrons of pitch burn in silence. [[_wiki_en/personagens/nyx_ara\|Nyx’Ara]], an oracle of shifting appearance, practices there. They are reached through the [[_wiki_en/locais/camara_de_othuun\|Chamber of Othuun]], one of the passages leading from [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], by a descent of five hundred steps or by the elevator.

### Stone and Metal

The predominant material throughout the city is living obsidian, enchanted volcanic stone that absorbs light during the day and releases it as a sickly glow at night. Metals are scarce, reserved for armaments, chains, and instruments of torture.
