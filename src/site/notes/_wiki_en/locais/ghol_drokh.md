# Ghol’Drokh

Ghol’Drokh is the underground dungeon complex of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], excavated beneath the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau at an unknown depth. Under the control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], it serves simultaneously as an imperial prison, an interrogation chamber, and an instrument of institutional terror. The name (*Ghol*, “pit,” and *Drokh*, “that consumes”) designates the place where the Empire deposits those it intends to forget without granting them the relief of death.

### Structure

The complex is organized into descending levels of severity. The upper floors house political and military prisoners in conventional cells with blackened iron bars; the corridors have grooved walls, floors polished by the passage of jailers, and an atmosphere that leaves its mark on any visitor: wet iron, old fungus, ammonia, stale sweat, spaced knocks coming from nowhere. The farther one descends, the more administrative civilization gives way to the apparatus of torture: rooms with stone tables, hooks, and instruments, doors sealed with containment runes. In the deepest levels, imperial necromancers keep the condemned conscious far beyond the natural endurance of the flesh; the sigils that feed this magic, drawn with clotted blood and soot, degrade over time.

### The Hollow Heart

The intermediate and terminal levels bear the name [[_wiki_en/locais/coracao_oco\|Hollow Heart]], where the acoustics of the rock distort sound more strongly and the twin cells reserved for prisoners of special interest are located. The name circulates by metonymy as a designation for the entire complex. The distortion can already be heard on the stewards’ floor, in the upper levels, and intensifies as one descends.

### Drokh’Malahar

Below the Hollow Heart, the abyssal floors bear the name [[_wiki_en/locais/drokh_malahar\|Drokh’Malahar]], the “rooms of damnation”: an extreme regime of torture where the consciousness of the condemned is prolonged indefinitely.

### Prison Hierarchy

The administration operates with a racial division of duties. Shadar stewards control the upper floors and the records; [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] serve as jailers in the lower levels, work that the Shadars consider degrading. After a security incident, new protocols restricted access, and only orcs were allowed to descend to the deepest levels. The office of head executioner is held by [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Velshard]], relegated to the underground for having invoked [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]] against Lieutenant [[_wiki_en/personagens/rhazamir\|Rhazamir]] inside the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], an art proscribed on sacred ground. [[_wiki_en/personagens/ulgorth\|Ulgorth]], an orc one fathom, two meters, tall, with layered scars and yellowed tusks, serves as overseer of the orc jailers. Beneath the jailers work the fajardores, orcs charged with cleaning the shallower levels, who move about with iron buckets and pass those descending without raising their eyes. The deepest levels are not cleaned: few descend that far.
