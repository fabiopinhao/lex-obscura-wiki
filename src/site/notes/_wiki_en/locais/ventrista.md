# Ventrista

Ventrista is an agricultural region at the base of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau, adjacent to [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and under the rule of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. Its fertile lands and peasant villages feed the Empire. The name derives from ancient Shadar *ventrus*, womb that nourishes.

### Geography

A strip of fertile land at the base of the plateau, crossed by the [[_wiki_en/locais/rio_parveni\|River Parveni]]. It is home to about fifty thousand inhabitants spread across agricultural villages. The settlement of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]] is located in the region.
