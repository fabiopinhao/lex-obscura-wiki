# Thamakard

Thamakard is the material plane, the physical world inhabited by its peoples. It contains the continents of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], in the center, [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], to the west beyond the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]], and [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]], to the east beyond the swamps. No recorded culture has reliable knowledge of what exists beyond these maritime boundaries.

### Shape and Contention

Thamakard is flat, not spherical. Earlier ages believed otherwise, and were wrong. No god owns it, but many vie for it: it is the battleground for all divine forces. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] functions as a universal alchemical corridor, linking Thamakard to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/planos_divinos\|divine planes]], and it is through it that [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] circulates.

### The Decline of Magi

The abundance of magi in Thamakard declines with each age following the extinction of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]]. In the present day, [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] and [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]] face less and less resistance to exercising their powers on the material plane.
