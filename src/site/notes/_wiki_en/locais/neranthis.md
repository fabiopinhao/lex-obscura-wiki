# Neranthis

Neranthis is a continent east of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], more than one thousand leagues east of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], beyond a region of swamps, inhabited by peoples of sidereal affinity and kept independent of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. Its tone is mysterious, erudite, and isolationist.

### Geography

The geography is hostile: an excess of lakes, along with swamps and rivers that hinder travel. The climate is too wet for direct [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] rule, which has helped preserve the continent’s independence.

### Peoples

Neranthis is the land of three peoples linked to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]]: the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]], and the [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidans]]. The Frenims distanced themselves from the shadows of Reinkard, fearing domination. The undines live in the far east, on the ocean coasts, unknown to the Shadars.

### Imperial Interest

Despite its independence, the continent does not escape the Shadow Empire’s attention. [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] harbors a plan to turn Neranthis into a vassal state through diplomacy, rather than direct conquest, pursued through an intermediary.
