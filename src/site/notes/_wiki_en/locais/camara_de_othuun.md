# Chamber of Othuun

The Chamber of Othuun is a complex in [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], and one of the passages to [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]]. It is under independent control and does not answer to the formal hierarchy of the Cult.

### Access

The facade is a marble seal engraved into the mountainside, with onyx gargoyles at the portals and iron gates under guard. The two routes to the deep level lead from the vestibule: a spiral staircase of five hundred steps, in the eastern spirals, and an elevator. The entrance to the staircase is in the second alcove after the Hall of Echoes, marked with the descent sigil. During the descent, the steps whisper names of buried secrets.

### The Oracle

[[_wiki_en/personagens/nyx_ara\|Nyx’Ara]], an oracle of shifting appearance and ambiguous nature, receives visitors at the end of the descent, in the lower galleries of Ghol’Mournak, beside the pitch pits. [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]] recommended that [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum]] visit her.
