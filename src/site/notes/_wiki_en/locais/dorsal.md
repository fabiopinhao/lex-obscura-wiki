# Dorsal

The Dorsal is an imposing mountain range that curves around the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau in an arc, from east to west through the northeast, north, and northwest, with most of its length within [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. To the south there is no mountain range: the southeast, south, and southwest form the descent from the plateau, and it is through there that one reaches [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]]. It is also called the Mountain Ranges of Reinkard, the Mountain Ranges of the North, the Mountains of the West, or the western frontier, depending on the section being discussed. Its passes are limited and dangerous, the crossing routes controlled, and few Shadar expeditions cross it regularly. [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]] is the only [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] seen flying over it, a singularity that no record explains.

### The Two Sections

The northern section is an internal frontier of Reinkard: it separates the Shadar plateau from the [[_wiki_en/locais/terras_geladas_do_norte\|northern frozen lands]], without dividing any kingdom. [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] raids descend through it.

The western section separates Reinkard from [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]]. The controlled crossings lie there, about five hundred to six hundred leagues from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], and it is there that the reach of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s purple seal ends, without crossing the mountain range.

The northernmost part of the arc touches the capital itself: there rises the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]], twin to the tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], which merges with its base, and a bridge without a railing connects the Ocularium to the trail up the peak. The peak belongs to both the Dorsal and Morvgrad.

### Twofold Barrier

In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|Forgotten Age]] and the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], the Dorsal served as a twofold barrier, geographic and arcane. The [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] imbued the rock with enchantments, sealing passes and preventing dragons from crossing the mountains toward the plains of Reinkard. The barrier required no active maintenance: residual [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] sustained it for eras, even after the fragmentation of the Luminars.

### The Breaking of the Barrier

In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], the shadow corruption accumulated on the Shadar plateau consumed the residual magi, and the arcane barrier dissipated. The immediate consequences were limited, since the dragons had already migrated to Veinkard, but over time the orcs began crossing freely, in a growing migratory flow toward the plateau. The Shadar orcish fortresses became the only real restraint along the northern frontier. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]], the Dorsal is exclusively a geographic barrier, and its arcane component is no longer detectable.

### The Peak of Nethys

Its highest point along the edge facing the plateau is the Peak of Nethys, a former dragon’s nest. [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]] inhabited the peak for most of the First Era, hunting orcs on the slopes and serving as an involuntary living barrier, until the shadow corruption made remaining there untenable and she migrated to [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]], in Veinkard. Since then no dragon has occupied it again.

### Boundary of the Empire

The western section of the Dorsal defines the functional boundary of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. Arothistar does not cross it out of strategic calculation: even if he could dominate the valleys of Varnak, Varna and the allied Draconids would make it impossible to maintain a province there, and Morvgrad fears losing battles above almost all else.
