# Ghol’Maveth

Ghol’Maveth is the district of the deepest chambers of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]], at the bottom of the underground complex of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. There lie the enchanted pitch wells, reservoirs of black pitch treated with magic, and from there are extracted the perpetual embers, which burn without being consumed.

### The Pitch Wells

The Wells of Ghol’Maveth are more than just a source of material. Enchanted to mirror the signals of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], they function as regional repeaters for the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]] communication network: transmissions converge at the [[_wiki_en/locais/poco_etereo\|Ethereal Well]] before being redirected, and the pitch wells extend the signal’s range.
