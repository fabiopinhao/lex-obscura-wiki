# The Lesser Gallery

The Lesser Gallery is an underground corridor in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. When passable, it is a preferred route for caravans; its unpredictability, however, makes it part of the logistical calculations for any transport operation in the Depths.

### Geography

The corridor is subject to periodic closure by underground tides. When the tides block it, travelers and caravans are forced to take detours along alternative routes such as [[_wiki_en/locais/eixo_de_drenn\|the Axis of Drenn]], which presents its own risks, including traces of [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídars]].
