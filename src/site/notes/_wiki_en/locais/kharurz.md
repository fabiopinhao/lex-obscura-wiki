# Kharûrz

Kharûrz is one of the [[_wiki_en/locais/fortalezas_orquicas_do_planalto\|orcish fortresses of the plateau]], a garrison on the perimeter of [[_wiki_en/locais/dhar-morveth\|Dhar-Morveth]] under the control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. In the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], it figures among the garrisons that reported clashes with the waves of wild [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] pressing against the plateau in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]]. It is also the fort that [[_wiki_en/personagens/grub\|Grub]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/mur\|Mur]], of [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]]’s guard, come from. Rumor has it that an old orc woman there filled half the fort with her children each season, which is not enough to say whether the two are brothers by blood.
