# Thervad

Thervad is a domain conquered by the Shadow Empire and subjugated by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], in central [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], in the orbit of [[_wiki/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], between two hundred and fifty and three hundred leagues from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Its cathedral was converted into a stable as part of the policy of eliminating symbols of spiritual resistance that also affected [[_wiki_en/locais/orandia\|Orandia]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/danvorn\|Danvorn]].

The alchemists of Thervad form one of the scholarly schools that debate the nature of the [[_wiki/magia/macula-sombria\|shadow-taint]]. In their view, the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] of the material plane simply runs out, like a vein of ore or a spring that dries up, in a natural process accelerated by excessive use. The druids attribute the phenomenon to a deliberate infection sown by [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]].
