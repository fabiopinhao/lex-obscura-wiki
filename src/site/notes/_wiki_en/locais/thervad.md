# Thervad

Thervad is a domain conquered by the Shadow Empire and subjugated by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], in central [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], in the orbit of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], between two hundred and fifty and three hundred leagues from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Its cathedral was converted into a stable as part of the policy of eliminating symbols of spiritual resistance that also affected [[_wiki_en/locais/orandia\|Orandia]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/danvorn\|Danvorn]].

The alchemists of Thervad constitute a school whose authority is recognized in the debate over the depletion of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] on the material plane. They maintain that it is exhausted like a vein of ore or a spring that dries up. They debate this explanation with the druids, who attribute the phenomenon to a deliberate infection sown by [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]].
