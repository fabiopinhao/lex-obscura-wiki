# Niedellin

Niedellin is a small farming village in [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]], the strip of fertile land at the base of the [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] plateau that produces grain and livestock for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]]. It lies isolated from the main routes, southeast of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau and near the forest of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], with about a thousand inhabitants. From the village to the foot of the plateau is a day’s ride, and from there another two days to the first gate of Morvgrad, totaling three days on the road. It is subordinate to the Cult of Shadows, and the banners of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] hang over its towers.

### Geography

The [[_wiki_en/locais/rio_parveni\|River Parveni]] cuts through the village in a lazy curve before widening into a lagoon. Fields of barley and wheat stretch in regular rows, and near the mill stands a fermenting house that produces beer in abundance. On the small hill above the lake stands the chapel, spacious and roughly built, with a squat tower, served by [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Father Abramis]]. Behind it, along a narrow path, lies the field of the dead: crooked stones behind a wall that does not reach knee height, a gate on leather hinges, boards with shallowly carved names and unhewn river stones, generations in the same grave. It is the only land a human owns in Niedellin. The central square, at the foot of the small hill, is packed earth, with a stretch paved with stone in front of the chapel steps.

### Under the Loose Yoke

The village is under the provincial lord [[_wiki_en/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Merari]], and imperial control is lax: the banners hang like old rags, the patrols drag their feet, and the garrison of [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] is fat and undisciplined, unlike the paranoid zeal of other settlements. The [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]] population lives under less oppression than usual, fed and relatively free, though watched. Command of the guard falls to [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]] of [[_wiki_en/locais/karanthia\|Karanthia]], a notable anomaly because he is a Pindoriar in a position of armed command. The unusual prosperity of the fields and the absence of patrols draw attention as a symptom of something out of place.

### Resistance

There is active resistance in the region: rebels such as [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] and human refugees operate from Thal’Serin, depending on supplies from the village. Niedellin is the starting point of [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]]’s investigation.
