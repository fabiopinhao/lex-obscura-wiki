# The Ocularium

The Ocularium is a ceremonial room at the top of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], and serves as [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s personal center of power. It houses the instruments of imperial communication, statues of dark entities, and a painted dome that serves as an interface for the monarch’s rites.

### The Chamber

At the center stands the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]], a giant onyx sphere used for imperial communications. Statues of dark entities occupy the room, and Arothistar carves a new statue there, still unfinished. The statues have physical effects on smaller creatures: those who approach may experience nausea, a change in weight, and heat, as happens to [[_wiki_en/personagens/fing\|Fing]].

### The Celestial Dome

The ceiling features a painted celestial dome that functions as a visual interface. Arothistar addresses the deities while looking up, never at the statues.

### The Bridge and the Effigy

A bridge without a railing connects the Ocularium to the trail to the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]]. An animated effigy of Arothistar guards the passage: its onyx eyes light up silver when activated, and it speaks with its master’s voice. It is through the bridge that his power reaches those who arrive: from inside the Ocularium, with his fist glowing cerulean, Arothistar closes a circle of runes on the bridge’s floor that traps the visitor, as in the capture of [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]].
