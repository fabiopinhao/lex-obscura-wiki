# Pit of the Purge

The Pit of the Purge is an execution site in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], under the control of the Shadow Empire. It is the default destination for [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]] and petty criminals: “summary dispatch” is the administrative term for execution without a formal trial, and the Pit is where these sentences end. The name is functional, not metaphorical. The Pit opens beneath the city, and the fall is fatal.

### Function

Imperial movement restrictions cite the Pit as a consequence for violations. Lesser races traveling through forbidden zones, such as Murans, [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]], and goblinoids, are detained and frequently dispatched here.

In [[_wiki_en/locais/ezrath\|Ezrath]], [[_wiki_en/personagens/korven\|Korven]] captured the Muran [[_wiki_en/personagens/skinitz\|Skinitz]] for stealing pralines; Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]] ordered him dispatched to the Pit of the Purge, and Korven took him there. Skinitz’s brother, [[_wiki_en/personagens/skanivik\|Skanivik]], escaped, turning his face back one last time before disappearing around a corner.
