# Well of Lamentations

The Well of Lamentations is an old salt mine beneath [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], now used as a prison. Iron cages hang over the well, the hanging cells where the prisoners are held.

### The Wind and the Silence

The rising wind carries crystals that dry out the throat until it becomes impossible to scream.

### Harveth, the Architect

Harveth, the architect of the well, tested the first cell on himself. What remained of him were bones polished by salt and silence.
