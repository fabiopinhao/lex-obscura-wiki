# Temple of Vozrador

The temple of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] is a ruin from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] in a district of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] now abandoned and off-limits by order of the monarch. It is a structure from a time when stone obeyed song, not the chisel, and holds a mystery that no one has been able to explain.

### The Anachronism

The temple was sealed before the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/protocolo_iconografico_da_coroa\|Iconographic Protocol of the Crown]], and yet an effigy of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] watches over the ruin. The image should not exist there. The question remains why the monarch’s effigy guards the sanctuary of a god he imprisoned.

### Vozrador and the Ruin

While he was worshiped, Vozrador had a unique characteristic among the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]]: he accepted only one [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templar]] at a time, while the other temples maintained several, some seven or eight. When [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] investigated the place, he found it infested with undead, the result of the necromancy that [[_wiki_en/personagens/qaelun\|Qaelun]] practiced among the ruins.

### The Interior

The entrance hall holds coffins, some standing and others lying down, made into an ossuary. The floor is basalt, in cyclopean slabs fitted together without mortar. In the high shadows between the arches, the gargoyles have the form of Vozrador himself; Orum remembers them leaping onto intruders and does not know whether they are still active. The nave was designed to amplify rhythmic echoes, used in the rites to mark litanies and silences.
