# Thandruk Varn

Thandruk Varn is a dwarven fortress city in southwestern [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], on the border between the Shadar territories and the Varnakian region of [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], about four hundred fifty leagues from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Built over an ancient dragon cave that held mineral riches, it is now in the custody of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] and under the control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]].

### Geography and Function

The citadel serves as a bridge between Reinkard and Veinkard and houses master forges that form the backbone of the imperial arsenals. Its isolated location makes defense challenging. The city’s [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]] dwarves, about five thousand in all, form a subjugated caste under [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] rule: they live confined to their own citadels and bound to production quotas, in supervised freedom, and the clans fuel frequent internal disputes. It is also the source of a mysterious dagger that [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]] seeks.

### Current Structure

The production of weapons and military equipment occupies the forges, supervised by Councilor [[_wiki_en/personagens/gorvam_threnk\|Gorvam]], who serves as the Master of the Forges. A permanent Shadar military garrison maintains order in the citadel, with individual [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] guards under its command; the old orcish garrisons were withdrawn after the revolts.

### Relations with the Draconids

Before the Shadar conquest, [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] tribes regularly attacked Thandruk Varn. Since the Cult took control, these attacks have ceased, in a tacit balance that has never been formally documented.

Long before, [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]], while migrating west from the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]], tried to settle in the volcano of Thandruk Varn. The dwarves already inhabited the mountain, and she found no peace there. The dwarves preserve their ancestors’ memory of the terror she inflicted generations ago. Varna continued on to Veinkard.

### Recent Crisis

The forges have been idle for two moons, with no public explanation, and military supply has been compromised. Even so, new Shadar legions continue to appear despite the idle forges, with weapons of unknown origin. Gorvam treats resolving the crisis as an absolute priority.
