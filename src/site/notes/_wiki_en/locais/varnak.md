# Varnak

Varnak is a mountainous region in the heart of [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], marked by perpetual conflict between Draconid tribes. No power has managed to impose lasting political unification over its valleys, which remain in constant dispute. The region holds significant mineral resources and takes its name from [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]], the elder dragon that inhabits it. It lies about seven hundred leagues west of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], beyond the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]].

### Tribal Organization

Leadership in Varnak rests on military strength and draconic lineage. The Saurian tribes form temporary alliances, frequently betray one another, and wage war without respite. This fragmentation prevents any unified resistance or government.

### Clan Myr’vethan

[[_wiki_en/organizacoes/cla_myr_vethan\|Clan Myr’vethan]] is the region’s militarily dominant tribe. Its leader is [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]], a red [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] and user of draconic magic, whose ambition is to subjugate all clans under his command. The main obstacle to this unification is Varna, whose presence the rival tribes invoke as a strategic weapon against Yzmareth. Some worship her; others turn to her only when it suits them.

### Varnakians

The [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]], of tiefling origin, live scattered throughout the region as shamans and sorcerers. They serve as intermediaries between the Draconids and demonic forces, bonded to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|red gods]], the demons, and not to the black [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]]. Their relationship with the Draconids is commercial and pragmatic, marked by mutual distrust.

### Imperial Opening

About fifty years ago, [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] opened the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] to the Varnakians: tieflings and Draconids began traveling through the Empire, a decision that provoked open indignation among the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]]. In return, the monarch guaranteed protection for the valley’s population against wild Draconids, arming local communities and allowing the region to expand into what is now officially known as Varnak.
