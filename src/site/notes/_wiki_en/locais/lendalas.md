# Lendalas

Lendalas was one of the largest sacred forests of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], in southwestern [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. Described as millennia old, it was the last sacred stronghold of the Sylvan druids of that region. Today only ash remains: its territory became [[_wiki_en/locais/ermo_de_lendalas\|the Waste of Lendalas]].

### The Sacred Forest

Unlike [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], Lendalas had no direct protection from titans, which made it vulnerable to the imperial strategy of destruction by alchemical fire. Sylvan druids and rangers defended it with green magic: animated roots, poisonous spores, and the earth itself raised as a weapon.

### The Battle

The Second Orcish Legion fought the Sylvan druids in their last stronghold. The forest responded with ferocity: [[_wiki_en/personagens/ghur\|Ghur]], brother of [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]], was among the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] found hanging by roots fifteen fathoms up, their lungs full of sap. Faced with the losses, general [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] ordered the use of alchemical fire, and the millennia-old forest burned for weeks.

### The Waste of Lendalas

The burned territory became the Waste of Lendalas, a gray expanse of open ground where the earth refuses to sprout and voices echo on stormy nights. The destruction confirmed the strategic lesson of Thal’Serin: forests without divine protection were doomed, and only the ancient pacts with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] guaranteed survival against imperial fire.

### Legacy

For the orcs, Lendalas is a memory of sacrifice and reciprocal brutality: the forest killed their own before it died. For the Sylvans, it is martyrdom and proof of imperial barbarity. Ghur recited the [[_wiki_en/personagens/grasnak\|Lays of Grasnak]] around campfires before the forest took him. Grothar, who since before Lendalas had not smiled without violence nearby, lost his brother there. Much later, the Drul [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] used that loss to recruit Grothar as an informant, treating him as an equal where orders would have failed.
