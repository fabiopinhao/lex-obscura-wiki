# The Square of Oaths

The Square of Oaths is a ceremonial square in the heart of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], the sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Its eastern gate connects to [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], which makes it an obligatory route for anyone leaving an audience with [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]. The square ends before the tower and was carved so that anyone crossing it arrives diminished at the portal of Mor’Severith.

### Crossroads of Power

Because of its position, the square is a crossing point for figures of imperial power, and there the tension between the judicial arm and the military arm of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]] comes to the surface. It is there that [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] spots General [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]]’s retinue emerging from the eastern gate, with two [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] captains, five [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] officers, and a standard-bearer bearing a lunar scythe on a field of blood. The general was coming from the sacred tower, where he had been with the monarch, or as close to him as one is allowed to get. The two stare at each other in silence beneath the cerulean light of a celestial rift that opens above [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|the Peak of Nethys]], spilling [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/noctiluz\|noctiluz]]. Vekraal brushes the pommel of his [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] sword in a veiled threat, while one of his captains spits on the stones, an act of irreverence that verges on sacrilege on sacred ground. Orum notes the spit with the contempt he feels for orcs.
