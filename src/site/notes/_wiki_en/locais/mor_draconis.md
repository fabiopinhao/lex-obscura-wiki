# Mor’Draconis

Mor’Draconis is the fortified ring that serves as the administrative and religious core of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], a former home of dragons now under the rule of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. Its architecture commands reverence: angular arches point downward, enchanted stained-glass windows depict conquests and executions, and resonant corridors amplify the echoes coming from the depths. About fifty thousand inhabitants occupy the ring.

### Structures

The Obsidian Circle, or [[_wiki_en/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]], is a monumental vault of living stone, with a circular table around a central pit of pitch that reflects the torches. Twelve obsidian statues of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] occupy the ceiling. Rough granite seats, marked with coarse runes, are reserved for [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] and generals, while seats of silk and gold accommodate the archons; a larger, rotating chair presides over the whole.

Other structures make up the ring:

- The sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], with access restricted to the high clergy, which houses the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], the setting for anointings, and [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], the isolated tower of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]].
- [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_tabreth\|Khar’Tabreth]], or the Tower of Tablets, the Cult’s recordkeeping center, whose name derives from the clay tablets on which the first denunciations of traitors were inscribed. It keeps archives, records, seals, and sealings for generations, and female [[_wiki_en/criaturas/diabrete\|imps]] are forbidden to enter.
- [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_morthar\|Khar Morthar]], or the Tower of Death, both a military bastion and a vertical necropolis, with walls decorated with statues of [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] generals from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], carved in obsidian at the moments of their deaths. Legend has it that, when war approaches, the statues ooze black blood.

Completing the district are the noble quarters, where Shadars of high lineage reside and conspire, and the libraries and laboratories dedicated to arcane and alchemical knowledge.

### Security and Access

Mor’Draconis protects itself through routine: patrols in groups of six and rotation of blind spots at every shift change. The ban on [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worgs]] is old, predating Arothistar’s reign, and has never been revoked. The vertical elevator to [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]] is in the district, before the entrance to Xarn’Aroth; its cargo platform operates with long chains of heavy metal, gravitational locks that groan as they engage, and cranks operated by [[_wiki_en/criaturas/ogro\|ogres]] chained on the lower levels. The top of the ring offers a strategic vantage point over the city and the plateau.
