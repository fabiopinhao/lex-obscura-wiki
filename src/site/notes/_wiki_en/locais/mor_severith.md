# Mor’Severith

Mor’Severith is [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s tower, fused to the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], within [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Its name means “Tower of Separation” in archaic [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]]. From outside, the windows are barely distinguishable in the stone, and no sound escapes its walls. From the highest balcony, the monarch surveys the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]]. His servants are lesser, hybrid, and broken creatures, such as [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]] and [[_wiki_en/criaturas/diabrete\|imps]], and never a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] or a Drul. The [[_wiki_en/racas/minotauro\|minotaur]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/odran\|Odran]] is an exception, since minotaurs are not a servile race.

### Structure

At the top is the [[_wiki_en/locais/oculario\|Ocularium]], the main room beneath a celestial dome painted on the ceiling. There rests the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]], a giant onyx sphere used for communication, among works of art by the master himself. A bridge without a railing connects the room to the trail on the Peak of Nethys, and an animated effigy of Arothistar watches over it, with onyx eyes that light up silver.

On the next-to-highest floor, a bathing room holds a Stygian stone bathtub, a floating tablet for writing, and a large south-facing window overlooking Morvgrad and the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]]. Pale lines run along the walls, the columns, and the vaulted ceiling and light up without anyone touching them, in patterns of maps, runes, and constellations that [[_wiki_en/personagens/fing\|Fing]] believes are the voice of the “papas”. The library displays wooden shelves, a heresy tolerated in the city of stone, beneath a ceiling streaked with luminous golden lines. Among its volumes are the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/livro_vermelho_dos_dragoes\|red book of dragons]], with a cover of scales and a blind dragon emblem, [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]] treatises, and campaign chronicles.

### Prisoners and Spoils

The guest rooms are luxurious prisons, decorated like the chambers of dignitaries of the old human empire of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]: wood, rugs, and shelves, equipped with [[_wiki_en/artefatos/grilhoes_de_contencao_arcana\|shackles of arcane containment]]. The wood is deliberate: Shadar architecture does not use it, and Arothistar allows it in some of the tower’s prison rooms, for reasons he does not explain. Odran manages the prisoners, among them [[_wiki_en/personagens/auren\|Auren]], an Avian [[_wiki_en/racas/aquilar\|Aquilar]], and [[_wiki_en/personagens/iluvrae\|Ilúvrae]], an [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarin]] healer taken during the fall of [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]] and kept to treat Auren, with whom she shares confinement. In one of these rooms, just below the library, [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]] is imprisoned, watched by Odran during the day.

On the first floor, two Shadar lieutenants stand guard outside the main door, whose stone leaves open without a creak. The entrance corridor, with low braziers and steady light, opens onto the trophy gallery: weapons of conquered peoples, one piece per bay along the walls, among broken elven spears, silent dwarven hammers, notched human halberds, and [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] axes that no longer taste flesh. Among them are a [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] spear, a dwarven hammer with a split head, and a gigantic axe with no arm to raise it. Thrax’s mace was placed there after his capture, without a plaque. In the final third of the gallery, the antechamber holds the largest of the effigies of the Crown, the one that does not move about: Arothistar standing behind a human-height block of obsidian, himself at least five times that height and with onyx eyes that light up silver when the monarch speaks through it.
