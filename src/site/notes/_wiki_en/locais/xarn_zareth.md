# Xarn’Zareth

Xarn’Zareth is a sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], and serves as the underground equivalent of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. With around three thousand inhabitants, it is the religious center of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]] on the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] side and is under the control of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/casa_durzken\|House Durzken]].

### Landscape

The House-Cathedral of [[_wiki_en/personagens/nylothrae_durzken\|Nylothrae Durzken]] dominates the district’s landscape, carved from spiral obsidian according to Vor-Ghol’s architectural tradition. Nylothrae, matriarch of House Durzken, governs the eparchy; her religious and political authority is shared with [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]], of the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], the twin center on the surface in Xarn’Aroth.

### The Gates

The Gates of Xarn’Zareth control internal access to the sacred district, on the way to the House-Cathedral: caravans bound for House Durzken pass through them for inspection before loading. They are distinct from the Gates of Ghilanna, which are the entrance to the citadel and control who travels between the surface and the underground, under the watch of the city guard.

The freight elevator that departs from [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], before the entrance to Xarn’Aroth, terminates in the district. Drul guards at the doors of the eparchy sniff out arcane artifacts on visitors.

### Ritual Life

[[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] departs from the district, bound for a mission on the surface, carrying a papyrus sealed with the seal of [[_wiki_en/panteao/azkaroth\|Azkaroth]]. The integration between the Alliance’s two centers is also evident in the rites: eight Drul hierophants from Xarn’Zareth participated in [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]]’s anointing ceremony at the Temple of Azkaroth.
