# Dromos Draconis

Dromos Draconis, also called Dromos Draconis Coliseum, was a coliseum for gladiatorial combat in the region of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Its arenas are defunct: they were suppressed by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], and the absence of any surviving record points to a deliberate political act, not simple abandonment.

### The Arena

The coliseum was excavated in the bed of an ancient dragon’s nest. The walls retain the grooves left by the claws of ancestral serpents, and the central floor preserves the obsidian vitrified by [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]] breath.

On the drome, the arena’s oval circuit, the condemned were pursued by smaller [[_wiki_en/criaturas/wyvern\|wyverns]] and hungry dragonets. Whoever survived the full lap earned the right to fight for their own freedom against the reigning champion. Later, the reptiles were released into the caves in the mountains of [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]].

### History

The arenas of Dromos Draconis belong to a time that the Shadow Council buried along with them. The suppression erased the practice of gladiatorial combat from that region, and the coliseum survives today only as a reference to an era that has ended.

### Traces

The only known trace of Dromos Draconis is a bronze and copper helmet of exotic design that completely conceals the wearer’s face. The piece, described as a relic of those buried times, was worn by [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]] during the incursion into [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]].
