# Ghilanna

Ghilanna is the principal [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] citadel underground, built in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] and governed by [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/casa_durzken\|House Durzken]], of matriarchal lineage. It houses about fifty thousand inhabitants and is a partner of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] in [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|the Underground Alliance]].

### Gates

The main entrance takes the form of a stone jaw: two basalt slabs polished by centuries of passage, tracks of petrified bone in the grooves, and counterweights hidden in dark niches. The sentinels detect approaching individuals by pressing an ear to the natural wall, and the sound of the stones running along the tracks serves as a test of alertness. Beside the gate, an inspection table with a shallow bowl and a stick of salted charcoal records the passage seals.

### Training Arena

The floor of the training arena is made of the same black basalt as the slabs of the Gates, with the same polish, produced by generations of Drul blood. [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]] dueled there during the descents to Vor-Ghol.

### Xarn’Zareth

[[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]] is Ghilanna’s sacred district, the underground equivalent of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]] and the religious hub of the Underground Alliance on the Drul side. It is governed by House Durzken, whose House-Cathedral, belonging to [[_wiki_en/personagens/nylothrae_durzken\|Nylothrae Durzken]], stands there; religious and political authority is shared with [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]], of the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]].

### Origin

It is in Ghilanna that [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] was born and forged themself.
