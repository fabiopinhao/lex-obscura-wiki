# Scriptorium of Morvgrad

The Scriptorium of Morvgrad is the wing of copyists and exegetes of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], located in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] itself. There, the codices of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]] are copied and glossed: the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]], the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]], and the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/tabula_umbrae_codex\|Tabula Umbrae]].

### Operations

Access is restricted to sworn acolytes, under the supervision of the archons. Each copy produced is initialed with a ritual cipher and a consecrated colophon, marks that guarantee authenticity and traceability. Forging codices is a capital crime.
