# The Axis of Drenn

The Axis of Drenn is an ancient [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] citadel in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], now uninhabited. At some point in history, it was taken over by [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídars]] and turned into a nest, and its original population was eliminated or forced to flee. The walls and ceilings still retain traces of webs.

### Today

The Axis serves as a bypass route in Vor-Ghol. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]] caravan, led by [[_wiki_en/personagens/borzrun\|Borzrun]], diverted its convoy through it to avoid a drídar in combat with [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/veio_morto\|the Dead Vein]]. The passage proves traversable when [[_wiki_en/locais/galeria_menor\|the Lesser Gallery]] is blocked by subterranean tides.
