# Arena of Drakmur

The Arena of Drakmur, or the Slaughterhouse of Drakmur, is a combat arena on the periphery of the Plateau, near [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], under the control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. It is the gladiatorial enclosure of the fortress of [[_wiki_en/locais/drakmur\|Drakmur]], one of the orcish fortresses surrounding that frontier.

There, [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] and [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnolls]] disembowel one another for sport, money, and fleeting glory, and its population varies according to the events. The main contest is the annual Spear of Three Skulls tournament, whose latest champion is [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]], a gnoll veteran of the Slaughterhouse and bodyguard for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]], in the [[_wiki_en/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]].

### Origin

The nickname Slaughterhouse arose from an orc mutiny that turned the fort’s corridors into a slaughterhouse. Upon regaining control, the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] channeled the violence and built the arena; the name remained as a reminder of who is in charge.
