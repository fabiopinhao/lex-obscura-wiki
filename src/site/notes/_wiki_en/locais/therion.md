# Therion

Therion was an ancient fortress, active during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] and now defunct. It is remembered as the place where the first [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/maleficarios\|Maleficários]] were compiled.

### Nature

In Therion, [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] scribes and human cultists assembled the first Maleficários, the demonological scriptures that cataloged demonic entities and their sigils. After the Shadar Exile, these codices were taken to and expanded in the temples of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]].
