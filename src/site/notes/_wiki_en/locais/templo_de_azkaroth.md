# Temple of Azkaroth

The Temple of Azkaroth is a sanctuary of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] carved into the mountain, in the sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], within the middle ring of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. It is there that the anointings of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] take place. Among the at least twelve diabolical temples in the capital, it remains one of the most active.

### Architecture

Embedded in the city’s entrails, the temple exhales incense through fissures in the black bone and preserves, in underground galleries, the memory of the [[_wiki_en/eventos/queda_dos_luminares\|Fall of the Luminars]]. The angular architecture compels reverence: the arches point downward and the corridors return echoes from the depths, and from the arches themselves emanates a low hum that makes the stone tremble before reaching the ears. The floor of the main hall is polished obsidian, rows of ancient pillars support the interior, and elevated galleries in half-light allow the ceremonies to be observed. At the high altar hangs a mural painting of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], with eyes of coal and hands raised as judge and executioner.

### Function and Hierarchy

Besides the anointings, the Temple is the center of the Cult’s mystical matters and arcane mysteries: there sorcerers and hierophants read entrails and necromancers deal with the dead. It produces and stores ritual compounds, such as the Breath of Lúrio, used in the trials that test the candidates’ aptitude. The Templar orders are independent of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], since they are parallel structures subordinate only to the monarch, and the Temple can invoke prerogatives belonging to [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]]. It is led by the ceremonial sorcerer [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]], at the head of the eparchy, and brings together [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] hierophants, disciples of [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]], scribes, and acolytes. The headquarters on the surface has an underground counterpart: the ceremonial chambers of Xarn’Zareth, in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]].

### The Anointing of the Justiciars

During the anointing, conducted in an ancient language, [[_wiki_en/panteao/abbhal\|Abbhal]] is invoked as supreme judge while ten disciples of Xarn chant a dissonant chant in a semicircle. In the gesture that marks the passage, the sorcerer lays the consecrated sword across the open palms of the anointed, with its edge turned toward him: the law is received by supporting it, without the hand taking it by the hilt. The anointed receives the three artifacts of consecration there: the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/spina_lexis\|Spina Lexis]], a scepter of moonstone and the bones of the condemned; the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]], a purple tome bearing Arothistar’s symbol; and the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus Morvgradi]], an onyx sphere. During the anointing of [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh]], the mural painting of Arothistar emanated an unusual radiance; from the elevated gallery, [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]] observed the ceremony, and a sixth chair remained empty among the Justiciars.

### The Seal of Azkaroth

The seal of [[_wiki_en/panteao/azkaroth\|Azkaroth]] carries institutional authority: a document bearing it gains recognized legitimacy, such as the papyrus of [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] on her mission to [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]]. From the bathing room of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], Arothistar has a direct view of the temple.
