# Peak of Nethys

The Peak of Nethys, also called the Tooth of Reinkard, is the highest point of the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] on the edge facing the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau. Once a dragon’s nest, it is now abandoned, although it remains a major geographic landmark in the landscape of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]].

### Geography and History

It was a dragon’s nest for most of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], home to the elder [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]]. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], the dark corruption of the Shadars made the region untenable, and Varna migrated to [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]], on the continent of [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]]. The peak has remained abandoned ever since.

### Mor’Severith and the Ocularium

[[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s tower, is fused to the base of the Peak of Nethys, in [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]]. A bridge without a railing connects the [[_wiki_en/locais/oculario\|Ocularium]] to the trail that ascends to the peak. Arothistar’s personal relationship with the place is not public knowledge, but he contemplates it frequently.

### The Scar in the Sky

Above the Peak of Nethys, rifts of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/noctiluz\|noctiluz]] open periodically, leaving silver scars in the firmament. The rift that opened during [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]]’s anointing, seen from [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], was a different one, and left a cerulean scar in the sky above the plateau.
