# Drûzhal

Drûzhal is one of the [[_wiki_en/locais/fortalezas_orquicas_do_planalto\|orcish fortresses of the plateau]], a garrison on the perimeter of [[_wiki_en/locais/dhar-morveth\|Dhar-Morveth]] under the control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. In the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], it is among the garrisons that reported clashes with the waves of wild [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] that put pressure on the plateau in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]].
