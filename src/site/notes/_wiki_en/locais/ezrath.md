# Ezrath

Ezrath is the upscale commercial district of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], an elite area where horrors are sold with impeccable fiscal aplomb. Necromantic clinics operate alongside antique dealers in hereditary curses and tailors of shrouds for the living.

### The Trade

The Two Hearts of the Kraken boutique displays a miniature amphibious dragon that whispers pleas for carnage to the delight of nobles. Crowds part to let the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tegmen\|tegmen]] of a Justiciar pass, and dwarf merchants recognize Judge [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]] from a distance, by name.

The district serves as a showcase for the obscene legality of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]]: [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s order reduced to stamps. Everything is legal because someone paid for the seal. The contrast with the [[_wiki_en/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]], where illegality is raw, reveals Ezrath as the perfumed version of the same trade, a district where even mercy needs a seal.

### Episodes

It was in Ezrath that [[_wiki_en/personagens/korven\|Korven]] captured the [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Muran]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/skinitz\|Skinitz]] for stealing pralines; Nidzina ordered him sent off to the [[_wiki_en/locais/fosso_da_purga\|Pit of the Purge]], which opens beneath Morvgrad, and Korven took him. [[_wiki_en/personagens/skanivik\|Skanivik]], Skinitz’s brother, escaped, turning his face one last time before disappearing around a corner.
