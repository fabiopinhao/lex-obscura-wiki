# Danvorn

Danvorn is a domain conquered by the Shadow Empire and razed by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], located in [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]], between twenty and forty leagues from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Everything was burned, and the ashes were salted. Its destruction is the extreme of the repression that also turned the cathedral of [[_wiki_en/locais/thervad\|Thervad]] into a stable and the temple of [[_wiki_en/locais/orandia\|Orandia]] into a grain storehouse.

Among the families affected is that of a child from Ventrista, whose mother stayed in Danvorn and did not return.
