# The Pit of the Supplicants

The Pit of the Supplicants is a deep [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|Void]] open within [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], an abyss that descends into the city’s darkness. The name evokes a past use as a site of execution or public penance, although its original function is unconfirmed. Above the city, a winged figure sometimes hovers there, contemplating the leap.
