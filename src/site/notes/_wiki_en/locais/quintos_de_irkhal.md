# The Fifths of Irkhal

The Fifths of Irkhal is the nickname for the five disposal shafts that descend from the surface of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] to the Depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. The refuse of the plateau flows through them: garbage, bodies, dead magic, and aged water. What goes down the Fifths does not return, and that reputation gives rise to the expression that has made them better remembered than any precise description.

### The Expression

“Hunt all the way to the Fifths of Irkhal”, or simply “all the way to Irkhal”, is said among [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]] to promise relentless pursuit: disappearance without an echo, without even the consolation of rumor. It serves as the subterranean equivalent of “to the ends of the earth”.

### What Is Known

No canonical record establishes why the shafts bear this name. “Fifths” may refer to an ancient division of Vor-Ghol, and “Irkhal” may have been the proper name of a founder or entity, whose meaning has been lost.
