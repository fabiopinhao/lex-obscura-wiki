# Orcish Fortresses of the Plateau

The orcish fortresses of the plateau are the military garrisons that surround the perimeter of [[_wiki_en/locais/dhar-morveth\|Dhar-Morveth]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau on which [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] was built. They serve as garrisons and centers of stewardry, and not as frontline fortifications or centers of shadow power. They inherit this role directly from the [[_wiki_en/eventos/formalizacao_militar_dos_orcs\|military formalization of the orcs]] during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]], when the peripheral forts ceased to be mere watch posts and became the network of barracks from which incursions departed, with planned routes and a well-supplied rear.

### The Scribblers

The detailed administration of these fortresses has never fallen to the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], who despise it, or to the Shadars, who consider it beneath them to deal with orcs directly. It falls to the goblinoids. Goblinoid scribes keep records of provisions, guard shifts, and troop strengths on papyri, and it is on these papyri that the fate of every orc who passes through the garrison is decided, with ink and a stamp. The orcs call them “scribblers”, with the contempt of those who see their own lives decided by creatures that scribble.

### The Discard Formalized

It is in the perimeter fortresses that the Discard ceases to be a diffuse practice and becomes a matter of record. When an orc is deemed unfit for military missions (old, crippled, broken in combat), it is there that the scribblers mark their fate on the papyrus. Most of those marked become pig feed, treated as dead weight not worth keeping alive; a minority, still useful, is repurposed for stewardry and support tasks within the fortresses themselves. For an orc, reporting to a plateau garrison in poor condition amounts to signing their own sentence.

### The Perimeter Garrisons

[[_wiki_en/locais/kharurz\|Kharûrz]], [[_wiki_en/locais/vrokhmakh\|Vrokhmakh]], and [[_wiki_en/locais/druzhal\|Drûzhal]] garrison the periphery of the plateau and are among the fortresses that reported to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] the clashes with the waves of wild orcs pressing Dhar-Morveth in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]]. [[_wiki_en/locais/drakmur\|Drakmur]], on the periphery of the plateau, is the most notorious: a fortress with a gladiatorial role, it houses the [[_wiki_en/locais/arena_de_drakmur\|Arena of Drakmur]].
