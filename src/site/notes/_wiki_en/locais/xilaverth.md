# Xilaverth

Xilaverth is a [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] citadel in western [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], in a hostile relationship with [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]]. It began as a satellite of the capital, like its sister citadels Nulbareth and Vel’Kora, sources of ore and young blood for Ghilanna; Xilaverth broke away and stopped sending its own. The rivalry between Xilaverth and Ghilanna is political and territorial, typical of Drul matriarchal dynamics, where betrayal is a virtue and alliances are temporary from their inception.

### Diplomacy

[[_wiki_en/personagens/nylothrae_durzken\|Nylothrae Durzken]], of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/casa_durzken\|House Durzken]], conducts secret diplomacy with Xilaverth. The goal is not conquest, but indebtedness: to accumulate obligations rather than territories, turning debts into leverage for the opportune moment. The caverns of the west, with their poor soil and rats, matter less than the promise of a favor owed.

### The Caravan

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]] caravan, led by [[_wiki_en/personagens/borzrun\|Borzrun]], transported a secret shipment from Nylothrae to Xilaverth, disguised as ordinary trade. [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] supervised the operation and took [[_wiki_en/personagens/khreek_fio-fino\|Khreek Fine-Thread]] as guarantor and hostage to ensure delivery.
