# Dhar-Morveth

Dhar-Morveth is the black plateau in northern [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] upon which [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] was built. The name is the original elven form, also adopted by humans and still used in formal records. Over time, it came to designate the very power based on the plateau, becoming conflated with Morvgrad. Only elves and humans use it; the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] never do. As a geographical designation, the common name is “Shadar plateau”, or simply “plateau”.

### Geography

Dhar-Morveth dominates the north of the continent, rising above the plains of [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]] like a crown of obsidian. The [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] marks its western boundary. Underground, the plateau connects to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] through a vertical elevator extending a thousand fathoms that links Morvgrad to [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]]. Virtually the entire population of the plateau, more than half a million souls, is concentrated in Morvgrad.

### Material of the Plateau

Wood rarely reaches the plateau. Shadar construction uses stone and obsidian, and a wood-lined room in Morvgrad would be an expensive eccentricity, besides using a material the Shadars do not care for. Outside the plateau, in villages on the slopes such as [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], wood is the common material.

Beneath the volcanic layer of the slopes lie pockets of limestone, and the hydrology of this karst terrain sustains the terroir of the wineries on the slopes.

### History

In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], Shadars persecuted on the central plains migrated to the plateau. There they subjugated local pagan communities and built the first diabolical temples. The encounter with the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] underground below led to the collaboration that would culminate in [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|the Underground Alliance]]. The dark Shadar presence corrupted the fauna and flora of the Dorsal’s slopes: dragons abandoned their nests, and corrupted eggs gave rise to the [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]].

### Noctiluz

Phenomena of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/noctiluz\|noctiluz]] tear through the sky above the plateau during significant events, visible from dozens of leagues away. The cerulean scar observed above Niedellin and the rift above the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]] are manifestations of this phenomenon.
