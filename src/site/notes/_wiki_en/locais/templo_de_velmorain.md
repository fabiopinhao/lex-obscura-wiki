# Temple of Velmorain

The Temple of [[_wiki_en/panteao/velmorain\|Velmorain]], also called the Temple of [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]], is one of the houses of the sacred complex of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], dedicated to the god of Cosmo and [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]]. It ranks among the twelve diabolical temples that make up the sacred geography of the imperial capital. The nature of the deity, associated with the manipulation of perception and reality, gives the temple a particular reputation among the initiates of the black pantheon. The current pontiff is Voxmere, whom [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] met when he was young, as an aspirant.
