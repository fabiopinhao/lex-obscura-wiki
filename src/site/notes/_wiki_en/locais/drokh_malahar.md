# Drokh’Malahar

Drokh’Malahar designates the abyssal floors of the dungeons of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], reserved for the extreme regime of torment that the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] calls “perpetual torture”. It is also called the Crucitórios or the Pits of Drokh’Malahar, and the name breaks down into *mala* (“damnation”) and *har* (“room”): the rooms of damnation.

### Nature

The Crucitórios do not kill. Necromancers maintain the consciousness of the condemned far beyond the endurance of the flesh, sustaining the body in a state of suffering that death would end but that magic prolongs indefinitely. The standard sentence prescribes confinement until the flesh forgets its own name.

### Penal Function

Drokh’Malahar is the destination prescribed by the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] for those convicted of [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], the demonic bond solemnized without imperial authorization. There is no review, there is no appeal, there is no time limit.

Morvgrad’s penal hierarchy distinguishes three destinations: the [[_wiki_en/locais/fosso_da_purga\|Pit of the Purge]] discards, through the summary execution of [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]] and petty criminals; Ghol’Drokh imprisons, holding political and strategic prisoners in custody; Drokh’Malahar punishes without end.
