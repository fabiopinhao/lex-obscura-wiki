# The Dead Vein

The Dead Vein is an underground passage in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], known for the presence of wild [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídars]]. Caravans avoid the route whenever an alternative exists, given the risk of ambush by giant arachnids. No population occupies it.

### The Anomalous Behavior

[[_wiki_en/personagens/borzrun\|Borzrun]], leader of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]] caravan, reported that in the Dead Vein he had encountered a drídar in combat with suicidal [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]], unprecedented behavior that led him to take a detour through [[_wiki_en/locais/eixo_de_drenn\|the Axis of Drenn]]. Normally, Murans flee from drídars without offering resistance. The suicidal attack suggests that something in the depths drives them more violently than their natural fear of the predator does, consistent with the still unresolved mystery of what makes Murans flee the depths of Vor-Ghol.
