# Drakmur

Drakmur is the most notorious of the [[_wiki_en/locais/fortalezas_orquicas_do_planalto\|orcish fortresses]] that surround the outskirts of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau, under the control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] and garrisoned by orcish troops. It lies near [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]].

### Vocation

While the perimeter garrisons ([[_wiki_en/locais/kharurz\|Kharûrz]], [[_wiki_en/locais/vrokhmakh\|Vrokhmakh]], [[_wiki_en/locais/druzhal\|Drûzhal]]) concern themselves with stewardry and watch duty, Drakmur is a fortress with a gladiatorial vocation. Its heart is the [[_wiki_en/locais/arena_de_drakmur\|Arena of Drakmur]], also called the Slaughterhouse of Drakmur, where [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] and [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnolls]] disembowel one another for sport, money, and fleeting glory, and where the annual Spear of Three Skulls tournament is the main event.

Outside the plateau, Drakmur is known chiefly for [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]], a gnoll veteran of the Slaughterhouse and the most recent champion of the Spear of Three Skulls, now a bodyguard for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]] in the [[_wiki_en/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]]. Beyond the arena, the Empire’s records say little about the fortress.
