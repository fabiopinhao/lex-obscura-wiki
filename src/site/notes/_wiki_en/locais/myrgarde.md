# Myrgarde

Myrgarde is the former human capital of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], which proclaimed itself “Empire of Reinkard, Capital of Men”. At its height, it was to its time what [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] is to the present: it was the imperial capital. The Central Empire, dominated by humans and yet multicultural and cosmopolitan, had its center there and, in the end, its last bastion; “Central Empire” was the common name for the domain it commanded. It was the last human city on the continent to fall in the Reinkardic Wars, withstanding a siege of at least three years before submitting to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. Now subjugated, its [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]] population lives under surveillance, and the Mark of Myrgarde, a brand seared with iron, burns on the chests of those condemned to death.

### The City

A city of pale stone and a crossroads of peoples, it brought together humans, dwarves, and gnomes before the conquest, and even today houses more than fifty thousand souls under the imperial yoke. Civic life revolved around the market, the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/aroada\|Aroada]] field, and the Scholarium adjoining the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/igreja_de_sao_lucian\|Church of Saint Lucian]], where boys learned sacred letters.

### Faith and Conquest

It was the seat of Reinkard’s human faith. In the Cathedral of Saint Lucian, marble stairways rose beneath stained-glass windows that fragmented the light across the limestone, and two warrior saints presided over worship: Saint Lucian, with a silver wing-blade, and Saint [[_wiki_en/panteao/borjean\|Borjean]], the celestial knight. During the mobilization against the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] of the north, the church of Saint Lucian was sealed, and its activities moved to the High Temple. With the conquest, [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] extinguished every sacred name in Reinkard, and the human gods burned with the city. [[_wiki_en/personagens/corvina\|Mother Corvina]], superior of the orphanage, is one of the few surviving hierophants: she still prays for the righteous and maintains an escape route for refugees.

### Memory

In the [[_wiki_en/locais/jardim_dos_inocentes\|Garden of the Innocents]], the section of the cemetery reserved for children, an Aroada sphere would be placed on the grave of those who departed young, and the [[_wiki_en/racas/aviano\|Avians]] would leave their most beautiful feathers there; in the imperial present, it breathes neglect. From the city also came the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/maleficarios\|Maleficaria]] of Myrgarde, demonological scriptures compiled in the fortress of [[_wiki_en/locais/therion\|Therion]] during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] by Shadar scribes and human cultists, and later expanded by [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarar]] clerics in the city’s temples, who added methods for identifying and banishing [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]]. From there also comes the classic play “The Widow of [[_wiki_en/locais/valdris\|Valdris]]”, which survives in a Shadar adaptation with inverted morality: where Myrgarde saw the sin of greed, Morvgrad sees virtue in ambition.

### The Legend of the Infiltrator

From the Reinkardic Wars survives the legend of a spy who is said to have lived undercover in Myrgarde for years in the years preceding the fall of the city and, with it, of the last bastion of the Central Empire. The identity remains classified in the imperial archives, with the records purged or sealed by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]]. Among veterans, there is still mention of a Shadar lieutenant who disappeared for years and returned with crucial intelligence.
