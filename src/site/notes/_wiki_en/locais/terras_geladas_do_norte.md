# Northern Frozen Lands

The Northern Frozen Lands are the belt of tundras, glaciers, and mountains that occupies the far north of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], above the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau. The northern stretch of the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] marks its border with the plateau, and it is through there that [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcish]] incursions descend. The kingdoms of the south know it only by what they see from outside, the frozen north; it has a proper name only among the orcs, who call it Gûmrakh, the womb of the horde. No power claims it: there is no permanent settlement, central authority, or tribute, only scattered orc tribes in recurring warfare with one another. The flower of Nyssis thrives in its tundras, a violet herb now scarce, from which the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/luto_de_nyssis\|Mourning of Nyssis]], a substance that nullifies magic, is distilled.

### The Land That Made Them

The region is the birthplace of the orcs. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|Forgotten Age]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] bonded to [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]] were expelled from [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], incompatible with the underground discipline of those with pacts with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]]. They migrated to the tundras and mountains of the surface, and over millennia, isolation and the brutality of the environment shaped their bodies and their culture until they produced the orcs. It was here that the race took shape.

### Eras of Pillage

As early as the Forgotten Age, when the orcs descended from the mountain ranges to raid the human frontiers, the military consolidation of the central kingdoms pushed them back to the peripheries and nearly exterminated them in the central regions. The scattered tribes remained in the mountains and tundras, lands that no kingdom considered worthy of conquest. To the peoples of the northern frontiers, the orcs became a scourge as predictable as the seasons, and the hostility of that time shaped the military culture of the northern kingdoms for eras.

In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], with the parent race already gone, the tundras remained wholly under orc dominion, and communities that tried to settle on the glaciers were destroyed within a matter of seasons. The arcane barrier of the Dorsal and the dragons that hunted on its slopes reduced the number reaching the plateau, without ever stopping them altogether. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], with the barrier dissipated, the way was clear, and the three races of the plateau began capturing goblinoids from the frozen edges to swell their hosts.

### The North Today

Currently, the far north is barely watched, if at all. The Shadars do not ascend, since nothing in the ice rewards the climb and what lies there is hostile tribes; the actual containment of the frontier takes place much farther south, at the [[_wiki_en/locais/fortalezas_orquicas_do_planalto\|orcish fortresses of the plateau]]. The flow of orcs migrating south is growing and has become an administrative problem for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], which debates solutions without finding any sufficient.

The causes are the subject of rumor. It is said that word of abundance in [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]] has spread among the tribes, or that wars among the orcs themselves leave the defeated homeless and drive them down the plateau. Neither has been confirmed, because the Empire does not keep sufficient watch to know. There is also a more colorful account, immediately dismissed by those who know the history: that [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]] has supposedly returned to prowl the far north and is the one driving the tribes. The dragon has not crossed the Dorsal toward Reinkard since settling very far from there, eras ago, but the theory survives on the fringes, among those who prefer a named monster to an administrative cause.

### The Banishment Account

Among the orcs in the Empire’s service, another story circulates: that they were supposedly banished from the fertile lands and driven into the frozen north where the sun barely reaches, and that violence against humans, [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]], and elves is supposedly retribution consecrated in blood. The Shadars tell their own: Lord [[_wiki_en/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Merari d’Khunvel]], in the square of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], speaks of his own people banished from the fertile lands and driven into the frozen north where the sun barely reaches, propagandistic hyperbole: the frozen north of his speech is the plateau, the destination of the [[_wiki_en/eventos/migracao_shadar_ao_planalto\|Shadar migration]]. Each people tells of its own banishment.

The record of the eras does not support the orcish account: the orcs originated in the north, and the only expulsion was from the central regions, millennia after the race had already formed on the glaciers. There is irony in the very name the tribes give their land. Orcanic calls it Gûmrakh, the womb that gave birth to them, while the banishment account insists on a prison.
