# River Parveni

The River Parveni is the main watercourse of [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]], the stretch of fertile land at the foot of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau that supplies the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. It runs through the settlement of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], three days by road southeast of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], where it follows the rhythm of riverside life, and forms part of the region’s agricultural waterway network.
