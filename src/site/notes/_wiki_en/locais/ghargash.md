# Ghargash

Ghargash is a tribal fort in the icy lands northwest of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] Plateau, territory of the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] tribes. It is remembered, above all, for a battle so brutal that it became known as the slaughter of Ghargash, a name the orcs do not use lightly.

### The Slaughter

The fighting at the fort took place when [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]] was still young and lived in those lands, before his recruitment into the Imperial Army. It was there that Grothar lost his left eye, which turned milky; over time, he learned to compensate for the loss and began to distinguish shapes with enough precision to tell allies from targets.

### Significance

Ghargash and [[_wiki_en/locais/lendalas\|Lendalas]] are the two wounds that define Grothar: at Ghargash he lost the sight in one eye; at Lendalas he lost his brother [[_wiki_en/personagens/ghur\|Ghur]]. Together, they shaped the disciplined veteran who stands apart from ordinary orcs by maintaining intelligence and restraint where others know only fury.
