# Khar Morthar

Khar Morthar, the Tower of Death, is a fortress-tower in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], one of the rings of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], under the rule of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. It is both a military bastion and a vertical necropolis, and at least three hundred souls occupy its floors. The outer walls display statues of [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] generals from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], carved in obsidian in the years of their deaths: some figures fall, others raise weapons in blows that never land, others simply stop. A persistent legend says that the statues bleed liquid obsidian in the presence of shadow heroes and remain in stony silence before promotions based on political criteria, fabricated glories, and empty boasts. In the popular variant, they ooze black blood when war approaches.

### The Generals’ Courtyard

The outer courtyard houses niches hollowed out of obsidian in which generals of the Second Era are carved, figures whose names are forgotten and who are preserved only in their final gesture. One general sits, his head drooping over his chest, his hands open on his knees with his palms facing up, empty of whatever they last held. Another stands, unarmed, eyes closed, his mouth sealed in accepted death. The two flank [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]]’s favorite spot in the courtyard: they survived countless campaigns and died in the period of peace that followed the fall of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], neither of them in combat, and the obsidian judges them as though they had fallen in it. There is no frozen heroism or combat pose: honor in Khar Morthar lies in having died, and the dead ask for no more than that. At the outer edge, far from the base of the tower, there is an isolated niche, a lone general, smaller than the others, with no stone neighbors and no glory on his face.

### Significance

The tower serves as a symbolic counterpoint to [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]]: where Khargoth celebrates knowledge, Khar Morthar celebrates sacrifice. It also serves as a place of contemplation, where thoughts are ordered before being turned into action, and its effigies are examined with almost liturgical attention, as though the dead owed answers that the living no longer know how to give.
