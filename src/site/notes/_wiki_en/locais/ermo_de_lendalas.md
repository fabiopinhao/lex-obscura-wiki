# The Waste of Lendalas

The Waste of Lendalas is an open expanse of ash in southwestern [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], in the former territory of [[_wiki_en/locais/lendalas\|Lendalas]]. It is dead land, with no controlling authority and no population: a barren desert where the earth refuses to sprout.

### Origin

The Waste is what remains of Lendalas, one of the largest sacred forests of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]]. About thirty-four years before the present, during the second phase of the Reinkardic Wars, the legions of [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] consumed the thousand-year-old forest with alchemical fire. It burned for weeks, and the green and sacred territory became a gray, barren, and permanently scarred expanse of open ground.

### Phenomena

Nothing grows in the Waste of Lendalas, as though the soil retained the memory of the fire. On stormy nights, voices seem to echo beneath the cauterized crust, a phenomenon no one has been able to explain and few have had the courage to investigate.

### Significance

Only the titanic forests, protected by ancient pacts with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], withstood the flames: [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] still breathes, while Lendalas turned to ash. For [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]], the Waste is the unmarked grave of his brother [[_wiki_en/personagens/ghur\|Ghur]], killed by druidic roots and poisonous spores when the forest was still fighting the invaders. The alchemical fire came afterward and sealed the battlefield, bodies, roots, and memories beneath the gray crust that now covers everything.
