# Thal’Serin

Thal’Serin is an ancestral sacred forest of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], in northern [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]], about twenty-five leagues from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Its millennia-old trees intertwine their roots like a vegetal scripture, and the woodland serves as a refuge for the elven resistance.

### Geography

A forest of millennia-old trees whose roots intertwine in the soil. The Cave opens within it, a lair of [[_wiki_en/criaturas/troll\|trolls]] linked to [[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]], the titan guardian of the forest. Black patches erode the woodland’s wild vigor. Among the fauna inhabiting it are birds with iridescent feathers, found only there.

### Refuge

Thal’Serin shelters the elven resistance led by [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]]. The rebel shelter, beside the creek of the Two Stones, is protected by druidic webs of invisibility that conceal those who take shelter there.

### Ancient Protection

The forest is under an ancient pact: [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] promised the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], Morlgrund and [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]], that the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] would not burn Thal’Serin. It was also in Thal’Serin that [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]] swore his oath to Morlgrund.
