# Central Archive

The Central Archive is the judicial repository attached to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/tribunal_de_morvgrad\|Supreme Tribunal]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], the sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. It holds every sentence handed down since the founding of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] and serves as the institutional memory of the judicial arm of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]].

### Function

Scribes maintain the records on continuous duty. Those who make errors in transcription are judged by the same codes they used: the law does not distinguish between those who apply it and those who write it.

### Distinction

The Central Archive is distinct from [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_tabreth\|Khar’Tabreth]], the Tower of Tablets in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], which serves as a registry center and repository of administrative jurisprudence. Khar’Tabreth processes the empire’s bureaucracy; the Central Archive preserves the judicial history of the Justiciars.
