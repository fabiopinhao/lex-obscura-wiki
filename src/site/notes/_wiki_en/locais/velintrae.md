# Velintrae

Velintrae is an elven kingdom of the western forests, conquered during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]]. Its millennia-old trees, once considered sacred and indestructible, were reduced to ashes by arcane fire.
