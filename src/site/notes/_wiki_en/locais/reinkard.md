# Reinkard

Reinkard is the central continent of [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]], the material plane, flanked by [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]] to the west and [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] to the east. It has [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] as its capital and is under the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] hegemony of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], consolidated after the Reinkardic Wars.

### Geography

Thamakard is not spherical, and Reinkard is divided into strategic zones. To the north lies the Shadar Plateau, [[_wiki_en/locais/dhar-morveth\|Dhar-Morveth]]; in the center, the plains of [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]; there are sacred forests in [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], and the east coast lies in [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]]. [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] extends throughout the underground, and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/noctiluz\|noctiluz]] tears through the sky during significant events. To the west, the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] separates the continent from Veinkard.

### History

Before the conquest, Reinkard was a fragmented continent: human kingdoms centered on Myrgarde, elven enclaves in Elenir and Thal’Serin, and the Shadar plateau to the north. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]] between Morvgrad and [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]] preceded the military expansion that would culminate in Shadar rule.

### Borders

The continent’s geopolitics are defined by natural borders. The Dorsal prevents an advance into Veinkard; swamps and a humid climate block the conquest of Neranthis; and Thal’Serin resists under titan protection, the result of the pact between [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] and the titans. [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s purple seal does not cross the Dorsal.
