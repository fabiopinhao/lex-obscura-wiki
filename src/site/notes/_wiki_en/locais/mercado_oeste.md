# West Market

The West Market is a semi-official black market in the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]], within [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. It occupies a fissure between limestone escarpments that suppurates like an open wound in the city, and its population fluctuates according to what is bought and sold there. The name is a euphemism, and no one uses it without irony: it originated in the tax reports of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|the Cult]], which preferred to tax what it could not eliminate.

### Control

The market is operated by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]], under the command of [[_wiki_en/personagens/dribbel_olgund\|Dribbel Olgund]], the Trade-Breaker. The [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derros]], deep gnomes, maintain money-changing burrows and seal-forging networks there, and call the place “the teat Morvgrad pretends not to suck”. [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]] works as a guard and collector. The operations are openly illegal, but the market functions under a three-line decree by [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] that legalized its activities: the Guild pays taxes and, in exchange, receives official tolerance. The Guild operates at the exact intersection of illegality and taxes paid on time, and [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]] describes the arrangement as the pus from a wound the monarch himself opened.

### Setting

Within the fissure, the market is a jumble of tarps stretched over cattle ribs, iron hooks driven into the stone, and charred wood, still smelling of the fire that burned it, serving as a column where the rock refuses to provide support. Faceless gargoyles witness the entrance. Among the stalls are flasks of organs, chattering [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobold]] skulls, blasphemous idols, and slave cages with official seals. Beside the threshold, the beggars make a display of their misery and keep sores open, because healing would cost them bargaining power. When a Justiciar’s [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tegmen\|tegmen]] appears, the vendors cease their conversations.
