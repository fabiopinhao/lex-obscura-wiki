# Khar’Tabreth

Khar’Tabreth, the Tower of Tablets, is the registry center of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], within [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. It holds the archives, records, stamps, and seals accumulated by the shadow administration over generations.

### Name and Function

The name derives from the clay tablets on which the first accusations against traitors were inscribed in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]]. Today the tower concentrates the Shadow Cult’s bureaucracy: about a thousand employees process case law, precedents, and the regime’s official documentation. The [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tabua_reversa\|Reverse Tablet]] and the red and black chromatic seals originate and are authenticated there, and councilors such as [[_wiki_en/personagens/eliakim\|Eliakim]] cite it as a legal repository of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]].

### Restricted Holdings

The holdings are not uniformly accessible. Specific sections of the tower hold material whose circulation is restricted, accessible only to shadow lords and certain hierarchical ranks of the Cult. Ordinary registry staff cannot access these rooms, and what is archived there does not circulate as ordinary case law.

### Rules of the Tower

[[_wiki_en/criaturas/diabrete\|Imps]] are forbidden from entering Khar’Tabreth; the rule is not explained, but it is strictly obeyed. Correspondence sealed with purple ink also does not pass through the tower, returning directly to the sender: a reserved channel, separate from the registry machinery. The purple seal comes from [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]].
