# Veinkard

Veinkard is a mountainous continent west of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], about six hundred leagues west of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and separated from it by the mountain range known as the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]]. With a harsh climate, chaotic territory, and no political unification, it is the place of origin of the [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] and the region to which dragons were driven ages ago. In practice, the names “Veinkard” and “[[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]]” (its only region inhabited by humanoids) are used almost as synonyms.

### Geography and Politics

A hostile region with little [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] presence, Veinkard escapes the direct rule of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]: the purple seal, the mark of direct Shadar authority, does not cross the Dorsal. The Empire’s natural expansion is checked by geography and the presence of non-Shadar forces, so any operation beyond this border depends on indirect methods, such as pragmatic alliances and smuggling. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/cla_myr_vethan\|Clan Myr’vethan]] operates from there.

### Varna, the Elder Dragon

[[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]] is a true dragon, not a Draconid, and the last of her kind to remain near Reinkard after the migrations. She was born in the Dorsal and migrated to Varnak in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], after inhabiting the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]] for most of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]]. Her stance is defensive: she protects her ancestral home and, by preventing Draconid tribal unification, preserves her own territory without directly attacking Shadar dominion.

### Balance of Power

Varnak rests on a triad of forces: Varna, with overwhelming individual power; Clan Myr’vethan, with organized military power; and the smaller tribes, fragmented but numerous. None of them can eliminate the others without catastrophic cost, and the region’s stability rests on mutual threat, not agreement. For the allied tribes, Varna is worshiped as a protector goddess; for Clan Myr’vethan, she is the obstacle to total domination; for the Shadow Empire, a latent threat.
