# Garden of the Innocents

The Garden of the Innocents is the cemetery of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], the former human capital about two hundred eighty leagues south of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], situated near the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/igreja_de_sao_lucian\|Church of Saint Lucian]] and the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/aroada\|Aroada]] field. The [[_wiki_en/personagens/coveiro-mor\|Sexton]] tended to the city’s dead there for decades, before and during the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] conquest.

The name suggests that it was originally intended for children and young people, although in the imperial present the cemetery receives all of Myrgarde’s dead.
