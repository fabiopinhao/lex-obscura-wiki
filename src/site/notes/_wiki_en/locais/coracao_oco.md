# Hollow Heart

The Hollow Heart is the band of intermediate and terminal levels of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], the underground dungeon complex of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], under the control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. The name is also used metonymically to designate the entire complex. It serves as a prison and interrogation site, and is known for acoustics that deform every sound that passes through it.

### Acoustics

The natural acoustics of the rock distort sound in a disturbing way, and the distortion can already be heard on the stewards’ floor, in the upper levels of Ghol’Drokh, growing denser as one descends. Words and footsteps do not return as they were produced: they come back delayed and warped, as though the stone chewed them before returning them. It is said there that a whisper grows fangs and comes back to bite.

### Twin Cells

Two mirrored chambers, separated by a narrow corridor, are reserved for prisoners of interest to the throne. In one of them is the [[_wiki_en/personagens/coveiro-mor\|Sexton]] of the [[_wiki_en/locais/jardim_dos_inocentes\|Garden of the Innocents]].

The Sexton occupies the cell on the left; the one on the right faces it. Both are closed off by bars, and it is through them that the prisoners in the two cells see and speak to each other.
