# Elenir

Elenir, known as the Crystal Fortress, is an [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarin]] elven city in eastern [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], about thirty-five leagues east of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], conquered by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] during the Reinkardic Wars. Rich in [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]], extracted there as an alchemical reagent, it became a symbol of the elves’ subjugation by the empire.

### Geography

The citadel stands high, built above the level of the plateau. Those departing from Morvgrad descend the plateau, cross the plains, and then climb to Elenir.

### History

Before the fall, Elenir was an ally of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]]. [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]] participated in the campaign that took it. Even subjugated, the fortress still houses more than twenty thousand inhabitants.

### The Crystal Fortress

The epithet comes from the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/nexus\|magi nexuses]] that run through the fortress, the largest known natural outcrop in [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]], from which the empire extracts the magi that drives its industry. These nexuses were silenced from the inside out, according to the report by [[_wiki_en/personagens/vyrensen\|Vyrensen]], and [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] reads the anomaly as deliberate interference.

### Crys’Aelin

Crys’Aelin is the Aelarian name for Elenir. The form derives from ancestral Luminar (*crys*, crystal, fused with *aelin*, a cognate of *Aelindra*, dawn) and translates approximately as “crystalline dawn”, in reference to the crystal towers that earn the city the epithet of the Crystal Fortress. A refuge for the Elarins after the fall of the Luminars, Crys’Aelin preserved for eras a language that the Empire later reduced to the murmur of slaves.

### The Fall

The crystal towers were taken by resonance, without an assault: days of siege without a single arrow fired, while the Empire sought the exact frequency until the crystal began to sing. The siege without arrows was the siege of the towers; outside the walls, [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]] and Elarins from the pockets raided the camp by night with blades, and the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] ranks slept with their weapons. The crystal yielded faster after the eastern rigging was loosened, and the machines sustained the rhythm because the supply route stayed alive day and night. Logistics fell to Milor, who maintained the routes and ordered the battering ram pulled back at the right time; diplomacy fell to [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], who negotiated with the Elarins without igniting war, traded water for passage, and upheld the pact when the gates gave way, opening escape corridors while the rest of the command called for blood.
