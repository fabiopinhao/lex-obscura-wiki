# Vor-Ghol

Vor-Ghol is the Underdark of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]: a vast network of caverns and underground cities, the ancestral home of the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]]. Its name joins “vor” (much) and “ghol” (depths). It is an inverted realm where light is blasphemy, connected to [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] by a vertical shaft a thousand fathoms deep.

### Geography

Spiral towers of obsidian rise among giant mushrooms and bioluminescent lichens, glow-slime. Obsidian is a Drul building material, worked and polished; the native underground rock is basalt, which forms the openings, slopes, and natural chambers. A geological cradle shared by [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] and Druls, Vor-Ghol also shelters [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]], [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]], and deep gnomes, tens of thousands of creatures in all. Underground tides rise and block entire galleries. Cylindrical creatures secrete an acid capable of dissolving solid rock, and the tunnels they abandon form networks of smaller passages that connect distant caverns. South of the plateau, a farming village guards one of the natural entrances to Vor-Ghol.

### Light

Two sources of light divide the underground, and the difference between them tells where one is. The busy routes (caravan junctions, citadel entrances, controlled stretches) receive spaced torches: the light of those who have enough people to keep fires burning. Beyond them, only the glow-slime on the walls remains, a dim green that does not reach the ground, all the light the underground offers at no cost. Passing from one to the other is a measure of how far one has gone off the map.

### Society

The Drul Matriarchal Houses rule Vor-Ghol, in alliance with the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]]. It is a culture where treachery is a virtue distilled in mother’s milk, and alliances are made and unmade at the whim of convenience.

### Sublocations

The underground network brings together citadels and routes with names of their own: [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], the main Drul citadel and Morvgrad’s partner in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]]; [[_wiki_en/locais/xilaverth\|Xilaverth]], a citadel in the west that began as a satellite of Ghilanna, broke with it, and is hostile to it; Nulbareth and Vel’Kora, sister citadels of Xilaverth that also began as satellites of Ghilanna and supply it with ore and young blood; the [[_wiki_en/locais/eixo_de_drenn\|Axis of Drenn]], a former citadel turned into a [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídar]] nest and a bypass route; the [[_wiki_en/locais/quintos_de_irkhal\|Fifths of Irkhal]], the remote reaches of the underground; the [[_wiki_en/locais/veio_morto\|Dead Vein]], a dangerous passage with wild drídars; and the [[_wiki_en/locais/galeria_menor\|Lesser Gallery]], a corridor subject to tides that forces detours through the Axis of Drenn.

### Today

Instability runs through the depths. The Murans flee something in the deeper layers, and the Druls agitate against the surface amid the Shadow Empire’s crisis.
