# Orandia

Orandia is a domain conquered by the Shadow Empire and subjugated by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], in the western forests, in the orbit of [[_wiki_en/locais/velintrae\|Velintrae]]. Its temple was converted into a grain storehouse, as part of the elimination of symbols of spiritual resistance in the occupied territories. The measure is part of the same policy that turned the cathedral of [[_wiki_en/locais/thervad\|Thervad]] into a stable and razed [[_wiki_en/locais/danvorn\|Danvorn]].

The domain is also known for the burning of Orandia, a military episode in which [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] of the Cult faced trained elven hunters. [[_wiki_en/personagens/mur\|Mur]] remembers the battle and recognizes in the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] Nivar the technique of master hunters that he associates with that burning.
