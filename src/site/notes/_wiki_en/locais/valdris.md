# Valdris

Valdris is a port city of dark stone on the southern coast of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], the only imperial outlet to the ocean and one of the provinces farthest from the plateau of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Stone smoothed by ages of breaking surf, it lives beneath the salt and now houses a garrison of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]].

### The City

Salt defines Valdris as shadow defines Morvgrad. The sea breeze deposits a constant brine on skin, clothing, and rooftops, a gray crust that no one removes. The port wakes before the sun, amid gulls, ropes snapping against the masts, and the clamor of fishermen.

### The Fall

Before the conquest, [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] tightened its grip on Valdris’s throat, splitting the city between the salt of resistance and the sweet gleam of surrender. The division ran through the taverns: on one side, those who tasted Myrgarde’s silver on their tongues, on the other, those who still preferred the city’s salt; the saints Saint Lucian and Saint [[_wiki_en/panteao/borjean\|Borjean]] were invoked by both factions between sips of thin wine. When the [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]] departed through the stone gate, they took the illusion of protection with them. Hunger then transformed the city: wells dried up, water came to be measured in gulps, and an unnamed count sealed the gates and withheld the soldiers’ pay.

### Dravhok and the Waves

About fifty-seven years before the present, general Dravhok’s second great campaign swept through Myrgarde’s eastern belt and reached the coast. When the legions reached Valdris, the sea answered: waves of a size that no natural storm could explain surged over the city and the encamped legion, carrying away soldiers and equipment without warning and without apparent cause. Dravhok ordered the retreat.

### The Trauma of Valdris

More than two decades before the present, before Milor graduated from [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], something happened in Valdris that turned soldiers into mute veterans. [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/ravhor\|Ravhor]] were present, and Ravhor bears a furrow from a rope on his neck that he touches without realizing it when the name Valdris comes up; when Milor asks him about the city, he trembles and raises his hand to the furrow. Milor, who bears a mark of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] on his wrist, asked Ravhor to remember Valdris and do what must be done if it awakened. The details of the event remain undocumented. Among lieutenants, assignment to the Valdris garrison is considered a punishment worse than the post of executioner in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]].
