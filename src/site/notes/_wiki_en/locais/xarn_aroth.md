# Xarn’Aroth

Xarn’Aroth is the sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and also an eparchy of the Cult, situated high up, within the middle ring of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]] and closer to the northern mountains. It houses the most central institutions of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] and is its religious heart, with access restricted to the elite of the Cult.

### City

Black basalt towers crowned with perpetual braziers of blue flames dominate the district. The air there is colder and thinner than in the rest of Morvgrad. There are sacred portals whose guarding is still not entrusted to [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], but a general’s personal escort crosses the district in his service, even when composed of orc captains. About five thousand inhabitants live in Xarn’Aroth.

### Structures

Among its main structures are the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], the site of the anointings of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]]; [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s tower fused to the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]]; the [[_wiki_en/locais/praca_dos_juramentos\|Square of Oaths]], where public ceremonies are held; the [[_wiki_en/locais/arquivo_central\|Central Archive]], which preserves the records of the Cult; and [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], the arcane tower where the shadow schools are taught. The [[_wiki_en/locais/camara_de_othuun\|Chamber of Othuun]] is also located in the district.
