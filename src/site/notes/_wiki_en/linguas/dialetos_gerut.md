# Gerut Dialects

The Gerut dialects are the set of everyday and ritual forms of speech of the [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Geruts]], a human people with titan roots from the forest regions. The variation between tribes is so extreme that it makes a unified phonological description impossible. They descend from [[_wiki_en/linguas/titanico\|Titan-tongue]], with no [[_wiki_en/linguas/luminar\|Luminar]] heritage.

### Sound and Structure

Shared roots with [[_wiki_en/linguas/sylvaen\|Sylvaen]] allow partial understanding between Geruts and [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], but the prosody diverges: where Sylvaen descends in elven cadence, the Gerut dialects tend toward irregular rhythms, marked by breathing and terrain. Some dialects incorporate animal sounds as functional grammatical elements, rather than decorative ones: roars, whistles, blows to the chest. Others mimic the specific forest they inhabit, from the riverside community that uses aquatic fricatives to the mountain community whose plosives echo like stone against stone. The grammar draws on pure titan roots, without Sylvaen’s chained subordination, with short, direct, and descriptive sentences. Each dialect sounds like the forest that created it.

### Register and Writing

The magical register is almost indistinguishable from the everyday register, paralleling [[_wiki_en/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]] and [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]]: the transition between common speech and ritual channeling is a matter of intention, not of distinct vocabulary or grammar. Gerut magic is affiliated with Symbiosis Naturae. Writing varies by tribe: some communities use systems of knots in plant fibers, others carve into stone with techniques close to Draconic, and still others have no written tradition. No system is mutually intelligible between distant tribes.

### In the World

The Geruts are a peripheral people of the forest regions, rarely found outside them.
