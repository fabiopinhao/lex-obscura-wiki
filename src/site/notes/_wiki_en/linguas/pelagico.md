# Pelagic

Pelagic is a ritual language of the First Era, associated with the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]] and used in communication with ethereal [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]]. It is practically impossible to transcribe accurately: overlapping harmonics, syllables that seem to vibrate at two frequencies simultaneously, and no pause between words, in a continuous flow like a current without banks. Mortals who attempt to reproduce it describe the experience as “speaking while drowning gently”. No mortal language serves as an adequate reference; the closest would be whale song with a syllabic structure.

### Linguistic Profile

To mortal ears, Pelagic is indistinguishable. Scholars theorize that sidereals have registers, but the human inability to perceive harmonic nuances prevents confirmation; for the Frenims who channel in the language through [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]], everything sounds like the same continuous flow. No writing system is known: sidereals do not record; they exist. Frenim transcription uses adapted musical notation, not an alphabet.

The grammar resists any conventional analysis. Pelagic distinguishes neither subject from object nor present from past: the speaker, the listener, the moment, and the message exist simultaneously, something consistent with the nature of sidereals, who move through the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] with no sense of temporal linearity. Some blue gods of the ethereal plane prefer Pelagic to [[_wiki_en/linguas/talassico\|Thalassic]], a preference that contributed to the formation of [[_wiki_en/linguas/neranthiano\|Neranthian]].
