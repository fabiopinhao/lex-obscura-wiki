# Thalassic

Thalassic is the primordial language of the proto-[[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]], born in the eastern seas during [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]] and still alive as the everyday and ritual language of the undine people. It is one of the most alien among the surviving primordial languages: its sounds evolved to resonate in water, not in air, and no terrestrial throat reproduces them in full.

### Sound and Sensation

Thalassic phonology is liquid. Wet lateral consonants, fricatives that imitate water passing through crevices, and vowels that oscillate between two nearby tones, in a constant vibrato, are produced through gill resonance. The language has no stops, sounds that halt the flow of air, and so its sentences do not so much end as dissipate, like ripples that lose energy on the sand.

To terrestrial ears, Thalassic spoken by an undine sounds like an underground river translated into speech and produces mild disorientation, as though one’s balance were wavering. This is not aggression, but a lack of adaptation: the terrestrial brain tries to process frequencies that evolved for water. When the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]] imitate Thalassic to trade with undines, the result is functional but impoverished, like a charcoal drawing of an oil painting.

### Structure

Thalassic grammar is based on flow. The language does not rigidly distinguish subject from object, and the relationship between speaker and listener shifts as the sentence progresses, like a current that changes direction. Its verb tenses are relative to the tide, not the sun: the past can mean “during the previous tide,” and the future, “during the next flood tide.” This structure makes translation into [[_wiki_en/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]] particularly imprecise.

There are two registers. The everyday register is fluid and fast, adapted to underwater and coastal communication; the magical register slows the oscillation of vowels and adds low frequencies that, according to Frenim accounts, make the water tremble. The distance between the two is perceptible but not radical: the magical register sounds like a solemn version of the everyday register, not like another language. The writing system remains unknown on the western continent; Frenims report marking systems based on currents, underwater knots, and pressure patterns, but no Reinkardic scholar has managed to document them.

### Reach

Thalassic is the native language of the undines, who do not speak Reinkardic, and the gods of the seas and swamps prefer to be contacted in it rather than in the later [[_wiki_en/linguas/pelagico\|Pelagic]]. Its survival is explained by the isolation of the undines along the far eastern coast of [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]]. The language also contributed to the formation of [[_wiki_en/linguas/neranthiano\|Neranthian]], the lingua franca of the eastern continent.
