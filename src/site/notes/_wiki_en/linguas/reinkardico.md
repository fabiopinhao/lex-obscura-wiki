# Reinkardic

Reinkardic is the continent’s lingua franca, the common language that crosses borders and races and provides a basis for understanding among peoples who would otherwise barely understand one another. Having emerged in the First Era, it carries no magical power and is considered the easiest to learn among the known languages.

### Origin and Reach

Reinkardic developed from human dialects, which carry greater weight in its structure, combined with legacies from [[_wiki_en/linguas/tenebraico\|Tenebraic]], [[_wiki_en/linguas/aelarian\|Aelarian]], and [[_wiki_en/linguas/luminar\|Luminar]], whose etymology survived, but whose magic did not. Its grammar is analytical and flexible, easy to learn and adaptable to the physiology of any race, and it was this plasticity, rather than magical prestige, that spread it. Its reach is universal: spoken everywhere, it was fully assimilated by the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], while imperial [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] speak it with an accent. Its spread made it less the language of one people and more the common ground on which the continent converses with itself.

### Writing

Its writing uses an adapted human alphabet, standardized as the administrative script of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]. Contracts, sentences, and military orders are written in it, and documents drawn up in other languages have no legal validity.

### Accents

Reinkardic has no pronounced phonetic profile of its own; what distinguishes its speakers is their accents. Among native speakers, these are regional, varying from region to region. Among the races that learned it as a second language, they result from interference: traces of the mother tongue that persist in pronunciation.
