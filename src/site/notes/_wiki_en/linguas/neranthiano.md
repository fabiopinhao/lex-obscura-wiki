# Neranthiano

Neranthiano is the lingua franca of the eastern continent, spoken in the region of [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] by [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]], [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]], and [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidans]]. It is an everyday language, without any magical power, marked by dominant nasals and laterals and by a fluid rhythm in which sentences flow into one another without a marked pause.

### Origin and Sound

Neranthiano arose from the meeting of older languages: [[_wiki_en/linguas/pelagico\|Pelágico]], [[_wiki_en/linguas/talassico\|Talássico]], and [[_wiki_en/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]], combined with words of Ranidan origin. The influence of Pelágico appears in the tendency toward partial harmonics when the speaker strings syllables together rapidly, and that of Talássico in the oscillating vowels, though without the full vibrato of the original. The result is more accessible than any of its ancestral languages, which aptly describes its nature: what happens when three foreign languages need to be spoken by ordinary throats. Many Frenims and Ranidans from the surrounding area also speak Reinkardic.

### Grammar

The grammar is analytic and regular, the most accessible on the continent. Where Pelágico dissolves subject and object and Talássico measures time by the tide, Neranthiano flattens these inherited structures into a straightforward syntax that speakers of any of the three source languages learn easily: it is the language of compromise, in which each people yields a little of its own language to fit into the shared one. It inherited the phonology of the ancestral languages, although the arcane charge does not survive the simplification. Without a ritual dimension, it developed no ceremonial writing; as a recent trade language, it is presumed to use a practical system, probably adapted from the Reinkardic alphabet of the trade route, with its own signs for the oscillating vowels.

### Counterpoint to Reinkardic

Reinkardic and Neranthiano are the two lingua francas without magic in the known world, but they arose from opposing forces. Reinkardic spread through imperial imposition; Neranthiano emerged from the convenience of peoples who needed to understand one another. Neranthis shows what the linguistic landscape of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] would be without that imposition: an ecosystem of languages that influence one another without any one devouring the others.
