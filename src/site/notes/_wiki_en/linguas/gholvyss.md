# Gholvyss

Gholvyss is the primordial language of the proto-[[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] and the first of the diabolical languages, predating [[_wiki_en/linguas/tenebraico\|Tenebraic]]. It serves as the everyday speech of the Druls and as the language of invocations of diabolical pacts. Its dominant sibilants (*ss*, *zh*, *vyss*), palatalized consonants, and cavernous nasal sounds produce whispers that seem to echo even in open spaces, punctuated by tongue clicks that imitate drops of water falling from stalactites.

### Sound and Atmosphere

The rhythm is undulating, with phrases that curve in spirals like serpents. In its everyday register, the language is dense and rapid, suited to the stone corridors where economy of sound is a form of survival. In its magical register, it slows down: it lengthens the sibilants and adds deep guttural vibrations that resonate through the walls of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] as though the rock itself were taking part in the invocation. On the surface, far from damp stone, it loses some of its reverberation and sounds drier and sharper. Among its unique sounds are sublingual clicks, lateral fricatives, and a class of whispered trills called *vyssim*.

### Registers

Gholvyss has three registers. Besides the everyday and magical registers, there is the mating register, led by Drul females while the males respond. Its sinuous, hypnotic cadence prolongs the sibilants to the threshold of hearing, and Drul culture associates it with the black widow, in which seduction and danger become indistinguishable. Sharing it with non-Druls is an absolute taboo, because unprotected exposure to the cadence can induce hypnotic paralysis in those who have no resistance to it.

### Writing and Grammar

The writing of Gholvyss is unconventional: it is transmitted through marks on spider silk, vibrations in ritual webs, and engravings in living stone, so reading it requires touch as much as sight. The grammar is circular, and its sentences do not truly end, but close one cycle and open another. Each invocation is structured as a spiral, with a declaration, varied repetition, and a conclusion that returns to the beginning, reflecting the matriarchal philosophy that power does not advance in a straight line, but encircles.

### Use in the World

Gholvyss is native to the Druls, and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|ophideias]] have mastered its magical register. It is the language of the chants of intangibility and revelation of the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], of the rituals conducted in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], and of the invocations associated with [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]]. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] respond to Gholvyss or Tenebraic according to preference. Verses such as *Shevh ai gholn, sselir zhul ssolhi* illustrate its spiraling sound.
