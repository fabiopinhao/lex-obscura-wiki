# Luminar

Luminar was the unified civil and magical language of the light elves, the now-extinct Luminars. Born in [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]], it did not separate ordinary speech from magical speech: every word carried the potential for power, and the boundary between conversation and spell lay in the speaker’s intent, not in the form of the language. [[_wiki_en/linguas/aelarian\|Aelarian]] and [[_wiki_en/linguas/sylvaen\|Sylvaen]] descend from it.

### Sound

Long vowels and diphthongs, soft consonants, a predominance of liquids and nasals, open syllables: Luminar has a musical rhythm and tends toward sentences that rise along a tonal scale, evoking song without being sung. Some preserved words give a sense of this: *Aelindra* (dawn), *Thal’vérien* (guardian of the grove), *Isilmathé* (eternal light). Those who hear it compare the sensation to a harp played underwater, beautiful and distant, with a crystalline coldness that serves as a reminder that its speakers were perpetrators of genocide as much as artists.

### Writing

Interconnected curved glyphs, drawn in continuous lines without separation between words. A text in Luminar resembles a musical score: it is read through visual rhythm, not through linear sequence. The fragments kept in the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], require specialized training to decipher.

### Grammar and Legacy

The complete grammar has been lost. Scholars have reconstructed enough to identify a structure founded on intent, rather than on fixed syntactic order: the same sequence of words changes meaning according to the purpose of the person who utters it. This ambiguity explains why fragments read by unsuspecting scholars occasionally produce residual effects, when the reader’s involuntary intent activates the dormant charge. What remains of the language consists of fragments in ancient texts, recognizable cognates in Aelarian and Sylvaen, and the contribution it made to the formation of [[_wiki_en/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]].
