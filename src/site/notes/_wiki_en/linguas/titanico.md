# Titan-tongue

Titan-tongue is the primordial language of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], now extinct as a living language. It survives as the scholarly name for the magical register of two languages descended from it, [[_wiki_en/linguas/sylvaen\|Sylvaen]] and the [[_wiki_en/linguas/dialetos_gerut\|Gerut dialects]]. Its sound is organic, made up of rasping trills, soft labial fricatives, and resonant nasals, and incorporates meaningful pauses: silences that form part of the grammar, like spaces between roots.

### Sound and Perception

The rhythm is slow and deliberate, with emphasis on the last syllable of each phrase, as though the earth were answering with an echo. The auditory impression is of a language of long vowels and a lilting cadence, words that seem to grow from the ground. Those who hear fragments of Titan-tongue reconstructed by druids report less a sound than a pressure, the impression that something immense has settled nearby.

### Register and Grammar

As a living language, Titan-tongue had a unified register. In the derived usage that survives in its descendant languages, two modes are distinguished: channeling, formal and ritualistic, and communion, informal and meditative. Druids alternate between them as needed, since channeling the power of the primordial titans requires the first mode, while sensing the health of a tree calls for the second. The grammar is based on states, not actions: where [[_wiki_en/linguas/tenebraico\|Tenebraic]] says “destroy” and [[_wiki_en/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]] commands “burn”, the Titan-tongue register describes “let it be destroyed” or “burning”. The difference is ontological, since Titan-tongue describes realities it wishes to exist, while the other ritual languages command actions they want performed.

### Writing and Use

The language has no formal writing system. Living or derived, it is inscribed in nature itself: roots that grow in meaningful patterns, rock formations that say something to those who know how to read them. The Sylvan cartographer [[_wiki_en/personagens/calel\|Calel]] maps these natural texts in the Titan-tongue register without realizing that he is practicing linguistics. Titan-tongue appears in the invocations of [[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/peredur\|Peredur]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], perceived less as speech than as resonance, a vibration in the air.
