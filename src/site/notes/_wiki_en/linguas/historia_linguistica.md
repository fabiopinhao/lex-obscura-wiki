# Linguistic History of Reinkard

The history of the languages of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] is the history of those who kept their territory and those who lost it. Five primordial languages predate all written records; among the derived languages that came later, each [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/cor_arcana\|arcane color]] formed its own; and over all of this, [[_wiki_en/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]] imposed itself as a single administrative layer. The pattern running through the eras is simple and brutal: languages die when their speakers lose their land.

### The Primordial Languages

In [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]], five primordial languages emerged as natural extensions of their speakers’ consciousness, as inherent to their physiology as scales or gills. None was invented by mortals. Three survived into the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]]; two died out with their creators.

[[_wiki_en/linguas/luminar\|Luminar]] was simultaneously a civil language and a magical medium: the light elves did not distinguish conversation from invocation, and the difference between everyday speech and a spell lay in intent, not grammar. Preserved fragments still produce residual effects when read aloud by unwary scholars, because the words retain a charge even out of context. The complete Luminar language survives in manuscripts locked away in the arcane tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], where scholars analyze it without reconstructing its full grammar.

[[_wiki_en/linguas/titanico\|Titan-tongue]] was the language of the primordial [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]]. It operated not through vocal intent, but through resonance: the titans did not so much speak as vibrate, and the earth responded. Their words described states rather than giving orders. As a living language, it died out with the decline of the titans, but its name survives as an academic term for the magical register of the green languages.

[[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]] depended on nonhumanoid anatomy: resonance chambers in the throat, vibrating membranes, and infrasound made it impossible for any bipedal race to speak without a cost. It survived thanks to the isolation of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]], the biological heirs of dragons, in the mountain ranges of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]]: the ancient dragons eventually headed for distant territories, and the Saurians remained on the peaks and upper slopes. It is preserved in two registers, a magical one retained from the Forgotten Age and an everyday one adapted to smaller throats. The [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]], humans from the valleys, also assimilated it, and their shamans use it as a ritual language for [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]].

[[_wiki_en/linguas/gholvyss\|Gholvyss]] is the first diabolical language. When the proto-Druls, dissidents from the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]], descended into [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] in the Forgotten Age, they corrupted fragments of Luminar in the depths, forging a language that absorbed the acoustics of the caves. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] did not teach it to the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]]; it was through this language that the Druls became diabolical and established the first channels with the black gods of the underground. It remains Drul property, at once everyday and ritual.

[[_wiki_en/linguas/talassico\|Thalassic]] emerged in the eastern seas among the proto-[[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]]. It is the most alien of the surviving primordial languages: its phonemes depend on aquatic resonance that no terrestrial throat can replicate. It functions as the everyday and ritual language of the undines, and the gods of the seas and swamps prefer to be contacted in it. It survived through the isolation of the undines in the far east of [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]], protected by hundreds of leagues of swamps.

The pattern is revealing. The three that survived (Draconic, Gholvyss, and Thalassic) belong to races that maintained cultural isolation. The two that died out belonged to races destroyed or diminished by human expansion.

### The Derived Languages

The fall of the Luminars forced a linguistic reorganization in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]]. The systems of access to magic became formalized, and each arcane color began to operate through its own languages, derived from the primordial languages or granted by divine entities. Unlike the primordial languages, these languages have two distinct registers: an everyday one, adapted to daily use, and a magical one, preserved unchanged because the gods demand the original pronunciation.

[[_wiki_en/linguas/tenebraico\|Tenebraic]] arose when surface humans began making pacts with devils, in the system now formalized as [[_wiki_en/magia/pactum_diaboli\|Pactum Diaboli]]. The devils offered humans a simplified version, adapted to surface throats and contractual thinking, distinct from the Gholvyss they already knew. It is exclusively ritual: the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] use Reinkardic in everyday life.

[[_wiki_en/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]] was born with the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] of the First Era, shaped for battlefields and blood rituals. The orcs descend from the same elven refuge in the depths that produced the Druls, but by a divergent path: they gravitated toward the demonic fissures, which offered immediate power through blood where the devils demanded contracts and time. The first to approach the fissures were volunteers, warriors who sacrificed themselves so that the others would have time; this is why the orcs reject being called elves, not out of shame over their Luminar past, but because what they are today was paid for in blood. Generational exposure to magma rewrote elven biology, and ancestral Luminar was abandoned. Orcanic crystallized from shouts that acquired cadence. Its magical register is almost identical to its everyday one: the same language serves to shout orders and channel [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], differentiated only by cadence and intent.

[[_wiki_en/linguas/sylvaen\|Sylvaen]] fused the Luminar heritage of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] refugees with the titanic influence of the territory they inhabited, producing a language of elven musicality and titanic organicity. It is the most versatile of the green languages: almost all gods of nature respond to it, making it the lingua franca of [[_wiki_en/magia/symbiosis_naturae\|Symbiosis Naturae]]. The [[_wiki_en/linguas/dialetos_gerut\|Gerut dialects]] followed a parallel path: the [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Geruts]] absorbed Titan-tongue through coexistence, without the Luminar heritage, producing dialects with purely titanic roots. Where Sylvaen is versatile, each Gerut dialect maintains a deep affinity with a specific green god, which is why the titans’ avatars choose to manifest in forests where a Gerut community still speaks their preferred language.

[[_wiki_en/linguas/aelarian\|Aelarian]] is the closest to the original Luminar. The [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarin]] refugees in [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]] preserved Luminar ritual formality with almost archaeological fidelity, and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]] reorganized the sacred fragments into a grammar of prayer. Álvarar paladins access the magical register for [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Devotio Sancta]]. [[_wiki_en/linguas/pelagico\|Pelagic]] is the most alien of the languages of the First Era: the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]] of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] have no vocal apparatus and communicate through ethereal resonance, which the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]] mages translate into approximate sounds. While the gods of the seas prefer Thalassic, sidereals of an abstract nature respond better to Pelagic.

### Reinkardic Imperialism

Reinkardic was born as the lingua franca of the continent’s humans. During the First Era, it was one of many regional languages, but it had three decisive advantages: it was easy to learn, it adapted to the physiology of any race, and it was spoken by the most populous and commercially active race. It absorbed contributions from Tenebraic, Aelarian, and fragments of Luminar, but only as etymology: the words carry history, not magic.

It was already the official language of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], the alliance of the five human citadels, even before Morvgrad existed, and the first Shadars already spoke it. Its imposition came later. The [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]], drafted by [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]] in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], established Reinkardic as the administrative language of the pact between Shadars and Druls. In the Third Era, [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] took this logic to its limit: his decrees made Reinkardic the sole administrative, commercial, and judicial language of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]]. Subjugated races that did not speak the language lost access to any level of power.

The result is a continent where Reinkardic functions as a uniform surface layer over substrates that resist to varying degrees. Druls speak it fluently, but their accent identifies them on the surface. Subjugated Sylvans and Elarins use it out of necessity and keep Sylvaen and Aelarian among themselves: the conquerors’ language in their mouths, the ancestral one in their hearts. Beyond imperial reach, on the eastern continent, [[_wiki_en/linguas/neranthiano\|Neranthian]] was born from a mixture of Pelagic, Reinkardic, Thalassic, and tribal words. With no magical power and no imperial prestige, it is what Reinkardic would be if it had not been weaponized by a tyrant: a language of convenience, born from the need for different peoples to understand one another.

### Genealogies and Legacies

The linguistic history of Reinkard is organized into two major lineages. The Luminar lineage is the most branched: the fall of the light elves shattered Luminar into two divergent paths, the Aelarian of the Elarins, who preserved ritual formality with almost archaeological fidelity, and the Sylvaen of the Sylvans, who fused the Luminar heritage with the titanic influence of the territory they inhabited. The difference between the two reflects the difference between their speakers: Aelarian preserved erudition, Sylvaen absorbed the forest. An Elarin and a Sylvan understand each other with effort, like speakers of sister languages whose roots are recognizable but whose prosody has already diverged.

The diabolical lineage is a matter of precedence rather than division. Gholvyss did not split from Tenebraic: it came before, forged in Vor-Ghol over millennia, long before any human uttered the first syllable of Tenebraic. The two languages share the same divine patrons, but not a genealogy; a Shadar hierophant fluent in Tenebraic would understand only distorted fragments of Gholvyss. Their relationship is one of shared service, not kinship: both speak to the same gods, through paths that never crossed.

The languages of the sidereals, Thalassic and Pelagic, do not constitute a lineage: they coexist within the same arcane color without sharing a genealogy, united by their divine clientele rather than their origin.
