# Tenebraic

Tenebraic is a ritual language of the First Era, associated with the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] and oriented toward diabolical pacts and curses. It is sometimes called “Old Shadar”, a misleading designation. It is a dark Latin, conceived for contracts and not for conversation: each word is a sentence, and double meanings do not exist because contracts do not permit them.

### Sound and Rhythm

Hard plosive consonants (*k*, *t*, *d*) and sibilant fricatives (*s*, *sh*, *th*) predominate, with short, dark vowels (*o*, *u*), rarely open. The rhythm is martial and the sentences are declarative, without ambiguity. The inspiration for its sound is ecclesiastical Latin with its softness removed: where the Latin of the Church rises in prayer, Tenebraic descends in decree.

### Structure

The register is exclusively formal; there is no informal register, since the language was conceived for contracts. The writing uses angular runes with straight strokes and no curves, each representing a contractual syllable, and the texts resemble legal clauses even when they describe natural phenomena. The grammar has a fixed order, with the verb preceding the object and the subject: the act comes before the target, which comes before the agent. There are no conditional conjunctions, and the language does not express “if”, only “when” and “how”.

### Vocabulary

The lexical field is extensive in terms of power, pain, obligation, and death, and extremely impoverished in terms of affection, nature, or philosophical abstraction. There is no word for “forgiveness”; the closest, *dolth-khem*, translates literally as “suspended debt”. Among the known formulas are *Veth-kalar mortem*, linked to vampiric touch, and *Khet-amn devoeth*, linked to a field of absolute darkness. The pattern is a verbal root followed by a modifier and a domain: three words, no articles, no courtesy.

### Archaic Register

Archaic Tenebraic is the oldest stratum of the language, the same one the Shadars call “archaic Shadar.” It is Tenebraic itself in its oldest form, which survives in names and in the formulas that operate ancient artifacts of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]]. It retains the hard plosives and hyphenation of the ritual register, and shares the root *khet* with it.

From archaic Tenebraic comes *Aroth*, “sacred” or “divine,” the first half of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s name. The directed call of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]] is also spoken in archaic Tenebraic: *Kheth-vor nalash. Morvgrad khet-yun. Eliakim vor-ghast.* (“Through the shadows I call. Morvgrad, answer. Let Eliakim be summoned.”). The formula is fixed in its ritual parts and names, in the open slots, the city that answers and the recipient being sought; in this formula, [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/eliakim\|Eliakim]].
