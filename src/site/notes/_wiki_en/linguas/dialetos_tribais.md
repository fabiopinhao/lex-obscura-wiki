# Tribal Dialects

The Tribal dialects are the everyday languages of the hybrid races. They do not form a single language, but a set of distinct systems that each race developed independently, intended for communication within its own species.

### Nature

No phonological description is possible for the category as a whole: each hybrid race created its own system, and these systems are frequently incompatible with one another. The common denominator is functionality. Tribal dialects exist for immediate communication within small communities, with no literary or ritual ambitions.

### Use

There is no lingua franca among the hybrids. For external communication, they turn to [[_wiki_en/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]] or [[_wiki_en/linguas/neranthiano\|Neranthiano]]. The exceptions to this pattern are the [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]], who use [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]], and the [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidans]], who turn to Neranthiano or tribal dialects.
