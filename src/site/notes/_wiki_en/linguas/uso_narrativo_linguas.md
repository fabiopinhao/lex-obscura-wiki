# The Use of Languages

The languages of Lex Obscura are more than means of communication: they mark identity, power, and cost. Speech reveals where the speaker comes from; whoever determines the language of a conversation determines the relationship; and every magical channeling performed in a ritual language exacts a price. [[_wiki_en/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]] is the common language, present without being noticed, and only becomes visible when someone switches to another language, when an accent draws attention, or when comprehension fails.

### Ritual Languages

Most magical systems require the correct ritual language: spoken in the wrong language, the conjuration simply does not work. Each path has its own. [[_wiki_en/linguas/tenebraico\|Tenebraic]], with its hard consonants and sentences without articles, serves the black schools accessed through [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]]. [[_wiki_en/linguas/gholvyss\|Gholvyss]], sibilant and spiral in structure, is the language of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophídea]] channelings. The titan register of [[_wiki_en/linguas/sylvaen\|Sylvaen]], composed of described states and meaningful pauses, belongs to druids in communion with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]]. [[_wiki_en/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]], guttural and imperative, is the language of berserker fury, whose grammar unravels as anger grows. [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Devotio Sancta]] is the exception: it does not require a single language, since it responds to the practitioner’s devotion and tradition, and language is merely the cultural vessel of prayer.

### Language and Power

Every dialogue across languages is a relationship of power, and whoever chooses the language controls the conversation. An interrogator who conducts the questioning in the common language asserts their authority; by switching to the ritual language of a channeling, they reveal that their power comes from another source. Silence, in turn, can be the most eloquent resource of all. Speaking one’s own native language before those who do not understand it is also a form of deliberate exclusion, and reverting to it under stress betrays a loss of control.

### Accents

Reinkardic is the native language of humans, [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], and [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]], among whom variation is more regional than racial. The striking differences appear among those who learned it as a second language. [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] speak it with excessive sibilants and a tendency to invert subject and verb. [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|Orcs]] vary by rank, from veterans with functional speech to recruits with minimal vocabularies. [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]] carry involuntary melody and formality, unable to sound colloquial. [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]] pronounce words with almost mechanical precision and stumble over false cognates. [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]] draw out sibilants and compress words. [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]], with long-standing trade contact with [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]], speak with a slight accent, betrayed by retroflex consonants from [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]].
