# Purple Serpent

The Purple Serpent is a criminal organization that operates between [[_wiki/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and [[_wiki/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], dedicated to trafficking in artifacts, trafficking in people, and smuggling. With its leadership unknown, it remains active and entrenched in the region’s black market.

### Public Face

The network has a connection to the black market of [[_wiki/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]]. Its lieutenants bear a horizontal scar at the base of the throat. Internal loyalties are sealed by blood oaths and perverted codes of honor. In the past, the organization was linked to some temples of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|the Cult]].

### Operations

The Serpent’s operations range from trafficking in [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriars]] to clandestine arenas, where fortunes change hands with each deadly fight. [[_wiki/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] dagger of [[_wiki/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], passed through the Serpent’s network after the disappearance of [[_wiki/personagens/draven-thol\|Draven-Thol]], until it was seized by [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]], who dismantled part of the operation during the investigation.
