# Ordo Zathar

The Ordo Zathar is a Templar-inspired secret society that challenges the centralization of power under [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]. Publicly, the name evokes the traditional order of disciples of [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]].

### Public Face

Its cause is the restoration of religious prerogatives and a political order less subordinate to the monarch, similar to the arrangement that existed before Arothistar. It does not, however, present itself as a liberating faction: it replaces the throne’s personal supremacy with another form of institutional domination. There is open tension between Arothistar’s monarchical order and the Templar power that the Ordo represents.
