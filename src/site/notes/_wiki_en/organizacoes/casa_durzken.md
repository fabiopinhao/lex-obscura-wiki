# House Durzken

House Durzken is the dominant matriarchal house of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], with thousands of members and its seat in the House-Cathedral of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], the sacred district that serves as the underground equivalent of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Its matriarch is [[_wiki_en/personagens/nylothrae_durzken\|Nylothrae Durzken]], and its matriarchs hold [[_wiki_en/artefatos/gema-viva\|living gems]] in particular esteem.

### Diplomacy

Nylothrae conducts secret diplomacy with [[_wiki_en/locais/xilaverth\|Xilaverth]], a hostile [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] citadel in the west of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. The objective is not territorial: Ghilanna does not covet the western caverns, with their excessive numbers of rats and poor soil, and wants only for Xilaverth to owe it a favor for when it is needed. Caravans of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]] transport valuable cargo to Xilaverth under the guise of ordinary trade.
