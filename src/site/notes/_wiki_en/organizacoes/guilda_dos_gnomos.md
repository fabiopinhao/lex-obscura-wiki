# Gnome Guild

The Gnome Guild controls the black market of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] from the [[_wiki_en/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]]. It has hundreds of members and is led by [[_wiki_en/personagens/dribbel_olgund\|Dribbel Olgund]], the “Trade-Breaker”. Although it operates on the fringes of the law, it operates under a decree from [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] that granted it a license to trade, on the grounds that the market strengthened the economy.

### The Black Market

The market occupies the western parts of Morvgrad, in urban crevices disguised as ruins, catacombs, and closed-off alleys. It operates day and night, but reaches its peak on moonless nights. Forbidden potions, corrupted grimoires, fresh corpses, magical ingredients, sealed artifacts, and slaves of every kind are traded there. The license brought the bulk of transactions under official seal, with goods initialed and tribute collected, but an unsealed flow continues to circulate on the fringes, and it is through this flow that the most compromising items pass. It is this gray area that keeps the old nickname of black market alive.

### Gnomes of the Depths

The members of the Guild are gnomes of the depths, the [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derros]], who live in the lowest levels of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], where light does not reach and moss glows with arcane radiation. They have pale skin, eyes adapted to dim light, and a shorter stature than surface gnomes. Ingenious and distrustful, they organize themselves into families that occupy “money-changer burrows”, a combination of trading post, vault, and currency exchange. They manage supply routes between Vor-Ghol and the surface, forge documents, corrupt patrols, and manufacture small, lethal, and inconspicuous artifacts.

### The Trade-Breaker

“Trade-Breaker” is the honorary title of the most influential gnome in Morvgrad’s underground network, who serves simultaneously as a hidden banker, mercantile leader, and enforcer of those who break agreements. The current holder is Dribbel Olgund, who is talkative, cunning, theatrical, quick to smile, and dangerously effective, and is always accompanied by a bodyguard; his, at present, is [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]].

### The Flesh Ledger and the Blood Seal

The Guild’s ledger is the “flesh ledger”, bound in tanned dermis, with debtors’ names carved with the tip of a razor, so that each debt becomes a documentary scar. Displayed on the counter, it serves as intimidation. Paired with it is the ritual “blood seal”: intact, it holds collection in custody; each drop that escapes signals that the allotted time is running, and only the broken seal authorizes violent execution. At more sophisticated stalls, the blood is treated with cold reagents that extinguish the fire when it falls into the brazier, making the dwindling time visible.

### Relationship with the Cult

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Cult]] supports the arrangement out of sheer pragmatism: the Guild supplies the shadowy elite with items that even the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] cannot produce at scale. In exchange for tolerance, the gnomes pay regular tribute, offer favors, and maintain informants infiltrated into every caste of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]], since information is currency to them. For the citizens of Morvgrad, the black market is both a curse and a relief: there, nobles obtain elixirs, unregistered weapons change hands, and truths are bought or erased.
