# The Collectory

The Collectory is the administrative arm of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] responsible for collecting debts and taxes, headquartered in [[_wiki/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and numbering a few dozen members. It answers de jure to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] and is headed by [[_wiki/personagens/vyrensen\|Malachar Vyrensen]], known as “The Collector of Debts”.

### Parallel Espionage Network

Beneath its fiscal facade, the Collectory maintains a network of informants that extends beyond its official jurisdiction. In Morvgrad, it is said, not all eyes answer to the same eyelids. The apparatus gathers intelligence on the State’s own arms: it monitors operations of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], detects leaks in the Empire’s communication channels, and circulates sensitive reports. It is this information of its own, more than its fiscal office, that makes the Collectory a power player.

### Institutional Relations

De jure, the Collectory answers to the Shadow Council; de facto, its possession of information guarantees it broad autonomy. Its leader’s friction with the Justiciars and his trajectory within the Council’s hierarchy are part of the institutional tension surrounding the office.
