# Clan Myr’vethan

Clan Myr’vethan is the dominant [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] clan of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], led by [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]].

### Leadership

Yzmareth is an imposing Draconid with dark red scales who wields a spear topped with a ruby and uses red draconic magic. He is the only one of his species seen flying over [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|the Dorsal]].

### Purpose

The clan seeks [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]] to defeat [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]], the elder dragon who prevents tribal unification. The undertaking runs up against a central irony: the Drakhonins, bonded to [[_wiki_en/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]] since they were eggs, cannot wield Fendris, a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] artifact that only works in hands bound by no pact. The clan even lost Fendris to [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]].
