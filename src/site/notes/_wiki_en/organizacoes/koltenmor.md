# Koltenmor

The Koltenmor was a unified elven regiment, composed of [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], that marched to its death carrying ceremonial spears of glass and golden wood. It broke in the first clash against [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] shields.

The regiment bore the name of the elven city of Koltenmor, a stronghold with centuries-old walls and a king of its own, which held out for another four days of siege after the defeat in the field. After the city fell, [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] hanged the king with the ropes of his own standard, the prisoners were massacred, and the walls became a quarry for the fortresses of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]. At the end of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]], Koltenmor burned.
