# Shadow Council

The Shadow Council is the legislative and administrative body of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], convened in the [[_wiki_en/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. It does not govern in its own right: it administers the monarch’s will. When [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] seized power, he did not dissolve the Council but turned it into an instrument. Most of the elite chose survival through submission, and those who resisted were eliminated in summary trials over which the monarch himself presided.

### Nature and Function

In practice, the Council operates as an advisory chamber with executive power: it debates crises, allocates resources, and oversees the arms of the State, including the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], the generals, and the provincial lords. Its decisions, however, can be revoked by the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]] at any time. This authority gives rise to the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/foro_privilegiado\|privileged jurisdiction]] of the shadow lords, whose fates are sealed by the Council’s official opinions and the monarch’s final word. It is subordinate to Arothistar but oversees everyone else.

### Composition

The number of councilors varies, with no fixed composition. Each bears a functional title indicating their area of jurisdiction, and the selection reflects the monarch’s technocratic pragmatism, seating executives charged with administering the State at the table, not merely religious leaders. Even the unpacted and people with no ties to temples hold seats. Among the councilors are the ceremonial sorcerer [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]], the “Collector of Debts” [[_wiki_en/personagens/vyrensen\|Malachar Vyrensen]], the Master of the Forges [[_wiki_en/personagens/gorvam_threnk\|Gorvam Threnk]], the Arcane Dean [[_wiki_en/personagens/ulrik_morzenn\|Ulrik Morzenn]], the Servant of Truth [[_wiki_en/personagens/suriev_molkhiir\|Suriev Molkhiir]], the Administrator of Resources [[_wiki_en/personagens/dessian_broel\|Dessian Broel]], the Strategist of Fortifications [[_wiki_en/personagens/noxmoor\|Noxmoor]], the economic voice [[_wiki_en/personagens/harlen_vriss\|Harlen Vriss]], the Lord of the Granaries [[_wiki_en/personagens/ciravok_tharumen\|Ciravok Tharumen]], and the Fire Command [[_wiki_en/personagens/kazem_vorgrath\|Kazem Vorgrath]]. Representatives of other arms of the State, such as the general [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] and the veteran Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], participate in specific sessions as permanent guests, without voting rights. Others attend only remotely, through the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculi]], as needed: [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]], the founder of the Cult, a silent observer of unusual longevity, and Orvhal, lord of [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]]. The same channel was available to [[_wiki_en/personagens/draven-thol\|Draven-Thol]], Justiciar of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], now missing.

### Wings and Factions

The Council operates along three informal alignments, none of them declared. The Templar wing brings together the longstanding councilors, tied to the temples, heirs to the composition before Arothistar, who lost relevance as the Empire advanced; among them are Jaakurs and Suriev Molkhiir. The pragmatic wing brings together the monarch’s direct appointees: newer members, technical specialists, expansionists, with no ties to temples, among them Dessian Broel. It usually allies itself with the military wing, which brings together Noxmoor and Kazem Vorgrath.

The wings are not monoliths. The Templars themselves are divided over Elenir’s magi crystals: some scorn them as an affront to the faith, Suriev Molkhiir’s position, and others do not, such as Jaakurs. Ulrik Morzenn, the Arcane Dean, professes allegiance to no wing: he is one of the longstanding members, has a technical temperament, and declares no affiliation.

### Expansion Without Consensus

The Council has no unified position on maintaining territorial expansion, and the rift runs through the wings. The pragmatic wing and the military wing advocate aggressive expansion, a position that the generals’ expansionist faction, embodied by Vekraal, upholds in the field. The Templar wing opposes it for two cumulative reasons: it sees the Empire slipping out of control, becoming disorganized as it grows, and it sees [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] culture being diluted in what it conquers. The argument is both ideological and self-interested, because it was precisely the Empire’s advance that deprived the temples of relevance.

The monarch’s position on the subject is unknown. No one in the Cult knows whether Arothistar intends to continue conquering, and the throne’s silence is part of the problem: without a word from him, each wing projects its own. What circulates are expectations. Some expect the order to cross the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] any day, and some already count legions with no destination.

### High Chair

The high chair is the Council’s rotating leadership position. Its occupant presides over sessions, distributes the agendas, acts as the institutional spokesperson, and safeguards the monarch’s word: in urgent matters bearing a purple seal, he acts without requiring a joint decision by the Council, and it falls to him to inform the dean of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] first when one of them is summoned before the monarch. It confers no power over the monarch. The seat is held by the veteran councilor [[_wiki_en/personagens/eliakim\|Eliakim]], whose reputation is built on erudition and rhetoric.

### Council Hall

The Council Hall occupies the Obsidian Circle in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], Morvgrad’s administrative ring. The table is circular, set over a pit of enchanted pitch, and the vaulted ceiling displays twelve effigies of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|black gods]], including that of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], whose wear contrasts with that of the others. The [[_wiki_en/artefatos/efigie_de_arothistar\|effigy of Arothistar]] was placed one span above the twelve, a visual declaration of hierarchy. The councilors’ chairs are made of obsidian; a granite chair is reserved for the representative of the Justiciars. The councilors communicate with remote agents through onyx Oculi, and the Council’s sealed archives open only with purple seals or direct authorization from the high chair.
