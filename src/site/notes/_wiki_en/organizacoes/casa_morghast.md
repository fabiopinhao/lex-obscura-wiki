# House Morghast

House Morghast is a minor Matriarchal House, subordinate to [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/casa_durzken\|Durzken]]. Its primary function is guarding the Gates of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], a structure resembling a stone jaw, made of basalt slabs, rails of petrified bone, and hidden counterweights.

### Role

Its subordination to Durzken is functional, not servile: Morghast provides guards and lower-ranking [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|ophideias]]. [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] was born into this House, of the Morghast lineage, sworn to [[_wiki_en/panteao/ulravaeth\|Ulravaeth]] from the womb. [[_wiki_en/personagens/vhraxil\|Vhraxil]], a male [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] and guard of the Gates, has been known to Syndrith since childhood, and she calls him “little beetle”.
