# Khelg’Dur

Khelg’Dur is a [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]] trading house based in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], where it operates caravans of giant [[_wiki_en/criaturas/isopode\|isopods]]. It has dozens of members and is known as the Black Anvil Convoy.

### Operations

Its caravans traverse the underground routes of Vor-Ghol under the direction of experienced drivers. Among them is [[_wiki_en/personagens/borzrun\|Borzrun]], a gray-bearded miner, pragmatic and dry-witted, who led a convoy through the [[_wiki_en/locais/eixo_de_drenn\|Axis of Drenn]] to avoid the [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídars]] of the [[_wiki_en/locais/veio_morto\|Dead Vein]]. The house also transported a private shipment of [[_wiki_en/personagens/nylothrae_durzken\|Nylothrae Durzken]]’s toward [[_wiki_en/locais/xilaverth\|Xilaverth]]. During one of its crossings, the [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derro]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/khreek_fio-fino\|Khreek Fine-Thread]], a specialist in marking tunnel traffic routes, remained as guarantor on [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]]’s orders.
