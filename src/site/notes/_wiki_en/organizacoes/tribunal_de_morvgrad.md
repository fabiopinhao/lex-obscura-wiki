# Tribunal of Morvgrad

The Tribunal of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] is the supreme court of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], also called the Supreme Tribunal, responsible for trying crimes that exceed the authority of individual [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] or that involve members of the upper hierarchy. It operates on three levels, each with its own jurisdiction and composition.

### The Three Levels

The Lower Chamber, composed of scribes and notaries, handles minor crimes and mundane punishments. The High Chamber tries transgressions against the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] and brings together veteran shadow lords and members of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]]. The Inner Circle, the highest judicial body, tries heresies and capital treasons under the presidency of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] or his archons.

### Archons

The archons are shadow lords individually chosen by the monarch to study and adjudicate crimes against the Lex Obscura and against the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]]. They differ from the Justiciars in being court judges, not field investigators. The office requires at least twenty years of service to the Cult, complete mastery of both codices, and unanimous approval from the Inner Circle. They preside over sessions, determine whether there are grounds for a formal trial, interpret precedents, draft binding judgments, and supervise the Scriptorium. Each archon has a team of notaries for procedural preparation, recording hearings, and enforcing administrative judgments.

### Scriptorium

The [[_wiki_en/locais/scriptorium_de_morvgrad\|Scriptorium of Morvgrad]] is the wing of copyists and exegetes where the codices of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]] are reproduced and glossed: the Verbum Arkhon, the Lex Obscura, and the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/tabula_umbrae_codex\|Tabula Umbrae]]. Access is restricted to sworn acolytes under the tutelage of the archons. Each copy receives a ritual cipher and a consecrated colophon, which guarantees authenticity and traceability; the forgery of codices is a capital crime.

### Procedural Instruments

The Tribunal maintains its own instruments. The [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tabua_reversa\|Reverse Tablet]] is a polished clay tablet for judicial records, written with a raven quill; there are special inks, which are revealed only under specific conditions. The [[_wiki_en/conceitos/veredictum_exkarthon\|Veredictum Exkarthon]] guarantees the accused the right to demand the presentation of evidence before sentencing, one of the few procedural protections in existence. The chain of custody requires a continuous record of possession and handling of evidence, and any break invalidates the evidence before the Inner Circle. These protections serve the integrity of the evidence, not clemency: in the doctrine of shadow justice, the trial enforces order, does not purify the condemned, and acquittal is not a procedural category. Among the penalties, the Sigil of [[_wiki_en/panteao/abbhal\|Abbhal]], eternal imprisonment of the soul within its own body, is the harshest sentence, reserved for traitors of the highest rank; the [[_wiki_en/magia/maledictus_fidejussor\|Mark of the Condemned]] binds the sentenced individual to serving their sentence under progressive penalties.

### Relationship with the Justiciars

The Tribunal and the Justiciars function as complementary systems: the Justiciars investigate, try cases in the field, and carry out executions swiftly, while the Tribunal conducts formal trials, handles complex cases, and establishes binding precedents. In the archons’ sessions, the archons are the judges; before them, the Justiciars act as prosecutors and attorneys and, depending on the case, even as defense lawyers. There is latent tension, as the Justiciars see the court as slow and political, and the archons consider the Justiciars impulsive. In practice they cooperate, because the system requires both. Among the notable precedents are the Heresy of [[_wiki_en/personagens/qaelun\|Qaelun]], a necromancer tried posthumously, and the Iconographic Protocol, which established rules on depictions of Arothistar.

### Lesser Races and Creatures

The Tribunal maintains a body of regulations for the legal classification of subjugated races and bestial creatures within imperial territory. Field Justiciars apply these rules in everyday practice, but complex disputes, such as classifying a species as sentient or bestial, are referred to the Inner Circle. The dividing line is political: creatures useful to the Cult tend to receive the status of lesser servants, while inconvenient ones are declared bestial and subject to summary extermination.
