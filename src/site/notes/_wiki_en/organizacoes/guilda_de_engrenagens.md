# The Gear Guild

The Gear Guild is a city-workshop and an illegal brotherhood of gnome inventors, famous for cheap replicas of dwarven artifacts. The name is a deliberate corruption of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], which legitimate dwarves consider a spit-worthy insult, and earns the city the nickname “Thrunk-K’har”.

### Forbidden Catalogs

Its forbidden catalogs circulate on the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/mercado_negro\|black market]], offering everything from assassin automatons to enchanted prostheses, all at a fraction of the price and quality of the dwarven originals.

### Symbol

An eye inside a broken gear. Displaying it is punishable by execution in [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]].

### Distinction

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]] bears a similar name and is a different organization. It is the black market syndicate of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], with no involvement in manufacturing dwarven replicas.
