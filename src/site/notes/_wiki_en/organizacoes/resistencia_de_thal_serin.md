# Resistance of Thal’Serin

The Resistance of Thal’Serin is the last significant pocket of opposition to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. It brings together hundreds of members in the sacred forest of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]]: free [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]] as the majority, human refugees, and Pindoriars who have escaped subjugation. In urban settings, it is also called the “Rebels of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]]”.

### Forest Protection

The resistance’s existence depends on an ancient pact between [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] [[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]] and [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]]: protection of the forest in exchange for Vozrador’s supremacy in [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]]. While the pact remains in force, imperial forces cannot penetrate Thal’Serin’s natural defenses. Morlgrund, the Titan of the Earth, has an avatar that awakens in the woods; Titânia, the Green Mother, is the patron of the Sylvans. The protection is not absolute: the Empire surrounds the forest, controls supplies, and carries out limited incursions, but does not achieve definitive conquest.

### Leadership

[[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]], a human and the daughter of the priest [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]], leads the resistance with pragmatism more than idealism. The chief ranger is [[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]], exceptionally talented in tracking, a devotee of Titânia, and bearer of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/marca_das_luas_gemeas\|Mark of the Twin Moons]]. The orphaned brothers [[_wiki_en/personagens/mirin\|Mirin]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/calel\|Calel]] grow up under his tutelage, apprentices who represent the new generation of fighters. A druidic circle led by [[_wiki_en/personagens/peredur\|Peredur]] serves functions of wisdom and communication with the titans.

### Limits

The resistance defends but does not attack: it is not an army. It depends on external supplies along an exposed route, and its mixed composition generates internal tensions between conservative Sylvans and human refugees. Its strength lies in the forest, not in the offensive, and that strength endures only as long as the pact that protects it holds.
