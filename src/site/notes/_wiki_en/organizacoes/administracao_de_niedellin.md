# Administration of Niedellin

The Administration of Niedellin is the local government of the village of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]], under the black color of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]. Small, with fewer than fifty members, it is organized around three offices: [[_wiki_en/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Lord Merari]], provincial governor; [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]], chief of the guard, one of the steward posts; and [[_wiki_en/personagens/eralmir_vess\|Eralmir Vess]], scribe and steward.

### Structure

The government concentrates the administrative functions of a frontier village. Nominal authority belongs to Merari, a weak and manipulable governor; the armed force answers to Maraxus, whose position as chief of the guard exceeds, in practice, the limits of the office; and recordkeeping and the stewardry fall to Eralmir Vess. Communication with [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] depends on an [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|oculus]], an instrument that the Administration keeps in its custody. Successive village stewards were killed over the years; Eralmir is the only one who remains in office.
