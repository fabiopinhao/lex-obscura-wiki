# Cult of Hazburim

The Cult of Hazburim is a clandestine demonic cult dedicated to [[_wiki_en/panteao/hazburim\|Hazburim]], the Lord of the Flies, deity of pestilence. By binding itself to a demon, it falls under the proscription that the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] imposes on this type of pact, the [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], and therefore operates in secret, underground.

### Detection Failure

Its continued existence illustrates a failure of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] to detect clandestine pacts: a proscribed cult sustains itself for years without the Shadow Cult identifying it. The magic capable of exposing such bonds is the same magic that would reveal inconvenient allies, which makes its use politically dangerous.
