# The Circles of Khargoth

The Circles of Khargoth are an arcane academy based in the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], with around two hundred members. They function as both a university and an arcane tribunal: there, schools of magic are studied, debated, and practiced under supervision, and the tower itself keeps watch over those who frequent it.

### Structure

The system of “circles” organizes access to knowledge into successive levels. Advancement is based on merit, but merit there is measured by loyalty to the Cult, mirroring the broader imperial logic. Among its students was [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]], who passed through the tower before his anointing as a [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]].

### Prohibitions

The tower prohibits the magic of [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]] within its walls.
