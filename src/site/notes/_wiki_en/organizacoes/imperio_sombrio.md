# Shadow Empire

The Shadow Empire, also called simply the Empire or the Shadow Dominion, is the sovereign state that governs the continent of [[_wiki/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] under the authority of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], from [[_wiki/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. It designates the political entity, distinct from the state religion, the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], although the two are inseparable.

### Foundation

The Empire did not begin as a monarchy. Its structure evolved from the Shadow Cult, founded by [[_wiki/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]] in the [[_wiki/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]] by unifying the diabolical temples scattered across the northern plateau; the creation of Morvgrad as the political and religious capital sealed the fusion of faith and government. Arothistar’s rise transformed the Cult into an instrument of absolute monarchy: the [[_wiki/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]] did not repeal the [[_wiki/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]], but aligned itself with it and overlaid it. It was revolution and continuity at the same time, since absorbing the old system ensured submission where destroying it would have generated resistance.

### Territorial Extent

With the fall of [[_wiki/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], the shadow dominion reached its maximum extent across Reinkard. The dwarven mountains of the west, the eastern belt, Dernessal, and the southern coastal cities were subjugated, and [[_wiki/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]] became the breadbasket that fed Morvgrad and its legions. Only isolated pockets of resistance remained, with [[_wiki/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] as the last significant refuge. The system of provincial lords, which predated the monarchy, allocated conquered territories to local lords, and the most consolidated territories became formal provinces.

### Power Structure

The Empire operates on six hierarchical levels. Arothistar rules from the heights of [[_wiki/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], isolated from the world, communicating through intermediaries and through the [[_wiki/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]]. Below him, the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] administers the monarch’s will without actually governing. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], generals, lords, and archons form the third level; [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]] and [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|ophideias]] the fourth; officers and magistrates the fifth; citizens, troops, and the subjugated the base. The philosophy that sustains the whole is simple: strength conquers, but only order maintains.

### Public Perception

For many, the Empire represents freedom, progress, and historical justice against the oppression of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|white gods]], and it attracted scholars, the ambitious, and renegades who saw it as a refuge for what the old world forbade. In practice, it merely exchanged one kind of repression for another: submission is the price of protection, and fear replaces faith as the social glue.

### Language and Metaphysics

The imposition of [[_wiki/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]] as the official language was a founding act of sovereignty, and the administration requires all documentation to be written in that language; the ritual layer, however, preserves [[_wiki/linguas/tenebraico\|Tenebraic]] and [[_wiki/linguas/gholvyss\|Gholvyss]] as the languages of diabolical contracts. On the metaphysical plane, decades of [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] rule fueled by diabolical pacts on an institutional scale generated the [[_wiki/cosmologia/manto_das_sombras\|mantle of shadows]], which altered Reinkard’s balance and weakened the influence of the non-shadow gods over the continent.
