# Order of Azazel

The Order of Azazel is a religious order of the luminous pantheon, devoted to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]] and the white [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/cor_arcana\|arcane color]]. Its status hidden and its leadership unknown, it secretly preserves fragments of angelic doctrine in a world dominated by the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|black gods]].

### Public Face

The Order has no military structure. Its doctrine survives as a clandestine counterpoint to the dominion of the black gods, and its signs are discreet: the rosaries of [[_wiki_en/panteao/azazel\|Azazel]], which serve as protection against evil, and the nearly extinct sacred names associated with the white faith, among them Azazel, [[_wiki_en/panteao/paralda\|Paralda]], and [[_wiki_en/panteao/israfil\|Israfil]]. Azazel himself may be the last active white god, although his cosmological status remains uncertain.

### Activities

[[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]] is a confirmed member of the Order. He was the one who gave [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] a rosary of Azazel, which she carries hidden beneath the amulet of [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]], for when the darkness became too much. Koliah’s dual faith, which brings together Titânia and Azazel, connects the Sylvan resistance to the human luminous tradition.

The material traces of the white faith appear in the chapel of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], where [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]] identifies weakened but resilient white magic: rosemary, sage, and faded stained-glass windows in white arcane colors. Abramis has mastered the Canticle of Restoration, capable of purifying arcane corruption, a power he used on Milor’s mark of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]]. The origin of this gift remains ambiguous: secret training within the Order or an innate gift.
