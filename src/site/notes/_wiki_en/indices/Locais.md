# Locations

Every stone of this Empire was laid upon something else. It is best not to ask what.

---

## Capitals and Cities

- [[_wiki/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]: imperial capital, seat of Arothistar’s power
- [[_wiki/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]]: settlement in Ventrista, on the banks of the River Parveni
- [[_wiki/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]: former human capital, subjugated by the Empire
- [[_wiki/locais/valdris\|Valdris]]: coastal port city, imperial outlet to the ocean
- [[_wiki/locais/varnak\|Varnak]]: mountainous region in Veinkard, fragmented tribal territory
- [[_wiki/locais/qadesh\|Qadesh]]: vassal city-state in the eastern desert

## Conquered Domains

- [[_wiki/locais/danvorn\|Danvorn]]: domain devastated by the Cult, with its ashes salted
- [[_wiki/locais/orandia\|Orandia]]: domain whose temple was converted into a grain storehouse
- [[_wiki/locais/thervad\|Thervad]]: domain whose cathedral was converted into a stable; home to a school of alchemists

## Fortresses and Seats of Power

- [[_wiki/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]]: Arothistar’s tower, fused to the Peak of Nethys
  - [[_wiki/locais/oculario\|Ocularium]]: ceremonial room at the top of Mor’Severith, houses the Oculus
- [[_wiki/locais/khar_morthar\|Khar Morthar]]: Tower of Death, vertical necropolis in Morvgrad
- [[_wiki/locais/khar_tabreth\|Khar’Tabreth]]: Tower of Tablets, archives and records of the Cult
- [[_wiki/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]]: arcane tower in Morvgrad, hierarchical circles of access
- [[_wiki/locais/dhar-morveth\|Dhar-Morveth]]: Shadar plateau where Morvgrad was built

## Elven and Ancient Sites

- [[_wiki/locais/elenir\|Elenir]]: elven castle, Crystal Fortress, rich in magi
- [[_wiki/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]]: sacred forest, refuge of the free Sylvans
- [[_wiki/locais/lendalas\|Lendalas]]: forest destroyed by Vekraal’s alchemical fire

## Underground and Mountains

- [[_wiki/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]: the Underdark, ancestral home of the Druls
- [[_wiki/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]]: Drul citadel in Vor-Ghol, matriarchal seat of House Durzken
- [[_wiki/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]]: sacred district in Ghilanna, Eparchy of Jaakurs
- [[_wiki/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]]: dwarven citadel, master forges of the imperial arsenals
- [[_wiki/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]]: subterranean level beneath Morvgrad; lower galleries with the tar pits, where Nyx’Ara receives visitors
  - [[_wiki/locais/camara_de_othuun\|Chamber of Othuun]]: passage to Ghol’Mournak; stairway of 500 whispering steps
  - [[_wiki/locais/poco_etereo\|Ethereal Well]]: central communications nexus of the Oculus
- [[_wiki/locais/ghargash\|Ghargash]]: tribal fort, carnage that marked Grothar’s youth

## Continents and Regions

- [[_wiki/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]]: material plane, contains Reinkard, Veinkard, and Neranthis
- [[_wiki/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]: central continent, Shadar hegemony
- [[_wiki/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]]: mountain range and continent west of Reinkard
- [[_wiki/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]]: agricultural region at the base of the Shadar plateau
- [[_wiki/locais/karanthia\|Karanthia]]: poor province on the southeastern coast, east of Valdris
- [[_wiki/locais/rio_parveni\|River Parveni]]: main river of Ventrista, runs through Niedellin
- [[_wiki/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]]: mountain range, natural border between Reinkard and Veinkard
- [[_wiki/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]]: highest peak in the Dorsal, former dragon’s nest
- [[_wiki/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]]: independent continent of the blue races
- [[_wiki/locais/therion\|Therion]]: First Era fortress, where the Maleficários were compiled
- [[_wiki/locais/xilaverth\|Xilaverth]]: Drul citadel hostile to Ghilanna in Vor-Ghol

## Sacred and Arcane Sites

- [[_wiki/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]]: temple of the devil Azkaroth, anointing of the Justiciars
- [[_wiki/locais/templo_de_velmorain\|Temple of Velmorain]]: temple of the devil Velmorain, Cosmic Veil
- [[_wiki/locais/templo_de_vozrador\|temple of Vozrador]]: abandoned temple of the devil Vozrador, infested with undead
- [[_wiki/locais/poco_etereo\|Ethereal Well]]: central communications nexus of the Oculus in Ghol’Mournak
- [[_wiki/locais/scriptorium_de_morvgrad\|Scriptorium of Morvgrad]]: wing of copyists and exegetes of the Codex Somniorum

## Urban Landmarks of Morvgrad

- [[_wiki/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]]: sacred district, basalt towers with blue braziers
- [[_wiki/locais/arquivo_central\|Central Archive]]: judicial archive, annex to the Supreme Tribunal
- [[_wiki/locais/praca_dos_juramentos\|Square of Oaths]]: ceremonial square in Xarn’Aroth
- [[_wiki/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]]: seat of the Shadow Council in Mor’Draconis
- [[_wiki/locais/arena_de_drakmur\|Arena of Drakmur]]: combat arena on the border of the Plateau
- [[_wiki/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]]: black market, operated by the Gnome Guild
- [[_wiki/locais/dromos_draconis\|Dromos Draconis]]: defunct gladiatorial arena, suppressed by the Shadow Council
- [[_wiki/locais/ezrath\|Ezrath]]: noble commercial district of Morvgrad
- [[_wiki/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]]: wall, fortified ring of Morvgrad, former home of dragons

## Dark Places

- [[_wiki/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]]: dungeons of Morvgrad, Hollow Heart and Drokh’Malahar
  - [[_wiki/locais/coracao_oco\|Hollow Heart]]: prison and interrogation room with distorting acoustics
  - [[_wiki/locais/drokh_malahar\|Drokh’Malahar]]: abyssal levels of Ghol’Drokh, chambers of perpetual torture
- [[_wiki/locais/fossa_dos_suplicantes\|Pit of the Supplicants]]: urban abyss in Morvgrad
- [[_wiki/locais/fosso_da_purga\|Pit of the Purge]]: site of summary executions
- [[_wiki/locais/ermo_de_lendalas\|Waste of Lendalas]]: open expanse of ashes where Lendalas was burned
- [[_wiki/locais/jardim_dos_inocentes\|Garden of the Innocents]]: cemetery of Myrgarde
- [[_wiki/locais/veio_morto\|Dead Vein]]: underground passage in Vor-Ghol, wild drídars
- [[_wiki/locais/eixo_de_drenn\|Axis of Drenn]]: underground bypass route in Vor-Ghol
- [[_wiki/locais/galeria_menor\|Lesser Gallery]]: underground corridor subject to tides
- [[_wiki/locais/quintos_de_irkhal\|Fifths of Irkhal]]: remote underground region of Vor-Ghol
