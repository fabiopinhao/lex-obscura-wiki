# Concepts

The words the Empire uses to govern were not chosen at random. Each term carries the weight of a decree.

## Judicial Instruments

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/veredictum_exkarthon\|Veredictum Exkarthon]]: extreme legal instrument that requires the Justiciar to examine evidence before passing sentence
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/sigilo_de_abbhal\|Sigil of Abbhal]]: eternal imprisonment of the soul in a living body
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/foro_privilegiado\|privileged jurisdiction]]: prerogative of the shadow lords, who are tried by the Shadow Council and the monarch

## Governance and Ceremony

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/protocolo_iconografico_da_coroa\|Iconographic Protocol of the Crown]]: system of effigies for projecting imperial omnipresence
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/corvos_espioes\|Spy Ravens]]: dispersed surveillance of the skies and roads, maintained by lords and Councilors, not a single state apparatus
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/politica_religiosa\|Religious Policy]]: favoritism toward devils and tension between cults within the imperial apparatus
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/politica_linguistica\|Linguistic Policy of the Shadow Empire]]: hegemony of Reinkardic through three layers of control
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/hierarquia_racial_imperial\|Imperial Racial Hierarchy]]: premise that races have ontological predispositions, the basis of State policy

## Parallel Economy

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/mercado_negro\|Black Market]]: parallel economy that spans the Empire, tolerated where it is profitable

## Imperial Calendar

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/varnum\|Varnum]]: annual unit of 365 days (thirteen Lúnias + Nullis)
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lunia\|Lúnia]]: monthly unit of 28 days in four lunar fáscias
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/nullis\|Nullis]]: Day of the Void, standalone day between Varnums
