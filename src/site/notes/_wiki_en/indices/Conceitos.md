# Concepts

The words the Empire uses to govern were not chosen at random. Each term carries the weight of a decree.

---

## The Codex Somniorum

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]]: first section, the Word of the Sovereign, containing Arothistar’s direct pronouncements
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]]: second section, the Law of Shadows, a legal code created by Xarn
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/tabula_umbrae_codex\|Tabula Umbrae]]: third section, the Tablet of Shadows, on spells and alchemy

## Judicial Instruments

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/veredictum_exkarthon\|Veredictum Exkarthon]]: extreme legal instrument that requires the Justiciar to examine evidence before passing sentence
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/sigilo_de_abbhal\|Sigil of Abbhal]]: maximum sentence, the eternal imprisonment of the soul in a living body
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/foro_privilegiado\|privileged jurisdiction]]: prerogative of the shadow lords, who are tried by the Shadow Council and the monarch

## Governance and Ceremony

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/protocolo_iconografico_da_coroa\|Iconographic Protocol of the Crown]]: decree that replaces Arothistar’s physical presence with effigies placed at sites in Morvgrad
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/corvos_espioes\|Spy Ravens]]: ravens used as eyes and couriers by lords and Councilors, outside any official apparatus
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/politica_religiosa\|Religious Policy]]: religious organization of the Empire around the Shadow Cult, which favors devils and tolerates demons with distrust
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/politica_linguistica\|Linguistic Policy of the Shadow Empire]]: hegemony of Reinkardic through three layers of control
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/hierarquia_racial_imperial\|Imperial Racial Hierarchy]]: premise that races have ontological predispositions, the basis of much of State policy

## Glossaries and Reference

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terminologia_essencial\|Essential Terminology]]: glossary of terms that span the setting and have no entries of their own
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/vocabulario_administrativo\|Administrative Vocabulary]]: official euphemisms that conceal the nature of procedures
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/sistema_de_medidas\|System of Measures]]: imperial standardization of distance, time, weight, volume, and currency

## Expressions and Usage

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/canalizador\|channeler]]: imperial term, both official and derogatory, for druids
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/khemor\|Khemor]]: Shadar term for the mask of fear
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/moeda_de_chumbo\|lead coin]]: Shadar slang for something that seems valuable but is fake or toxic
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/cerco_de_khordun\|Siege of Khordun]]: episode that gave rise to the Orcish expression “carry a barrel”

## Parallel Economy

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/mercado_negro\|Black Market]]: parallel economy that spans the Empire, tolerated where it is profitable
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/livros_razao\|Ledgers]]: black market records, which note only what their owners admit exists

## Imperial Calendar

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/varnum\|Varnum]]: annual unit of 365 days (thirteen Lúnias + Nullis)
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lunia\|Lúnia]]: monthly unit of 28 days in four lunar fáscias
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/nullis\|Nullis]]: standalone day between two Varnums, colloquially called Vaz Day

## Culture and Daily Life

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/aroada\|Aroada]]: team game from Myrgarde in which a leather sphere must be sent through the hoop using only the feet, head, and shoulders
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/a_viuva_de_valdris\|The Widow of Valdris]]: classic play from Myrgarde
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/charaba\|charabanc]]: provincial animal-drawn vehicle with four wheels and an elongated body
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/casca_viva\|living bark]]: plant material used in the Sylvans’ light armor
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/carvalho_mae\|Mother Oak]]: the first oak planted in each sacred grove
