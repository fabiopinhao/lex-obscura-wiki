# Magic System

All magic requires payment. The caster’s body is the currency.

---

## Access Systems

The paths through which mortals channel arcane power:

- [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]]: technical study, pure arcane understanding
- [[_wiki_en/magia/pactum_diaboli\|Pactum Diaboli]]: contract with devils
- [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]]: bond with demons
- [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Devotio Sancta]]: divine devotion
- [[_wiki_en/magia/symbiosis_naturae\|Symbiosis Naturae]]: symbiosis with nature
- [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]]: ethereal meditation

## Schools of Magic

Thirty schools, divided among the five colors of the arcane spectrum. Each school has ten cataloged spells.
