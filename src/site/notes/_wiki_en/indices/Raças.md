# Races

The Empire does not recognize equality among the races. It recognizes usefulness.

---

## Civilized Races

- [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]]: humans of vampiric appearance who govern Reinkard with the calculated efficiency of those who traded light for control, and never looked back.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]]: devilish elves from the depths of Vor-Ghol, organized into Matriarchal Houses where treachery is a virtue and every courtesy conceals a blade.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]]: a derogatory term for subjugated humans and elves, second-class citizens whose shortened lifespans are the first sign that the Empire exacts even time from those it rules.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]]: humans of infernal blood, whose demonic markings make them conditionally tolerated citizens in an empire that prefers devils to demons.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]]: demonic elves forged in the tundras and mountain ranges of the north, who despise the term “elf.” They embrace fury and the brief life cycle that accompanies it.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]]: sidereal humans from Neranthis, isolationist blue mages who fear the shadows with the same intensity with which they command the aether.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undine]]: sidereal elves aligned with oceans, rivers, and lakes in the far east of Neranthis, so detached from the material world that the Shadars are unaware of their existence.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Gerut]]: titanic humans who inhabit woodlands, hunters and druids who rejected imperial expansion in the Forgotten Age and have never reconsidered.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]]: forest elves, who traded Luminar purity for the protection of green and live as rebels in a land that was once entirely theirs.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarar]]: humans touched by celestial gods, paladins of the luminous pantheon whose angelic devotion makes them instruments of implacable judgment.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarin]]: angelic elves from Elenir, the closest to the Luminars in blood and arrogance, preserving fragments of a tradition the rest of the world has already buried.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]]: dwarf miners, smiths, and caravan traders, subjugated in the forges of Thandruk Varn, where clans that hated one another from a distance now share dormitories.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]]: primordial light elves whose prechromatic civilization saturated Reinkard with magi for eras. Extinct.

## Bestial Races

- [[_wiki_en/racas/vulfano\|Vulfan]]: a family of canine hybrid humanoids.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worg]]: large Vulfans, trapped between the consciousness that prevents them from being beasts and the beastliness that prevents the world from accepting them as people.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/varglin\|Varglin]]: gnomish Vulfans in continual decline, so rare and despised that imperial censuses do not even bother to distinguish them from Murans.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurian]]: a family of reptilian hybrid humanoids.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]]: elite Saurians with thick scales, prehensile tails, and elemental breath, organized into Drakhonin clans whose draconic lineage is a source of fierce pride.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobold]]: gnomish Saurians of artificial origin, manufactured through Shadar manipulation of corrupted dragon eggs.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/lynxiano\|Lynxian]]: a family of feline hybrid humanoids.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/rajhar\|Rajhar]]: large Lynxians, nomadic predators of tiger, lynx, and panther sublineages, without clan, territory, or patron. They are guided by instinct and the next contract.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/margau\|Margáu]]: arboreal gnomish Lynxians symbiotic with flora, more integrated into the civilized green world than their Rajhar cousins, but no less carnivorous for it.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/aviano\|Avian]]: a family of avian hybrid humanoids.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/aquilar\|Aquilar]]: large Avians, eagle and falcon hybrids, oviparous and urban before the conquest, marked by the angelic blessing of their lineage.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/fringal\|Fringal]]: gnomish Avians with vestigial wings who do not fly, territoriality that compensates for their size, and a tendency to be underestimated by those who have not seen them fight.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidan]]: a family of amphibian hybrid humanoids.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/saag\|Saag]]: large Ranidans, massive humanoids of sapian lineage, with a rare ability to cross the ethereal veil through contact with water.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/boglin\|Boglin]]: gnomish Ranidans of chelonian lineage, humanoid turtles with natural shells and limited telepathy.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Muran]]: ratlike humanoids who communicate through ultrasound and pheromones, invisible in censuses, despised as vermin, and valuable only while disposable.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/minotauro\|minotaur]]: nearly extinct bovine hybrids, so rare in the Third Era that most imperial scholars classify them as legend.

## Small Folk

- [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derro]]: deep gnomes degenerated by centuries of arcane isolation in Vor-Ghol, who have turned insanity into a vocation.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/goblin\|goblin]]: gnomes corrupted by demonic influence during the Racial Wars, domesticated by the Empire for tasks that Shadars refuse to perform.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/spriggin\|Spriggin]]: woodland gnomes once guardians of forests and ruins, nearly extinct in the Third Era when Shadars burned everything the titans did not protect directly.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/hallirin\|Hallirin]]: urban surface gnomes, artisans and alchemists who constitute the gnomish baseline. Almost all dead or enslaved after the Reinkardic Wars.
- [[_wiki_en/racas/nalvirin\|Nalvirin]]: gnomes from Neranthis who live confined to Frenim citadels as an auxiliary population and are unknown outside the eastern continent.

## Creatures

- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/basilisco\|basilisk]]: a monstrous reptile with a petrifying gaze that inhabits the continent’s unmapped depths, existing in the bestiary as a known and feared threat without ever setting foot onstage.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/grifo\|griffin]]: a war mount with a lion’s body and an eagle’s wings, domesticated by humans and Álvarars in the Reinkardic Wars.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/troll\|troll]]: a territorial predator of colossal mass and extreme regeneration, which only truly dies when burned or dissolved.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/sombralva\|Sombralva]]: a large feline with light-absorbing fur, domesticated exclusively by ophideias as a combat mount and status symbol among the Drul hierophant-assassins. Also called a “shadow panther” in popular speech.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/servo_hediondo\|Hideous Servant]]: a necromantic construct animated from corpses, amalgams of flesh, or entities from lower planes, as varied in form as the summoner who created it and the material at hand.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídar]]: a giant arachnid from Vor-Ghol bred by Drul matriarchs in lineages selected for aggression and intelligence, planted as an egg in rival citadels to destroy them from within when it matures.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/hidna_ressonante\|resonant hidna]]: a bioluminescent mineral organism that runs through Vor-Ghol like a mirrored tunnel; marching and voices make the underground resonate until it splits, which is why Drul troops never march in formation through the corridors.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnoll]]: a hyena-humanoid hybrid of overwhelming stench, brute ferocity, and short life, classified as “second-degree domestic fauna” by the Empire and treated exactly as that implies.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/ogro\|ogre]]: massive humanoids of limited intelligence, used as disposable brute force in the lowest strata of the Empire.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/salamandra\|salamander]]: a reptile of volcanic environments whose flammable blood is the most coveted and scarce resource in any campaign against trolls.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/diabrete\|imp]]: winged creatures, raised in captivity and used as servile messengers.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/urtor_de_calcita\|calcite urtor]]: a crustacean-reptilian predator of Vor-Ghol’s deep galleries, which disguises itself as a stalactite and lures travelers with a false reflection of firelight; feared enough to justify armed escorts.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/vitraxil\|vitraxil]]: a beetle from Vor-Ghol whose mosaic elytra break glow-slime into living stained glass, and whose delicate appearance has become synonymous with fragility in the vocabulary of the depths.
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/isopode\|isopod]]: a giant arthropod with a chitinous carapace used as a pack animal on Vor-Ghol’s caravan routes, with bells wrapped in felt when silence is worth more than speed.
