# Races

The Empire does not recognize equality among the races. It recognizes usefulness.

---

## Civilized Races

- [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]]: devilish humans of vampiric appearance, the dominant race of Reinkard and full citizens of the Empire
- [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]]: devilish elves from the depths of Vor-Ghol, organized into Matriarchal Houses where treachery is a virtue, who have practiced the devil pact since before any other race did
- [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]]: a derogatory term for subjugated humans and elves, second-class citizens without full citizenship, with a baseline lifespan of about eighty years
- [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]]: humans of infernal blood from the mountain ranges of Varnak, bound to the red gods and living in a fragmented tribal society
- [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]]: demonic elves from the tundras and mountain ranges of the north, who despise the term “elf”; soldiers with berserker fury and short lives, the spearhead of the imperial armies
- [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]]: sidereal humans from Neranthis, scholarly, isolationist mages who fear the shadows and move between the material world and the edges of the ethereal plane
- [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undine]]: sidereal elves aligned with oceans, rivers, and lakes in the far east of Neranthis, unknown to the Shadars
- [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Gerut]]: titanic humans from the woodlands and swamps of the southeast, hunters and druids who, in the Forgotten Age, rejected the expansion of the human kingdoms and remained in the forests
- [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]]: titanic forest elves, devotees of Titânia, who made Thal’Serin the center of their resistance
- [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarar]]: angelic humans, paladins of the luminous pantheon whose order dates back to the Forgotten Age; the ontological opposite of the Shadars
- [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarin]]: angelic elves from Elenir, the closest descendants of the Luminars in tradition and arcane power
- [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]]: dwarf miners, smiths, and caravan traders, subjugated in Thandruk Varn, where clans that hated one another from a distance share forges and dormitories
- [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]]: primordial light elves, ancestors of all elven lineages, whose civilization saturated Reinkard with magi for eras; they disappeared as a unified people

## Bestial Races

- [[_wiki_en/racas/vulfano\|Vulfan]]: a family of canine hybrid humanoids
- [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worg]]: large Vulfans, trapped between consciousness and beastliness, who live on the margins of the Empire’s cities, tolerated for their usefulness and never accepted as people
- [[_wiki_en/racas/varglin\|Varglin]]: gnomish Vulfans in continual decline, rare and despised, almost always mistaken for Murans and absent from imperial censuses as a category of their own
- [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurian]]: a family of reptilian hybrid humanoids
- [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]]: elite Saurians with thick scales, prehensile tails, and elemental breath, organized into Drakhonin clans that claim a divine heritage from dragons
- [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobold]]: gnomish Saurians of artificial origin, manufactured through Shadar manipulation of corrupted dragon eggs
- [[_wiki_en/racas/lynxiano\|Lynxian]]: a family of feline hybrid humanoids
- [[_wiki_en/racas/rajhar\|Rajhar]]: large Lynxians, nomadic predators of tiger, lynx, and panther sublineages, without clan, territory, or patron. They are guided by instinct and the next contract
- [[_wiki_en/racas/margau\|Margáu]]: arboreal gnomish Lynxians, symbiotic with flora and strictly carnivorous, more integrated into forest communities than their Rajhar cousins
- [[_wiki_en/racas/aviano\|Avian]]: a family of avian hybrid humanoids
- [[_wiki_en/racas/aquilar\|Aquilar]]: large Avians, eagle and falcon hybrids, oviparous and urban before the conquest, marked by the angelic blessing of their lineage
- [[_wiki_en/racas/fringal\|Fringal]]: gnomish Avians with vestigial wings that have never sustained flight, marginalized on the urban fringes of the Empire
- [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidan]]: a family of amphibian hybrid humanoids
- [[_wiki_en/racas/saag\|Saag]]: large Ranidans, massive humanoids of toad lineage, with a rare ability to cross the ethereal veil through contact with water
- [[_wiki_en/racas/boglin\|Boglin]]: gnomish Ranidans of chelonian lineage, humanoid turtles with natural shells and limited telepathy
- [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Muran]]: ratlike humanoids who communicate through ultrasound and pheromones, recorded in imperial censuses as noxious urban fauna, with no other recognized role than subsistence espionage
- [[_wiki_en/racas/minotauro\|minotaur]]: bovine hybrids of monumental size, nearly extinct and regarded as legend by most peoples of Reinkard

## Small Folk

- [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derro]]: deep gnomes, shaped by centuries of isolation and arcane exposure in the deep strata of Vor-Ghol; the name was coined by the Shadars
- [[_wiki_en/racas/goblin\|goblin]]: gnomes corrupted by demonic influence during the Racial Wars, who sustain the Empire’s logistics as fortress stewards
- [[_wiki_en/racas/spriggin\|Spriggin]]: woodland gnomes aligned with the titans, guardians of the forest understory, on the brink of extinction in the Third Era since the Reinkardic Wars and Shadar expansion burned the unprotected forests
- [[_wiki_en/racas/hallirin\|Hallirin]]: urban surface gnomes, artisans and alchemists who constitute the gnomish baseline. Almost all were killed or enslaved after the Reinkardic Wars
- [[_wiki_en/racas/nalvirin\|Nalvirin]]: gnomes from Neranthis who live confined to Frenim citadels as an auxiliary population and are unknown outside the eastern continent

## Creatures

- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/basilisco\|basilisk]]: a monstrous reptile with a petrifying gaze, from the depths of Vor-Ghol and the continent’s unmapped caves
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/grifo\|griffin]]: a war mount with a lion’s body and an eagle’s wings, domesticated by humans and Álvarars in the Reinkardic Wars
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/wyvern\|wyvern]]: a lesser, bipedal dragon with membranous wings, a distant and degenerate cousin of true dragons
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/manticora\|manticore]]: a beast with a lion’s body, a scorpion’s tail, and a face that parodies human features
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/harpia\|harpy]]: a creature with a female torso and a vulture’s body, known for the extreme filth of its nests
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/estrige\|strige]]: a batlike winged creature with a long, bloodsucking beak
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/corvelho\|corvelho]]: half crow and half rat, common in Myrgarde’s cemeteries
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/cinereta\|cinereta]]: a beast of burden bred by the Shadars for the highlands
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/troll\|troll]]: a territorial predator of colossal mass with extreme regeneration that only fire or acid can interrupt
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/sombralva\|Sombralva]]: a large feline with light-absorbing fur, domesticated exclusively by ophideias as a combat mount and status symbol among the Drul hierophant-assassins. Also called a “shadow panther” in popular speech
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/servo_hediondo\|Hideous Thrall]]: a necromantic construct animated from corpses, amalgams of flesh, or entities from lower planes, varying in form according to the summoner and the material available
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídar]]: a giant arachnid from Vor-Ghol bred by Drul matriarchs in lineages selected for aggression and intelligence, planted as an egg in rival citadels to destroy them from within when it matures
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/hidna_ressonante\|resonant hidna]]: a bioluminescent mineral organism that runs through Vor-Ghol like a mirrored tunnel; marching and voices make the underground resonate until it splits, which is why Drul troops never march in formation through the corridors
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnoll]]: a hyena-humanoid hybrid of overwhelming stench, brute ferocity, and short life, classified by the Empire as “second-degree domestic fauna”
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/ogro\|ogre]]: massive humanoids of limited intelligence, used as disposable brute force in the lowest strata of the Empire
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/salamandra\|salamander]]: a reptile of volcanic environments whose flammable blood is the most coveted and scarce resource in any campaign against trolls
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/diabrete\|imp]]: winged creatures, raised in captivity and used as servile messengers
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/urtor_de_calcita\|calcite urtor]]: a crustacean-reptilian predator of Vor-Ghol’s deep galleries, which disguises itself as a stalactite and lures travelers with a false reflection of firelight; a known danger on caravan routes, warranting armed escorts
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/vitraxil\|vitraxil]]: a beetle from Vor-Ghol whose mosaic elytra break glow-slime into living stained glass, and whose delicate appearance has become synonymous with fragility in the vocabulary of the Depths
- [[_wiki_en/criaturas/isopode\|isopod]]: a giant arthropod with a chitinous carapace used as a pack animal on Vor-Ghol’s caravan routes, with bells wrapped in felt when silence is worth more than speed
