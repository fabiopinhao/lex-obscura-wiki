# Verbum Arkhon

*The Living Words.*

---

The first and most sacred volume of the [[_wiki/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]]. It contains direct pronouncements from [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]: visions, decrees, and dark parables, written in gold ink on black parchment. Each word is considered infallible and immutable. Reading is mandatory during judicial vigils.

The [[_wiki/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]] is not an inert text. When a [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] invokes it, the words fall like a hammer: the Word of the Sovereign takes precedence over all laws written by mortals. Orders, pacts, decrees, promises, threats, sentences: the Lord of the Tower fulfills them all as pronounced. It is precisely this inflexibility that serves as the foundation of the Empire.

In practice, any decree of the Verbum can suspend any clause of the [[_wiki/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] when the sovereign so decides. The Verbum is simultaneously the source of the Justiciars’ authority and their absolute limitation: the same word that justifies each sentence can remove them from office without recourse.

The handover formula during the Anointing expresses this subordination: *“Arothistar est lex. Abbhal est vox. Tu es martellus.”*

The title of **Book I** of the trilogy comes from this volume, because the story begins where the word of the sovereign is law.
