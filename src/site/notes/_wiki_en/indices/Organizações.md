# Organizations

The Empire functions because every cog believes itself indispensable. None is.

---

## Imperial Structure

- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]: the sovereign State of Arothistar
- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]]: the Empire’s legislative and administrative body
- [[_wiki/conceitos/hierarquia_imperial_niveis_complementares\|Imperial Hierarchy]]: generals, lords, officers, and the tripartite base
- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/coletoria\|the Collectory]]: administrative and fiscal arm, collection of debts and taxes, and parallel espionage network
- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|the Underground Alliance]]: political pact between Shadars and Druls, engineered by Xarn
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/administracao_de_niedellin\|Administration of Niedellin]]: dissolved local government

## Justice and Order

- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]]: judicial arm, judges with power over life and death
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/tribunal_de_morvgrad\|Tribunal of Morvgrad]]: the Cult’s supreme court, three levels

## Military and Priestly Orders

- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]]: old military-religious order, weakened by the Justiciars
- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]]: Drul spy-assassin hierophants

## Cults and Religious Factions

- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]]: official religion of the state, worship of the black gods
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/culto_de_hazburim\|Cult of Hazburim]]: clandestine demonic cult, outlawed by the Shadow Cult
- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ordem_de_azazel\|the Order of Azazel]]: luminous religious order of the angel Azazel
- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/igreja_de_sao_lucian\|Church of Saint Lucian]]: surviving luminous faith, nearly extinct

## Noble Houses and Clans

- [[_wiki/organizacoes/casa_durzken\|House Durzken]]: Drul Matriarchal House, governance of Ghilanna
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/casa_morghast\|House Morghast]]: lesser Matriarchal House, guard of the Gates of Ghilanna
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/cla_myr_vethan\|Clan Myr’Vethan]]: Draconid clan from Varnak, seeks to recover Fendris, lost to Nidzina

## Secret Societies and Resistance

- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/resistencia_de_thal_serin\|the Resistance of Thal’Serin]]: free Sylvans and human refugees
- [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]]: organized crime, artifact trafficking, and smuggling
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/ordo_zathar\|Ordo Zathar]]: secret society inspired by the Templars; a platform for restoring religious prerogatives and an order less subordinate to the monarch

## Guilds and Circles

- [[_wiki/organizacoes/circulos_de_khargoth\|the Circles of Khargoth]]: arcane academy, hierarchical system of knowledge
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]]: organized crime, black market, and counterfeiting
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]]: Karashim trading house, isopod caravans in Vor-Ghol
- [[_wiki/organizacoes/tradicao_de_sirana\|Tradition of Sirana]]: contemplative discipline that trains Nalvirins for Meditatio Aetheris, in the Frenim citadels
