# Lex Obscura

*The Laws of the Shadow.*

---

The second volume of the [[_wiki/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]]. Legal code of the Shadow Empire, created by Xarn in the Second Era as a legal-religious foundation that subordinated local cults to a central structure. Organized by category (treason, heresy, disobedience, negligence) and updated every decade with precedents and binding case law.

The [[_wiki/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] defines punishments and exceptions. It catalogs liturgical infractions (unsanctioned conjurations, invocations of forbidden entities, heretical rituals, desecrations of sacred symbols) and prescribes sentences, from perpetual torture in the Crucitories of Drokh’Malahar to obliteration. It is the law that neither forgets nor forgives.

But any clause can be suspended by the [[_wiki/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]] when the sovereign so decides. This tension between Xarn’s foundational law and the word of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] is the fissure that the Ordo Zathar exploits: the faction advocates Templar prerogatives and a political order less subordinate to the monarch.

It is the volume whose pages, within the Codex, turn on their own when a formal trial requires the letter of the law.

The trilogy’s title comes from this volume, because every order casts a shadow, and it is the laws of that shadow that truly govern.
