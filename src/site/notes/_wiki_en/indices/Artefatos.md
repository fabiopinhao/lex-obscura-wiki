# Artifacts

Objects that outlived their creators rarely did so by accident.

---

## Justiciar’s Kit

- [[_wiki/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]]: sacred tripartite grimoire (Verbum Arkhon, Lex Obscura, Tabula Umbrae)
- [[_wiki/artefatos/spina_lexis\|Spina Lexis]]: judicial scepter that shatters, compels, and sentences
- [[_wiki/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus Morvgradi]]: onyx sphere for communication via the Ethereal Well
- [[_wiki/artefatos/tabua_reversa\|Reverse Tablet]]: polished clay tablet for judicial recordkeeping in ink

## Major Relics

- [[_wiki/artefatos/oculus_maior\|Oculus Maior]]: command center for imperial communications
- [[_wiki/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]]: Luminar dagger capable of cleaving planes
- [[_wiki/artefatos/efigie_de_arothistar\|Effigy of Arothistar]]: animated sentinel of the bridge of Mor’Severith

## Weapons, Armor, and Insignia

- [[_wiki/artefatos/armadura_templaria_de_orum\|Templar Armor of Orum]]: enchanted with strength, endurance, and vigor
- [[_wiki/artefatos/capacete_de_maraxus\|Helmet of Maraxus]]: relic of the defunct arenas of Dromos Draconis; completely conceals its wearer’s face
- [[_wiki/artefatos/bombarda_ananica\|Dwarven Bombard]]: hybrid dwarven-Shadar artillery
- [[_wiki/artefatos/tegmen\|Tegmen]]: standard armor of the Shadar aristocracy, a status symbol
- [[_wiki/artefatos/insignia_durzken\|Insignia of Durzken]]: ring of authority of the matriarch of House Durzken

## Containment Artifacts

- [[_wiki/artefatos/grilhoes_de_contencao_arcana\|Shackles of Arcane Containment]]: chains that suppress flow from the ethereal plane
- [[_wiki/artefatos/algemas_de_supressao_arcana\|Manacles of Arcane Suppression]]: bracelets that drain magi (or a convincing bluff)

## Religious and Arcane Artifacts

- [[_wiki/artefatos/estatueta_de_vozrador\|Statuette of Vozrador]]: obsidian idol from ancient Morvgrad

## Substances and Alchemy

- [[_wiki/artefatos/luto_de_nyssis\|Mourning of Nyssis]]: antimagic alchemical substance distilled from the flower of Nyssis

## Documents and Texts

- [[_wiki/artefatos/de_anima_vendita\|De Anima Vendita]]: treatise by Ambrosin on the Shadars and the pledging of the soul
- [[_wiki/artefatos/livro_vermelho_dos_dragoes\|Red Book of Dragons]]: draconological treatise bound in scales
- [[_wiki/artefatos/tratado_de_wijmarv\|Treatise of Wijmarv]]: reveals the true function of Fendris
