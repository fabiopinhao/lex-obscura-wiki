# Chronology

History is written by the victors. In this Empire, the victor has never changed.

---

## Eras

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|Forgotten Age]]: before the records; rule of the Luminars
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]]: fall of the Luminars; rise of humans and Shadars
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]]: continental plague, founding of Morvgrad, alliance with Druls
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]]: Arothistar’s Empire; the present era

Overviews of the Reinkardic Wars: [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_sombra_desce\|The Shadow Descends]] · [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_ultima_muralha\|The Last Wall]] · [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_ascensao_do_monarca\|The Rise of the Monarch]]

## Forgotten Age

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/reinkard_na_idade_esquecida\|Reinkard in the Forgotten Age]]: rule of the Luminars; abundant magi, gods walked among mortals
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/fragmentacoes_dos_luminares\|Fragmentations of the Luminars]]: internal schisms gave rise to Druls (Vor-Ghol), orcs (mountains), and undines (east coast)
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_raciais\|Racial Wars]]: struggle between gods fought with mortal flesh; predates organized civilization
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/barreira_magica_luminar\|Luminar Magical Barrier]]: arcane seals in the mountain ranges that prevented dragons from crossing them
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/cruzadas_contra_os_luminares\|Crusades against the Luminars]]: militarized arm of the Álvarar church against the elves of light

## First Era

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/queda_dos_luminares\|Fall of the Luminars]]: final fragmentation of the elves of light; gave rise to Elarins and Sylvans
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/surgimento_dos_primeiros_shadar\|Emergence of the First Shadars]]: humans who accepted blame for the massacre refused Álvarar absolution and sought diabolical pacts
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/pax_alabastrina\|Pax Alabastrina]]: voluntary cooperation between Elarins and humans; maturation of the Sancta system
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/fundacao_de_myrgarde\|Founding of Myrgarde]]: five human citadels united after the expulsion of the Shadars from the central lands
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/migracao_shadar_ao_planalto\|Shadar Migration to the Plateau]]: persecuted by the orthodoxy, they migrated to the northern plateau and encountered the Druls
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/expulsao_cultistas_draconicos\|Expulsion of the Draconic Cultists]]: Shadars expelled tribes with demonic pacts to Varnak; origin of the Varnakians
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/corrupcao_ovos_dragao\|Corruption of the Dragon Eggs]]: Shadars corrupted abandoned nests; deliberate selection gave rise to the kobolds
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/pacto_vozrador_titas\|Vozrador’s Pact with the Titans]]: protection of Thal’Serin in exchange for supremacy in Patho
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/primeira_migracao_dragoes\|First Migration of the Dragons]]: dark corruption forced elders to migrate; Varna remained alone on the Peak of Nethys

## Second Era

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/sopro_do_eclipse\|Breath of the Eclipse]]: continental plague that decimated humans; disputed origin; plateau unaffected
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/quebra_da_barreira\|Breaking of the Barrier]]: dark corruption consumed residual magi; northern border opened to the orcs
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/migracao_de_varna\|Varna’s Migration]]: corruption made the Peak of Nethys untenable; Varna migrated to Varnak
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/reconstrucao_pos_peste\|Reconstruction and Rise of Myrgarde]]: interracial cooperation after the plague; Myrgarde became a continental power
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/fundacao_de_morvgrad\|Founding of Morvgrad]]: Xarn drafted the Lex Obscura and unified the temples; Morvgrad built as the seat of the Cult
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/alianca_subterranea_evento\|Underground Alliance]]: Shadar-Drul Pact, in which the Druls provided the magic and the Shadars, the sword
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/segunda_migracao_dragoes\|Second Migration of the Dragons]]: great tribes departed beyond the mountain ranges; Varna remained

## Reinkardic Wars

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Beginning of the Wars]]: the Cult depleted the plateau; about fifty years of continental conflict
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/submissao_da_ventrista\|Submission of Ventrista]]: fertile lands at the base of the plateau; first target of the descent
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/ataque_fracassado_a_thalserin\|Failed Attack on Thal’Serin]]: sacred forest resisted; Vozrador’s pact with the titans protected it
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/frentes_de_guerra_e_destino_das_racas\|Fate of the Races]]: Sylvans, humans, and gnomes enslaved; Gerut survived because of the titanic terrain
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/formalizacao_militar_dos_orcs\|Military Formalization of the orcs]]: turning point at which tribal fury became battlefield discipline
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerra_formal_e_impasse_continental\|Formal War and Stalemate]]: Myrgarde declared war; continental coalition against Morvgrad
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/campanha_ocidental_de_dravhok\|Western Campaign]]: swept the mountain range from north to south; culminated in the subjugation of Thandruk Varn
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/subjugacao_de_thandruk_varn\|Subjugation of Thandruk Varn]]: negotiated surrender, with the forges redirected toward Morvgrad in exchange for survival
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/campanha_oriental_e_queda_de_dernessal\|Eastern Campaign]]: conquest of the cities in Myrgarde’s eastern belt
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/purga_do_conselho_e_autodeclaracao_como_arothistar\|Purge of the Council]]: Dravhok eliminated rival Councilors and declared himself Arothistar
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/instituicao_do_verbum_arkhon\|Establishment of the Verbum Arkhon]]: constitution of the Empire; Lex Obscura absorbed without alteration
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/fundacao_dos_justiciares\|Founding of the Justiciars]]: judicial arm with power over life and death; Orum-Rahru was the first
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/proibicao_do_rito_de_ossarium\|Prohibition of the Rite of Ossárium]]: declared a capital heresy by decree of the monarch; conversion of humans officially ended
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/instalacao_em_mor_severith\|Taking Up Residence in Mor’Severith]]: Arothistar emptied the Council’s old library and took it as his tower
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/destruicao_de_lendalas\|Destruction of Lendalas]]: forest burned by alchemical fire; became the Waste of Lendalas
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/queda_de_elenir\|Fall of Elenir]]: Elarin citadel conquered; arcane nexuses incorporated into imperial infrastructure
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/cerco_de_myrgarde\|Siege of Myrgarde]]: three years of siege; famine, plague, and silencing of the luminous gods
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/queda_de_myrgarde_e_fim_das_guerras_reinkardicas\|Fall of Myrgarde]]: Arothistar’s last appearance in the field; end of the wars

## Third Era

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/reorganizacao_imperial_e_provincias\|Imperial Reorganization]]: Arothistar governs through intermediaries; Iconographic Protocol of the Crown
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/tensoes_e_ameacas_da_terceira_era\|Tensions of the Third Era]]: weakened Drul alliance; orc migration; twenty of twenty-seven Justiciars dead
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/cadeia_da_fendris\|Chain of Fendris]]: theft of the Luminar dagger from Dessian Broel’s private museum; the person who commissioned it and the motive remain unknown
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/sabotagem_nas_forjas_de_thandruk_varn\|Sabotage in the Forges]]: drop in production in Thandruk Varn; nature and perpetrator unknown
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/uncao_de_milorsevogh\|Anointing of Milorsevogh]]: seventh living Justiciar; received Spina Lexis, Codex, and Oculus in the Temple of Azkaroth
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/nexus_silenciados_na_fortaleza_de_cristal\|Silenced Nexuses]]: Elenir’s crystals active but silenced from the inside out; deliberate sabotage
