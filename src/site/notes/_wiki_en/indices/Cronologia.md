# Chronology

History is written by the victors. In this Empire, the victor has never changed.

---

## Eras

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|The Forgotten Age]]: period before the eras began to be counted, under the rule of the Luminars
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]]: period that began with the Fall of the Luminars and saw the rise of humans and Shadars
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]]: period that began with the Breath of the Eclipse and saw the founding of Morvgrad and the alliance with the Druls
- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]]: period of consolidated rule by Arothistar’s Empire, the present era

Overviews of the Reinkardic Wars: [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_sombra_desce\|The Shadow Descends]] · [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_ultima_muralha\|The Last Wall]] · [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_ascensao_do_monarca\|The Rise of the Monarch]]

## Forgotten Age

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/reinkard_na_idade_esquecida\|Reinkard in the Forgotten Age]]: overview of the continent under the Luminars, with abundant magi and titans walking among the forests
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/fragmentacoes_dos_luminares\|Fragmentations of the Luminars]]: series of schisms that gave rise to Druls (Vor-Ghol), orcs (mountains), and undines (east coast)
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_raciais\|Racial Wars]]: struggle between gods fought with mortal flesh, the origin of the hybrid humanoid races
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/barreira_magica_luminar\|Luminar Magical Barrier]]: arcane seals in the mountain ranges that prevented dragons from crossing them
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/cruzadas_contra_os_luminares\|The Crusades against the Luminars]]: war waged by the human kingdoms against the elves of light, legitimized by the Sancta faith

## First Era

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/queda_dos_luminares\|Fall of the Luminars]]: final fragmentation of the elves of light; gave rise to Elarins and Sylvans
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/surgimento_dos_primeiros_shadar\|Emergence of the First Shadars]]: break by the humans who accepted blame for the massacre, refused Álvarar absolution, and sought diabolical pacts
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/pax_alabastrina\|Pax Alabastrina]]: golden age of the First Era, marked by cooperation between Elarins and humans and refinement of the Sancta system
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/fundacao_de_myrgarde\|Founding of Myrgarde]]: union of five human citadels after the expulsion of the Shadars from the central lands
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/migracao_shadar_ao_planalto\|Shadar Migration to the Plateau]]: the Shadars’ flight from repression to the northern plateau, where their first alliances with the Druls took shape
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/expulsao_cultistas_draconicos\|Expulsion of the Draconic Cultists]]: the Shadars’ banishment to Varnak of dragon-worshiping tribes with a demonic bond; origin of the Varnakians
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/corrupcao_ovos_dragao\|Corruption of the Dragon Eggs]]: Shadar experiment with abandoned dragon nests; deliberate selection gave rise to the kobolds
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/pacto_vozrador_titas\|Vozrador’s Pact with the Titans]]: agreement that spared Thal’Serin from destruction and made the forest the source of Vozrador’s power
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/primeira_migracao_dragoes\|First Migration of the Dragons]]: westward exodus of the dragons amid dark corruption; only Varna remained on the Peak of Nethys

## Second Era

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/sopro_do_eclipse\|Breath of the Eclipse]]: continental plague of disputed origin that decimated humans; it is said not to have reached the plateau
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/quebra_da_barreira\|Breaking of the Barrier]]: depletion of the Luminar barrier by dark corruption, which opened the northern border to the orcs
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/migracao_de_varna\|Varna’s Migration]]: Varna’s move to Varnak after corruption made the Peak of Nethys untenable
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/reconstrucao_pos_peste\|Reconstruction and Rise of Myrgarde]]: period of interracial cooperation after the plague, in which Myrgarde established itself as the dominant power on the plains
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/fundacao_de_morvgrad\|Founding of Morvgrad]]: Shadar reorganization in which Xarn drafted the Lex Obscura and unified the temples; it gave rise to Morvgrad, the seat of the Cult
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/alianca_subterranea_evento\|The Underground Alliance]]: formal pact between Shadars and Druls, in which the Druls provided the magic and the Shadars, the sword
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/segunda_migracao_dragoes\|Second Migration of the Dragons]]: wave of migration by the great dragon tribes beyond the mountain ranges; Varna remained

## Reinkardic Wars

- [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]]: the continental conflict that ended the Second Era and gave rise to the Shadow Empire
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Beginning of the Wars]]: the Cult’s descent onto the continent after the plateau was depleted; it marked the beginning of about fifty years of conflict
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/conversao_de_dravhok\|Conversion of Dravhok]]: the Rite of Ossárium by which a human became a Shadar and took the name Dravhok
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/submissao_da_ventrista\|Submission of Ventrista]]: fall by starvation of the fertile lands at the base of the plateau, the first region taken by the Cult
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/ataque_fracassado_a_thalserin\|Failed Attack on Thal’Serin]]: first major clash between the Cult and the forces of nature, in which the titans repelled the attack
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/frentes_de_guerra_e_destino_das_racas\|Fate of the Races]]: uneven impact of the war on the peoples, with Sylvans, humans, and gnomes enslaved and the Gerut protected by the titanic terrain
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/formalizacao_militar_dos_orcs\|Military Formalization of the orcs]]: turning point at which tribal fury became battlefield discipline
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerra_formal_e_impasse_continental\|Formal War and Stalemate]]: Myrgarde’s declaration of war, which united the continent against Morvgrad
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/campanha_ocidental_de_dravhok\|Western Campaign]]: Dravhok’s first major offensive, which swept through the western mountain range from north to south as far as Thandruk Varn
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/subjugacao_de_thandruk_varn\|Subjugation of Thandruk Varn]]: negotiated surrender, with the forges redirected toward Morvgrad in exchange for survival
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/campanha_oriental_e_queda_de_dernessal\|Eastern Campaign]]: Dravhok’s second major offensive, against Myrgarde’s eastern belt; culminated in the fall of Dernessal
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/purga_do_conselho_e_autodeclaracao_como_arothistar\|Purge of the Council]]: coup in which General Dravhok purged the Shadow Council and declared himself Arothistar
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/instituicao_do_verbum_arkhon\|Establishment of the Verbum Arkhon]]: promulgation of the Empire’s constitution, which incorporated the Lex Obscura without alteration
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/fundacao_dos_justiciares\|Founding of the Justiciars]]: creation of the judicial arm with power over life and death; Orum-Rahru was the first Justiciar
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/proibicao_do_rito_de_ossarium\|Prohibition of the Rite of Ossárium]]: Arothistar’s decree banning the conversion of humans into Shadars; the conversion became a capital heresy fourteen years later
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/instalacao_em_mor_severith\|Taking Up Residence in Mor’Severith]]: Arothistar’s seizure of the Council’s old library, which was emptied and converted into his tower
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/destruicao_de_lendalas\|Destruction of Lendalas]]: burning by alchemical fire of one of the Sylvans’ largest sacred forests, now the Waste of Lendalas
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/queda_de_elenir\|Fall of Elenir]]: conquest of the Crystal Fortress of the Elarins; arcane nexuses partially incorporated into imperial infrastructure
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/cerco_de_myrgarde\|Siege of Myrgarde]]: three-year siege of the human capital, with famine, plague, and the silence of the luminous gods
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/queda_de_myrgarde_e_fim_das_guerras_reinkardicas\|Fall of Myrgarde]]: end of the wars with the conquest of the human capital, where Arothistar made his last appearance in the field

## Third Era

- [[_wiki_en/eventos/reorganizacao_imperial_e_provincias\|Imperial Reorganization]]: territorial consolidation of the Empire under provincial lords; Arothistar governs through intermediaries
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/tensoes_e_ameacas_da_terceira_era\|Tensions of the Third Era]]: sources of instability in the imperial period, such as the weakened Drul alliance, orc migration, and the deaths of twenty of twenty-seven Justiciars
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/cadeia_da_fendris\|Chain of Fendris]]: theft of the Luminar dagger from Dessian Broel’s private museum, which the official investigation never solved
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/sabotagem_nas_forjas_de_thandruk_varn\|Sabotage in the Forges]]: series of deliberate acts that reduces production at the forges of Thandruk Varn; nature and perpetrator unknown
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/desaparecimento_de_draven-thol\|Disappearance of Draven-Thol]]: disappearance of the Justiciar assigned to Thandruk Varn, without a body, witnesses, or clues
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/uncao_de_milorsevogh\|Anointing of Milorsevogh]]: ceremony in the Temple of Azkaroth in which the seventh living Justiciar received Spina Lexis, Codex, and Oculus
- [[_wiki_en/eventos/nexus_silenciados_na_fortaleza_de_cristal\|Silenced Nexuses]]: arcane anomaly in which Elenir’s crystal nexuses, still active, were silenced from the inside out by deliberate interference
