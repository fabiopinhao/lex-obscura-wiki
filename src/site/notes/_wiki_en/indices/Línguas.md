# Languages

The Empire imposes an official language. The others survive through stubbornness or military usefulness.

---

## Languages of the Races

- [[_wiki_en/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]]: lingua franca of the continent, official language of the Empire
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/luminar\|Luminar]]: civil-magical language of the Luminars, ancestor of Aelarian and Sylvaen (extinct)
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/titanico\|Titan-tongue]]: primordial language of the titans, ancestor of Sylvaen and Gerut Dialects (extinct)
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]]: primordial language of dragons, everyday language of Saurians, ritual language of Vinculum Daemonis
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/gholvyss\|Gholvyss]]: primordial language of the Druls, first diabolic language of Pactum Diaboli
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/talassico\|Thalassic]]: primordial language of undines, communication with gods of the seas
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/tenebraico\|Tenebraic]]: ritual language of the Shadars for diabolic pacts and curses
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]]: everyday and ritual language of orcs
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/sylvaen\|Sylvaen]]: everyday language of the Sylvans, ritual language of Symbiosis Naturae
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/aelarian\|Aelarian]]: everyday language of the Elarins, ritual language of Devotio Sancta
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/dialetos_gerut\|Gerut Dialects]]: languages of the Geruts, titanic roots with a druidic register
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/pelagico\|Pelagic]]: ritual language of the Frenims, communication with ethereal sidereals
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/neranthiano\|Neranthian]]: lingua franca of Neranthis, spoken by Frenims, undines, and Ranidans
- [[_wiki_en/linguas/dialetos_tribais\|Tribal Dialects]]: intraspecies communication among hybrid races
