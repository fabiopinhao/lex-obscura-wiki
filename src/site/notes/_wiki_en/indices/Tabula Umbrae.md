# Tabula Umbrae

*The Tablet of Shadows.*

---

The third volume of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]]. If the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]] is the sovereign’s voice and the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] the body of the law, the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/tabula_umbrae_codex\|Tabula Umbrae]] is the hidden arsenal, the operational arm that turns word and law into material consequence.

A repository of spells, magical formulas, and alchemical recipes. It contains execution rituals, truth serums, sigils for summoning spectral witnesses, and a subsection dedicated to proscribed deities. In the field, it is a reference tool: a [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] opens the Tabula to identify what they face and learn how to destroy it.

Access is restricted to Justiciars and the High Clergy, the only ones who possess complete copies of the Codex.
