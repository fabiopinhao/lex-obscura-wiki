# Cosmology

The structure of the planes, the divine hierarchy, and the forces that sustain or corrode reality.

---

- [[_wiki/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]]: the space between that connects the material plane to the divine domains
- [[_wiki/cosmologia/interacoes_entre_planos\|Interactions Between Planes]]: how the planes touch, bleed, and contaminate
- [[_wiki/cosmologia/planos_divinos\|Divine Planes]]: the individual domains of each entity, shaped by its nature
- [[_wiki/cosmologia/manto_das_sombras\|Mantle of Shadows]]: the metaphysical phenomenon that altered Reinkard’s planar balance

## Cosmogony and Theology

- [[_wiki/cosmologia/cosmogonia__tradicoes_orais_sobre_a_origem\|Cosmogony: Oral Traditions]]: contradictory accounts of the origin of Thamakard, none confirmed
- [[_wiki/cosmologia/incendio_primordial\|Primordial Conflagration]]: the red tradition, according to which the universe was born of excess, of combustion without limit
- [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|The Void]]: the dark tradition, according to which a conscious blackness preceded all existence
- [[_wiki/cosmologia/doutrina_da_alma\|Doctrine of the Soul]]: all magic is born of the soul (*Naiu’Wula*)
- [[_wiki/cosmologia/teoria_materialista\|The Materialist Theory]]: magi as a measurable alchemical force (Jospin Valheorn)
- [[_wiki/cosmologia/maleficarios\|Maleficaries]]: the demonological scriptures of the ancient empire

## Arcane Foundations

- [[_wiki/cosmologia/magi\|Magi]]: the arcane energy that permeates Thamakard, the substrate of every invocation and divine bond
- [[_wiki/cosmologia/canal_magi\|Magi Channel]]: the link between extradimensional entities and mortals, crossing the ethereal plane

## The Five Orders

- [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|Devils]]: lords of contracts and souls. Their order does not serve justice: it serves dominion. To a devil, everything that exists, including another devil, is a bargaining chip
- [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|Demons]]: fire made flesh. Where a devil calculates, a demon sets fires. They do not want dominion: they want it to burn
- [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|Titans]]: the gods who inhabit Thamakard. Older than empires, older than writing. Their cults speak of fecund rot and roots that devour stone
- [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|Angels]]: guardians of a moral order that Reinkard has almost forgotten. Light and judgment, mercy and rigor. The few who still revere them pay the price of isolation
- [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|Sidereals]]: formless consciousnesses, patterns of pure thought that exist beyond matter. They do not ask for devotion; they demand understanding

## Deities

### Devils

- [[_wiki/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]], Thorn Crown *(Crown of Thorns)*: obsidian-studded vampire; his curses embed themselves in the target and do not come loose

- [[_wiki/panteao/azkaroth\|Azkaroth]], the Archdevil *(Archdevil)*: black raven over Morvgrad, the site of anointing ceremonies where each Justiciar symbolically dies and is reborn in blood

- [[_wiki/panteao/xanthrak\|Xanthrak]], Artisan of Pain *(The Artisan of Pain)*: ossiarch whose curses keep the victim conscious while the body rots, rebuilds itself, and rots again

- [[_wiki/panteao/naelkur\|Naelkur]], the Fecund Grave *(Fertile Grave)*: rooted revenant who treats each corpse as a seed; his decomposition is agriculture, every field of death is cropland

- [[_wiki/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], Captive Lightning *(Captive Lightning)*: tempestuous gargoyle whose power is born of Thal’Serin rather than the celestial domains

- [[_wiki/panteao/ulravaeth\|Ulravaeth]], the Custodian of the Between *(Vigil of the Between)*: twin shadow who inhabits thresholds between life and death; his illusions work by subtraction, erasing points of reference instead of creating mirages

- [[_wiki/panteao/gul_vakresh\|Gul’Vakresh]], Caustic Order *(Caustic Order)*: scapegoat drenched in bile; his shackles force the bearer to stand still while the acid of the law dissolves them

- [[_wiki/panteao/quelvorax\|Quelvorax]], Abyssal Terror *(The Abyssal Terror)*: colossal worm that consumes space; wherever he passes, geometry breaks and the distance between two points loses meaning

- [[_wiki/panteao/velmorain\|Velmorain]], Cosmic Veil *(Cosmic Veil)*: mitered mummy of a dual nature between devil and sidereal; his veils separate what must never touch again

- [[_wiki/panteao/abbhal\|Abbhal]], Profaned Grace *(Profaned Grace)*: fallen angel of a dual nature between devil and angel; clemency has reversed without being extinguished, and what was mercy has become automatic condemnation

- [[_wiki/panteao/shaevan\|Shaevan]], Spinner of Plague *(The Spinner of Plague)*: pestilent tarantula of a dual nature between devil and titan; she weaves diseases like deliberate webs, turning victims into vectors of contagion

- [[_wiki/panteao/warguleth\|Wargûleth]], Guardian of the Pit *(Keeper of the Pit)*: cerberus of a dual nature between devil and demon; he isolates through fear and curses the bonds that remain, guardian of absolute confinement

### Demons

- [[_wiki/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]], Winged Scourge *(Winged Scourge)*: funereal manticore; he demands continuous destruction, purges, and terror as a method of government

- [[_wiki/panteao/tzarakul\|Tzarakul]], Archdemon *(Archdemon)*: calcining autarch: sovereign humanoid figure in perpetual combustion whose power dissolves arcane pacts at the root; the wound Rupto opens, Anatho infects, and any attempt to restore the bond is born corrupted

- [[_wiki/panteao/bal_hadut\|Bal’Hadut]], Incandescent Throne *(The Incandescent Throne)*: volcanic dragon whose fire does not illuminate: it reduces to ash, and the throne upon which he sits is the ashes of those who approached

- [[_wiki/panteao/thurgash\|Thûrgash]], the Devourer of Bastions *(The Devourer of Bastions)*: siege giant who exists for eternal war; where other demons set fires, Thûrgash crushes until no structure remains standing

- [[_wiki/panteao/korbarakh\|Korbarakh]], Untouched Crucible *(Untouched Crucible)*: votive salamander whose fire purifies without consuming the salamander itself; the crucible that melts everything and remains intact

- [[_wiki/panteao/fevrazuni\|Fevrazûni]], Rending Delirium *(Rending Delirium)*: lunatic succubus who does not seduce: she tears perception apart until madness seems like relief

- [[_wiki/panteao/arikhandis\|Arikhandis]], Prophetic Gale *(Prophetic Gale)*: temporal winged serpent who crosses wind and time; his prophecies do not reveal the future, they drag it into the present

- [[_wiki/panteao/ixragor\|Ixragor]], Abominable Womb *(The Abominable Womb)*: gangrenous chimera who spawns impossible forms; each offspring is anatomy rewritten without regard for the original

- [[_wiki/panteao/hazburim\|Hazburim]], Lord of the Flies *(Lord of the Flies)*: larval insectoid of a dual nature between demon and titan; proscribed by the Cult, he reaches the world through consecrated altars that function as a window between planes

- [[_wiki/panteao/solvara\|Solvara]], Immortal Ash *(Ash Undying)*: immolated phoenix of a dual nature between demon and angel; she burns and is reborn, but each rebirth preserves the previous burn

### Titans

- [[_wiki/panteao/orendhal\|Orendhal]], Sealed Heartwood *(The Sealed Heartwood)*: ancient treant whose roots sustain and imprison at the same time; the protection he offers is a living cage

- [[_wiki/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]], Bone of the World *(Bone of the World)*: stone golem, guardian of Thal’Serin; each avatar manifestation requires a sacrifice of his own domain

- [[_wiki/panteao/sulkavrim\|Sulkavrim]], Enduring Wall *(The Unyielding Bulwark)*: marble serpent who petrifies himself to protect; absolute defense at the cost of total immobility

- [[_wiki/panteao/elethvaen\|Elethvaen]], Shepherd of Dreams *(Shepherd of Dreams)*: crepuscular stag who shepherds cycles of dormancy on the scale of a biome; what sleeps under his vigil grows, what he awakens did not choose to wake

- [[_wiki/panteao/durathain\|Durathain]], Filthy Hand *(The Grimy Hand)*: putrid behemoth whose name echoes in the curses of the common people; patron of manual labor and the filth that sustains it

- [[_wiki/panteao/vraskilien\|Vraskilien]], Shifting Viscera *(Shifting Viscera)*: bloody basilisk who rewrites the flesh of others as one reshapes clay; the transformation is always painful and never reversible

- [[_wiki/panteao/ghol_urarn\|Ghol-Urarn]], Diluvian Contagion *(The Diluvian Contagion)*: suppurating hydra whose infected waters rise instead of falling; the flood he brings does not drown, it contaminates

- [[_wiki/panteao/brondakhar\|Brondakhar]], Wrathful Aegis *(Wrathful Aegis)*: armored minotaur who protects by destroying; his defense is a perpetual counterattack

- [[_wiki/panteao/titania\|Titânia]], Mother of Roots *(Mother of Roots)*: inviolate dryad of a dual nature between titan and angel; patron of the Sylvans and guardian of [[_wiki/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]]

### Angels

- [[_wiki/panteao/azazel\|Azazel]], the Archangel *(Archangel)*: piercing seraph, the last white god with an active presence in Reinkard; his luminous faith has almost died out beneath the mantle of shadows

- [[_wiki/panteao/paralda\|Paralda]], Lady of the Shroud *(Lady of the Shroud)*: Veiled Mourner with hands that restore through touch and a shroud that consecrates the passage

- [[_wiki/panteao/yelvanna\|Yelvanna]], Consecrated Requiem *(The Hallowed Requiem)*: misty siren whose funeral song protects and judges; those who are worthy hear melody, those who are unworthy hear silence

- [[_wiki/panteao/corvanthos\|Corvanthos]], Judgment of the Zenith *(Zenith Judgement)*: sepulchral lion who judges from the highest point; the sentence descends like vertical light, with no shadow in which to hide

- [[_wiki/panteao/zephrak\|Zephrak]], Merciless Breath *(Merciless Gust)*: feral griffin whose sacred wind does not cool: it tears out, exposes, and carries away what has no root

- [[_wiki/panteao/israfil\|Israfil]], Herald of the Dawn *(Herald of Dawn)*: oliphaunt with incandescent tusks whose restoration cauterizes and sterilizes through light

- [[_wiki/panteao/ophemus\|Ophemus]], Devastating Radiance *(Devastating Radiance)*: coruscating cyclops whose single eye concentrates all available light into a single point of destruction

- [[_wiki/panteao/borjean\|Borjean]], Ascending Oath *(The Skyward Oath)*: knight upon an alicorn whose oath literally elevates whoever pronounces it; the fall, for those who break it, is proportional to the ascent

- [[_wiki/panteao/eiryndh\|Eiryndh]], Boreal Shield *(The Boreal Shield)*: ordeal-bound valkyrie of a dual nature between angel and sidereal; her protection comes from celestial cold, and the shield she raises burns like ice

### Sidereals

- [[_wiki/panteao/ruhavel\|Ruhavel]], Mirage of Return *(Returning Mirage)*: fateful djinn of a dual nature between sidereal and demon; he manipulates time and perception until the traveler confuses the way back with the way out

- [[_wiki/panteao/geldrion\|Geldrion]], Glacial Apex *(The Glacial Apex)*: stellar colossus whose cold does not preserve: it crystallizes, and what crystallizes does not flow again

- [[_wiki/panteao/zuvaneth\|Zuvaneth]], Celestial Enigma *(The Celestial Enigma)*: oracular sphinx who does not answer questions; he reformulates them until the question contains the answer and the cost of understanding it

- [[_wiki/panteao/nocthydral\|Nocthydral]], Unfathomable Pupil *(The Fathomless Gaze)*: caliginous kraken whose gaze crosses planes; he does not observe the cosmos, he digests it

- [[_wiki/panteao/jervaust\|Jervaust]], Hungry Solstice *(Starving Solstice)*: famished scylla who devours frozen time; the longer the stagnation, the more plentiful the feast

- [[_wiki/panteao/faelyndre\|Faelyndrë]], Coral Cipher *(Coral Cipher)*: coral nereid who encodes secrets in the structure of reefs; the knowledge she guards grows slowly and cuts whoever tries to tear it out

- [[_wiki/panteao/aethravos\|Aethravos]], Vitreous Breath *(The Vitreous Breath)*: pearlescent wyvern whose breath vitrifies the air into translucent blades; the sky he touches becomes fragile

- [[_wiki/panteao/dremharak\|Dremharak]], Lucid Fever *(Lucid Fever)*: feverish wendigo who puts the body to sleep through hyperthermia and opens the mind to unfiltered perception

- [[_wiki/panteao/lyldraeth\|Lyldraeth]], Howling Prayer *(The Howling Prayer)*: sacrilegious banshee whose lament is simultaneously prayer and profanation; those who hear her do not know whether they have been blessed or cursed

- [[_wiki/panteao/myralune\|Myralune]], Dormant Tide *(The Dormant Tide)*: slumbering leviathan of a dual nature between sidereal and titan; her sleep governs tides and currents that obey the rhythm of a dream no one dares interrupt
