# Minotaur

Minotaurs are bovine hybrids of monumental size, a race now nearly extinct and considered legend or myth by most peoples of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. Of the few remaining, the best known is [[_wiki_en/personagens/odran\|Odran]], one of the last of his species.

### Physiology

Large bovine hybrids, minotaurs have curved horns and dense musculature beneath dark brown fur, with articulated hands capable of fine manipulation despite their size. Odran is described as having horns that nearly scrape the doorframes. Their lifespan of sixty to eighty years is the longest among hybrids. They have no defined divine bond, which suggests a disconnection from conventional magical systems or the loss of this classification with their near-extinction.

### Culture and History

The race is nearly extinct and survives chiefly as folklore. Nothing about the original minotaur society has survived to the present: whatever they were before their near-extinction was lost along with most of their members, and the circumstances of their decline have been lost. For many peoples, minotaurs are merely stories told to frighten their young.

### Odran, the Last

Odran is the best-known minotaur. A servant of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], he serves as librarian and jailer to the fortress’s involuntary guests, with steel chains on his collar and surprisingly gentle eyes for a creature of his size. Decades of experience have made him a civilized administrator and a voracious intellectual who reads Varnakian chronicles, campaign accounts, and merchants’ journals. He has already tried to break [[_wiki_en/artefatos/grilhoes_de_contencao_arcana\|shackles of arcane containment]] out of empirical curiosity. He treats the prisoners under his guard to generous meals and respectful conversation, yet does not hesitate to apply the chains of containment. He philosophizes about the nature of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]], defending the inflexibility of the master’s word as the foundation of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]], and reads [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tratado_de_wijmarv\|Wijmarv’s treatise]] on [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], which reveals that the dagger was forged against dragons. His existential solitude permeates every interaction: too civilized to be a beast, too servile to be free, too much the last to be a people.
