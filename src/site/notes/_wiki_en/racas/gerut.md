# Gerut

The Geruts are titanic humans of the woodlands and swamps of the continent’s southeast, hunters and druids who, in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|Forgotten Age]], embraced the green instead of the throne. As primitive humans expanded from tribes into kingdoms and pressed against elven borders, the Geruts refused the march and remained in the forests, while the majority formed the Central [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]], the future [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]. The [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]], recognizing their peaceful stance, permitted them to inhabit the edges of their territories, and over time the Geruts ventured into the woodlands and never left.

### Origin

The primordial choice defines everything: they are humans who bound themselves to the earth. During the [[_wiki_en/eventos/pax_alabastrina\|Pax Alabastrina]], the border tribes maintained trade with the kingdoms, exchanging hides and forest knowledge for tools and salt, in pragmatic coexistence without submission. Other tribes, in remote, dense forests, lived as isolated as the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]] themselves. The distinction proved decisive in the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Reinkardic Wars]], when the Geruts were the race least affected by the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] conquest. The reason is geographic: where there was a Gerut community, there was a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titan]], and where there was an awakened titan, the Cult’s fire met resistance that made conquest unfeasible. They survived because of the terrain they chose.

### Physiology

The Geruts bind themselves to the titans. Their skin has earthy tones, from dark olive to clay brown, marked by greenish veins that grow denser with age in a pattern resembling wood grain. Their build is robust, shaped by generations of life in forests and swamps: broad, calloused hands, fingers that seem made for digging, a keen sense of smell for fungi and decaying vegetation. Many have hair interwoven with dry moss and bark fibers, an accidental symbiosis, never ornament.

Their lifespan ranges from ninety-six to one hundred years. Aging follows a human pattern until sixty, when the skin thickens and darkens like sun-cured leather; in the final years, the joints stiffen and the Gerut loses mobility from the inside out. In individuals who exceed the natural cycle through prolonged symbiosis with the titans, a terminal transformation of another order is reported: bones turn into wood, muscle hardens into woody fiber, and the individual merges with the soil where they took root. [[_wiki_en/personagens/peredur\|Peredur]] describes the process as irreversible; its full extent and cost remain unknown.

### Social Structure

The basic unit is the circle, between ten and forty individuals who share a sacred clearing and answer to an elder. There is no formal hierarchy above this. When circles need to coordinate, the elders gather in open council, without a fixed title, and authority is situational: whoever knows the terrain leads the hunt, and the elder leads the ritual. The Geruts acknowledge responsibility for the land, never ownership; each circle tends a stretch of forest and answers to the titans for it. A stretch that rots brings shame upon the circle, and no one claims credit for prosperity.

The Pact of the Two Moons formalized the division of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]]: Geruts to the east, where the swamp meets the land, and Sylvans to the west. The two peoples share devotion to the green, but differ in method. The Sylvans serve [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]] as extensions of the goddess; the Geruts shape the earth and merge with it.

### Culture and Customs

Names consist of two roots, the first from the mother, the second from the forest where the Gerut was born, and there are no surnames: a Gerut introduces themself by forest before name. The ritual greeting establishes a connection with the soil’s mineral consciousness, with clay smoothed by the fingertips between the two individuals; the grooves left behind serve as counted prayers, a tactile diary, and an anchor of presence. Whoever does not touch the ground when greeting declares themself a foreigner or an enemy.

Death is returned to the circle. The body is buried standing, with hands open, so that roots find it sooner. Weeping is private and brief, and prolonged mourning is considered selfishness, since claiming the dead is stealing what has been returned to the earth. The economy is based on subsistence and barter: they fish and grow wild rice in flooded clearings, mint no currency, and accumulation is culturally repulsive. The central taboo forbids cutting living wood without the elder’s permission; dead wood may be used freely, but felling a healthy tree is equivalent to murder, and the person responsible must plant three in its place and tend them until they exceed their own height.

### Magic

The Gerut access system is [[_wiki_en/magia/symbiosis_naturae\|Symbiosis Naturae]], with its own philosophical foundation. Where the Sylvans bind themselves to plant life, the Geruts bind themselves to minerals, clay, rock, and sediment, believing that stone and earth possess a memory that predates roots. Gerut symbiosis is deeper and older because it does not pass through an intermediary deity: it connects directly to the titans and the forces of the soil. The ritual of Harmony does not involve elaborate offerings; the aspirant buries their hands in the earth and waits, and if the forest accepts them, fine roots grow between their fingers. The resulting magic is territorial and slow: the Geruts sing to the earth and the earth responds, hours or days later, in slow healing and passive defenses, unspectacular and devastatingly effective in dense forest. The racial cost takes its toll on identity: the longer a Gerut remains bound to a stretch of forest, the more their body absorbs the terrain, until the terminal transformation.

A tradition parallel to Symbiosis circulates among the Geruts, the doctrine of sacred [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]]. The master [[_wiki_en/personagens/naiu_wula\|Naiu’Wula]], with neither an academic chair nor a surname, taught that all magic originates in the awakened soul and that [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] is merely the soul’s trace in the world. In opposition to the materialist views of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], he preached that the source of power resides in the caster’s inner being, and his teachings survive only in secret circles.

### Relationship with the Empire

The Shadars classify the Geruts as [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriars]] of an inferior grade, and the pejorative nickname “mudfoot” originated in the Reinkardic Wars, when refugees arrived at the camps with their feet covered in swamp mud. To Morvgrad’s bureaucratic apparatus, the Geruts barely exist as a category: censuses ignore them and military reports mention them only when one is captured. This invisibility is deliberate, since the titanic forests are the only ones the Empire has not directly invaded. The cost of confronting an awakened titan exceeds the territorial gain, and the imperial strategy is one of slow encirclement, allowing the [[_wiki_en/magia/macula-sombria\|Shadow-Taint]] to eat away at the roots before the soldiers arrive.

Geruts captured outside the forests are treated as curiosities or repositories of ancient knowledge. At the time of [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]]’s interrogations, [[_wiki_en/personagens/jalu_nvara\|Jalú’Nvara]], of the last generation to live free before the subjugation of the Geruts of Myrgarde, was a prisoner in the dungeons of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], and what he knew about the old Empire was worth more than his death.

### Current Situation

The central crisis is demographic. Each forest burned in the Reinkardic Wars took an entire circle with it, and the survivors migrated to the titanic forests, concentrating the population in a few sanctuaries, of which Thal’Serin is the largest. The Shadow-Taint worsens the contraction: roots rot, clearings lose vitality, and symbiosis weakens. The awakening of the titan [[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]] transformed leagues of forest into dead territory. The youngest Geruts, born in forests already compromised, report difficulty establishing a bond with the earth, and there is tension between those who advocate staying, because the earth needs them, and the few who argue that survival requires seeking forests still intact.
