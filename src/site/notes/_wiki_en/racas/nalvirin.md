# Nalvirin

Nalvirins are [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereal]] gnomes, diminutive arcane beings confined to [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]]. They have the greatest magical aptitude among the gnomish races and live in [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]] citadels as an auxiliary population, virtually unknown on the continent of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]].

### Origin

Nalvirins are gnomes who migrated to Neranthis in one of the earlier eras, probably following trade routes or fleeing conflicts on the central continent; the exact circumstances have been lost. What is known is the result: they found the Frenims, and the Frenims found a use for them. Integration followed the same pattern that the [[_wiki_en/racas/hallirin\|Hallirins]] replicated with humans in Reinkard, a coexistence based on mutual usefulness. Where Hallirins practiced alchemy and crafts for humans, Nalvirins provide magical assistance and technical work for Frenims.

### Physiology

Of standard gnomish build, standing between one point one and one point three meters tall, Nalvirins developed subtle adaptations through generational exposure to the ethereal environment of Neranthis: pale skin with a slightly silvery sheen, light eyes, and fine hair tending toward white or silver-gray. These traits are not deformities like those of the [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derros]], shaped by extreme environmental pressure, but subtle variations. A Nalvirin in Reinkard would look like a pale Hallirin, although no one in Reinkard has ever seen one to notice the difference. Years of meditative effort leave a physical mark in the form of callused knees.

### Relationship with Magic

Nalvirins access magic through [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]]: mental discipline, expansion of consciousness, and attunement to entities of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]]. Their aptitude is the greatest among the gnomish races, but still places them below Frenims and [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]]; the difference is one of degree, not kind, as they follow the same meditative paths and simply do not reach as far. Their defining trait is the tension between their curious, restless gnomish nature and the demands of Meditatio Aetheris, which calls for decades of mental stillness. Nalvirins are not naturally contemplative; they are gnomes who learned to contemplate, and the effort is visible: where a Frenim meditates as naturally as breathing, a Nalvirin meditates with the concentration of someone holding their breath. Most work as ritual assistants, attunement instrument technicians, or catalogers of ethereal phenomena. Training follows the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/tradicao_de_sirana\|Tradition of Sirana]], a pedagogical discipline that teaches how to transform the restless mind into a finely tuned instrument, according to the teaching attributed to Master Sirana.

### Isolation

Nalvirins have never set foot outside Neranthis, and the reason is not prohibition but irrelevance: Neranthis is the only place where their aptitude has value. In Reinkard, they would be Hallirins with a strange accent; in [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], no one would know what to do with a gnome who meditates. Specialization has bound them to the only ecosystem that uses them. In Reinkard, they are mentioned in scholarly treatises with the same frequency and the same interest as [[_wiki_en/racas/boglin\|Boglins]]: they exist, technically, and what little is known comes from Frenim accounts filtered through distance and indifference.
