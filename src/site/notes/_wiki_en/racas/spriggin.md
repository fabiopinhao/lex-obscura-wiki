# Spriggin

Spriggins are woodland gnomes, guardians of the lower forest layers and aligned with the titans. Inhabitants of the world close to the ground, roots, fungi, hidden trails, and burrows among stones, they lived in harmony with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] through [[_wiki_en/magia/symbiosis_naturae\|Symbiosis Naturae]]. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]], they are a race on the brink of extinction.

### Origin

Spriggins descend from gnomes who settled in forests during earlier eras and came into harmony with the green gods. The relationship was one of natural affinity, not imposition: they gravitated toward green because they lived in it, and green, in return, welcomed them. Before the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Reinkardic Wars]], they maintained a functional partnership with [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] communities, sharing the same ecosystem. Spriggins tended the lower layers, while Sylvans operated on the scale of the canopies and rivers, in an organic complementarity without a formal pact. The forests they occupied, however, were ordinary forests, dense and ancient, but without the direct protection of the titans that safeguarded [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]].

### Physiology

Of standard gnomish stature, between one meter ten centimeters and one meter thirty centimeters, Spriggins have skin in earthy tones, from ocher to greenish brown, with seasonal variation in individuals exposed to environments with dense concentrations of vegetation. Their thick hair is often interwoven with plant debris that serves as camouflage, and their dark eyes, with wide pupils, are made for vision in the undergrowth. Broad, calloused feet adapt them to uneven terrain. They are not strong in absolute terms, but hardy: dense bones, compact musculature, and a resilience to cold, damp, and food deprivation that other gnomish subraces do not possess. Their hundred-year lifespan is rarely reached, as predators, deforestation, and dispersal reduce their actual life expectancy.

### Culture and Customs

Spriggin culture was inseparable from its habitat. With no writing system of their own, their tradition was oral and gestural: root songs, deep vocalizations that imitated the sound of wood under pressure, marks on living bark, carved symbols that healed with the tree and became part of it, and patrol routes passed down through repetition across generations. There was no concept of property, only territory, and territory was what one tended. The partnership with the Sylvans produced cultural borrowings in both directions: Spriggins adopted elements of the [[_wiki_en/linguas/sylvaen\|Sylvaen]] language to name plant species and lunar cycles, and Sylvans incorporated Spriggin techniques for soil management and fungus identification.

### Relationship with Magic

Spriggin magic works through Symbiosis Naturae, a symbiotic relationship with nature spirits whose power is geographically limited and tied to the territory’s natural network. Their magical alignment is lower than that of Sylvans and [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]], as in every gnomish race. Most operated with heightened environmental perception, sensing the health of a forest, detecting intrusion, locating groundwater, and some individuals mastered druidic magic, including the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] of shapeshifting, although this was never a universal racial trait.

### Extinction

The Reinkardic Wars and the subsequent [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] expansion systematically destroyed the Spriggin habitat. Forests were burned to open marching routes, deny cover to resistance movements, and fuel the forges of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Protected forests, such as Thal’Serin, survived; the others did not. Creatures of the undergrowth in open terrain are prey, and Spriggins had nowhere to go. Those who survived dispersed among the forest fragments that remain in [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] and possibly in [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], in places no one knows and no one searches for. The partnership with the Sylvans dissolved along with the forests that sustained it. In the Third Era, Spriggins are an entry in treatises that few read, and their extinction is slow, silent, and probably irreversible.
