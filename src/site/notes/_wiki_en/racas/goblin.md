# Goblin

Goblins are demonic corruptions of the gnomish lineage, the product of the same forces that reshaped humanoids during the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_raciais\|Racial Wars]]. They sustain the logistics of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] as fortress stewards: while the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] fight, goblins build, maintain, and improvise.

### Origin

Each pantheon produced its own gnomish subrace: the [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derros]] of the devils, the goblins of the demons, the [[_wiki_en/racas/spriggin\|Spriggins]] of the titans, the [[_wiki_en/racas/hallirin\|Hallirins]] of the angels, the [[_wiki_en/racas/nalvirin\|Nalvirins]] of the sidereals. Goblins emerged from generational exposure to demonic emanations in the volcanic regions of the mountain ranges, where the same fissures that transformed the orcs corrupted the neighboring gnomish populations. Where demonic fire rewrote elves into war machines, it rewrote gnomes into something else: smaller, faster, more numerous, marked by a compulsion to accumulate and engage in frantic activity that replaced their original gnomish curiosity.

Before the founding of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], goblins existed as scattered populations in the mountains and along the edges of orc territories. [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]] included them in the domestication of goblinoids that accompanied the creation of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]], recognizing in them a usefulness that orcs did not possess. During the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Reinkardic Wars]], when the attempt to use [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]] as stewards failed, goblins filled the gap, and ever since, the symbiosis between orcs and goblins has defined imperial logistics.

### Physical Identity

A compact gnomish build, with a typical height between one point one and one point three meters. Their skin ranges from olive green and moss green to grayish ocher, with a rough, oily texture. Their eyes are disproportionately large, yellowish or orange, with pupils that contract into slits under intense light. Their ears, pointed and exaggeratedly mobile, function as threat radar. Their fingers are long and nimble, ending in hard nails that serve as tools and a last line of defense, and their teeth are small and numerous, without the prominent canines of orcs.

Their metabolism is frantic: they eat little at a time, but with constant frequency, and when deprived of food for more than half a day, they become erratic. Their sense of smell is keen, especially for volatile substances and underground gases, which makes them useful in mines and tunnels, and their night vision is an inheritance from the gnomish lineage adapted to life underground. Their lifespan is around sixty years, longer than that of orcs and shorter than that of surface gnomes, but actual mortality is determined less by biology and more by the brutality of imperial service.

### Social Structure

In [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], goblins are almost completely subordinate to the imperial structure, without any significant internal organization. There are no clans, guilds, or hierarchies of their own: the basic social unit is the work group assigned by [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] officers or orc captains, and each goblin’s position is determined by the function it performs. Cooks answer to the tower’s steward, tunnel engineers to the fortress commander, trap-setters to whoever is closest to shouting an order. In [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], wild goblin populations still exist in significant numbers, organized into opportunistic bands with fluid leadership that occupy ruins, caves, and marginal territories.

### Culture and Customs

Goblin culture in Reinkard has been absorbed by the imperial machine. Domesticated goblins have no recognizable traditions of their own: their rhythms are those of the fortress, their calendars are supply calendars, their rites are maintenance procedures. What remains is behavioral, not ritualistic. Goblins accumulate food scraps, broken tools, pieces of metal, copper wires, lost screws. The compulsion is not avarice, as among the Derros, but anxiety made material: they stockpile because the next period of scarcity is always imminent. The death of a goblin interrupts nothing; the body is removed, the role is filled, and the dead goblin’s possessions are redistributed among its coworkers before the body grows cold.

### Role in the Empire

Goblins occupy the space that Shadars refuse and that orcs are incapable of filling. In fortresses, they maintain escape tunnels and traps, and their chaotic but effective engineering consists of constant improvisation and temporary solutions that become permanent. In the towers of Morvgrad and other cities, they operate kitchens, storerooms, and the lower floors that no one else wants to inhabit. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] sees them as necessary parasites and periodically debates their elimination, always running up against the same conclusion: no one else wants to do what they do. Goblin domestication differs from that of orcs: instead of breaking down tribal hierarchy, it operates through conditioning with food rewards and the assignment of roles.

### Relationship with Magic

As a gnomish subrace linked to demons, goblins have access to [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]] on the same terms as orcs, but bonded goblins are rare in fortresses: the cost of the bond is disproportionate to the gain in creatures of such small stature, and the Empire prefers functional goblins to powerful and unstable goblins. Where they excel is in improvised alchemy and trap engineering: without arcane training, they work naturally with volatile materials, explosive mixtures, and trigger mechanisms. [[_wiki_en/panteao/thurgash\|Thûrgash]] is the demon most associated with goblinoids in general, but the goblins’ relationship with him is less devotional and more transactional.

### Voice

Goblins speak quickly, in short, truncated sentences, swallowing syllables when hurried. Their vocabulary is utilitarian, concrete, devoid of abstraction: where an orc speaks of honor, a goblin speaks of food; where a Shadar speaks of order, a goblin speaks of repairs. Complaining is the default mode of communication, and the intensity of the complaint tends to be inversely proportional to the actual severity of the problem. [[_wiki_en/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]] is understood by goblins who serve in orc fortresses, but goblin pronunciation is higher-pitched and faster.

### Internal Conflicts

The central conflict of goblins is not dramatic, it is existential through erosion. Generations of domestication have erased any collective identity that may have existed, and the imperial goblin does not know what it is beyond its function. When that function disappears, when a fortress is abandoned or a kitchen closes, the goblin assigned to that post neither rebels nor migrates: it freezes. The compulsion to accumulate is the only vestige of something predating the Empire, and even that has been channeled toward practical ends.

In Veinkard, wild goblins retain a vitality that imperial goblins have lost: aggression, territory, competition for resources. The distance between the two groups grows with each generation, and a wild goblin encountering an imperial one would probably not recognize it as an equal.

Beneath all this lies disposability, which is the most concrete tension of all: goblins survive in the Empire through the inertia of their own invisible indispensability.
