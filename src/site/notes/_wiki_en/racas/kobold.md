# Kobold

Kobolds are a gnomish subtype of [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]], exclusive to the plateau of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] and absent from [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]]. They are the only race in the region whose existence results from deliberate engineering: they were manufactured over centuries by the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] to serve, and they serve with a devotion that seems like instinct but is a scar.

### Origin

With the Shadar rise on the plateau, the massive presence of dark energy corrupted the slopes of the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]]. The dragons that inhabited those slopes wasted away and abandoned their nests, and the Shadars collected the eggs and tried to corrupt them in the shadows, hoping to domesticate dragons. The creatures born under diabolical influence were not dragons: diminutive draconic beings, docile, incapable of breath weapons or flight. Over generations, the Shadars shaped them through selection: they crossed bloodlines, bred the docile, eliminated the aggressive and those that tried to escape, and fed those that obeyed without hesitation. The process lasted centuries until it produced a stable form, small, agile, obedient, and incapable of conceiving of existence without a master. They were called kobolds and enslaved, and they never chose the name.

### Physiology

Seventy centimeters to one meter in height, with fine scales in shades of rust, ocher, or greenish gray, lacking the protective thickness of those of [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]]. Large yellow eyes, with vertical pupils that dilate in darkness; a short, flattened snout. They have neither breath glands nor wings: the elemental capacity was eliminated during selection. The tail, long and thin, serves balance and gestural communication among kobolds, and is an extension of identity: a kobold without a tail loses motor coordination and suffers permanent disorientation. Their small hands have vestigial claws and dexterity beyond what their size would suggest, capable of manipulating mechanisms, sewing, and organizing efficiently.

### Psychology

Manufactured devotion is the defining trait. Kobolds develop an absolute bond with a master, and the bond operates with the intensity of a religious pact: [[_wiki_en/personagens/fing\|Fing]], a kobold in the service of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], prepares baths, fetches books, and anticipates requests with a dedication that observers mistake for love. It is not love, but the result of generations of selection that eliminated every alternative: kobolds that do not bond do not survive, because they receive no food, shelter, or purpose. The bond is a biological cage.

Kobold identity is inseparable from the master. “I” is defined by “whom I serve,” and a kobold without a master, to itself, is nothing. Hence the severity of the trauma caused by the phrase “you are a thing”: it confirms what the kobold silently fears, that devotion is not a relationship but a function, and that functions can be replaced. Kobolds that serve the same master compete for the closest position, within the only hierarchy they understand. Fear is a permanent state: they fear masters, other servants, shadows, noises, the future. It is not cowardice, but the end product of centuries of selection in which the fearless died; when fear is insufficient to protect it, the kobold freezes.

### Social Structure

Kobolds have no society of their own. The social unit is the master and their collection of servants, without clans, families, or autonomous groups, and most kobolds without a master waste away or wander until they find a new bond. The escapees that survive descend into caves and underground, where they live wild and scattered, without clan or organization, and some count them among the cave peoples, alongside dwarves and [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derros]]. In [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], kobolds serve as domestic servants, internal messengers, cleaners, and laboratory assistants; Shadar lords keep them as low-cost employees, and some collect them out of vanity. In the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Reinkardic Wars]], the attempt to use them as military stewards failed: the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] killed them out of an instinctive aversion to reptiles, and the [[_wiki_en/racas/goblin\|goblins]] took over the role.

### Culture

Kobolds do not produce culture in the conventional sense: they have no music, art, founding narrative, or ritual of their own. What they possess are habits of service transmitted through imitation among those who serve in the same house, cleaning techniques, and ways of becoming invisible when the master is irritated. They receive a name from the master, and the previous name ceases to exist with each change of master, because keeping it would imply an identity of their own. They accumulate tiny, worthless objects, stone chips, strands of fabric, fragments of wax, the residue of a draconic heritage distorted into a compulsion that has lost its original purpose.

### Status in the Empire

Kobolds are listed as movable property in the inventories of Shadar lords, alongside furniture and utensils. No law protects or mentions them, and killing someone else’s kobold is a crime against the owner’s property, not against the kobold. Demand is constant: they reproduce readily in controlled incubators, and mortality ensures turnover. Specialized breeders in Morvgrad select desirable traits, and the selection that created the race has never stopped.

### Relationship with Magic

Although of demonic lineage through corrupted draconic heritage, kobolds do not practice magic: the capacity was eliminated along with breath weapons and aggression during selection. What remains is residual sensitivity, the perception of arcane fluctuations and agitation in the presence of supernatural beings or powerful artifacts. Fing interprets the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/noctiluz\|noctiluz]] instinctively, a sign that the draconic heritage still pulses beneath the imposed docility.

### Life Cycle

Kobolds are oviparous, with clutches of four to eight eggs and an incubation period of thirty days in controlled incubators. Hatchlings walk within the first hours, say functional words at six months, and are adults at four years. Longevity is thirty to forty years, but actual life expectancy falls between twenty and twenty-five: they die from exhaustion, accidents, disposal, or violence by other races. There are no funerals, and the bodies are removed as waste.

### Voice

Kobolds speak little, quietly, and in the third person when referring to themselves, because “I” requires the autonomous identity that selection eliminated. Sentences are short and functional, vocabulary restricted to service. When frightened, their speech is reduced to isolated syllables and their tails coil around their bodies; when praised, they respond with stunned silence, incapable of processing approval. Where [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]] speak to exist and Draconids to declare, kobolds speak to confirm that they still serve. In the presence of a Draconid, a kobold confronts what it should have been, and the instinctive reaction is to shrink back.
