# Sylvan

The Sylvans are titanic elves of the forests, devoted to [[_wiki/panteao/titania\|Titânia]]. Fragments of an ancient elven people who turned to the primordial [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] and the cycles of nature, they made [[_wiki/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] the heart of their resistance and serve the green with a devotion that keeps them in the shade of the trees, far from palaces and empires.

### Physiology

With skin in earthy tones, from olive to greenish brown, and eyes in shades of green, amber, or gold, Sylvans often wear their hair interwoven with leaves and lichens or braided with plant fibers. They are more robust than the [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]] and adapted to life outdoors, with a lifespan of approximately one hundred twenty years.

### Origin

The Sylvans are fragments of the ancient [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]]. After the destruction of the light elves in the transition to the [[_wiki/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], they sought the great forests of the south and east and turned to the primordial titans. They refused integration with humans and the assimilation of Sancta, distrustful of the rapid progress and religious vanity of the new empires, and remained in the shade of the trees.

### Culture and Society

Thal’Serin is the sacred forest that shelters the Sylvan people, protected by an ancient pact with [[_wiki/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]]. The Pact of the Two Moons divides the forest between the Sylvans, to the west, and the [[_wiki/racas/gerut\|Geruts]], to the east, in an agreement of mutual respect. The two races serve the green in opposite ways: the Sylvans serve the leaves and become extensions of Titânia, while the Geruts shape the earth and become what they shape.

Sylvan social structure is based on rangers, druids, and elders. Elder druids are repositories of ancient wisdom, such as [[_wiki/personagens/peredur\|Peredur]], and among the rangers, [[_wiki/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]] stands out, regarded as the best in Thal’Serin. A Sylvan who masters movement through the treetops receives a distinct honorific, like Gaelwyn, called Wind-Between-Leaves. The next generation is already emerging in apprentices and scouts who will inherit the charge of guarding the forest. In the forest battles of the Reinkardic Wars, the Sylvans employed thornseeds, projectiles cultivated in the groves that germinate upon contact with the skin and within seconds grow into poisoned thorns, a decisive weapon against the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]].

### Tone and Voice

The Sylvan voice is melancholic, filled with constant references to nature, roots, wind, and decomposition, and disdainful of imperial “order”. Sylvans are sparing with words, but when they speak, they do so with the authority of those who serve something greater than empires.

### Current Crisis

The [[_wiki/magia/macula-sombria\|Shadow-Taint]] rots the roots and makes even the most basic enchantments difficult, and the visible black veins it leaves precede [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] attacks. Green magic is dying. According to the wisdom of the elders, the green gods, unlike the white gods that went extinct through purity, chose to survive through compromises, and that choice takes its toll on the people who depend on them. Fragmented into uncoordinated pockets of resistance and bound to a pact that protects it and at the same time confines it, Sylvan civilization faces the slow strangulation of everything that sustains it.
