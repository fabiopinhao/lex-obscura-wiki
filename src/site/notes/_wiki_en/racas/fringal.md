# Fringal

Fringals are a gnomish subtype of [[_wiki_en/racas/aviano\|Avians]], small humanoid birds with a white arcane mark. They arose from the same angelic interference during the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_raciais\|Racial Wars]] that produced the [[_wiki_en/racas/aquilar\|Aquilars]], but where the uplift produced raptors with great wingspans, it also produced diminutive passerines with wings that never sustained flight. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]], they live marginalized on the urban fringes of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], treated as alley fauna.

### Nature

Fringals’ origin is identical to that of Aquilars: the same genuine white mark, the same angelic ancestry, without artificial manipulation or degeneration. What differs is the result. The smaller phenotype is no defect, but the distinction between variation and defect depends on who has the strength to impose it, and Fringals have never had that strength. Aquilars treat them as incomplete Avians, the Empire treats them as waste, and [[_wiki_en/racas/goblin\|goblinoids]] treat them as food. Fringals regard themselves as survivors and ask no one’s opinion.

### Physiology

They stand sixty to ninety centimeters tall, with compact proportions: short, thick legs, a pronounced chest (a remnant of the musculature that sustains flight in Aquilars), and short, dense feathers in brown and gray tones that provide camouflage. Their large eyes retain the same acuity as those of Aquilars. Their wings are vestigial, incapable of flight or gliding, but far from useless: they cushion jumps, stabilize running and climbing, and serve as an instrument of gestural communication, much like the prehensile tails of [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]]. Their small hands possess dexterity disproportionate to their size. Their fast metabolism imposes chronic hunger, and in the darkness of alleys, their brown down makes a Fringal blend in with trash, stone, and shadow. Their theoretical lifespan ranges from forty to fifty years; on the urban margins, actual life expectancy is between twenty and thirty, and most die before full maturity.

### Psychology

Their defining trait is the pragmatism of prey. Fringals do not philosophize about their origins or cultivate resentment as an agenda: they think about immediate survival, and every thought that does not serve it is a distraction that kills. They defend minimal territories, an alley corner, a crevice, a hollow, with surprising intensity, because the place where they sleep is the only thing over which they exercise control. Their distrust is calculation, not paranoia: they assess every interaction by its risk, and unexpected generosity sounds like a trap to them. The only relationship they fully understand is a transactional one. Faced with Aquilars, they feel no hatred, which would require energy, but practical irritation: Aquilar melancholy, noble to the larger ones, seems to them an offensive luxury of those who have never had to compete with rats for trash.

### Relationship with the Empire

To the Shadow Empire, Fringals are administratively nonexistent. They are not listed in censuses, do not appear in decrees, and occupy no legal category: neither slaves, nor second-class citizens, nor property, merely urban fauna of the same stratum as rats and [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]]. Their angelic devotion defines them as antagonists of the regime, but hostility rarely becomes organized persecution, because it is not worth the effort. [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] ignore them or kick them, guards mistake them for goblins with strange fur, and goblins hunt them opportunistically. Those who survive in imperial cities do so through invisibility, memorizing patrol routines and safe alleys: an urban Fringal’s most valuable skill is knowing when to be nowhere at all.

### Relationship with Magic

Their angelic heritage remains intact, but Fringals do not practice magic. [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Devotio Sancta]] requires priestly instruction, temples, and years of structured faith, none within reach of someone marginalized: the devotional channel exists in potential and has never been opened, a lock without a key. A residual sensitivity remains. Fringals perceive angelic presences with a clarity that surprises them, a warmth before consecrated objects, discomfort in places desecrated by black magic. This perception has no evident practical use, but functions as an involuntary compass that steers them away from areas of concentrated shadow and marginally increases their chance of survival.

### Internal Conflicts

The same arcane sensitivity brings a moral component that pragmatism cannot stifle: hesitation when abandoning another wounded Fringal, discomfort when stealing from someone who has less, a burden when ignoring a plea for help. They lack the vocabulary to name the conflict, so they ignore it, and the burden of ignoring it accumulates. Reproduction compounds the harshness of Fringal life: they lay eggs in hiding places and often have to abandon clutches when their territory is no longer safe. Of two to four young, rarely does more than one reach maturity, in a selection that is not deliberate, merely environmental and relentless.

### Life Cycle

They are oviparous. Clutches contain two to four eggs, smaller than those of Aquilars, with an incubation period of thirty to forty days. Chicks hatch covered in brown down and able to move immediately, unlike Aquilar chicks, and already exhibit vestigial wings at birth, with no significant subsequent growth. They reach adulthood at around six years of age.
