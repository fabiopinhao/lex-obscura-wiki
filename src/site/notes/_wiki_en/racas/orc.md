# Orc

Orcs are demonic elves: soldiers driven by berserker fury who descend from the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] and despise the term “elf.” They were reshaped over the ages into war machines, and today serve as the spearhead of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]’s armies, abundant and disposable. Along with [[_wiki/racas/goblin\|goblins]] and [[_wiki/criaturas/ogro\|ogres]], they make up the goblinoid family, the green-skinned people whom imperial records call *green-skins*. The term is an umbrella, not a synonym for goblin: applied to an orc, it is correct, and in the mouth of a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]], it usually carries contempt.

### Origin

Orcs descend from the Luminars, though no living orc accepts that assertion without violence. During the fragmentation that scattered the ancient elves, a large faction descended to [[_wiki/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] in search of refuge, and there two forces competed for attention: diabolical entities, which offered order and contract, and demonic fissures, which offered immediate power through blood. The proto-Druls gravitated toward the diabolical whispers; the proto-orcs gravitated toward the fire, not out of stupidity, but out of pragmatic desperation, for they were the most wounded and persecuted, and the demons gave power now. The incompatibility between demonic bonds and diabolical pacts made coexistence untenable, and the proto-Druls expelled the proto-orcs from the depths. Exiled, they migrated to the [[_wiki/locais/dorsal\|northern mountain ranges]], where generational exposure to magma and blood bonds rewrote elven biology: compressed longevity, fury as a baseline state, throats redesigned by heat. Orcs reject being called elves because what they are now was paid for in blood, and calling them that means saying the sacrifice was worth nothing.

### Physiology

Orc skin is greenish and coarse in texture, the lower canines curve over the upper lip, and muscle density is high even in sedentary individuals when compared with humans; among orcs, prolonged idleness takes its toll, and the sedentary lose muscle to fat. The ears, once elongated like those of their elven ancestors, shortened and thickened over the generations, becoming rounded. Their blood is thicker than elven blood and smells of iron and cave mold, and their body odor, comparable to rotten onion mixed with sour beer, provokes instinctive disgust in the other races. Orcs do not die easily: they must be brought down two or three times, for the explosive metabolism that fuels berserker fury keeps the body functioning under damage that would paralyze any other race. Their natural thermal resistance makes them adaptable from volcanic heat to the cold of the plateau.

### Life Cycle

Life expectancy is around forty years, reduced in practice by violent death. At ten years old, an orc already has the build of an adult human and is considered ready for war; between ten and eighteen, an orc is a young adult, from eighteen to thirty reaches full vigor, and from thirty to forty is a revered elder, marked by decades of combat. Beyond forty, an orc is virtually nonexistent. The Shadars exploit this accelerated cycle as a military advantage, with rapid production of soldiers and efficient discarding of veterans: when tusks yellow and green skin fades, the Empire “retires” its old orcs with praise and a discreet blade. [[_wiki/personagens/ulgorth\|Ulgorth]], overseer in the dungeons of [[_wiki/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], is the typical case of a veteran worn down by decades of service.

Discarding is not limited to old age. Orcs unfit for military service, crippled or broken in combat, receive the same verdict by another route: most become pig feed, treated as dead weight, and a still-useful minority is repurposed for stewardry and support duties in the plateau’s orcish fortresses. In both cases, the Discard is recorded and formalized on the papyri of the fortresses’ goblinoid scribes.

### Reproduction

Males are born in litters of three to six. Females are born individually, are larger and bulkier than males, less suited to direct combat, but socially far more valuable. The scarcity of females defines tribal politics, and their abduction is the main cause of war among wild tribes, for possessing them confers status that no victory in battle surpasses. Leadership falls to the strongest orc in combat, but he rules with the tacit permission of the females, who control reproductive logistics. Females reach at least one fathom and two spans, two and a half meters, well above the male limit of one fathom, have bulk that predominates over musculature, and spend most of their lives seated, on thrones and ritual platforms, bearing more elaborate status markers than those of males.

### Social Structure

In the northern mountain ranges, wild tribes maintain the ancestral structure: clans led by the strongest, constant rivalries, loyalty defined by blood and the last fight. North of the plateau, beyond imperial reach, these tribes remain free and unaffiliated with the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]]. The Empire altered this structure in two ways. Captured orcs undergo a process of “domestication” conducted by the Shadars, which breaks the tribal hierarchy and replaces blood loyalties with institutional obedience; those born under imperial rule grow up within the hierarchy without ever knowing the tribe. In both cases, subordination is political and military, never religious, for orcs remain aligned with the red gods. Military formalization occurred during the [[_wiki/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|Reinkardic Wars]], when orcs became the vanguard of the shadow campaigns while Shadars and [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] withdrew to command. The legions maintain orc captains subordinate to Shadar officers, with goblins handling fortress logistics.

### The Empire

Orcs distrust arcanists and despise subtle strategies, but obey the representatives of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] without question when there is a promise of combat, plunder, or advancement. Their loyalty is not ideological, but functional: the Empire offers direction, equipment, and enemies, and the orcs offer blood and brutality in return. Outside this transaction, they are kept far from the centers of shadow power, and the only time orc troops entered [[_wiki/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]] was when they marched alongside Arothistar during the Purge of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Council]]. A general’s personal escort is different: his orc captains cross the sacred district and the [[_wiki/locais/praca_dos_juramentos\|Square of Oaths]] in his service, a concession the Cult’s elite barely tolerates. There are sacred portals whose guarding is still not entrusted to orcs.

The provincial garrisons reflect the orcs’ actual position in the system: abundant and disposable soldiers. The troops’ idle time is filled with urban trades (smithing, tavern work, butchery, construction), never with agricultural labor, which is the servitude of [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriars]] and is beneath a soldier of the Empire. The distinction is one of status, not aptitude: the orc who puts down the sledgehammer to take up the spear remains a soldier, whereas putting down the hoe would mean demotion to the condition of the vanquished. During a call to arms, the pattern appears in full, with smiths abandoning forges, tavern keepers leaving barrels behind, and the butcher hanging up his apron. The garrison of [[_wiki/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]] under [[_wiki/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]] exemplifies the pattern, with fat, unarmed, undisciplined orcs kept far from combat. Generals such as [[_wiki/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] command more disciplined legions, with captains such as [[_wiki/personagens/gorvash_tres-cicatrizes\|Gorvash Three-Scars]] and [[_wiki/personagens/hugur_comedor-de-ferro\|Hugur Iron-Eater]], but even in these elite formations, the relationship remains the same: the orcs bleed, the Shadars command.

### Culture

Orc culture revolves around combat and oral tradition. There is no written literature, only marks carved in wood, bone, or stone for practical records; the epic tradition is vocal, and the [[_wiki/personagens/grasnak\|Lays of Grasnak]] are the military epics every orc knows, recited before battle as naturally as sharpening a blade. Heavy weapons dominate, axes, clubs, cudgels, and hammers, and the fighting style prioritizes impact and intimidation over precision. Death lacks ritual: the corpse is usable material, stripped of weapons before any mourning, and mourning is brief, a collective shout followed by silence. Orc names are guttural and short, and descriptive epithets replace surnames, changing as the orc accumulates deeds or losses. Collective memory also settles into an expression: “carrying a barrel” is an insult for degrading work, and comes from the [[_wiki/eventos/cerco_de_khordun\|Siege of Khordun]], where prisoners carried barrels of boiling pitch under a rain of arrows. Directing it at a captain is equivalent to calling him a slave.

### Magic

Orcs access arcane power through [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], a direct bond with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]] without contract or negotiation: blood offered, power received. Mental corruption is progressive, manifesting as fury, paranoia, and sadism, and the mark never disappears. Those who master the power fall into two categories. Shamans master schools of tactical application, [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]], and [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]], and are rare because few orcs live long enough to accumulate mastery. Berserkers channel schools of combat and transformation, [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]], and [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]]; the berserker trance is the ultimate expression of this channeling, in which ancestral fury dissolves consciousness, the body grows, and pain disappears, but the price is sanity. [[_wiki/panteao/thurgash\|Thûrgash]], the Bloody Warlord, is the demon most closely bonded to orcs, and governs eternal war and fury as a sacrament.

### Conflicts

The central tension among orcs is the fracture between the domesticated and the wild. The imperials serve, obey, and die within a structure that feeds and discards them; the wild orcs of the mountain ranges maintain tribal organization, freedom, and hunger. The irregular migrations that put pressure on the plateau are a symptom of a demographic crisis in the mountains, depleted territory, and conflict among tribes, and the Council treats them as a logistical problem. The discarding of veterans erodes the loyalty of any orc attentive enough to count the years. And there is the wound no one mentions: the Luminar ancestry shared with [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]]. The hatred orcs harbor for elves has the specific intensity of someone who hates a mirror.

### Rivalries

The orcs’ hostility toward the [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]] is carried in ancestral memory: the two races have fought each other for ages, and even under the Cult’s rule, the rivalry remains alive, channeled through the shadow armies. The antipathy is reciprocal and visceral. Against the [[_wiki/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]], the aversion is instinctive and extends to anything with scales: during the military formalization of the Reinkardic Wars, [[_wiki/racas/kobold\|kobolds]] were appointed stewards of the orc legions, but the orcs killed them reflexively, and goblins eventually replaced them in the role.

### Voice

Orcs speak as they fight: directly, without flourish or metaphor, each word chosen for its weight rather than its beauty. Their references draw on strength, honor in combat, hierarchy, and blood, and silence is preferable to useless words: when an orc elaborates, it is because he is threatening; when he simplifies, it is because he has already decided. [[_wiki/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]] surfaces in speech whenever imperial discipline fails, in moments of fury, extreme pain, or ritual, and imperial orcs speak [[_wiki/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]] with constant Orcanic interference, like an accent impossible to eliminate.
