# Frenim

The Frenim are [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereal]] humans from [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]], known outside their continent as “blue mages”. An erudite and isolationist people, they are distinguished by simultaneously inhabiting the material world and the edges of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], a passage that has become the defining mark of the race’s identity. Their magic flows from the sidereal gods who wander through the ethereal, such as Zuvaneth, Aethravos, and Nocthydral.

### Physiology

With yellowish-green skin and slender, elongated builds, the Frenim have features that differ slightly from those of humans and distinguish them at first glance. Their unusual skin tone is consistent with their sidereal nature and long exposure to the ethereal plane; their slender build is adapted to the humid, swampy climate. They live around ninety-six to one hundred years, a lifespan close to that of ordinary humans, although their way of life, oriented toward the ethereal, suggests a different vitality, less robust and more permeable to the supernatural.

In neutral terms, a Frenim appears to be a slender, elongated, contemplative being, with yellowish-green skin and features subtly outside the ordinary human range. When invoking magic or actively traveling between planes, however, certain visual markers may emerge: eyes like deep slits of rippling crimson fire, veils that float without any apparent wind in response to the air of another plane, and ethereal overlays in which the silhouette seems to shift sideways from the body. These are not constant racial traits, but signs of the magical state.

### Origin

The Frenim descend from humans who migrated to the eastern regions of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], moving away from the wars that consumed the center of the continent. The crossing took them to the marshlands where the [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]] had already lived for generations, and this encounter defined the race’s destiny.

Many of the newcomers aspired to the communion with the sidereals that the undines cultivated, but were largely rejected: communion required surrendering their own vitality, and the humans refused to shorten their lives in exchange for transcendence. The refusal did not lead to war. Those who remained in the eastern lands sought another path to elevation, pure knowledge and the systematic study of the ethereal plane. Through wisdom and discipline, they learned to travel through the ethereal without relinquishing their bodies, coexisting in both planes at the same time.

### Culture and Society

The Frenim inhabit Neranthis, a continent east of Reinkard, separated by a vast region of swamps whose hostile geography has always protected them. They are a mysterious, erudite, and isolationist people who favor arcane knowledge and diplomacy over brute force. Among the races with a sidereal bond, which also include the undines and the [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidans]], the Frenim are the dominant group, exercising political and arcane leadership over the continent.

Their system of magical access is [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]], based on meditation and the expansion of consciousness into the ethereal plane, which requires neither pacts nor devotion to shadows. The ability to travel between planes shapes their entire culture: for a Frenim, the material world is only half of reality. This dual perception makes them both wise and distant, able to see patterns that other races overlook, but prone to a certain indifference toward purely earthly urgencies.

### Relationship with the Undines

The relationship between Frenim and undines is foundational and asymmetrical. The undines possessed the knowledge that the first Frenim coveted, but refused to pass it on under human terms, believing that communion with the sidereals required a sacrifice that humans would not make. There was no animosity, and over the centuries the two races developed a peaceful, if not particularly close, coexistence.

The undines retreated to the ocean coasts of the far east, becoming increasingly ethereal and remote, while the Frenim consolidated their presence in the inland regions, in swamps and lakes. They share the same sidereal gods, but their paths to reaching them are opposite: total surrender on one side, rational understanding on the other.

### Relationship with the Empire

Neranthis remains independent during the Lex Obscura. The Frenim are isolationist by instinct and calculation, fear shadows, and make geographical distance their best defense, since the continent’s wetlands are unsuitable for direct [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] rule. The detachment that travel between planes affords them has its reverse side: they tend to underestimate purely material threats until it is too late.
