# Pindoriar

Pindoriar is not a race, but a social condition: the imperial pejorative designation for ordinary humans and subjugated elves who live under the rule of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] without full citizenship. The term is a creation of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], formed from two words in ancient [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]], pindor (cattle) and iare (that walks), and literally means cattle that walks. Physically indistinguishable from unevolved humans and elves, with a baseline lifespan of about eighty years, Pindoriars are the baseline against which all evolved races measure themselves.

### Social Status

Pindoriars are second-class citizens. They have no citizenship rights, cannot hold positions in the Cult, and have no formal access to institutional magic, existing in a range between “tolerated” and “property” that varies according to the province and the local Lord. In [[_wiki/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], they serve as laborers, servants, and low-ranking artisans, invisible by design. In the provinces, their status varies enormously: in [[_wiki/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], under [[_wiki/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Merari]]’s neglectful rule, they maintain a semifunctional life with parallel leadership. In rebel territories, they form the base of resistance movements, especially in [[_wiki/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], where human refugees live alongside free [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]].

### Culture of Resistance

Deprived of institutional power, Pindoriars have developed survival strategies that operate in the shadows of the system. Many maintain secret devotion to the white gods, the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]], and the green gods, the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]]: [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]], a priest of Niedellin, exemplifies this spiritual resistance, keeping the faith alive under the Shadow Cult’s nose. Invisible support networks circulate information, shelter, and supplies beyond the imperial gaze, and figures such as [[_wiki/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] exercise real leadership without formal titles, navigating partial truths to protect their own.

### Relations with Other Races

In the presence of Shadars, Pindoriars practice outward submission with deep underlying resentment, having learned to appear harmless. With Sylvans they form a natural alliance, sharing their subjugated condition and the memory of times before shadow rule. With [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], they feel genuine fear: orcs treat them with open contempt, without the pretense of civility that Shadars maintain.

### Weaknesses

The absence of institutional magic leaves Pindoriars dependent on rare individual talents, without access to pacts or arcane academies. Without a unified structure, each community survives on its own, and legal vulnerability is constant: any upper-caste Shadar can do as they please with a Pindoriar with little or no consequence.
