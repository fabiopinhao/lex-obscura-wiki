# Avian

Avians are a family of humanoid bird hybrids, linked to angels, divided into two natural subraces of common origin: [[_wiki_en/racas/aquilar\|Aquilars]], of humanoid stature, and [[_wiki_en/racas/fringal\|Fringals]], of gnomish stature. Oviparous and originally urban in habitat, they have magical access exclusively through [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Devotio Sancta]].

### Cosmogonic Origin

Avians date back to the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_raciais\|Racial Wars]], when angelic entities intervened in the material plane and transformed humans into something they did not ask to be. Unlike [[_wiki_en/racas/vulfano\|Vulfans]], forged by [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]], soldiers of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], and [[_wiki_en/racas/lynxiano\|Lynxians]], absorbed by [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], Avians were neither manufactured for war nor shaped by predation: they were elevated. Angelic interference imposed wings, feathers, and a mark of purity upon humans who did not consent, and called the process a blessing. Where the other colors abandoned their hybrids after the Wars, the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]] remained, and the angelic mark is no scar of abandonment, but a weight of expectation: Avians carry a vow they never made and the distant presence of patrons who do not protect them, though neither do they disappear.

The evidence of this presence is tangible. Rosaries of [[_wiki_en/panteao/azazel\|Azazel]] still work in [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] even under the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/manto_das_sombras\|mantle of shadows]], and the angelic mark does not fade with the generations, for Aquilar chicks born in captivity hatch with the same golden feathers as their free ancestors. Devotio Sancta responds to Avians with an ease that no other race replicates, and the angels do not explain why. Avians believe they are being watched; the alternative, that the angels merely marked them and moved on, is worse, and so no Avian considers it aloud.

### Familial Nature

The two subraces share a common origin and divergent fates. Aquilars are full-fledged Avians: tall, with functional wings at maturity and aerodynamic builds made for flight, with phenotypes of raptors, eagles, and falcons predominating. Before the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] conquest, they held integrated positions in mixed cities such as [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], serving as sentinels, temple guardians, and hierophants of Devotio Sancta. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]], they are rare, scattered, and mutilated, for [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]] stripped them of flight through multiple methods.

Fringals are diminutive Avians, with vestigial wings that never enabled flight, with phenotypes of smaller passerines, wrens, and finches. They arose from the same angelic interference, neither from artificial manipulation nor from environmental degeneration, and the smaller phenotype is a natural expression of variation within the white lineage, not a defect. This does not prevent Aquilars from treating them as incomplete Avians, nor the rest of the world from treating them as prey.

### Protection and Resentment

The relationship between Aquilars and Fringals is one of asymmetric protection: Aquilars have always treated Fringals as lesser members of the family who need guardianship. The guardianship is real and sincere, but sincerity does not erase condescension. Before the conquest, the dynamic maintained a precarious balance, with Aquilars patrolling the skies while Fringals occupied urban niches where their reduced size was an advantage. Paternalism existed, tempered by proximity, for both subraces lived together in the same cities and sometimes in the same towers, and Fringals tolerated the protection because it came with presence.

In the Third Era, protection evaporated. Mutilated Aquilars can barely protect themselves, and Fringals were left to their own devices on the urban margins, without the aerial shield they never asked for but depended on. The resentment that was once a muted irritation crystallized: the protectors failed, and the protected pay the price. Avians who meet in the Third Era carry a heavy silence between them, the kind that exists only between relatives who know that one abandoned the other, even if the abandonment was not a choice.

### Debestialization of the Material Plane

Avians are part of the phenomenon of debestialization that affects all hybrid races, but their decline is particularly abrupt. There was no slow erosion as among Lynxians, nor endemic fragmentation as among Saurians: there was conquest. The mixed cities that sheltered Avians fell one by one under the Shadar advance, and with them fell the race’s entire civilizational infrastructure. Vulfans never had territory to lose, and Lynxians retreat into ever smaller forests, but Avians lost cities, with everything cities contain: temples, guilds, support networks, generational nests. Among Aquilars, the decline is visible in severed wings, scattered populations, and eroding faith; among Fringals, it is invisible, for they die in alleys, become food for [[_wiki_en/racas/goblin\|goblinoids]], and no one counts them.
