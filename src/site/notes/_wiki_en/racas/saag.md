# Saag

Saags are the large-bodied subgroup of the [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidans]], descendants of the sapian lineage. Where [[_wiki_en/racas/boglin\|Boglins]] are compact chelonians, Saags are the version built to withstand, keep watch, and endure: broad, dense, and made to occupy space and stay there. They are the largest humanoids in [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] and also the quietest, a combination that disturbs any outsider.

### Physiology

Upright, they measure from one point nine to two point four meters, although their natural posture is rarely fully upright. Their mass is comparable to that of a [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]], but distributed more broadly: the torso is flattened and massive, with no clear separation between head and shoulders, and the mouth opens far beyond what would seem possible. Their horizontal pupils detect peripheral movement with predatory efficiency and, in the dark, reflect light like polished copper disks. Their skin ranges from moss green to muddy brown to swamp gray, with darker irregular patches that serve as camouflage.

Their forelimbs are short relative to the massive torso, with webbed hands bearing four fingers and a vestigial opposable thumb: functional for grasping, wielding, and manipulating, but never with the dexterity of a [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]] or a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]]. What a Saag’s hands do well is hold on, and not let go. Their hind limbs, in contrast, are enormous and bent under tension: they power the explosive leaps that launch from stillness and make ambush the race’s signature. Beneath the throat, an elastic sac serves for nearly inaudible low-frequency calls, threat displays, and breathing under pressure. Elders retain the race’s massive stature and general bone structure; aging manifests as loose skin and atrophied musculature, not as a reduction in size. An elder does not shrink: they lose tone while retaining their size.

### Psychology

Patience is the cardinal virtue. Saags think in seasons, not days, and stillness is a method, neither laziness nor apathy: a Saag motionless for hours in the same position is processing sound, vibration, smell, atmospheric pressure, and changes in water level, and when they move, the decision has already been made long before the movement. This extended perception of time explains the difficulty of communicating with faster races, which tend to mistake slowness for stupidity. A Saag who has not reacted to a provocation has not ignored it: they are deciding whether it is worth reacting, and, if they decide it is, the reaction comes with a force the provocateur did not anticipate.

### Social Structure

The basic social unit is the pool, the permanent body of water around which the community organizes itself. To lose the pool is to lose the community: droughts force migrations that dissolve bonds, and floods create new pools that attract scattered Saags. Geography dictates politics more than any conscious decision.

### The Drowned Below

Saag ritual life revolves around the Drowned Below (the Drowned Below), the presence that the pool’s shamans say they hear beneath the still water. They do not pray to him aloud or in assembly: they listen. Access comes through three paths — blood, breath, and still water — and it is through them that shamans claim to call, bind, and devour. The rites bind three things in a single vow: the blood of the pool’s lineage, the territory of the body of water, and the Drowned Below himself; to break one is to break all three. The race’s cardinal patience takes on a religious extension here, for the Drowned Below does not answer those in a hurry, and the water only listens in return to those who have learned to wait motionless for as long as it requires.

### Communication

Saags do not speak with other races, except through telepathy. Even for the Frenims who access their minds, communication is not dialogue but a current: it advances, recedes, deposits information, and withdraws, felt by the recipient as a change in pressure. A Saag’s mind projects what they feel (pressure, image, direction, urgency), and the other translates. Silence is part of their characterization: the Saag is usually the object of conversation, rarely the subject.

### Relationship with the World

In formal terms, the Saags’ relationship with the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] is nonexistent. Neranthis remains independent, and Saags are unknown to the vast majority of Shadars; at most, the Empire records Ranidans as a zoological curiosity in intelligence reports.
