# Draconid

Draconids, or Drakhonins, are the elite subgroup of [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurians]]: the largest, strongest, and proudest of the reptilian humanoids. Bonded to demons, they claim a divine heritage from dragons and act in accordance with that belief. Their build exceeds that of [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] in mass and height, their scales deflect tempered steel, and their elemental breath turns ambush into cremation. They are the closest creatures to dragons that the world still produces, and they never let anyone forget it.

### Origin

Draconids claim direct descent from the elder dragons that inhabited the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] and the mountains of [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]] before the first migration, and each Drakhonin clan traces its lineage to a specific ancestral dragon. The cosmogonic truth is more complex: like all hybrid humanoids, Saurians trace their origins to the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_raciais\|Racial Wars]], when red entities ([[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]]) crossbred humans and reptiles to produce soldiers. The draconic lineage is real, but filtered through demonic interference. Draconids are children of dragons in the same way that [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worgs]] are children of wolves: the animal seed is genuine, the process that transformed it into a race was not natural. The treatise by [[_wiki_en/personagens/wijmarv\|Wijmarv]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]] shaman, worsened the identity crisis by demonstrating that [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] weapon, kills Draconids as efficiently as it kills dragons.

Shadars have two names for them: *Drakhonin* is the term for feigned politeness, and *scalies* the one for when courtesy runs out. Both registers coexist in the same mouth, and switching from one to the other measures the diplomatic distance of the moment.

### Physiology

Standing upright, they measure from 1.90 m to 2.40 m, with dense musculature beneath scales that vary by lineage: dark red in fire clans, earthy brown in earth clans, moss green in swamp clans, and volcanic black in the oldest. The scales are overlapping keratinous plates, thick enough to deflect arrows and low-quality blades, but not impenetrable: weapons forged by [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]] or enchanted weapons pierce them. Elemental breath, fire in most clans, originates in glands at the base of the throat that produce a volatile compound, and is a resource limited to three or five discharges per day, each requiring recovery; excessive use burns the throat from within. Metal muzzles neutralize the breath and serve as instruments of restraint. Their prehensile tails function as weapons, supports for balance, and means of communication, since tail position signals intent. Lifespans range from 50 to 65 years, shortened by endemic violence between clans, and old Draconids are rare and respected.

### Psychology

Pride in lineage is the identity structure that sustains clan cohesion: to question the lineage is to question the clan’s reason for existing. Added to this is a predatory pragmatism in which the clan’s continuity takes precedence over honor, and betraying an ally is acceptable when the alternative is extinction. Draconids measure the world by size and treat smaller races with automatic condescension; Shadars receive reluctant respect for their power, not their stature, and [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]] provoke a visceral reaction, seen by the Draconids of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]] as an offensive parody of the draconic lineage.

### Social Structure

Drakhonin clans organize themselves by draconic lineage, each claiming an ancestral dragon or demonic patron and ruling territory in Varnak or around Veinkard. Leadership falls to the strongest with the purest blood, and succession disputes are settled through ritualized combat or ambush. Clan [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/cla_myr_vethan\|Myr’vethan]], led by [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]], has absorbed defeated tribes over generations and seeks to unify the Drakhonins under one banner. The obstacle is [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]], an elder dragon who keeps the clans fragmented through brute force; whoever holds Fendris can defeat her. Yzmareth sought Fendris for this purpose, but lost it to [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]]. Internally, Draconids occupy the top of the hierarchy, with common Saurians serving as warriors and artisans.

### Culture

The Drakhonin bonding ritual binds each individual to the clan’s dragon-god from the egg: eggs are bathed in ritual blood and exposed to the fire of an elder’s breath, and those that do not survive the heat are regarded as natural purification. Each Draconid receives a brood name at birth and a battle name after the first victory; [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]] is “Warrior of the Deep Flames”, while Yzmareth operates under his birth name, which may suggest that his victories are political, not physical. They collect trophies from defeated enemies, teeth, claws, and scales from rivals, as a record of deeds. The clans maintain chroniclers who record lineages, battles, and blood debts, and Varnakian shamans such as Wijmarv serve as scholars.

### Magic

Draconids access magic through [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], a demonic bond established from the egg, and their magic tends toward fire, destruction, and fear, in the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]]. Elemental breath is the most visible magical manifestation, inscribed in their very physiology. The cost manifests as progressive internal burning: Draconids who overuse their breath or fire invocations develop necrosis in their internal tissues, and their scales remain intact while their organs char within. The old and powerful frequently die of internal collapse, not combat.

### Life Cycle

Draconids are oviparous: females lay one to three eggs per brood, incubated for about ninety days in constant heat, in the clan’s furnaces or in volcanic soil. The young hatch with soft scales, which harden over the first year, and reach adulthood at around twelve years of age.

### Conflicts

Clan fragmentation has prevented unification for generations, and without Varna the clans would already have decimated one another, but with her they remain fragmented by design. Fendris is an existential threat: if it circulates, any race can slay a Draconid, and the monopoly on brute force is no longer guaranteed. Wijmarv’s treatise delegitimizes the founding myth by demonstrating that heirs and ancestors share the same vulnerability, and if Draconids are merely smaller versions of what dragons once were, the claim to heritage becomes a confession of inferiority. Thrax hates Wijmarv with personal intensity; Yzmareth has read the treatise and does not say what he thinks.
