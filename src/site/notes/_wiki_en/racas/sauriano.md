# Saurian

Saurians are a family of reptilian hybrid humanoids, aligned with demons. Under this name, imperial scholars grouped two scaled peoples who have little in common, the [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] and the [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]], separated by continent, origin, and purpose. What unites them is reptilian blood and the mark of a divine intervention that brought them into being.

### Cosmogonic Origin

Like all hybrid humanoids, Saurians trace their origins to the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_raciais\|Racial Wars]], when demonic entities intervened in the material plane, crossing humans and reptiles to produce soldiers aligned with red. The reptilian lineage is genuine, since [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|draconic]] blood runs through Saurian veins, but the process that transformed this blood into a race was divine interference, and the interference left a mark. Saurians bear the red of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], and that red carries a cost.

Unlike other hybrids, abandoned by the gods who brought them into being, Saurians maintained their connection with their animal ancestors. Dragons still exist, are still venerated, and the relationship between creator and creature remains active: [[_wiki_en/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]] still accepts bonds, and the dragons of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]] still cast a shadow over their lesser descendants.

### Nature of the Family

The Saurian family is divided into two subraces with radically different origins. Draconids, of humanoid build, are the natural product of the Racial Wars: they have thick scales, elemental breath, and prehensile tails, organize themselves into Drakhonin clans that claim direct descent from ancestral dragons, and inhabit Varnak and [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], outside imperial dominion. They consider themselves the rightful heirs to the draconic lineage.

Kobolds, of gnomish build, are the product of [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] manipulation. They do not descend from the lineages of Varnak, but were instead shaped from dragon eggs corrupted by the shadowy presence on the plateau of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], subjected to deliberate selection over generations until they attained a stable form. They are exclusive to Reinkard and do not exist in Veinkard. To a Draconid, a kobold is an offensive parody of the Draconid’s own lineage.

### The Original Rift

The relationship between the two subraces has never been one of coexistence. They arose on different continents, through different processes, and for different purposes: Draconids were born of the Racial Wars in Veinkard, and kobolds were manufactured by Shadars in Reinkard ages later. The very idea of a Saurian “family” is an external classification, imposed from outside: Draconids reject the association, and kobolds do not even know it exists.

When the two peoples cross paths, the encounter tends to be rare and violent. In [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], the Draconid [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]] severed the kobold [[_wiki_en/personagens/fing\|Fing]]’s tail and kept it as a trophy. To a Draconid, seeing a kobold is seeing what the Shadars did to eggs that should have hatched into dragons.

### Decline

Saurians are part of the phenomenon of debestialization that affects all hybrid races, but their decline operates through two distinct paths. Among Draconids, clan fragmentation and endemic violence reduce the population with each generation. Among kobolds, artificial manipulation eliminated the ability to exist outside servitude. Both subraces dwindle, each for its own reason. The dragons that gave them blood migrated or isolated themselves, and what remains is scale without fire.
