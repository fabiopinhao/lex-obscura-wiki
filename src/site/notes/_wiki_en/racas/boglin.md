# Boglin

Boglins are [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidans]] of gnomish stature, descended from the chelonian lineage: humanoid turtles protected by a dorsal shell. They are the smallest and most reclusive inhabitants of [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]], sighted less frequently than any other race on the continent.

### Nature

A smaller subgroup of Ranidans, Boglins rarely exceed one meter in height. Where [[_wiki_en/racas/saag\|Saags]] are broad and exposed, Boglins are compact and armored, protected by dorsal shells of thick keratin.

### Physiology

Height ranges from eighty centimeters to one hundred ten centimeters, with short, sturdy limbs and hands with five functional fingers and greater dexterity than those of Saags. The dorsal shell covers the area from the shoulder blades to the base of the spine, thick enough to deflect arrows and blows from light blades, and the head can partially retract beneath the shell’s upper edge, an involuntary defensive reflex that Boglins perform before any conscious decision. The skin is leathery, in brown, olive, or grayish tones, and the eyes, smaller and more forward-facing than those of Saags, have vision adapted to low-light environments. Their lifespan, forty to fifty years, is shorter than that of Saags. Dependence on moisture is shared with the family, although the shell offers partial protection against desiccation, extending their tolerance for dry environments by a few hours.

### Limited Telepathy

Boglins share the Ranidan telepathic ability, but to a diminished degree. Its range is short, functional within the same body of water and imprecise beyond it, and the projections are vague, closer to emotional impressions than to the clear sensory images that Saags transmit. [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]] describe Boglin telepathy as hearing someone speak underwater: the intent comes through, and the nuances are lost. This limitation reinforces their isolation, as Boglins operate within a small radius, tied to their immediate surroundings, and do not serve as long-distance intermediaries, which excludes them from the role of bridging Frenims and [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]] that defines the usefulness of Saags.

### Behavior and Role

Reclusive by temperament and circumstance, Boglins inhabit the most remote interior regions of Neranthis, in shallow marshes and along muddy banks where other races do not venture. Retreating beneath the shell is not just a physical reflex but a social disposition: Boglins avoid contact, exposure, and attention. The strategy works, and they are the least documented race in Neranthis and possibly in the entire known world.
