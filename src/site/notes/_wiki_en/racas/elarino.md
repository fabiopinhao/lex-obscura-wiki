# Elarin

Elarins are angelic elves from [[_wiki/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], the closest descendants of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] in tradition and arcane power.

### Physiology

They have delicate features and fair skin with a subtle luminescence, visible under moonlight or in dark surroundings. Their eyes come in shades of silver, amber, or sky blue; their ears are elongated, their proportions slender, and their hair often silver or platinum blond. Their longevity is around one hundred twenty years, and their bond is with angels. Their innate affinity for magic is visibly superior to that of any other mortal race.

### Culture and Society

They took refuge in Elenir after the destruction of the Luminars in the [[_wiki/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], choosing magical regions that are difficult to access. Their society is organized around the preservation of ancestral knowledge: they maintain arcane libraries, celestial observatories, and workshops that attempt, unsuccessfully, to reproduce the works of the Luminars. In the First Era, they were the first elves to assimilate aspects of human culture, merging elven traditions with [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Sancta]] magic, and from this rapprochement came the first half-elves, the offspring of unions between Elarins and [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]]. In the [[_wiki/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], Elenir allied with [[_wiki/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] as a strategic partner against threats from the plateau.

### Relationship with the Empire

After the Reinkardic Wars, Elenir was conquered and its arcane nexuses were partially incorporated into the imperial infrastructure. Elarin nexuses in the Crystal Fortress were even silenced from the inside out, through sabotage of an unknown nature. Elarins survive under varying conditions: some as valuable prisoners, others as scattered exiles. [[_wiki/personagens/iluvrae\|Ilúvrae]] is an Elarin imprisoned in [[_wiki/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], endowed with profound knowledge of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] and the cosmology of the world; her gift for elven healing keeps her useful to her captors, who exploit her in captivity.

### Weaknesses

Chronic isolation, the tendency to take refuge rather than fight, made them vulnerable to conquest. Traditionalist rigidity, attachment to the forms of the Luminars, hinders adaptation to new threats. And their small numbers take a toll: few pure Elarins remain, most scattered or subjugated.
