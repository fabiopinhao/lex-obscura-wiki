# Ranidan

Ranidans are a family of amphibian hybrid humanoids from [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]], divided into two subraces by lineage: the [[_wiki_en/racas/saag\|Saags]], of humanoid size, and the [[_wiki_en/racas/boglin\|Boglins]], of gnomish size. They date back to the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_raciais\|Racial Wars]], when the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]] crossbred primitive humans and amphibians to produce soldiers aligned with the sidereals, in a wartime experiment that gave rise to two lineages, the sapian and the chelonian.

### Origin

The defining mark of Ranidans is their creator’s indifference. The sidereals who shaped them simply lost interest, since entities of pure consciousness do not grow attached to physical results. Where [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] claim divine descent, Ranidans claim nothing: the gods shaped them and the gods left, and the swamp remained. They were an experiment that survived their god’s disinterest.

### Physiology

They are humanoids with moist skin in shades of green, brown, or mottled patterns, with large, laterally positioned eyes, wide mouths, and limbs adapted to terrestrial and aquatic locomotion, naturally camouflaged in swamp environments. Their lifespan ranges from fifty to sixty-five years, and mortality results more from droughts, skin infections, and aquatic parasites than from violence. The family’s central vulnerability is its dependence on moisture: the skin dries out within a few hours in dry heat, causing cracks, loss of sensation, and, at an advanced stage, necrosis. Continental [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] is hostile territory, and the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau is lethal. The geography of Neranthis is not merely home, it is a condition of existence.

### Communication

Oral speech is used exclusively among Ranidans, in a low-pitched language of guttural vibrations and clicks that other races describe as articulated croaking and that, to outside ears, is indistinguishable from the natural sounds of the swamp. This indistinguishability is deliberate, camouflage extended to language. Beyond speech, an innate telepathy connects Ranidans to the other races of Neranthis that share the same sidereal mark, the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]] and the [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]]: the communication conveys not words, but intent, sensory impressions, and urgencies. This duality makes Ranidans irreplaceable intermediaries among the peoples of the region.

### Culture and Society

Ranidan society is organized into lakeside and riverside communities, coexisting with Frenims and undines. The defining political relationship is the symbiosis with Frenims: they maintain the arcane infrastructure that stabilizes bodies of water, while Ranidans provide a physical presence and territorial surveillance. It is not a relationship of servitude, since Frenims need Ranidans as much as the reverse, though they admit it less often. Undines complete the triangle, connecting the ocean coast to the swampy interior.

Saags, descendants of the sapian lineage, are massive and silent, the largest humanoids in Neranthis, and the most documented Saag is [[_wiki_en/personagens/babua\|Babua]]. Boglins, descendants of the chelonian lineage, are humanoid turtles of short stature, protected by natural shells, rarer and more reclusive, rarely sighted outside the interior regions; their exact origin is uncertain. Like the [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurian]] family, the Ranidan “family” is in part an external classification, but, unlike Draconids and [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]], who violently reject the association, Saags and Boglins coexist with the placid indifference of those who share the same swamp without competing for the same pool.

### Magic

Ranidans access magic through [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]], passive attunement to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], without pact, contract, or devotion. Where Frenims study and rationalize, Ranidans feel: the Ranidan submerges, slows its breathing, and the boundary between the material plane and the ethereal plane thins. Access is organic, but control is imprecise, since they perceive the ethereal plane clearly and manipulating it requires an effort that most cannot sustain. The magical cost manifests as progressive desiccation: intense ethereal magic drains bodily moisture, and Ranidans who abuse the connection age visibly, with cracked skin and dull eyes. The source of the power and what the cost consumes are the same thing.

Some Ranidans develop the capacity for ethereal concealment, hiding in the veil of the ethereal plane while their skin remains in contact with water on the material plane. It is not invisibility, but partial existential displacement, a rare ability that seems to require innate sensitivity combined with decades of meditation. The most documented case is that of Babua, whose enchantment breaks magical bonds, including connections of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]]. It is not known for certain whether this capacity is exclusive to Saags or also shared by Boglins.

### Debestialization

Ranidans are part of the phenomenon of debestialization that affects all hybrid races, but their decline proceeds through irrelevance. They are not hunted, persecuted, or fought: they are forgotten. The same camouflage that protects them individually erases them collectively, and their geographical dependence worsens the situation, since they cannot expand beyond wet territories, and each drought dissolves entire communities.
