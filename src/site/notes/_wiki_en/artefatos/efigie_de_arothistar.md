# Effigy of Arothistar

The Effigy of Arothistar is an animated statue on the bridge of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]]. Its onyx eyes light up silver, and it replicates the master’s voice. It watches everything between the bridge and the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]], functioning as an automated sentinel and a symbolic projection of the monarch’s omnipresence.

It is not the only one. There are many speaking effigies of the monarch, such as the one in the niche in the hall of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|the Council]], the one in the antechamber on the first floor of Mor’Severith, the largest of all, and those of the Crown, present everywhere under the Iconographic Protocol of the Crown. The one on the bridge is the sentinel.
