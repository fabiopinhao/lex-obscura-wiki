# Reverse Tablet

The Reverse Tablet is a polished clay tablet, the size of a codex, that serves as a mandatory procedural instrument for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]]. Investigations and field sentences are recorded on it. Each justiciar carries their own, and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/tribunal_de_morvgrad\|Tribunal of Morvgrad]] also uses it as a standard instrument in its sessions. The name comes from the belief that it reverses the natural flow: it turns the end, death, into a beginning, case law.

### Form and Function

The polished surface accepts ink. The justiciar writes with a crow quill, and a flawed line is erased with a wet finger before it sets. The procedural code requires that a duplicate of each record be sent to the Tribunal’s central archives, ensuring the traceability of decisions made far from the court; in some cases, the copy also goes to the sealed chambers of [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_tabreth\|Khar’Tabreth]], where the judicial record is preserved without any hand being able to alter it. There are special inks. Some are only revealed under specific conditions, and one is self-destructing: its marks glow faintly in the dark and dissolve into smoke under direct sunlight, suitable for notes that must not outlive the bearer.

### Use

[[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]] uses it consistently, maintaining protocol even in extreme situations. Its origin is tied to the Shadow Cult, and it remains in active use among the Empire’s judge-enforcers.
