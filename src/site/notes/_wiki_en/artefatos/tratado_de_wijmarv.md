# Treatise of Wijmarv

The Treatise of Wijmarv is a scroll of reptile hide, the writing medium of [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]] shamans, authored by one of them, the tiefling [[_wiki_en/personagens/wijmarv\|Wijmarv]], a scholar of [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]], dragons, and [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]]. The text reveals the true function and limits of [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], and thereby delegitimizes the [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]] founding myth.

### Content

The central thesis of the treatise is condensed into a single sentence: “The blade recognizes no oath. Where there is a pact, there is blindness. Fendris answers only to the hand that owes nothing.”

### Circulation

Written in Varnakian, the treatise can be read only by scholars. The document is kept in the library of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]]. [[_wiki_en/personagens/odran\|Odran]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/minotauro\|minotaur]], reads Varnakian treatises, including this one. [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]] hates Wijmarv deeply and calls him *mraz’kor*, “Heart of Ash”: the one who has lost fire and honor.
