# Red Book of Dragons

The Red Book of Dragons is a [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]] grimoire with a cover made of stitched scales, marked with the emblem of a blind dragon with gouged-out eyes and an open mouth. It contains knowledge about dragons and red magic, and is [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s current favorite.

Almost hot to the touch, it holds a muffled breath between its pages, as though it kept something alive within itself. It remains in the library of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]].
