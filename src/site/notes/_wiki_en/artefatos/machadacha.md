# Machadacha

The machadacha is [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]]’s double-bladed battle-axe: a piece of steel as thick as a guillotine post, with poorly maintained runic carvings. It is an ordinary weapon, with no known magical properties, made of orcish war steel.

“Machadacha” is the nickname the captain himself gave the weapon, and the name caught on among his companions. Grothar has a sentinel’s tic involving it, his fingers checking the haft and returning, and the weapon is rarely more than a span from his hand.
