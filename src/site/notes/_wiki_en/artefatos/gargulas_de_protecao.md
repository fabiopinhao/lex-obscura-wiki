# Gargoyles of Protection

The Gargoyles of Protection are figurines carved from stone, known throughout [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] for repelling evil spirits and corrupted energies.

### Use

Traditionally, they are placed in temples and castles to create spiritual barriers.
