# Manacles of Arcane Suppression

The Manacles of Arcane Suppression are a pair of bracelets that drain [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]]. When the activation rune is touched, the band closes to fit its wearer, but no smaller than a human wrist. There is a record of one of them being used as a makeshift collar in a bluff that worked: the coerced creature believed it was actually under suppression.
