# Orum’s Templar Armor

Orum’s Templar Armor is a silver-colored cuirass of Templar manufacture, forged in the pre-imperial era, when the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]] still operated as an autonomous military force of the temples. Worn by [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], it is the most visible of the surviving Templar relics and announces, in every room he crosses, his status as a Templar among [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]].

### Form

The steel is old, in a style predating the reforms of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], with each plate polished in religious devotion. The runes engraved in the metal gather the ambient light, however scarce, and return it in a subdued halo; in dimly lit surroundings, the veins cast patterns on the faces of those who approach. Over the cuirass, Orum wears the justiciar’s seal, the credential that the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tegmen\|tegmen]] gives the others in the order and that the Templar armor does not. The enchantments that imbue it, of strength, endurance, and vigor, are permanent and sustain Orum’s body even at ninety-nine years of age. Its upkeep is obsessive and ritualistic: each rivet polished, each joint oiled with sacred ointment. After decades of use, the steel creaks under strain, but holds. Orum rarely removes it, since taking it off is equivalent to stripping away his own identity.

### History

The piece belongs to the tradition of the Templars of Vozrador, an order that ceased to exist as a military force more than four decades ago, when [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] founded the Justiciars as a calculated replacement. Orum first wore it at twenty-three years of age, during the ceremony of the temple of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], in the rite that sealed him as the sole Templar of the god who accepted one servant at a time. Preserved for more than seventy-six years, it has endured Vozrador’s disappearance, the order’s military extinction, Orum’s conversion into a justiciar, and decades of imperial service.

It provokes opposing reactions in those who see it. Young acolytes revere it, since most have never seen an intact original suit of Templar armor. In hostile surroundings, some consider it beautiful and others despise it as a profane relic. The silver-colored cuirass is anachronistic among the dark vestments of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|the Cult]].

### Vulnerability

The pre-imperial enchantments withstand time and creatures raised by necromancy. They can, however, be temporarily neutralized by the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/luto_de_nyssis\|Mourning of Nyssis]]: the substance coats the steel and suppresses the metal’s magic. The countermeasure is water, which dissolves the paste and restores the enchantments.
