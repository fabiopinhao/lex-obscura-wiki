# Spina Lexis

The Spina Lexis, the “Column of the Law”, is the judicial scepter carried by each [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. Bestowed during the Anointing that ordains the judge, it is an instrument of interrogation, sentencing, and ritual execution, and amplifies the shadow magic of its wielder.

### Nomenclature

The name works through two images. As an architectural column, the scepter supports the Empire’s judicial structure; as a spinal column, it keeps the body of justice upright. In this reading, each Justiciar is an individual vertebra connected to the greater column, the will of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]].

### Form

It is a short, portable scepter for judicial use, wielded in one hand, and not a staff or long rod. Its length equals that of a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] sword, the standard weapon of shadow lords. Its compact form follows its function: interrogations, ritual executions, and mobility in the field.

### Composition

The core is moonstone extracted from the sacred caves of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], chosen for its resonance with black magic. Bone fragments, relics of the first traitors executed by the Cult, were incorporated into the shaft. Runes engraved in a descending spiral bear passages from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]] in microscopic script.

The moonstone goes dark when the bearer pays the cost to the limit: it loses its pulse, turns opaque, and the scepter slips from the hand that can no longer close around it.

### Binding

During ordination, the scepter is presented to the new Justiciar with the formula “Spina Lexis. Canalem voluntatis. Percute, dirige, sententia.”, which consecrates it as a channel of will and names the triad of its powers. From then on, it responds only to the touch of its designated judge; anyone else who attempts to wield it experiences excruciating pain and, in extreme cases, paralysis.

### Powers

Its powers form a triad. Percute shatters lesser magical protections and leaves permanent marks on the condemned, scars that glow in the presence of other scepters. Dirige compels obedience during interrogations and guides the judge’s hand during ritual executions. Sentencia activates the judicial curses of the Codex and seals pacts of conditional clemency.

### Taboos

It is forbidden to use the scepter against another bearer without a mandate from the Council, channel sentences not provided for in the Codex, or allow it to touch ground consecrated to the gods of light.

### Symbolism

The Cult’s tradition reads the scepter as a tooth: the bite of the law made matter, the canine tooth of justice that pierces, holds, and does not let go until the sentence is carried out.
