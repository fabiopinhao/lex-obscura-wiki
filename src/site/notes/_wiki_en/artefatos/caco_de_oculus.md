# Shard of Oculus

The Shard of Oculus is a fragment of an onyx sphere used as a magical tracker. It turns ice-cold when the target crosses the marked line. [[_wiki_en/personagens/vyrensen\|Vyrensen]], the Collector of Debts, uses these shards with a surety-curse, the *[[_wiki_en/magia/maledictus_fidejussor\|maledictus fidejussor]]*, which binds delivery and secrecy in a pact: whoever fails to comply, the curse makes them pay.

There is a record of a bearer coerced into carrying a shard as a guarantee of delivery and secrecy. The fragment turns ice-cold as it signals that the target has crossed the tracking zone, activating the bearer’s mission.
