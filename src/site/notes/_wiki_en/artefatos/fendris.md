# Fendris

Fendris is a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] weapon from [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]], forged by Luminar elves, now an extinct race, with the original purpose of exterminating dragons and [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]]. It is the only weapon of its kind still in existence in the world.

### Function

It is the only blade capable of piercing Draconid scales, and it creates rifts between planes. Elder dragons recognize it as the weapon of extermination, and the targets against which it is effective include dragons, Draconids, [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]], and their descendants.

### The Hand That Owes Nothing

Fendris works only in hands bound by no pact, according to the principle stated in the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tratado_de_wijmarv\|Treatise of Wijmarv]]: “The blade recognizes no oath. Where there is a pact, there is blindness. Fendris answers only to the hand that owes nothing.” Here, “pact-bound” designates only mortals bonded through [[_wiki_en/magia/pactum_diaboli\|Pactum Diaboli]] or [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]]; anyone bearing a diabolic pact or demonic bond holds only an ordinary dagger, with no power at all. Those connected to [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]], or [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]], as well as users of [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]], can wield it normally.

The restriction was deliberately designed. The Luminar artificers knew that their greatest threats were their renegade cousins: the proto-Druls, who descended to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] while making pacts with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], and the proto-orcs, who bonded themselves to [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]] through brute force. The weapon was forged to reject those hands, and the Draconids, already bonded to the red gods, were excluded by the same rule. Revealed by the [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]] shaman [[_wiki_en/personagens/wijmarv\|Wijmarv]], the limitation discredits the draconic myth, since it demonstrates that Fendris kills Draconids as well as dragons.

### Recent History

The dagger was in Councilor [[_wiki_en/personagens/dessian_broel\|Dessian Broel]]’s private museum, passed into the hands of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]], and was confiscated by [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] after a confrontation. It is the central objective of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/cla_myr_vethan\|Clan Myr’vethan]]: [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]] seeks the dagger to eliminate [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]] and unify [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]]. Fendris awakens spontaneously in the presence of crystal, with no need for a receptacle or verbal command, a phenomenon that contradicts the standard mechanics of forged artifacts: [[_wiki_en/personagens/gorvam_threnk\|Gorvam Threnk]], the Master of the Forges, stated that he had never seen an equivalent piece, which corroborates its Luminar origin. Crystal trapped in a weapon does not awaken it: the dagger remains silent even when pressed against Nidzina’s [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] sword, whose magi is imprisoned in the ribs of the blade. The dagger awakens near loose crystal, offered or carried separately. When it awakens, the hilt turns icy, the ridge of the blade vibrates, and it begins to respond like a living thing; away from crystal, it becomes cold, silent metal again.

### Strategic Importance

Whoever holds Fendris holds the power to eliminate Varna, the elder dragon whose presence maintains the balance of power in Varnak through an individual threat of apocalyptic scale. The dagger is therefore a key piece in the geopolitics of the mountainous region.
