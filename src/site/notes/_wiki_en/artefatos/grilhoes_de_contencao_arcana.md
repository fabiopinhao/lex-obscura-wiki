# Shackles of Arcane Containment

The Shackles of Arcane Containment are restraining chains infused with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] crystals and reinforcing runes, recognizable by their pulsating bluish glow. They originate in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]].

### Function

They suppress magic rather than nullify it, blocking the flow from the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] for those they bind. Straining against them beyond the point of bone fracture does not break them: flesh gives way before metal. They are used in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]] to contain dangerous guests.

### Use

They bind [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/cla_myr_vethan\|Clan Myr’vethan]], who wears them together with a metal muzzle intended to prevent fire breath. [[_wiki_en/personagens/odran\|Odran]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/minotauro\|minotaur]] librarian, once tried to break them out of empirical curiosity, without success.
