# Statuette of Vozrador

The Statuette of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] is a cultic relic of obsidian, a devotional effigy of the imprisoned devil. Its lines recall the illuminations of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/maleficarios\|Maleficários]], the demonological codices compiled in [[_wiki_en/locais/therion\|Therion]] during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], which suggests a pre-imperial origin.

### Properties

Cultic folklore attributes to the statuette the ability to grow warm when near sacred places or divine residue, as if it reacted to the presence of what is holy or what is left of a god. It is a rare object, entrusted only to the chosen.
