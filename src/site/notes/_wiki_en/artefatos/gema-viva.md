# Living Gem

Living gem is the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] term for the eggs of the [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídar]], the giant spider. They have the appearance of a precious gem: they are translucent, with pulsating internal veins. The matrons of the Cathedral Houses prize them as a symbol of power, both ornamental and literal, because carrying a living gem demonstrates a latent capacity for destruction. When the egg hatches, the creature attacks indiscriminately.

### As Contraband Cargo

[[_wiki_en/personagens/tibaut\|Tibaut]] transports two living gems. The first was taken from the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]] caravan; the second, from a destroyed arsenal in the Mountains of the West.
