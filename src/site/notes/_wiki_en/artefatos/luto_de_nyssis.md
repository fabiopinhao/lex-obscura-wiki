# Mourning of Nyssis

The Mourning of Nyssis is an antimagic alchemical substance with an opalescent appearance, distilled from the Nyssis flower.

### Function

It temporarily suppresses [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]], neutralizing enchantments and magical protections for the duration of the effect. Applied to enchanted metal, the pitch coats the piece and silences the magic imbued in it. It has a simple countermeasure: water. Washing away the substance removes the effect.

### Origin and Chemistry

It is distilled from the Nyssis flower, a violet herb that thrives in the plateau’s [[_wiki_en/locais/terras_geladas_do_norte\|northern tundras]]. The extract is precipitated in lilac vitriol and cured with hygroscopic vacuum salt until it forms an opalescent pitch with a bitter almond odor. The flower has become scarce due to overharvesting, which has made the substance more expensive, and less stable counterfeits circulate, staining without fully neutralizing magi.

### The Name

The name is eponymous: the imperial herbarium recorded the species in honor of [[_wiki_en/personagens/nyssira_avaroth\|Nyssira Avaroth]], of the nobility of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], and this is how a tundra herb came to bear the name of an imperial house. In trade and alchemical practice, the distinction between the flower and the pitch has been lost, and “Nyssis” refers to both.
