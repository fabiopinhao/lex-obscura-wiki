# Dwarven Bombard

The Dwarven Bombard is a hybrid firearm developed in the forges of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]] after the conquest of the citadel by the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]]. It arose from forced cooperation between the metallurgical mastery of the subjugated [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]] dwarves and the Cult’s alchemical advances, and is an elite artifact, not a weapon for the masses.

### Construction

The barrel is forged from tempered black steel, an exclusive alloy that combines iron from the deep mines with fragments of volcanic obsidian. The material has a natural affinity for enchantments, which allows the engraved runes to retain their power for extended periods. The combustion chamber is fueled by crystals of compressed [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]], crystallized fragments of alchemical energy that release a controlled explosion when activated by a runic spark. Ignition runes spiraling along the barrel channel the magi, stabilize the explosion, and allow its power to be modulated, so that an experienced operator adjusts the force of the shot according to the situation.

### Ammunition

The weapon accommodates several types of projectiles: explosive orbs, hollow spheres containing alchemical gunpowder that detonate on impact; piercing projectiles, made of reinforced dwarven alloy, that pass through magical protections; corrosive spheres, which spread alchemical acids; signaling ammunition, with bright light for communication or temporary blinding; and containment projectiles, magical nets or paralyzing substances.

### Limitations and Possession

The weight requires a two-handed grip, and the violent recoil can injure inexperienced operators. Accuracy decreases with consecutive shots as the barrel heats up. Magi crystals are expensive, and the weapon is vulnerable to antimagic fields and ethereal interference. Maintenance is constant: runes recalibrated and the mechanism cleaned after each use, to prevent the buildup of alchemical residue. Only [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], high-ranking generals, and regional lords are authorized to carry it, and limited production reinforces its elite status; Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] wields one in combat. The inscription in the workshops of Thandruk Varn reads: “In the forge, iron learns to obey. In the bombard, it teaches the target to do the same.”
