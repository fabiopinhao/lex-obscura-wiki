# Helmet of Maraxus

The Helmet of Maraxus is a relic of the defunct arenas of the [[_wiki_en/locais/dromos_draconis\|Dromos Draconis]], forged from bronze and copper, of exotic design. [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]] wears it during the incursion into [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], completely concealing his face.
