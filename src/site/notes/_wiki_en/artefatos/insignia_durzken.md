# Insignia of Durzken

The Insignia of Durzken, also called the Ring of Durzken or the Matriarch’s Ring, is the ring of authority of matriarch [[_wiki_en/personagens/nylothrae_durzken\|Nylothrae Durzken]] of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/casa_durzken\|House Durzken]]. It is political insignia, not decorative costume jewelry, and a symbol of command over the house.

### Form and Power

The ring may be enchanted or emit [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]]. This possible arcane charge makes it dangerous to carry at the borders of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], where [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] guards inspect visitors for arcane artifacts: displaying a matriarch’s insignia without authorization prompts lethal interrogations.

### Chain of Custody

The ring belonged to matriarch Nylothrae Durzken and was found by [[_wiki_en/personagens/tibaut\|Tibaut]] in the rubble of a cave in the Mountains of the West. A matriarch’s ring is not an object that is simply lost, which suggests that it may have been planted or torn away. Tibaut hid it in a stone crevice in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], covered in sludge and dust, before descending to Ghilanna. Returning it to the matriarch would be a gesture of good faith; keeping it would create a debt or a favor.
