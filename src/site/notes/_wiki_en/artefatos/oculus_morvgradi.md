# Oculus Morvgradi

The Oculus Morvgradi, “Eye of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]],” is a sphere used by the Shadow Cult for remote magical communication. The name reflects both its surveillance function and its umbilical connection to the shadow capital. Receiving an oculus represents absolute trust from the Cult, and the oculus is, at the same time, a tool and a chain: it connects the bearer to the central power and subjects them to constant surveillance.

### Appearance

It is a perfectly polished sphere, the size of a clenched fist. When inactive, it remains opaque and cold to the touch; when activated, silvery veins pulse across its surface. Inside, a “storm,” a miniature vortex of shadow energy, becomes visible through its translucency.

### Construction

It is made of pure onyx, extracted from veins touched by planar energy. Its manufacture includes consecration rituals conducted during lunar eclipses, the inscription of microscopic runes in a spiral across the entire inner surface, and final immersion in enchanted pits of pitch, in the deep chambers of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]].

### Operation

To use it, one places the sphere on a flat, stable surface, traces the symbol of the sentence in the air, and speaks the invocation *“Ut Oculus videat, ut vox transeat, ut presentia resonet”* (“May the eye see, may the voice pass through, may the presence resound”). The ceremony allows two equivalent Latin wordings of this formula, the other being *“Ut Oculus videat, ut vox transit, ut praesentia echoet”*. In a directed call, the invocation can also be spoken in archaic Shadar, naming the recipient. A black flame manifests before the connection opens.

The spheres do not communicate directly with one another, but through a central nexus, the [[_wiki_en/locais/poco_etereo\|Ethereal Well]], a metaphysical construction anchored in the depths of Ghol’Mournak. It functions as a vertical reflecting pool of condensed energy, where all transmissions converge before being redirected. The Council could, in theory, monitor any communication, but in practice the volume makes this impossible.

### Possession and Signature

Possession is not restricted to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]]: all high-ranking shadow lords carry a sphere. What links two communicating parties is a “signature,” a public-private key pair, so that the transmission reaches only the recipient whose key matches the sender’s. The exact workings of this cipher remain deliberately obscure: it is known to bind sender and recipient, but not how. Possession of someone else’s key is therefore a serious breach, since it allows one to reach someone who believed themselves unreachable, breaching the system itself from within. The key establishes a permanent link between the spheres, distinct from the communication channel, which forms only during each call; the energy for the transmission comes from the consecrated artifact itself, rather than from the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] of whoever operates it, which is why even a non-spellcaster can use it.

### Phenomenology

In use, the sphere floats and projects a three-dimensional image that speaks, and drains the surrounding light as it awakens. The connection is slow to stabilize, accompanied by a low sound that is the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] encountering resistance, and when it ends, the image dissolves into silvery fragments. In the Oculus of a [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] necromancer, the signal arrives corrupted and takes on a green tint, and the image emerges from an emerald mist. The external effigy of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] functions as a fixed terminal synchronized with a sphere, a satellite of the same network.

### Limitations

Proximity to fields of wild magic distorts transmissions, and planar storms occasionally interrupt connections. Neglected spheres gradually lose their luminosity until they become simple stones, a process known as “blindness of the eye.” Specific high magic enchantments can sever the Oculus’s links.
