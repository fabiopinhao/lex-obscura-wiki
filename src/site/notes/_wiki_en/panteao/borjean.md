# Borjean

Borjean, the Ascending Oath, is an angel of the pantheon who manifests as a knight in sealed armor mounted on an alicorn that does not touch the ground. Worshipped as Saint Borjean, he is venerated as the protector of the just in battle. His domains are [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]]: the negation of magic and the liberation of the soul from what magic has done to it.

### Appearance

The armor is silver darkened by use, without ornaments or a coat of arms, and shows real wear: metal filed down by friction, rivets replaced with wire patches, one shoulder lower than the other, as if the body inside were leaning on the lance to compensate for the weight. The helm is welded to the gorget and does not open. The alicorn is gray as weathered bone; its horn emits a low vibration that makes the gums of anyone who approaches bleed, and its hooves repeat a gallop that never ends, even while motionless in the air. The right wing extends intact; the left ends in a broken tip that exposes the internal structure, where in place of feathers there are rows of thin blades inscribed in a language no one has deciphered. The fracture has never healed.

### Domain

The knight carries a lance that serves as an instrument of negation: enchantments struck by its tip dissolve into shards of energy. The alicorn bears the horn, an instrument of liberation: oaths imposed through coercion, hereditary curses, and arcane shackles weaken in its proximity and break upon contact. The lance negates what has been done; the horn frees whoever has been bound.

### The Broken Wing

The fracture in the left wing has always been part of his iconography, and it has never been explained why it remains open in a world where healing schools such as [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] exist. Scholars of Dernessal have suggested that the wound is voluntary, proof of an oath that demands permanent sacrifice. Tradition venerates Borjean as an angel who protects the just in battle, but the evidence neither confirms nor disproves this identity: it merely accumulates questions that the knight, if he is a knight, has never answered.

### Manifestation

When someone upholds an oath at great cost, a rhythm of hoofbeats is heard above the roof with no horse in sight, accompanied by the same low vibration that makes the gums tingle.
