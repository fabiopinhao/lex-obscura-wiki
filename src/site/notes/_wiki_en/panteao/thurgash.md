# Thûrgash

Thûrgash, the Devourer of Bastions, is a demonic deity of demolition and siege, aligned with the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]]. He exists to tear down what was built to last, and the title describes his appetite: he consumes what he destroys.

### Manifestation

Thûrgash manifests as a siege giant, a colossal humanoid of interwoven stone and flesh, with a torso as broad as a siege tower and disproportionate arms ending in fists of fractured rock. His skin is living mortar: boulders embedded in exposed muscle, stitched together by veins of dried blood that crack with every movement. His head is too small for his body, sunk between massive shoulders, and his only visible eye burns like a perpetual ember (the other is scarred rubble). Fragments of destroyed walls sprout from his back, embedded in his flesh like absorbed trophies. Each step leaves the impression of a demolished foundation on the ground.

### Domain

From Belli come martial fury, channeled aggression, and blood as fuel, the pack instinct that turns siege into a collective hunt. From Rupto comes the method: the fissure in the wall followed by a chain collapse, the crack that turns a tower into rubble. The combination produces the perfect siege, where the sapper’s patience meets the berserker’s hunger. Where [[_wiki_en/panteao/bal_hadut\|Bal’Hadut]] burns to establish and [[_wiki_en/panteao/tzarakul\|Tzarakul]] breaks through entropy, Thûrgash tears down for the pleasure of watching things fall. Ruin is the end product and the reward.

### Appetite

The title of Devourer of Bastions is descriptive. Thûrgash consumes what he tears down, and the wall fragments embedded in his back are the remains of digested fortresses, each stone absorbed by the god’s body as a permanent record of conquest. His appetite scales with the target’s resistance: huts give way without pleasure, villages bore, cities satisfy for moments, fortresses nourish. The firmer the foundation, the slower the demolition and the more prolonged the gratification. Thûrgash’s bonded share this compulsion: the visceral satisfaction of watching structures crumble and the growing restlessness in the presence of intact walls.

### The Orc Berserkers

Thûrgash is the god to whom many orc berserker warriors bind themselves. The bond operates through [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], the bond of demonic strength, and grants immediate access to Belli’s martial fury, at the price of the corruption that the frenzy exacts: reason dissolved, sanity suspended for as long as the trance lasts. The bond is visible in the body, and during the frenzy the berserker’s veins rise crimson from his neck to his arms. Captain [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]], of [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]]’s guard, is one of these bonded: the frenzy he invokes in ancient [[_wiki_en/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]] channels his bond with Thûrgash. Revering Thûrgash as a god, a common practice among [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], does not imply the bond, which must be earned. The young [[_wiki_en/personagens/mur\|Mur]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/grub\|Grub]] worship him without being bonded and aspire to one day receive the bond and the battle frenzy that the veteran Grothar already possesses.

### The Siege

Campaigns conducted under the invocation of Thûrgash follow a recognizable pattern. The vanguard does not attack the gates: the sappers open fissures at the base of the wall and retreat, and Rupto does the rest. The crack spreads at a speed disproportionate to the initial damage, and the collapse of the first section demoralizes the garrison before the second gives way. Defenders report that the stones seem to want to fall, as if the wall remembered that it had once been a mountain and preferred to become rubble again. The day before, rhythmic blows with no source strike gates and foundations, and newly applied mortar cracks in a straight line, as if something already knew where the wall would give way.
