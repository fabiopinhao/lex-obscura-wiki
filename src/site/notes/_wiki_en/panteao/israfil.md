# Israfil

Israfil, the Herald of the Dawn, is an angel of the luminous tradition whose domain unites healing and light. He manifests as an oliphant with translucent pearl-gray skin, and his restoration works through radiant exposure: wounds close under intense light that cauterizes, sterilizes, and regenerates in the same act.

### Nature

Beneath the herald’s translucent skin runs a network of incandescent veins through which something hotter than blood flows. His tusks are long, made of vitrified bone that emits constant heat; touching them closes wounds at the point of contact and cauterizes the surrounding tissue, leaving a whitish scar. Pale vegetation sprouts beneath each step the herald takes and withers within stilhas when his presence withdraws, a side effect of his restorative power rather than a domain over flora. His trunk branches at the tip into filaments that locate wounds by temperature, converging on the injury before the wounded person notices. When Israfil bellowed, the sound spread like a wave of dry heat that brightened the horizon, and anyone wounded within range felt their cuts burn and contract like the lips of a wound sewn together by an invisible needle.

### Domain

Israfil’s power unites [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]]. The herald heals differently from the devotees of [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], without ritual, without prayer, and without moral judgment of the wounded person’s worthiness: restoration is physical, radiant, indifferent to the soul. Salvo closes the wound; Helio irradiates the exposed tissue with enough intensity to destroy any biological or arcane contaminant through excess energy. The process is effective and brutal, and devotees described the experience as solar fever, heat that begins inside the bones and rises until the skin reddens. The difference between Israfil and [[_wiki_en/panteao/azazel\|Azazel]] is operational: Azazel protects and reveals, Israfil restores and cauterizes. The shield belongs to the Archangel; the suture belongs to the herald.

### The Dawn

Israfil’s bellow ushers in the day. The luminous tradition attributed the origin of the diurnal cycle to the herald, the first sound that separated the original night from the first morning, and the bellow carries Salvo in its very sound frequency. Dawn, in white theology, was the moment when the entire continent received a minimal dose of restoration: wounds healed faster at sunrise, and newborns cried with greater vigor. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/manto_das_sombras\|mantle of shadows]] suppressed the effect without suppressing the phenomenon. The sun continues to rise, and the bellow, if it still echoes, echoes below the threshold of perception. Those who sleep in the open still wake with their shallow cuts closed by the first ray of morning, their skin marked by a whitish scar, and a circle of pale vegetation sprouts around their bodies and withers as soon as the light fully rises. The papyri in the chapel of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]] still preserve the herald’s name alongside those of Azazel and [[_wiki_en/panteao/paralda\|Paralda]].

### The Blessing

Israfil’s blessing is pure Salvo: continuous restoration that keeps the body functional beyond its natural span. Applied through Elarin-Uranic technique, it is a principle antithetical to that of the degradation curses of the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], which corrode bonds and memory. When radiant blessing and Anathic curse meet in the same host, they collide at the ontological level: they are forces that repel each other by nature, and neither is strong enough to overcome the other immediately.
