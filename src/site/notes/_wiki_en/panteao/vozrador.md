# Vozrador

Vozrador, the Captive Lightning, is one of the twelve [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] of the pantheon, whose domains are [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]]. He manifests as a storm-wracked gargoyle and is considered the most complex and the most feared of the twelve. Today he is imprisoned, sealed, and conscious, though weakened.

### Nature

Vozrador manifests as a storm-wracked gargoyle: a body of living black stone, wings of torn membrane that crackle with static discharges, eyes that pulse like lightning imprisoned in obsidian. The storm that surrounds him functions as an extension of his will. Even imprisoned, his form persists in the imagery of the Cult, and the twelfth statue on the ceiling of the Shadow [[_wiki_en/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]] still represents him.

### Manifestation

Vozrador’s most recognizable sign is a dry storm: heavy clouds with lightning burning trapped within them, never descending to the ground.

### Domain

Electro granted him instant judgment, the lightning bolt that neither negotiates nor warns; the shadows served as a sensory network, perception that crossed planes without occupying space. Where other devils contested territories or demanded expansion, Vozrador pursued supremacy in his domains of interest, following the philosophy that control over the soul matters more than dominion over the world. The combination made him the most feared devil among the twelve.

### The Sole Templar

Vozrador maintained many pact holders, but accepted only one Templar at a time. The Templar occupies a position apart among them: he receives the entirety of the bond, undiluted, and serves as the devil’s representative on the material plane, which explains the restriction to one at a time. In return, the loss of a Templar left him without a representative, in a slow and selective cycle of choosing. The pact’s curse applied to all pact holders, was permanent, and outlived the god himself: none of them can set foot on consecrated alabastrine ground without their skin darkening and blistering. The clause is eternal.

### The Pact and the Silence

Making a pact with Vozrador never involved speaking with him. The bond is established without a voice: the devil chooses, the pact seals, and the pact holder serves without receiving any direct word. [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], his last Templar, never had a channel of any kind, and what he knew of the god was the power, the demand, and the curse. Direct communication with the black gods is the privilege of very few, among them temple leaders, and does not arise from any pact. Nor does Vozrador need an object to bind or collect his due: the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/estatueta_de_vozrador\|obsidian statuette]] that bears his name is a devotional relic, and no Templar ever received one.

### Agreement with the Titans

Vozrador made an agreement with [[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]] and [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]] that was unthinkable to the rest of the pantheon: the protection of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] in exchange for concessions that consolidated his power among the devils. Morlgrund wanted the forest intact; Titânia, her devotees protected; for Vozrador, the prize was a domain that no other devil would dare contest. The source of his power lay in the forest itself.

### Imprisonment

Vozrador was attacked on his own plane of existence and sealed approximately fifty-five years ago. He remains conscious and intact, though weakened. His temple in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] is abandoned. The original [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]] was written in his blood.
