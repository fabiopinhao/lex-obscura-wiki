# Eiryndh

Eiryndh, the Boreal Shield, is a deity of the sidereal pantheon who manifests as an Ordeal Valkyrie. She governs the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]], and offers conditional protection: the shield interposes itself for those who face the threat head-on and abandons those who retreat.

### Nature

She manifests as a warrior of human proportions whose flesh is translucent like frosted glass. Beneath the surface there is neither muscle nor bone, only atmosphere compressed into layers of varying density, visible like strata of hot air over sunlit stone. Fissures run across her torso and arms. They are not battle scars: each records a moment when someone under her protection fled, and the blow the shield would have absorbed struck the Valkyrie herself. The protected person survived, the crack remained. Some are as fine as a hair, others cut across the body from shoulder to hip, and none have healed. Eiryndh carries no weapon. The shield is the only piece: round, the diameter of a torso, with bands of blue-green and white that ripple like the aurora borealis. The air around her is thin like that of a mountain peak, breathing grows shallow, and sounds arrive muffled. Her feet do not touch the ground, and the distance to the ground never varies.

### Manifestation

A subtler sign is also attributed to her: above a siege where those under her protection hold out, blue-green bands ripple across the sky outside the latitude where the aurora should appear, and the air thins as on a mountaintop, even in open terrain.

### Domain

Eiryndh’s shield does not block the blow: it removes whoever is behind it. When the protection activates, the protected person’s body experiences an ascending vertigo, the ground seems to give way, the air thins, and the threat passes through the space they occupied an instant before. The protected person has not moved; the plane in which the blow exists has ceased to coincide with the plane in which they are. Aegis raises the barrier, Urano shifts the protected person above it. The protection responds to courage. Those who face the threat feel the shield interpose itself; those who flee discover that the air regains its normal density and the blow strikes them without mediation. The Valkyrie neither assesses merit nor demands prayer. She asks only one thing: is the protected person willing to hold their gaze in the face of what is coming? If the answer is no, the shield is not there. The criterion is courage, distinct from the faith of [[_wiki_en/panteao/azazel\|Azazel]], the justice of [[_wiki_en/panteao/borjean\|Borjean]], and the consecration of [[_wiki_en/panteao/yelvanna\|Yelvanna]].

### The Shield

Where Eiryndh’s protection manifests, the sky changes. The sense of direction never fully stabilizes again: the body insists on knowing where up is with a precision that did not exist before, as though it had learned a coordinate it cannot forget. Some begin to wake with the impression that the ceiling is lower than it was the night before. Others avoid underground places for the rest of their lives, because the distance between the body and the sky has become unbearable to feel. The shield itself is aurora compressed into a portable disk. Seen up close, the light within arranges itself in layers, each with a distinct density and hue. One theological hypothesis holds that each layer corresponds to a witnessed act of courage, compressed into a luminous stratum like geological sediment. No one has found the deepest layer.
