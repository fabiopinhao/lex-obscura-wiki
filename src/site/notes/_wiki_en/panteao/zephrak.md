# Zephrak

Zephrak, the Merciless Breath, is an angelic deity of consecrated wind, aligned with the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]]. His breath acts as a sieve: the air separates those who carry corruption from those who do not, and the mercilessness of the title denotes not cruelty, but a complete lack of regard for context.

### Manifestation

Zephrak manifests as an emaciated [[_wiki_en/criaturas/grifo\|griffin]], with the body of a dried-out bird of prey: visible ribs beneath stretched skin, sparse fur interspersed with patches of wind-burned hide, sinewy musculature without any fat reserves. The feathers are thin blades of petrified keratin that vibrate continuously and detach easily, and each feather torn free becomes a cutting projectile that the wind itself directs. The head combines a bird of prey’s beak with a feline jaw; the beak is long and serrated along its inner edges, and the air that enters through the throat emerges transformed, saturated with Sacro. In place of eyes, horizontal slits as narrow as gills cut across the sides of the skull, and the wind whistles through them without ceasing. Zephrak does not see: he tracks disturbances in the airflow, and the slits contract when his breath detects corruption in the field.

A subtler sign is also attributed to him: a cutting gust that tears away only the banner, insignia, or garment of someone who carries the taint, without touching anything else nearby, followed by a thin whistle in the air that a moment earlier was still.

### Domain

Zephrak’s breath is consecrated wind that separates. Arcane corruption, active curses, demonic bonds, and profanation of any origin cause friction against the air, and the friction generates heat, and the heat generates pain proportional to the severity of the taint. The unblemished believer feels only a cold breeze; the pact-bound person feels the wind scrape the skin like coarse sandpaper; the committed desecrator feels the air enter the lungs and burn from within. The discrimination is automatic and indifferent to intent, without appeal, confession, or mitigating circumstance. The wind does not listen. The wind separates. The angel who blows the sacred gale over a field of innocents and guilty alike does not deliberately protect the innocents: they survive because the wind does not find in them what it needs to cut.

### The Breath

In military campaigns, invoking Zephrak amounts to declaring a purge without prior judgment. Commanders who authorize the prayer know that the wind will sweep the entire field, and that the separation between allies and enemies will depend exclusively on the spiritual state of each individual present. Soldiers with minor curses bleed; soldiers who carry profane amulets lose the skin of the hands holding them. The prayer is rare because no army survives a complete and simultaneous spiritual audit of all its members. The result made Zephrak an angel of last resort, invoked only when the field is already lost and a total purge is preferable to a tainted defeat.
