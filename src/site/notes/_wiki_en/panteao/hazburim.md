# Hazburim

Hazburim, the Lord of Flies, is a deity in the pantheon of Lex Obscura, classified as a demon-titan. He brings together the domains of [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] in a theology of parasitic warfare, and his cult is proscribed in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] under the [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]].

### Nature

He manifests as a larval insectoid: a segmented body of black, translucent chitin, beneath which larvae move in continuous waves. The torso is broad and hunched, articulated like the abdomen of a colossal wasp, supported by six unequal limbs that alternate between legs and almost human arms. The head is a cluster of compound eyes in asymmetrical sockets, each facet reflecting a different image of the world. From the mouth, a bundle of mandibles and proboscises retracts and extends in feeding cycles that never cease. The wings exist only as a suggestion: clouds of flies maintain a tight formation around the shoulders, forming the silhouette of membranes when seen from a distance. When heard for too long, the buzzing almost forms words.

### Domain

Belli governs the fury of the swarm, the distributed intelligence of thousands acting as a single organism. Morpho governs transformation: invaded flesh and rewritten tissue, biological identity replaced from the inside out. The combination produces parasitic warfare: the swarm penetrates, the larvae consume, and what emerges from the cocoon already belongs to the hive. The metamorphosis is permanent and verifiable, unlike the illusion of [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], and Belli’s martial drive ensures that each transformed body becomes a soldier in an army that grows by feeding. The title of Lord of Flies is metonymy: the flies serve as scouts, the larvae as an army.

### Proscription

There is an additional reason for Hazburim’s proscription beyond the general Vinculum Daemonis, and it lies in the silent nature of Morpho’s metamorphosis. The bonded of [[_wiki_en/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]] give off heat and the stench of sulfur; those bonded to [[_wiki_en/panteao/tzarakul\|Tzarakul]] exhibit visible fissures in their skin, veins of black mineral sprouting from their flesh. With Hazburim, the change is internal. The larvae lodge beneath the dermis without breaking the surface, the organs reorganize without external symptoms, and compound eyes grow behind the human eyes. When the transformation becomes visible, the host is already part of the swarm. A bonded infiltrator poses a biological threat that no conventional inspection detects.

### Manifestation

Hazburim reaches the world through consecrated vessels, sustained by periodic offerings of blood. One of the altars he uses is a legacy of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]]: a window between planes that belongs to no deity. At its center rests a skull carved with flies, surrounded by ampoules of blood arranged in an inverted pentagram.
