# Geldrion

Geldrion, the Glacial Apogee, is a sidereal deity of the background pantheon, described as a stellar colossus. Its domain unites the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]], and its passage brings matter and space to the same state of cessation.

### Nature

From afar, Geldrion justifies the name: a silhouette of astronomical proportions outlined against the Ethereal, with sharp contours and a vaguely humanoid shape. Up close, the surface reveals itself as the boundary between functioning space and space at rest. The limbs are topological features of this discontinuity, regions where the boundary between movement and cessation forms curves that the eye interprets as anatomy. Whoever crosses the threshold perceives the transition in layers: first silence, then the draining of colors, from blue to a muted red that fades away, until the temperature does not fall gradually, but ends.

### Manifestation

A subtler sign is also attributed to it, the glacial quiet: one night, sound stops propagating through the valley before the colors drain away, and at dawn frost covers the ground in perfect geometries.

### Domain

Geldrion drains heat and gravitational curvature in a single operation. The ice it produces requires no water, as it crystallizes space itself. The affected regions exhibit what ethereal cartographers call dead geometry, areas where trajectories become absolute straight lines because curvature has frozen along with temperature. Cosmo determines the scale, and Cryo carries out the cessation. Living beings that enter dead geometry do not die, because dying requires biological processes and processes require movement. They remain.

### The Apogee

Navigators of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] use the term for regions where Geldrion remained long enough for local space to lose its elasticity for good. In these zones, restoring movement costs more energy than the region contained before freezing, and this excess grows with each attempt. In one of them, expedition members found the remnants of an interrupted [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/canal_magi\|magi channel]], with each beam locked on an escape trajectory that no force had sustained for centuries. [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|Sidereal]] scholars treat the phenomenon as evidence that absolute zero functions as a drain, an irreversible point of drainage.
