# Sulkavrim

Sulkavrim, the Everlasting Wall, is one of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], a defensive deity that raises stone barriers impervious to physical force and magic. It manifests as a colossal serpent of living marble, and its domain combines the negation of hostile enchantments with matter raised from beneath the ground: what Sulkavrim encloses remains enclosed.

### Nature

The serpent’s scales are individual blocks of white stone veined with gray that fit together with the precision of stonework, without mortar and without gaps. Its body moves with mineral slowness, each movement producing the deep sound of slabs dragged over slabs. It has no eyes, and its head is a smooth dome of polished limestone, featureless and without an opening. It senses the world through vibrations transmitted through soil and rock, and responds to disturbances with the same inevitability with which a mountain responds to erosion: slowly, but completely. Where its body passes, the ground becomes smooth and hard, and grooves from its scales remain imprinted in the stone like bas-reliefs that no chisel produced.

### Domain

Sulkavrim’s power unites [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]], which cancels magic upon contact with the surface and dissipates enchantments before they penetrate the outer layer, with [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]], which supplies the matter of the barrier: rock extracted from beneath the ground, compacted and raised by the pressure of the moving body. The combination is defensive by nature. The serpent does not attack and does not pursue; it encircles. What triggers the encirclement remains uncertain, as Sulkavrim’s walls have been found around strategic mountain passes, but also around isolated wells, clearings with nothing inside, and cliffs of no apparent value, following no military or commercial logic. What the serpent encircles with its coils remains contained within a wall that rejects brute force through the weight of the stone and arcane force through the negation inscribed in each block: those who try to knock it down with hammers encounter marble, and those who try to dispel it with spells encounter silence.

### The Wall

The epithet is a trace and a legacy. When Sulkavrim coils around something and then moves away, its outer coils remain as a petrified structure. Mountain passes have been closed by a single circuit of its body, and entire valleys exist within circular walls that no one built and that no army has managed to bring down. The stone does not erode, and rupture spells dissipate upon touching the surface, returned to silence by the layer of negation that Sulkavrim imprinted in the rock before departing. Ancient cartographers recorded these formations as natural features until they noticed the pattern: circular, of veined white marble, impervious to every arcane school without exception. What Sulkavrim once protected remains protected, and no record documents one of its walls being brought down.

### Manifestation

When someone forces a breach in the wall, the damage does not remain. At the following dawn, the fissure appears sealed by veins of marble that no mason laid, arranged in bas-relief that repeats the pattern of scales on the serpent’s body.
