# Naelkur

Naelkur, the Fecund Grave, is a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devil]] of the pantheon, sovereign of biological death. His divine form is the Rooted Revenant, and his domain combines the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]]. For Naelkur, death is not an end, but a planting season: he governs what happens after the body falls, the decomposition of flesh, and what is born from it.

### Manifestation

Naelkur appears as a colossal corpse in an advanced state of decomposition, pierced by black roots that sprout from within the bones and spread in every direction. Luminescent fungi colonize the open cavities of the torso, and the remaining flesh serves as a substrate for dark mosses and lichens that contract and expand in their own rhythm, like plant respiration. Naelkur does not rot: he has already rotted, and what grew over the remains has become the entity. The surrounding air is thick, sweet with ripe decay, the smell the earth gives off when it accepts a body and begins to digest it. The soil beneath the divine form blackens and softens, saturated with nutrients that should not exist outside an open grave. Following recent deaths, the most recognizable sign is an out-of-season bloom: mushrooms sprout in a perfect ring over the grave, a phenomenon known as Naelkur’s ring.

### Domain

Patho dissolves tissue and liquefies organs until only pure nutrients remain; Necro recycles the remains, turning what decomposes into material for new undead, which feed the next generation as they rot. The distinction from other devils who touch upon death is functional: where pure Necro preserves or reanimates, Naelkur requires the body to break down first. An undead creature of Naelkur is not an animated corpse, but what sprouted from the corpse after Patho finished its work. The philosophy is agrarian: destroying the seed prevents the harvest, and wasted death is the only blasphemy Naelkur recognizes.

### The Cultivation

Naelkur’s undead are a crop, not servants. Bodies deposited under the right conditions, left for the necessary time, germinate without command. The Fecund Grave is both title and method: where there is accumulated death and receptive earth, Naelkur harvests. The quality of the crop depends on the substrate. Bodies of those killed by disease produce fragile undead; bodies of those killed by violence, with viable tissue, generate resilient forms, and battlefields are the most fertile grounds. The combination of Patho and Necro at scale transforms the terrain into an exclusion zone, where Reap kills what remains alive and Contagion prevents anything new from growing. Necromancers trained in both schools are the scorched-earth agents of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]. Among those who serve him are the necromancers of the Ossuary of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], such as [[_wiki_en/personagens/qaelun\|Qaelun]], who raise legions in large numbers from the harvest of death, without refinement.

### The Pact

The individual bound by a pact with Naelkur receives accelerated advancement in Patho and Necro. The price takes hold through two paths that converge on the same destination. Patho opens the eyes to entropy: everything seems to be actively decomposing, and beauty provokes anticipatory melancholy over its end. Necro closes the eyes to pity: corpses cease to be people who once were and become working material. The pact-bound individual ends up seeing the world as Naelkur sees it, in constant decomposition, and this does not trouble them. They do not suffer from the deaths of others. They suffer from the delay.
