# Shaevan

Shaevan, the Plague Spinner, is the only devil-titan among the twelve [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] of the pantheon, and her divine form is the Pestilent Tarantula. Her domain combines the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]], and she is the only deity who weaves diseases like deliberate webs: where other devils operate on bodies, Shaevan operates on entire populations.

### Manifestation

Shaevan appears as a colossal tarantula of black chitin, with jointed legs ending in hollow claws through which living venom flows. Her abdomen, distended and translucent, pulses with fluids in perpetual decomposition, and what ferments within never dries, never stops producing. Filaments of solidified disease emerge from her spinnerets, each thread carrying a different strain of pestilence. Her entire body exudes a damp miasma that rots organic matter through proximity, and her legs produce an arrhythmic clicking; wherever they tread, the soil develops a film of mold that spreads for hours. Nearby rodents collapse with clouded eyes, and trees shed bark in damp strips, like skin peeling from a burn.

A subtler sign is also attributed to her: a film of mold spreads in a radial pattern, like a web, across a surface that was sound hours earlier, and nearby rodents collapse with clouded eyes at the edges of the pattern.

### Domain

Shaevan operates at the intersection of poisonous substance and biological degradation. Venom and neurotoxin come from Toxo, which dulls and corrodes; Patho converts the damage into a process, fever that spreads, putrefaction that turns healthy flesh into fertile ground for contagion. Her plagues begin in a single host and end in entire populations, each victim connected to the next by the same invisible thread of disease. The combination produces poisons that replicate: on its own, Toxo requires direct application and Patho operates slowly, but the fusion makes venom contagious and disease lethal.

### The Spinning

Shaevan does not release plagues: she spins them. Each epidemic is a web with its own architecture, with calculated points of origin and vectors of transmission. The spider’s patience is literal. Shaevan places the first thread in a victim and waits for contagion to weave the others on its own, for intervening more than necessary is wasteful, and a good plague, like a good web, sustains itself once the structure is in place. The pact-bound reproduce this method: they identify the node of a network, the itinerant merchant, the village preacher, and place the first thread within that person. Spinning requires patience that military urgency does not tolerate; in the open field, Shaevan’s servants lose their function, but in prolonged campaigns of attrition none of the other pact-bound can replace them.

### Position in the Pantheon

The designation of devil-titan indicates a scale of operation beyond individual intervention, and that scale breeds distrust, because uncontrolled plagues do not distinguish between servants and enemies. Shaevan’s response, according to her devotees, is that disease always distinguishes; the problem is that mortals do not understand the criteria.

### Philosophy

For Shaevan, disease is the only truth. Every organism carries within itself the conditions of its own decomposition, and plague merely accelerates the inevitable. Health is an illusion of stability that ignores the parasites and processes of putrefaction already operating in silence. To spin an epidemic is to reveal what flesh has always been: a temporary host.
