# Nocthydral

Nocthydral, the Unfathomable Pupil, is a sidereal deity that takes the form of a caliginous kraken. Its domain encompasses the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], and in it space and information alike tend toward the unfathomable.

### Manifestation

Nocthydral appears as a lidless eye suspended in a gravitational void. What observers take for tentacles are spatial distortions radiating from the center: curved corridors where light bends and the sense of scale dissolves. From afar, these distortions form the silhouette of a colossal kraken; up close, the body disappears and only the pupil remains, a disk of darkness too dense to be shadow, surrounded by a ring of compressed light that pulses at low frequencies. The iris is a band of information in motion, data visible as luminous filaments that orbit the center in layers, each rotating in the opposite direction from the preceding one. The kraken does not swim: it persists. The space around it curves to accommodate it, and each step toward the eye increases the distance instead of reducing it.

A subtler sign is also attributed to it: on moonless nights, a circle of stars disappears from the sky, tracing the outline of a black pupil open in the firmament, and those who notice it afterward carry the certainty of being watched, a certainty that does not go away.

### Domain

The gravitational field around the pupil compresses knowledge into bands of increasing density, like sediment made of information instead of rock. Perception expands as the observer penetrates the bands. The first reveals local facts; the second makes hidden connections between distant events legible; the third encompasses entire generations in patterns that no mortal mind would construct through deduction. The subsequent bands have not been documented, because no mortal has returned from them capable of recounting the experience. The cost of deep knowledge is a proportional loss of reference: those who see too far lose the ability to see up close, and everyday details become incomprehensible to minds that have processed cosmic scale.

### The Pupil

Looking into Nocthydral’s center is equivalent to falling. The pupil functions as a gravitational well for attention: once the gaze is fixed, the ability to avert it diminishes as exposure accumulates. Each filament of information understood pulls the observer half a step deeper into the field, expanding the frame of reference and shrinking everything that fit within the previous one. Survivors describe the return as relearning how to see rooms and faces that once had a familiar size and that, after the pupil, seem diminished, too small to contain meaning.
