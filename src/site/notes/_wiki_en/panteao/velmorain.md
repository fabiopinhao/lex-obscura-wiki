# Velmorain

Velmorain, the Cosmic Veil, is a deity of the devil-sidereal category who manifests as a mitered mummy. His domains are [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], and his power operates at the intersection of space and illusion.

### Nature

Velmorain’s body appears wrapped in cosmic bands that undulate like fabric submerged in dark water, crowned with a miter of cold spectral light. The bands do not touch the body: they float millimeters from its surface, and between them there is neither flesh nor bone, only a star field, points of light that blink so slowly they seem fixed until they are not. The miter emits a radiance that neither warms nor illuminates the ground, and illuminates only the bands, light that exists only for itself. There is no face beneath the bands, only the suggestion of a form that may never have existed. Anyone who stays nearby feels the distance between their own bones increase, as though the space inside their body had become larger than it should be.

### Domain

Cosmo sustains the structure, folding the space around an arrangement until it occupies a verifiable place; Spectro fills that structure with apparent substance, clauses, penalties, and guarantees that respond to touch as though they were solid. His arrangements seem like laws, but they are veils: spectral constructions sustained by cosmic will. Those who negotiate with Velmorain believe they are entering into pacts when, in practice, they inhabit an architecture of appearances that the god can withdraw at any moment. The devil-sidereal category reflects this duality, as Velmorain shares the ambition of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] without sharing their method: where [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]] dominates through shadow and [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] through control of the soul, Velmorain dominates through the setting, altering the stage until the actors take the rules of the performance for laws of the world.

His devotees also call him Lord of the Perihelion and of the Silent Vacuums: he governs vectors, orbits, and tides of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] between celestial bodies. His cult, austere and mathematical, survives among astronomers and cartographers of the ether.

### Rivalry with Vozrador

Velmorain and Vozrador competed over domains such as [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] before Vozrador’s imprisonment. The alliance with Malagoth against Vozrador was motivated by revenge: Vozrador maintained an agreement with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] that Velmorain never managed to replicate. Velmorain sustains Malagoth’s curse on Vozrador, keeping it strong and active through cosmic-spectral reinforcement.
