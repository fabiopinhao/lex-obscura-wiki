# Wargûleth

Wargûleth, the guardian of the Pit, is a deity of the devil-demon category associated with the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]]. He manifests as a three-headed dog with bristling black fur, each head fixed in a distinct expression of fury, and governs the domain of isolation: the state in which someone loses every tie to the surrounding world.

### Manifestation

Wargûleth’s form is that of an enraged cerberus. One head growls incessantly, gums drawn back over fangs that drip dark saliva; another keeps its eyes shut, sniffing the air and tracking what only it perceives; the third watches with dilated, motionless pupils, without blinking. The body is muscular and taut, as though poised to leap for millennia without the leap ever coming. Iron chains embedded in the flesh bind the three heads together and force them to act in unison: when one lunges forward, the others are dragged along.

A subtler sign is also attributed to him: when a forbidden boundary is crossed, three identical barks sound in the distance where there are no dogs, chains seem to drag beneath the ground, and real dogs refuse to cross the threshold.

### Domain

Wargûleth operates at the intersection of curse and fear. Anatho corrodes the target’s bonds, and loyalties rot from the inside out; Phobos confirms the corrosion as a threat, making the target see hostility in every gesture. The dual classification reflects the method: Anatho operates through the diabolical repertoire, methodical and corrosive, while Phobos responds to the demonic repertoire, visceral and immediate. The combination produces isolation at two speeds, the curse slowly and fear quickly, and the synergy is total isolation: the target believes they are alone and ends up truly alone.

### Seals

The Anatho sealings anchored in his name emanate dry, constant heat and burn the hand that forces them. The burned hand is the least of the harm: anyone who forces one of his seals is returned to the moment when they were most alone, and remains there for as long as the seal allows. A variant of these sealings is taught in [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]].

### The Pit

The Pit that Wargûleth guards is not a prison, but the state of confinement itself, and it takes hold in stages. First comes fear, the distrust that makes the target hesitate before asking for help. Then comes the curse, the misfortune that turns hesitation into justification: friends summoned do not appear, favors called in are lost in accidents that seem coincidental. By the time the target realizes they are isolated, the Pit has already closed. Wargûleth sits at the edge and keeps watch, one head sniffing out escape attempts, another growling to deter anyone who approaches, the third merely watching. Entering the Pit requires no bars: losing the last tie is enough.

### The Vulfans

In [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] cosmogony, Wargûleth is the creator of the [[_wiki_en/racas/vulfano\|Vulfans]]. In the primordial caverns of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], the cerberus fused human and wild canine blood to produce soldiers of the black [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/cor_arcana\|arcane color]]. The [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worgs]] were the intended product, sentient canine bipeds made for the threshold between man and beast. The [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]] were the mistake: material contaminated by cave rodents gave rise to scrawny creatures, which Wargûleth never corrected. The worgs inherited the form; the Murans inherited Phobos, the school of fear, and the cognitive dissolution that comes with it.
