# Lyldraeth

Lyldraeth, the Howling Prayer, is a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereal]] entity that manifests as an instrument rather than a figure. Its domain combines [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]], protection, with [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], partial displacement into the Ethereal. Protection operates through frequency: the scream exists between planes, and what crosses the boundary acts as a barrier.

### Nature

It manifests as two columns of corroded ivory that rise parallel to each other, twisted by internal pressure, scored with dark fissures like veins of carvite. In the gap between them, translucent membranes stretch in overlapping layers like vocal folds on an impossible scale, vibrating without visible stimulus, responding to something on the other side. The vibration produces a continuous sound at a frequency the mortal ear registers as pressure before registering it as a note. Those who approach feel this pressure first in the sternum, a note without a source vibrating inside the chest, before their ears recognize it as sound. The ivory bears devotional markings, repetitive grooves arranged like prayers engraved through persistence, which no one carved and which grew with the structure. At the center, a dark vertical slit cuts through every layer, and through it passes air from another plane, cold and smelling of burned incense, residue of liturgy that no one celebrated on the material plane.

### Domain

The scream propagates on the material plane and in the Ethereal simultaneously, and the portion that returns arrives out of phase. The result is interference: where the two waves meet, a zone of cancellation emerges that rejects matter and intent with the same indifference. Projectiles lose their trajectory, spells dissipate like an echo that has encountered a wall. The barrier is acoustic, persists as long as the frequency is sustained, and the frequency is sustained as long as the prayer continues. The content of the prayer is indecipherable to mortals; transcribed fragments reveal a coherent liturgical structure, addressed to entities that no sidereal record catalogs. The sacrilege that gives the banshee its name lies in the possibility that the recipient exists and refuses to answer, or that it has never existed, and the devotion sustaining the barrier is directed toward [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|the Void]].

### The Prayer

The phenomenon persists after Lyldraeth moves. Zones where the scream resonated retain what sidereal archivists call “liturgical residue”, a low vibration that the bones of the skull detect before the ears do. The cadence of the howling prayer overlays any subsequent liturgy, like plainchant seeping through the wall of a neighboring temple. In the most affected temples, the marble of the altars darkens in thin veins where the celebrant takes their place, as if the prayer were corroding the consecration from within. Hierophants of various orders have documented worshipers interrupting rituals for no apparent reason, seized by the urge to listen to something they could not identify. The more sincere the mortal’s prayer, the clearer the interference, as if the vibration recognized its intensity and parasitized it. Theologians debate whether the residue is contamination or competition.
