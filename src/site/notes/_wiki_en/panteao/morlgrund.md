# Morlgrund

Morlgrund, the Bone of the World, is the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titan]] guardian of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], whose domain combines [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]], the substance of earth and stone, with [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]], which reconfigures the inorganic into new forms. Where other titans cultivate life or poison, Morlgrund reconfigures the very foundation on which everything rests.

### Nature

He manifests as a golem of stone and living earth. Stones gravitate toward a central point and collide until they fuse together, and the ground opens in circular wounds from which black earth gushes. Uprooted roots weave themselves into tendons. His legs are basalt columns, his torso is made of plates that shift like tectonics in miniature, four arms end in suspended avalanches, and his head has no face, only fissures that burn with the heat of subterranean furnaces. The name vibrates in the marrow of the world without being spoken by any mouth.

### Domain

Geo gives Morlgrund dominion over tremors, tectonic walls, and the weight of rock in all its forms; Morpho transforms the inorganic into a body, reconfiguring gravel into tendon and sediment into geological flesh. The avatar is the ultimate expression of this convergence, and each manifestation demands a sacrifice from the domain itself: millennia-old trees fall, roots break, the ground opens. The guardian destroys what he protects to give himself form.

### The Guardian

Thal’Serin endures because Morlgrund protects it, and that protection is not free. [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] made an agreement with Morlgrund and [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]]: the forest would remain inviolable as long as Vozrador maintained supremacy over [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]], the school of putrefaction. The pact worked through a convergence of interests, since Morlgrund wanted the forest intact and Vozrador wanted a domain that no other [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devil]] dared contest. A titan negotiating with a devil was unthinkable to most of the pantheon, but green philosophy operates through survival, not purism. Vozrador’s imprisonment destabilized this balance.

### The Forbidden Name

Among [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], no one utters “Morlgrund”. The prohibition reflects scale: Morlgrund predates worship and predates the words mortals invented to describe the divine, and naming him is invoking something that belongs to a category predating organized religion. The theophany is selective, and what each witness perceives depends on the deity in question and the way he manifests: the form condensed into stone and earth is seen by those before it, while the voice reaches only the one whom the titan addresses.

### Manifestation

When the forest is wounded, the name vibrates: a low tremor, felt in the bones and never heard, passes through those nearby.

### The Symbiosis

“Green does not destroy black when it depends on it for contrast.” The relationship between Morlgrund and Vozrador is not an alliance, but ecological necessity transposed to the scale of the divine: nature that survives in the shadows needs the shadow that feeds on nature. Removing either term causes both to collapse.
