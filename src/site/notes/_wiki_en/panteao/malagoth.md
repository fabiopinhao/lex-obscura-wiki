# Malagoth

Malagoth, the Thorn Crown, is a central deity of the diabolical pantheon who manifests as the Studded Vampire. He governs the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], and is the only god with a vision of total domination of the material plane through shadows.

### Nature

He manifests as a pale, elongated body, pierced by obsidian thorns that sprout from within the flesh, crowned with a wreath of black thorns that bleeds without ever drying. The thorns are extensions of solidified curse, and each carries a different Anatho. The blood flowing from the wreath slides over the skin and spreads into dark filaments that seem to seek nearby surfaces, like roots seeking soil. Near Malagoth, old wounds ache again, scars itch, and the body remembers damage the mind had already discarded. He is reputed to be among the most treacherous of the diabolical pantheon.

### Domain

Where other [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] negotiate influence or contest specific domains, Malagoth wants everything. The darkness he projects functions as territorial occupation: Umbra takes the space, Anatho subjugates those who inhabit it. The shadow claims what it covers, and the curse that embeds itself in the target turns the victim into possessed territory. His powers of cursing are the strongest in the entire pantheon, and the Thorn Crown is both title and method: the curses he imposes embed themselves in the target and do not come loose. The mechanism is continuous degradation, Anatho eroding bond and memory in a slow cycle until the marked one no longer remembers what they were before the curse. They do not die immediately. They dissolve.

### Imprisonment of Vozrador

Malagoth attacked [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] by surprise on his own plane of existence. The attack would not have sufficed on its own: an alliance with [[_wiki_en/panteao/velmorain\|Velmorain]], who sustains the imprisonment through cosmic-spectral reinforcement, was necessary. Their motivations differed. Velmorain acted out of vengeance, because Vozrador maintained an agreement with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] that Velmorain never managed to replicate; Malagoth acted out of territorial calculation, since the same agreement limited the expansion of shadow. With Vozrador sealed, the obstacle disappeared. The consequence was immediate: [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] lost the divine protection that kept other devils at a distance, and the border Malagoth coveted most opened.

### Manifestation

When Malagoth watches, old scars begin to ache again all at once, and the shadow cast by whoever he watches briefly takes on the outline of thorns. In any place where a pact was sealed in his name, a bloodstain remains and never dries.
