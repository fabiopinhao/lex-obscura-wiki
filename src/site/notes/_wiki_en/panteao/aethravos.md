# Aethravos

Aethravos, the Vitreous Breath, is a sidereal deity of the background pantheon, manifest as a nacreous wyvern. Its domain combines the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], and its presence transforms matter through sublimation: the solid turns to vapor without an intermediate stage.

### Nature

Aethravos manifests as a winged structure of overlapping nacre blades, each layer thin enough to be translucent and thick enough to refract light into shades that oscillate between violet and a nameless color. Its body is hollow; striking it produces a resonance that is not heard but felt, as a vibration in the bones of the skull. Its wings are mineral accretions formed over ages of atmospheric crystallization in the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], with neither joints nor membranes. When the deity moves, the blades rub against one another and the surrounding air vitrifies: the trail it leaves is atmospheric glass, air solidified into permanent transparency.

### Domain

Aethravos’s cold is selective. That which accumulates disorder receives crystallization; that which is already simple remains intact. A wooden board exposed to its cold sublimates, while the iron nails within it remain suspended in the air, in the exact positions they occupied. The direction of the process comes from Urano and its state, from Cryo; together they produce elevation through freezing. Bodies touched become translucent, and complete sublimation leaves no corpse, only a vitreous outline where someone once stood, which the wind of the Ethereal may carry away.

### The Breath

Where Aethravos breathes, the air solidifies into glass that preserves its shape perfectly and blocks all transmission, as neither heat nor sound passes through it. The regions where the deity has breathed retain corridors of atmospheric glass: some shatter at a touch, others are thick enough to entomb an entire fortress. The [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]] describe these corridors as solidified transcendence. Smaller fragments of this glass also appear in isolation, far from any corridor: found on peaks and in ravines, iridescent blades like the inside of a shell, cold as something that has never been alive.
