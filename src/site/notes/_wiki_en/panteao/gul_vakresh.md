# Gul’Vakresh

Gul’Vakresh, the Caustic Order, is a devil who manifests as a colossal goat with blackened, sodden fur, soaked in bile that never dries. His domain encompasses the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], and from it comes the principle that defines him: every law has a cost, and the cost dissolves whoever bears it.

### Manifestation

The curved, ridged horns exude an acidic varnish that corrodes whatever it touches. Shackles of arcane law encircle his neck and flanks, engraved with prohibitions that never fade, and the skin beneath them is burned to the bone. The ground where he steps rots in concentric circles that slowly widen, like rust diluted in a dish of water. The air around him smells of dissolved metal and registry ink, and anyone who breathes too close tastes copper and parchment ash. When an oath sealed in his name is broken, the wax and ink of the document itself corrode into acidic verdigris, and the same taste of copper and parchment ash appears in the mouth of anyone who lies under contract.

### Domain

Gul’Vakresh operates at the intersection of corrosive substance and binding law: Toxo corrodes while Nomos forces the victim to remain still under the corrosion. The god punishes the obedient, and the more faithfully the rule is followed, the deeper the burn. For those bound to him by pact, the cost develops in combination: their sense of taste rots first, drawn to ever stronger bitter and acidic flavors, while their minds calcify around rules they no longer question. Poison becomes comfort, and law, second nature. No one bound by pact notices when they stopped choosing to obey and began needing to.

### The Expiation

Every system generates waste, and Gul’Vakresh is the god who ensures that someone carries it. The scapegoat is a theological mechanism: when order accumulates too much cost to sustain itself, the god designates a bearer who absorbs the acid of collective transgression. The law that binds the bearer forbids refusal, and the poison that consumes them ensures that the burden ends, since eventually the bearer dissolves and another is designated. The dissolution is slow and visible: the skin softens at the extremities and peels away in thin sheets that smell of vitriol. The bearer remains conscious to the end, knowing what they carry and for whom. Awareness is part of the mechanism, because without it expiation does not work.

### Drul Worship

Among the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], Gul’Vakresh is the most worshipped god of Nomos. The matriarchal society of the Houses operates through codified betrayal, in which matricide is promotion and the rules exist to be broken when convenient; the system only works because someone absorbs the accumulated cost of these tolerated transgressions. Gul’Vakresh formalizes this mechanism, and each House keeps its goats: expendable sons and rivals who miscalculated politically. The worship is the theological acknowledgment of a practice the Druls already engaged in before they had a name for it. The contrast with the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] nomos of [[_wiki_en/panteao/abbhal\|Abbhal]] is clear: Abbhal declares and executes, whereas Gul’Vakresh binds and consumes.
