# Brondakhar

Brondakhar, the Wrathful Aegis, is a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titan]] whose protection manifests entirely through combat. He manifests as a [[_wiki_en/racas/minotauro\|minotaur]] whose shadow covers entire formations, clad in a cuirass that seems to have grown with his body: plates of keratin and bone fused to his skin cover his torso and shoulders up to his forehead, beneath horns that curve forward in an arc like a battering ram. The cuirass bleeds at its joints, and the blood clots in successive layers that harden like enamel. He reeks of heated iron and exposed flesh, and the air around the titan burns in the lungs before any blow is struck.

### Domain

His domain brings together [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]]. Belli amplifies fury and martial instinct; Salvo closes wounds and neutralizes damage to allies within the field of influence. The combination produces a rare effect: when Brondakhar strikes, the tremor that spreads through the ground closes open wounds in those nearby, as though the earth were returning what the battle took. The fiercer the combat, the more potent the regeneration around him, and the healing ceases when the fighting stops. The cost falls entirely on the titan: Salvo operates through transfer, and all pain removed from allies migrates to the healer, while Belli ensures that the transferred pain does not paralyze him, converting it into fuel. The circuit closes when the pain of others becomes rage, rage sustains combat, combat generates healing, and healing deposits more pain. Peace extinguishes the circuit, and the accumulated pain exacts its full toll at once.

### The Wrathful Aegis

Brondakhar’s aegis is activated by rage, and that rage escalates with the perceived threat to those under his protection. The wounded nearby heal as the titan advances, and fractured bones realign to the sound of hooves against the earth. The cuirass absorbs damage that should kill him and converts each impact into fuel for Salvo. He must be wounded to heal and must fight to protect; when the threat ceases, his body gives way to the accumulated damage that fury masked. The cuirass cracks in places that were intact during combat, blood runs from joints that seemed welded shut, and the titan collapses where he stands as the earth around him heals one last time. Flowers sprout where his body fell, nourished by divine blood. Among the titans, he is the only one whose protection is indistinguishable from self-destruction: [[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]] protects through stone, [[_wiki_en/panteao/orendhal\|Orendhal]] through growth, and Brondakhar through his own ruin.

### Manifestation

In the battle ranks alongside Brondakhar, the heat beneath the plates spreads: allies’ wounds clot quickly, almost feverishly, while the sweat of the nearby enemy arrives before the blow. Soldiers call the effect “vanguard fever”.
