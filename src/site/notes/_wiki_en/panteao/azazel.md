# Azazel

Azazel, the Archangel, is the last white god with a presence in [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], reduced to near institutional irrelevance. A seraph of the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]], he embodies a luminous faith now almost extinct on the continent, preserved in fragments by an order that operates in secret.

### Manifestation

Azazel manifests as a seraph with six wings made of overlapping blades that vibrate at different frequencies. The inner blades emit white heat; the outer ones, cold to the touch, reflect anyone who approaches with cruel clarity, revealing scars and marks the observer did not know they had. Two wings cover his face, two cover his feet, and two extend to the sides, cutting through the air with a continuous hum. Proximity to the seraph hurts: worshipers reported nosebleeds and retinal burns. No one in living memory has seen his face, and the oldest devotees taught that it opens only at the final judgment, when protecting and destroying become the same act.

### Domain

Pre-conquest theologians called Azazel’s radiance “the sword and shield of the same arm”. Helio penetrates dark matter, breaks down [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] enchantments, and strips away disguises. Aegis operates through belief: each devotee carries an echo of a barrier that absorbs attacks in proportion to the sincerity of their faith, and the aggressor feels a physical repulsion, as though reality refused to let the harm occur. The justice his devotees professed allowed no nuance: the guilty were not punished, they were exposed, and punishment came from what the exposure revealed. In [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], every chapel of Azazel was both a hospital and a fortress.

### Decline

Azazel was neither defeated, imprisoned, nor destroyed. Where the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] negotiated compromises with the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|shadow dominion]], the seraph refused any concession. His refusal preserved his doctrinal integrity and cost him all his influence. The druid [[_wiki_en/personagens/peredur\|Peredur]] put forward the view that the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]] perished through unbending purity. The seraph remains beyond the boundaries of mortal perception, answering increasingly rare prayers with a power that dwindles with each generation.

### Vestiges

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ordem_de_azazel\|Order of Azazel]] survives as an operational relic, and [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]], priest of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], is one of its members. The doctrine is passed down in fragments: consecrated rosaries, canticles memorized by those who no longer understand the original language, chapels that someone insists on keeping clean. [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] carries a rosary of Azazel concealed beneath the amulet of [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]]. Abramis has mastered the Canticle of Restoration, capable of purifying arcane corruption, whose origin remains disputed. In one of these chapels, beneath the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/manto_das_sombras\|mantle of shadows]], the only ray of sunlight that still pierces the cloud always falls on the same altar.
