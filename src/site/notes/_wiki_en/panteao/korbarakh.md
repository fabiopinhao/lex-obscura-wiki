# Korbarakh

Korbarakh, the Untouched Crucible, is a deity of the red pantheon that manifests as a Votive Salamander. It governs the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], and its fire neither conquers nor terrifies: it reduces matter to its fundamental components, consuming only what it accepts.

### Nature

It manifests as a votive [[_wiki_en/criaturas/salamandra\|salamander]]: a long, sinuous body of magma solidified into scales that liquefy and resolidify in slow cycles. Four short limbs support the torso close to the ground, each foot ending in claws of glassy obsidian that scratch any surface on contact. The tail extends in an arc and ends in a blue-white incandescent tip, hot enough to distort the air around it. The head is narrow, almost skull-like, with fused jaws and eyes that are direct openings into the body’s interior, where the core reveals itself as a furnace of contained plasma that never goes out. The skin does not burn those who approach. Korbarakh radiates heat, but only consumes what it accepts: the fire of the Untouched Crucible is selective and ignores what has not been offered to it.

### Domain

Pyro provides combustion, the fire that feeds on matter and turns solids into ash and ash into nothing. Plasma exceeds that limit: where Pyro consumes, Plasma melts, with energy so intense that the distinction between states of matter ceases to exist. Tissue breaks down into its constituent elements and vitrifies under thermal pressure. The combination makes Korbarakh the purest fire in the red pantheon. [[_wiki_en/panteao/bal_hadut\|Bal’Hadut]] burns to conquer, founding kingdoms upon the embers; the fire of [[_wiki_en/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]] serves terror, a language of fear made of flames. Korbarakh burns to reduce. What survives the crucible is essence; what does not survive was excess.

### The Crucible

The title describes the method. Korbarakh is the receptacle of absolute sacrifice. Offerings are consumed without residue: no ash, no smoke, no smell of burning, no mark on the ground where they lay. The flame operates at such an extreme temperature that matter passes directly from solid to plasma, skipping the intermediate states, and everything is converted into pure light and heat. The paradox of the Untouched Crucible lies in its selectivity. The salamander decides what enters the furnace: rejected offerings remain intact on contact, cold even when placed upon the scales, while accepted offerings disappear before contact, vaporized by the body’s ambient heat. In the votive brazier, the flame consumes the offering and spares the straw touching it; in an altar candle, the flame burns without ever melting the wax. The bonded of Korbarakh, if they existed at the time, would bear the inverse mark: the progressive vitrification of their own bones, which would harden and grow paler over time until they became as translucent as volcanic glass. The body of the bonded individual would slowly become the crucible itself.
