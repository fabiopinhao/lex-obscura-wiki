# Azkaroth

Azkaroth, the Archdevil, is the devil who presides over rites of passage between states of existence. He manifests as a raven-devil: an immense figure with smooth black feathers, a beak of polished bone, and eyes that reflect no light, solemn and ceremonial. His presence suppresses sound: where Azkaroth alights, the air thickens and voices become dry, as though the space between speaker and listener were filling with ash. The cold he radiates arises from proximity to the threshold between what still lives and what has already ceased to live.

### Domain

His domain brings together [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], on the boundary between death and manifestation. Necro governs passage, what dies and what persists beyond the flesh; Spectro governs the form that passage takes, the ghost and the presence that inhabits the threshold. Together, the two schools make him the god of transit between states of existence. Azkaroth takes an interest in the exact instant when one thing becomes another; the dead themselves and illusion itself belong to others.

### The Archdevil

The title designates a ceremonial function, and nothing more. Other [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] surpass Azkaroth in strength or terror, but he is the one who presides over the rites that define what a devil is and what an anointing means. The others grant power and punish transgressors; Azkaroth officiates the transformation itself. The consequence is structural: consecration of a Templar, formalization of a pact, alteration of a bond, any rite of passage between states falls within his domain. He governs the door through which everyone bound by a pact must enter, and none of the twelve can dispense with it.

### Temple and Anointing

The [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], is the seat of the anointing ceremonies of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]]. Anointing is symbolic death followed by ritual rebirth: the candidate enters as what they were and leaves as something else. Necro dissolves the previous identity; Spectro projects the new one. The process is irreversible, and no one who has undergone anointing can return to what they were before, even if they abandon the title. The mark is spectral and permanent, and manifests as a perceptual residue: the anointed report moments when they doubt the solidity of what they touch, shadows that seem denser at the periphery of their vision, and a subtle cold that pulses from the chest when silence lingers.

### Cold-Stone Rites

Beyond anointing, the house-cathedral preserves sacrificial cold-stone rites, made of blood, deprivation, cold, and vigil. Those who perform them receive internal prerogatives within the house’s hierarchy.

### Manifestation

In the rites he presides over, sound dies completely, a sign known as ash muteness: candles burn without crackling, voices emerge without an echo. Above the altar, or above the already consecrated corpse, a single black feather then appears, left by no bird.
