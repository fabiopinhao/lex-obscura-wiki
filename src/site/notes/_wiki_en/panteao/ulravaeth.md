# Ulravaeth

Ulravaeth, the Twin Shadow, is the Custodian of the Between: the devil who guards the thresholds between planes. A background god in the pantheon, he rules through the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] and inhabits the intermediate states between wakefulness and sleep, life and death. He is called the black god of Spectro, the school of which he is patron, and shares the Between, the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], with the blue powers.

### Manifestation

Ulravaeth manifests as two identical silhouettes, one facing the observer and the other facing away, fused at the base like a shadow cast in opposite directions. Neither side has a face, only the suggestion of features that change with the angle, like reflections in dark water. The Twin Shadow is never seen in full: one half always slips into the periphery of vision. His presence erodes spatial reference points, shadows stop pointing toward the light source, reflections show rooms different from the actual ones, and the sound of one’s own footsteps arrives late. When he observes someone, the effect concentrates on the target: the person being observed begins to cast two shadows that point toward no light source.

### Domain

Ulravaeth rules the thresholds, spaces where wakefulness blurs with sleep and the dead do not yet know they have died. His illusions work by subtraction: they erase reference points and dissolve certainties until the mind loses every anchor for distinguishing the real from the projected. Those who enter his domain do not see things that do not exist, but cease to see things that do exist. The Custodian feeds on the uncertainty of those who have lost the ability to confirm that they are still alive. The synergy between the schools reinforces the method: Umbra animates real shadows and Spectro projects illusions onto them, producing detailed shadows capable of simulating soldiers or the target themself. The [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] employ this combination as tactical disinformation.

### Role in the Pantheon

Among the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], Ulravaeth fills the role no other wants: custody of the interstices between planes. The other gods treat these fissures as dead zones, and Ulravaeth inhabits them. It is in these spaces that messages are lost or arrive altered, that pacts unravel through ambiguity, and that lesser entities disappear without a record. The Custodian’s power is positional: whoever controls the corridors controls what passes through them. Ulravaeth knows what the other devils communicate to one another, and decides what arrives intact and what does not arrive. No devil trusts him, and none can forgo the transit he administers.

### The Pact

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]] invoke Ulravaeth for spectral projection and control of their own shadow on infiltration missions. The pact-bound individual gains acuity in Spectro and Umbra, with an emphasis on subtractive illusions, but the cost corrodes on two fronts. Spectro instills epistemological doubt: the pact-bound individual touches objects to confirm that they are solid and distrusts what they have just seen. Umbra instills growing photophobia, until light becomes an assault. The two erosions reinforce each other: those who doubt what they see prefer dim light, and those who live in dim light have fewer and fewer reference points for confirming what is real.
