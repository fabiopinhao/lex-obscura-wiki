# Xanthrak

Xanthrak, the Artisan of Pain, is a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devil]] of the pantheon whose divine form is the Torturer Ossiarch. His domain combines the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]], and his specialty is prolonged suffering: curses that keep the victim alive to maximize pain. His servants do not die; they are remade.

### Manifestation

Xanthrak appears as a colossal skeleton covered in fragments of others’ flesh, stitched with threads of hardened sinew. Each visible bone once belonged to a different devotee, offered in life as a tribute of pain. The lower jaw is absent, torn off, according to the liturgy, by Xanthrak himself as a demonstration that the god’s pain surpasses that of any mortal, and in its place instruments of torsion hang from chains of vertebrae. The body emanates the cold of flesh separated from bone, and the scraps attached to the skeleton still twitch, as though the muscles remembered the exact moment of removal.

A subtler sign is also attributed to him: an old pain returns with surgical precision, lucid and localized, leaving no mark on the skin, while metal instruments hanging nearby clink on their own, one by one.

### Domain

Xanthrak does not govern death: he governs the interval in which death is denied. His curses preserve, keeping the victim conscious while the body rots, rebuilds itself, and rots again, in an iteration that ends only when Xanthrak decides the work is complete. The Anatho curse keeps the nerve functional when the surrounding tissue has already given way, and Necro restores what pain has destroyed, so that it can be destroyed again. The combination produces victims who neither die nor go mad, and who never grow accustomed to pain. To the Artisan of Pain, suffering is raw material, and each victim is a project with an indefinite deadline. Bone serves the craft at both ends: it is the raw material of both torture and reanimation.

### Method

Where other gods impose torments as punishment or deterrence, Xanthrak treats pain as a craft. His devotees learn to read suffering as a musician reads a score: timbre and intensity, the thresholds where the body gives way before the mind and the mind before the body. The result is precision, and Xanthrak’s tortures are recognizable because they leave the body functioning. The pact-bound inherit this literacy. The costs of Anatho and Necro produce torturers who instinctively map vulnerabilities and have lost their revulsion at opened bodies. What remains is technical attention, and it is what Xanthrak demands.

### Philosophy

To Xanthrak, pain is information. Punishment presupposes transgression, and most of the Artisan’s victims have committed no transgression. Each body contains a repertoire of suffering that reveals itself only under pressure: thresholds of nervous collapse, the point at which consciousness tries to shut down and fails. Xanthrak collects these sequences, and his devotees call the result a work, believing that a perfect suffering exists that the god has not yet found.

### Position in the Pantheon

Xanthrak occupies a niche that is tolerated but avoided. His methods require time that other devils consider a waste, and produce results too granular for those who prefer mass destruction. His proximity to Necro creates friction with [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]], whose necrology serves the purposes of an army, not a laboratory. Xanthrak does not compete for territory. He competes for time: the resource every torturer needs and every war wastes.
