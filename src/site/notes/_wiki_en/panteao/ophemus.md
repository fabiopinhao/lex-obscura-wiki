# Ophemus

Ophemus, the Devastating Radiance, is an [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angel]] of the pantheon whose divine form is the Coruscating Cyclops. His domain combines the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]], and the destruction he channels requires a just cause: the single eye carries out sentences out of conviction, and the morality of whoever invokes him serves as a targeting system.

### Manifestation

Ophemus appears as a cyclops of colossal proportions whose skin emits an intermittent glow, like a breathing ember. The surface of his body is vitrified in irregular plates, and between them fissures pulse with static that makes the air crackle dozens of paces away. The eye is a sphere of contained energy, with neither iris nor eyelid. The eye socket is vitrified by centuries of firing, the edge of the skull fused into opaque crystal that emits constant heat. The eye rotates slowly when it is not firing, tracking heat and movement, and the light it emits is painful to anyone who holds its gaze. When Ophemus fixes his gaze on a point, the air between the eye and the target ionizes, and anyone in its path feels their teeth vibrate before any sound arrives.

The mouth is sealed. The lower jaw is fused to the upper in a plate of vitrified enamel, without lips, without an opening. Theological tradition attributes the damage to a single event: Ophemus spoke, and the sound ionized the air inside his own throat. No one recorded what he said, and the consequence suggests that it should not have been said.

A subtler sign is also attributed to him: a white flash that holds on the horizon for whole seconds without any thunder, leaving a metallic taste in the mouths of those watching from a distance.

### Domain

The eye of Ophemus concentrates energy until matter can no longer contain it. Plasma superheats the point of impact, and rock and metal vitrify while flesh melts into a sealed crust before pain registers. Electro channels the discharge along the path of least atmospheric resistance, tracing branching arcs that leap between nearby conductors. The destruction obeys a judgment. Whoever invokes Ophemus with legitimate wrath against desecration receives the discharge directed at the target; whoever invokes him with impure intent discovers that the energy finds no path outward and seeks the nearest conductor, the invoker’s own body.

### The Radiance

The Devastating Radiance is the full discharge. The eye stops rotating, the air between Ophemus and the target compresses until it becomes visible, and sound disappears. Then the column of air turns into light connecting the eye to the point of impact, and electrical arcs branch out from it like the roots of an inverted tree. The thunder arrives compressed into a single report that ruptures unprotected eardrums. The ground at the point of impact vitrifies and remains magnetized for generations: metal tools stick to the surface, compasses go haywire, and smiths avoid building forges on Radiance soil because steel tempered there comes out polarized and cracks on first use. After firing, the eye darkens, the sphere of energy goes out, and Ophemus remains motionless while the charge rebuilds, a vitrified colossus without an eye, a statue of war awaiting the next sentence.
