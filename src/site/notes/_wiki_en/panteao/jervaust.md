# Jervaust

Jervaust, the Hungry Solstice, is a deity of the sidereal pantheon that manifests as the Famished Scylla. It governs the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], and devours time and temperature as a single resource: where it feeds, things un-happen and frost remains.

### Nature

It manifests as something that should have enough body to support six heads and does not. Its structure is cadaverous beyond what anatomy allows: grooves in the exposed rib cage, each corresponding to a century consumed, incised with the precision of someone who counts what they eat, and limbs tapered like bent gnomons pointing toward devoured suns. The six mouths are permanently open. Frost covers the edges, frost that never melts because the heat that would melt it has already been consumed. Inside each mouth there is no throat, there is visible recession: looking inside means observing a corridor that extends backward in time, each centimeter deeper corresponding to a more distant instant. No mouth closes. The lower jaw of each head fused in the open position, locked by the very hunger that deformed it.

### Domain

Where Jervaust feeds, things un-happen. The cold is a byproduct of temporal consumption: when seconds are devoured, the heat contained in them dies along with them. Duration and temperature are consumed at the same time because, on the scale at which Jervaust operates, they constitute the same resource. Entropy needs time to function; with time removed, it loses its substrate. The result resembles ice, but it is the residue of devoured chronology. Jervaust’s hunger is structural and definitional: what it eats has insufficient caloric or temporal value to sate it, because satiety requires duration, and duration is exactly what is being consumed. The distinction from [[_wiki_en/panteao/ruhavel\|Ruhavel]], who unites [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] and Chrono, lies precisely here: Ruhavel recycles time in a psychic loop, while Jervaust consumes it until frost remains.

### The Solstice

The solstice marks the longest or shortest day of the year. The Hungry Solstice is the day that never ended because it was not allowed to end. Jervaust’s victims neither freeze nor age. What they suffer is incompleteness: a gesture begun that never concludes, a half-formed thought that will remain half-formed because the time needed to complete it has been devoured. The frost that remains is the cold confirmation that there was duration there and now there is none. Investigators examining affected regions report objects cleanly cut in half: a candle whose flame stopped in midair, the wick still glowing on one side and dry frost on the other, as if someone had removed the seconds needed for combustion to complete.

Entire villages report the same lost hour without perceiving the splice: the afternoon jumps from one instant to another, and no one notices the missing interval. Along the edges of doors, even in the height of summer, frost grows that the sun does not melt.
