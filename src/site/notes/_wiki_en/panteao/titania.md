# Titânia

Titânia, the Mother of Roots, is a deity of the pantheon, classified as a titan-angel and patron of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]]. She brings together the domains of [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] in a defensive theology: she protects by negating, heals by responding.

### Nature

She manifests as an inviolate dryad, a female form embedded in the forest itself and inseparable from the plant tissue that composes it. Where [[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]] is stone and impact, Titânia is root and permanence. Her presence is not clearly seen: it is felt as maternal warmth in a clearing where light filters through the treetops, as the smell of the forest before rain, as the certainty that someone watches with ancient attention. Titânia does not articulate words. She communicates through what grows and what refuses to die.

### Domain

Abjuro dispels hostile magic, suppresses invasive enchantments, and maintains zones where dark magic simply does not work; Salvo closes wounds, neutralizes poisons, and restores vigor to those who serve beneath her roots. The two schools converge in the defense of the forest. An arrow consecrated to Titânia can carry both: the invocation in [[_wiki_en/linguas/sylvaen\|Sylvaen]] asks for guidance and blessing, the impact erupts in a green flash, and radiant roots tear the target’s flesh from the inside out.

### The Mother of Roots

The Sylvans who serve Titânia do not worship her as supplicants, but as extensions. The druid [[_wiki_en/personagens/peredur\|Peredur]] described the difference: the [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Gerut]] shape the earth and become what they shape; the Sylvans serve the leaves and become extensions of Titânia. Devotion is not hierarchical; it is symbiotic. [[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]] bears the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/marca_das_luas_gemeas\|Mark of the Twin Moons]], the insignia of druidic channelers. The green philosophy that sustains the deity favors survival through adaptation over unyielding purity: those who chose purity without compromise perished for it, and the greens chose life, however impure. In Sylvan invocations, the epithet Mother of Roots alternates with Green Mother and First of the Trees, popular forms of the same devotion. In Gaelwyn’s invocation in the sanctuary, the epithet is accompanied by Lady of the Eternal Green: “Titânia, Mother of Roots, Lady of the Eternal Green.”

### The Forest That Chooses

[[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] displays a will of its own under Titânia’s influence. The roots open gaps for those the forest wants to pass and close around those it wants to stay, and this selectivity is not benign: roots summoned by druidic channelers bind invaders without distinguishing those who have surrendered from combatants. Titânia preserves all life, but preserving the forest may require certain lives to end within it. An ancient agreement with [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], made alongside Morlgrund, sustained this protection for ages. With Vozrador imprisoned, the protection weakens: the [[_wiki_en/magia/macula-sombria\|shadow-taint]] advances along the edges of Thal’Serin, black veins pulsing beneath the bark of once-pure roots. Titânia resists, but resisting without the dark contrast that fed the balance costs more with each season.

### Manifestation

Around an innocent sleeping under her protection, vegetation weaves itself into a barrier, the Mark of the Twin Moons grows hot on whoever bears it, and shoots emerge in the footprints of those Titânia chooses to guard.
