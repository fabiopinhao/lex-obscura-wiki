# Kaelgrym

Kaelgrym, the Winged Scourge, is the demon who turns fear into politics and fire into pedagogy. His domain combines [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], which calibrates dread, with [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], which materializes the lesson in flame. He is the dragon-god of the Drakhonins, and his affinity with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] distinguishes him among the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]].

### Nature

He manifests as a funereal manticore: a leonine body with gray, singed fur, wings of scorched leather that drip liquid embers as they beat, a scorpion tail segmented into plates of incandescent obsidian. The face is vaguely humanoid, the skull elongated and skinless, the jaw articulated on a double hinge that opens beyond what is possible. Three rows of recurved teeth gleam a dull red, and heat escapes between them, distorting the air. The eyes are horizontal slits where fire burns without a pupil, and the smell of burned skin precedes the sight when the wings open.

### Manifestation

A subtler sign is also attributed to him, without requiring a sighting of the manticore: warm soot falls beneath a clear sky, shadows of wings cross the ground without any bird in flight, and extinguished funeral pyres reignite on their own before dawn.

### Domain

Kaelgrym turns fear into fuel and fire into language. Phobos calibrates dread, from diffuse unease to the phobia that embeds itself like a scar; Pyro materializes the lesson in fire. The combination produces pedagogical terror: the winged scourge teaches through fire and examines through fear, burns to test and terrifies to select. Those who survive the burn and the dread without breaking are worthy of the bond. From those who serve him, he demands continuous destruction and purges, and makes terror a method of government. His affinity with devils stems from Phobos: fear serves both chaos and order. Fear is the lingua franca between the planes.

### Patron of the Drakhonins

Kaelgrym is the dragon-god of the Drakhonins of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]], and the bond predates any mortal power by millennia. [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] are bonded to Kaelgrym from the egg: in the mandatory rite of passage, eggs are bathed in ritual blood and exposed to an elder’s breath of fire; those that survive the heat hatch, and those that do not survive are considered natural purification. This universal binding has a direct arcane consequence: no Drakhonin can wield [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] artifacts. [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], forged in [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|the Forgotten Age]] to wound elder dragons, responds only to hands free of a pact.

### Proscription

Kaelgrym slowly corrodes those who cannot resist. The Drakhonins demonstrate the generational effect: progressive internal burning that carbonizes the organs while the scales remain intact. Old Draconids die of internal collapse. Kaelgrym’s cost is fire that consumes from the inside out.
