# Elethvaen

Elethvaen, the Shepherd of Dreams, is a titan of the background pantheon who manifests as a twilight stag. His domain unites dormancy and organic growth: what sleeps under his watch grows, and he shepherds the cycles of sleep and awakening of entire ecosystems.

### Manifestation

Elethvaen appears as a massive, slow stag with moss-gray fur, his antlers covered in luminescent lichens that pulse in time with the breathing of those sleeping nearby. Each branch supports layers of moss and pale filaments that hang down to his shoulders, and his entire silhouette resembles a walking canopy. His eyes close completely only when the stag stops walking; in motion, they remain open, opaque, unfocused. His hooves leave marks in the soil where shoots break through the surface before dawn, and aerial roots extend from his short, dense tail, dragging along the ground and entwining themselves in the vegetation with a will of their own. His body emanates the moist heat of a greenhouse, and the air around him carries the weight of dusk at any hour of the day.

### Domain

Elethvaen’s domain unites [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]], which suppresses consciousness and induces deep dormancy, and [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]], which instills vigor and accelerates organic growth, on the principle that organisms grow faster when they sleep. Seeds in soil where the stag has stepped germinate overnight; wounds close during the sleep the titan imposes, with bone knitting and tissue regenerating while the body rests without choice. The cost is stolen time: the hibernation Elethvaen induces can last entire seasons. Mortals caught in the cycle awaken restored and with their memories intact, but the world around them has aged without them, children grown and alliances broken.

### The Shepherd

The epithet is literal. Elethvaen travels through the forests between seasons, guiding cycles of dormancy and awakening on the scale of a biome. In fall, entire groves enter a torpor as the stag passes, leaves falling in a sudden silence, sap receding, animals lowering their heads in the exact spot where they were. In spring, the reverse route awakens everything again: shoots burst from the hardened soil, burrows open, birds resume songs interrupted months earlier. The shepherding does not distinguish flora from fauna: everything that lives within the zone of influence obeys the cycle the stag imposes, and the cycle does not ask permission. The most recognized sign of his passage is dusk at the wrong hour: evening light appears in a clearing at noon, the village falls into a heavy sleep, and those who awaken find shoots breaking through the ground beside the thresholds.
