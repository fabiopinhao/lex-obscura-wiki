# Dremharak

Dremharak, the Lucid Fever, is a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereal]] deity of the background pantheon that manifests as a feverish wendigo. Its domain combines torpor and lucidity: it puts the body to sleep through hyperthermia while opening the mind to unfiltered perception. What it consumes is attention, not flesh.

### Manifestation

Dremharak appears as a bipedal skeleton that radiates dry heat, exposed ribs glowing the orange hue of metal fresh from the forge, pulsing in an irregular rhythm. A fibrous gray-green fungus grows between the bones and sporulates when the temperature rises, and the densest sporulation rises from the eye sockets, smoke that darkens the air around the skull. The skull is elongated and cervine, with a horizontal rack of antlers whose asymmetrical tines are pointed like a dowser’s rods. It lacks a lower jaw, in whose place fungal filaments hang like a damp beard. Where it walks, the ground dries out and cracks, and each footprint becomes a circle of vitrified earth, brittle as a clay biscuit.

### Domain

Dremharak’s domain combines two schools: [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] suppresses wakefulness through temperature and [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] replaces the suppressed consciousness with an invasive lucidity. The air heats up first. Dremharak’s torpor operates through hyperthermia: the target’s body temperature rises in constant increments until the nervous system capitulates, the muscles go slack, and the posture gives way. The mind, however, does not follow the body into sleep. The instant the body stops, perception sharpens, and the target thinks with a clarity it has never experienced while awake, every detail of the surroundings gaining cruel resolution. Dremharak replaces the filters of consciousness with unrestricted access, and information without priority becomes noise of perfect resolution. The victim sees everything at once, trapped in a feverish, motionless body. The wendigo’s hunger remains, redirected toward unfiltered consciousness.

### The Fever

Regions where Dremharak has lingered exhibit what sidereal scholars record as a zone of thermal lucidity: vegetation dries out and curls, fungal spores cover surfaces like a film of sweat. Those who cross these zones report inverted chills, the skin heating up while the inside of the body cools, with a dry nausea that precedes torpor. The best-documented cases involve seal engravers and treatise copyists, occupations requiring uninterrupted focus, found unconscious over their worktables, with elevated temperatures and pupils in constant motion beneath their eyelids. Upon waking, they all described the same experience: absolute wakefulness trapped inside a sleeping body. None could work for days, because the mind needed to relearn how to ignore.

In entire villages, the fever runs a mild course, with no apparent severity, and upon waking, the sick describe the same dream, identical in every detail, too vivid to be a coincidence. Where the fever passes, the ground retains circles of vitrified earth, brittle as a clay biscuit, scattered with no apparent relation to any trail or footstep.
