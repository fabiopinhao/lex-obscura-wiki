# Tzarakul

Tzarakul, the archdemon, is a demonic deity who manifests as the Calcining Autarch. His domain encompasses the schools of [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]]: structural dissolution and the existential terror that accompanies it.

### Manifestation

Tzarakul rises as a humanoid figure of colossal scale, in a sovereign stance that no erosion has managed to bend. He bears no crown, scepter, or insignia; authority resides in his bearing, in the verticality of something that remains standing while its very substance consumes itself.

The body is flesh in slow combustion: the skin calcines in plates that peel away like burned parchment and reveal live embers beneath, and each new layer emerges already cracked. Remnants of ceremonial clothing cover his shoulders and torso, solidified ash that crumbles at the hem. The face anchors Phobos: recognizable features fade slowly enough for the observer to follow each stage of dissolution, and the eye sockets are fissures that keep opening. The ground cracks beneath his feet, and his body emanates a heat that does not burn but subtracts: moisture abandons everything that draws near.

A subtler sign is also attributed to him: a fine crack spreads in a chain through stone or ceramic, before witnesses, without a crash, while the air dries and moisture abandons bread, skin, and eyes.

### Domain

Tzarakul is the force that existed before there was anything to break, entropy in its purest state. Where [[_wiki_en/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]] employs fear and fire as political instruments, Tzarakul is the rupture that precedes fear and the fear that survives rupture. Rupto does not act solely on matter: it dissolves pacts and sealings that sustain the relationship between mortal and god. Phobos occupies the void that rupture leaves, and what remains is the vertigo of free fall, the dread of someone who has lost the ground beneath them and knows that nothing exists below. A devil pactholder who approaches someone bonded to Tzarakul feels the fragility of their own connection to their patron, fissures where there was once certainty; doubt becomes a crack, and a crack is all Rupto needs.

### The Bonded

The rare survivors of contact with Tzarakul bear dual marks. Rupto’s fissures appear on the skin as cracks that never heal and darken over time, like veins of black mineral emerging from within the flesh. The residual mark of Phobos is subtler and crueler: a permanent hypersensitivity to structural fragility. The bonded individual perceives fissures in everything, cracks in walls, tensions in alliances, breaking points in contracts and foundations. The perception is accurate, and that accuracy corrodes: the entire world reveals itself as something about to break, and the awareness that everything can come apart at any moment never ceases, even during sleep.

Currently, [[_wiki_en/personagens/wijmarv\|Wijmarv]] is the only known individual bonded to Tzarakul. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] proscribes this bond: bonding oneself to the archdemon is punished in the [[_wiki_en/locais/drokh_malahar\|Pits of Drokh’Malahar]].
