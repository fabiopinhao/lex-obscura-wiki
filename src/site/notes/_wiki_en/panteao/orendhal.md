# Orendhal

Orendhal, called the Sealed Heartwood, is a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titan]] who manifests as a treant of primordial scale. His domains are [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]], which generates life and growth, and [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]], which negates and dispels hostile magic. Everything he creates carries heartwood resistant to arcane manipulation.

### Nature

Orendhal’s trunk displays visible growth rings where the bark has split, each ring corresponding to an era mortals have already forgotten. His branches support canopies dense enough to darken the ground like the roof of a vegetal cathedral, and his roots sink into layers of rock that predate any civilization. Lichens cover the surface in patterns that change over decades, too slowly for mortal eyes to register. At the center of the trunk, visible only in deep wounds, the heartwood shines with a pale green glow; the wood there is as dense as iron and warm to the touch, and no blade cuts it for long, for the wound closes before the steel reaches the necessary depth.

### Domain

Orendhal generates what lives and seals what he has generated against arcane interference. Vita allows him to create forests from nothing, infuse barren soil with vigor, and command roots as extensions of his own will. Abjuro protects each creation at the most fundamental level: the heartwood of each tree he plants resists magical manipulation as though the wood remembered what it should be and refused any alteration imposed from outside. The combination makes his forests functionally immune to arcane corruption in the long term. Physical damage and poison affect the surface, but the heartwood survives and regenerates the rest, in a cycle that can last centuries with the balance tipping toward growth, because the point of origin remains intact.

### The Sealed Heartwood

The epithet is both method and nature. Orendhal’s heartwood is sealed against every known form of magic: physical attacks damage the sapwood, bark, and branches, and Vita heals the damage from the untouched center, while arcane attacks dissipate before penetrating the sealed layer. The result is a form of vegetal immortality that does not depend on pacts, blessings, or external intervention, for the titan persists as long as the point from which he grows remains inviolable. This property extends to what he creates: forests he plants carry an echo of the sealed heartwood in each tree, protection weaker than that of the original, degraded by distance and time, but sufficient to slow magical corruption for generations. In zones where the [[_wiki_en/magia/macula-sombria\|shadow-taint]] advances, trees with sealed heartwood are the last to succumb and the first to sprout again when the pressure eases.

### Manifestation

In the grove under his protection, the cut fails before it is complete: the axe bounces off, the blade dulls, and the wound in the tree has already closed by the time the steel is withdrawn.
