# Mark of the Twin Moons

The Mark of the Twin Moons, also called Intertwined Moons, is a druidic tattoo that intertwines a waxing moon and a waning moon. It is the mark of initiates in the third circle of [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]] and always remains visible, since concealing it would amount to denying the goddess. It glows a faint green when near natural magic and identifies its bearer as a legitimate channeler of the bond through [[_wiki_en/magia/symbiosis_naturae\|Symbiosis Naturae]].

### Canonical Bearer

[[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]], a veteran ranger of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], is the documented bearer of the mark, and devotion to Titânia is the center of his life. The mark is detectable by other beings connected to the forest: where Gaelwyn passes, the trees whisper. This devotion is put to the test when he heals [[_wiki_en/personagens/grub\|Grub]] under coercion from Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]], torn between embracing all life and only the life that is convenient.
