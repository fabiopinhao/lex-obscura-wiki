# Divine Mechanisms

The Divine Mechanisms are the comparative map of the five systems of divine bonds, one for each category of higher entity. Each category has its own mechanic, and the whole sets out side by side the ways in which a mortal accesses divine power. There is also a sixth access system, [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]], which involves no god.

### The Five Systems

The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], of the color black, operate through [[_wiki_en/magia/pactum_diaboli\|Pactum Diaboli]]: an immutable contract in which soul and service are pledged, with power granted in proportion to what is given. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], of red, operate through [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], a bond of force that grants immediate access at the cost of progressive mental corruption and a grotesque mark. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], of green, operate through [[_wiki_en/magia/symbiosis_naturae\|Symbiosis Naturae]], reciprocal, fault-tolerant symbiosis, and are the most present in [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]]. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]], of blue, operate through [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]], mental attunement through ethereal meditation, without moral judgment, and cannot be imprisoned. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]], of white, operate through [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Devotio Sancta]], devotion and faith without a contract, which abandons those who transgress and is vulnerable to a crisis of faith.

### Coercive and Non-Coercive

The systems are divided into two groups. Pactum Diaboli and Vinculum Daemonis are coercive: they are the only ones in which the mortal is “pact-bound” in the strict sense. Symbiosis Naturae, Devotio Sancta, and Meditatio Aetheris are non-coercive.
