# Nexus Magi

A nexus magi is a concentration of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] fixed in matter: a site where an outcropping of dense magi transforms ordinary mineral into magi crystal over centuries. It is from this crystal that the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] extracts the reagent that drives its fledgling industry, and this makes nexuses among the most contested sources on the continent. The term circulates in equivalent forms, including arcane nexus and crystal nexus.

### Natural and Artificial

A natural nexus is an outcropping where the veil between [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]] and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] is thin enough for magi to condense on its own; the largest grew over ancient [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] foundations imbued with ancestral magi. The largest known natural nexus lies beneath [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], the Crystal Fortress. An artificial nexus is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] structure built on the foundations of Luminar ruins, forcing condensation where nature has not completed it; those of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] are the exemplary case.

### The Magi Crystal

Magi crystal is ordinary mineral transformed by centuries of exposure to dense magi, a mineral capacitor whose formation takes centuries. It absorbs magi from the environment slowly, over ages, and discharges it quickly as raw work when activated; this is why it exists in quantity only at ancient outcroppings, where the charging time was geological. The extraction of this accumulated magi is a technique that has been mastered, and it is what sustains the fledgling technological revolution. Deliberate charging, however, recharging a depleted crystal through ritual, is a recent art and still little explored. Since what is extracted is not replenished on the scale of an empire, magi crystal is a finite resource, a material echo of magi’s own decline.
