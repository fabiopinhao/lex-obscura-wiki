# Magi Channel

The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] channel, also called the arcane channel or connection beam, is the temporary interface that links an extradimensional entity to a mortal on the material plane. All interplanar magic, from diabolic pacts to divine devotion, from symbiosis with titans to ethereal meditation, must cross the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] through this channel. It is through this channel that a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devil]] grants power to its pact-holder, that a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titan]] responds to a druid’s symbiosis, that a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereal]] anchors itself in a meditator’s consciousness.

### Nature

The channel is not permanent. It forms during the invocation and dissolves when the spellcasting ends: it is an event, not a place, and each invocation generates its own, carrying the signature of the arcane school used and of the entity that responds. No channel is permanently open, and the pact-holder is not a conduit always connected to their god; each invocation is an opening and each ending a closing. Frequent use can make opening the channel almost reflexive for experienced practitioners, but the flow is never continuous.

### Operation by Access System

The channel operates differently according to the bond. In [[_wiki_en/magia/pactum_diaboli\|Pactum Diaboli]] it is contractual, and the devil grants access according to the terms of the pact. In [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]] it is raw and unmediated, providing immediate but unstable and corrupting access. In [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Devotio Sancta]] it responds to faith, with doubt narrowing the channel and transgression closing it. In [[_wiki_en/magia/symbiosis_naturae\|Symbiosis Naturae]] it operates through resonance with the natural cycle and rarely breaks. In [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]] it is mental, and the sidereal entity anchors itself in the spellcaster’s consciousness through purely conceptual communication.

[[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]] is the special case. In it, magi does not cross the ethereal plane, as it comes from within the practitioner, but communication with the divine plane remains interplanar and crosses the ethereal plane like any other. For this reason, the Teru practitioner is not immune to ethereal instability, and in regions where the ethereal plane is compromised, communication can degrade, causing a [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] to temporarily lose access to one or more schools.

### Entity Agency

The existential plane responds automatically to any valid communication, which combines a compatible color, a key emotion, and the correct invocation. This is the basic mechanism and the only one Teru uses. In pact systems, however, the bound entity can exercise agency over the response, amplifying, distorting, or blocking it. The Teru practitioner, by contrast, is invisible to the god: there is no bond or mechanism for intervention, and the plane responds on its own. It is, by nature, the most stable and least powerful system.

### Artifact Channels

The principle that the channel is an event, not a place, also applies to communication through an artifact. Each transmission through the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus Morvgradi]] forms a temporary communication channel, never a magi channel, routed through the [[_wiki_en/locais/poco_etereo\|Ethereal Well]] and existing only during the call. What persists between calls is the signature-key bond between the spheres, and a bond is not a channel. The energy for the transmission comes from the consecrated artifact itself, not from the operator’s magi, and for this very reason even a non-spellcaster can use it.

### Vulnerabilities

The channel can be blocked, suppressed, or severed. The [[_wiki_en/artefatos/grilhoes_de_contencao_arcana\|shackles of arcane containment]] suppress magic by blocking the flow from the ethereal plane, and the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/luto_de_nyssis\|Mourning of Nyssis]] temporarily neutralizes the ability to form channels. The school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] devotes spells to intercepting and extinguishing arcane connections, from Suppression, which attacks the source, to Extinction, which permanently eradicates the capacity for channeling.

### Sealed Planes

When a god is imprisoned on its own existential plane, as is the case with [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], sealed about fifty-five years ago, the seal blocks the plane’s interface with the outside and the automatic response ceases. The seal blocks the plane, not the school: Vozrador’s schools remain accessible through other gods who share them, since the school is a universal pattern and the plane is a particular instance. The pact-holders of a sealed god lose access to magi channeling, although the fate of the pact itself remains disputed between those who consider it broken and those who see it persisting as a weakened scar.

### Decline

The decline of magi with each era also extends to the channel. Where channels once responded with generosity and immediacy, today forming bonds demands more of the spellcaster and the divine response arrives weaker.
