# Materialist Theory

The Materialist Theory, or Arcane Materialism, is the magical philosophy formulated by [[_wiki_en/personagens/jospin_valheorn\|Jospin Valheorn]] in the work *De Magia Alchemica: Fundamenta Physica*. It maintains that [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] is a real and invisible alchemical force, and opposes the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/doutrina_da_alma\|Doctrine of the Soul]], which roots all magic in the spirit.

### Foundations

According to the theory, magi is a subtle energy matrix that permeates [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]], and all magic, whatever the access system, is manipulation of this force. The gods are defined by the abundance of magi they possess, not by spiritual status or transcendent nature, and alchemy would be the only conscious and autonomous way to manipulate it, dispensing with divine intermediaries. Jospin ignored the concept of the soul and treated [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]] as a “metabolic anomaly”, denying it any spiritual dimension.

### Opposition to the Doctrine of the Soul

The opposing position is the Doctrine of the Soul of [[_wiki_en/personagens/naiu_wula\|Naiu’Wula]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Gerut]] master from the end of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], for whom all magic is born of the soul. The two philosophers never crossed paths: Naiu’Wula lived earlier, and Jospin later, during the Reinkardic Wars. Materialism and spiritualism thus form two poles that never acknowledged each other, although the system of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] depends on the inner awakening that the opposing doctrine described.

### Adoption and Suppression

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] officially adopted the Materialist Theory, incorporating it into the arcane doctrine of the Circles of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]] and into the training of engineers of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|the Cult]]. The view of magi as a quantifiable resource serves the imperial mindset: what can be measured can be controlled. Jospin worked in underground laboratories with [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] for military and energy purposes, which earned him accusations of treason from [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]; he was executed by the very city that would later fall under Shadar rule. Copies of his treatises were destroyed or banned in several kingdoms, but his influence spread among rebellious humans, goblin technomages, cult engineers, and independent scholars.
