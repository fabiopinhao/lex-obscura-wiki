# The Void

The Void, also called Primordial Blackness, is a central cosmogonic and theological concept in the shadow tradition of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. More than the mythical origin of the universe, it operates as an active force in the world, a political justification, and even a measure of time. No cosmogonic tradition is confirmed or denied by the world’s system, and the coexistence of contradictory versions is deliberate.

### Cosmogony

In the Shadow Version, an oral tradition of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], the Void is a conscious blackness that preceded all existence. It did not create out of kindness or with a purpose, but out of necessity, because absolute absence is unstable; matter arose as residue, a defect on the surface of the Void.

In this interpretation, the gods are fragments of the Void that gained wills of their own. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] are the closest to its original nature, those who best understand that control is the only answer to the chaos of existence. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]] are deformations, fragments that refused to accept the truth of the Void and invented concepts such as light and purity to justify their weakness. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] clung to the defect, matter, instead of transcending it. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]] chose not to choose, wandering between the Void and the defect.

Shadar cosmogony is functional: it serves to justify the supremacy of diabolical pacts and the legitimacy of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] as a restoration of the natural order. For the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]], who share the Shadow Version with subterranean inflections, the Void is literally the darkness of the deepest caves, and [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] would be the place closest to the origin.

### The Inverted Prayer

The swear word “Vaz,” an insult common among the servant races ([[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnolls]], [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]], Druls, [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derros]], and [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worgs]]), has its roots directly in this cosmogony. For the Shadars, who see the Void as sacred, it is a minor blasphemy, though it sometimes slips out of their mouths. Derived from Vazio, it carries theological weight without most speakers realizing it: the everyday blasphemy of those who use it as a curse is, unbeknownst to them, an inverted prayer. Variations in usage include “Vaz!” as a general curse, “vazzed” for someone in a bad situation, “what the vaz is this?”, “go to vaz” in the sense of going to hell (literally, returning to the origin), and “by the voids!” as an expression of surprise, invoking primordial nothingness. More intense is “Vaz’kren!”, a curse shouted through clenched teeth in the face of acute pain or fright.

### The Day of the Void

[[_wiki_en/conceitos/nullis\|Nullis]], colloquially Vaz Day, is the standalone day between the last [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lunia\|Lúnia]] of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/varnum\|Varnum]] and the first of the next. It belongs to no Lúnia or season. It is a day of superstition, seclusion, and imperial rituals. The calendrical connection reinforces the presence of the Void as a structuring concept of Shadar civilization: not only theology, but a measure of time.

### Metaphysical Manifestation

The Void is not just a cosmogonic narrative: it manifests on the material plane as active energy. Its regional expression is the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/manto_das_sombras\|Mantle of Shadows]], the saturation of shadow energy that made Reinkard the continent of shadows and weakens the gods of the opposite spectrum; its visible face in the forests is the [[_wiki_en/magia/macula-sombria\|Shadow-Taint]]. [[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] raised among Shadars, carries within him the mark of this conflict, a fragment of void inside someone whose nature should be green: the forest partially rejects him, the trees whisper when he passes, and the roots recoil from his presence.

### Simultaneous Layers

The Void operates in multiple registers that feed one another: cosmogonic, as the origin of the universe in the shadow tradition; theological-political, as justification for diabolical supremacy and the Empire; linguistic, as everyday blasphemy that unconsciously echoes theology; calendrical, in Nullis as a ritual marker; metaphysical, as an active force in the world through the Mantle of Shadows and Taint; and personal, as an individual mark in Gaelwyn.
