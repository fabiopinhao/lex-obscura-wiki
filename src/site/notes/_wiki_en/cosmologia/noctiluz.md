# Noctiluz

Noctiluz is a luminous phenomenon that emanates from ruptures in the celestial veil, a light that belongs to no celestial body and seems to come from the depths between worlds. Recorded since the Fall of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]], it predates the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] and does not obey mortal will.

### Nature

When the sky splits, nearby stars retreat as if repelled, and noctiluz spills through the opening. As it closes, the rupture leaves a cerulean or silvery scar in the firmament, which remains visible for an indeterminate time. The ancient annals described it as “the weeping of darkness”. In its documented manifestations, noctiluz accompanies moments of consequence: a rift opened and stitched itself shut again during the consecration of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]], and the cerulean scar remained visible from the plateau to the continent. The saying was repeated from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] to the rest of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]: even the sky can bleed. Another rift, vertical, opened over the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]] while [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] stared at each other under the cerulean light in the [[_wiki_en/locais/praca_dos_juramentos\|Square of Oaths]]; seen from atop [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], the light descended in a silent cascade, and the tear, as it stitched itself shut from the inside out, left a silvery scar that crossed the sky to the end of the plateau, pulsing in a slow rhythm.

### Interpretations

No interpretation of the phenomenon is entirely satisfactory, and each people interprets it in its own way. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] treats noctiluz as an extension of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s power, evidence that his sovereignty reaches the firmament. Among Pindoriars and rural populations, it is an omen, the tremor those below feel when those above move the pieces. For [[_wiki_en/personagens/fing\|Fing]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobold]] in the monarch’s service, it is the voice of the “papas”: the pink light would be a scolding, cerulean blue would be weeping, and the eye-shaped tear would indicate that someone up there is irritated or snooping. Where even scholars hesitate, the simple interpretation suffices.

### Mysteries

The cause of the fissures remains unknown, as does the exact nature of the light that escapes from them. Why the phenomenon is concentrated over the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau, and whether it is a blessing, a curse, or a side effect of something greater, remains an open question.
