# Demons

Demons, also called Red Gods or Gods of Chaos, are the divine category associated with the red [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/cor_arcana\|arcane color]]: destruction, chaos, and pleasure in violence. They inhabit existential planes that are true hells, landscapes of fire, blood, and perpetual ruin.

### Nature

For demons, the material plane is an amusement park. Direct and emotional, they have a hunger for power as intense as that of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], but lack the patience or discipline to build empires. They find fulfillment in war, in massacre, in destruction with no purpose beyond destruction itself. They bond with mortals who align with their principles: those who do not think in the long term, those who live in the now, those who prefer the intensity of flame to the safety of stone.

### Access System

The bond with demons operates through [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], a bond of force, not a contract. The relationship is less bureaucratic than its diabolical counterpart: access is immediate, without elaborate negotiation, but comes with progressive mental corruption, fury, paranoia, and sadism. The grotesque physical marks it produces, horns and chaotic scars, never disappear.

### Imperial Proscription

In the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], the demonic bond is a crime. [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] classified it as Vinculum Daemonis, punishable in the [[_wiki_en/locais/drokh_malahar\|Pits of Drokh’Malahar]], with the purpose of eliminating sources of chaos within the Empire. The system, however, lacks an efficient means of detecting clandestine bonds.

### Relationship with Draconids

Dragons and [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] had served the red gods since time immemorial. In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|Forgotten Age]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] classified both species as a scourge aligned with destruction and forged artifacts, such as [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], that rejected hands corrupted by demonic bonds. The Draconids, already bonded to the red gods, were never able to wield these weapons against their own lords.

### Named Demons

[[_wiki_en/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]] brings together the domains of [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] and demands continuous destruction, fear, and purges; he has an affinity with devils through Phobos. [[_wiki_en/panteao/hazburim\|Hazburim]], the Lord of the Flies, is the demon of pestilence, proscribed by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]].
