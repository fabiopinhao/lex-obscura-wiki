# Magi

Magi is the universal magical energy that permeates [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]]. It functions as the invisible fuel for all magic, regardless of the access system. Each spellcaster has a limited reserve that must be replenished after use, and attempting to cast with an empty reserve results in overload, with severe harm or death.

### Two Doctrines

The nature of magi is a subject of philosophical dispute. [[_wiki_en/personagens/jospin_valheorn\|Jospin Valheorn]]’s [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/teoria_materialista\|Materialist Theory]] describes it as a real and invisible alchemical force, a subtle energy matrix; Jospin defined the gods by the abundance of magi they possessed, not by spiritual status, and saw alchemy as the only conscious means of manipulating this force. The theory was officially adopted by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], although copies of his treatises have been destroyed or banned in several kingdoms. Opposing it is the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/doutrina_da_alma\|Doctrine of the Soul]], by [[_wiki_en/personagens/naiu_wula\|Naiu’Wula]], for whom all magic is born of the soul and magi is merely its trace in the world, not its flame.

### Channeling

The passage of magi between planes occurs through the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/canal_magi\|magi channel]], a temporary interface that forms during invocation and dissolves at its end. Magi stored in an object, such as a crystal, weapon, or reservoir, cannot be used to cast magic through [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]]: in this system, functional magi comes from within the practitioner, accompanied by the key emotion, while stored magi is raw energy, without an emotional signature.

### Historical Decline

In the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|Forgotten Age]], magi was abundant: [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]] walked among the forests, [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]] conversed with scholars, and the divine response to rituals was generous and immediate. Since the fragmentation of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]], this abundance has been diminishing with each age, and elder dragons, the only beings long-lived enough to compare eras, notice magi growing thinner and the gods manifesting with perceptible effort. The exact cause remains debated: druids attribute it to the destruction of the Luminars, sidereal scholars to the narrowing of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], and hierophants of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] to the gods of light losing relevance, with no explanation excluding the others.

### Material Applications

Magi also has concrete manifestations in the world’s technology. Magi crystals — a common mineral transformed by centuries of exposure to dense magi — fuel the combustion chambers of [[_wiki_en/artefatos/bombarda_ananica\|dwarven bombards]], releasing a controlled explosion when activated by a runic spark. The [[_wiki_en/artefatos/grilhoes_de_contencao_arcana\|Shackles of Arcane Containment]] suppress magic by blocking the flow from the ethereal plane, and the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/luto_de_nyssis\|Mourning of Nyssis]] is the alchemical substance that temporarily suppresses it. The crystal that powers this technology comes from [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/nexus\|magi nexuses]], where the mineral became charged through centuries of exposure to dense magi: extracting it is a mastered technique, and it is what sustains the incipient technological revolution, but deliberately charging a depleted crystal is a recent and still little-explored art. Scepters, wands, and unusual weapons, in turn, amplify or direct magic through their intrinsic properties, not by functioning as batteries.
