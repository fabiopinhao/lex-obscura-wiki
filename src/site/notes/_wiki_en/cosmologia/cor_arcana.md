# Arcane Color

Arcane color describes the affinity between a race and the gods it can bind itself to: each race gravitates toward the god of its color, and that is usually its primary bond.

### Affinity

Color does not function as a metaphysical constraint, and no divine plane rejects those who do not carry the right color: affinity is a statistical tendency, never absolute. The [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] tends toward the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devil]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Gerut]] toward the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titan]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]] toward the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereal]].

The vast majority of magic users have a single bond because the gods prefer exclusivity and may reject those who already serve another (see [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/monogamia_divina\|divine monogamy]]); nothing forbids a second. This rejection extends to any god, including those of the same color. When a second bond occurs, its probability decreases according to the affinity between the divine natures: bonds between gods of the same nature are the most common, followed by those joining allied natures and, finally, those joining opposing natures, which are exceedingly rare. A Shadar bound to a devil and a sidereal, such as [[_wiki_en/personagens/nyx_ara\|Nyx’Ara]], is a plausible exception; a Shadar bound to an [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angel]] is almost inconceivable.

### Humans and the Gray Substrate

Humans are gray: with no predominant arcane color, they are flexible enough to bind themselves to a god of any color, generally angels and devils. This openness comes at a price, since forming the bond is more difficult for a human than for the races of color, and only a minority possess magical power. [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]], a human priest bound to an angel, is an example of a human spellcaster. The truly colorless race is that of the dwarves ([[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]]).

Human flexibility is also the origin of the races of color. Generations of exposure to a pantheon specialize the lineage and produce subraces that bind themselves to their own nature with far less difficulty: exposure to devils turned humans into Shadars; to [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], into [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]]; to titans, into Geruts; to angels, into [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]]; to sidereals, into Frenims. Specialization is generational, but can be accelerated through ritual, as in the case of Ossárium, which converts a human into a Shadar in a single generation. The process alters body, soul, and mind.

Pindoriars form a social category; since arcane color depends on race, that status does not change it: an enslaved [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] is a [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]] and remains green; a subjugated Álvarar is a Pindoriar and remains white.

### The Elven Lineages

The [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]], the ancestral elven race, were the opposite of humans: they carried the five colors, and tradition describes them as pentachromatic. It is unknown whether this meant simultaneous access to all schools or a form of prechromatic magic. The records are fragmentary.

What is known for certain is the fragmentation. When the Luminars split, each lineage retained one color and lost the other four: those who made pacts with devils in the Depths became [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]]; those consumed by chaos became [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]]; those who communed with the primordials in the forests became Sylvans; those who drew close to the angels became [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]]; those who isolated themselves in ethereal contemplation became [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]]. The fragmentation was an irreversible loss. A Sylvan attempting a black school today would not even generate the necessary key emotion, because their remaining soul resonates only with green.

### Conversion Between Colors

Conversion is possible and constitutes a total metamorphosis. The exemplary case is [[_wiki_en/magia/rito_de_ossarium\|the Rite of Ossárium]], which rewrites the being’s nature. Conversion between elven lineages is even more drastic, since it corrupts the original essence and usually involves racial mixing, producing hybrids that do not belong entirely to any lineage. It is also not symmetrical: descending from white to black seems easier than ascending from black to white. The white tradition reads this as proof that the fall is the natural direction of spiritual entropy; the dark tradition, as proof that black is the true state and the other colors are unstable deviations. Neither reading prevails over the other.

### The Fifty Gods

The pantheon has fifty gods and is exactly symmetrical. Each of the five divine categories (angels, titans, sidereals, devils, and demons) has eight pure gods, forty in all. The other ten are bridge gods: they belong to two categories at the same time, and there is exactly one for each pair of colors, with none left over and no repeats. [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]] joins green and white; [[_wiki_en/panteao/warguleth\|Wargûleth]], black and red; [[_wiki_en/panteao/velmorain\|Velmorain]], black and blue. Each bridge has a dominant color, which determines the system through which he is accessed. Since each color pairs with the other four, each has twelve gods: eight pure gods and four bridges that touch it.

### Creatures and Color

Arcane color is an attribute of races and gods. Natural creatures have no color: cave predators, mounts, and mountain beasts live outside the color system. As a rule, only a creature born of magic or of a god carries color, and what it carries depends on who made it.

### Access to the Schools

Access to the schools comes from the gods one is bound to, not from the color as a whole. Each god grants two schools (see [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/entidades_bicolores_e_cor_dominante\|dominant color]]), and a pact-bound individual accesses exactly the two schools of each entity they bind themselves to. A Shadar bound to a single devil accesses only that devil’s two schools out of the ten black schools; if they also bind themselves to a sidereal, they add his two.

[[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]] is the exception that encompasses the entire color, with broad versatility and shallow depth, the opposite of a pact: the practitioner accesses the ten schools of their own racial color without a divine bond, because the color’s key emotion already inhabits their soul.
