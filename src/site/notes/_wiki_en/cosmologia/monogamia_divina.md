# Divine Monogamy

Divine monogamy is the tendency of gods toward exclusivity in their bond with mortals. It is not a metaphysical law, but an inclination proportional to the value of each mortal, and applies to all divine categories, not only to [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]].

### Nature

The gods prefer exclusivity. An ordinary mortal rarely obtains even one bond, and most of those who do fail to attract the interest of a second god. Mortals of exceptional value may attract multiple deities willing to share them, but at an increasing cost: the more bonds, the greater the demand each god places on the bearer. In practice, two simultaneous bonds are already exceedingly rare, occurring in around 2.5% of those who possess magic. Three is regarded as a historical aberration, with no known precedent.
