# Ethereal Plane

The ethereal plane, also called the Ether or Interspace, is the intersection of all planes: an interspace that connects the material plane, [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]], to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/planos_divinos\|Divine Planes]]. It functions as a universal alchemical corridor through which [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] circulates. There is no ontological hierarchy among the planes: they are distinct domains that coexist.

### Cosmological Nature

All interplanar magic crosses the ethereal plane through the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/canal_magi\|magi channel]]. This applies even to [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]]: although the practitioner’s magi comes from within, communication with the divine plane is interplanar and crosses the Ethereal. Where the plane is compromised, this communication can be degraded or blocked, and even the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] are not immune to this instability.

### Inhabitants

The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]], the blue gods, are the divine entities most associated with the ethereal plane, and they are believed to inhabit it. Even so, the Ethereal is an interface between the planes: no being has it as its own plane of existence. They roam between planes according to their interests, without a fixed domain, which makes them impossible to imprison: there is no dimension in which they can be confined. They are beings of pure or nearly pure consciousness, djinn, leviathans, sphinxes, thought patterns, living geometries, conscious concepts; their interest in Thamakard is intellectual, for they seek minds, not lands.

### Cosmogonic Interpretations

Each tradition assigns the plane a different function in the structure of the universe. In the green, druidic version, the ethereal plane is the nervous system of the living organism that is the universe, and the sidereals are its pure thought. In the ethereal, sidereal version, the universe is a mathematical pattern and the Ethereal is the intermediate variable between the material plane and the Divine Planes. In the shadow version, that of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], the sidereals are fragments of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|the Void]] that chose not to choose. No interpretation is confirmed or denied.

### Mortal Access

The magical access system that operates directly on the plane is [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]], practiced by the [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenims]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/undine\|undines]] of [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]]. Attunement is usually passive, bidirectional only for powerful mages, and training requires decades of rigorous mental discipline: breathing, focus, emotional control, and the study of philosophy, mathematics, and universal patterns. The Frenims inhabit both the material world and the edges of the Ethereal at the same time, and this dual perception is the defining mark of the race’s identity. The undines took the process further, renouncing materiality in favor of direct communion with the sidereals, at the cost of a drastically reduced lifespan of about forty years. Two schools directly manipulate the plane: [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], the latter capable of banishing targets to the Ethereal.

### The Veil

The boundary between Thamakard and the ethereal plane is called the veil, and it has no fixed thickness: it varies with the place and with what has happened there. The veil is thinned by geological depth, the centuries-long consecration of temples and sacred ground, the accumulated arcane presence in [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] ruins (the reason why [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/nexus\|magi nexuses]] arise over them), repeated crossings at the same point, and death or blood on a ritual scale. It is thickened by the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/luto_de_nyssis\|Mourning of Nyssis]] and other magi suppressors, the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, whose Gap creates a zone where no channel exists, and the simple absence of arcane history.

### Physical Crossing

A mortal can physically cross only into the ethereal plane, and only through dedicated means: the Spectro school, the practices of the Frenims and undines of Neranthis, or a material anchor that binds them to the world while they remain on the other side. No living mortal enters a divine plane: contact with the Divine Planes is always mediated, through a channel, dream, projection, or avatar. The rule is absolute.

### Instability

The ethereal plane is not static, and its stability can be compromised. [[_wiki_en/magia/fluxus_aetheris\|Fluxus Aetheris]], an attempt to manipulate the plane’s conduits directly, is an extremely dangerous practice and does not constitute a formal school: most catastrophic magical accidents recorded in history involve failures in it. Sidereal scholars further suggest that the plane itself has been narrowing, which contributes to the gradual decline of magi in Thamakard.

### Relationship with the Mantle of Shadows

The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/manto_das_sombras\|Mantle of Shadows]], a regional phenomenon that weakens the influence of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|white gods]] and green gods in [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], does not directly reach the ethereal plane. This is why the sidereals are less affected by it: they operate in a layer that shadow energy does not penetrate. For the same reason, they are immune to extraplanar attacks, since there is no fixed domain where they can be struck.
