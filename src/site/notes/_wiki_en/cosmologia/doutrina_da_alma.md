# Doctrine of the Soul

The Doctrine of the Soul, also called Arcane Spiritualism, is the magical philosophy formulated by [[_wiki_en/personagens/naiu_wula\|Naiu’Wula]], a wandering [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Gerut]] master of the late [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]]. It postulates that all magic is born of the soul, and directly opposes [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/teoria_materialista\|the Materialist Theory]] of [[_wiki_en/personagens/jospin_valheorn\|Jospin Valheorn]], which reduces magic to a measurable force.

### Foundations

According to the doctrine, [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] is merely “the soul’s trace in the world, not its flame”. The gods are not reservoirs of energy, but ancient and vast souls; mortals are young souls, potentially capable of awakening. This awakening arises in moments of profound emotion and cannot be taught, predicted, or forced. Naiu’Wula founded neither a school nor an institution: he preached in open spaces, taught by demonstrating rather than arguing, and offered “inner listening” as a method, the stilling of the mind until the soul responded.

### Foundation of Teru

The Doctrine of the Soul is the philosophical basis of [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]], the system of magic without a divine pact. Where the other five access systems depend on a bond with external entities ([[_wiki_en/magia/pactum_diaboli\|Pactum Diaboli]], [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], [[_wiki_en/magia/devotio_sancta\|Devotio Sancta]], [[_wiki_en/magia/symbiosis_naturae\|Symbiosis Naturae]], and [[_wiki_en/magia/meditatio_aetheris\|Meditatio Aetheris]]), Teru operates through the inner awakening that Naiu’Wula described, communicating with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/planos_divinos\|divine planes]] without requiring submission or devotion. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] master magic exclusively through this path, a condition imposed by [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], who required his judges to have no pact. [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]] is the doctrine’s most prominent heir in practice; [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], a singular case, developed Teru belatedly, embodying the tension between the divine bond and the awakening of the soul.

### Reception

Naiu’Wula was received in opposing ways depending on his audience. Religious people called him a heretic, alchemists called him a charlatan, and academic mages ignored him, while [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] elves, Geruts, woodland rebels, and hermits treated him as a prophet. The divergence persists in a contradiction that is not accidental: the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] adopted the Materialist Theory as its official doctrine, but trains its Justiciars in the system born of the opposing philosophy. Disciples of the doctrine still gather in secret circles to teach inner listening, and it survives on the margins, nurturing precisely the kind of power that the Empire uses as an instrument without acknowledging its origin.
