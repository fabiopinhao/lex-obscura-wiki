# Interactions Between Planes

Interactions between existential planes govern how gods and mortals reach one another across dimensions. All interplanar magic depends on these rules, and they determine both what one plane can project onto another and what each god can protect within its own domain.

### The Magi Channel

All interplanar magic passes through the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] by means of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/canal_magi\|magi channel]], the temporary interface between the extradimensional entity and the mortal. Direct manipulation of the Ethereal falls to the schools [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] and the dangerous [[_wiki_en/magia/fluxus_aetheris\|Fluxus Aetheris]].

### Attacks Between Planes

[[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|Devils]] and [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]] can attack other gods’ existential planes, but rarely do so, as it is costly and there is little to gain. The imprisonment of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] is an exception, and required an alliance between two devils and extreme motivation. The white gods ([[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]]) and green gods have mechanisms for protection against extraplanar attacks. The blue gods ([[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]]), having no defined existential plane, are immune to this type of aggression.

### Alignment Detection

Specialized spells can reveal the spiritual mark of pacts and the emotional color of Ardorados. Such spells are politically dangerous: Inquisitors capable of [[_wiki_en/magia/deteccao_de_alinhamento\|detecting pacts]] can identify traitors, spies, or false devotees. The same tool that protects the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] can destabilize it, depending on who uses it and against whom.
