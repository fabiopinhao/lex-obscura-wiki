# Divine Manifestations

A manifestation is any way in which a god touches the [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|material plane]] without being invoked by a mortal. It ranges from the involuntary side effect of mere divine attention, a signature in the environment, to a fraction of the god itself crossing the veil. Every manifestation is rare and costly, and even more so since the fragmentation of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]]: with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] growing thinner with each era, the gods manifest with perceptible effort.

### Gradient by Proximity

How corporeal a manifestation can be depends on the proximity of the divine category to the material plane. For the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], whose territory has a thin veil, the avatar is a condensed presence: a real fraction of the god crosses over, and therefore responds neither to bargaining nor to threats. For [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]], [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], and [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], who are more distant, the practical limit is an anchored projection, which requires a foothold in the material world, a marked one, a condemned one, or a consecrated relic. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]], without a plane or a body, do not manifest corporeally: they operate through consciousness, in dreams, fever, or perceived patterns.

### The Modes

Between attention and the avatar lies a scale of increasing cost. At the lowest end is the environmental signature, a side effect of divine attention, without an anchor. Next come the mark engraved on a consecrated or pact-bound body; the dream or vision in a bound mind; the voice or echo tied to a rite, temple, or relic; the active relic that houses a presence; the projection anchored in a marked one who is present; and, at the top of what is seen today, the avatar, a condensed presence that requires consecrated territory and an extreme trigger. Each mode costs more than the previous one, the decline of magi has made them all costlier, and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/manto_das_sombras\|Mantle of Shadows]] makes the steps for the white and green gods even costlier in [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]; the sidereals, who operate in the ethereal layer, escape the Mantle. When a rival divine power is already anchored in a target, it limits or blocks another god’s manifestation upon the same target.
