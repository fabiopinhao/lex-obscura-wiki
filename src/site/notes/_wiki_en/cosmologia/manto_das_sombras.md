# Mantle of Shadows

The Mantle of Shadows, also called the Continent of Shadows, is the regional metaphysical phenomenon that altered the planar balance of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. Decades of [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] rule, fueled by diabolical pacts on an institutional scale, saturated the continent with shadow energy. If the decline of luminous divine influence is a slowly receding tide, the Mantle is the dam that accelerated this process in Reinkard.

### Effects by Spectrum

The massive presence of shadow energy weakens the influence of the gods of the opposing spectra. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|white gods]] and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|green gods]] encounter greater resistance to exercising power in Reinkard: those bound to them by pacts feel that faith is more distant, their rituals require more effort, and their miracles become rare. It is not that these gods have lost power, but that the continent has become hostile ground for their energies. On the scale of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/manifestacoes_divinas\|divine manifestations]], each appearance of a white or green god, from environmental signature to avatar, costs more in Reinkard, and the rarity of their signs on the continent tells the story of this weakening on its own. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|red gods]] and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|blue gods]] are less affected: the demons because their chaotic nature does not depend on planar stability, and the sidereals because they inhabit the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], operating in a layer that the Mantle does not reach directly.

### Visible Manifestation

The [[_wiki_en/magia/macula-sombria\|Shadow-Taint]] in the forests is the visible face of the process: corrupted roots with black veins, blackened earth, withering flora. The blight predates the Shadars, but accelerated dramatically under [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]. [[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] raised among Shadars, bears the mark of this conflict within himself, a fragment of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|the Void]] inside someone whose nature should be green.

### Distinction from the Global Decline

The Mantle should not be confused with an older and broader process that precedes it. Since the fragmentation of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]] at the end of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|Forgotten Age]], the influence of white, green, and blue entities over [[_wiki_en/locais/thamakard\|Thamakard]] has been diminishing slowly and continuously with each age. The Mantle of Shadows is the regional acceleration of this global decline, not its cause. The distinction matters for understanding the different states of divine weakening in Reinkard: [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] is sealed and conscious, imprisoned through the actions of other gods, and could in theory be freed through a specific action; [[_wiki_en/panteao/azazel\|Azazel]] is free, but irrelevant in Reinkard as a consequence of the Mantle, and would need an entire civilizational transformation to regain a presence.
