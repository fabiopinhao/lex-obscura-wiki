# Nausea

Nausea is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school. It consists of a putrid emanation that induces malaise, dizziness, and vomiting: the target does not become ill, merely feels their own body rejecting itself. It differs from Discomfort, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which irritates without incapacitating, whereas Nausea can even knock the target down.

### Cost

The caster suffers an echo of malaise in their own stomach.
