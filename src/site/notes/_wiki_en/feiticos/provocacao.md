# Provocation

Provocation is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school. It awakens the target’s aggressive instinct, a primitive stimulus that compels the target to attack the nearest threat.

It differs from Injunction, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, which imposes a specific command: Provocation releases raw aggression without directing it. Disciplined opponents resist the impulse; ordinary soldiers yield to it. The cost is a surge of adrenaline followed by residual irritability.
