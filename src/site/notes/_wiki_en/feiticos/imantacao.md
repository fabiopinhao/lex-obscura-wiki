# Magnetization

Magnetization is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school that raises a defensive magnetic field over an area: metallic projectiles veer off course, blades lose accuracy, and armor weighs more. It differs from Deflection, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, which deflects by means of an arcane barrier: Magnetization acts through magnetism and affects only metal. The caster dictates the polarity, attracting metal to concentrate it at the center or repelling everything metallic.

### Cost

It leaves a residual charge: metal objects stick to the caster’s skin for hours.
