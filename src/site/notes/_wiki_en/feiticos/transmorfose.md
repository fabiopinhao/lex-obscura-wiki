# Transmorphosis

Transmorphosis is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school, at the Major scale. It transforms the target completely into an animal or creature, rewriting it entirely: the skeleton dissolves and reconfigures itself, mass is redistributed, and the organs re-form. Life and consciousness are preserved, trapped in alien instincts.

It differs from Transmutation, which produces partial deformation, and from Beast Skin, which transforms the caster themselves. It is also distinct from Petrification, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which converts the target into inert matter. Reversal requires the same caster or an equivalent [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]].

### Cost

For hours after the invocation, the caster feels their own body to be provisional.
