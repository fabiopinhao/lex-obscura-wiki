# Omniscience

Omniscience is a Maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school. The caster formulates a question and the answer is revealed: location, mystery, another person’s memory, the distant past. No informational barrier withstands the inquiry.

### Principle

Omniscience answers questions about the present and the past with absolute precision, and this is what distinguishes it from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/profecia\|Prophecy]], which shows a symbolic and uncertain future. Where Prophecy delivers images to interpret, Omniscience delivers the fact.

### Costs

The price of the answer is devastating. The caster suffers debilitating migraines and loses recent memories, as though another’s knowledge were driving out their own. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV, and leads to the condition of someone who knows everything and decides nothing.
