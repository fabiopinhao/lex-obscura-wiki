# Intoxication

Intoxication is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, a psychoactive poison that attacks the ability to reason. The target does not lose consciousness: they lose context. They forget what they were doing, mistake ally for enemy, and repeat purposeless actions.

It differs from Dulling, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which blunts cognition through mental pressure: Intoxication is chemical, a poison in the blood that affects the brain through the body. It lends itself to interrogation, extracting unfiltered answers, and to tactical sabotage, leading a commander to issue absurd orders. It can be removed by Catharsis or Purge. As a cost, the caster suffers empathic confusion, with their thoughts briefly becoming jumbled.
