# Muzzle

Muzzle is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school. It imposes silence on a mouth or an area: the voice dies in the throat without pain, verbal spells abort, and screams become pantomime. When applied to an area, it makes combat unfold in silence. It differs from Resonance, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school, which suppresses sound waves in the medium; Muzzle prohibits emission at the source.

### Cost

The caster’s own voice thickens over the spell’s duration, and words come out with increasing effort.
