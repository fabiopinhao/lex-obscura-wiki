# Catacronia

Catacronia is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school. The caster fragments time across an extensive area, and past and future collide: stone ages millennia in seconds, wounds reverse, the recently dead draw breath before collapsing. The creatures caught in it experience simultaneous chronologies. Where Devastation, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, destroys matter, Catacronia destroys linearity. The caster dictates the epicenter, not the outcome.

### Cost

The price is devastating. The caster loses temporal linearity for days, memories become disorganized, cause and effect are reversed. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
