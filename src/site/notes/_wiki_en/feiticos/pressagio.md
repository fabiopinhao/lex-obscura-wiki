# Omen

Omen is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school, of Medium scale. It grants a glimpse of the immediate future: the blow that will come from the left, the escape through the window, the knife hidden under the table.

### How It Works

It is not body reading, like [[_wiki_en/feiticos/instinto\|Instinct]] of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school, but perceived causality. Its effectiveness degrades as the variables increase: in a clear-cut duel, the glimpse is clear; in a pitched battle, it becomes noise.

### Cost

A sharp headache ensues, as though the brain were processing two simultaneous present moments.
