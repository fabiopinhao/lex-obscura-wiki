# Murmur

Murmur is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, on the Minor scale. It produces illusory sounds that come from where there is no source: footsteps behind an empty wall, a name whispered without a mouth to utter it.

### Functioning

Unlike [[_wiki_en/feiticos/ressonancia\|Resonance]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school, which manipulates real sound waves, Murmur creates purely illusory sound. The frequency and timbre are perfect; only the origin is false.

### Cost

A residual buzzing remains in the caster’s ears.
