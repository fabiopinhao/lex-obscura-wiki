# Thorns

Thorns is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school, of Major scale, that turns vegetation into a weapon. Through it, thorns erupt from the ground, roots burst forth like stakes, and branches splinter into piercing points. The affected area becomes lethal terrain, and the vegetation keeps growing for as long as the spell lasts.

It differs from Stake, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which launches stone projectiles: Thorns are organic, sprout from nothing in fertile ground, and continue to multiply.

### Cost

The caster suffers visible aging and joint pain, as if bones were sprouting inside their own body.
