# Sediment

Sediment is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, on the Minor scale. It alters the cohesion of a surface: loose soil compacts into rock, mud hardens, porous stone becomes dense. The effect also reverses, and solid rock becomes friable.

### Effect

The magic acts on a property of the material itself, not on its integrity. This is why Sediment differs from Fissure, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, which causes structural damage: where Fissure breaks, Sediment changes the nature of matter.

### Cost

The price falls on the caster’s body as joint stiffness, with the muscles hardening for a brief instant.
