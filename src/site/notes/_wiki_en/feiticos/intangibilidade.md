# Intangibility

Intangibility is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school through which the caster shifts partially into the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]]. The body becomes translucent, its substance rarefied, and walls, blades, and projectiles pass through it without resistance. Unlike Immersion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which travels within solid matter, Intangibility transcends matter completely.

### Limits

In exchange, the caster cannot touch anything on the material plane while the effect lasts. The state is unstable, and each second intensifies the feeling that solidity is a choice.

### Costs

The cost is ontological: doubt about what is real and a residual translucency that remains in the hands.
