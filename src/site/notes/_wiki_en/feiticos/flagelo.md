# Scourge

Scourge is a greater curse of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, an autonomous affliction that parasitizes the victim independently of the caster. It manifests as insomnia that does not abate, hunger that no meal satisfies, or chronic pain that migrates through the body. Unlike Hex, which requires the sustained will of the one who casts it, Scourge is a mature curse, loose in the world and capable of continuing on its own.

### Nature

Scourge integrates into the target’s arcane signature, and for this reason a simple nullification fails against it: the affliction is already part of the victim’s own magical structure. Removing it requires Catharsis, from the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, or Exorcism, from the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school.

### Costs

The price is permanent. Each Scourge cast leaves a black scar on the caster’s flesh, a mark that does not fade.
