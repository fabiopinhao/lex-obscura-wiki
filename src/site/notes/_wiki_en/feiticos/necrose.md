# Necrosis

Necrosis is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school. It rots living tissue while the body still functions: a localized gangrene that advances from the periphery to the center, the flesh darkening and dying in waves.

### Mechanics

It is distinct from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/necrotoxina\|Necrotoxin]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which corrodes from the outside like acid: Necrosis rots from within, with the organism itself producing the decomposition. It also differs from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/ceifar\|Reaping]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school, which drains life over an area; Necrosis is surgical and progressive.

### Cost

Spots appear in the caster’s own flesh that darken and ache, mapping the damage inflicted.
