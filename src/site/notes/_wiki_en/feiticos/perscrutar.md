# Insight

Insight is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school. Through direct access to truth as an arcane substance, the caster perceives a target’s lie, hidden emotion, and true intention, without penetrating their thoughts. The target may not even realize they have been read.

It differs from Intrusion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which invades the mind: Insight reads the truth of the situation, not thought itself.

### Cost

The practice leaves the caster temporarily unable to accept appearances. Faces begin to seem like masks and courtesy, calculation.
