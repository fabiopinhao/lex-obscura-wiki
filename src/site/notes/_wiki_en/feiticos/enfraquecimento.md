# Weakening

Weakening is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school. It directly degrades the target’s functional capacity: strength, perception, endurance. The victim simply fails, with no apparent organic cause.

### Distinction

Unlike Suppression, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which blocks the arcane, Weakening erodes the body without touching magic. The target does not rot, as under [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]], or fall ill: they merely lose capacity.

### Diagnosis and Reversal

Diagnosis detects the effect. Safeguard, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, and Nullification, of the Abjuro school, can reverse it.

### Cost

The caster feels the drained capacity as dead weight in their own limbs.
