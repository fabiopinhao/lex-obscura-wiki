# Detonation

Detonation is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school. The caster converts their own physical integrity into a destructive wave that radiates from the body: bones fracture, muscles tear, organs bleed internally, and the blast radius grows in proportion to the sacrifice. It is neither fire nor pure arcane force, but propagating rupture, with the caster’s flesh at the epicenter.

Unlike Obliteration, which destroys a single target with precision, Detonation is indiscriminate and strikes everything around it, fueled by the caster’s own body. The cost scales with the radius, from partial fractures to certain death. At its limit, it is a suicide weapon.
