# Necrotoxin

Necrotoxin is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school. It produces an organic acid that attacks living tissue exclusively: it rots flesh, dissolves muscle, and corrodes cartilage, but ignores metal, stone, and wood. It is a dark liquid that bubbles on contact with skin, darkening the flesh, forming blisters, and breaking the tissue down into a foul-smelling paste. Its specificity is its weapon: it can be applied to armor without damaging it, reaching the flesh through joints and gaps. It differs from Necrosis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school, which causes rot from within like a disease, while Necrotoxin corrodes from the outside like acid.

### Cost

The caster’s skin darkens at the points of projection, and tactile sensitivity decreases.
