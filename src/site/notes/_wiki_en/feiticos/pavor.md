# Dread

Dread is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school. It imposes on the target a fear of a specific object: it differs from Unease, which is diffuse and has no target, and from Apprehension, which is directed at an abstract catastrophe. Dread points, and *that over there* becomes the threat. Every fiber of the body demands distance, and the legs obey before judgment.

It is useful for dispersing formations and isolating targets, and can be resisted through martial discipline.

### Cost

The caster sees what the target fears, a brief apparition that unsettles without paralyzing.
