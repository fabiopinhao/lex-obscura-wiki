# Transcendence

Transcendence is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, of maximum scale, that allows the caster to temporarily transcend the physical plane. During the effect, he becomes pure essence: immune to any mundane effect and capable of passing through any barrier.

Unlike Intangibility, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, which merely shifts the caster partially into the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] and maintains his visible presence, Transcendence is total transcendence. The body dissolves into light and the caster comes to exist as pure consciousness, unable to affect the material world while he remains transcended.

### Costs

The return is devastating and violent. Back in matter, the solid body seems like a prison and the material world is repulsive. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV, when transcendence becomes the only goal and the mundane becomes unbearable.
