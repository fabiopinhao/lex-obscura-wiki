# Infection

Infection is a minor spell of the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]]. It acts on an existing wound, contaminating it to prevent natural healing: the flesh becomes inflamed, suppurates, and resists healing. It differs from Bloodletting, of the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]], which prevents clotting through chemical poison; Infection is biological, and the wound rots from within.

### Cost

The caster pays with a mild fever and skin tenderness.
