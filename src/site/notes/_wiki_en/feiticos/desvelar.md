# Unveiling

Unveiling is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school that saturates an area with radiance capable of dissolving concealment. Shadows of [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] recede, invisibility unravels, and illusions of [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] lose opacity; shapeshifters feel their false form slip. Unlike Diagnosis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which detects magic through reading, Unveiling reveals through light, forcing visual truth.

### Cost

The caster pays with visual hypersensitivity: insignificant details distract and overwhelm.
