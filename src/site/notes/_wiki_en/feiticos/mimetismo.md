# Mimicry

Mimicry is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school. It grants the caster a functional ability of a natural creature: gills to breathe underwater, night vision, protective scales, claws suited to climbing. One ability at a time, for a limited duration.

### How It Works

Unlike Visage, which is cosmetic and provides no new function, Mimicry alters the body’s actual biology. The adaptation manifests visibly: the skin becomes moist, the pupils narrow into slits.

### Cost

Reversion brings bodily disorientation, itching where the scales were, and the feeling that one’s own physical identity wavers.
