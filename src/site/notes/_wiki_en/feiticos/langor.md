# Languor

Languor is a lesser spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school. It makes the target’s mind grow heavy before the body does: the eyelids droop, thoughts turn to wet cotton, and attention falls apart. Sentinels lose focus; negotiators forget their argument. It differs from Lullaby, which dissolves physical tension; Languor attacks mental wakefulness.

### Cost

Drowsiness lingers in the caster, with reflexive yawning.
