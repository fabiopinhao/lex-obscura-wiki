# Extinction

Extinction is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school. It permanently eradicates the arcane essence of a target, whether an artifact, location, or creature.

### Function

Unlike Lacuna, which suppresses magic temporarily and over an area, Extinction is permanent and surgical: whatever it touches never channels magic again. The caster seizes the arcane flow and tears it out by the roots, and the target feels the effect as mutilation, silence where there was once music. No magic reverses an Extinction.

### Cost

The cost is devastating. The caster permanently sacrifices part of their own arcane capacity, and repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
