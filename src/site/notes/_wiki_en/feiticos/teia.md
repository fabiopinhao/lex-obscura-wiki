# Web

Web is a Medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school. It projects an adhesive organic secretion over an area, in viscous strands that trap, restrain, and suffocate.

### Effect

Brute force does not tear the strands: it stretches them and makes them stick more. Prolonged contact causes a mild chemical burn. This distinguishes it from Roots, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school that immobilizes through physical force: Web immobilizes through chemical adhesion. It can be removed by fire (it is flammable, and dangerous to anyone trapped in it), by a solvent, or by Nullification.

### Cost

The caster sweats a sympathetic secretion, a sticky substance that flows from their own skin.
