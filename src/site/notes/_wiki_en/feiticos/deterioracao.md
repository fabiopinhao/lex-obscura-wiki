# Deterioration

Deterioration is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school that sabotages objects and equipment at a distance: a weapon’s blade cracks, armor loses its temper, rope unravels fiber by fiber. It is gradual corrosion, not an instantaneous rupture.

### Principle

Deterioration acts at a distance, through slow corrosion. It differs from Severance, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, which ruptures immediately, and from Corrosion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which requires chemical contact.

### Cost

The caster’s personal belongings deteriorate in the same proportion.
