# Bubble

Bubble is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school. It imprisons the target in a suspended sphere of water, within which the captive floats motionless, visible but untouchable. The bubble is liquid, suffocates, and comes apart when the caster’s concentration ceases.

It differs from Suspension, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which removes gravity from the target. Bubble envelops the target in real water. The cost manifests in the caster as dehydration and pressure in the lungs.
