# Flow

Flow is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school and the most basic form of water control. It moves existing water: redirects currents, pulls puddles, diverts rain, separates volumes.

### Effect

Unlike Attraction, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which pulls any matter using gravity, Flow commands only water, but with greater precision.

### Cost

It causes mild dehydration, with a dry mouth.
