# Tremor

Tremor is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school. It causes a localized vibration in the ground.

### Effect

The vibration throws anyone touching the ground off balance, knocks them down, and interrupts their concentration. It can be precise or broad depending on the caster’s intent. It uses the earth as a medium and affects only those in contact with the ground, which distinguishes it from Pulse ([[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]]), which pushes by means of gravity.

### Cost

The caster feels stiffness in their feet, and their own balance wavers.
