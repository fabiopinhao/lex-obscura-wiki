# Veil

Veil is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school that conceals whoever or whatever receives it from magical detection. Under its effect, the arcane presence falls silent: artifacts cease to radiate a signature, and casters seem mundane.

### Range and Limits

It conceals magical presence, not physical presence. It differs from Pall, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which hides the body in shadow: Veil erases the arcane signature and leaves the body in plain sight. It yields to superior Diagnosis and to [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] or Scrutiny, which pierce the silence it imposes.

### Cost

The price is arcane isolation: while it lasts, whoever casts it temporarily loses their own magical sensitivity.
