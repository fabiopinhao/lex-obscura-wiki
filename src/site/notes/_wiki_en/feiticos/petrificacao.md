# Petrification

Petrification is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school. It transforms the target into stone from the inside out: the process advances from the extremities to the center, the fingers harden, the skin takes on the texture of granite, and the joints lock. The target feels each stage, with consciousness persisting until the last instant.

Unlike Glaciation, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, which immobilizes through external ice, Petrification transmutes the flesh itself, and the target becomes its own prison. The effect is reversible through [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] of equivalent potency. The cost to the caster is phantom calcification in the joints that progresses with repetition.
