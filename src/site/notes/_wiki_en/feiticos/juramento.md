# Oath

Oath is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school. It is a promise inscribed in flesh and soul: the pledged word becomes law over the speaker’s own body, and breaking the pact triggers a proportional consequence, ranging from pain to illness, from debilitation to death.

### Principle

Oath’s strength lies in the letter, not the spirit. The spell checks only action, not intent. Whoever swears “never to raise a weapon” pays the same price for acting on reflex as for deliberate betrayal, since what counts is the completed act, not the motive. It differs from Damnation, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which corrupts regardless of any action: Oath punishes only the breach of the pact that was sealed.

### Costs

The caster who resorts to Oath accumulates rigidity. Metaphors begin to confuse him, and ambiguity becomes a physical discomfort, as though the mind hardens to the same degree that the pledged word becomes absolute.
