# Immersion

Immersion is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school. The geomancer merges with the rock and travels within it: solid stone becomes a liquid medium, the body sinks, slides, and emerges at another point. No stone wall is a barrier, no fortification is secure, and the rock closes without leaving a trace.

The spell differs from Threshold, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which travels through shadow. Immersion travels through solid matter, without any need for darkness. The price is severe muscular stiffening upon emerging, with heaviness in the limbs as though the body remembered having been stone.
