# Immolation

Immolation is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school. With it, the warrior abandons all restraint: wounds do not register, fatigue does not exist, and each blow received fuels the next one delivered. It differs from Cruor, which scales with damage sustained, because Immolation is total surrender and the body burns vital reserves in real time. The muscles tear and rebuild themselves, and the bones absorb impacts that should break them. The cost comes when the effect ends and everything hits at once: collapse, internal bleeding, and masked fractures.
