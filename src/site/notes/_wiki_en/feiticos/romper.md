# Break

Break is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school. It breaks apart whatever binds: ropes, chains, locks, shackles, seals.

It differs from Seal, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, which locks through an arcane bond: where Nomos seals, Break frees through destructive force. The cost is a momentary stiffness in the hands, as though the joints were protesting.
