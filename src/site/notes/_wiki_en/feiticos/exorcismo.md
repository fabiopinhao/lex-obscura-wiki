# Exorcism

Exorcism is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school. It expels an invading entity from a body, object, or location: [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], spirits, anchored [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] possessions, fragments of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] with wills of their own.

### Practices

Unlike Purge, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which removes a minor enchantment from the caster themselves, Exorcism confronts a conscious entity that resists, and the spiritual combat sends the host into convulsions. If successful, it expels the presence; if unsuccessful, it strengthens it.

### Costs

The caster suffers partial contamination: they absorb residue from the expelled presence for days.
