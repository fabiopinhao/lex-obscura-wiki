# Flash

Flash is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school. It produces a brief burst of pure radiance: eyes close reflexively, tears flow, and the world turns white for a few seconds. It causes no permanent damage. It differs from Spark, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school, which interrupts through shock; Flash blinds with light.

### Cost

The caster suffers a reverse flash, a dark spot that remains in their vision.
