# Reconstitution

Reconstitution is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school that rewrites the biological composition of a living target. Bones become chitin, skin becomes scales, blood becomes sap, lungs adapt to water. The creature survives and remains functional, but with an anatomy radically different from the original, recognizable only by the consciousness that persists beneath the strange flesh.

It differs from Transmorphosis, which transforms the target into an already existing creature: Reconstitution creates a biology without precedent. The process is slow and agonizing for the target. The cost also falls on the caster, who experiences body dysmorphia for days, feeling that their own anatomy is arbitrary.
