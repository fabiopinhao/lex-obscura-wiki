# Seal

Seal is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school that locks a physical object by touch: a door, a safe, a drawer. Once sealed, matter refuses to open.

### Effect

The lock is arcane, not mechanical. Brute force acts against the object itself, never against the seal: the door is broken down, not the spell that holds it shut. It differs from Sealing, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which blocks access to a school of magic; Seal binds physical things.

### Cost

The caster feels stiffness in their fingers, as though the lock itself were mirrored in the body.
