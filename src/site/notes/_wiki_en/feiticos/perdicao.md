# Doom

Doom is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school. It inflicts the visceral certainty of cosmic annihilation, a terror that surpasses any fear of something specific and reaches beyond mortal endurance. Where Terror paralyzes the body and Horror distorts perception, Doom strikes the soul. Weak targets die when their hearts stop; strong targets survive broken, mute, catatonic.

### Cost

The caster is not immune to what they invoke: they suffer recurring nightmares, dissociation, and the growing suspicion that the horror they glimpsed was real. The ontological cost accelerates brutally toward Stage IV.
