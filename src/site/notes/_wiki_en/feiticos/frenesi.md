# Frenzy

Frenzy is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, on the Major scale. It injects homicidal rage into the target’s mind and suppresses the target’s recognition of allies and enemies.

### Effect

Under the spell, the target attacks the nearest creature with increasing violence, without pause and without calculation. It differs from Cruor, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school, which amplifies the affected individual’s own aggression: Frenzy replaces the target’s will with a rage imposed from outside. It is resisted through extreme mental discipline and through Safeguard, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school.

### Cost

The caster feels echoes of the rage they unleashed: an irritability that escalates over the course of hours, with violence coming to seem a natural response.
