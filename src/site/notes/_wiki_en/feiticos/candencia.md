# Brilliance

Brilliance is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school that imbues a weapon or object with concentrated radiance. The blade glows white, and its edge cauterizes as it cuts, leaving luminous streaks in the flesh. Against the undead, the touch of Brilliance is agony.

It differs from Ember, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school, which enchants with fire: Brilliance enchants with light and cauterizes instead of igniting. The caster bears the cost themselves, as the weapon heats up in their hands and prolonged use raises blisters on their fingers.
