# Blessing

Blessing is a medium-scale spell of the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]]. It grants sanctified vigor that amplifies a specific ability of the blessed individual: strength, endurance, perception, speed, or mental clarity. The choice is made during the invocation and remains fixed for the duration of the effect.

Unlike Fury, from the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], which amplifies through raw adrenaline and exacts a severe crash, Blessing amplifies with balance, without distortion. Against profane creatures, the blessed individual’s touch burns.

### Costs

The caster becomes hyperaware of the blessed individual’s weaknesses.
