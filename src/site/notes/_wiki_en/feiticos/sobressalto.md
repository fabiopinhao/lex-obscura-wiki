# Alarm

Alarm is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school that pours pure alarm into the target’s nervous system. The victim jumps, stifles a scream, reaches for their weapon, feels their heart race: a genuine physiological reaction with no real threat to sustain it.

It is instant panic, and in this it differs from Vertigo, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which produces disorientation. In practice, it serves to break an enemy’s stealth or to create a tactical distraction at precisely the right moment.

### Cost

The caster experiences a reflexive startle of their own when casting the spell.
