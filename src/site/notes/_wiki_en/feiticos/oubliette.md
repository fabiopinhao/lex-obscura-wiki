# Oubliette

Oubliette is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school. It creates a sealed pocket dimension: a chamber outside conventional geography, into which the target is pulled, and whose entrance closes like a scar in the air.

### Functioning

The internal space is finite and [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|empty]], with no door and no direction leading out. Time there flows normally, and therein lies the spell’s refined cruelty: the prisoner ages and goes mad at a human pace. Unlike Stasis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school, which freezes time, Oubliette keeps it running. Opening the chamber requires an [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] of equivalent power.

### Cost

The caster bears a literal weight on their spine, which increases with the duration of the imprisonment.
