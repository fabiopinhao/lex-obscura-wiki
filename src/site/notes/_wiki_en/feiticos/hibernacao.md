# Hibernation

Hibernation is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school. A wave of collective sleep spreads like a tide, and every conscious creature within the radius of effect collapses where it stands. There is no gradual resistance: the wave passes and the world falls asleep. The caster remains awake at the center of the silence.

The spell differs from Coma, which affects a single, controllable target. Hibernation affects an area and is indiscriminate. The cost is devastating: a drowsiness that blurs the boundary between sleep and reality for days, and repeated use accelerates Stage IV, in which the caster loses the certainty of being awake.
