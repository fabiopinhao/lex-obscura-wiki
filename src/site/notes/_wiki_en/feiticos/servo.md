# Thrall

Thrall is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school. It reanimates a corpse as a mindless automaton, powered only by [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]].

### Effect

The reanimated corpse obeys simple commands and is capable of rudimentary combat, but neither speaks nor feels: it is an empty shell. It differs from Revenant, which retains fragments of memory and will; Thrall retains none. The bond that animates it requires continuous maintenance.

### Cost

Casting and maintaining Thralls exacts from the caster a persistent chill and a sense of imminent death, proportional to the number of Thralls under their control.
