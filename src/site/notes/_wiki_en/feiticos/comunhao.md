# Communion

Communion is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school that weaves a dream network among multiple minds: allies coordinate in real time, transfer knowledge, and plan without words. Unlike the telepathy of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, a one-way invasion of one mind by another, Communion is a shared space. Unwilling targets can become trapped in the network, with their thoughts leaking out as a readable landscape. Its cost is exhaustion proportional to the number of connected minds, followed by devastating drowsiness upon ending the connection.
