# Confinement

Confinement is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school. It imposes absolute imprisonment: the body is completely immobile and the mind, completely present. The target neither sleeps nor faints, merely exists, conscious and immobilized.

It is distinct from Stasis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school, which removes consciousness, and from Oubliette, which still allows movement. Confinement occupies the cruelest intersection of the two, presence without agency. The cost is migratory stiffness in the caster, and prolonged Confinements eliminate their ability to make fine gestures.
