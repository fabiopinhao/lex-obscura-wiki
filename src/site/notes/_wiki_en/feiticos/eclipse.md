# Eclipse

Eclipse is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, large-scale darkness that actively consumes light: the sun, stars, and magical torches go out as though they had never existed. The darkness has weight, presses on the chest, and makes walking difficult, and sources of life weaken beneath it, with plants that wilt and animals that fall into torpor.

Where Blackness creates only a localized zone, Eclipse covers a vast area. The cost is devastating: the caster bleeds from the eyes and nose while sustaining it, and the area remains cold and sterile for days. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV, until light becomes an intolerable assault.
