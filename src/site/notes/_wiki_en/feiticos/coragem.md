# Courage

Courage is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, minor in scale, that dispels fear, despair, and emotional paralysis through touch or a word of faith. It removes what prevents bravery, without manufacturing it.

### Principle

Courage restores the ability to act under threat. It differs from Serenity, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, which removes aggression: one restores the drive to confront, the other soothes. Against [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], it functions as a direct counterattack.

### Cost

It leaves the caster with a residual apprehension from the dispelled fear.
