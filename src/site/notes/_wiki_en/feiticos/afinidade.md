# Affinity

Affinity is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school. It allows the caster to sense the presence of nearby living beings and establish rudimentary communication with fauna and flora: animals convey impressions, and plants reveal what touched their roots.

### Principle

Unlike Vigil, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school, which detects presences through arcane knowledge, Affinity senses life as a frequency, not as a mind.

### Cost

The price of the spell is hypersensitivity to the pain of others: whoever senses the life around them also feels its suffering.
