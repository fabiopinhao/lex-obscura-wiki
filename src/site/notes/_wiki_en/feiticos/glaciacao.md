# Glaciation

Glaciation is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, of maximum scale. It is absolute cold: everything freezes within a devastating radius, the air solidifies, sound disappears, and movement ceases.

### Practices

Unlike Stasis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school, which freezes time, Glaciation freezes matter, and the world becomes ice and silence. What does not die remains preserved, intact, and eternally trapped.

### Costs

The cost is devastating: hypothermia that threatens the caster’s life and emotions that grow cold for days. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
