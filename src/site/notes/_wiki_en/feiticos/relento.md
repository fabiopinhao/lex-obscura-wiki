# Frost

Frost is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school that lays a thin layer of ice over surfaces. The ground begins to betray one’s footing, locks seize, ropes turn to wire. It is a subtle, silent, and treacherous effect.

### Range

It differs from Resin, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which holds fast through adhesion. Frost does not stick: it betrays through the loss of friction.

### Cost

The price falls on the caster’s extremities, with cold and stiff fingers.
