# Shroud

Shroud is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school, of Medium scale. It coats the caster’s body in necrotic energy like a suit of armor: blows that strike the caster drain vitality from the attacker, and superficial wounds close with the stolen vigor.

### Effect

The defense does not absorb the impact; it retaliates against it. This distinguishes Shroud from Cuirass, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, which stops the blow through force: where Cuirass resists, Shroud strikes back by draining. While the spell lasts, the caster is cold to the touch, with grayish skin and dull eyes.

### Cost

The price is the sense of imminent death that accompanies the caster for as long as the spell is sustained.
