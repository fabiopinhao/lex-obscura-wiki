# Revenant

Revenant is a spell of greater scale from the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school that reanimates a dead person while preserving fragments of memory and a limited will. The creature recognizes faces, follows complex instructions, and wields weapons with dexterity.

### What Distinguishes It

It differs from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/servo\|Thrall]], an empty husk that only obeys simple commands: the Revenant retains an echo of the person it once was. It also differs from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/transe\|Trance]], from the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which controls the living through sleep; the Revenant animates the dead through necromancy. Without reinforcement, it deteriorates in a few days.

### Cost

It exacts a cost of deep cold and nightmares filled with the faces of the reanimated.
