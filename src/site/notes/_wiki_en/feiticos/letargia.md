# Lethargy

Lethargy is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school that simulates the state of death in the caster’s own body or in a willing target. The pulse stops, breathing becomes imperceptible, the body grows cold.

It differs from Lullaby, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which induces relaxation for sleep: Lethargy mimics death, making the body indistinguishable from a corpse to ordinary observers. The cost is a sensation of imminent death and a disoriented awakening.
