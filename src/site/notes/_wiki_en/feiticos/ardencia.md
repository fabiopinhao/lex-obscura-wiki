# Searing

Searing is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school. It produces oppressive heat without visible flame: the air stifles, sweat runs, and the skin dries out. It causes fever and heat exhaustion without anything glowing or burning, and even so the body gives way.

It differs from Incandescence, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school that heats solid matter through energy. Searing heats the air and bodies. Its cost is flushing and sweat on the caster’s own face.
