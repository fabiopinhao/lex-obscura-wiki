# Induction

Induction is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school. It infuses a stable electric current into a body, weapon, surface, or between two points. It differs from Brilliance, a spell of [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] that enchants a weapon with light, because Induction enchants it with electricity. A weapon conducts the discharge on contact, a wet surface transmits the shock, and the current stretched between two points creates a barrier. Its cost is a residual charge in the caster’s body, and metal objects adhere to the caster’s skin.
