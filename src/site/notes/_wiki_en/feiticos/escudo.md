# Shield

Shield is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school. It manifests as a disk of translucent force fixed in space, which absorbs physical blows and projectiles with resistance proportional to the caster’s investment.

Unlike Carapace, from the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, which protects the body with a layer of ice, Shield is pure force: stationary and frontal. Successive blows crack its surface until it collapses. Casting it comes at the cost of a persistent feeling that one’s back is exposed.
