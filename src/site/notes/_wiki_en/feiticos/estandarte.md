# Banner

Banner is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school, of Major scale, through which the caster projects a martial presence that anchors allies to the battlefield. While he remains standing, everyone within range resists flight, fear, pain, and exhaustion. If the caster falls, however, the effect reverses: morale collapses, and what sustained the troops begins to break them.

It differs from Clamor, which offers only a brief boost: Banner is a sustained presence as long as the caster stands firm.

### Cost

The caster cannot retreat without undoing the effect; mobility is sacrificed in exchange for anchoring.
