# Exhaustion

Exhaustion is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school. It induces psychosomatic fatigue in which the mind orders the body to stop: the muscles go limp, coordination fails, and every effort costs twice as much.

Unlike Weakening, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which degrades the target through a curse, Exhaustion is neurological, and the body obeys the mind’s surrender. Unlike [[_wiki_en/feiticos/febre\|Fever]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school, which drains through an immune response, Exhaustion is a collapse of motivation. The cost is residual fatigue in the caster, accompanied by involuntary yawning.
