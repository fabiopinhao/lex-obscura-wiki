# Dome

Dome is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, at maximum scale, that raises a sphere of absolute force: nothing enters, nothing leaves. Projectiles, blades, magic, sound, and air all stop at its surface. It provides perfect hermetic security, and perfection is precisely the problem, because the air is not replenished and no ally can pass through.

### Range

It differs from Lacuna, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which nullifies only magic within an area. Dome blocks everything, including matter and air.

### Cost

The price is devastating and grows over time. With each second, an ever-deepening conviction takes hold in the caster that lowering the protection means dying. Domes sustained for too long must be broken from outside, because those who raised them can no longer release them. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
