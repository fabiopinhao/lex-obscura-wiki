# Atrophy

Atrophy is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school. It degrades a specific muscle, tendon, or joint: the limb weakens, the grip loosens, the step falters. The tissue withers as though aging months in minutes.

It differs from Weakening, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which drains overall capacity through a curse. Atrophy is organic and localized, acting on the body and not on the will. The cost is sympathetic stiffness in the caster’s corresponding limb.
