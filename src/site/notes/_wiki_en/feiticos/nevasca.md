# Blizzard

Blizzard is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school. It raises a storm of ice and snow over an area: visibility drops to zero, the cold becomes lethal, and the buildup buries what was beneath it. The terrain ceases to exist beneath meters of white.

It differs from Cyclone, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school, which destroys through wind, and from Blackness, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which blinds through darkness: Blizzard kills through cold and burial, and blinds through total whiteness.

### Cost

The caster pays in severe hypothermia and tremors that persist for hours.
