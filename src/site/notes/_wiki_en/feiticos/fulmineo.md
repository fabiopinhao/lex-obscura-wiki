# Thunderbolt

Thunderbolt is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school at Maximum scale. It is an electrical discharge of catastrophic proportions: lightning strikes in succession where the caster points, voltaic arcs leap between metallic targets, and the air ionizes and burns.

### Effect

The magnetic field generated crushes armor and tears weapons from hands. It differs from Tempest, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school, which destroys through the force of wind: Thunderbolt destroys through pure electricity.

### Cost

The price is devastating: total nervous overload, temporary partial paralysis, and risk of cardiac arrest. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
