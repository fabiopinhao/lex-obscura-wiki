# Clamor

Clamor is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school. It functions as a battle cry that infuses allies with martial determination: the muscles sustain greater exertion, fear recedes, and the group’s coordination improves.

### Functioning

The effect is that of shared adrenaline, in which the body responds to the call of the pack. It differs from Courage, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, which dispels fear through faith; where Courage acts on the soul, Clamor acts on the body.

### Cost

The caster pays in hoarseness. The voice falters in proportion to the power of the shout.
