# Cleansing

Cleansing is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, a destructive radiance directed against corruption: arcane darkness, necrotic energy, possession. The light purges layer by layer, and undead exposed to it disintegrate like ash beneath the midday sun.

### Principle

It differs from Exorcism ([[_wiki_en/feiticos/exorcismo\|Exorcism]]), of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, which combats the invading entity through faith: Cleansing annihilates through pure radiance, without spiritual negotiation.

### Costs

The price falls on the caster’s body in the form of internal burns, high fever, and burst blood vessels in the eyes. The purity required is reflected in the flesh as a fever of light.
