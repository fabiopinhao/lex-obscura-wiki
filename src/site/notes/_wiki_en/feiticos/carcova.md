# Grasp

Grasp is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school that projects extensions of semisolid shadow to grab, constrict, and drag. Where the shadow tightens, the skin darkens and becomes necrotic: the marks take days to fade, and fingers trapped for too long do not regain their color. It differs from Shackle, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, which immobilizes through arcane prohibition, whereas Grasp restrains through matter of darkness.

### Cost

The caster pays with migrating numbness in the limbs and dark patches on the palms.
