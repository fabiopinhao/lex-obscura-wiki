# Tempest

Tempest is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school. Winds on a regional scale rip structures from their foundations, hurl bodies like debris, and make the horizon disappear into a rotating wall of darkness. In the eye of the storm, absolute silence reigns; around it, annihilation. Nothing that is not anchored to bare rock survives. Where Cataclysm, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, reshapes the earth, Tempest reshapes the sky.

### Cost

The price is devastating. The caster burns from within, the pressure in the skull ruptures blood vessels, and permanent deafness is likely. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
