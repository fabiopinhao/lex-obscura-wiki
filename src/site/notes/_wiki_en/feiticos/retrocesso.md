# Reversal

Reversal is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school. It reverts a target or object to an earlier state: wounds close in reverse, blood returns, tissue reconnects, bone fuses again.

### Effect

Unlike Mending, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, which accelerates natural healing, Reversal rewinds time and restores what once existed. It does not create a state that never existed or regenerate a limb that was missing from birth, and its temporal reach depends on the caster’s power. The cost is inverted memory: fragments of the target’s past become confused with one’s own memories.
