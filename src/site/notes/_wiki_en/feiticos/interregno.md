# Interregnum

Interregnum is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school, a temporal pause with greater range than Pause. Objects come to a halt for minutes; living beings, for agonizing seconds during which consciousness persists but the body does not respond. The target hears voices and sees movement without reacting.

It differs from Stasis, which removes consciousness along with time: Interregnum keeps the target awake within their own prison. The cost is that the present feels displaced to the caster, and the notions of “already happened” and “will happen” become confused.
