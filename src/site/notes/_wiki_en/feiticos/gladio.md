# Gladius

Gladius is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school. It condenses plasma into a blade that the caster wields. The weapon has no weight, only heat and an edge: it cuts without resistance, cuts metal open with molten edges, and seals flesh as it divides it. It differs from Brilliance, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school that enchants an existing weapon, because Gladius creates the entire weapon from plasma. It emits a continuous hum. Its cost is a tingling sensation that travels up the arm and burns on the forearm with prolonged use.
