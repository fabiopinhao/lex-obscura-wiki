# Obliteration

Obliteration is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school. It completely destroys a single target: an object, a barrier, a section of wall. Structural integrity ceases within that precise volume, and no fragment or dust remains.

It differs from Detonation, which strikes an indiscriminate area, and from Demolition, which causes sequential collapse: Obliteration targets a single point and is absolute. Such precision requires extreme concentration.

### Cost

The price is a sharp pain in the joints, as though the bones remembered what it means to cease to exist.
