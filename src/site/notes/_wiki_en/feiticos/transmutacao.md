# Transmutation

Transmutation is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school that alters a target’s form against its will: it shrinks, deforms, and partially animalizes the target.

### Effect

The target feels its own flesh rearranging itself without consent: skin stretching, bones shrinking, proportions distorting. The change is reversible only by the original caster or by the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school. It differs from Grotesque, which the caster applies to themselves; Transmutation is an imposition on another.

### Cost

A phantom echo of the deformations they imposed remains in the caster.
