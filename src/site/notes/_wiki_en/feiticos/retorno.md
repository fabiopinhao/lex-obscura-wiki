# Return

Return is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school. It reverses a recent death while the body remains present and viable and the spirit is still nearby, a matter of minutes rather than hours. The caster presses both hands against the dead person’s chest, and the heart resumes beating.

It differs from Reprieve, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, a prior enchantment that intercepts death: Return is a subsequent intervention and requires the healer’s presence. The fatal wound persists and requires immediate Mending.

### Cost

The healer gives up years of their own life, in the form of real and irreversible aging.
