# Interdiction

Interdiction is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school. It blocks a casting in progress before it manifests: the caster perceives the buildup of energy in the opponent and interposes a negation that aborts the process. The targeted spell fails, and the original caster bears the cost without achieving any result.

It differs from Suppression, which blocks the ability to cast for a time: Interdiction nullifies a single spell at the exact instant of its formation. Against effects already active, it is useless. The cost falls on whoever casts it in the form of constant anticipatory tension, which wears them down over time.
