# Bloodletting

Bloodletting is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school. It applies an anticoagulant poison that prevents healing: open wounds do not close, and superficial cuts bleed as though they were deep.

### Effect

Unlike Infection, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school, which contaminates tissue with disease, Bloodletting is chemical and attacks clotting without causing illness, and requires prior damage to act. The [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school can force the wound closed, but must first overcome the poison. The cost is the caster’s vascular fragility: spontaneous bruising and wounds of their own that are slow to close.
