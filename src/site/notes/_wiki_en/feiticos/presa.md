# Prey

Prey is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school that designates a target as the group’s prey. The caster’s allies begin to perceive the marked target as a priority threat: attacks converge on it, blows find the gaps, arrows seek the joints in the armor.

The effect is not telepathy, but a pack instinct that coordinates the collective attack. It differs from Mark, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which tracks the target for the purpose of cursing: Prey serves the group’s convergence, not a lone caster’s pursuit. The cost falls on whoever casts the spell, who becomes fixated on the target and loses peripheral awareness.
