# Lapse

Lapse is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school. It imposes a brief disruption in the target’s temporal flow: movement slows, the arm delivering a blow loses speed halfway through, the legs drag invisible mud.

### Use and Cost

It is a momentary effect, a single instant of disrupted timing, enough to dodge or strike the first blow; it differs from the sustained slowness of other spells of the school. The cost falls on the caster, whose reflexes dull for a few seconds.
