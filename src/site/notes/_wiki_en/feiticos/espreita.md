# Lurking

Lurking is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school used to scan an area for conscious minds. Each thinking presence registers as a point of heat, and the caster distinguishes intensity, agitation, and number.

It differs from Vigil ([[_wiki_en/feiticos/vigilia\|Vigil]]), of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school, which detects hostile intent and movement: Lurking reads the mind, not the body. The cost is a residual murmur of others’ thoughts.
