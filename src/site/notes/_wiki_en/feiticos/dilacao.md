# Dilation

Dilation is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school. It stretches time over the target until each second lasts a subjective eternity: it does not stop time, but slows it down. Steps become a dragging shuffle, words stretch out, and blows lose force.

Unlike Lapse, which is instantaneous and localized, Dilation is sustained over time. Unlike Burden, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which increases the target’s weight, Dilation preserves mass and only slows time. The cost is lethargy in the caster’s own limbs, proportional to the depth of the effect.
