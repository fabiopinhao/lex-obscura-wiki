# Ember

Ember, also called Infusion, is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school that envelops weapons and objects in flames that burn without consuming the base material. The blade’s edge burns as it cuts, and the projectile sets its target alight as it embeds itself. Unlike Brilliance, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, which imbues the object with radiance, Ember is real fire: it spreads and sets fire to whatever it touches. It also differs from Gladius, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, which forges a blade of pure energy, while Ember enchants a weapon that already exists.

### Cost

Fire exacts a toll from whoever carries it: an internal burn in the arm that wields the weapon.
