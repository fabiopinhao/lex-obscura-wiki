# Meteor

Meteor is a spell of maximum scale from the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]]. The caster points to the sky and a rock is torn from orbit, or raised from the earth and melted by its own speed. The projectile is not controlled: once summoned, it descends in a straight line. The impact is a gravitational collapse, with the crater forming before contact and the ground giving way under the pressure of what is approaching. Stone vitrifies, structures flatten, and bodies are incorporated into the soil. The radius of destruction is vast and the precision, moderate.

### Cost

Existential. The caster feels their own body as a falling object; for hours, walking requires remembering that the ground is below.
