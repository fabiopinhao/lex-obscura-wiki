# Discomfort

Discomfort is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school that imposes persistent irritation without incapacitating: migrating itching, burning eyes, intermittent ringing in the ears. Unlike Infection, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school, which causes actual disease, Discomfort neither sickens nor weakens, only bothers without cease. Days under its effects break resistance that physical torture cannot reach.

### Cost

The caster suffers a muted empathic echo of the discomfort they impose.
