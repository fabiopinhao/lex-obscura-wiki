# Gust

Gust is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school. It produces a single, directional gust of air.

### Effect

The gust pushes light objects, disperses smoke and gases, and extinguishes exposed flames. It uses real air and affects only what the wind reaches, which distinguishes it from Pulse ([[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]]), which pushes through gravity.

### Cost

The caster feels back pressure in the chest and a sensation of shortness of breath.
