# Flight

Flight is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school that grants full aerial movement, sustained by transcendence. There are no wings, wind, or mechanisms: the body moves through the air silently, stable, indifferent to the weather, and direction and speed respond to the intent of the one flying.

### Principle

Movement arises from detachment. The freer the caster is from earthly anchors, the more natural flight becomes; strong passions pull the body back down. It differs from Levitation, which is restricted to the vertical axis and contemplative in nature: Flight is free movement.

### Cost

The price is ontological. Each flight instills in the one who performs it the conviction that the ground is a prison, and returning to the ground comes to require a reason.
