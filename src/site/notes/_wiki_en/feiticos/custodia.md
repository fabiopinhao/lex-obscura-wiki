# Custody

Custody is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school. It creates a protective bond with an ally: part of the damage the ally receives is redirected to the caster, in the form of exhaustion, nausea, and pressure in the bones.

### Mechanics

The bond shares damage continuously for as long as it persists. It differs from Reprieve, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, which intercepts death; Custody divides damage over time. An extreme variant absorbs a fatal blow, which breaks the bond and lays the caster low.

### Cost

In addition to the shared damage, an ontological cost takes root: protecting becomes a necessity, not a choice.
