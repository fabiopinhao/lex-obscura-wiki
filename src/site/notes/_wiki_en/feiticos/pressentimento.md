# Intuition

Intuition is a Minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school. It offers the caster directional certainty about the presence of corruption, desecration, or evil: undead, demonic creatures, cursed objects.

### Principle

Intuition detects only evil, and with a moral precision that other forms of detection lack. It differs from Diagnosis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which indicates any magic without distinguishing its nature. Where Diagnosis registers the presence of the arcane, Intuition judges its intent.

### Costs

The price is intimate: when the impurity of the world is indicated, the caster’s own impurity briefly becomes visible to them.
