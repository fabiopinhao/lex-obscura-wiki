# Lash

Lash is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school that projects jets, whips, and blades of pressurized water capable of cutting flesh, splitting wood, and scoring stone. Unlike Slash, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school, which cuts with invisible compressed air, Lash is visible, traceable, and depends on available water, but cuts deeper. Its cost is the caster’s dehydration, proportional to the volume of water projected.
