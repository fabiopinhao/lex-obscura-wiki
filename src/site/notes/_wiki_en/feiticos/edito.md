# Edict

Edict is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, of maximum scale, in which the caster declares a law and reality obeys. The imposed rule becomes an environmental condition: when “no one lies” is declared, throats seize up; when “no blade wounds” is declared, edges grow dull. Anyone who violates the law is punished, and Edict does not distinguish ally from enemy.

### Principle

Edict imposes an arbitrary rule through arcane authority. It differs from Armistice ([[_wiki_en/feiticos/armisticio\|Armistice]]), of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, which imposes peace through spiritual elevation: Edict does not elevate; it decrees, and punishes those who disobey.

### Costs

The defining cost of Nomos is internalization: the caster begins to live under the very law they imposed. Anyone who decreed “no one lies” becomes incapable of lying. Frequent Edicts calcify the mind to the point of absolute rigidity.
