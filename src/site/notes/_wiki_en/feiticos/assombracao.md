# Haunting

Haunting is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school. It projects an illusion that persists after the caster leaves and reacts to its surroundings as if it had a will of its own: a sentinel that patrols, a shadowy figure that flees when followed, a face that appears in the window.

Unlike Mirage, which must be continuously sustained, Haunting is autonomous and lasts for days without maintenance. It is not consciousness, but a script inscribed in the illusion itself. It can be destroyed by intense light, by the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, or by the absolute certainty of whoever faces it.

### Costs

The illusion left behind draws away a fragment of the caster’s attention. Their concentration remains fragmented for the days that Haunting persists.
