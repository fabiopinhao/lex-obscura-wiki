# Reliquary

Reliquary is a greater-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school. It captures the soul of someone newly dead before it dissipates, trapping it in a prepared receptacle. The trapped soul can be interrogated, drained as [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] fuel, or used as an anchor for greater reanimation. Destroying the receptacle frees the soul.

It differs from Communion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which connects living minds: Reliquary imprisons dead minds.

### Cost

The price is a spiritual burden: each stored soul whispers at the edges of the caster’s consciousness.
