# Eruption

Eruption is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school. The caster plants their feet and the ground obeys: rock liquefies within a devastating radius, columns of magma erupt in incandescent geysers, and the air ionizes until it glows purple.

### Practices

Everything solid becomes fluid. Unlike Cataclysm, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which reshapes the terrain, Eruption liquefies it. It is not fire: it is the state of matter when energy overwhelms it.

### Costs

The cost is devastating. The skin peels off in sheets and the eyes lose their whites. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
