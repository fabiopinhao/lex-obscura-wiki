# Apogee

Apogee is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school in which the caster becomes a source of radiance: the body shines until the bones become visible beneath the translucent skin, and the light expands in a wave that sweeps through everything around it. Unlike Eclipse, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which consumes light, Apogee releases it in absolute excess, annihilating darkness, unmaking creatures of shadow, and heating matter to the point of ignition. The cost is devastating: blood vessels burst, the retinas burn, and color perception is permanently damaged. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV, a condition in which the world without light becomes intolerable.
