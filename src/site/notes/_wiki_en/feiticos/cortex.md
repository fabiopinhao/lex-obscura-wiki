# Cortex

Cortex is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school that hardens the body’s surface with an organic coating: the skin takes on the texture of bark, scales, or chitin. It is a natural protection that absorbs impact without restricting movement.

### Principle

Cortex is biological and warm, growing from the flesh itself. It differs from Cuirass, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, which protects through arcane force, and from Carapace, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, which coats the body in ice.

### Cost

It causes mild aging and stiffness when it ends.
