# Suppression

Suppression is a medium-scale spell of the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] that temporarily blocks a living target’s magical ability. A field of negation chokes the victim’s arcane connection: invocations fail, enchantments come out weak, energy slips through the fingers.

The attack acts on the arcane source itself, not on the emission. This distinguishes it from Muzzle, of the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], which silences the voice and blocks the verbal component. The effect also varies with the target’s strength: weak casters remain mute for minutes, while powerful ones feel only momentary choking.

### Cost

Through a sympathetic bond, the caster feels their own magic stifled to the same degree they impose on the target.
