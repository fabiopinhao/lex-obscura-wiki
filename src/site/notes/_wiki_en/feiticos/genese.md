# Genesis

Genesis is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school that instantly creates plant life on a massive scale: a forest rises from bare soil, roots shatter stone, the canopy blocks the sky.

### Principle

It differs from Cataclysm ([[_wiki_en/feiticos/cataclismo\|Cataclysm]]), of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which reshapes the terrain through destruction: Genesis reshapes it through creation.

### Costs

The caster yields their own life force as a seed and visibly ages in doing so: years in seconds. The price includes a hunger for vitality that drives the caster to be near the young, and repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
