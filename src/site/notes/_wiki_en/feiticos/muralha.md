# Rampart

Rampart is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school. It raises a barrier of earth or stone from the ground: a wall, trench, or improvised barricade. It is real stone, with weight and mass, and projectiles ricochet off it, blades bend against it, and fire loses range. It differs from Barrier, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, which blocks through arcane force.

### Cost

The caster feels weight on their shoulders and back, as though the raised mass were pressing down on them.
