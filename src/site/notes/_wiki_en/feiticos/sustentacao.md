# Lift

Lift is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school that raises updrafts to cushion falls, extend jumps, and allow brief gliding.

It differs from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/pluma\|Feather]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, which transcends weight through will; Lift relies on real wind, and the air slows the fall without transcending it. When it ceases, it exacts vertigo and the phantom sensation of continuing to fall.
