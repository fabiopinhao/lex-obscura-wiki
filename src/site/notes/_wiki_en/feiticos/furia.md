# Fury

Fury is a Minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school. It triggers a surge of adrenaline in the caster’s own body: pain is dulled, reflexes quicken, and hesitation is suppressed.

### Effect

It differs from Blessing, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, which enhances ability in a balanced way: Fury is a brief burn, a few seconds of raw power.

### Cost

When it ends, the adrenaline crash follows: exhaustion and trembling.
