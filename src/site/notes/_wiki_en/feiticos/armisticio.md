# Armistice

Armistice is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school. The caster declares a zone of peace and space obeys: violence becomes impossible, hands lose strength midway through a blow, weapons miss, offensive spells dissipate. It does not distinguish aggressor from defender, since all violence is forbidden, including defensive violence.

It differs from Edict, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school that imposes a rule through arcane law. Where Nomos punishes those who transgress, Urano removes the very ability to transgress, imposing peace through elevation. It works best under an open sky and in sacred places. Its cost is spiritual exhaustion, and each prevented blow weighs on the caster.
