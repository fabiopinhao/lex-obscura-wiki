# Radiance

Radiance is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school: a concentrated beam of radiance that burns wherever it touches. The skin reddens, blisters form, metal heats up. Against creatures of darkness, the light not only burns: it devours.

It differs from Lightning ([[_wiki_en/feiticos/relampago\|Lightning]]), of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school, which wounds through electrical discharge: Radiance burns through pure light, without flame and without smoke. The cost is a phantom burn on the caster’s palm, with a persistent burning sensation.
