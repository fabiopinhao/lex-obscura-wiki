# Discernment

Discernment is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school. Where Scrutiny reveals what a thing is, Discernment reveals how it works: its internal logic, its vulnerabilities, and its history of use.

### Function

The magic exposes a target’s mechanics and the conditions for its dissipation. It goes beyond Diagnosis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which only identifies school and intensity.

### Cost

The cost is proportional to the target’s complexity. Ancient artifacts drain the caster like books that refuse to close.
