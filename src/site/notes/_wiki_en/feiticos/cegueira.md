# Blindness

Blindness is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school that covers the target’s eyes with darkness that clings like liquid tar. It is not darkness all around, but darkness within: stuck to the eyelids, seeped into the pupils. Unlike Blur, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, which merely confuses outlines, Blindness negates sight completely. Its cost falls on the caster, in the form of dark spots in the field of vision and increasing photophobia.
