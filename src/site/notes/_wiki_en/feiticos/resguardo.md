# Shelter

Shelter is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school. It creates an emanation that filters arcane effects before they reach the flesh: curses arrive diluted, magical poisons weakened, supernatural cold becomes tolerable. Unlike Safeguard, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, which purifies through holiness, Shelter merely attenuates. It does not cleanse what it filters.

### Cost

The protection demands hypervigilance: every unexpected sound triggers an alert in whoever sustains it.
