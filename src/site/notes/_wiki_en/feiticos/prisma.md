# Prism

Prism is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school. It fragments a beam of radiance into independent vectors, each tracking a distinct target. It can be charged: the portions of light orbit the caster until they are launched. Unlike Shard, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, which fragments matter, Prism fragments light itself into guided projectiles.

### Range

Each fragment is weaker than a Radiance, but the spell’s strength lies in numbers: six simultaneous vectors leave nowhere to run.

### Cost

After use, the caster’s vision fragments. The world breaks down into spectra for a few minutes.
