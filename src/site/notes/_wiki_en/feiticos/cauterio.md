# Cautery

Cautery is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school. It burns to seal: it closes wounds with heat, brands flesh with iron, and welds metal. It is the utilitarian and brutal side of fire.

The spell differs from Suture, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, which closes tissue through healing. Cautery closes through destruction, burning what bleeds. The price falls on the caster: a burning sensation in the palms and dry skin.
