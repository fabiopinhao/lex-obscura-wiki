# Stake

Stake is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school. It rips stone from the ground and hurls it: spikes, sharp splinters, columns that burst forth to impale.

### Distinction

Unlike Shard, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, which breaks what already exists, Stake creates the projectile from the terrain itself. The more mineral material available, the more ammunition.

### Cost

The caster feels their arms grow heavy and their muscles stiffen in proportion to the volume hurled.
