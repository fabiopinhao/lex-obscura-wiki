# Evanescence

Evanescence is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, of Medium scale, through which the caster erases himself from visual perception. It is neither darkness nor transparency: it is irrelevance. The eye passes over the space where he is and decides there is nothing to see. The shadow persists, the floor creaks beneath his feet, and the smell remains. Direct attention to the caster, or the spell Unveiling, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, dispels the effect.

It differs from Disguise, which replaces appearance: Evanescence removes presence instead of exchanging it.

### Cost

The caster becomes partially translucent to himself, gripped by doubt about his own solidity.
