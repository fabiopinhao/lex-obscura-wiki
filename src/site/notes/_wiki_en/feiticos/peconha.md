# Venom

Venom is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school. It poisons a weapon, object, or ingestible substance with a poison that dulls the senses, numbs reflexes, and induces torpor.

Unlike Ember, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school, which adds elemental damage, Venom debilitates without inflicting injury. When applied to a weapon, the poison is depleted after the first strike; in substances, it retains its potency until consumed. The cost to the caster is a metallic taste and numbness of the tongue.
