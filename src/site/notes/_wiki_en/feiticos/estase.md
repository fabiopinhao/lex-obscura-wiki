# Stasis

Stasis is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school. It freezes the target completely in time: heart, blood, thought, and aging all stop. While it persists, the target remains untouchable and indestructible, but inert.

Unlike Confinement, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, which immobilizes the body while preserving consciousness, Stasis suspends both. Unlike Glaciation, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, which freezes matter through cold, Stasis freezes time itself. It can serve as protection or as a prison. The cost is desynchronization: recent events begin to seem like old memories.
