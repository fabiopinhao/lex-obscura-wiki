# Vigil

Vigil is a Minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school that expands the caster’s perception to register presences, hostile intentions, and movements as pressure on the back of the neck. Unlike Lurking, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school that detects conscious minds, Vigil senses movement and intent: it works against automatons and the undead, and operates even in total darkness.

The cost is residual insomnia, a hypervigilance that does not switch off.
