# Rending

Rending is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, on the Major scale, that applies opposing gravitational forces to the same target at the same time. The body is pulled in opposite directions: the limbs move away from the torso, the joints separate, and matter breaks apart into fragments that move away from one another without explosion or heat. It is a silent effect, surgical on objects, in which a suit of armor comes apart piece by piece, and catastrophic on living bodies.

### Distinction and Cost

It differs from Obliteration ([[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]]), which negates the cohesion of matter. Rending exceeds it: matter remains intact, merely displaced to the wrong place. The cost falls on the caster, who begins to feel their own body as poorly fitted pieces.
