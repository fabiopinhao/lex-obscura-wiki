# Desiccation

Desiccation is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school. It extracts water from living matter or the environment: plants blacken, the soil cracks, skin wrinkles like parchment. The target feels their mouth go dry, their eyes burn, and their muscles refuse exertion.

### Distinction

Unlike Siphon, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school, which drains life force, Desiccation steals water as a physical substance, not essence. Where [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] nourishes, Desiccation subtracts.

### Cost

It causes severe dehydration in the caster, whose skin cracks and peels.
