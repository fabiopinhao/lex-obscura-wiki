# Singularity

Singularity is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, Major in scale. It fixes a point of intense gravitational attraction in space: everything within a radius is pulled toward the center, bodies, objects, projectiles, and the air itself. The point does not move; it is the world around it that contracts around it.

### Effect

Light objects fly first; heavy ones slide and then accelerate. Anyone who crosses into the inner radius cannot escape without outside help. The effect is indiscriminate, making no distinction between ally and enemy. This sets it apart from a whirlpool of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school, which pulls by means of a current: Singularity pulls by gravity and works in any medium. Sustaining it requires total concentration.

### Cost

The price is a distortion of the caster’s own perception: the caster feels their own body as a point of gravity.
