# Haste

Haste is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school that compresses the target’s personal time and accelerates their perception and reactions. The surrounding world seems to slow down: blades that were blurs become trackable, and openings in the opponent’s guard last long enough to be exploited.

It differs from Fury, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school, which amplifies reflexes through adrenaline: Haste alters time, not physiology, and therefore improves neither strength nor endurance. When it ends, a perceptual shock ensues, with seconds lost and chronology jumbled.
