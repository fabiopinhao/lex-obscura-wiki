# Catharsis

Catharsis is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school. It purges what ordinary remission cannot reach: magical diseases, lesser curses of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, and resistant plagues of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school. The caster tears out the affliction layer by layer.

It differs from Purge, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which removes an enchantment from one’s own body through technique. Catharsis purifies another through empathy, and the expulsion is visceral, in dark pus and black sweat. The cost is high: the healer experiences the symptoms of the purged disease for hours.
