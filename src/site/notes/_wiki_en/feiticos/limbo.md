# Limbo

Limbo is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school. The caster forces the target into the space between the planes, neither material nor ethereal, where no plane recognizes the presence of the banished one. The world looks like frosted glass: shapes move, sounds arrive muffled, and nothing responds to touch.

It differs from Oubliette, a spell of [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] that creates a sealed dimensional pocket: Limbo is the interval, not an enclosure. Returning from it requires external intervention, whether an [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]], a Portal of [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]], or natural dissolution.

### Cost

Devastating. The caster feels the pull of the boundary as translucency in their own hands and uncertainty about which side they ended up on. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
