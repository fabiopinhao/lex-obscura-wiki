# Crushing

Crushing is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school that turns solid matter itself against a target. Through it, the caster closes stone walls in on whoever they target, compresses a body between rocky surfaces, or causes a cave’s ceiling to collapse onto whoever is below. The pressure is slow and inexorable, and bones give way before the scream.

It differs from Burden, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which increases the target’s weight through gravity: Crushing moves actual matter against the body, instead of making it heavier.

### Cost

Those who cast it feel squeezing pain in their chest and joints, and a sense of heaviness that persists for hours after the spell.
