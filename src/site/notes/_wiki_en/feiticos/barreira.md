# Barrier

Barrier is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school. It raises a semitransparent wall of force that blocks a corridor or protects a formation. It is impenetrable to projectiles and only partially permeable to high-powered magic, and it ripples under impact like vertical water.

Unlike Rampart, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, it has no substance: it disappears as soon as the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] sustaining it ceases. The cost scales with the pressure it withstands, and constant bombardment instills in the caster the certainty that the protection is never enough.
