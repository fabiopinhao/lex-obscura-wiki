# Demolition

Demolition is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school that identifies a structure’s critical load-bearing point and destroys it, initiating a sequential collapse: the pillar gives way, the arch collapses, the wall topples, the tower crumbles. Each failure triggers the next.

### Nature

It differs from Detonation, which radiates a destructive wave from the body, and from Obliteration, which completely removes a single target. Demolition is architectural destruction, intelligence applied to ruin: it requires structural assessment, not brute force.

### Cost

Casting causes joint pain that scales with the size of the structure, and the sensation that one’s own skeleton has lost a point of support.
