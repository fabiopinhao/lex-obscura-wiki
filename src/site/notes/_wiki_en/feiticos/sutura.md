# Suture

Suture is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, intended to close external wounds. Under its effect, the edges of a cut meet, ruptured vessels seal, and the skin fuses into a pink line; bleeding and surface lacerations subside.

### Range and Limits

It acts on what is external. It differs from Mending, which works deep inside the body: Suture is superficial, reserved for cuts and visible bleeding.

### Cost

The price falls on the caster. Their own fingers burn, and the burning increases in proportion to the severity of the wound being treated.
