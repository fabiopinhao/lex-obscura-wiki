# Release

Release is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school. It unlocks a lock, seal, or arcane bond through authority, not force: locks yield, knots come undone, Seals of Nomos dissolve.

### Effect

Where Break, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, breaks apart through destruction, Release undoes through the same logic that created the bond, the Law revoking the Law. It fails, however, against seals cast by a more powerful caster.

### Cost

The next door the caster closes will not seem secure to them.
