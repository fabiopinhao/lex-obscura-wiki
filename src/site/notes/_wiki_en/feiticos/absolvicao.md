# Absolution

Absolution is a spell of greater scale from the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school. At the caster’s touch, arcane bonds come undone: [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] shackles crack, forced oaths dissolve, and [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] curses lose their hold.

It does not operate through negation, like [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]], which nullifies magic, but through transcendence: the bond continues to exist, and the target rises above it. It works against external impositions, while voluntary bonds resist in proportion to the sincerity of those who accepted them. The cost is a spiritual weight, a phantom echo of another’s obligation, and frequent absolvers have a stooped posture.
