# Horror

Horror is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school. The target’s perception is contaminated by their worst fears, and the real world distorts until it confirms every dreaded horror: shadows become threats, friendly faces seem like predators, innocuous sounds sound like harbingers of death.

Unlike Simulacrum, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, which creates an external illusion, Horror rewrites internal perception. The target sees reality through a filter of terror, and there is nothing to dispel in the surroundings because the distortion is mental. The cost extends to the caster, who experiences flashes of the target’s contaminated perception, with reality briefly tinged with another’s horror.
