# Inferno

Inferno is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school. It is a devastating conflagration that consumes everything within a radius, a firestorm in which the air itself becomes fuel. The caster, at the center, is the last to burn, but does burn.

The spell differs from Eruption, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, which melts matter into magma. Inferno chars through combustion and leaves ashes and blackened ruins, not glass. The price is devastating: severe internal burns, fever that persists for days, and, with repeated use, the acceleration of Stage IV.
