# Phobia

Phobia is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school, on the Major scale. It works like an arcane scar etched into the psyche: the caster implants in the target a permanent fear of a specific stimulus, whether fire, darkness, blades, or the sound of a particular voice.

### Effect

The magic itself ends, but the fear remains. Each new exposure to the stimulus triggers panic as if the spell had just been cast. It differs from Scourge, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which is an autonomous and generic affliction: Phobia is a surgical and permanent fear, anchored to a precise trigger. It can be removed by Catharsis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, or by Safeguard, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school.

### Cost

When implanting the fear, the caster absorbs a fragment of it, leaving the caster with a mild aversion to the same stimulus.
