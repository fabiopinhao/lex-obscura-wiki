# Decrepitude

Decrepitude is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school that degrades the structural integrity of the entire body: brittle bones, skin as thin as parchment, atrophied muscles. A blow that would previously have wounded now crushes.

### Principle

Decrepitude degrades the structure without causing aging, and the body falls apart like a poorly maintained building. It differs from Senescence, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school, which advances the biological clock.

### Cost

It causes diffuse joint pain in the caster and a feeling of fragility in their own bones.
