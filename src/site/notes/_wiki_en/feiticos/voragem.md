# Hunger

Hunger is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school: sentient flames that pursue targets, maneuver around obstacles, and feed on what they consume.

### Effect

The fire cannot be extinguished with water. It ceases only when nothing remains to devour. It differs from Consumption, which is selective by material, because Hunger is selective by target: the fire hunts. It also differs from Bolide, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, a single projectile; Hunger is a persistent predator.

### Cost

The caster suffers from a high fever and increasing irritability, their temperament mirroring the fire’s hunger.
