# Rigor

Rigor is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school, minor in scale, cast through contact. It drains the target’s vital heat and stiffens their muscles, bringing numbness, weakness, and grayish skin. The cold radiates from the inside out.

### Range

It is distinct from Numbness, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, which cools through external temperature. Rigor is death emanating: the body cools because life recedes, not because the environment freezes it.

### Cost

The price is a cadaverous cold that takes hold of the caster’s fingers.
