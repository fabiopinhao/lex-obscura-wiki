# Sigil

Sigil is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school that inscribes a law on a surface and leaves it awaiting violation. The caster engraves a rune on a door, a threshold, or an object, and stores a smaller-scale Nomos effect in it: a Seal that locks, an Injunction that compels, a Muzzle that silences. The Sigil lies dormant until the condition is met and then triggers, without the caster needing to be present.

### Range

It differs from Shackle, which inscribes the prohibition on the target itself. The Sigil inscribes the rule in space, not on the person.

### Cost

Each active Sigil weighs on the back of the caster’s neck like a pending obligation. The mental rigidity it imposes is proportional to the number of inscriptions maintained.
