# Vigor

Vigor is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school, of Minor scale. It infuses the target with vital energy: fatigue recedes, muscles respond, energy returns. It does not heal wounds, but makes the body function as though it were uninjured.

It differs from Balm, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, which suppresses pain. Vigor restores energy instead of masking damage.

### Cost

The caster suffers brief exhaustion, as though giving up their own reserves.
