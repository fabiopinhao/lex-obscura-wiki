# Pall

Pall is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school. It envelops the caster in a clinging darkness that absorbs outlines and deadens sound. Whoever wears it becomes a blot, a blur, something the eye dismisses.

It differs from Evanescence, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, which erases one from perception: Pall conceals in physical shadow and works best in dim light. Its cost is brief partial blindness, and the preference for darkness persists afterward.
