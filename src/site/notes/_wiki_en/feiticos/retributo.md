# Reprisal

Reprisal is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school. It raises a reactive force field that returns part of the blow’s energy to the aggressor: pain, shock, shards of force. Against it, fists crack, blades vibrate, and maces reverberate in the attacker’s wrists.

### Effect

Unlike Retribution, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which returns the magic in full, inverted, Reprisal returns physical impact as proportional retaliation: the more powerful the protection, the more violent the response. The cost is constant anxiety in the protected person, the fear that the defense will fail on the next blow.
