# Fortune

Fortune is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school. It tilts chance in the target’s favor within narrow margins: an arrow veers, a foot finds purchase, a sentinel looks the wrong way. It is the inverse of Misfortune, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school that perverts coincidences, while Fortune favors them. Forcing the effect beyond its limit exhausts it and reverses luck. Its cost is a heightened moral conscience: invoking grace requires deserving it.
