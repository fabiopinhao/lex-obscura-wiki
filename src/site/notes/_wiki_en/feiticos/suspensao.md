# Suspension

Suspension is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school that nullifies gravity on a target or within a localized area. The affected individual floats without direction or support, unable to propel themselves.

Since it removes gravity and leaves the target floating in dry air, it differs from Bubble, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school, which imprisons the target in a sphere of water. In combat, the effect is decisive: without support there is no force, so the suspended combatant can neither strike nor dodge.

### Cost

Vertigo follows. The sense of “down” wavers for minutes, and spatial dissociation takes hold.
