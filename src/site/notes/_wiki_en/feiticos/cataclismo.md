# Cataclysm

Cataclysm is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, of maximum scale, in which the geomancer plants himself on the ground and the earth’s crust obeys. Mountains erupt where there were plains, valleys open where there were roads, and rivers change course. Structures are swallowed whole, and armies disappear into fissures that close.

It differs from Devastation, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, which destroys through rupture: Cataclysm redraws geography. The cost is devastating. The caster sinks along with it, trapped in the earth he shaped, his bones calcified, and repeated use accelerates Stage IV, immobilism, in which change itself becomes aversive.
