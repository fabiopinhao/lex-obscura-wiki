# Devastation

Devastation is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, rupture taken to catastrophic proportions. The ground cracks, structures collapse, and the magical bonds throughout the area shatter: everything built, imposed, or sustained there ceases to exist.

Where Edict, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, imposes an inviolable rule, Devastation declares that no rule survives. It is indiscriminate, and allied infrastructure perishes along with enemy infrastructure. The cost is devastating: multiple fractures, dislocated joints, cracked bones; without immediate [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]], the injury becomes permanent. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
