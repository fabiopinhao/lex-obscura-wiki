# Putrefaction

Putrefaction is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school, medium in scale, that rots nonliving organic matter. Under its effect, wood softens, leather falls apart, food blackens, and rope frays: decomposition that would take seasons is completed in moments.

### Range

The spell acts only on organic materials. It differs from Deterioration, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which operates at a distance as a curse on any material; and from Corrosion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which is a chemical process. Putrefaction is biological and accelerated, decomposition imposed on the dead flesh of matter.

### Cost

Casting Putrefaction returns to the caster the very rot they invoke: nausea and a putrid smell that permeates the skin and does not easily fade.
