# Trail

Trail is a Minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school. For fractions of a second, the caster exists at multiple simultaneous instants, and their silhouette splits into temporal echoes. The enemy sees three versions of the same arm and cannot distinguish now from half a second ago.

It differs from Blur, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, which blurs spatial outlines: Trail blurs temporal outlines.

### Cost

When the effect ends, the caster is left disoriented.
