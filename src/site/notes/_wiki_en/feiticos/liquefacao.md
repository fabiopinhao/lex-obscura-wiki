# Liquefaction

Liquefaction is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school that converts a solid surface into a puddle of molten matter: stone turns into lava, metal turns into incandescent mercury. Unlike Sinkhole, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which sinks into soft ground, Liquefaction melts the solid matter itself into magma. The puddle glows while the effect persists and, when it ends, solidifies into a brittle crust.

### Costs

The cost is felt in the body: intense heat in the soles of the feet and a persistent sensation of unstable ground.
