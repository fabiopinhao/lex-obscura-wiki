# Gloom

Gloom is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school. It darkens a limited area, smothers small flames, and reduces light sources to trembling ripples. It does not act on those within it: unlike Blindness, which attacks the target’s eyes, Gloom darkens the surroundings, not the perception of those who pass through it.

### Cost

Whoever casts it pays with a brief visual distortion: the edges of their own field of vision darken for a few moments after use.
