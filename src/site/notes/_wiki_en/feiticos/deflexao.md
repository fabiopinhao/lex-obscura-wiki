# Deflection

Deflection is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school. It functions as an instinctive deflection against a single blow: for an instant the air hardens and the attack’s trajectory changes, a blade slips to the side, an arrow changes course.

### Nature

It differs from Shield, which fixes itself in space and absorbs multiple blows. Deflection is a reflex: it responds to a single impact and dissipates shortly afterward.

### Cost

It exacts from the caster a tremor in the fingers and anxiety that comes later.
