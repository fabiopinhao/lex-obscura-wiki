# Paranoia

Paranoia is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school. Under its effect, the target stops trusting: allies seem like conspirators, silences become judgments, innocuous gestures become signs of betrayal.

It differs from Intrusion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which reads thoughts. Paranoia does not access the mind; it distorts the interpretation of reality. Everything the target sees is true; everything he concludes is poisoned. It is effective against leaders, since a paranoid commander destroys his own chain of command.

### Cost

The caster is left with residual distrust: for hours, he reads threat in familiar faces.
