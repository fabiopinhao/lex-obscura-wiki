# Deluge

Deluge is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school: an area flood. Waves drag things along, the current tears things away, and the water level rises without falling, so that flat terrain becomes a lake in seconds.

It differs from Blizzard, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, which buries beneath the ice, and from Earthquake, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which destroys the ground: Deluge drowns in water and submerges whatever it encounters. The cost is paradoxical dehydration, in which the caster’s body gives up water while the conjuration multiplies it.
