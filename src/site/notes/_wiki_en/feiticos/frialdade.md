# Chill

Chill is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school on the Major scale. It drains the target’s body heat: the blood thickens, the organs slow down, and the skin turns blue.

### Effect

Freezing progresses from the inside out, with no visible ice, only a slow death. It differs from Rigor, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school, which drains vital heat through necrotic energy: Chill is pure thermodynamics; the body loses heat until it stops. Also unlike Numbness, which is superficial, Chill reaches the center of the body.

### Cost

The caster suffers severe hypothermia, with uncontrollable shivering.
