# Senescence

Senescence is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school. It accelerates the target’s temporal entropy: decades pass in seconds. The skin wrinkles, the muscles atrophy, the bones become brittle, and the hair turns white and falls out. It is not disease, like Decrepitude of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school: it is time. The body actually ages, and Senescence merely advances the clock.

### Reversal

The effect is partially reversible through Reversal, never completely. The body remembers the years it has lived.

### Cost

The price is mirrored: while aging the target, the caster feels their own bones creak, their vision blur, and their joints protest.
