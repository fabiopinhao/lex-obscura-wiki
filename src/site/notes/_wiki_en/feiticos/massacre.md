# Massacre

Massacre is a spell of greater scale from the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school. It unleashes a chain of violence: each death within the area amplifies the ferocity of those still fighting, allies and enemies alike. The entire battlefield grows more brutal, defenses are abandoned, and the instinct for survival gives way to the instinct to kill.

It differs from Provocation, which attacks a single target; Massacre is an area-wide martial contagion. The price falls on the caster, who absorbs the echo of each death as nausea and trembling.
