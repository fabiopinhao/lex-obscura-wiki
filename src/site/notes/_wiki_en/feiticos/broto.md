# Sprout

Sprout is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school. It accelerates plant growth: seeds germinate in seconds, roots grow deeper, branches extend. Unlike Roots, which commands existing vegetation as a weapon, Sprout is creation, not command. When casting it, the caster feels the sap flow like their own blood. The cost is momentary aging, wrinkles that disappear in minutes.
