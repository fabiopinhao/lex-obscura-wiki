# Crucible

Crucible is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school in which the caster envelops their own body in flames. It becomes an offensive aura and a lethal touch, leaving a trail of fire wherever it passes. Anyone who approaches it burns.

### Nature

Unlike Corona, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, which radiates energy without fuel, Crucible is real fire: it consumes the surrounding air and can spread through the environment.

### Cost

The cost is proportional and literal. The flesh beneath the flame is not immune, merely the last to burn, and internal burns intensify with the duration of the casting.
