# Grotesque

Grotesque is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school that partially transforms the caster into an unnatural creature. The hybrid body retains bipedalism and speech but gains monstrous attributes: leathery wings, a chitinous carapace, extra limbs, jaws that unfold. It differs from Beast Skin, which produces a complete mundane animal: Grotesque is hybridization, an unstable form between states.

### Risks and Cost

The form tends to remain after the casting has ended and requires conscious effort to reverse; frequent use makes reversal more difficult. The cost falls on identity: the caster’s self-image becomes fluid and unstable.
