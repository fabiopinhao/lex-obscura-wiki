# Retribution

Retribution is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school. It captures magic directed at the caster and redirects it, reversed, at the original caster: the spell strikes the field, reverses its trajectory, and returns with the same power. A curse of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] falls upon whoever cast it; a lightning bolt of [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] burns whoever conjured it. The window is narrow, and the defense must be prepared before impact; against area or indirect magic, its effectiveness diminishes.

It differs from Reprisal, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, which returns physical damage as a reactive shock: Retribution returns the entire spell, reversed.

### Cost

The reverberation brings the phantom pain of the returned magic.
