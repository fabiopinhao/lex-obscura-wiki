# Numbness

Numbness is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, on the Minor scale, a cold that slows and degrades: fingers that will not close, muscles that respond late, reflexes that fail. It is the prelude to everything Cryo does.

### Distinction and Cost

It differs from Lapse, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school, which slows through temporal manipulation. Numbness slows through hypothermia: the cold penetrates from the outside. The cost is a tremor in the caster’s hands.
