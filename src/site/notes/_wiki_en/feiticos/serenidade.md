# Serenity

Serenity is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, of Medium scale. It imposes peace upon a creature or area: aggression dissolves, fists open, blades lower, and shouts fall to a murmur. The target is not dominated, but calmed, and the desire to hurt begins to seem strange to it.

### Effect

The affected creature remains lucid, awake, and capable of acting; only violence eludes it. This distinguishes Serenity from Lullaby, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which induces relaxation to bring on sleep, and from Courage, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school itself, which restores bravery. Serenity removes aggression without putting anything in its place.

### Cost

The price is a residual passivity that remains after the effect.
