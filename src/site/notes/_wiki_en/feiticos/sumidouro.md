# Sinkhole

Sinkhole is a Medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school. It makes the ground give way beneath the target’s feet.

### Effect

The earth opens in fissures, the soil turns to mud, the sand sucks things in. The spell imprisons through actual earth, and the walls close in on whoever falls. It is not destruction: the terrain rearranges itself.

### Cost

The caster’s feet sink slightly, and the sensation of unstable ground persists for hours.
