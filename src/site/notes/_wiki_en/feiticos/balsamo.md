# Balm

Balm is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, intended to relieve physical pain. Through the caster’s touch, the wounded person’s agony recedes and gives way to a moist warmth, although the wound remains unchanged beneath the relief: the spell dulls the pain without treating the cause. It differs from Quietude, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, which silences mental anxiety instead of bodily pain.

### Cost

Suppressing pain does not dissolve it. The caster carries a dull echo of the silenced pain in their own body.
