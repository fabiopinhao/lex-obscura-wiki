# Glint

Glint is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school. It produces brief plasma discharges that leap from the fingers as white points, bursting against surfaces and leaving vitrified marks.

The spell differs from Spark, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school, which shocks the nerves with electricity. Glint melts what it touches through plasma heat. The cost falls on the caster: a phantom burn on the fingertips and dry skin.
