# Shatter

Shatter is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, aimed at breaking solid, durable objects into pieces: weapons, armor, reinforced doors. The caster perceives the material’s lines of stress and concentrates force along them. It is not brute force like Shard, but informed destruction, directed at the precise point where matter gives way.

### Cost

Casting exacts a toll of sharp pain in the joints of the hands, as if the caster’s own bones felt the fracture he inflicts on the target.
