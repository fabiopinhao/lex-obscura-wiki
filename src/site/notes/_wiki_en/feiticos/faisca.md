# Spark

Spark is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school. It is a localized static discharge: a shock that hurts, startles, and interrupts the target’s concentration.

### Function

Unlike Glint, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, which melts through heat, Spark strikes the nerves with electricity. It manifests as a visible arc and a sharp crack, followed by involuntary muscle contraction.

### Cost

A residual tremor remains in the caster’s fingers.
