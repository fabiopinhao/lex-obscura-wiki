# Safeguard

Safeguard is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school. It protects a target against curses, control effects, and debilitating effects, either preventing them or removing afflictions already in place.

### Effect

Against [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], divine authority overrides the curse; against [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] or [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], grace dispels the effect. Unlike Nullification, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which negates the magical flow through technique, Safeguard purifies through holiness. The cost is spiritual: each impurity removed briefly clings to the caster’s soul.
