# Torment

Torment is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school. It inflicts pure mental pain, with no physical correlate: the target feels blades, burns, bones breaking, while the body remains intact.

It differs from neighboring spells in how it inflicts harm. Scourge, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, acts autonomously after being cast; Torment requires the caster to sustain it actively. Terror, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school, paralyzes through threat; Torment destroys through pain itself. Because it leaves no marks, it lends itself to interrogation. The cost returns to the caster as diminished echoes of the pain they inflicted and a growing headache.
