# Attraction

Attraction is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school. It pulls small objects toward the caster through a localized gravitational alteration: keys, daggers, and flasks slide into their hand. The range is limited and the maximum weight, modest.

It differs from Magnetize, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school, which attracts only metal; Attraction affects any matter. The cost is an increase in the weight of the caster’s own body and brief dizziness.
