# Consecration

Consecration is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school that inscribes persistent sacred protection on an object or location. Consecrated ground repels the undead, wounds [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]], and weakens curses of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]]; consecrated weapons become capable of wounding creatures resistant to ordinary metal. It is a slow ritual.

### Principle

Consecration protects through sanctity, with a broad effect on everything that opposes the sacred. It differs from Sealing, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which blocks a specific school through negation: where Sealing negates, Consecration sanctifies.

### Cost

It exacts from the caster a growing conviction that the unconsecrated world is impure.
