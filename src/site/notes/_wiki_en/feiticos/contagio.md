# Contagion

Contagion is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school. It transforms an infected target into a disease vector: anyone who touches the target, breathes nearby, or drinks from the same water becomes ill with the same symptoms.

### How It Works

Each newly infected individual becomes, in turn, a source of the disease. It differs from Necrotoxin, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which corrodes through direct chemical contact; Contagion propagates an organic disease that spreads on its own.

### Cost

The caster experiences the first symptoms of the disease they spread, milder but persistent.
