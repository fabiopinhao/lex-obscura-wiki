# Spike

Spike is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school. It launches fragments, spears, or bursts of ice as projectiles, and the more humid the environment, the more ammunition the caster has available.

Unlike Blaze, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school, which projects fire, Spike hurls solid matter that pierces and remains in the body. Unlike Stake, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which is made of stone, Spike’s ice forms faster, but is vulnerable to heat. The cost is trembling from cold in the arms.
