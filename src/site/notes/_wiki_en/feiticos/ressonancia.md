# Resonance

Resonance is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school, dominion over the pressure waves that air carries. In combat, it manifests as a concussive pulse that ruptures eardrums, disorients, and shatters fragile surfaces. In espionage, it carries whispers over absurd distances. Air is the vehicle for all audible communication, and whoever controls the medium controls the message.

It differs from Muzzle, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, which silences through arcane prohibition: Resonance manipulates the physical medium of sound.

### Cost

The practice results in hyperacusis: for hours, ordinary sounds come through amplified and painful.
