# Corona

Corona is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school that envelops the caster’s body in a self-contained, incandescent aura of plasma. The surrounding air ripples and crackles, and anyone who approaches feels their skin dry out before any contact. Direct contact melts armor, blade, and skin.

### Principle

Corona is energy that does not consume, only radiates. It differs from Crucible, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school, which is real fire fed by fuel.

### Cost

It burns the caster from within: the skin reddens and blisters form on the forearms.
