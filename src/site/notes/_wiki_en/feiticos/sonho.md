# Dream

Dream is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school that plants a message in a sleeping target’s sleep: an image, a voice, or a sensation that becomes part of the ongoing dream.

It differs from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/influxo\|Influx]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which plants suggestion in the waking mind; Dream operates only during sleep. In exchange, fragments of another’s dream invade the caster’s own sleep.
