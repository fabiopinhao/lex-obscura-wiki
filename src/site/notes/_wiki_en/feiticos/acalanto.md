# Lullaby

Lullaby is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school. It manifests as a wave of calm that dissolves muscle tension and agitation in the target: fists relax, jaws slacken, breaths lengthen.

The effect differs from Serenity, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, which removes aggression through spiritual elevation. Lullaby works through the body, relaxing it to lead it into rest. The cost falls on the caster: excessive relaxation that dulls their own reflexes.
