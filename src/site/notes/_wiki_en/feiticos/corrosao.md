# Corrosion

Corrosion is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school that degrades material through arcane contact. Under its effect, leather dries out and cracks, metal oxidizes within minutes, rope frays, and wood softens. It is a slow, localized process, useful for sabotaging equipment or weakening a structure without attracting attention.

### Nature

It differs from Deterioration, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which acts at a distance as a curse: Corrosion requires direct contact and is chemical-arcane in nature, acting on the very matter it touches.

### Cost

The price falls on the caster’s hands: brittle nails and dried-out skin on the fingers.
