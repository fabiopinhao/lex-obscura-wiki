# Regeneration

Regeneration is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school. It regenerates tissue that no longer exists: severed fingers regrow, a destroyed eye reforms in the empty socket, an organ reconstructs its missing portion.

### Effect

The magic creates new matter from the healer’s vitality, and does not merely repair what remains. The flesh grows quickly and pink, and the nerves reconnect with spasms. This distinguishes it from related practices of the same school: Mending reconnects what still exists, and Restoration reactivates a lost function, while Regeneration forms what has been entirely destroyed.

### Cost

The price falls on the healer himself, who drains his own vitality to build the new flesh. The effort leaves a visible mark: apparent aging and prematurely gray hair.
