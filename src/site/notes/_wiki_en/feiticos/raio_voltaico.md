# Voltaic Bolt

Voltaic Bolt is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school. The discharge jumps in a chain between conductive targets, and each jump causes a severe muscle spasm. The energy seeks the path of least resistance, preferentially striking targets in metal armor, wet targets, or targets close to one another, and loses power with each jump.

It differs from Lightning, which has a single target: Voltaic Bolt has multiple targets.

### Cost

The arm that projects the discharge goes numb, and the involuntary spasms that seize the caster are proportional to the number of jumps.
