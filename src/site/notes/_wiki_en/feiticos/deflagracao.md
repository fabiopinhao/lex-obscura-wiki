# Blaze

Blaze is the standard offensive form of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school, a medium-scale spell that launches jets, spheres, or cones of concentrated fire at targets. It differs from Fulgurite, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, which fires energy capable of vitrifying: Blaze is projected combustion, which burns and chars, leaving ashes and smoke.

### Cost

The fire wrenched outward turns inward: a sudden fever proportional to the intensity and an internal burn in the chest.
