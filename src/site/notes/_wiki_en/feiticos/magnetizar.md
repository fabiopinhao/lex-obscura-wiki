# Magnetize

Magnetize is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school that attracts or repels small metal objects from a distance: blades slide across the floor, keys leap out of pockets. Unlike Attraction, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which pulls any matter through gravity, Magnetize affects only metal. Its range is limited and its force is proportional to the size of the object.

### Costs

Compasses go haywire near the caster for hours after the spell.
