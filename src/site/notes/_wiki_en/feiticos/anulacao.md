# Nullification

Nullification is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school. It serves to undo an active enchantment or end a persistent magical effect, with the surgical precision of someone who interrupts what sustains a spell instead of confronting it head-on. Under its effect, [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] barriers dissolve, [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] blessings lose vigor, and [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] curses peel away.

Its effectiveness depends on the scale of the target: it efficiently undoes medium-scale and smaller effects, but only weakens larger ones. It is neither purification, like Sacro, nor destruction, like [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]]; it is technical negation. The cost is exhaustion proportional to the enchantment dispelled.
