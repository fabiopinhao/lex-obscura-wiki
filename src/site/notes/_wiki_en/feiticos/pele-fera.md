# Beast Skin

Beast Skin is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school. It allows complete transformation into a mundane animal of medium size or smaller, such as a wolf, raven, snake, or deer. The caster retains consciousness but loses speech, and the animal’s instincts begin to influence the caster’s behavior. The process is visceral: bones crack as they reconfigure, and the skin stretches. It differs from Transmorphosis, which transforms another rather than oneself, in being a voluntary transformation of one’s own body. Apex predators require the Major scale.

### Cost

Upon reverting to the original form, the caster suffers bodily disorientation and disproportionate hunger.
