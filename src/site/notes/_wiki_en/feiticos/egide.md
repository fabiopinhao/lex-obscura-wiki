# Ward

Ward is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, on the Major scale, a protective mantle that nullifies magic directed at the target. Where Shelter filters and attenuates, Ward rejects: spells dissipate on contact, like sparks against wet iron, curses slide off, and projections of force dissolve. It is a specialized protection, effective against the arcane and vulnerable to physical attack.

### Cost

Each rejected spell builds up pressure in the caster’s skull. Vigilance becomes obsessive, and relaxing becomes impossible.
