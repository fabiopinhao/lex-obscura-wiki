# Mending

Mending is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school that closes wounds beyond the reach of Suture. Under its effect, the fractured bone realigns, the torn muscle reconnects, and the punctured organ seals its own breach.

It differs from Regeneration, which regenerates lost tissue: Mending reconnects only what still exists. The process is painful because it compresses each day of recovery into seconds, and the body feels that entire convalescence all at once.

### Cost

A phantom fracture remains, a stabbing pain in the flank proportional to the severity of the healed wound.
