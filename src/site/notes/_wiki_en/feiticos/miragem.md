# Mirage

Mirage is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, on the Major scale. It constructs an illusion that deceives all the senses at once: sight, hearing, smell, surface touch, and temperature. It can create a room that does not exist, an advancing army, or a fire with heat and smoke of its own.

### Functioning

Unlike [[_wiki_en/feiticos/assombracao\|Haunting]], which persists autonomously, Mirage requires active maintenance by the caster. The observer experiences the illusion until something contradicts it: stepping on ground that cannot support their weight, striking a soldier who does not bleed. The first real test dispels it.

### Cost

The caster begins to perceive the real world as slightly false, and the boundary between perception and projection blurs.
