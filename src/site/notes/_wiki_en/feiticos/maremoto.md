# Tidal Wave

Tidal Wave is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school. It commands water on a geological scale: rivers reverse their course, lakes overflow, the sea recedes and returns with a fury that rewrites the coastline.

It differs from Cataclysm, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which reshapes the terrain; Tidal Wave drowns it. What Deluge floods, Tidal Wave erases.

### Cost

The price is devastating: life-threatening dehydration, cracked skin, dry and burning eyes. Repeated use accelerates progression to Stage IV.
