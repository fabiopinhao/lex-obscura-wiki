# Sealing

Sealing is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school. It grants permanent protection that makes a target (person, object, or location) impenetrable to a specific school of magic.

### Effect

The barrier responds to the school against which it was raised. A Sealing against [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] prevents reanimation; against [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], curses slide off; against [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], flames are extinguished as they cross the boundary. Casting it requires a long and precise ritual, and each Sealing protects against a single school. The protection is not absolute: the sealed school’s maximum scale can still force its way through.

### Cost

While the effect persists, the caster carries a blind spot: they lose sensitivity to the very school they sealed.
