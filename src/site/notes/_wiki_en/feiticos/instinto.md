# Instinct

Instinct is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school. It grants an involuntary reading of the opponent: muscle tension, weight on points of support, direction of gaze. The caster senses where the next blow will come from before the opponent’s body moves. It differs from Lurking, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which reads minds; Instinct reads the body.

### Cost

It leaves residual hypervigilance: every movement nearby triggers an alert.
