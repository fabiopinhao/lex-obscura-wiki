# Legion

Legion is a spell of maximum scale from the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]]. It reanimates dozens or hundreds of the dead at once on a battlefield: mindless servants, bound by a fragile bond, and requires proximity to a site of mass death. The caster sustains the entire network until their own collapse.

### Cost

The price is devastating. A cadaverous chill overtakes the caster completely, the distinction between living and dead dissolves in their perception, and repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
