# Possession

Possession is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, of maximum scale. Through it, the caster inhabits the target’s mind, suppressing their will and autonomy to the point of controlling another’s body as if it were their own, with access to the host’s memories and abilities. The target may remain conscious of the invasion, trapped within themselves, or never perceive it.

It differs from Trance, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which puts the will to sleep: Possession subjugates it by force, and the host can still fight from within.

### Cost

The price is devastating: the boundary between the self and the other dissolves. The caster’s personality is contaminated by traits of the host, and repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
