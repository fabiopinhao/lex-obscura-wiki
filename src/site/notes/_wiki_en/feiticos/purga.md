# Purge

Purge is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school that tears a magical effect out of the caster’s own body. Whoever casts it locates the invading enchantment and expels it with a pulse of will, restricted to minor effects and only on themselves.

It differs from Catharsis, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, which purifies afflictions in another through empathy: Purge is technical self-negation, quick and cold. The cost is momentary vulnerability, while the caster’s attention turns inward.
