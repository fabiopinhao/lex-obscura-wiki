# Malleability

Malleability is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school that grants temporary bodily plasticity: hyperflexible joints, bone compression, and extreme contortion. It allows escape from restraints and passage through narrow spaces.

It differs from Freedom, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school that dissolves bonds through transcendence: Malleability physically alters the body.

### Cost

Loss of coordination when it ends and bones that have forgotten where they belong.
