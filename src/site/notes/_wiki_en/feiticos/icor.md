# Ichor

Ichor is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school. It grants control over the fluids within a living body: blood slows, joints lock, and muscles contract against their owner’s will. The target is betrayed by their own biology.

The spell differs from Confinement, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, which immobilizes through arcane law. Ichor immobilizes through control over internal fluids. The price falls on the caster: unstable blood pressure, dizziness, and nasal bleeding.
