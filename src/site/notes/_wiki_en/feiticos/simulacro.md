# Simulacrum

Simulacrum is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school. It projects a small, silent image at the point the caster chooses: a coin gleaming on the ground, a rat at the edge of vision, a silhouette crossing a doorway.

The image is made of light without substance and does not withstand direct scrutiny, dissolving under full attention. It differs from Silhouette, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which animates real shadows; Simulacrum animates nothing, merely forms an image. The cost falls on the caster, who becomes partially translucent for a few moments.
