# Easing

Easing is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school. It reduces the gravity acting on an object or a person: jumps cover improbable distances, falls become landings, and heavy armor comes to weigh as little as light clothing. When the effect ends, the weight returns abruptly.

The spell differs from Feather, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, which transcends weight through will. Easing manipulates physical gravity. The cost falls on the caster: while the target weighs less, the caster weighs more.
