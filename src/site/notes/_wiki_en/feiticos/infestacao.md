# Infestation

Infestation is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school. It summons a swarm of insects, worms, or parasites that attack, devour, and lodge themselves in flesh. The conjured organisms act out of hunger, not on command.

The spell differs from Roots, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school, which uses vegetation with purpose, and from Scourge, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which is an autonomous and abstract curse. Infestation is uncontrolled biological chaos, visible organic horror. The price is a migrating itch and the sensation of things crawling beneath the caster’s skin.
