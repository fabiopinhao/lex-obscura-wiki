# Clairvoyance

Clairvoyance is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school. It allows perception to be projected to a distant location: the body remains motionless and vulnerable while the mind observes from afar.

### Mechanics

The vision obtained is accurate but limited, without sound or touch. It differs from Eidolon, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which sends an extension of shadow; Clairvoyance is pure mental projection. Barriers of [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] block the projection, and Umbra shadows distort it.

### Cost

Upon returning, the caster suffers spatial disorientation and momentary difficulty recognizing their own body.
