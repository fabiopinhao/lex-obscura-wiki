# Cuirass

Cuirass is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school. It forms a full suit of armor of arcane force that envelops the body, moves with it, and protects from every angle. Each absorbed blow appears as a luminous crack on the translucent surface, a visible countdown to collapse.

The spell differs from Shield, which is static and frontal: Cuirass allows melee combat. The price falls on the caster: an emotional vulnerability proportional to the physical protection, an anxiety that escalates with each new crack.
