# Beacon

Beacon is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school. It envelops the target in a clinging outline of light that makes the target shine even behind cover and even under an illusion, so that allies can tell where to strike.

The spell is distinct from Mark, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which tracks the target through an invisible arcane bond. Beacon marks with light visible to everyone. The price falls on the caster: the glow reflects in the caster’s eyes, making stealth difficult.
