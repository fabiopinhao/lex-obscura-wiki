# Anima

Anima is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school that infuses temporary consciousness into a plant or animal. The being gains a will of its own, understands commands, and acts with limited autonomy: trees keep watch, shrubs attack, animals perform complex tasks.

It differs from Revenant, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school that animates the dead with fragments of memory. Anima awakens consciousness in existing life, not in death. Its cost is severe aging, as the vitality surrendered exacts its price in years.
