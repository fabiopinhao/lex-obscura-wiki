# Miracle

Miracle is a Maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school. It is the absolute restoration of a living body: every wound closes, every bone knits, every organ reconstitutes itself, every disease ceases, and every curse dissolves. Scars disappear, limbs regrow, and the senses return to their fullness in the same instant.

### Principle

Miracle restores the living, not the dead. In this it differs from Resurrection, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, which reverses a death that has already occurred through divine intervention. Miracle’s scope is an organism that is still alive, however devastated it may be.

### Costs

The price is proportional to the wonder. Miracle transfers the entirety of the healed damage to the healer’s body in a single instant: each fracture, each instance of necrosis, each moment of agony removed from another comes to weigh on the one who heals. Repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
