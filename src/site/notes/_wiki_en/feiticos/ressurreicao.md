# Resurrection

Resurrection is the highest-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, capable of reversing a death that has already occurred: days, weeks, and, in extreme cases, years later. The destroyed body reconstructs itself and the distant spirit is summoned back.

### What Sets It Apart

It differs from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/indulto\|Reprieve]], which is preventive and activates only at the moment of death, and from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/revenant\|Revenant]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school, which reanimates the body without restoring the person. Resurrection restores memory and will intact.

### Invocation and Cost

It requires direct divine intervention: the caster kneels, invokes, and the god decides. The cost is immeasurable, for the caster sacrifices purity, faith, and certainty. No hierophant who has invoked it has ever felt worthy again.
