# Restoration

Restoration is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school. It reverses sensory and functional losses from which the body would not recover on its own: eyes focus again, ears rediscover silence, the numb hand closes into a fist again.

### Effect

It does not regenerate lost tissue, as Regeneration does; it reconnects what has been disconnected. The recovery is slow and partial, and vision returns in patches before settling. The cost falls on the healer, who feels the deprivation being reversed: momentary blindness or partial deafness.
