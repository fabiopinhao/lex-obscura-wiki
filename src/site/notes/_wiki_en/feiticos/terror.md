# Terror

Terror is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school. It is the fear that paralyzes: the mind screams to run, but the body refuses to obey. The target’s eyes widen, their breath stops, and their muscles stiffen, and they remain conscious, trapped within themselves, witnessing without reacting.

It differs from other fear spells of the same school. Where Dread compels flight and Panic disrupts action, Terror freezes.

### Cost

After the invocation, terrifying apparitions haunt the caster for hours: shadowy figures in the corner of their vision that disappear when looked at directly.
