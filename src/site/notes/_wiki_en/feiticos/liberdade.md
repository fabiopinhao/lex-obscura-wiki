# Freedom

Freedom is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school. Under the unleashed will, physical bonds give way: ropes loosen, shackles open, arcane seals of [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] crack, paralysis yields, and magical immobilization loses its grip.

### Principle

It is not brute force. Freedom declares that the target is not bound, and reality hesitates to disagree. It works best against bonds imposed by others; self-imposed restrictions resist. The cost is a persistent restlessness, in which the caster’s own commitments begin to seem like provisional chains.
