# Hex

Hex is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school. It is a targeted curse that binds the caster to the target. Whoever casts it chooses the affliction: attracting misfortune, sabotaging relationships, draining vitality, disturbing sleep or thought. The effect is sustained by will and lasts as long as the caster maintains the bond.

It differs from Shackle, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, which punishes a specific action: where Shackle reacts to a gesture, Hex corrupts existence in a continuous and open-ended manner. The cost lies in the very nature of the bond, which is bidirectional: fragments of the imposed affliction leak back into the caster.
