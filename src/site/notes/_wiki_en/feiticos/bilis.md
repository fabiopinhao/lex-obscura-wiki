# Bile

Bile is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school. It conjures a corrosive liquid that melts organic matter and metal, projected in several ways: spat, secreted from the hands, or condensed in the air. Skin blisters and dissolves, thin metal runs like wax, and the substance continues corroding until neutralized by a solvent, by Purge, or by dilution.

Unlike Corrosion, which acts through slow degradation on contact, Bile is projected and aggressive. The cost falls on the caster in the form of a burning sensation in the esophagus; repeated use corrodes the enamel and ulcerates the mucous membranes.
