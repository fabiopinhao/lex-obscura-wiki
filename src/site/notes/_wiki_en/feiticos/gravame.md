# Burden

Burden is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school. It multiplies the gravitational weight bearing down on a target: knees buckle, the spine bends, and armor becomes a metal coffin for its wearer. It ranges from light pressure to lethal compression, scaling with the caster’s concentration.

It differs from Confinement, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school, which immobilizes through arcane prohibition: where Confinement prevents movement by decree, Burden crushes through real weight. The cost falls on the caster, who bears weight proportional to what they apply, with increasing difficulty of movement as the pressure rises.
