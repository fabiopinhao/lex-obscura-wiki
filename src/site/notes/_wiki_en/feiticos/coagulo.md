# Clot

Clot is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school that solidifies shadow into dense, temporary matter. From darkness, the caster molds shapes with real weight and a cutting edge: a blade, a shield, a shackle, a stake.

### Nature

Unlike Gladius, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, which projects a blade of energy, Clot is matter formed from darkness: it has mass, an edge, and the hardness of a physical object for as long as it lasts. When concentration ceases, the form dissolves into black dust.

### Cost

The effort leaves numbness in the hands, and the skin darkens at the points of contact with the solidified shadow.
