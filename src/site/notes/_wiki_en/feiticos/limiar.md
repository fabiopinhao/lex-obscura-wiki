# Threshold

Threshold is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school. It allows one to sink into one’s own shadow and emerge from another connected shadow, within a radius that depends on the available darkness. The transit lasts an instant, but the traveler feels the cold and absolute silence of the in-between.

It differs from Portal, a spell of [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] that folds space: Threshold travels through the shadow dimension and requires darkness at both ends.

### Cost

Partial blindness upon emerging and temporary photophobia.
