# Fissure

Fissure is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school. It creates cracks in surfaces and weakens load-bearing points.

### Function

The magic does not break: it prepares. The next force applied encounters reduced resistance. Unlike Deterioration, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which gradually corrodes through a curse, Fissure is instantaneous structural damage. Cracked armor gives way to the blade; a cracked barrier offers less resistance to fire.

### Cost

The caster feels brief pain in the joints and popping in the fingers.
