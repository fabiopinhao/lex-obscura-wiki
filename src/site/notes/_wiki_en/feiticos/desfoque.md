# Blur

Blur is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school. The caster blurs their own outlines, and their silhouette trembles like a reflection in disturbed water: blows pass within centimeters of the body, and arrows skim past the skin without touching it.

It differs from Pall, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which conceals the caster in shadow. Blur does not conceal; it confuses position. The cost is perceptual: for a few moments, the caster’s own vision of the world is blurred.
