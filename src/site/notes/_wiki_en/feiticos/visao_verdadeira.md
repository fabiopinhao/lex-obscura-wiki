# True Sight

True Sight is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school that causes the veil to fall: illusions of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school dissolve, disguises lose coherence, invisible creatures gain phosphorescent outlines.

### Effect

It is gnostic perception: the caster sees the world as it is. The intensity scales with the sustaining of the spell, revealing progressive layers of deception. It differs from Unveiling, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, which exposes through forced radiance; here revelation comes from the gaze itself.

### Cost

For a time, the caster loses the ability to accept appearances: faces seem like masks, courtesy seems like calculation.
