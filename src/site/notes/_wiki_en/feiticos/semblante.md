# Visage

Visage is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school that produces real cosmetic changes to the caster’s own body: facial features, eye color, hair, skin, and scars that disappear or appear. The change is physical, not illusory, and touch confirms it.

It differs from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/disfarce\|Disguise]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, which projects an image over the form without altering it. The cost is the estrangement of not recognizing oneself in the mirror.
