# Graft

Graft is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school. It transfers vitality between living beings: the caster drains vigor from a source and infuses it into another target. The source weakens, and the recipient grows stronger.

### Practices

Unlike Siphon, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school, which parasitically draws energy from death, Graft redistributes life among the living. Unlike Mending, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school, which heals damage, Graft transfers vigor without repairing tissue.

### Costs

The caster suffers aging proportional to the amount of vitality transferred.
