# Insomnia

Insomnia is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, and functions as the inverse of its gift. Where Hypno induces sleep, this spell forbids it: the target remains awake against their every wish, their mind spinning nonstop.

It differs from Torment, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which causes direct mental pain: Insomnia is passive torture through deprivation. After days without rest, hallucinations emerge and judgment collapses. As a cost, the caster’s own sleep quality declines for days, in fragmented wakefulness.
