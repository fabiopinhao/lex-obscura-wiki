# Slash

Slash is a Medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school. It forms a blade of compressed air, an invisible, cutting, and silent projectile.

### Effect

The strike has neither light nor sound until impact, and the wound appears before the target understands what hit them. In this it differs from Stake, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school that launches visible rock: Slash arrives without warning.

### Cost

The caster feels a cutting pain in their fingers, as if the edge were passing through their own hand.
