# Lacuna

Lacuna is a spell of the Major scale from the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school. It creates an arcane [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|Void]]: a zone where magic simply cannot exist. Within the perimeter, active enchantments dissolve, summonings are aborted, and artifacts become dormant.

### Principle

No school functions within Lacuna, whether allied or hostile. The suppression is absolute and indiscriminate, which distinguishes it from Sealing, which is restricted to a specific magic. The price of this breadth also falls on whoever casts it: the caster loses access to their own magic while sustaining the effect, which makes Lacuna a desperate measure, capable of silencing the entire room at the cost of also silencing the caster. The duration depends on the caster’s power and the location’s arcane density.

### Costs

Maintaining Lacuna imposes a profound disconnection. The absence of magic is felt as sudden deafness, and reconnection, when the effect ends, is accompanied by pain.
