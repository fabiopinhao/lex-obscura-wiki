# Current

Current is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school that creates whirlpools, artificial tides, and currents that drag. The water does not need to be deep: a span is enough to knock down anyone who steps in it. Unlike Gale, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school, which pushes with wind, Current pushes with liquid mass, dragging and submerging.

### Cost

The caster suffers dizziness and a sensation of an internal tide.
