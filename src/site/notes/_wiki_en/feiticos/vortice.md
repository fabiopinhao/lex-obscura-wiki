# Vortex

Vortex is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school: a localized whirlwind that traps the target in a spiral of air.

### Effect

Debris lifted by the current obscures vision and cuts exposed skin. The more loose material there is nearby, the more lethal the rotation becomes. It differs from Singularity, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which pulls through gravity: Vortex pulls through an air current and only works where there is an atmosphere.

### Cost

When the spell ends, the caster feels pressure in the eardrums and spatial disorientation.
