# Pulse

Pulse is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school. It produces a brief gravitational wave that pushes or pulls: objects slide, bodies stumble, shields shift.

It differs from Telekinesis, with its fine, sustained control: Pulse is instantaneous, imprecise brute force. The cost is a momentary increase in the weight on the feet, as if gravity were exacting the recoil.
