# Siphon

Siphon is a Medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school. It sucks the life force from a living being through proximity or contact and transfers a portion of it to the caster.

### Effect

Under the spell, the target weakens while the caster feels a temporary warmth. What is drained is the vital essence: the target does not dehydrate, but withers. This distinguishes it from similar practices. Desiccation, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school, drains water from the body; Graft, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school, redistributes vitality among volunteers. Siphon is parasitism, not consensual transfer.

### Cost

When the effect ceases, a corpse-like cold follows, as if the stolen heat were charging interest.
