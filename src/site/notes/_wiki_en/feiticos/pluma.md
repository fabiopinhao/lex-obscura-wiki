# Feather

Feather is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school. It makes the body respond to the spirit before it responds to gravity: jumps cover twice the distance, falls slow down, and the landing is gentle where there should be a fracture. It differs from Easing, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which reduces weight through gravity, while Feather transcends weight through will.

### Cost

The caster develops a subtle contempt for the mundane: walking on the ground begins to seem like a concession.
