# Magnetarca

Magnetarca is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school that controls metal within a wide radius with devastating precision. It rips weapons from hands, immobilizes warriors inside their armor, and lifts and hurls metal objects as projectiles. The more metal in the environment, the greater its power: in an arsenal or a forge it is apocalyptic, in a forest without metal it is useless.

It differs from Burden, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school that controls any matter through gravity: Magnetarca is limited to metal, but within that specialty, it surpasses Burden in precision and strength.

### Cost

Severe and cumulative: nervous system overload, persistent tremors, and temporary loss of fine motor control.
