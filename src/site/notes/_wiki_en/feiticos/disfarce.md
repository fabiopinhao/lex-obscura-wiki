# Disguise

Disguise is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school. It cloaks the caster or a target in the complete appearance of another person, including voice and stature.

### Function

Unlike Visage, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school, which physically alters the body, Disguise is an illusory projection and fails under touch. It withstands casual observation and reproduces scars when the caster knows them. It is dispelled by Unveiling, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, or by True Sight, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school.

### Cost

The caster feels a sense of estrangement at their own reflection, and their visual identity wavers.
