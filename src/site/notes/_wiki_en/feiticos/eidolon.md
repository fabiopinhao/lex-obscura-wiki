# Eidolon

Eidolon is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school. The caster detaches their own shadow from their body, animates it with a fragment of will, and sends it out as an extension of themselves.

### Practices

In its basic form, the Eidolon glides across surfaces, climbs walls, and passes under doors: it sees and hears, transmitting everything to the caster. It neither speaks nor fights. Unlike Haunting, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, which is an autonomous illusion, the Eidolon is a real extension of whoever casts it.

With a greater investment of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]], the construct gains volume, more defined contours, and offensive capability: it pursues, restrains, and devours the light around the target. In this configuration, it no longer transmits sensory perceptions; it hunts. It is vulnerable to direct natural light.

### Costs

The connection between caster and construct is visceral. The distance traveled increases the cost, and the destruction of the Eidolon rebounds as a physical rupture in the caster. The caster feels increasing cold and exhaustion, as though half their own substance were absent, escalating to collapse depending on the magi invested. If the construct is destroyed by light, the caster feels a sudden burn.
