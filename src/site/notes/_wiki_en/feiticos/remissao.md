# Remission

Remission is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school that eliminates disease already established in the body. It does not treat symptoms: it reverses the pathological process itself, so that the fever subsides, the infection recedes, and the inflamed tissue’s swelling goes down.

### Range and Limits

Against common diseases, it provides complete resolution. Against magical plagues or infections of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school, it functions as containment: it stabilizes the condition and buys time without eradicating the cause. It differs from [[_wiki_en/feiticos/depuracao\|Purification]], which expels foreign substances from the body; Remission corrects the diseased body itself.

### Cost

It exacts the ghost of the cured disease: trembling, cold sweat, and weakness proportional to the affliction reversed.
