# Injunction

Injunction is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school. It compresses a command into word and will to compel organic obedience: the legs stop, the hand releases the weapon, the mouth closes, the gesture hesitates halfway. The body obeys before refusal takes shape.

It differs from Provocation, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school, which awakens the aggressive instinct: Injunction imposes a specific action or inaction, not an impulse. Its range has a clear limit, and complex commands fail. As a cost, the caster carries hesitation into their own next decision.
