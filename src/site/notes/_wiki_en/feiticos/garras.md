# Claws

Claws is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school. It transforms fingers into claws, nails into blades, and teeth into fangs: the bones lengthen beneath the skin with audible cracks, and the keratin hardens and sharpens. It differs from Ember, a spell of [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] that enchants an existing weapon, because Claws turns the caster’s own body into a weapon. Its cost is bodily disorientation when the transformation is reversed, with numb fingertips.
