# Exhalation

Exhalation is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school. The caster’s body transforms into a source of toxins: the pores secrete volatile poison, the breath carries paralytic agents, and sweat becomes a contact irritant.

Unlike Miasma, an autonomous environmental cloud, Exhalation is centered on the body and moves with the caster. Mere proximity poisons, causing dizziness, nausea, and blurred vision, and is indiscriminate: allies suffer equally. The cost falls on the caster themself, whose filtering organs work beyond their limits and accumulate internal damage.
