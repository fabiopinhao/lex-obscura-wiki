# Shard

Shard is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, of Minor scale, aimed at breaking fragile or already compromised objects: glass, ceramic, ice, exposed bone, rusted chain. The shards propelled by the rupture injure anyone nearby.

It differs from Shatter, which requires concentration on the target’s stress lines: Shard is brute force against what is already giving way.

### Cost

The caster feels a phantom fracture in their fingers.
