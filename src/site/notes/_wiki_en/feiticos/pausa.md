# Pause

Pause is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]] school, minor in scale. It freezes a moving object at the exact instant: the projectile in the air, the closing door, the glass falling toward the floor.

### Mechanism

Unlike Suspension, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which fixes objects in space through gravity, Pause fixes them in time. When it ends, the object resumes its motion exactly where it stopped, as if the interval had never existed. Living beings resist the effect.

### Cost

The caster pays with reflexive sluggishness in their hands.
