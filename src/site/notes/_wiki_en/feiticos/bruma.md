# Mist

Mist is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school. It disperses water into mist that covers an area: visibility drops, sounds are muffled, outlines disappear. It is not an illusion, as in [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], but real water suspended in the air. Unlike Gloom, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which conceals through darkness, Mist conceals through saturation and works even in sunlight. The cost is light: damp skin and a brief chill.
