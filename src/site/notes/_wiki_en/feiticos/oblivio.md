# Oblivion

Oblivion is a greater-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school. It tears memories from the target’s mind: specific moments, faces, learned skills, entire years.

### Functioning

The forgetting is surgical. The memory existed, and in its place there is now [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|emptiness]], a hole the target feels but cannot name. Unlike Fabrication, which replaces the memory with another, false one, Oblivion only empties. Precision depends on the caster’s skill: masters remove an entire conversation without touching the day that surrounded it.

### Cost

The caster absorbs echoes of the memories torn out, fragments of someone else’s life that begin to haunt them.
