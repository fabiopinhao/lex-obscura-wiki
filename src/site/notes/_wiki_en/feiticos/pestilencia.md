# Pestilence

Pestilence is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school. It causes massive deterioration that spreads through contact, proximity, and terrain: the soil rots, the water turns murky, vegetation withers, and every living being in the area deteriorates in a cascade.

Unlike Miasma, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which is a lethal chemical cloud, Pestilence is an organic disease that spreads through life, each infected body amplifying the next. The cost is devastating: the caster absorbs the disease as patient zero, their organs deteriorate, and repeated use accelerates the most advanced stages of their own ruin.
