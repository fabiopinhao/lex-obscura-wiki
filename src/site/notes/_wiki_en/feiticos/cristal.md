# Crystal

Crystal is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school. It freezes moisture in the air into solid formations: sharp crystals, icy mist, and stalactites that grow out of nothing.

### Mechanics

The more humid the environment, the more material is available for casting. It differs from Stake, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which tears projectiles from the ground; Crystal forms its projectiles from the air.

### Cost

The caster suffers mild hypothermia, with bluish skin on the lips.
