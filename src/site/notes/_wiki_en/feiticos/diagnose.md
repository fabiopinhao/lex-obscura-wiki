# Diagnosis

Diagnosis is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, minor in scale. It allows the caster to perceive active magic nearby, revealing its school, its intensity, and its general intent.

### Practices

Each school has its own texture when read by the spell: [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] throbs like a wound, [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] smells of damp earth. Unlike Vigil, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school, which detects movement and intent, Diagnosis reads the magic itself, not whoever cast it.

### Costs

The caster is left with residual hypersensitivity: for a time, all magic bothers him like noise.
