# Brittleness

Brittleness is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school that subjects matter to extreme cold capable of making it brittle. Metal cracks at a touch, wood snaps, bone turns to glass. The target does not freeze: it becomes as fragile as crockery, and the next impact shatters it.

It differs from Fissure, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, which creates cracks through applied force: in Brittleness, embrittlement comes from temperature, and matter falls apart on its own. The cost falls on the caster, whose joints creak like ice-cold metal.
