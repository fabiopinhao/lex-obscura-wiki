# Shackle

Shackle is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]] school. It inscribes a specific prohibition on the target (not crossing a line, not raising a weapon, not uttering a name) and punishes each transgression with an escalating penalty: the first violation causes sharp pain, the second knocks the target down, and the third causes actual damage.

### Nature

Unlike Hex, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school, which corrupts through a continuous curse, Shackle punishes a specific action without contaminating the target’s existence. Its ontological cost germinates within the caster themselves: ambiguity becomes uncomfortable, and exceptions begin to seem like errors.
