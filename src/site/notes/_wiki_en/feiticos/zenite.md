# Meridian

Meridian is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, on the Major scale. It is not a beam but a fall: a pillar of radiance plummets from the sky as pressure exerted by light with weight. The point of impact cracks, the stone splits, and everything that is shadow within a radius of ten paces ceases to exist. Unlike Radiance, a horizontal precision beam, Meridian is vertical, area-wide devastation, and only works under an open sky.

The cost is temporary partial blindness: the retinas burn from the invocation itself.
