# Purification

Purification is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school that extracts from the body what does not belong there: toxins, ingested substances, impurities in the blood. The body expels them through sweat, vomit, or urine. Where Remission cures a disease that has already developed, Purification removes the foreign substance, and does not distinguish poison from medicine: it cleanses everything.

### Cost

The process takes a toll on the healer, causing reflexive nausea and a metallic taste in the mouth.
