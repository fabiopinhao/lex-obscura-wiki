# Scrutiny

Scrutiny is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school, on the Minor scale. By touching or observing an object, the caster makes its nature reveal itself: its composition, the presence of enchantments, its primary function. Weapons betray their school and poisons give away their components.

### Distinction and Cost

It differs from Discernment, which reveals how a thing works: Scrutiny reveals what the thing is. The cost is pain in the temples and sensory information overload.
