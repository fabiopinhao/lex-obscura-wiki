# Misfortune

Misfortune is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school. It twists coincidences against the target: the arrow veers half a span, the foot slips, the rope snaps at the worst moment. The effect is cumulative while it lasts.

It is the inverse of Fortune, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, which favors chance; Misfortune sabotages it. The cost is mirrored: symmetrical misfortunes befall the caster themselves.
