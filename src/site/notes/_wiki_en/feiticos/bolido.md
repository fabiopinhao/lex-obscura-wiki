# Bolide

Bolide is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school. The caster rips a projectile of molten rock from the ground, superheats the matter to white incandescence, and hurls it.

Unlike Meteor, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which plummets under gravity, Bolide is molten matter launched by plasma energy. On impact, it explodes into vitrified shards and a wave of heat that ignores armor. The craters it leaves remain hot for hours.

### Costs

Casting the spell causes burns on the caster’s arms and severe heat exhaustion.
