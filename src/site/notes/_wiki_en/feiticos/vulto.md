# Silhouette

Silhouette is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, at the Minor scale. It manipulates existing shadows: elongates them, distorts them, separates them from the body that casts them, and animates them with false movements.

It differs from Simulacrum, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, which projects a visual image. Silhouette animates the real shadow of something that exists.

### Cost

The caster’s own shadow moves on its own for a few moments.
