# Intrusion

Intrusion is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school that forces its way into the target’s mind to read what floats on its surface: immediate intentions, the dominant concern, the newly formulated lie. Unlike Insight, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school, which detects the truth without invasion, Intrusion invades, and the target feels the spell as pressure behind the eyes. It does not reach deep memories.

### Resistance

Mental discipline resists Intrusion, as do [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] and Shelter.

### Costs

The caster’s mental boundaries become porous after the spell, and other people’s thoughts begin to leak through like a murmur.
