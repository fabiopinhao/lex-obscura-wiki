# Whisper

Whisper is a Minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school. It extracts fragmentary memories from a recent corpse. It is not a dialogue; it is an echo: blurred images, the last emotion, a name repeated on a loop.

### Effect

The information degrades with the time elapsed since death, and what is recovered is always residue, never testimony. This separates Whisper from Insight, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school, which reads the truth in the living: Whisper reads only the remnants left in the dead.

### Cost

The price is a chill at the nape of the neck and the sense that death is near.
