# Consumption

Consumption is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school that produces a selective fire capable of devouring only the chosen matter: only wood, only flesh, only metal. It is destruction with applied intelligence, not a blind blaze.

### Nature

Unlike Hunger, which pursues targets like a predator, Consumption selects a material and ignores everything else. It also differs from Severance, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, which separates through rupture: here separation occurs through directed combustion, burning only what has been marked.

### Cost

Casting causes an internal burn that scales with the mass consumed, followed by a fever that persists for hours.
