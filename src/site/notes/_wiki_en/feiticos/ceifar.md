# Reaping

Reaping is a large-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] school. It sends a wave of necrotic energy over an area: vegetation withers and blackens, small animals die, and larger living beings lose vitality. Unlike Desiccation, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school, which drains water, Reaping drains life, and the land remains barren for days, as though death had passed through. The cost is a severe cadaverous chill, and the caster’s skin takes on a deathly pallor for hours.
