# Fabrication

Fabrication is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school. It implants false memories in the target’s mind that are indistinguishable from real ones: conversations that never happened, invented betrayals, fabricated loyalties.

### Effect

Where Oblivion empties memory, Fabrication fills it. Simple Fabrications are stable and last; complex ones degrade under scrutiny, with inconsistent details that gradually cause unease in those who carry them. It is a political weapon par excellence.

### Cost

The caster loses clarity about their own memories, left uncertain about what they actually experienced and what they fabricated.
