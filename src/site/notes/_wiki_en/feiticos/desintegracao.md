# Disintegration

Disintegration is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school. It converts matter into pure energy.

### How It Works

The target is not strictly destroyed: it is dissolved. The edges glow white, the center becomes translucent, and, within seconds, no substance remains, no ash, no fragment, no organic residue. Only the vitrified outline of where the object ended and [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|the Void]] began remains. The spell acts on inanimate matter without resistance; in living beings, it requires prolonged contact, and the cost is proportional to the mass converted.
