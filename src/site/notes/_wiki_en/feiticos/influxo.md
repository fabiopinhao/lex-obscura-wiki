# Influx

Influx is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school. It projects a thought into the mind of a visible, awake target: words, images, or brief sensations. The recipient perceives the message as someone else’s idea. It differs from Dream, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school that requires a sleeping target. Its cost is mild dizziness and the thought’s exposure.
