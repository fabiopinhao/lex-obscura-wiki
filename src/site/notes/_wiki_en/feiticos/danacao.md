# Damnation

Damnation is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school. It is a curse that transcends body and time, marking the soul, the lineage, or the name. It can prevent resurrection, taint descendants, and turn a legacy into shame. It strikes at the concept of the person, and therefore the target may be alive, dead, or yet to be born. Reversing it requires an equivalent or superior [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] spell.

The spell differs from Scourge, which parasitizes the body of a living target. The price falls on the caster: the erosion of their own legacy, with memories of ancestors being erased as though the curse were collecting payment in kind.
