# Fulgurite

Fulgurite is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school that fires an orb or beam of concentrated plasma. The projectile tears through the air in a blue-white line and, where it strikes, vitrifies the surface: flesh melts into a smooth crust, metal deforms into a blister. It differs from Radiance, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, which burns through radiance: Fulgurite melts through plasma energy itself, without smoke or flame, only light and silence.

### Cost

It leaves a phantom burn and growing numbness in the arm that fired the shot.
