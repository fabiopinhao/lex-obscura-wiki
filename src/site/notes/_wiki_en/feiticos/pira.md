# Pyre

Pyre is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school. It raises walls, circles, and cages of fire: whoever crosses burns; whoever stays inside suffocates. It differs from Barrier, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, which blocks by force, while Pyre blocks by threat, with real fire that consumes anyone who dares to pass. It is a prison that can be seen and felt.

### Cost

While sustaining the flame, the caster suffers a high fever, with sweat running down their face.
