# Vertigo

Vertigo is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school that fires a pulse of disorientation into the target’s mind: the ground seems to tilt, the eyes lose focus, and notions of up and down waver. The effect is enough to interrupt a casting in progress or cause a blow to miss.

### Distinction

Vertigo acts on perception, not on the world. It differs from Suspension, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, which alters actual gravity: Vertigo merely convinces the mind that space has come undone. Its cost is brief spatial confusion.
