# Roots

Roots is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school. It commands existing vegetation to grab, bind, block, or restrict: branches wrap around, vines bind, a passage closes.

Unlike Web, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which immobilizes through chemical adhesion, Roots immobilize through the physical force of the plant itself, which obeys without gaining consciousness. The cost to the caster is stiffness in the legs and the sensation of taking root.
