# Nightmare

Nightmare is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school. Through it, the caster enters a sleeping target’s dream as a conscious intruder, capable of interacting, interrogating, or torturing without leaving a mark on the body. The pain experienced in the dream is real to the mind.

Unlike Dream, which conveys a passive message, Nightmare is an active presence within another’s unconscious mind. The target awakens exhausted, unable to distinguish memory from dream. The cost falls on the caster themselves: fragments of the nightmare contaminate their sleep for days.
