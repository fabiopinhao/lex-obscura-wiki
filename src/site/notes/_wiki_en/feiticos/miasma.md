# Miasma

Miasma is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school. It is a cloud of death that combines poison and acid on an environmental scale: the air poisons, surfaces corrode, and soil and water become sterile for days. Autonomous and territorial, it persists without being sustained by the caster.

It differs from Exhalation, centered on the body of whoever invokes it, and from Pestilence, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school, which spreads organic disease; Miasma is lethal chemistry. Dispersing it requires [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]], a Consecration of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, or a Lacuna of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school. The price is devastating: the caster absorbs a lethal concentration of their own toxin, organs fail in a cascade, and, without [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]], death is certain.
