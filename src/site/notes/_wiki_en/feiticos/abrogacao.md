# Abrogation

Abrogation is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school, intended to sever nonphysical bonds: undo seals, break oaths, nullify restrictions imposed by [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]].

It differs from Nullification, a spell of [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] that neutralizes the enchantment through technical negation. While Abjuro negates, Rupto breaks, destroying by force whatever binds. Its cost is a visceral satisfaction upon severing the bond, an echo of a destructive impulse that makes subsequent restraint more difficult.
