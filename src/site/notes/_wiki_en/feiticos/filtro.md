# Filter

Filter is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school, of Minor scale, that separates water from its impurities: removes poison, removes salt, purifies mud. It is the utilitarian side of the school, the one that keeps armies on the march.

### Distinction and Cost

It differs from Purification ([[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]]), which purifies the body from within: Filter purifies the liquid outside the body. The cost is dryness in the throat.
