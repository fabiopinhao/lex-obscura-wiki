# Reprieve

Reprieve is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school. It is an enchantment set in advance that activates at the instant of death, intercepting the spirit and returning it to the body: the heart restarts, the lungs draw in air, the eyes open. The lethal wound remains, for Reprieve revokes death, not what killed.

The spell differs from Resurrection, which revokes a death that has already occurred. Reprieve is preventive, inscribed before death, and works only at the exact instant of passage. The price is twofold: the inscription drains the caster’s vitality and instills a protective obsession, and when activated causes death to be felt as a stabbing pain in the chest.
