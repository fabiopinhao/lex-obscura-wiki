# Breeze

Breeze is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school, dedicated to manipulating light air currents. It serves to ventilate spaces, dissipate odors, or form a pocket of breathable air, always in a utilitarian and silent manner. Unlike Breath, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, which transcends respiratory obstruction, Breeze acts on actual air and does not alter the body’s physical condition.

### Cost

Anyone who casts it remains excessively sensitive to any air current for hours.
