# Glossa

Glossa is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school that grants the temporary gift of tongues: unknown languages become comprehensible to whoever invokes it. Unlike Communion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which shares meaning through dreams, Glossa translates through gnostic means, functionally and instantaneously.

### Range

The translation is functional, not scholarly: it captures meaning but loses nuance.

### Costs

The cost is proportional to linguistic distance. Related languages cost little; dead languages cost dearly.
