# Panic

Panic is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school. It causes judgment to collapse under pressure: the target’s mind does not stop, but functions incorrectly. Commanders give absurd orders; soldiers drop their shields to draw the wrong weapon.

It differs from Dread, which compels flight, and from Terror, which paralyzes; Panic causes the target to act erratically and counterproductively. The caster pays the price in the form of echoes of irrational decisions, brief impulses that must be consciously suppressed.
