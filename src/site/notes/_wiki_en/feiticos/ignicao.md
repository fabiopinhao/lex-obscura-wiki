# Ignition

Ignition is the most elementary spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school, minor in scale. It lights flames in flammable materials, creates sparks, and rekindles embers. It differs from Glint, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, which melts using energy: Ignition is chemical combustion and needs material to burn.

### Cost

It leaves residual heat in the fingers and a brief fever.
