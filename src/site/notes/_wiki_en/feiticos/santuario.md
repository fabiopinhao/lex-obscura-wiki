# Sanctuary

Sanctuary is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school on the Major scale. It demarcates an inviolable area where no hostile intent can manifest: weapons grow dull, offensive spells dissipate, and hostile creatures are repelled at the very boundary of the consecrated space.

### Effect

The protection arises from the sanctity of the ground itself, not from the suppression of magic itself. Sanctuary therefore differs from Lacuna, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which indiscriminately nullifies all magic: the space within Sanctuary continues to allow benign magic. It also differs from Armistice, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, which deprives creatures of their very capacity for violence; Sanctuary does not disarm those who enter, but repels them through the sacred nature of the ground. The protection is absolute as long as the caster remains motionless at the center.

### Cost

The price is psychological confinement: with each minute, the world beyond the boundary seems like a growing threat, trapping the caster at the center he himself created.
