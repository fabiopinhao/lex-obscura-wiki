# Lightning

Lightning is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school that launches an electric arc directly at a single target. It produces a burn at the point of entry, a violent muscle spasm, and nerve overload, and its effect is amplified by the presence of metal and moisture on the target.

It differs from Radiance, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, which burns with light: Lightning wounds with electricity. The cost falls on the caster, who suffers a muscle spasm in the arm and temporary numbness.
