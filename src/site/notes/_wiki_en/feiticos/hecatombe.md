# Hecatomb

Hecatomb is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school, of maximum scale, in which the caster transcends the limits of mortal combat. The body becomes infused with absolute martial power: every movement becomes lethal, every defense, instinctive. It differs from Grotesque, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school, which transforms the body: Hecatomb preserves the form and turns it into a vehicle of pure destruction. The state lasts as long as there is combat.

### Cost

The cost is devastating. During the effect, the caster falls into an uncontrollable frenzy and loses the ability to distinguish ally from enemy; when it ceases, total physical collapse follows. Repeated use hastens Stage IV, in which violence becomes the only comfortable state.
