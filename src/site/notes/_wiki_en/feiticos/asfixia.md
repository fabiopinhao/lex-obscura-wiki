# Suffocation

Suffocation is a larger-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school. It extracts the air from the target’s lungs and creates a vacuum pocket around their head, silently and invisibly: there is no scream or final breath. On a smaller scale, the concentrated pressure crushes eardrums and collapses internal cavities.

The spell differs from Bubble, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]] school, which suffocates with water. Suffocation removes the air itself. The caster pays the price: pressure in the chest, shortness of breath lasting minutes, and a phantom sensation of suffocation.
