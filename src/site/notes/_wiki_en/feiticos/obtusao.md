# Dulling

Dulling is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, on a Medium scale. It crushes the target’s cognitive capacity: complex thought dissolves, vocabulary regresses, planning becomes raw impulse. The target does not lose consciousness, but loses sophistication. A strategist is reduced to reacting by instinct; a diplomat, to babbling.

### Mechanics

Unlike [[_wiki_en/feiticos/intoxicacao\|Intoxication]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which confuses through chemical means, Dulling directly dulls the intellect.

### Cost

The caster suffers proportional cognitive slowing, and their own thoughts become simpler for hours.
