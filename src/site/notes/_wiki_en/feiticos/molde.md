# Mold

Mold is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school. It manipulates loose earth, clay, sand, or mud: it excavates, moves, and sculpts simple shapes. It grants control over where matter is, not over what it is.

It differs from Clot, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which solidifies shadow; Mold works with real matter. The price falls on the caster in the form of increased weight in the hands and stiffened muscles.
