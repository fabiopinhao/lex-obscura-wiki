# Levitation

Levitation is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school, of Medium scale, through which the caster rises off the ground without a push and without wind. The feet leave the surface and the body floats, a span to a few meters above it. Movement is only vertical: up and down.

### Nature and Cost

It differs from Feather, which provides a push with a soft landing, and from Flight, which allows full movement: Levitation is suspended contemplation. The body does not feel the lightness of Easing, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school, but irrelevance, as though the ground ceased to matter. The cost is a subtle detachment, in which earthly matters seem distant.
