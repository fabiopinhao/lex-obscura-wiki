# Breath

Breath is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school. It makes air enter the lungs as though no obstruction existed: suffocation subsides, smoke becomes breathable for moments, and the pressure in the chest of someone who has been buried or drowned recedes.

It is not healing, as in the [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] school. Breathing merely transcends the physical condition for a limited time, without reversing the damage that caused it. The cost falls on the caster personally, in the form of vertigo from air that is too pure.
