# Mirrors

Mirrors is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] school, of Medium scale, that surrounds the caster with multiple illusory copies of themselves. Each copy replicates the original’s movement, casts a shadow, and emits sound, but puts no weight on the ground and does not bleed when struck. A blow that hits a copy shatters it into fragments of light.

It differs from Eidolon, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which is a real extension of the caster: the Mirrors are disposable images.

### Cost

The caster experiences disorientation about which reflection is real, a brief ontological doubt about their own position.
