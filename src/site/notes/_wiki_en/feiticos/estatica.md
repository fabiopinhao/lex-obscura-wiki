# Static

Static is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school. The caster accumulates an electrical charge in their own body, so that the next being to touch them receives a shock.

### Effect

The charge remains stored in the caster’s skin, functioning as a reactive deterrent and passive defense: it discharges only on contact. It differs from Ionization, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school, which saturates the surrounding environment; Static, instead, concentrates the electricity in the body and waits for a touch.

### Cost

The caster experiences persistent tingling while maintaining the charge.
