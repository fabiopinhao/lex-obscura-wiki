# Unease

Unease is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school. It plants in the target the feeling of eyes on his back: he does not know what he fears, only that something is wrong. His muscles tense, his breathing shortens, and his gaze wanders in search of a threat he does not find.

It differs from Lurking, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which detects minds: where Lurking senses real presences, Unease implants a false detection. The cost falls on the caster, who absorbs the spell’s echo as residual paranoia.
