# Chimera

Chimera is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school that completely rewrites the physical form of its caster, a target, or an area. It combines traits of multiple creatures into an anatomy with no natural precedent, fusing forms into something that has never existed. Everything emerges functional, everything cohesive: under Chimera, biological reality bends to the caster’s will.

### Scope

It differs from Reconstitution, which alters biology while maintaining a base model. Chimera neither corrects nor adapts an existing form: it invents.

### Cost

The price is devastating. Identity dissolves, and mirrors become unanswered questions. Repeated use produces shapeshifters who no longer remember the face they were born with, accelerating Stage IV.
