# Coma

Coma is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school that plunges the target into a bottomless sleep from which no natural stimulus awakens them. Where Trance keeps the body functional and obedient to commands, Coma imposes total paralysis: the target does not act. Within this sleep, the caster can construct a dream-prison in which the victim lives without knowing they are asleep, and therefore prisoners in Coma need no bars.

### Cost

Another’s wakefulness weighs on the caster as drowsiness proportional to the spell’s duration, with a tendency toward involuntary microsleeps.
