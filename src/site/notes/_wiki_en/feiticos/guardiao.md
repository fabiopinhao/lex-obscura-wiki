# Guardian

Guardian is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school in which the caster manifests a semi-autonomous sentinel of force: a translucent, faceless silhouette positioned between the protected individual and danger. The Guardian does not attack; it intercepts: it moves to block blows and pushes away threats that come too close.

### Principle

It differs from Banner ([[_wiki_en/feiticos/estandarte\|Banner]]), of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school, which anchors the group’s morale through the leader’s presence: the Guardian interposes a physical protective entity.

### Costs

The caster feels each absorbed impact as a tremor in the chest, and the sentinel’s dissolution produces a disproportionate sense of loss.
