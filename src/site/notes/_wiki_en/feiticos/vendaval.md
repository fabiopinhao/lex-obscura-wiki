# Gale

Gale is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school that raises a strong, continuous wind capable of knocking down, pushing, and throwing targets off balance within an area. Projectiles launched against the current go off course, and small creatures can even be dragged along.

### Effect

Where the spell Gust acts briefly and directionally, Gale is sustained and broad. A common comparison among casters sums up the difference: if Gust is a fist, Gale is a wall. The cost falls on whoever invokes it, since the caster struggles against the very wind they raise and their physical endurance is worn down as they sustain the current.
