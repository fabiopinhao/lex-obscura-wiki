# Blackness

Blackness is a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, on the Major scale. It creates a zone of impenetrable darkness where no natural or ordinary magical light survives. The caster can see within it; everyone else is blinded.

### Mechanics

Unlike [[_wiki_en/feiticos/lacuna\|Lacuna]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] school, which nullifies all magic, Blackness nullifies only light: the other schools function normally within it. The darkness has substance. The air seems thick, sounds are muffled, and the sense of direction dissolves.

### Cost

The caster is left with a persistent visual distortion and a preference for darkness that takes hold for hours.
