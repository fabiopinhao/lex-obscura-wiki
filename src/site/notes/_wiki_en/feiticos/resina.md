# Resin

Resin is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school. It secretes an adhesive substance onto a surface, trapping whatever touches it through chemical adhesion: the hand holding a doorknob sticks, the boot stepping on the floor locks in place, the mechanism jams.

### Effect

Unlike Bite, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school, which immobilizes through growing ice, Resin traps without cold or damage, using only glue. Brute force does not break the adhesion immediately: it tends to spread it. The cost falls on the caster in the form of excessive salivation and a sticky secretion in their sweat.
