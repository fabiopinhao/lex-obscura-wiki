# Telekinesis

Telekinesis is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school that manipulates objects at a distance through fine, sustained gravitational control. With it, the caster positions, rotates, suspends, and guides objects precisely, feeling the object as an extension of their own weight.

This precision sets it apart from other gravitational uses such as Pulse, brief and crude, and Attraction, which only pulls the target toward the caster. Telekinesis neither strikes nor drags: it commands movement through precise intent.

### Cost

The price is bodily dissociation proportional to the control exerted. The caster begins to feel their own limbs as objects moved by another’s will.
