# Glow

Glow is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school that creates a stable light source attached to an object or surface, capable of lasting hours without requiring concentration. Unlike Flash, a brief and offensive burst of light, Glow is utilitarian, intended for sustained illumination: whoever controls the light controls what is seen. Its cost is residual photosensitivity, after which the subsequent darkness seems denser.
