# Quietude

Quietude is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] school. At the caster’s touch, the target’s mental noise is suspended: breathing deepens, shoulders lower, anxiety falls silent without any change in consciousness.

It differs from Lullaby, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which relaxes to induce sleep: Quietude merely silences what was preventing clarity, without extinguishing wakefulness. The effect lasts minutes and solves nothing on its own. The cost is proportional to this relief: urgent matters lose their urgency for too long.
