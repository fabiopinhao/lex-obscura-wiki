# Apprehension

Apprehension is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] school. It instills in the target the emotional certainty of an imminent catastrophe, without any evidence to support it: the oppressive conviction that something terrible is approaching. Since the target does not know what is approaching, they do not flee.

It differs from Unease, which is diffuse and has no object, and from Dread, fear directed at a defined target. The cost is that the caster inherits an echo of this apprehension, in the form of diffuse anxiety.
