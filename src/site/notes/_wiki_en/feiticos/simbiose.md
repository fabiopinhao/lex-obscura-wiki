# Symbiosis

Symbiosis is a greater spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] school that temporarily fuses the caster with a living being. During the fusion, the two bodies remain distinct, but perception becomes one: the caster feels what the host feels, moves what the host moves, and shares its senses, its strength, and its instincts.

Fusion distinguishes it from Beast Skin, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]] school, which transforms the caster’s own body into an animal. Symbiosis transforms neither: it preserves both bodies and fuses only the perception between them. Abrupt separation takes its toll immediately, leaving both participants disoriented.

### Cost

The caster ages in proportion to the duration of the fusion. When Symbiosis ceases, a hunger for vitality remains.
