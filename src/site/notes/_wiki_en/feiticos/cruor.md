# Cruor

Cruor is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] school that converts spilled blood into fuel. Each wound sustained amplifies the caster’s strength, speed, and endurance: the more he bleeds, the more dangerous he becomes.

### Nature

It differs from Immolation, which burns vital reserves for all-out performance: Cruor scales with damage sustained, not with the complete surrender of oneself. It is a power that feeds on the wound itself.

### Cost

When the magic ceases, exhaustion takes it all back in a collapse proportional to the blood lost.
