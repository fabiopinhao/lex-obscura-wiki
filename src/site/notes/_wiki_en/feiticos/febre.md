# Fever

Fever is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] school. It induces high fever, delirium, sweating, and tremors: the body fights a disease that does not exist and consumes its own reserves in defense against a phantom threat.

Unlike [[_wiki_en/feiticos/exaustao\|Exhaustion]], of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which drains through neurological fatigue, Fever drains through a forced immune response, leading the body to destroy itself fighting nothing. The cost is chills and cold sweat, proportional to the imposed temperature.
