# Ruin

Ruin is a maximum-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school. It does not destroy the target: it corrupts it through inversion, making the target’s very existence turn against itself.

### Effect

Under Ruin, strength becomes weakness, loyalty becomes betrayal, and achievements crumble. The body sickens, the mind turns against itself, allies withdraw, and everything the target has built unravels as if it had never existed. It differs from Devastation ([[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]]), which destroys through physical rupture: where Rupto breaks, Ruin perverts.

### Cost

The cost is devastating. The caster absorbs corruption proportional to the effect: personal relationships rot, the trust around them erodes, and repeated use accelerates Stage IV.
