# Bite

Bite is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school. It makes ice grow around the target and immobilizes it: feet welded to the ground, arms pinned to the torso, jaw locked. The more the target struggles, the tighter the ice squeezes.

It differs from Resin, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which binds through adhesion; Bite binds through icy constriction, and the cold weakens as well as immobilizes. The cost falls on the caster in the form of hypothermia in the extremities.
