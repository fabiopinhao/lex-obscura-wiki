# Severance

Severance is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school. It is selective destruction: it separates a specific material from others in contact with it. It shatters the steel of a suit of armor without touching the leather underneath; it breaks bones without tearing the skin. Unlike Corrosion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]] school, which degrades through indiscriminate chemical contact, Severance chooses what it breaks. The more precise the separation, the greater the cost.
