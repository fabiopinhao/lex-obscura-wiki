# Mark

Mark is a minor-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] school. It creates an invisible arcane link that tracks a living target or an object: the caster senses its direction and distance as a dull pulse at the nape of the neck, persistent for as long as the mark lasts.

It differs from Vigil, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school, which detects movement throughout an entire area. Mark follows a single, fixed target, and subsequent curses gain precision against whoever has been marked. The price falls on the caster: each active Mark fragments concentration.
