# Cyclone

Cyclone is a larger-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]] school: a devastating column of wind that tears off roofs, knocks down trees, and hurls creatures and debris in an ascending spiral. It is localized, but has a wide radius. Unlike Vortex, which is horizontal and contained, Cyclone is vertical, and its damage comes from above and from the sides at the same time. Where Earthquake, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, destroys from below, Cyclone destroys from above.

### Cost

The caster is the center of their own storm: they pay with pressure in the skull, nosebleeds, and exhaustion proportional to the duration.
