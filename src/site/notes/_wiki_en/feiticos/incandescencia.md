# Incandescence

Incandescence is a small-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school. It forces matter to absorb energy until it glows: red-hot metal, red rock, sand that sparkles. The object radiates heat due to internal excess, not combustion.

It differs from Searing, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] school, which heats the surrounding air: Incandescence heats matter from the inside out. The caster bears the cost in the form of heat in the hands and excessive sweating.
