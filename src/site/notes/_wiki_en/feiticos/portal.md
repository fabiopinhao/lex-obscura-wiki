# Portal

Portal is a major spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] school. It tears the fabric of space between two points and opens a traversable passage, a circular frame of distorted air whose edges shimmer and whose interior shows the destination in an unstable image. The crossing is instantaneous and requires the caster to know or see the arrival point.

Unlike Threshold, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] school, which travels through the shadow dimension, and Immersion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school, which passes through matter, Portal folds space and eliminates distance. During the crossing, the body arrives before the mind accepts having departed. The cost is proportional to the distance: short portals cause dizziness; long ones produce severe dissociation.
