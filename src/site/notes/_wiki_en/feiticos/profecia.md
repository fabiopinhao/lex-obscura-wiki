# Prophecy

Prophecy is a spell of the Major scale in the [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] school. It is a vision of the distant future, and it comes fragmented, symbolic, and treacherous. The caster does not choose what they see, and the image may not provide enough context to act.

### Principle

Prophecy shows weeks or months ahead in images that require interpretation: a burning tower may signify destruction, purification, or merely a metaphor for pride. It differs from Omen, which literally foresees seconds ahead; Prophecy operates over the long term and through enigma. Once fulfilled, it seems obvious in retrospect; while still pending, it seems like madness.

### Costs

The price of Prophecy is analysis paralysis. Every decision begins to reveal too many ramifications, and the prophet hesitates where others would act without thinking.
