# Convulsion

Convulsion is a major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school. It releases an electromagnetic wave over an area that overloads the nervous system, causing loss of motor control, violent spasms, and disorientation. The pulse is invisible: the target knows what they want to do, but the body refuses to obey.

The spell differs from Exhaustion, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school, which drains through neurological fatigue. Convulsion interferes with the connection between nerve and muscle through electricity. The cost falls on the caster: a sympathetic spasm, with tremors that make precision impossible for a duration proportional to the power.
