# Earthquake

Earthquake is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]] school that causes a seismic tremor over a wide area. The ground undulates, fissures open in a radial pattern, and structures collapse when their foundations move in opposite directions. Unlike Demolition, a spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] school that destroys through structural analysis, Earthquake is indiscriminate: the earth decides where the damage will be worst. Military formations fall into disarray, cavalry falls, towers collapse.

The cost takes the form of a severe loss of balance for the caster. The legs tremble, and the sensation of unstable ground persists for days.
