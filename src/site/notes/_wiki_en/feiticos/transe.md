# Trance

Trance is a Major-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] school. Under its effect, the target acts without conscious awareness, obeying commands like a functional sleepwalker: eyes open, body functioning, will absent. The target performs complex tasks but loses subtlety, and upon waking remembers nothing.

It differs from Possession, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] school, which subjugates the mind by force. Trance puts it to sleep: the target does not resist because they do not know they should.

### Cost

Fragments of residual obedience remain in the caster, who feels impulses to follow orders for hours.
