# Ionization

Ionization is a minor spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] school. It saturates the air or matter with energy until the surroundings crackle: static snakes between metallic surfaces, hair stands on end, and the smell of ozone announces what is coming.

It differs from Static, a spell of [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] that builds up electrical charge: Ionization excites matter with plasma energy.

### Cost

Tingling in the fingertips and the sensation of accumulated charge.
