# Carapace

Carapace is a medium-scale spell of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] school that coats a body, object, or surface with protective ice. It serves to create personal armor, walls, domes, and temporary bridges.

It differs from Shield, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] school, which blocks through arcane force: Carapace protects through solid ice, and whoever strikes the barrier feels the cold rise up their arm. The cost manifests in the caster as stiffness in the covered limbs and tremors when the effect ends.
