# Troll

The troll is a large creature found in dense forests and caves. It has gray-green skin and a body mass that exceeds twice that of a veteran [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]]. Its defining characteristic is extreme regeneration: wounds close in seconds and severed limbs grow back. Only fire or acid interrupts regeneration and allows permanent death.

### Behavior

They are territorial predators of limited intelligence. By nature they live and hunt alone: they occupy caves, sleep for seasons, and awaken hungry. They do not gather in packs or coordinate their hunting, and the marks they leave on trails are territorial, with no linguistic value. Trolls in packs, surrounding prey from several directions, are an anomaly, and this is how they appear in [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]].
