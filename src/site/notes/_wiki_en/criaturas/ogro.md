# Ogre

Ogres are large humanoids, bulkier than [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] and less intelligent than most sapient races. In the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], they exist primarily as a labor force: bodyguards, security guards, and porters, an expendable and intimidating presence in the lowest strata of the hierarchy.

### Nature

They have thick skin, ranging from yellowish gray to greenish brown, and dense musculature beneath layers of fat that function as natural armor. Their height ranges from two point two meters to two point eight meters. Their jaws are broad, their teeth flattened for crushing, their eyes small and deep-set. Their body stench is intense, distinct from that of [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnolls]]: where gnolls stink of carrion, ogres stink of rancid tallow and sour sweat.

### Behavior

Their intelligence is limited but functional. Ogres understand simple orders, recognize hierarchies, and develop rudimentary loyalties. They have no documented culture of their own, oral tradition, or complex tribal structure. Where orcs build clans and gnolls occupy dens, ogres gravitate toward whoever feeds them and gives them purpose.

### Place in the Empire

They are employed as bodyguards for wealthy traders, litter bearers for the nobility, security guards at arenas and black markets, porters in military operations that require brute force without stealth, and heavy muscle in orcish fortresses. The Edict of Bestial Servitude classifies them as “first-degree domestic fauna”, one step above gnolls: they may move through cities with a brand and collar, with no cage required. In practice, the distinction matters little, and most urban ogres serve until they die, replaced without ceremony. One illustrative case is Brumgak, an ogre bodyguard for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]] of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], killed over a gambling debt; his cloak, wide enough to cover an adult gnoll, passed to [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]] as an urban disguise.

In the elevators descending from Morvgrad to the Depths, chained ogres turn the cranks. In the shaft of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], a thousand fathoms to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], four of them move the platform under the gaze of a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lieutenant. The whip is rarely necessary: the ration is only handed out if the mechanism does not stop, and the fasts consume the fat that serves as their armor. A crank ogre turns until death and is replaced without ceremony.
