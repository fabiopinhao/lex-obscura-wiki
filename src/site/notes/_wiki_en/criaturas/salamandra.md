# Salamander

The salamander is a small to medium-sized reptile similar to the real-world salamander, found in hot and volcanic environments. It differs from mundane salamanders in one distinctive characteristic: highly flammable blood capable of igniting organic matter on contact.

### Use

Salamander blood is collected and stored in sealed flasks as a military and alchemical resource. It is the most effective and portable method for eliminating [[_wiki_en/criaturas/troll\|trolls]] in the field. It is a scarce resource, with no more than five drops per troll, which makes it precious on campaign, and it circulates as an alchemical reagent among apothecaries and barracks. [[_wiki_en/personagens/grub\|Grub]], a young [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]], is a master of the tactical use of salamander alchemical fire. Outside alchemical jargon, the preparation is called an incinerating potion, and its price reflects its scarcity: one drop is worth a guard’s monthly salary, and emptying an entire flask is a campaign expense.
