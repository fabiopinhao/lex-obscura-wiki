# Imp

The imp is a lesser creature of diabolic origin, small in size, with blood-red skin and functional scarlet wings. It hatches from eggs and serves [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lords as a messenger and working creature; sustained long-distance flight makes it a natural messenger in the urban centers of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. Common usage treats the species as feminine: the imp, the imps.

### Physiology

Small in size, too large to be a bird and too small to count as a threat, the imp has blood-red skin and scarlet membranous wings that fold with a leathery snap. A smell of sulfur permeates its skin, perceptible even after hours of flight. It is capable of sustained flight over long urban distances, spiraling down to land, which makes it a natural messenger. It hatches from eggs. Little is known about its longevity: most die by violence before any biological limit manifests itself.

### Distribution

It is common around [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]] and rare among the towers of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], virtually unknown beyond the orcish fortresses at the edge of the plateau. A sighting outside these domains is enough of an anomaly to put veterans on alert.

### Servitude

Imps exist almost exclusively as servile creatures, bound to servitude from the egg and treated as disposable property, trivial to replace. They serve in Shadar towers alongside [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobolds]], classified among the “lesser, hybrid, broken creatures” that work there; no Shadar or [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] shares this role with them. They have a reputation for insolence, and a kobold proverb crystallizes the prejudice: a good servant does not smile without orders, as imps do. Their characteristic occupation is winged messenger service, carrying scrolls and sealed papyri in direct flight between sender and recipient. They are forbidden to enter [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_tabreth\|Khar’Tabreth]], a restriction neither explained nor disobeyed; correspondence sealed with purple ink does not pass through the registry center, and travels by imp, from hand to hand, outside the official record.

### Culture

There is no imp culture. There are no communities, traditions, or organizations of the species’ own: imps appear as individuals only when useful to a master. If a society exists outside Shadar dominion, it is unknown, and the species lacks collective identity, a people, or memory.

### Quaz’har

[[_wiki_en/personagens/quaz_har\|Quaz’har]] is the best-known imp, a court messenger in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]] and the fourth to serve in the post, as the previous three died in service. She knows all of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] better than any previous messenger, with enough agility in flight to maneuver around towers and land in narrow windows. She stutters when speaking, although her internal thoughts are sharp, bitter, and lucid; the distance between what she thinks and what she can articulate defines her presence.
