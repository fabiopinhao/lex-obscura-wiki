# Isopod

The isopod is a giant arthropod from the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. It has a chitinous carapace and multiple legs, and moves along the natural tracks in the caves.

### Use

It is used as a pack animal by [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derro]] caravans, which attach transport platforms to their carapaces. During silent journeys, its bells are wrapped in felt. One example is Helk, an isopod from the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]] caravan.
