# Corvelho

The corvelho is a common creature in the cemeteries of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], half crow and half rat, with sparse feathers and a scaly tail.

### Habits

It sleeps on gravestones, and from time to time swings its tail to keep flies away. Gravediggers tolerate the species because it keeps the cemetery free of smaller pests.

### In the Garden of the Innocents

In the abandoned children’s cemetery of the [[_wiki_en/locais/jardim_dos_inocentes\|Garden of the Innocents]], where mothers at prayer and hired mourners are no longer found among the gravestones, a corvelho is the only living being in sight.
