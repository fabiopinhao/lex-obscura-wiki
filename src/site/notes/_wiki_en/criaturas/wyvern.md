# Wyvern

The wyvern is a lesser, bipedal dragon with membranous wings, a distant and degenerate cousin of true dragons. It inhabits the mountains.

### Behavior

An opportunistic predator with an indiscriminate appetite. It bears a venomous stinger on its tail.

### Divine Form

The wyvern gives form to the manifestation of [[_wiki_en/panteao/aethravos\|Aethravos]], the Nacreous Wyvern, which is an order of being distinct from the beast of the mountains.
