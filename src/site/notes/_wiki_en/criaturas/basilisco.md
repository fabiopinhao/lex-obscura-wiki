# Basilisk

The basilisk is a monstrous reptile with a petrifying gaze. Direct eye contact with the creature results in partial or total petrification of the victim, depending on the specimen’s potency and the duration of exposure. Predominantly subterranean in habitat, it has as its natural territory the Depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] and the continent’s unmapped caves.
