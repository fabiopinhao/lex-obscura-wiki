# Vitraxil

The vitraxil is a small, fragile beetle from the subterranean regions of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], with mosaic elytra that break the glow-slime into cold windows. When it opens its wings, the gallery gains living stained-glass windows, and its flight streaks the air with liquid flashes that dissolve in the cave’s next blink.

Its delicacy gave rise to expressions in common use among the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]]: “little vitraxil face” describes a delicate appearance, and “closed vitraxil” evokes something drawn in and tiny. The name of [[_wiki_en/personagens/vhraxil\|Vhraxil]], guard of the Gates of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], echoes that of the beetle, and it is from the beetle that [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith Morghast]] draws the two taunts directed at him: calling him “little beetle” and saying that he has a “little vitraxil face”.
