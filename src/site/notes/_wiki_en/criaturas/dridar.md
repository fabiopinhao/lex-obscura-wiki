# Drídar

The drídar is a giant arachnid from [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], considered one of the most dangerous creatures underground. It develops with extreme speed, from the egg stage to colossal proportions. Unlike ordinary giant spiders, the drídars raised by the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] matriarchs belong to lineages selected for aggressiveness and intelligence.

### Behavior

They are highly intelligent. At birth, they hide with a singular ability to remain undetectable. When they reach adult size and are discovered, they attack indiscriminately and turn the site into a nest, covering it in webs. They wrap live victims as a food reserve.

### Military Use

The matriarchs of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]] employed drídars as weapons of mass destruction against rival citadels. The tactic consisted of planting [[_wiki_en/artefatos/gema-viva\|living gems]], the eggs, at strategic points in enemy cities. When the drídars matured and were discovered, the resulting devastation weakened the citadel enough for Ghilanna to subjugate it. Smaller citadels still exist as abandoned nests, unrecognizable beneath layers of webbing.

Called “the dragons of Vor-Ghol”, adult drídars are fed spider black magic by the matriarchs and released when something needs to cease to exist. Without this magic, a living gem is an uncontrolled disaster, and therefore has no buyer on the surface.

### Recent Anomaly

There are reports of [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]] attacking drídars suicidally, unprecedented behavior, since Murans normally flee. The cause is unknown, possibly linked to the “something” that makes them flee the Depths.
