# Strige

The strige is a winged creature resembling a bat, with a long, bloodsucking beak. It hunts in flocks and drains its victim to death when it is not removed in time.

### Habits

It sleeps upside down, hanging by its claws, with its wing membranes pulsing at rest. Its activity is nocturnal: the flocks remain inert throughout the day and take flight when night brings the hour of the hunt.

### Presence in Morvgrad

The nests occupy the highest cornices of the wall of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], along the stretch where the top remains devoid of sentinels. Cohabitation is tolerated: the city does not clean the cornices, and the top of the wall belongs to the striges before it belongs to the guard.
