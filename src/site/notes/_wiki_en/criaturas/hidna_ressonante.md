# Resonant Hidna

The Resonant Hidna is a bioluminescent mineral organism that moves through [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] in the form of a mirrored tunnel, alive with sound and heat. Marching, voices, or bare metal make the earth below resonate until it splits apart, and whoever answers the song is buried by their own reflection.

It is the reason why [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] troops do not march in formation through those galleries. Among them, the common warning is that the hidna explodes at the sound of a boot.
