# Manticore

The manticore is a beast with a lion’s body, a scorpion’s tail, and a face that parodies human features. Its venom paralyzes the victim before dissolving their organs.

### Behavior

Some manticores learn to imitate a child’s cries or calls for help to lure victims.

### Use in Language

“Manticore’s kiss” is a common expression for fatal betrayals, the kind that smiles before killing.

### Divine Form

The manticore gives form to the manifestation of [[_wiki_en/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]], the Funereal Manticore, which is an order of being distinct from the beast described above.
