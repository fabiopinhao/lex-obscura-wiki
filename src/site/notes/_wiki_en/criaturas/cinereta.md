# Cinereta

The cinereta is a beast of burden bred by the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] for the highlands. It has a short neck, cloven hooves, and clearly visible ribs beneath sparse fur, and pulls a cart over terrain that would break a plains animal.

### Silence

It neither neighs nor snorts. Silence is a trait of the species and does not depend on training, which makes the cinereta the preferred draft animal for those who need to move cargo without announcing their route.

### The Name

The name comes from its slate-gray coat. Some prefer another interpretation, that of temperament: cold as dead ash.
