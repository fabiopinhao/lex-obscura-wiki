# Gnoll

Gnolls are hyena-shaped humanoids, massive and taller than most humanoids, with raw, dense musculature, black muzzles, tangled manes, and bulging yellow eyes. An overwhelming, permanent stench is the species’ hallmark. Their racial origin is undetermined, and they do not descend from the [[_wiki_en/racas/vulfano\|Vulfans]]. They are associated with devils and demons, and live short lives, between twenty-five and thirty-five years.

### Culture

Violent, direct, and without civilization in the formal sense, they live in dens, occupying caves or abandoned shelters; losing their den reduces them to desperate nomads, for a gnoll without a lair is a carcass that forgot to fall. Brute ferocity compensates for limited intelligence, and they fight with heavy or improvised weapons, such as lead flails studded with spikes. The arena is their only route to social advancement: the Slaughterhouse of Drakmur and the Spear of Three Skulls tournament bring renown to names such as that of [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]], a veteran and champion whose ethics sum up the species: a good rat comes back alive, a dead rat doesn’t buy.

### Legal Status

The Edict of Bestial Servitude classifies them as “second-degree domestic fauna,” prohibited in cities without a brand, collar, or cage. For this reason, Fandegarrón operates in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] disguised beneath an [[_wiki_en/criaturas/ogro\|ogre]] cloak, which belonged to Brumgak, a former bodyguard killed over a gambling debt, and serves as a bodyguard for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]], with Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]] ignoring the illegality out of investigative necessity. Their weaknesses are known: limited intelligence makes them predictable in prolonged combat, their status as fauna leaves them legally disposable, and the stench prevents any discretion.

### Relationships

Fandegarrón lost his den twice, first to the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] and then to a creature he describes as a toad, against which he holds a lasting grudge: he repeats that the toad pushed the world, and whoever tells the story describes it as a demon dressed as a toad. The loss of his territory thrust the gnoll into the wandering life of a mercenary. He developed an unexpected loyalty to Justiciar Nidzina after she helped him recover his lair. In the [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worg]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/tibaut\|Tibaut]], he has a cousin by pack convention, a bond of honor between distinct races and not blood kinship; it is Tibaut who holds the gnoll back when noise can prove costly.
