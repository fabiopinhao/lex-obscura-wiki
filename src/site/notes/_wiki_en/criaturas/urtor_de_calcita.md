# Calcite Urtor

The calcite urtor is a cave-dwelling predator of the crustacean-reptilian type, common in the deepest galleries of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. It lives attached to cracks in calcite, where it disguises itself as a stalactite, and its transparent pincers discharge a secretion that attracts heat and vibration, confusing travelers with a false reflection of firelight.

It is a known danger along caravan routes, feared enough to justify armed escorts.
