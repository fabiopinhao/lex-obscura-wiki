# Hideous Servant

The hideous servant is a creature summoned through necromantic magic or dark pacts. It is not a natural species, but an animated construct, with forms that vary according to the summoner and the available material: reanimated corpses, amalgams of flesh and bone, or entities summoned from lower planes.

### Use

[[_wiki_en/personagens/qaelun\|Qaelun]], a necromancer of the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_vozrador\|temple of Vozrador]], used the [[_wiki_en/magia/circulo_de_sutura\|Circle of Suture]], a necromantic heptagram, to animate the dead as hideous servants. He was eliminated by [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]].
