# Khemor

Khemor, in [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] usage, is the mask of fear. Whoever wears it creates fear, and with fear comes power. No smith forges it, because the mask is not an object. It precedes the feeling it itself creates. The exemplary massacre, calculated absence, and cruelty staged in precisely the right measure are cuts in the same piece. Under Khemor, obedience requires no presence from the lord: one fears what the mask has done, and what the mask has done rules in his place.

### Doctrine

Khemor is the doctrinal complement to [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s imperial mantra, “order demands blood”: blood establishes order, and the mask manufactures the fear that maintains it. The scribes of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] teach the former, and the soldiers know the latter. Imperial peace is sustained beneath the mask of fear.

The doctrine has an established liturgical form, *Pax Vult Khemor*, a maxim that condenses this vernacular formulation into the ritual language of the Cult.

### Incarnations

The foremost example of Khemor is Arothistar himself, a monarch who has not been seen for thirty years and who is feared in every square on the continent. His mask is absence itself, and it covers the entire [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]]. Khemor is within reach of any leader: each carves his own to his own measure. Among the military men of the current generation, [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]], the Fist of Arothistar, is regarded as the one who wears it best: in the barracks, it is said that his troops respect him for Khemor, above the man and his conquests.

### Barracks Usage

In the mouths of the soldiery, a commander’s Khemor is the sum of the stories that arrive before him: the tally of the dead from previous campaigns, recounted from camp to camp. Wearing a Khemor that measures up is, for a Shadar officer, a credential that no rank confers.
