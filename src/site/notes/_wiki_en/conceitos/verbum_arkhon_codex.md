# Verbum Arkhon

The Verbum Arkhon, or the Word of the Sovereign, is the first and most sacred section of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]]. It gathers the direct pronouncements of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], including visions, decrees, and dark parables, written in golden ink on black parchment. It is held to be infallible and immutable, and reading it is mandatory during judicial vigils.

### Supremacy over the Lex Obscura

The Verbum Arkhon did not repeal the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]]: it aligned itself with it and superseded it, being both revolution and continuity, radical change in the form of administrative reform. In practice, any decree of the Verbum can suspend or supersede any clause of the Lex when the sovereign so decides. This dynamic enacts a notion of sovereignty in which the sovereign is not the one who applies the law, but the one who decides when the law does not apply: Arothistar can declare even [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]], the very creator of the Lex, a heretic, because the sovereign decision precedes the norm.

### Relationship with the Justiciars

For the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], the Verbum Arkhon is both a source of authority and an absolute limitation. Its word justifies every interrogation, every execution, and every sentence, but that same word can revoke them, remove them from office, or condemn them without appeal. The formula pronounced during the Anointing expresses this subordination: “Arothistar est lex. [[_wiki_en/panteao/abbhal\|Abbhal]] est vox. Tu es martellus.”

### The Language of Exception

The sovereignty that the Verbum Arkhon embodies has a linguistic counterpart. Just as the sovereign decides when the Lex Obscura does not apply, he also decides in which language the exception is declared. The imperial hegemony of [[_wiki_en/linguas/reinkardico\|Reinkardic]] is this same principle of sovereignty transposed to the linguistic plane: the Word of the Sovereign and the single language of government are two faces of a single gesture, that of deciding what holds. It is the principle that structures the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/politica_linguistica\|Language Policy of the Shadow Empire]].

### Recorded Word

Among the sayings attributed to the sovereign is the maxim that sets the tone for his body of doctrine: “Where the light fails, the shadow judges.” (Verbum Arkhon, 3:17)
