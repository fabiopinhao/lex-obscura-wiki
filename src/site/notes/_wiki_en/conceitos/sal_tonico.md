# Tonic Salt

Tonic salt is a caustic mixture of mineral salts and ironroot extract, developed during the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]] to revive soldiers who have fainted in the field.

### Inquisitorial Repurposing

[[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] alchemists and executioners refined the formula for inquisitorial use, with the same principle as its military application and the purpose reversed: because it prevents the victim from losing consciousness, it ensures that torture is not interrupted by fainting. It is among the interrogation instruments of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]].

### Application

Applied to a collapsed interrogation subject, it restores consciousness without restoring composure. The effect is immediate and is not analgesic.
