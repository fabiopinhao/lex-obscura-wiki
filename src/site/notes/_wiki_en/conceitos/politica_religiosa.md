# Religious Policy

The religious policy of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] is organized around the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], which is not monotheistic. The Cult favors the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|black gods]] as its institutional foundation, for the orderly evil, functional hierarchy, and cruel legalism they offer, and tolerates, but distrusts, the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|red gods]], whose chaos and impulsiveness threaten the stability of the empire.

### Tension between Order and Chaos

The opposition between the diabolical and the demonic is structural. Those bound by pacts to different gods receive contradictory commands: the ordered expansion of a patron of order and the destruction of a patron of chaos serve the same empire under incompatible principles. The prohibition on demonic pacts responds to this tension. [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] classified such pacts as the crime of [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]], punishable in the Pits of [[_wiki_en/locais/drokh_malahar\|Drokh’Malahar]], with the declared purpose of eliminating sources of chaos within the empire.

### Justiciars and Divine Neutrality

The reason the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] are not bound by pacts is political, not theological: they cannot be divided between obedience to the monarch and the particular wishes of divine patrons. The [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]] system, magic without a pact, born of an innate awakening and honed through technical study, ensures that the Justiciars answer only to Arothistar and the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]].

### Internal Factions

Within the Cult itself, factions vie over the philosophical direction of the empire. The orderly faction, aligned with [[_wiki_en/panteao/velmorain\|Velmorain]] as its ideological patron, advocates consolidation through legalism, bureaucracy, and strict order, and uses the Justiciar system, the Lex Obscura, and the Templar hierarchy as its tools. The expansionist faction, aligned with [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]], prioritizes territorial conquest at any cost, through perpetual military expansion and total subjugation, and has complete domination of the material plane as its philosophical horizon. [[_wiki_en/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]], a demon outlawed under the crime of Vinculum Daemonis, is not officially a patron of any faction; his influence operates clandestinely, feeding impulses of destruction and terror that both factions exploit when it suits them, without either openly claiming him. This tension between the factions is not public: it operates behind the scenes, in rivalries among councilors, in the promotions of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]], and in the selective interpretation of the Lex Obscura.
