# Applied Racial Hierarchy: The Empire’s Operational Premise

The racial hierarchy of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] is the premise that races possess ontological predispositions that determine their compatibility with a centralized order. [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] operates under this conviction, and much of the monarchy’s state policy derives from it.

### The Premise

In the imperial view, [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] are perfected humans, orderly and adaptable to vertical power, and therefore the ideal subjects of the empire. The [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]], corrupted elves with a matriarchal and traditionalist culture, are tolerated but kept under watch because of the autonomy attributed to them. The [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], demonized elves characterized by brute strength and direct obedience, are useful as instruments. The [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], however, preserved elves bound to the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], refuse submission and are treated as irreconcilable enemies, while the [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]], luminous elves who retain a memory of greatness, are seen as a race to be driven to extinction.

The operational translation is direct: elves, in all their variants, would be structurally unsuited to a centralized empire, because their nature privileges autonomy over obedience, tradition over adaptation, and horizontal bonds over vertical hierarchy.

### Political Application

Arothistar’s distrust of the Druls functions as a racial diagnosis applied as policy. Since he cannot exterminate them, given that [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|the Underground Alliance]] is a military pillar, he manages them as a controlled risk: the matriarchs retain autonomy in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], but are excluded from key positions in the surface empire; the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|ophideias]], loyal Druls, serve as a bridge kept under watch; and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], pure Shadars, exercise oversight without relying on Drul pacts.

### Systemic Function

Within its own logic, imperial racism is not arbitrary, but a functional system. Arothistar believes that the concentration of power among the Shadars is necessary for stability, and treats the racial hierarchy as social engineering, not as cruelty. The Justiciars execute Elarins and Sylvans without guilt because they operate within a worldview in which elves are considered incompatible with civilized order.

The structure itself, however, contains counterpoints: [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]], a loyal Drul, demonstrates that individual loyalty transcends the predisposition attributed to the race, and [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]] questions, from within, a system that confuses control with necessity.
