# Charabanc

The charabanc is an animal-drawn provincial vehicle with four wheels and an elongated body. At the front, bench seats accommodate passengers; at the back, an open space holds cargo. It occupies the middle ground between a work cart and a lord’s carriage.

### Provincial Use

In a village such as [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], a single charabanc serves the entire administration: it transports stewards, tribute, and, when the occasion calls for it, the condemned.
