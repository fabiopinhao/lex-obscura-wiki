# The Widow of Valdris

*The Widow of [[_wiki_en/locais/valdris\|Valdris]]* is a classic play from [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]. The plot follows a female elf, married to a man, who manipulates the family inheritance.

### Two Moralities

The same character supports opposing interpretations depending on who stages her. In the human original, the widow is a villain, punished for her greed. In the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] adaptation, she becomes a heroine celebrated for turning cunning into fortune.

Where Myrgarde sees the sin of greed, [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] sees virtue in the ambition to conquer.
