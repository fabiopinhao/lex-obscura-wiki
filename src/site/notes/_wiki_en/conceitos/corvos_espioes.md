# Spy Crows

Spy crows are crows employed as eyes and couriers by those with the power to maintain them, a surveillance practice widespread across the skies and roads of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. Black and patient, they carry small metal objects attached to their bodies, gather what they see, and take what they gather to where such things are read. An attentive traveler notices them; a prudent traveler does not presume to know whom they serve.

### Owners

They are not an official apparatus of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]: there is no unified network or state monopoly. They belong to those with the resources to maintain them, including shadow lords, councilors, sorcerers, necromancers, and influential [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], and each flock answers to its owner. No one on the road knows how many owners the sky carries on any given day. The aggregate effect is diffuse surveillance: every pace a person of consequence takes outside the walls of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] is known, not because anyone centralizes the information, but because there is always someone’s crow.

### Range and Limits

The crows do not read minds and cannot pick up the voice of someone riding in silence. They watch only what is visible: routes, companions, stops, and cargo.
