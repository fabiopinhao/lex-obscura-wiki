# Black Market

The black market is the parallel economy that runs through the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], tolerated where it is profitable and persecuted where it is inconvenient. It has no single institution or central command. Under this name, the imperial administration groups together all exchanges that evade [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/coletoria\|the Collectory]].

### Main Marketplace

Its most visible marketplace is the [[_wiki_en/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]], in the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]], where it operates on a semiofficial basis under the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]] of [[_wiki_en/personagens/dribbel_olgund\|Dribbel Olgund]]. Semiofficialization is the characteristic arrangement of the imperial black market: forbidden trade takes place at a known address, with a collector at the door, because total suppression would cost more than negotiated tolerance brings in.

### Goods

The forbidden catalogs of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_de_engrenagens\|the Gear Guild]] circulate through it, offering anything from assassin automatons to enchanted prostheses at a fraction of the price and quality of the dwarven originals. Counterfeit seals of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] also circulate, reproduced frequently enough that the Cult keeps permanent auditors, even though seal forgery is a capital crime.

### Accounting

The black market’s [[_wiki_en/conceitos/livros_razao\|ledgers]] record, by convention, only what their owners admit exists. The veracity of these records is a matter of dispute between those who audit them and those who write them.
