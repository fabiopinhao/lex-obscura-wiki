# Living Bark

Living bark is a plant-based material used by [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]] to make light armor. It remains alive after being shaped, with the texture of moist cortex, and breathes against the body of its wearer.

### Use

It lends itself to reinforced light armor, which clings to the body like a second skin and follows movement without the rigidity of plate components. It is the characteristic equipment of Sylvan rangers, suited to those who need to cross dense forest without noise and without losing protection.

### Social Value

Among Sylvans, a piece of living bark serves as a training milestone: the master gives it to the apprentice at a recognized point in training, and no piece is purchased. The material is not inert, and the armor ages alongside its wearer.
