# Nullis

Nullis is the intercalary day that separates the last [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lunia\|Lúnia]] of a [[_wiki_en/conceitos/varnum\|Varnum]] from the first of the next. It belongs to no Lúnia and no season: it is the calendar’s arithmetic remainder, the day that completes the Varnum’s three hundred sixty-five days after the thirteen Lúnias total three hundred sixty-four. It is colloquially called “Vaz Day”, a corruption that links it to [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|the Void]] and to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]]’s vocabulary of curses.

### Cultural Significance

Nullis is a day of superstition, seclusion, and imperial rituals. The population avoids important decisions, travel, and negotiations, and the most superstitious lock doors and windows at nightfall. For the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], it marks ceremonies of renewal and atonement, a day when order is suspended before beginning anew. The connection to the Void is no coincidence: in a world where the void is the absence that precedes and outlasts order, a day that belongs to no temporal structure carries proportional symbolic weight.

### Calendar and Power

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]]’s transitions of power are counted in Varnums, rather than Lúnias, which suggests that the imperial calendar operates in annual cycles for matters of succession.
