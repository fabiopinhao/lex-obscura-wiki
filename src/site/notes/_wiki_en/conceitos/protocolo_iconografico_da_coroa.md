# Iconographic Protocol of the Crown

The Iconographic Protocol of the Crown is the decree by which [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] replaced his physical presence with a symbolic omnipresence in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Instituted after the monarch withdrew permanently to the tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], it mandates that effigies of the sovereign leave the tower from time to time and be placed at important locations throughout the city.

### Context

With the fall of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], about thirty years ago, Arothistar reached the maximum extent of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|shadow dominion]] over [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. With no walls left to tear down or armies to defeat, the monarch withdrew to the tower and never descended again. He abandoned the [[_wiki_en/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]] and began governing through intermediaries and the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]]. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] operates as an instrument of the monarch’s will, not as a partner in government.

### Channels of Omnipresence

Under the decree, each active temple of the shadow gods bears a work by the monarch, a painting or statue, wrought by Arothistar’s own hands. Through them he sees and hears, and sometimes speaks; what weighs most heavily on the faithful is his silent gaze. The exact nature of the enchantment is a mystery; all that is known is that they are windows, and every window looks both ways.

The effigies are no mere decoration: they function as channels of omnipresence, symbolic and, in some cases, arcane projections of the monarch’s presence. The [[_wiki_en/artefatos/efigie_de_arothistar\|Effigy of Arothistar]] on the bridge of Mor’Severith is an animated statue with onyx eyes that light up silver, capable of watching everything between the bridge and the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]] and replicating the master’s voice. In the Shadow Council Hall, the effigy was erected one span above the line of the twelve [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], a detail that [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] tries to rationalize as uneven masonry, but whose message is unequivocal: the monarch places himself above the gods.

At the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], the site of the anointing ceremonies of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], a mural painting of Arothistar emanated an unusual radiance during the anointing of [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh]]. The painting in the bathing room of Mor’Severith offers a direct view of the same temple.

### Political Implications

The Protocol is the material expression of a philosophy of government: force conquers, but only order sustains. Arothistar’s physical absence is compensated for by a presence that does not sleep, does not become distracted, and does not negotiate. The effigies are permanent reminders that the monarch’s gaze reaches every corner of the Empire, even where he never sets foot.

Combined with the Oculus system and the surveillance of the ravens, the Protocol forms a triad of remote control that dispenses with the ruler’s bodily presence. Arothistar transformed his isolation into an instrument of power.
