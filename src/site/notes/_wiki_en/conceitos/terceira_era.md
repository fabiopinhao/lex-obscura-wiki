# Third Era

The Third Era is the historiographical designation for the period in which the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] consolidated its dominion over [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. There is no formal consensus on its demarcation: some chroniclers date its beginning to the fall of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], while others maintain that the Reinkardic Wars never ended, and that the conflict persists as long as the Empire continues to expand. In the liturgy of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] and in common usage within the Empire, the era is referred to simply as “the New Era”, in deliberate opposition to the old world it replaced.

### The Consolidated Dominion

With the fall of Myrgarde, [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] achieved the maximum extent of shadow dominion over Reinkard. With no more walls to tear down or armies to defeat, the monarch withdrew permanently to [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]] and never again descended from the tower. The sovereign’s physical presence gave way to a symbolic omnipresence, formalized in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/protocolo_iconografico_da_coroa\|Iconographic Protocol of the Crown]]: effigies of Arothistar periodically leave the tower and are installed at significant locations in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]].

### Government

Arothistar abandoned the [[_wiki_en/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]] and began governing through intermediaries and through the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]]. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] operates as an instrument of the monarch’s will, not as a partner in government, and no one dares challenge him, even indirectly. The philosophy underpinning the period is straightforward: force conquers, but only order maintains. The territorial organization of the dominion was established in the form of [[_wiki_en/eventos/reorganizacao_imperial_e_provincias\|imperial provinces]].

### Crises of the Present

Beneath the appearance of absolute dominion, instabilities accumulate along the Empire’s borders. In [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], the arcane nexuses, though active, have been silenced from the inside out through deliberate interference whose nature remains unknown. In [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], sabotage among the dwarven forges causes a decline in production, and [[_wiki_en/personagens/draven-thol\|Draven-Thol]], the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] assigned to the dwarven citadel, has disappeared without a trace. In Morvgrad, [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] relic that had been in the custody of Councilor [[_wiki_en/personagens/dessian_broel\|Dessian Broel]], was stolen from his private museum and passed through the hands of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]] before being confiscated by [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]].

### The Present

In the era’s present, Arothistar inhabits Mor’Severith at the height of his power. [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] has just been anointed Justiciar in the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], where he received the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/spina_lexis\|Spina Lexis]], the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]], and the Oculus, and departs for [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]] on his first official mission. [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], at ninety-nine years old, is the dean of the Justiciars, with forty-four years of service, while Nidzina, young and ambitious, rises rapidly within the order.

[[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]] watches from the shadows of his temples, and the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] wait. In the depths of his plane of existence, [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] remains sealed, conscious and weakened, but not destroyed.
