# Mother Oak

The mother oak is the first oak planted in each sacred grove. It becomes the ancestral tree around which the druids build their sanctuaries. Its death ends the druidic power of that forest.

### Druidic Circle

Also called the Druidic Circle, it is the ritual structure of roots and ancient stones that surrounds the mother oak. The [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] druids prepare it to concentrate and protect the power of the sacred ground.

It functions as a barrier against evil, but, without constant maintenance, it succumbs to the same corruption it is supposed to contain.
