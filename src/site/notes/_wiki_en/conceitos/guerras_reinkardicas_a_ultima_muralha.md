# Reinkardic Wars: The Last Wall

The Reinkardic Wars designate the long conflict between [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] that ended with the fall of the alabastrine capital and the consolidation of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]’s dominion over [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]]. The first fifteen years of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s monarchy were not years of conquest, but of consolidation: from fifty-five to forty years ago, the two empires oscillated between cold wars and localized battles on the plains, while Myrgarde, already unable to reach the plateau, watched Morvgrad erect occupation forts and subjugate surrounding villages.

### Internal Consolidation

Arothistar used the period to reshape Morvgrad from within. Imperial decrees began to coexist with the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]], and the tax system, previously fragmented among temples and lords, was centralized under the crown. Tieflings and [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] began to move freely throughout the empire, a decision that provoked open outrage among the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]]; in return, the monarch guaranteed protection for the population of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]] against wild Draconids. Hybrid and subterranean races gained free spaces in the capital, just as they had already been moving about in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]] for millennia, except in the sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]].

Shortly afterward, the monarch instituted the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]], which began to serve as the Shadow Empire’s constitution; the Lex Obscura was incorporated without alteration, alongside the new sovereign decrees. Forty-four years ago, to overcome resistance from part of the shadow lord elite, Arothistar founded the institution of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], a judicial arm with power of life and death over the old and the new law. The Templars ceased to exist as separate military forces, and at first the term survived only for [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|ophideias]], sorcerers, and necromancers of the temples. [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], a former Templar of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], was the first Justiciar appointed. Around the same time, Arothistar prohibited the [[_wiki_en/magia/rito_de_ossarium\|Rite of Ossárium]], which he himself had undergone decades earlier, and the prohibition reduced the flow of converted humans into the empire.

In the early years of the Justiciars, several shadow lords considered dissidents were executed: the monarch’s law did not distinguish between external enemies and internal resistance. Morvgrad also began sending spies into rival territory, [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] disguised as humans, mostly young, whose shadowy features had not yet become pronounced; on the other side, spies from Myrgarde discovered in Morvgrad were almost all executed, some sent to the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]]. Forty years ago, Arothistar emptied [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], formerly the library and residence of the Council’s elite, and took it as his private tower.

### The Conquest Phase

Beginning thirty-nine years ago, with the capital restructured, Arothistar returned to the field with new generals and orcish legions better trained than at any previous point in the Cult’s history. Ten years of devastating conquests followed. Reinkard’s densest forests burned; [[_wiki_en/locais/lendalas\|Lendalas]], one of the largest sacred forests of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], was consumed by alchemical fire unleashed by [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]]’s legions, and what remained became the [[_wiki_en/locais/ermo_de_lendalas\|Waste of Lendalas]], a gray expanse of open ground where the earth refuses to sprout. Only the titanic forests, protected by ancient pacts with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], withstood the flames, and [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] remained inviolate.

The magical citadels of the [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]] fell one by one. [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], the most renowned, allied with Myrgarde since the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]], fell around thirty-three years ago; its [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/nexus\|arcane nexuses]], the continent’s largest raw sources of magical reagents, were partially incorporated into the imperial infrastructure, and the number of elf slaves soared. The dwarves of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], subjugated decades earlier, were already producing for the Cult in their own forges, where [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]] rested, a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] relic kept since the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]] without the dwarves ever understanding its nature; after the conquest, a shadow lord took it to his private gallery in Morvgrad, treating it as a trophy. The monarch planned to turn [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] into a vassal state through diplomatic means.

### Siege and Fall of Myrgarde

Around thirty-three years ago, for the first time in history, Morvgrad’s troops had the size, organization, and logistics to encircle the entire capital of the old empire. The siege lasted three years. Within the walls, hunger, plague, and despair consumed the population, and the luminous gods fell silent one by one, prayers losing potency as though the deities were retreating before what was approaching. Only the [[_wiki_en/locais/jardim_dos_inocentes\|Garden of the Innocents]], Myrgarde’s children’s cemetery, remained operational throughout the siege, the last refuge of dignity in a city losing its reason to resist.

When the gates gave way, Arothistar appeared in person, the last time he took the field before his permanent isolation in Mor’Severith, and on that occasion decreed in a speech the definitive abolition of the Rite, now a capital heresy. The seat of the church that had once persecuted the first Shadars bowed beneath the shadow, and the conquest marked the end of the Reinkardic Wars. Gnomes who had prospered beneath the walls were enslaved in the deep forges; humans who resisted or knew too much were imprisoned in Ghol’Drokh, some sustained by necromancy so that oblivion would not free them. The continent’s resistance dwindled to isolated pockets, and Thal’Serin remained the last significant refuge.
