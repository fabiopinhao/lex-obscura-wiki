# Reinkardic Wars

The Reinkardic Wars were the continental conflict that ended the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]] and produced the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. They lasted about fifty years, consumed all of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]], and pitted [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], the religious and military capital of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], against [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] and the kingdoms of the plains.

### Cause

The war arose from a geographical limit. In the last century of the Second Era, the Cult had exhausted the possibilities offered by the plateau: the outlying fortresses were garrisoned, the temples unified under the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]], and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]] consolidated. It was a power with nowhere to expand, except downward.

The descent began with hunger, in [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]]: the first region to fall under the Cult fell without a single formal battle being fought.

### Phases

The conflict is divided into three periods. [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_sombra_desce\|The Shadow Descends]] covers the [[_wiki_en/eventos/guerras_reinkardicas_inicio\|descent from the plateau]], the [[_wiki_en/eventos/submissao_da_ventrista\|submission of Ventrista]], and the conquest of the smaller human kingdoms under Templar command. [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_ascensao_do_monarca\|The Rise of the Monarch]] encompasses Dravhok’s campaigns, among them the [[_wiki_en/eventos/campanha_ocidental_de_dravhok\|fall of the dwarven forges]], and the purge that led him to declare himself [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]. [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_ultima_muralha\|The Last Wall]] covers the fifteen years of consolidation of the monarchy and the final siege of Myrgarde.

### Consequences

The war altered the map of the continent and redefined who counted as people within it. From it emerged the [[_wiki_en/eventos/formalizacao_militar_dos_orcs\|military formalization of the orcs]], the use of the other goblinoids as logistical machinery, the slavery of the defeated races, and the racial hierarchy that the Empire would later apply as an operational premise. Its conclusion, with the fall of Myrgarde, opens the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]].
