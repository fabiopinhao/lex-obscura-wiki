# Essential Terminology

Essential Terminology brings together terms used across the world of Lex Obscura, words that span multiple contexts and whose nomenclature affects reading, but that have no dedicated entry. For terms specific to a particular domain, consult the corresponding article.

### Terms

- **Justiciar**: Official title of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]]’s judges-enforcers. “Judge” in formal and bureaucratic registers; “Enforcer” in blunt, military, or cruel registers.
- **shadow lord**: Status designation for high-ranking [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]].
- **[[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]]**: “The Word of the Sovereign.” First section of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]]: direct decrees from [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], with supremacy over the Lex Obscura.
- **[[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]]**: “Law of Shadows.” The Empire’s legal code, created by [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]] in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]]. Second section of the Codex.
- **[[_wiki_en/cosmologia/noctiluz\|noctiluz]]**: Shadowy light emitted by fissures in the sky above the Shadar plateau. Appears during significant events.
- **[[_wiki_en/conceitos/canalizador\|channeler]]**: Imperial term for druids, used officially and pejoratively. Designates a social category, not the mechanics of magic; the imperial classification divides channelers into three grades according to the power detected in [[_wiki_en/magia/deteccao_de_alinhamento\|Alignment Detection]].
- **[[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]]**: Pejorative imperial designation for subjugated humans and elves. It is not a race: it is a social condition.
- **Stygian Stone**: Black material used in Shadar structures and ritual objects.
- **[[_wiki_en/conceitos/protocolo_iconografico_da_coroa\|Iconographic Protocol of the Crown]]**: Rules governing representations of Arothistar, instituted after his isolation in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]].

### Quotations and Mottos

Formulas of a doctrinal, philosophical, and political nature that circulate throughout the Empire and its cults.

- *“Glory does not move my hand; order demands blood.”*: Mantra of the Justiciars
- *“Arothistar est lex. [[_wiki_en/panteao/abbhal\|Abbhal]] est vox. Tu es martellus.”*: Anointing formula of the Justiciars
- *“In somniis veritas, in umbris lex, in codice imperium.”*: Motto of the Codex Somniorum
- *“Where light fails, shadow judges.”*: Verbum Arkhon, 3:17
- *“Betrayal of the Cult is betrayal of oneself.”*: Lex Obscura, Section Prima
- *“The guilty person’s blood cries out, but only the judge hears.”*: [[_wiki_en/conceitos/tabula_umbrae_codex\|Tabula Umbrae]], Ritual VII
- *“In the light, all are equal. In the darkness, only the superior survive.”*: [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] philosophy
