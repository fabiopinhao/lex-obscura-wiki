# Privileged Jurisdiction

The shadow lords enjoy “privileged jurisdiction”: their fates are sealed by the official opinions of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] and by the monarch’s final word.

Whether the jurisdiction is in fact “privileged” is a question debated since the beginning of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]].
