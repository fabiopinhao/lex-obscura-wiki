# Second Era

The Second Era is the period of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] ushered in by the [[_wiki_en/eventos/sopro_do_eclipse\|Breath of the Eclipse]], the plague that wiped out a significant portion of the continental population and struck humans, the most numerous and most exposed race, with disproportionate ferocity. Remembered in opposing ways depending on perspective (the Era of Conquest for the imperial power, the Deluded Peace for the subjugated), it is the time when the continent fragments under disease, rebuilds in a cooperative and weakened state, and sees the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau consolidate into a single power.

### The Breath of the Eclipse

According to popular belief, the disease supposedly emerged on a night of an eclipse and spread through the air or through direct contact with the infected. Blame fell on the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|black gods]] and, with them, on Shadars and [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]]: two isolated populations, the former on the northern plateau, the latter in the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], who had no regular contact with the central plains. It is said that the Breath did not reach the plateau. Some attribute this to geographic isolation, while others maintain that Shadars and Druls manufactured the plague to exterminate the races they considered inferior. The truth, if it ever existed in any record, was buried with the dead.

### A Continent Surrounded

For those who tried to flee, there was nowhere to go. Reinkard closed in on itself: the dwarves sealed the interiors of their mountains and severed all contact with the plains, and overnight the continent lost its main supplier of weapons, tools, and precision engineering. The mountain ranges to the west blocked passage, and the [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]] intensified their violence against the desperate who tried to cross outside the valleys of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]]. The [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]] welcomed the humans who reached them and paid dearly: much of their population died after being infected by the refugees they were trying to save. The swamps to the east became traps, and the crossing toward [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]] became suicide. Those who tried the seas were met with gigantic, deadly waves. Gnomes descended into Vor-Ghol in record numbers and found predators, not salvation. To the north, Shadars and [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] welcomed migrants with steel.

### The Sealed Plateau

Animosity toward Shadars and Druls grew into fury. Still within the era’s first centuries, campaigns of vengeance marched on the plateau. Humans and [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarars]], weakened by the plague and driven by hatred, failed against scattered, poorly coordinated forces, and the plateau confirmed its reputation as a natural fortress. The Shadars responded by encircling the entire perimeter with small fortresses, sealing access permanently. During the same period, the shadow corruption accumulated over generations consumed what remained of the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]]’ magical barrier: the residual [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] exhausted itself against the growing diabolic presence. The breach had limited consequences, since the dragons the barrier had been erected to contain were no longer in the vicinity. [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]], young and territorial, resisted longer than any other dragon before abandoning the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]] and migrating to Varnak, in [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]]. With this departure, the northern orc tribes began crossing the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] in ever larger groups, and the fortresses erected against the campaigns of vengeance became, by strategic accident, the only real restraint along the northern border.

### Reconstruction

With the end of the human incursions, the continent began to rebuild. The dwarves reopened the mountains and resumed trade. The shared catastrophe dissolved rivalries that centuries of diplomacy had failed to shake: reconstruction required hands, and no one asked where those wielding the hammers came from. The cities of the plains became more cosmopolitan, with gnomes, humans, half-elves, and [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarins]] living together in mixed neighborhoods. The racial hierarchy persisted, and prejudices did not die with the plague, but survival reshaped the social fabric in a way ideology had never achieved. It was on this weakened and cooperative continent that [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] rose as the dominant power, expanding into a great [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|empire]] that annexed almost all the human city-states. Its secret was twofold, with both elements inherited from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]]: the influence of the Álvarars and the lasting alliance with [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], which supplied it with superior magical equipment and arcane knowledge from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/idade_esquecida\|Forgotten Age]].

### Unification of the Plateau

On the plateau, the absence of external threats allowed an unprecedented reorganization. [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]], the oldest and most cunning of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templar]] leaders, drafted the first document of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] and imposed the unification that the diabolic temples had never tolerated, absorbing cults previously divided into rival orders into a single structure. [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] was born of this unification, deliberately built as the seat of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]]. The Cult had a Shadar core, but was not exclusively Shadar: in an unprecedented development, Druls began living alongside Shadars on the surface, and the exchange transformed both races. The Druls brought millennia of subterranean arcane tradition and a network of influence in Vor-Ghol; the Shadars offered military discipline, administrative ability, and expansionist ambition. Xarn orchestrated the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]], formalizing this symbiosis. The relationship with the orcs evolved in stages, from free passage to raid to the south to logistical support and provisions, cemented by their shared vengeance against humans and elves.

### Legacy

The Second Era ended without Reinkard recovering the population numbers of the First. The demographic deficit left by the Breath of the Eclipse was not reversed by centuries of reconstruction: the plains remained less populated, the armies less numerous. On the plateau, the Shadars had done the opposite, consolidating, unifying, and arming themselves. The imbalance between a weakened continent and a rising shadow power made conflict inevitable, and it was at this threshold that the Reinkardic Wars announced themselves.
