# Imperial Hierarchy: Complementary Levels

The hierarchy of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] is organized into complementary levels of political and military power. Below bodies such as the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/tribunal_de_morvgrad\|Tribunal of Morvgrad]], the intermediate echelons of imperial power are organized.

### Generals

The military arm operates through legions commanded by generals subordinate to the Shadow Council. The best known is the Third Eastern Legion, under [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]], the “Fist of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]”. Generals accumulate de facto territorial power in the regions where their legions are stationed, which creates constant friction with provincial lords: the formal distinction is clear, lords administer and generals wage war, but a stationed legion consumes granaries, recruits the subjugated, and intimidates magistrates. The relationship with the Justiciars is one of institutional hostility. The generals regard the Justiciars as intrusive legalists, and the Justiciars regard the soldiers as instruments too blind to perceive subtle betrayals. The Justiciars’ investigative authority over the generals is a source of permanent resentment. [[_wiki_en/personagens/kazem_vorgrath\|Kazem Vorgrath]] exemplifies the course of a general’s neutralization: Arothistar removed his destructive capacity and channeled his ambition into the imperial bureaucracy.

### Provincial Lords

A system of territorial governance predating Arothistar’s monarchy. Conquered territories are assigned to [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lords responsible for local administration: tribute, maintenance of order, and enforcement of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] on a regional scale. The quality of governance varies by lord. [[_wiki_en/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Merari d’Khunvel]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], is an example of institutional degeneration: weak and manipulable, he lost effective control to his chief of the guard, [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]]. His [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]] was stolen and communications with [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] were intercepted, and the administration disintegrated to the point of requiring intervention by a Justiciar. Provincial lords have medium-high political power and limited autonomy, supervised by the Council, investigated by the Justiciars, and judged by the archons of the Tribunal. The position is coveted for guaranteeing wealth and local influence, and dangerous for exposing its holder to scrutiny from three higher authorities at the same time.

### Officers and Magistrates

The operational layer that executes decisions from above without political autonomy. Military officers command tactical units under the generals’ orders, with a mixed composition, with Shadar officers leading troops consisting predominantly of [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] and goblinoids. [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]], an orc captain in [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]]’s guard, illustrates the exception, since orcs rarely rise to the officer ranks and, when they do, remain subordinate to Shadars of higher rank. The chain of command is vertical and inflexible, and disobedience is punished by summary execution. Local magistrates administer justice in villages and smaller cities under the provincial lords, without autonomous investigative power or jurisdiction over capital crimes.

### The Tripartite Base

The lowest level is divided into three categories with distinct legal statuses. Citizens are Shadars and [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] with full rights: property, commerce, access to temples, and protection under the Lex Obscura; Druls have formal citizenship but operate under permanent tension, allied by pact and not by equality. The troops are orcs, goblinoids, and other martial races recruited into the legions: they are not citizens, have neither legal property nor procedural protection, and serve in a transactional relationship, food and equipment in exchange for bodies. The orcs themselves regard service as a path to honor and hierarchy, not as oppression, and their submission to the Empire is pragmatic, not ideological. The subjugated, at the absolute bottom, are [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriars]], [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]], hybrid humanoids, and lesser races: they have no citizenship, procedural rights, or meaningful legal protection, and any Shadar of a higher caste can dispose of one of them with little or no consequence. Hereditary soul contracts bind entire lineages to perpetual servitude, and the imperial economy depends on this compulsory labor: the granaries of [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]], the forges of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], the construction works of Morvgrad.
