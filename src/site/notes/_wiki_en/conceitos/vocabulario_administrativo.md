# Administrative Vocabulary

The Administrative Vocabulary is the set of institutional euphemisms of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]: official terminology that masks the real nature of coercive procedures. These are terms in a bureaucratic register whose true meaning is deliberately concealed, the doublespeak through which the imperial administration names violence without ever saying it.

### Terms

- **“Seclusion”**: Permanent confinement
- **“Mission of Honor”**: Suicidal task
- **“Final Consecration”**: Ritual execution
- **“Transfer”**: Silent elimination
- **“Eternal Watch”**: Magical suspension
- **“Promotion to the Inner Circle”**: Isolation as an oracle
- **“Contemplative Leave”**: Monitoring before disposal
- **“Permanent Assignment”**: Confinement disguised as a position
- **“Voluntary Service”**: Accepted suicide mission
- **“Honorable Retirement”**: Death from natural causes

### Social Functioning

The vocabulary’s effectiveness lies in the tacit complicity it organizes. Civilians are unaware of the reality behind the words; novices repeat the terms without knowing what they are saying; veterans understand everything and remain silent; families ask no questions. The euphemism does more than deceive: it distributes degrees of knowledge, and the silence of those who understand is part of the procedure.
