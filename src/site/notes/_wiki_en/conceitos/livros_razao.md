# Ledgers

The ledgers of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/mercado_negro\|black market]] record, by convention, only what their owners admit exists.

The dispute over the veracity of these records plays out between those who audit them and those who write them, and remains a philosophical question until a [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] turns it into a criminal matter.
