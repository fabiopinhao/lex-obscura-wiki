# Channeler

Channeler is the imperial term, both official and derogatory, for druids and practitioners of green magic. It functions as a sociopolitical category: it classifies the practitioner’s position in relation to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], without describing their method of spellcasting. The term’s precision matters to the Empire because registration, surveillance, and the tolerance quota applied to each practitioner depend on it.

### Official Classification

The imperial administration divides channelers into three degrees, according to the scale described in [[_wiki_en/magia/deteccao_de_alinhamento\|Alignment Detection]]. The imperial apparatus detects, recruits, and refines the awakened, without creating them: the capacity precedes registration, and this is what makes the count always incomplete.

### Mark and Legitimacy

Outside the imperial framework, a channeler’s legitimacy is attested by signs the Empire does not control. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/marca_das_luas_gemeas\|Mark of the Twin Moons]] identifies its bearer as a legitimate channeler of [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]] even in hostile territory, and is detectable by other beings connected to the forest.

### Vulnerability

Channeling depends on the bond with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], and therefore falls ill when that bond falls ill. Under [[_wiki_en/magia/macula-sombria\|Shadow-Taint]], basic enchantments fail, and rituals that previously required one channeler come to require two, still producing stunted results.
