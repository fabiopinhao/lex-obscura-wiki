# Aroada

Aroada is the favorite game of the youth of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]: two teams compete to send a leather sphere through a hoop fixed between two wooden posts, using only feet, heads, and shoulders. The hoop rests on the chalk line that marks the goal, and a keeper defends it. A foul is contact that knocks an opponent down or stops them; when committed near the defending side’s hoop, it results in a penalty, taken from the chalk mark in front of the hoop, against the keeper alone. Played on makeshift fields, it brought together young people of all races, with humans, dwarves, gnomes, and [[_wiki_en/racas/aviano\|Avians]] sharing the same team, a reflection of the human capital’s cosmopolitanism before the fall.

### Memory of the Siege

A game of Aroada survives in the diary of [[_wiki_en/personagens/coveiro-mor\|the Sexton]] of the [[_wiki_en/locais/jardim_dos_inocentes\|Garden of the Innocents]], written during the third year of the Siege of Myrgarde as a remembrance of times of peace. In it, a twelve-year-old orphan boy included [[_wiki_en/personagens/ioni\|Ioni]], the gravedigger’s son, as the team’s lucky charm. Accidentally knocked down near the hoop when [[_wiki_en/personagens/auren\|Auren]] came down to cut him off, the orphan gave Ioni the decisive penalty, secretly arranging the play with the keeper. [[_wiki_en/personagens/pip\|Pip]] and a young dwarf were also playing, while Sir [[_wiki_en/personagens/grimald\|Grimald]] and Mother [[_wiki_en/personagens/corvina\|Corvina]] watched from the bell tower of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/igreja_de_sao_lucian\|Church of Saint Lucian]].
