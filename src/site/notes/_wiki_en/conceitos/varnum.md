# Varnum

The Varnum is the annual unit of the imperial calendar: three hundred sixty-five days, comprising thirteen [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lunia\|Lúnias]] (three hundred sixty-four days) plus [[_wiki_en/conceitos/nullis\|Nullis]], a separate day that belongs to no Lúnia or season. It is the official basis for measuring time in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], imposed over the local calendars that preceded it.

### Structure and Seasons

The year is divided into four seasons of unequal length, each with a formal name used in scholarly and ceremonial contexts and a common designation in everyday use. Germa, spring, has three Lúnias and eighty-four days, a time of thawing, planting, and the start of military campaigns. Zenith, summer, also three Lúnias and eighty-four days long, marks the solar peak and the early harvest, and shares its name with the watch that marks the peak of the day. Quédia, autumn, three Lúnias and eighty-four days long, brings the late harvest, preparation for the cold, and the withdrawal of troops. Brûma, winter, is the longest season, with four Lúnias and one hundred twelve days of prolonged cold, seclusion, and defensive sieges, reflecting the severity of the climate on the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau and in the mountains of the north.

### Political Use

The Varnum serves as a reference for milestones of power and transitions in government. Succession to the highest seat of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] is counted in Varnums, not Lúnias. Imperial milestones such as the founding of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] and the fall of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] are recorded in years relative to the present. The imperial motto “To measure is to dominate” applies to time as much as to space: standardizing the calendar, imposing Lúnias and Varnums over the systems of [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriars]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]], was one of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]’s first administrative acts.
