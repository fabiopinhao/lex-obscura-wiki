# Lúnia

The Lúnia is the monthly unit of the imperial calendar, also called Month or Lunar Cycle. Each Lúnia has twenty-eight days, divided into four fáscias of seven days, each coinciding with a phase of the moon: New, Waxing, Full, and Waning.

### Structure

Thirteen Lúnias make up the body of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/varnum\|Varnum]], the imperial year, totaling three hundred sixty-four days. The remaining day is [[_wiki_en/conceitos/nullis\|Nullis]], which belongs to no Lúnia.

### Seasonal Distribution

The seasons of the Varnum are measured in Lúnias. Germa, Zenith, and Quédia receive three Lúnias each, that is, eighty-four days. Brûma, the winter of the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] plateau, receives four, totaling one hundred twelve days, reflecting the severity of the northern climate. The asymmetry is not arbitrary: the prolonged winter dictates the rhythm of imperial life, from troop withdrawals to the duration of defensive sieges.

### Usage

Reckoning in Lúnias is used in everyday life and in the military. Contracts, terms of service, patrol cycles, and guard rotations operate in fáscias and Lúnias. The fáscia of the New Moon, the first week of each Lúnia, carries a connotation of darkness and opportunism, while the Full Moon marks ceremonies of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]]. For matters of State and succession, the Varnum prevails as the unit of reference.
