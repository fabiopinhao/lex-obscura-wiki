# Veredictum Exkarthon

The Veredictum Exkarthon is an extreme legal instrument that precedes armed actions under the authority of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]]. It constitutes one of the very few procedural protections in the judicial system of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], where the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]]’s sentence tends to be sovereign and immediate. It is, however, a caste protection: only a shadow lord, a high-ranking [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]], can invoke it.

### Nature

Once it is invoked, the Justiciar is required to examine the evidence before passing sentence. If the alleged threat is confirmed, the protocol requires immediate military mobilization from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. This recourse is rare because invoking it falsely constitutes high treason. It is the loophole through which a cornered lord imposes the burden of proof on the court, a principle that the invoker himself derives from the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]]: a judge may judge deeds, but the law protects even the weak from judgments without evidence, with the weak understood to mean the diminished lord and not the voiceless subject.

### Procedural Mechanics

The invocation is verbal and public, pronounced before the Justiciar conducting the proceedings. Its effect is binding and does not depend on the judge’s will: once the Veredictum is invoked, the sentence is suspended until the evidence has been examined in person. The Justiciar may determine that there is no threat and deny military mobilization, but cannot refuse to examine the evidence without violating the Lex Obscura itself. The asymmetry of risk explains the rarity of this recourse. For the cornered lord, it is the only way to delay an imminent sentence and force the judge to confront evidence that may acquit him; but the cost is absolute, for if the examination does not confirm the threat, the invocation proves false and the invoker incurs high treason. The instrument protects those who stake their lives on their own innocence and condemns those who bluff.

### Significance

The Veredictum encapsulates the paradox of legality within tyranny: the Empire that executes by summary sentence nevertheless preserves a clause that requires proof, but reserves it for its own ruling caste. The right to due process does not belong to the subject; it belongs to the shadow lord. The protection is real, and for that very reason dangerous: the same law that saves the invoker from a summary sentence may drag him beyond the ground where he felt safe.
