# Meditatio Aetheris

Meditatio Aetheris is the system of access to blue magic, established neither by contract nor by sacrifice, but by mental discipline. Through continuous training of consciousness, the practitioner reaches the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/siderais\|sidereals]], entities of pure intellect from the [[_wiki/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], and receives from them power proportional to the clarity of their meditation.

### Nature

Blue entities are beings of pure consciousness and intellect that inhabit the ethereal plane. They have no physical form: they are patterns of thought, living geometries, conscious concepts. Because they have neither ego nor ambition, they do not betray the practitioner as other entities do.

### Provision

The channeling of Meditatio Aetheris is called Provision, and it is neutral and impersonal. The sidereals have no plane of existence of their own and operate through the ethereal plane. Provision amplifies the key feelings of the granted schools in proportion to the practitioner’s mental attunement, not the entity’s will: the response depends on the clarity of the meditation, not the sidereal’s generosity.

### Types of Entities

Blue entities vary in affinity. Some are linked to a single school, such as the Ethereal Architects, linked to [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], and the Conscious Glaciers, linked to [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]]. Others combine blue with a second essence: Cosmic Observers, Mental Specters, Celestial Guardians, Prophets of the Flow, Collective Psyche, Thinking Currents, and Dreams of the Forest.

### Meditation Process

The bond begins with lengthy initial training, lasting years: the master teaches how to silence the mind, and the apprentice devotes themself to breathing, focus, emotional control, and the study of philosophy, mathematics, and universal patterns. The first connection requires many hours of deep meditation, in which consciousness passes through layers until it touches the Aether; the entity perceives the presence, assesses the mind’s clarity and discipline, and, if it approves, anchors itself in that mind. Mental communion follows, without words, in which the entity transmits patterns and truths, and the ethereal mark appears: silver eyes and an aura of clarity.

### Obligations and Costs

The practitioner must maintain continuous mental discipline, with daily meditation, and emotional control, since fury or extreme passion disturbs the entity. They are expected to seek knowledge and, depending on the sidereal, to preserve order against irrational chaos. The cost falls upon identity: the training is extremely long, and the practitioner tends toward social isolation as their personality becomes more abstract and distant. In return, power scales with dedication, the bond strengthens the intellect and grants access to ethereal knowledge, and the entity may share memories of entire eras.

### Breaking the Bond

The bond dissolves without resentment. Prolonged neglect causes the entity to disengage; severe trauma leads it to withdraw out of self-preservation; mental corruption drives it away. In all cases, the bond can be restored with months of retraining and meditation.
