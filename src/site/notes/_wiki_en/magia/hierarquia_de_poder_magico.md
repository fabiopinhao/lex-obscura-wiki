# Hierarchy of Magical Power

Magic is not distributed equally. The vast majority of people have no access to any power, and among those who do, the number of bonds forged with entities from other planes defines a narrow hierarchy, in which each tier is rarer and more dangerous than the previous one.

### Distribution in the Population

Taking the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] as an example, about eighty-five percent of the population has no access to magic whatsoever: they are ordinary people, soldiers, and bureaucrats, with entirely mundane power. Another twelve percent maintain a single pact, typically hierophants and [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]], with moderate specialization in one or two schools. Only two point five percent sustain two pacts, the tier of the high clergy and elite [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]], with mastery of two to four schools. Rarer still, at about zero point four percent, are practitioners of [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]], a category encompassing all [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] and arcane scholars, whose path grants broad access to many schools, with high versatility and medium depth.

### Pyramid of Power

The base of the pyramid is the population without magic. Above it come the specialized pact-bound with a single bond; then the practitioners of Teru, defined by versatility; and, higher still, the elite necromancers and hierophants who sustain two pacts with deep mastery. Each tier above corresponds to a smaller fraction of the population and a greater risk for those who occupy it.

### Multiple Pacts

Maintaining two or more pacts is exceptional. Each additional bond multiplies the dangers: conflict between obligations to different entities, progressive corruption, and gradual loss of humanity or sanity. The accumulation of power exacts a proportional price, and few sustain it for long.

### Natural Limits

Magical capacity runs up against natural limits. Each race accesses only one primary color, which establishes a theoretical ceiling of ten schools, one-third of the thirty that exist; in practice, very few exceed four. Every entity, in turn, has a dominant color, which determines the system through which it is accessed and the schools it can grant. A Shadar, aligned with devils, can enter into a pact with an entity with diabolical dominance, even if it combines more than one color, but not with entities of incompatible colors.
