# Consequences of the Discard

The discard is how the institution ends the functional existence of one of its members. Each method leaves a distinct residue, redistributes what the individual possessed, and teaches those who observe a lesson about their own fate.

### What Remains

Each form of discard produces its own trace. The Final Consecration leaves artifacts imbued with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] and relics. The Mission of Honor leaves a name on a memorial and a legend of heroism. The Transfer leaves nothing, as though the person had never existed. The Seclusion leaves rumors about “forbidden wings” and a diffuse institutional fear. The Eternal Vigil leaves the myth of “those who sleep”, a veiled threat.

### Inheritance

Nothing that belonged to the discarded person remains unclaimed for long. Equipment is redistributed without ceremony. Knowledge is extracted before the discard, when possible. The position is filled immediately, and no office remains vacant. Debts are assumed by the institution or collected from the family.

### The Lesson of Fear

The system also functions as a pedagogy of fear. Novices learn that “promotions” are not always good news. Veterans recognize the signs and know when someone is about to “be transferred”. Superiors use the mechanism as a veiled threat. Families ask no questions when a relative “is promoted to the Inner Circle”.

### Phantom Transferees

Some “transferees” reappear years later under other names, with no official record. This gives rise to administrative ghosts, people who “should not exist”, and the institutional paranoia that surrounds them. No one confirms it. No one denies it.
