# Vinculum Daemonis

Vinculum Daemonis is one of the systems of access to magic, a bond of force forged with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]] in exchange for raw power, and is proscribed by imperial doctrine. Where other systems depend on a contract or hierarchy, this one dispenses with bureaucracy: demons are pure chaos, violence and destruction without law, and respect no contracts, only force. Access is immediate, and the price is instability.

In the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]], the bond is forbidden, except with the personal blessing of the monarch, [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]: the Word of the Sovereign requires this blessing for the gods of chaos. The prohibition applies to [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] and [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]], and there are bonded [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] in the Empire’s service.

### Nomenclature

In common speech, mortals rarely distinguish Vinculum Daemonis from [[_wiki_en/magia/pactum_diaboli\|Pactum Diaboli]]. The term “pact” is used for both the contract forged with a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devil]] and the bond of force forged with a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demon]], in informal and imprecise usage. Strictly speaking, the pact is the devils’ legalistic instrument, with a clause, a signature, and a soul pledged by contract; the demonic bond has no contract or fixed term, only the exchange of blood and the demon’s hunger. The people sense the practical difference, the chaotic instability of one and the cold predictability of the other, without naming it precisely, and it falls to Inquisitors and jurists to maintain this distinction, because sentences depend on it.

### Channeling

The transfer of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] in Vinculum Daemonis is visceral and invasive. The demon pours out raw power without contractual mediation, in a process called Infusion, amplifying the schools’ key emotions with capricious intensity. Infusion can fluctuate according to the will of the demon, which amplifies for amusement or sabotages out of boredom. The bond is inherently unstable: the practitioner receives more power, but with less control over the dosage.

### The Bond

The bond is forged not through summoning, but through attraction. The invocation dispenses with a containment circle, which demons ignore, and requires an act of extreme violence as a portal, with the caster in a state of rage or despair; the demon is drawn by the chaos. A physical ordeal follows, in which the demon tests the caster through combat or torture: surviving forms the bond, succumbing surrenders the soul. The sealing takes place through an exchange of blood, and a chaotic mark appears on the body, a burn, an irregular scar, or sprouting horns. The bond is empathic, and the caster begins to feel the demon’s destructive hunger.

### Obligations and Costs

There are no fixed terms. The demon changes its demands on a whim, but they always revolve around destruction: burn something valuable, never go a month without committing violence, feed chaos with conflict and turmoil. The practitioner can grow in power through violence itself, which feeds the demon, but pays with progressive mental corruption, in the form of rage, paranoia, and sadism. The mark is grotesque and difficult to hide, and the bond attracts other demons, which see it as a challenge or prey.

### Rupture

There is no formal break. The demon simply attacks to kill or render the body unusable. The caster can try to dominate it, an extremely dangerous maneuver, and the bond breaks if the demon is banished. The mark, however, never disappears.
