# Ontological Progression

The practice of magic exacts a cost paid in the caster’s own identity, apart from [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]]. This ontological cost advances with time and use, gradually reshaping the character of whoever casts until the school’s mark becomes inseparable from the person.

### The Four Stages

The progression passes through four stages, measured in years of practice.

- **I** — Nature: Strange trait, a mild and still controllable tendency | Time: 1 to 5 years
- **II** — Nature: Behavioral pattern that requires effort to contain | Time: 5 to 15 years
- **III** — Nature: Characteristic that comes to define the identity | Time: 15 to 30 years
- **IV** — Nature: Complete and irreversible transformation | Time: 30 years or more

### Progress Through Use

The cost of a school progresses through use, not through merely possessing access. Those who master many schools but concentrate their practice on two or three progress quickly in those and hardly at all in the others. Those who distribute their use equally advance slowly in all of them. Versatility merely divides the cost.

### Access Systems

Each path of access to power shapes this progression. [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]] grants broad access to many schools with less depth, and its costs are diluted because use is distributed, which produces mixed tendencies in the most frequently used schools, without complete transformation. Pacts concentrate power in a few schools and intensify the cost, since they add the amplification of the feeling the entity nurtures to the concentration of use, so that the transformation comes faster and goes deeper. The same practitioner can operate under more than one system at a time, and each bond adds its own cost. In these cases, ontological costs add up according to the use of each school, without the combination of systems producing any multiplication in itself. What accumulates in full is the cost specific to each path, with its obligations, marks, and demands. The tension between entities competing for the same practitioner manifests in conflicting demands and divine jealousy, without additional harm.

### Mutually Reinforcing Combinations

Some combinations of schools produce costs that add up dangerously. [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] lead to complete emotional death, the inability to feel. [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] generate the terror that nothing may be real. [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] erode the capacity for genuine interaction. [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] create the compulsion to drain and heal without end.

### Neighboring Schools

Different schools can produce costs that feel similar internally. The cold of Necro radiates from the inside out, while that of Cryo penetrates from the outside in. [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] brings detachment through feeling too much, in active melancholy; Cryo, through feeling too little, in numbness. And the questioning of reality shifts focus according to the school: Spectro asks whether that is real, Psycho whether the thought is one’s own, [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] whether one is actually awake.
