# Final Principle

The Final Principle is the maxim that governs the economy of disposal in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]: the Empire does not waste, does not punish, optimizes. Each method of elimination extracts some residual value, and no one is simply removed. Everyone is converted into a resource.

A practitioner’s [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] can serve artifacts, their death propaganda, their silence security, their isolation consultation. The logic rejects pure loss: where there would be an end, the Empire sees a use.
