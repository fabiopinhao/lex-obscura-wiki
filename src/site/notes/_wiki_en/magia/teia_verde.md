# Greenweave

The greenweave is an ancient druidic magic that bends light and sound, creating a pocket of the world set apart within the forest. Under its effect, the dense woods ripple and open for those allowed passage: stones covered in centuries-old moss hide hewn walls, intertwined roots serve as a framework, and whatever is protected vanishes for those who should not find it.

### Function

It serves as a veil for druidic refuges. The concealment is an alteration of the stretch of forest itself, and therefore does not come undone when someone stops looking. No observer needs to be deceived for it to work.

### Sensitivity

The greenweave responds to disturbances in the natural balance, and responds at a distance: dark corruption, however slight, spreads through it like a wave in a calm lake until it touches distant shores. A bearer of a fragment of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|the Void]] degrades the weave by mere presence, without intent or action. The signs of degradation are visible in the environment: lichens that turn from moss green to gray green, luminous mushrooms that flicker and dwindle like smothered candles.

### Cost

Since it is the greenweave that hides the refuge, each visit by a bearer of corruption exacts part of the hiding place. Protection and threat run along the same thread.
