# Alignment Detection

Alignment detection is the specialized magic that reveals the spiritual mark left by a pact. This mark is invisible to the naked eye, but dedicated arcane readings can expose the existence of an active bond, the predominant emotional color of an Ardorado, and, in some cases, the nature of the patron god.

### Applications

The ability is strategic, and those who master it are highly valued. Hierophants and inquisitors trained in it identify traitors, spies, and false devotees. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] maintains detectors at key posts, and Ardorados are among the easiest targets: their color is visible to those who know how to look.

### Imperial Screening

The same ability underpins talent screening in [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]. Since the awakening of [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]] is innate and unpredictable, detecting it is the only way to find it, and arcane academies recruit and hone those who reveal the gift. Detection also classifies [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terminologia_essencial\|channelers]], the imperial term for druids, into three grades according to the power detected; the grade measures the intensity of the arcane signature, not the moral worth or political danger of its bearer.

### Limitations

The reading has limits. It reveals the existence of a pact, not the content of its clauses. Pacts made with dead or imprisoned gods may produce ambiguous readings, and certain artifacts or rituals mask the mark for some time.
