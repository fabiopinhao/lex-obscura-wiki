# Symbiosis Naturae

Symbiosis Naturae is the magic access system of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]], and is neither a pact nor a contract, but a symbiotic relationship with them and other nature spirits, founded on balance. The practitioner receives power like sap shared between root and crown, in exchange for fidelity to nature’s cycles.

### Nature

Symbiosis is established through balance rather than agreement: it is a symbiotic relationship with natural spirits and forces. Since the titans do not demand submission, they do not betray; they merely withdraw when balance is violated.

### Influx

The channeling of Symbiosis Naturae is called Influx and is organic and reciprocal. The titan shares power like sap between root and crown, in a rhythm that follows natural cycles. Influx amplifies the key emotions of the granted schools in a gradual and tolerant manner: the titans rarely interfere with the response, and the channel operates through natural resonance, tolerating minor failures.

### Living Sparks

Green-gold sparks appear when the titans’ magic, druidic or divine, touches the material plane. They condense from the air itself, shine in the sun, and orbit the source of power.

### Symbiosis Process

Symbiosis begins with a ritual of harmony, performed at a natural sacred site, such as an ancient grove, a waterfall, or a cave, in which the caster fasts, meditates, and offers nonviolent gifts, such as seeds, honey, or songs. The spirit assesses whether he respects balance. Communion follows, without a verbal agreement: the spirit touches the caster, roots grow along his body or water flows in his veins, and the natural mark appears, leaves on the skin and eyes that reflect the forest. The connection is maintained through periodic returns to the sacred site and grows stronger with time and respect, allowing communication through dreams and visions.

### Obligations and Costs

The practitioner must protect nature, never pervert life with magic, offer regular gifts, and respect natural laws. The cost falls on identity, and the power has limits of its own: it weakens far from nature, requires constant maintenance, and is vulnerable to desecration of the sacred site; cities and civilization drain the caster’s strength. In exchange, symbiosis heals slowly, grows with dedication, and connects the practitioner to the natural network, allowing them to sense forests and speak with animals.

### Imperial Hostility

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]’s stance toward practitioners of Symbiosis is structurally hostile, but this hostility varies over time. During the [[_wiki/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas_a_sombra_desce\|Reinkardian Wars]], persecution was strict and absolute: druids and shamans were treated as a strategic threat to imperial expansion into the sacred forests, and summary execution was the rule. The current period of peace has progressively eroded this stance, through an economy of attention: with the frontier stabilized, pursuing rural practitioners yields little for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], and the doctrine remains intact, merely neglected. Tolerance grows where the practice does not confront imperial expansion; in rural or frontier settings, a druid moves about discreetly, but in [[_wiki/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and the provincial capitals the practice remains clandestine or negotiated. [[_wiki/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] remains a point of acute tension.

### Breaking Symbiosis

Violating the natural balance causes the spirit to withdraw in sorrow, and the caster plunges into a deep [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|void]]. Symbiosis can be rebuilt with effort and genuine repentance.
