# Antagonistic Pairs

The thirty known schools of magic are organized into fifteen antagonistic pairs, distributed across six categories of opposition. Each pair brings together two schools with opposing domains, which interfere with one another when they meet.

### Opposition and Interference

Antagonistic pairs indicate thematic opposition and preferential interference, not absolute nullification. In direct confrontation, there is mutual resistance, increased cost, or partial dissipation, never automatic cancellation; the environment, preparation, and the caster’s mastery influence the outcome.

### Elemental Oppositions

Four pairs pit elemental forces against one another. [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], of fire and combustion, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]], of water and currents: fire devours, water extinguishes and adapts. [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], of ice and glaciation, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]], of energy and fusion: ice paralyzes, plasma transforms. [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]], of wind and storms, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]], of earth and stone: wind displaces, earth anchors. [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]], of darkness and obscurity, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]], of light and radiance: darkness conceals, light exposes.

### Vital Oppositions

Two pairs pit life against death. [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]], of death and reanimation, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]], of life and growth: death ends, life multiplies. [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]], of decay and putrefaction, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]], of healing and restoration: disease corrupts, healing rebuilds.

### Mental Oppositions

Two pairs pit states of mind against one another. [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]], of sleep and hypnosis, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]], of the mind and the psyche: hypnosis suppresses consciousness, the psyche invades it. [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], of fear and terror, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], of knowledge and premonition: fear obscures, knowledge illuminates.

### Oppositions of Order and Chaos

Three pairs pit order against chaos. [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], of curses and corruption, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], of grace and holiness: the curse corrupts, the blessing sanctifies. [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]], of destruction and rupture, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], of binding and restraint: destruction frees, restraint imprisons. [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], of war and combat, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]], of protection and barriers: war attacks, protection resists.

### Spatial and Temporal Oppositions

Two pairs pit matter against spirit, acceleration against delay. [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]], of space and gravity, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]], of transcendence and elevation: gravity binds the body, transcendence frees the soul. [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]], of electricity and magnetism, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]], of time and flow: electricity propels, time slows.

### Oppositions of Substance

Two pairs pit appearance against essence. [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], of ghosts and illusion, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]], of metamorphosis and transformation: illusion deceives, metamorphosis alters. [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]], of poison and acid, opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]], of negation and dissipation: poison degrades, nullification protects.

### Summary

In all, the six categories total fifteen pairs and all thirty schools: four elemental pairs, two vital, two mental, three of order and chaos, two spatial and temporal, and two of substance.
