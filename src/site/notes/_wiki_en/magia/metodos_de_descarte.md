# Disposal Methods

Disposal Methods is the name of the set of procedures by which the imperial apparatus rids itself of practitioners who have become inconvenient, dangerous, or disposable. Each method carries a euphemistic official term that disguises the reality and a bureaucratic record that closes the case without leaving a trace of violence.

### Categories

Functional Isolation, recorded as “Seclusion”, is the permanent confinement of a practitioner who still retains residual usefulness. Lethal Redirection, the “Mission of Honor”, is a suicidal task disguised as a privilege. Ritual Neutralization, or “Final Consecration”, is a ceremonial execution involving the extraction of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]]. Administrative Disappearance, the “Transfer”, is silent elimination without a record. Perpetual Containment, called the “Eternal Watch”, suspends the practitioner between life and death.

### Protocols

The Final Consecration summons the practitioner to the temple under a ceremonial pretext, imposes a ritual fast of three days while he believes he is being elevated, drains his magi through specialized necromancers, and ends in death by exsanguination, with the blood used in imperial artifacts. The official record speaks of “spiritual ascension” or “martyrdom in service”.

The Mission of Honor selects the practitioner for a prestigious mission, provides an incomplete briefing with an apparently achievable objective, and sends him against disproportionate resistance or into a trap. The body is rarely recovered, and the name goes on a memorial as “fallen in service with honor”.

The Transfer notifies the practitioner of a relocation and loses him during transit, under escort. The body is never found, and the investigation remains inconclusive.

Seclusion diagnoses the practitioner as needing care and admits him to a sealed wing of a tower or temple, where he is kept alive and occasionally consulted, with visits prohibited.

The Eternal Watch magically sedates the practitioner, preserves the body between life and death through necromancy, and stores him in an underground chamber, awakened only for specific consultations. Each awakening exacts its price: what wakes is not always what fell asleep.
