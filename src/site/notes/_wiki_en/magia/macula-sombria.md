# Shadow-Taint

The shadow-taint, also called the Shadow Mark or Shadow Rot, is the arcane decay that precedes and accompanies [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] imperial expansion into territories protected by green magic. Black curses cast over zones of resistance infiltrate the roots long before the Shadars appear at the borders, causing magic to age like a tree without water, drying from the inside out. Druids identify it by pulsing black veins in infected roots, accompanied by a smell of sulfur and a fermented sweetness.

### Nature

The corruption does not destroy the forest directly. It degrades the connection between the land and the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]], suffocating the capacity for druidic channeling. Basic enchantments fail; rituals that once required only one channeler to perform now require two, and even then yield stunted results.

### Territorial Scale

All the titan forests of [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] suffer from the taint to varying degrees, and [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], the closest to the Shadar plateau, is the most affected. The process has accelerated since [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] consolidated his dominion over Reinkard. The imperial strategy is patient: to let the growing corruption force the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|titans]] to abandon the sanctuaries, exposing the forests. Druids observe that the taint strengthens over time instead of weakening, making it a weapon for slow sieges, not for battle.

### Individual Scale

The taint can also be planted directly in living beings as an instrument of coercion: the victim is prevented from raising a hand against whoever planted it. Druids classify it among compound and cruel curses, combining curse, control, and slow poison, without ruling out something they do not yet know. The individual mark is described as a fragment of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|the Void]] lodged within oneself.

### Arcane Classification

The shadow-taint is not explicitly classified as a spell belonging to a school in the grimoire. Its accursed nature, composed of corruption, degradation, and tracking, aligns it with [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], the school of Curse and Corruption, whose primary color is black. Spells such as Mark, Hex, and Scourge share traits with the taint: persistent tracking, continuous degradation, and bidirectional cost. The formal classification remains open.

### Mysteries

Why the Shadars spare some forests is a question with no firm answer, wavering between cowardice and some agreement in distant spheres. The relationship between the territorial taint and the titans’ ancient pacts with [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]] remains an active mystery.
