# Glossary of Magical Terms

A collection of the basic terms that organize the study and practice of magic. Each entry defines a recurring concept, from arcane fuel to forms of access to power.

**[[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|Magi]]:** universal magical energy, the invisible fuel of all spellcasting. It is a limited reserve that must be replenished after use.

**Mark:** physical or spiritual sign of a bond with an entity. It varies according to the type of system.

**Pact:** agreement between caster and entity, formal in the case of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] and informal in the case of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]].

**Symbiosis:** mutual relationship with a natural spirit, based on balance and respect.

**Devotion:** genuine faith in a deity. Power arises from purity of intention and a moral code.

**Meditation:** mental discipline for touching the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] and connecting with entities of pure consciousness.

**[[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]]:** technical system of arcane study that requires no entities. It has high versatility and moderate depth.

**School:** magical specialization. There are thirty schools in total, ten per [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/cor_arcana\|arcane color]].

**Dominant Color:** primary aspect of a two-colored entity, which defines its access system.

**Overload:** attempt to cast with the magi reserve depleted. The result is severe damage or death.
