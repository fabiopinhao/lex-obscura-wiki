# Devotio Sancta

Devotio Sancta is the system of magical access anchored in religious faith: power comes from genuine devotion to entities of light, order, and purity, granted by the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/anjos\|angels]] as a blessing. With high stability and medium difficulty, it is doctrinally tolerated under certain conditions in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]].

In common usage, the system is called simply Sancta, “the magic that rises through faith”; Devotio Sancta is the formal name. Sancta reverence, a rite of the hands among the free peoples, is a distinct practice.

### Channeling

The transfer of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] in Devotio Sancta is called the Gift, and it is sacred and conditional. The angel grants power in proportion to the devotee’s faith and amplifies the key feelings of the schools granted as long as devotion remains pure. Doubt narrows the channel; transgression closes it. The angel’s response varies according to the devotee’s spiritual state, through resonance with faith and not through whim.

### Process of Devotion

The path begins with priestly initiation: years of doctrinal study, ordination in a temple by a higher-ranking cleric, public vows that vary by deity (chastity, poverty, obedience), and anointing with sacred oil or divine light. Consecration follows, a period of trial through fasting, prayer, and tests of faith in which the deity judges purity of intent; if the devotee is approved, a divine mark may appear (a faint halo, luminous eyes, a white aura), confirmed by a first small miracle. Maintaining faith requires daily prayers, rituals in a temple, and service to the deity’s purposes.

### Obligations and Costs

The cost falls on identity. The devotee observes a strict moral code (not lying, not killing innocents, protecting the weak), performs mandatory rituals such as masses, prayers, and confessions, and maintains personal purity according to the vows. In return, the devotee can count on reliable power as long as faith remains, a supportive community in the temple or order, protection against demonic and diabolical corruption, and the possibility of ascending the ecclesiastical hierarchy. The weaknesses are symmetrical: the code is inflexible and transgression immediately costs the devotee their power, doubt weakens magic, and miracles may fail in moments of hesitation.

### Loss of Devotion

Transgressing the code causes the deity to withdraw the blessing: the divine mark darkens or disappears and the former devotee experiences absolute abandonment, a spiritual emptiness. Restoration is possible through sincere penance, which varies according to the severity of the offense, but may not come when the sin is grave.
