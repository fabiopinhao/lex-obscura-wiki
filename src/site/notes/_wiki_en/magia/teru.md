# Teru

Teru is a non-theocratic system of access to magic: it reaches the arcane schools through communication with the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/planos_divinos\|divine planes]], without requiring submission, a pact, or devotion to any entity. It is the path of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], learned at arcane academies over decades of study.

### Principle

In Teru, [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] comes from within the practitioner, accompanied by a feeling associated with the color and school the practitioner wishes to invoke. Without the correct feeling, even the right invocation in the right language produces nothing. Communication with the divine planes takes place without a bond: the plane responds automatically, without choice and possibly without consciousness. Access encompasses the ten schools of the practitioner’s [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/cor_arcana\|racial color]], making Teru a path of high versatility and moderate depth, that of the competent generalist rather than the specialist.

### Awakening and Refinement

Access to Teru has two stages. The first is awakening, an innate and nontransferable gift that emerges in moments of profound emotion and cannot be taught, predicted, or forced. The second is refinement, the decades of academic study that turn raw awakening into disciplined access to the schools of the color. The imperial academies do not create awakened practitioners: they detect, recruit, and refine those who have already awakened, which explains the rarity of the path. The Empire teaches the technique while its official doctrine, [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/teoria_materialista\|the Materialist Theory]], denies the source on which it depends.

### Practices

Learning begins with enrollment in an arcane academy or university, with years of study of the theory of magical patterns, arcane geometry, and languages of power, supervised practice of basic spells, and rigorous proficiency exams. At the level of mastery attained, the practitioner masters multiple schools of their own color, but power in each remains moderate, geared toward tactical versatility rather than raw power. It is a long and expensive path that demands exceptional intellect; its refinement can be taught and institutionalized, even though the awakening that precedes it cannot, and it carries none of the social stigma of pacts.

### Costs

Teru leaves no physical mark and imposes no obligations to any entity. Its ontological costs are diffused through use distributed among the various schools: there is no automatic mitigation, only distribution (see [[_wiki_en/magia/progressao_ontologica\|Ontological Progression]]). The trade-off is the ceiling on power. Whereas a necromancer bound by a pact through [[_wiki_en/magia/pactum_diaboli\|Pactum Diaboli]] accesses few schools but achieves mastery, capable of raising hundreds of the dead, a Justiciar with Teru reaches all ten schools of their color at a merely competent depth. Teru allows the practitioner to do a little of everything; a pact allows mastery of very little. For the mechanics of magi reserves, recovery, and overload, see the [[_wiki_en/magia/sistema_de_combustivel_magico\|Magic Fuel System]].
