# Imperial Discard Policy

The Imperial Discard Policy is the doctrine by which the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] decides whether to retain or eliminate its practitioners of magic. Its fundamental principle is that the Empire does not discard for corruption, but for loss of functional predictability: what matters is not how far someone has lost themselves in their own ontological cost, but whether they still answer to the chain of command.

### Thresholds

The policy assigns each practitioner to one of four bands. At the functional threshold, the cost increases efficiency without breaking obedience, and the practitioner is retained or promoted. In containment, the cost is useful but brings collateral risk, requiring supervision, partial isolation, or narrowly defined missions. In the discard band, the cost breaks predictability, loyalty, or control, and the response ranges from permanent isolation to elimination. There is also a forbidden band, in which certain advanced stages are treated as an irreversible systemic risk and subjected to preventive discard even before they manifest.

### Risk by School

The Empire assesses each school of magic through the compatibility between its ontological cost and the intended function. Schools such as [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]], and [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] are high risk, subject to frequent discard because their costs tend to sabotage legitimate violence, reject commitments, expose operations, or lead to the abandonment of duties. Between the two extremes lies a medium-risk band, in which discard tends to occur only at the final stage and allows exceptions: [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], and [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]]. Others, such as [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]], and [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]], are low risk, with advanced stages considered functional or even desirable. The organizing principle is deliberate: the cost desirable for one function is usually prohibitive for another, and this is personnel engineering, not chance.

### Administration

The authority to decide on discards is distributed across levels. Tower preceptors bring in practitioners under their jurisdiction at the tactical level; the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]] matriarchs handle transfers and missions at the operational level; the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] is responsible for strategic decisions; and [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], through his councilors, intervenes in cases of high political profile. The process follows a fixed course: detection of signs by an immediate superior, assessment by report, a decision by the competent authority, execution by a specialized unit, and official recording. No practitioner knows the complete architecture; each level sees only its own part of the system.
