# Circle of Suture

The Circle of Suture is a necromantic heptagram employed in controlling the undead. Inscribed beneath the slabs of a floor, it lights up and breathes when fed, as though the space were gaining a second heart, and keeps the dead under the command of whoever operates it.

### Activation

Activation occurs by releasing amber-colored ossuary dust over the channels inscribed in the floor, marking a triple rhythm with crystal-rings. The dust snakes like low-lying mist, is absorbed by the channels, and closes the heptagram. Once the ritual formula, in which bone calls to bone, is pronounced, the dead assemble themselves: the joints find their sockets, and each is sealed by a resinous film that glimmers amber.

### Continuous Suture

More than animating, the Circle stitches anew. Each bone is sealed by amber filaments, so that blows capable of severing limbs are then undone, with the halves seeking each other again. The necromancer conducts the beat with the crystal-rings, and the ring on the floor pulses in faithful cadence: while the conductor maintains the rhythm, the seal repairs itself, and even displaced dust returns to its place.

### Vulnerability

Fire is the counterpoint to the Circle. The flame makes the resin boil and takes the place of the fastening, opening gaps before the sutures close again. Complete rupture comes from breaking the heptagram itself: when fire devours the connection that joined vertebra to vertebra, the lines fracture, the amber cools to the color of cold bone, the sutures give way, and the dead collapse. Conducting also depends on the operator’s composure: caught by surprise, he lets the crystal-rings lose the beat, and under fire the heptagram falls out of step before collapsing.

### Origin and Appearances

The ritual was performed by the necromancer [[_wiki_en/personagens/qaelun\|Qaelun]] at the abandoned [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_vozrador\|temple of Vozrador]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. On that occasion, [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum]], deprived of magic by the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/luto_de_nyssis\|Mourning of Nyssis]], defeated the Circle with impact and fire, pushing a burning coffin into the center of the heptagram until the structure was extinguished.

### Logic and Gaps

The name combines the ritual geometry, the circle, with the function of Suture, from the school of [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]], subverting the logic of healing: where Suture closes wounds in the living, the Circle stitches the necromancer’s will to dead flesh, sealing obedience where there should be decomposition. Its formal school classification, the limit on the number of dead a single Circle controls, and the ritual’s historical origin are unknown.
