# Pactum Diaboli

Pactum Diaboli is the system of access to black magic established through a contract with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]], legalistic entities of the Nine Hells. It is a stable and predictable path to power, paid for with rigid obligations and, in the end, with one’s own soul.

### Nature

Devils are evil legalists who revere binding contracts, hierarchy, and legal precision. The pact they offer exists simultaneously on the material plane and in the Nine Hells, and the infernal hierarchy guarantees its fulfillment, even offering protection to the person bound by the pact.

### Bestowal

The channeling of Pactum Diaboli is called Bestowal and is formal and contractual. The devil grants power according to the terms of the pact, in a predictable, stable flow proportional to obedience. Bestowal amplifies the key feelings of the schools granted, deepening the emotional exposure of the person bound by the pact; the devil does not replace the feeling, but intensifies it, sustains it, and makes it harder to turn off.

### Invocation Process

The invocation begins with a summoning ritual, with a containment circle, runes, and an initial offering, and requires the utterance of the devil’s true name under the observation of lesser diabolical witnesses. Negotiation follows: the devil presents a formal contract, with clauses in an ancient language, and negotiations can last hours or days, since every word matters and devils exploit loopholes and ambiguities. The sealing takes place through the conjurer’s signature, and the contract takes effect simultaneously on the material plane and in extraplanar regions. The obligation lasts for as long as the devil’s reach extends to the material plane: once that influence ceases, most clauses dissolve, and a minority persist, continuing to exact the price in his wake. This is why the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] maxim that the clauses die with the god works as a guess and fails as a rule.

### Obligations and Costs

The obligations are negotiated in advance and inflexible: specific services, regular sacrifices, strict prohibitions, or a continuous tribute of life force. Failure brings immediate punishment. Devils honor the literal letter of the pact, and it is through technicalities and loopholes that they betray. The pact mark, an infernal rune burned into the skin, is detectable by paladins and hierophants. The cost falls on the soul and on third parties: the soul of the person bound by the pact is pledged as collateral and, after death, belongs to the devil. In return, the power is stable, the devil fulfills his part and offers protection against other entities.

### Pact Breach

The rupture is severe and rarely recoverable. The devil can revoke the powers instantly, and the mark corrupts the body of the person bound by the pact. Diabolical hunters, others bound by pacts in the service of the hierarchy, are sent to capture or punish the defaulter, and the soul can be claimed even before death.
