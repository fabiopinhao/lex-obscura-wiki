# Zarae

Zarae is a [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] apprentice of the ophideia order in [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], trained in the Chambers of Trial under the matriarch [[_wiki_en/personagens/belarbreza_vhaskrith\|Belarbreza Vhaskrith]]. A companion of [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith Morghast]] since childhood, she dies young in a training duel.

### Origin

She grew up in the Chambers of Trial of Xarn’Zareth, where the ophideia order trains its apprentices. Her bond with Syndrith Morghast dated back to their earliest memory: they slept side by side and shared everything, described as two scorpions under the same rock. Mastery of the trial environment gave Zarae absolute confidence, and she tolerated no affront, least of all from someone from the surface.

### Death

Zarae dies in a duel against [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] then training in the order. The confrontation arises from an act of cruelty by Zarae against the young Drul [[_wiki_en/personagens/vhraxil\|Vhraxil]], an act Nidzina calls cowardice. The circle of apprentices closes, and Belarbreza Vhaskrith watches from above. The duel ends in seconds: Nidzina spins, parries, and pierces Zarae’s torso, below the ribs, where the leather did not cover her. The matriarch descends, examines the body, and validates it as legitimate combat.

### Legacy

Zarae’s death marks those who outlive her. It is the first death Nidzina causes, and Zarae’s face returns as an involuntary memory in battles years later, imprinted even as other names fade. For Syndrith Morghast, the loss is a rupture: her only emotional bond from childhood is gone, and a decades-long obsession directed against Nidzina is born.
