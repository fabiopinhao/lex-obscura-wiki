# Auren

Auren is an [[_wiki_en/racas/aquilar\|Aquilar]] [[_wiki_en/racas/aviano\|Avian]] with white wings, a soldier in the Temple Guard of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] and an aspiring paladin. He wears winged armor of overlapping plates that accommodate the movement of his wings, with sacred engravings on the shoulders and joints of elven workmanship.

In his youth, he played [[_wiki_en/conceitos/aroada\|Aroada]] in the fields of Myrgarde. He was competitive, generous, and theatrical, a “peacock.” As Avian tradition dictates, he honors those who pass on young by placing one of his most beautiful feathers on their grave.
