# Orvhal

Orvhal is the provincial lord of [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], the old Crystal Fortress, conquered in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]] and exploited by [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]] as a source of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]]. He has no seat at the table of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]]: he is among the remote participants, who address the hall as the agenda requires.

### At the Shadow Council

During one of the sessions, Orvhal’s figure appears in the pitch pit of the [[_wiki_en/locais/salao_do_conselho\|Council Hall]], and the shadows behind him move, alive. His report opens the discussion of Elenir: the city’s magical presence is unstable, two alchemical magi laboratories have exploded, the earth rejects the excavations, and the ingredients rot in the hands of those who gather them, that same day, for no apparent reason.

The account divides the table. [[_wiki_en/personagens/kazem_vorgrath\|Kazem Vorgrath]] orders the city razed; [[_wiki_en/personagens/ulrik_morzenn\|Ulrik Morzenn]] defends Elenir as the largest raw source of reagents on the continent and the key to freeing the Empire from dependence on divine will; [[_wiki_en/personagens/suriev_molkhiir\|Suriev Molkhiir]] replies that pacts do not fail. The impasse gives rise to [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]]’s mission, as he asks to see with his own eyes what is happening in Elenir, and to [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]]’s warning that the city’s rebellious magic is not natural.
