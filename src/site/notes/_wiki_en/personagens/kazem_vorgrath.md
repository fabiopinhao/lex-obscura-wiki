# Kazem Vorgrath

Kazem Vorgrath, called the Fire Command, is a councilor on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], affiliated with the military wing. Before holding this office, he was a general, and the epithet comes from his scorched-earth tactics using demonic flames, fueled by red pyromantic magic.

### Career

Vorgrath was promoted to councilor after [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] banned red pyromantic invocations. The maneuver elevated his status and, at the same time, stripped him of his destructive capability in the field. Once a general who solved problems with fire, he moved into the Council’s bureaucracy.

### Role on the Council

At the Shadow Council session, Vorgrath advocates the destruction of [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]] with blunt brutality, proposing to consecrate the city with smoke. His military mindset contrasts with the caution of the other councilors.
