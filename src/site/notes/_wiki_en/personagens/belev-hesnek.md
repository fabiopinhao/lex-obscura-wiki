# Belev-Hesnek

Belev-Hesnek is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] and [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] with jurisdiction over the northern arc, in the icy strait between the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], through which the goblinoid tribes descend onto the plateau before winter.

### Jurisdiction

Containing the migration is the military’s responsibility. Belev-Hesnek’s purview is what orc pressure produces within the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Cult]]: garrison officers who sell passage to the tribes, supplies diverted from the perimeter fortresses, and falsified figures in the reports that go up to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]]. He investigates, makes a record on a [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tabua_reversa\|Reverse Tablet]], and sentences those who answer to the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]], never the migrant. It is the order’s most isolated jurisdiction and its least glorious: crime increases with the thaw, and a judgment rarely provides a public example.

### Name

The name is spelled with a hyphen, like those of [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/draven-thol\|Draven-Thol]].
