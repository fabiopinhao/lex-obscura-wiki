# Kaedhras Morvil

Kaedhras Morvil is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]], a lieutenant in the imperial army, captured by the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]] for violating the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]]. He identifies himself with his military arrogance intact, stating his rank before offering any defense, and justifies his actions as service to the monarch’s will.

### The Crime

He used his military rank to traverse restricted routes in the Lateral Tower with runic keys and plundered living gems, the eggs of [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídars]], from the hall of [[_wiki_en/personagens/quavarra_ulvondra\|Quavarra Ulvondra]] and those of other matrons. Under interrogation, he claims that the reagents would be “for the Cult” and provokes those judging him, insulting the jurisdiction holding him.

### Judgment

Under the laws of [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]], the Druls cannot directly punish Shadar military personnel, and so Kaedhras is handed over to Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]]. It was Quavarra Ulvondra who handed him over, calling for the Justiciar and not another lord. The sentence is sealed with the sword: “There is no doctrine that protects the traitor.”

### The Scar

Kaedhras bears a magical scar imprinted over his speech nerve. During the interrogation, something snaps in his throat.
