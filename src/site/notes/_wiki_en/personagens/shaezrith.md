# Shaezrith

Shaezrith, a [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] ophideia who died in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], is [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]]’s mission companion.

### Appearance

She wore the veil of a hierophant and the order’s purple tunic. The Breath of Lúrio marked her body: pallor, a tongue stained with lúrio, subtle petechiae in the whites of her eyes.

### Origin and Role

She belonged to the Ophideias and served alongside Syndrith on the mission to the surface. Shortly before dying, she underwent the Breath of Lúrio, a rite that progressively weakens whoever endures it. She was found dead in the corridors of Ghilanna, her body serene and without any sign of a struggle, her veil torn at the corner.

She believed that the burden of the mission to the surface was hers alone, and told Syndrith so: she should go alone. But she bowed to fate with fatalistic faith, convinced that [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]] chooses correctly.

### Method and Voice

Doubt voiced quietly was her defining trait. Between her and Syndrith, it was Shaezrith who voiced the distrust: the matrons murmured that [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] was behaving erratically, and she repeated the murmur aloud. Among the ophideias, she upheld the formula that they are the invisible hand in the world.

### Relationships

Syndrith knows the limits of the Breath of Lúrio, and Shaezrith’s death remains a point of reference for the risk the rite poses to any ophideia. The two used to debate [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]]’s reputation together. To Syndrith, Shaezrith is the companion Ghilanna cost her.
