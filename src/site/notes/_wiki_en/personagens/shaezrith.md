# Shaezrith

Shaezrith is a [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] ophideia, [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]]’s mission companion. She dies in the corridors of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], weakened by the Breath of Lúrio.

### Appearance

She wears the veil of a hierophant and the order’s purple tunic. The Breath of Lúrio marks her body: pallor, a tongue stained with lúrio, subtle petechiae in the whites of her eyes.

### Origin and Role

She belongs to the ophideias and serves alongside Syndrith on the mission to the surface. Shortly before dying, she undergoes the Breath of Lúrio, a rite that progressively weakens whoever endures it. She is found dead in the corridors of Ghilanna, her body serene and without any sign of a struggle, her veil torn at the corner.

She believes that the burden of the mission to the surface is hers alone, and tells Syndrith so: she should go alone, the weight was hers, not her companion’s. But she bows to fate with fatalistic faith, convinced that [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]] chooses correctly.

### Method and Voice

Doubt voiced quietly is her defining trait. She, and not Syndrith, is the one who voices the distrust: the matrons murmur that [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] is behaving erratically, and she is the one who repeats the murmur aloud. Among the ophideias, she upholds the formula that they are the invisible hand in the world.

### Relationships

Syndrith’s mission companion, whose companion knows the limits of the Breath of Lúrio. Shaezrith’s death remains a point of reference for the risk the rite poses to any ophideia. The two used to debate [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]]’s reputation together. To Syndrith, Shaezrith is the companion Ghilanna cost her.
