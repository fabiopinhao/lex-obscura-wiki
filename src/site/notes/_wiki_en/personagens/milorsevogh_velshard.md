# Milorsevogh Velshard

Milorsevogh Velshard is a newly anointed [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] and a veteran of the Reinkardic Wars, where he served in the campaigns of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]]. A [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] with keen analytical perception, he finds connections where others see only noise: patterns in delirium, confessions in silences, meaning in fragments of minds eroded by necromancy or torture. His interrogation methods are calculating, and his reputation for reading what destroyed minds hold precedes him.

### Appearance

A Shadar with an upright bearing and an imposing presence that requires no overt violence. He appears to be about forty years old, although Shadar longevity conceals more age than it shows. A broad jaw, a jutting chin, and a predatory gaze beneath lowered eyelids: his face is imposing through its structure, not its softness. His eyes are metallic silver, cold as a freshly polished blade, which reflects light without emitting it. Dark hair. He wears the black [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tegmen\|Tegmen]] of high-status Shadars, with inlaid obsidian and runes, over a long cloak draped with ritual elements. He carries the Justiciar’s [[_wiki_en/artefatos/spina_lexis\|Spina Lexis]] scepter at his belt. Beneath the skin of his wrist sleeps a dark mark of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]]; his Shadar veins darken only when he uses magic.

### The Method

Milor interrogates to understand: where another Justiciar would apply pressure, he observes, cross-references fragments, and waits for the subject’s mind to betray itself. His voice is surgical and economical, more inclined toward questions than statements, and he rarely says more than his presence already dictates. He reads space as a metaphor for power and prefers the precise term to the image. There is an unsettling wrinkle to him: he protects the vulnerable who cross his path and, in the same gesture, turns them into instruments, saving and using without either canceling out the other.

### Magic

He specializes in Anatho, the school of curses, his signature, and also casts [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] and [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]]. He masters [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]], a system of magic independent of divine pacts developed by [[_wiki_en/personagens/naiu_wula\|Naiu’Wula]]. He is currently unpacted, and mastery of Teru without a pact is his hallmark. He carries a subcutaneous shadow of magic forbidden in [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]] on his wrist, a mark of Anatho that sleeps for now. He also has circular scars on his palms, which [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] reads as a sign of excessive use of power. In [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], he swears an oath to [[_wiki_en/panteao/morlgrund\|Morlgrund]] that does not constitute a divine pact.

Vampiric touch is the contact-draining technique in his repertoire, a combination of Necro and [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], and has two modes. The cold that drains sends the victim into a coma and transfers fragments of the victim’s memory to the caster, where they cling like psychic parasites: after a few days, Milor can no longer distinguish his own memories from those he stole. The cold that seeks induces a nightmare whose content arises from the victim’s fears, a death that did not happen, fabricated from fear; from it the caster harvests the scream and a thread of vitality.

### Trajectory

In the Elenir campaign, logistics fell to him: he kept alive the supply route that sustained the pace of the siege engines and ordered the battering ram pulled back when appropriate. [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], who was negotiating on the other side, regards this work as courageous. He emerged from his demotion in Khargoth without a blade: the loss of his weapon accompanies his fall to the post of the Head Executioner of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], which did not strip him of his rank of lieutenant. The [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] sword that Orum gives him afterward is the first he wields, because lieutenants did not carry them. He reaches his anointing still a lieutenant and already the Head Executioner; the new post does not erase the old one, and belowground they still call him lieutenant.

He is anointed Justiciar in the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|temple of Azkaroth]], reciting the order’s vow and the mantra that closes it: glory does not move his hand; order is what demands blood. His first assignment as a Justiciar takes him to the province of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/ventrista\|Ventrista]]. In the forest of Thal’Serin, he swears an oath to Morlgrund.

### Relationships

With Orum-Rahru he maintains a relationship of tense respect: the two disagree over the fate of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]], and Milor does not romanticize the era Orum defends. The two meet again in Ghol’Drokh, where Milor operates as the Head Executioner. He maintains a longstanding rivalry with [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]], dating back to the Reinkardic Wars, and an ally of steadfast loyalty in [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]], whose trust he earned rather than received.
