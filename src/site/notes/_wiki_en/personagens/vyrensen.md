# Malachar Vyrensen

Malachar Vyrensen is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] councilor on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], known as the “Collector of Debts”. He is responsible for [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/coletoria\|the Collectory]] and is based in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. Feared on the black market, he makes politeness an instrument of threat.

### In the Council

During a session of the Shadow Council, he proposes extreme measures against the dwarf leaders of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]].

### Voice

His politeness carries menace, and he uses protocol as a weapon: he omits titles as an insult and deliberately includes information that serves as a warning. When confronting [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], he omits the title “Justiciar” in a display of formal deference that is, in fact, an insult. What goes unsaid is the message.

### Relationships

He clashes with Orum-Rahru over obedience to the monarch’s word.
