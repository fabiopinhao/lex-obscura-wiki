# Mirin

Mirin is a young Sylvan ranger of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/resistencia_de_thal_serin\|the Resistance of Thal’Serin]], an apprentice to [[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]] since the age of eleven. At sixteen summers, she is part of the new generation of resistance members of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]]. She says what she sees before calculating what she ought to leave unsaid.

### Appearance

She has dark copper hair and wears armor made of [[_wiki_en/conceitos/casca_viva\|living bark]], a gift from Gaelwyn in her third summer of training. She carries an elven bow.

### Origin and Role

An orphan, she lost her parents early and grew up in the resistance alongside [[_wiki_en/personagens/calel\|Calel]], two years her junior. Gaelwyn took her in at eleven and made her a ranger, and it was in this calling that she found the purpose death had taken away.

The weight of her role emerges in the druidic circle. It is Mirin who reports the forest’s reaction to Gael: the trees whisper when he passes, the roots retreat from his presence. By saying this, she brings to light the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] mark the master bears.

### Method and Voice

She acts on impulse, but with competence. When the forest whispers against the master, she is the one who repeats the whisper aloud.

### Relationships

Gaelwyn is the mentor who made her a ranger and gave her ground to stand on again after her parents’ death. With Calel, she forms a contrasting pair that sustains them both: she is the arrow in flight, he is the trap awaiting the wrong step. Where Mirin advances without looking back, Calel sets traps in the terrain and waits.

The arrival of [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] in Gaelwyn’s circle takes a toll on her: Mirin resents the newcomer’s presence alongside the master, jealous of the time he devotes to her. When she speaks of her, the complaint comes covered in the praise she has heard from others. Once, when Calel defended Koliah by pointing out that even the older druids listened to her, Mirin added: and obey.
