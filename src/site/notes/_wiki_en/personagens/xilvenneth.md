# Xilvenneth

Xilvenneth is a [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] ophideia stationed at the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], where she circulates from the poison laboratory of the third circle. She moves with the playful fluidity of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|ophideias]], trained from childhood to be living blades: obsidian skin, long fingers, nails painted blood-black. A thin scar crosses her neck and disappears beneath the fabric, its origin undisclosed.

### Bond with the House

She maintains a direct connection with the matriarch [[_wiki_en/personagens/zilvra_xund_ala\|Zilvra Xund’ala]], of House Xund’ala. The way she speaks of the matriarch reveals a relationship of tutelage, beyond mere command: the agent’s discipline is always under Zilvra’s corrective gaze.

### Role

She appears in the poison laboratory to provoke the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/valhedras\|Valhedras]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]], seduction serving as a political instrument characteristic of the ophideia method. When [[_wiki_en/personagens/rhazamir\|Rhazamir]] confronts her with racial hatred, she claims the equality that [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]]’s edicts would guarantee her and humiliates him on three fronts: his arcane incompetence, the purity of lineage that his family preaches without upholding, and his sister’s sexuality, as she has been to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] and submitted to Druls there of her own free will. The provocation sets off the chain of violence that ends with Rhazamir’s fall through the twentieth-floor window.

### Predatory Instinct

When Milor’s curse blasts Rhazamir’s arm, Xilvenneth advances to finish killing him, but Valhedras forcibly restrains her. With Rhazamir already dead in the courtyard, Zilvra silences her and takes control: the agent sets things off, the matriarch manages the consequences.
