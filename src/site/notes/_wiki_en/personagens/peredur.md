# Peredur

Peredur is an elder [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]] druid from [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], with gray eyes, and a devotee of [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]]. He leads the forest’s druidic council and is regarded as possessing great wisdom about ancient times, especially about the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/titas\|titans]].

### Role

In the council of Thal’Serin, he is the authority who asserts himself without raising his voice: measured, enigmatic speech, filled with references to the Times Before. He is cautious when speaking certain names aloud, as though the very ground could betray him.

### Reputation

According to [[_wiki_en/personagens/iluvrae\|Ilúvrae]], he is said to hold the wisdom of the Times Before. Measured speech and veiled references form the image of an elder who knows more than he says.
