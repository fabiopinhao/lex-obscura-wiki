# Valhedras

Valhedras is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lieutenant in the Imperial Army, stationed at the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], where he serves alongside [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]], whose ally and informant he is. Younger than Milor and, in Khargoth’s courtyard, still without visible marks of combat, he has overly attentive eyes that absorb everything as though each word were evidence in a future trial. His temperament is that of an observer, not an enforcer. Over time he gained battle scars, which he prefers to the marks of magic because they tell stories he can choose to reveal.

### Principle

Valhedras measures hierarchy by what it should be, not by what it is: to him, an officer’s authority does not justify disrespecting the legitimate authority of a Justiciar. When provoked by those who seek power without a foundation, he responds with the conviction that ambition without a foundation is a fall waiting to happen, and that he prefers to build before climbing.

### In Khargoth

In Khargoth’s courtyard, during a formation alongside [[_wiki_en/personagens/ravhor\|Ravhor]], he watches Major [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] humiliate the child [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]] and disguises his anger in a restrained gesture, later questioning the major’s actions with Ravhor. The most perceptive reading of the episode is his: he supposes that the Justiciar is testing how far the major will dare to go. Later, in the library on the twentieth floor, he informs Milor of Ravhor’s summons to Vekraal’s Third Legion and finds it strange that his ally remains at a lower rank despite the power he demonstrates. When [[_wiki_en/personagens/rhazamir\|Rhazamir]] confronts the ophideia [[_wiki_en/personagens/xilvenneth\|Xilvenneth]] and she advances to kill him when he is already down, it is Valhedras who restrains her by force, sustaining a bite that [[_wiki_en/personagens/zilvra_xund_ala\|Zilvra]] recognizes when she takes charge of the scene.

### Relationships

He shares the trust of fellow lieutenants with Milorsevogh Velshard, the peer with whom he thinks out loud. He harbors a restrained aversion to Vekraal, applying the same standard: he measures power by legitimacy, not by force.
