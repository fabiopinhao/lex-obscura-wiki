# Voxmere

Voxmere is the pontiff of the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_velmorain\|Temple of Velmorain]], the house of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] dedicated to [[_wiki_en/panteao/velmorain\|Velmorain]]. He is old and remains alive.

### Relationships

In his youth he was an aspirant, and [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] saw great potential in him. The Justiciar met him at that time and still retains the memory of what he expected of him.
