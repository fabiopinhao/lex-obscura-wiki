# Quavarra Ulvondra

Quavarra Ulvondra, called the Obsidian Spider, is matriarch of House Ulvondra and one of the six ruling matriarchs of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. A [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] from [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], she rules from a house-cathedral carved into the living rock.

### Appearance

A lilac mist surrounds her, and tattoos that seem alive run beneath her skin. The nature of these marks remains ambiguous: divine incarnation, personal magic, or both. Her seat is the house-cathedral, a concave cavern opened in the rock of Vor-Ghol, hung with enchanted webs and pierced by stained-glass windows of bioglass; along the walls, ornamental cages hold [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derro]] gnomes. In the hall itself she keeps [[_wiki_en/artefatos/gema-viva\|living gems]], her own and those of other matrons, and counts the eggs with the precision of someone counting enemies. Among the matriarchs, she is the one who would pay the most for a gem, because she needs to prove that she can, and the one who would most quickly sniff out its provenance.

### Philosophy and Position

Quavarra considers herself a philosopher of power before she considers herself a matriarch. She upholds the historical primacy of the devil [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]]: he inscribed the original [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]] in blood before any council knew how to write, and the version in ink that the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] uses is an imperfect translation. Hence her distinction between the monarch, who rules by decree, and Xarn, who founded through blood: [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] reigns, but he is not Xarn.

She also identifies a structural anomaly in the imperial order: a sign of distortion in the darkness, an indication that something in the balance between the races has been twisted by arcane means. She acknowledges the omnipresence of the monarch, whose presence is in every shadow that does not dare move without permission, without thereby submitting to imperial ideology.

### Method and Voice

Quavarra summons the Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] to judge Lieutenant [[_wiki_en/personagens/kaedhras\|Kaedhras Morvil]], a Shadar who violated the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]] by invading restricted routes with runic keys and plundering living gems, the [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídar]] eggs taken from the matrons’ hall. Under Xarn’s laws, Druls do not directly punish Shadar military personnel: the hammer is the judge’s. The choice of Orum, and no other, is deliberate, as much a request for justice as an assessment of the Justiciar himself. When she needs the defendant to speak, she snaps her fingers and undoes the magical silence that gags him, conducting the interrogation in her territory, surrounded by her female guards and her stained-glass windows.

### Relationships

Orum-Rahru (instrument and object): she summons the Justiciar as an enforcer, but measures his character under the pretext of demanding justice.

Arothistar (monarch): she acknowledges his power without bowing to the ideology, setting Xarn’s primacy against imperial authority.

Kaedhras Morvil (expendable piece): handed over to the Justiciar’s judgment as evidence of Shadar military corruption and as bait to assess Orum, knowing execution to be inevitable.
