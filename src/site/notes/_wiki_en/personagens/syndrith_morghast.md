# Syndrith Morghast

Syndrith Morghast is a [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] ophideia of the [[_wiki/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]], of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]]. She bears the purple seal of the Council, an authority that overrides the jurisdiction of other officers, and with it supervises [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] in [[_wiki/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]].

### Appearance

She has black-purple skin and violet eyes with a faint glow, half-closed when she assesses. Her long hair is platinum, fine and pale as a spiderweb. She wears the attire of a Drul ophideia: draped cloth over leather, sashes, and strategic openings that deliberately reveal the body, never the heavy cuirass that would hinder a turn or a fall. The sigil of [[_wiki/organizacoes/casa_morghast\|House Morghast]] appears as a ritual ornament on her cloak. She wields a repeating crossbow and twin rapiers, and rides a [[_wiki/criaturas/sombralva\|Sombralva]], a large feline with fur that drinks in light instead of reflecting it.

### Origin and Role

She is both a spy and a hierophant: the liturgical weight of the ophideia combined with fieldwork. She conducts high-level infiltration and nighttime reconnaissance and recruits field intelligence. In Niedellin, she descended at night and read the terrain. Her next rite is the Oblátio Ereba.

### Method and Voice

Syndrith rarely improvises. She reads her surroundings before acting and prefers to let another’s mistake yield returns like currency rather than correct it. She almost never insults directly; her venom comes wrapped in politeness, sarcasm as inverted courtesy, praise that degrades while sounding like a compliment. Calling a superior a senior officer, in her mouth, sounds less like deference and more like a warning. Reciting a luminous prayer aloud becomes, with the right intonation, the sacred reduced to comic absurdity. Genuine fascination, when it appears, emerges disguised as ironic astonishment, as in the presence of a learned [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]], as rare as mercy in [[_wiki/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]].

### Magic

Specializing in [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], the school of passing through matter, she moves where walls would stop anyone else. She invokes [[_wiki/panteao/ulravaeth\|Ulravaeth]] to bend and manipulate her own shadow, a tool for someone who works in the dark and rarely wants to be seen approaching. Advanced Spectro exacts its price: the illusion of passing through matter erodes her trust in her own perception, and she touches objects now and then to confirm that they exist. She knows the repertoire of curses of the sorceresses of [[_wiki/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]]: she recognizes Drul work in a mark and knows to dismiss the hypothesis when the mark is not the work of her own people.

### Relationships

She has known [[_wiki/personagens/vhraxil\|Vhraxil]] since childhood, and he is one of the very few figures toward whom she shows affection. She approached [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]] by acknowledging his erudition, and that was how she recruited him as an informant. Toward Milor, whom she supervises without being subordinate to him, she was initially provocative; later, when she offered to explore the village under the cloak of night, she showed him unprecedented deference, without irony.
