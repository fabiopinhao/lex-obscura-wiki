# Vekraal

Vekraal is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] general of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], known as the “Fist of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]” at the head of the Third Eastern Legion. Brutal and arrogant, he upholds as declared policy the belief that terror is worth more than armies, and wields a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] sword. The uncle of [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]], daughter of [[_wiki_en/personagens/nyssira_avaroth\|Nyssira Avaroth]], he is one of the Empire’s generals. In the barracks, he is called “General Vek”, a nickname used in his absence.

### Command

He ordered alchemical fire against [[_wiki_en/locais/lendalas\|Lendalas]] and, during a crisis, executed a Shadar general considered a traitor.

He commands through humiliation: he turns victories into humiliating displays and leaves subordinates silently resentful. His overbearing manner, which turns every order into a sentence, makes him feared and, at the same time, difficult to replace, and the same arrogance that brings down enemies alienates allies. Fewer than half of his lieutenants actually admire him; the rest merely tolerate him.

Among the military figures of the current generation, he is considered the one who best wears the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/khemor\|Khemor]], the mask of fear in imperial doctrine. It is said among the troops that the respect he receives is directed at the Khemor before it reaches the man or his deeds. The deep voice he adopts before the troops is a deliberate performance, the Khemor in his throat.

### Voice

Direct and brutal, he resorts to physical demonstrations of power as an argument, and declares terror his method, without concealing it.

Before speaking, he snaps his fingers in sequence, index, middle, ring, little, in a cadence of menace, each snap like a neck breaking. The gesture calls for an audience: it is a calculated expenditure of fear, consistent with the economy of horror he preaches. In one episode with his niece while she was still a child, he presented her to the lieutenants as an example of noble roots and, as he let go of her, disarranged her braid with a deliberate tug.

### Relationships

He was chosen for the post by Arothistar himself, and not by etiquette, as he himself declares. Among the lieutenants, his impunity before [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] is attributed to a distant kinship with the monarch. House Avaroth bears the suffix “-aroth”, reserved by decree for relatives of Arothistar up to the seventh degree; no such tie is confirmed.

Blood ties him to Nidzina, with no visible affection. He harbors a long-standing rivalry with [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh]], who had wished him dead since the Reinkardic Wars. Both were present at the fall of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], when Arothistar abolished the [[_wiki_en/magia/rito_de_ossarium\|Rite of Ossárium]] for good.
