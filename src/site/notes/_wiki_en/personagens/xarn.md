# Xarn

Xarn is an elder [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]], a grand master cleric, and the spiritual leader of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]]. Author of the Codex that governs the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/alianca_subterranea\|Underground Alliance]] between Shadars and [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]], he wrote the laws that others now recite. He lives in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]].

### Role

He leads the twin houses of the Cult: the eparchy of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], in the underground citadel of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], and the [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]] eparchy, in the sacred region of Morvgrad. It was he who unified the scattered diabolical temples under the Shadow Cult and engineered the alliance between Shadars and Druls. It is said that he may have been deified, although his status remains uncertain.

### Official Acts

[[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] forbade bonds with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/demonios\|demons]] (the practice of [[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]]), and Xarn endorsed the prohibition by edict. The endorsement added the spiritual force of the Shadow Cult to the political force of the throne. The prohibition is ancient, and punishment is meted out in the [[_wiki_en/locais/drokh_malahar\|Pits of Drokh’Malahar]]. Under it, a bond with demons is a legal scandal before it is a danger.

### Voice

He speaks with the weight of centuries and questions more than he declares, but his questions carry the weight of a verdict: his is the authority of one who wrote the laws of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] that others merely recite.

### Relationships

His relationship with Arothistar is that of architect and heir: he wrote the laws and the Codex that the monarch inhabits.
