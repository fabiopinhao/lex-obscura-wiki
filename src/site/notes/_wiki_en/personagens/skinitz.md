# Skinitz

Skinitz is a young [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Muran]], brother of [[_wiki_en/personagens/skanivik\|Skanivik]]. He was arrested in [[_wiki_en/locais/ezrath\|Ezrath]] while stealing pralines alongside his brother.

### The Ezrath Incident

[[_wiki_en/personagens/korven\|Korven]] pinned his tail beneath his boot, and Skinitz squealed without breaking free. The lieutenant’s slap came next, and then a blow with a belt from the wronged trader. Skinitz cried out for his brother, and the cry mingled with the blood; Skanivik hesitated for an instant and fled. [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] witnessed the scene, ordered the beating stopped so they would not dirty the streets of Ezrath with rat blood, and ordered Korven to send him straight to the [[_wiki_en/locais/fosso_da_purga\|Pit of the Purge]], where Skinitz died.

### Consequence

Skinitz’s death leaves its mark on Skanivik, who carries the memory of his brother and the incident. Encountering Korven again at the market in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] rekindles the memory of the arrest and the fall into the Pit, over half a dozen pralines.
