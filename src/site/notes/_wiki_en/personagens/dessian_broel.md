# Dessian Broel

Dessian Broel is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] and a councilor on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], where he holds the functional office of Administrator of Resources. He is a man of pragmatic inclinations and maintains a private museum.

### Activities

At the Council session, he proposes the cleansing of surplus populations as an administrative solution, aligning himself with the management logic his title suggests. At the same session, [[_wiki_en/personagens/noxmoor\|Noxmoor]] counters the proposal and takes a jab at him over the relics from [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]] that Dessian keeps in his gallery.

### The Fendris Chain

His private museum housed the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] relic that the dwarves of Thandruk Varn had in their custody without understanding its nature. The dagger was stolen and passed through the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]] network before being confiscated by [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]]. A successful theft from the museum of a councilor on the Shadow Council demonstrates the reach and audacity of the criminal organization.
