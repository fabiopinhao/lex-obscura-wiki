# Sarquin

Sarquin is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] and [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|justiciar]] stationed in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], absorbed in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/tribunal_de_morvgrad\|Tribunal]]’s schedule of public trials.

### Office

He serves in court, far from the field. He knows the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/lex_obscura_decreto\|Lex Obscura]] and the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/codex_somniorum\|Codex Somniorum]] as written, reads in public session the sentences others have determined, and is responsible for a set docket. The distinction carries weight within the order itself, because the justiciar’s office originated in investigating in hostile territory, and the Tribunal is the only jurisdiction where the law is applied sitting down.

He bears no house name. Like [[_wiki_en/personagens/tashkur\|Tashkur]], he rose without lineage, through the three pillars of selection: martial and arcane mastery, legal erudition, and devotion to the cult.

### Relationships

He was trained by [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], who codified the rite of consecration and trained most of today’s justiciars.
