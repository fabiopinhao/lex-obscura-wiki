# Rhazamir

Rhazamir is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lieutenant in the imperial army, stationed at the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]] and a practitioner of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]] school. He bears the school’s insignia on his chest, but his competence has never matched the emblem: House [[_wiki_en/personagens/zilvra_xund_ala\|Xund’ala]] has recorded seven incidents that [[_wiki_en/personagens/xilvenneth\|Xilvenneth]] publicly summarizes as crooked vectors. He is a staunch racist and a defender of pure bloodlines, a position his own family does not support. He is Archon Dorthea’s brother, a relationship that only comes to light after his death.

### The Fall

At the Tower of Khargoth, Rhazamir drew his sword against the ophideia Xilvenneth after a provocation that exposed his arcane incompetence and the sexuality of his sister, who had been to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] and returned from there with marks that the Drul hierophants describe as pleasure runes, taught only to those who beg. [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milor]] intervened: against the lieutenant’s Electro, he invoked a proscribed curse of [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]] that rendered his right arm useless, deflected the blow, and hurled him through the twentieth-floor window into the courtyard below.

### Consequences

Milor did not plan to kill him and merely reacted to the escalation. The curse of Malagoth he invoked is recognized by its signature, and the House of Zilvra needs to intervene to keep the matter contained. His relationship to Archon Dorthea, revealed after his death, turns a death in a tower corridor into a political problem on a different scale. As Zilvra puts it, certain falls begin before the window.
