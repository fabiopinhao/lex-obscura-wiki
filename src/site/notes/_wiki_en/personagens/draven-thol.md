# Draven-Thol

Draven-Thol is a [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] stationed in [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], on the southwestern border. He appeared before the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] as an image in the tar pit during a session in which he reported the crisis at the forges; afterward he returned to silence, and days later there was no longer any news of him. The fortress is the last place he was.

### Appearance

He is thin and has nervous fingers, always moving, the physical mark of someone who works through a records tablet.

### Origin and Role

He was sent to the mountain on the border of [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]] because of the cold forges. From Thandruk Varn, he reports to the Council, through the tar pit, what seethes among the dwarf clans confined to the same level of the mountain: the disputes that never cease, the sabotage between smithies, and the drop in production for the third lunar cycle. He is a bureaucrat to the bone, and therein lies his weight: without him, the provincial lord is lost.

### Method and Voice

The method is recordkeeping, and the record is everything. Draven-Thol trusts the tablet: every dispute and every name set down in order before the meaning escapes. Bureaucratic meticulousness is how he holds his ground. The notes he left guided interrogations in his absence, including that of [[_wiki_en/personagens/jalu_nvara\|Jalú’Nvara]] in the dungeons, whom he himself had already interrogated with the records tablet.

### Relationships

[[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] is the one who keeps the name alight. In the Council, he defends Draven-Thol against the military hammers of [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]]: the justiciar, he says, knows what he is doing. The defense shows that Orum trusts those whom the others ignore. He is also the one who notices the absence, when the forges stop and the man who would know how to explain them does not answer.

The first public sign that something has happened comes from his name, spoken in a conversation. [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]], who still assumes he is on duty, recommends him to [[_wiki_en/personagens/gorvam_threnk\|Gorvam Threnk]] as the man best positioned for the diplomatic front of the forges, someone with a knack for dealing with wounded pride and suspended hammers. The Master of the Forges stalls halfway through the name, coughs twice as though choking on it, averts his eyes, and hastens the end of the conversation. Nidzina almost asks what is wrong, and the name remains caught between her teeth.

The case remains open: the pressure falls on [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]], and [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]] remains captive in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]]. Nidzina ends up tasked with discovering what happened to him.
