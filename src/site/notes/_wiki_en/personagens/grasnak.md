# Grasnak

Grasnak is a legendary figure in the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] oral tradition, the protagonist of the Lays of Grasnak, an epic about the defense of the dwarven fortress of Khaz-Ankor over seven nights. Grasnak cannot be placed precisely in time and survives exclusively as a cultural legacy, passed down by word of mouth through generations of orcs. It is not known in which era he lived, or whether Grasnak is a proper name or an honorific title.

### The Lays

The Lays of Grasnak constitute an oral tradition that has never been transcribed; to an orc, writing down a song would be as absurd as bottling the wind. The epic recounts the defense of a dwarven fortress by orcs, although the origin of this defense, given the ancestral hatred between the two races, remains uncertain. The preservation of the Lays is, paradoxically, a product of imperial contempt: the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] never asked for a translation or tried to suppress them, and [[_wiki_en/linguas/orcanico\|Orcanic]] survives because no one bothered to destroy it.

### Reverberation

[[_wiki_en/personagens/ghur\|Ghur]], [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]]’s brother, used to recite the Lays around the campfires before dying in [[_wiki_en/locais/lendalas\|Lendalas]]. Grothar inherited this knowledge and revealed it to [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]], surprising her by asking whether she knew the Lays of Grasnak. Grothar’s erudition, rare among orcs, is a direct result of this inheritance.
