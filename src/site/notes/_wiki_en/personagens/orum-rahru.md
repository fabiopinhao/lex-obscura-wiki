# Orum-Rahru

Orum-Rahru is a veteran [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|justiciar]], dean of the order, known as the “hammer of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]”. A ninety-nine-year-old [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]], the same age as [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], he is the first justiciar appointed by the monarch, some forty-four years ago, and the figure who most shaped what the order has become. Conservative and nostalgic for the pre-imperial era, he wears silver Templar armor preserved since those times, enchanted with strength, endurance, and vigor, which he rarely removes: the armor is almost an extension of his identity.

### Origin

Before becoming a justiciar, he was a Templar of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], the only servant the devil accepted at a time; he received the position after killing [[_wiki_en/personagens/bactrak\|Bactrak]] in a ritual duel. When Vozrador was imprisoned, some fifty-five years ago, Orum lost the powers granted by the god and still does not know the cause. He thus became the only Templar to become a justiciar and the only one among them without an active pact.

He entered service at nineteen, when he was anointed a Templar in a collective ceremony of the Cult, with some forty postulants from various temples, held that year at the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_vozrador\|temple of Vozrador]]; he has worn the armor ever since. His selection as Vozrador’s Templar and the duel with Bactrak came later, at twenty-three. He was anointed a justiciar at fifty-five. The thirty-six years of Templar service added to the forty-four in the black mantle amount to eighty years of his life dedicated to the office.

### Role

He codified the Justiciars’ consecration rite, whose vow ends with the monarch’s mantra, “Order demands blood”, Arothistar’s ritual words since the beginning of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/terceira_era\|Third Era]]. He profoundly influenced what the order has become and how it acts, and trained most of the Justiciars; over the last two decades, he was the only one to instruct new members. At ninety-nine, he lives with growing paranoia. Before the Shadow Council, he volunteered for a mission in [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]].

### Presence

Orum’s authority carries weight through accumulated history and requires no loudness. When emotion reaches him, it seeps out in silence or unexpected brevity. A living memory of nearly a century, he speaks as one who is what remains of an era that no longer exists. He harbors open contempt for goblinoids, a trait he does not conceal. He fears falls and voids, a fear he never confesses. In the elevator of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], suspended at a height of more than a thousand fathoms, he silently grips the structure’s icy steel, and to get out he prefers to climb on foot, along routes of solid stone that preserve his dignity. He even asks for a staircase that avoids the elevator, and where the shaft opens, he avoids looking down. In [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], a mad prisoner reads this fear and uses it against him; for an instant the relationship reverses, and Orum nearly loses control.

He cleans his glove like someone restoring the layer between himself and the world: he wipes away the blood, wipes away the film on metal that others have touched, and the gesture is one of distance, not adjustment. He also readjusts his armor, pauldron, breastplate, and weight, almost always before changing his tone of voice, because the Templar silver is imposing on a ninety-nine-year-old body and the enchantment supports what his back no longer can.

He never gets the name of the [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Muran]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/skanivik\|Skanivik]] right: he calls him Skavnik, Skaninik, Skanivek, Skavivitz, or Skanuik.

### The Two Hammers

The justiciar mantra Orum voices is that of the two types of hammer: one drives nails, the other shapes, war allows both, and true victory depends on the second. Of the two, he lays claim to the second, and this is the key to the epithet he bears.

The method has a name and a place: it was through this method that Elenir fell without an assault, with diplomacy in his charge. A glass offered at the right time, he used to say, brings down more walls than a battering ram, and [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] sums up the outcome of that campaign: it saved more lives than all the swords combined.

### Magic

He commands [[_wiki_en/magia/teru\|Teru]], magic without a pact, which he developed at an advanced age. His Templar armor bears enchantments of strength, endurance, and vigor. He wields a dwarven steel sword with [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] concentrated in its ribs, following the Shadar arcane art of imprisoning energy in crystals; it cuts through the undead where the Strategicon, the Shadar tactical manual, calls for impact.

### Relationships

He was the tutor of [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]], whom he has trained since childhood. He maintains a relationship of tense respect with Milorsevogh Velshard: he recognizes in him an heir to his understanding, though not to his office. The two disagree over the fate of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]], and meet again in Ghol’Drokh, where Milor works as the head executioner. On the walls of [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], he shares reflections with [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]].
