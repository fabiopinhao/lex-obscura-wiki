# Svyarnov

Svyarnov was the operational leader of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]] in the cave arsenal in the Mountains of the West. A [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] in a purple cloak, he carried a high-quality [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] sword, inconsistent with the profile of an ordinary smuggler.

### Mountain Arsenal

From the cave arsenal, Svyarnov commanded the Purple Serpent’s arms trafficking. [[_wiki_en/personagens/korven\|Korven]] operated as one of his lieutenants.

### Confrontation and Death

When [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] revealed herself as a Justiciar and offered him the chance to surrender, Svyarnov refused without hesitation and ordered the attack. The operation collapsed from within: during the fighting he turned against one of his own allies, who threw him off the platform and ran him through with the spear, without killing him. Dying, he was questioned by Nidzina about those who had given the orders, but died without revealing their identities.
