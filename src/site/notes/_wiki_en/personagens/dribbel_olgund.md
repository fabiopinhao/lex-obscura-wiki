# Dribbel Olgund

Dribbel Olgund, the Trade-Breaker, is a [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derro]] who makes the black market of [[_wiki/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] his narrow kingdom. At the [[_wiki/locais/mercado_oeste\|West Market]], he operates the [[_wiki/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]] under a forged seal, selling dubious cures and keeping, buried beneath his chatter, a rare knowledge: the routes through the Depths of [[_wiki/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. He survives by cunning, not strength, and is afraid enough to be dangerous precisely when he talks too much.

### Appearance

He is an adult Derro with a slender bone structure, shaped by life in narrow tunnels. His skin has the pale gray of his race, outside the pink range of the surface peoples, and his large eyes have dominant pupils, made for a darkness without light. His pointed ears move at the slightest noise, alert like the rest of him. Fine hair, white and gray, only partly tied back. He stands barely over one meter and seventeen centimeters, shorter than a gnome, and faint veins run beneath the skin at his temples and wrists. He dresses as what he is: a surface merchant-swindler, a hybrid register of the Depths and the city.

### Role

Familiar with the underground veins that connect Morvgrad to the Depths, Dribbel sells information and passage with the same ease with which he sells fake medicine. His stall in the underworld thrives because he knows where the tunnels of Vor-Ghol run, and because few descend there without a guide. That was how he took in the gnoll [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]] after the latter lost his den, attributing the attack to a meteor of red magic, and that was how he found himself, one day, dragged away from his own stall: the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] coerced him into guiding a group through the Depths in search of a clandestine shipment.

### Voice and Method

Fear, in Dribbel, does not translate into silence: it turns into logorrhea. His sentences grow long and digressive, peppered with interjections, and in them he gives away more than he intended, because he cannot stop talking. Useful information comes buried in the drivel, and anyone who knows how to listen gathers it almost without his noticing. Sometimes realization comes too late, along with what has already slipped out: the Serpent’s witnesses do not grow old, he murmurs on one occasion, the words of someone who has just condemned himself with his own mouth.

### Merchant’s Alchemy

Dribbel does not cast spells. His power fits into flasks. The healing potions he sells are of questionable quality, and even so they have already saved lives in the Depths, where there is no better choice. When cunning fails and flight is not enough, he resorts to the trick of his trade: a flask of acid gas that bursts into a yellow mist and disorients those around him, long enough for a small Derro to disappear into the dark.

### Relationships

With Fandegarrón, the dependence is mutual and dysfunctional. The gnoll calls him boss and fails at the only task he has, giving warning of patrols, and even so the two remain bound to each other, rescued and rescuer with no better fate in sight. With Nidzina, there is no bond, there is coercion: forced to guide her under threat, Dribbel descended with her into the Depths and nearly paid for the journey with his life. That he returned from it is, perhaps, his greatest achievement.
