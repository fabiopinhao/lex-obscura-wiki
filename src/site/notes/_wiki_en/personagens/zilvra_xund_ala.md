# Zilvra Xund’ala

Zilvra Xund’ala, known by the epithet the Poisoned Hand, is matriarch of House Xund’ala and one of the six ruling matriarchs of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. The other rulers govern from underground; she operates on the surface, in the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], the heart of [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] arcane power. Maintaining a [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] presence on Shadar soil, surrounded by preceptors and hierarchies that owe her no formal obedience, indicates influence built through information, poison, and calculation, without relying on brute force.

### Method and Voice

Zilvra appears in the Tower after the fatal fall of [[_wiki_en/personagens/rhazamir\|Rhazamir]], a Shadar lieutenant hurled from the twentieth-floor window by Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]], who invoked a proscribed curse of [[_wiki_en/panteao/malagoth\|Malagoth]] on sacred ground. Her gait is precise. She crouches beside the glass, touches the floor with two fingers, and examines every trace: the ozone of [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]], the cold of [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], the shards, the dust from a charred bookcase. She distinguishes the two schools and, beyond that, the fact that they did not come from the same hand. She examines every face present and pieces together the picture before speaking a word.

The confrontation that follows is with Preceptor Elzakar, the tower’s arcane authority, who recognized Malagoth’s signature and is preparing a denunciation for Councilor [[_wiki_en/personagens/ulrik_morzenn\|Ulrik Morzenn]] through the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]]. Zilvra intercepts him with a suggestion that is, in practice, a threat dressed as philosophy: that he think carefully about what he will report. And she reframes the facts. It was Rhazamir who attacked first with Electro and destroyed the tower’s property, so the fall comes to be presented as a consequence, not murder. The idea that certain falls begin long before the window encapsulates her entire method: to her, truth is malleable material, and she possesses the tools to shape it.

### Relationships

Zilvra is the reason Velshard politically survives Rhazamir’s death. The use of Malagoth’s magic on sacred ground has been a capital crime since the Zarukh Veymar incident, in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/segunda_era\|Second Era]]; without someone with the power to rewrite the account of events, the Justiciar would have been tried and perhaps executed before receiving his mission to [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]]. The protection has an unstated price, but it is not accidental: a young Shadar who commands Malagoth’s curses without a pact is too valuable an asset for a preceptor to destroy with a report.

Elzakar is the adversary held in check, the formal arcane hierarchy against informal ophideia power, in a dispute over control of the account of events that will reach the Council. Connected to Zilvra is the ophideia [[_wiki_en/personagens/xilvenneth\|Xilvenneth]], present in the corridor provoking Rhazamir, moments before the conflict, with revelations about his sister and about Vor-Ghol. It is unclear whether she was a deliberate catalyst or a piece out of control. The connection suggests that Xilvenneth’s presence in the tower was not incidental, and that the matriarch need not be where her hand reaches.
