# Yzmareth

Yzmareth is a [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] [[_wiki_en/racas/sauriano\|Saurian]] of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/cla_myr_vethan\|Clan Myr’vethan]], a user of draconic red magic and the only member of his species seen flying over [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|the Dorsal]]. He appears on the plateau in search of the dagger [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]] and loses it to [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]].

### Appearance

He is imposing, with dark red scales. He carries a spear with a large ruby set into the end opposite the blade. His armor is forged with openings for his wings, although the race rarely flies.

### Origin and Role

He comes from [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]], lands of the Draconids beyond the Dorsal, and belongs to Clan Myr’vethan. He is the only one seen crossing the Dorsal in flight, a distinction among members of his species. He arrives in [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] with a narrow mission: to find Fendris, the [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] dagger from [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]] capable of opening rifts between the planes and wounding even ancient creatures. According to the shamans, it is the key to killing an ancient dragon such as [[_wiki_en/personagens/varna\|Varna]], whose enemy Yzmareth is: he plots to destroy Varna to unify the clans under the Myr’vethan. He negotiates over the cargo and the dagger with [[_wiki_en/personagens/svyarnov\|Svyarnov]], of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]], at the clandestine arsenal west of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]].

### Method and Voice

Yzmareth speaks like a pragmatic dragon. He states the objective and the obstacle, never the feeling. Each sentence of his measures what is still needed for the mission and what stands in its way, and he negotiates over Fendris as someone closing a shipping deal would, without digression. When he decides not to attack, he strikes his own breastplate with a claw, once, and speaks in an almost casual tone; it is with this gesture that, after Nidzina’s ultimatum, he passes the decision about the Purple Serpent to Svyarnov. His defeat at Nidzina’s hands is an unsettled account to him, something to make right.

### Magic

He conjures draconic red magic, of fire and magma. Large-scale invocations take their toll on him from within: a thin, continuous vapor escapes between the scales of his neck while the plates remain intact, as is characteristic of the toll on his race. At the same arsenal, he employs this magic against Nidzina and her allies, hurling flames and molten rock into the encircled operation. When the fight turns, he flees in flight at dusk and escapes the predatory Eidolon she summons to stop him.

### Relationships

Nidzina Avaroth is the rival who defeats him: she confiscates Fendris at the arsenal and forces him to flee, and it is to this loss that he attributes the lack of credibility he needs to remedy. With the Purple Serpent, he maintains the deal for the dagger, handled by Svyarnov. When he is pressed about his passage through the underground, it is the name of [[_wiki_en/personagens/wijmarv\|Wijmarv]] that he raises: the [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]] shaman who served the clan and fled back to Varnak. He is also the meteor of red magic that previously drove [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]] from his den.
