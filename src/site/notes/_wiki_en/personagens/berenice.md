# Berenice

Berenice is a human girl from [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], an orphan and the older sister of [[_wiki_en/personagens/levi\|Levi]]. She belongs to the [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]] community and lives under the care of the village chapel, where the two siblings hide from the world that left them with no one. She has the gaze of someone who has learned to observe before speaking, and it is through this watchful gaze that she becomes a witness: small in the face of things far too big for her age.

### Appearance

Berenice’s skin is very pale, with a whiteness reminiscent of a porcelain doll, and her eyes hold a lively, childish curiosity that lights up even amid fear. Her voice is high and sweet, a child’s voice that has not yet unlearned tenderness. She wears a skirt, and the overall effect is that of a slight girl who seems more fragile than she proves to be.

### Origin and Role

An orphan, Berenice grows up in the chapel of Niedellin alongside Levi, each the other’s only remaining family tie. She was the one who witnessed a rare celestial phenomenon over the village, and she was nearby when [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith Morghast]] passed through the village. What happens in Niedellin, she sees, records, and does not forget.

### Method and Voice

Berenice is afraid, and her fear is more visible than Levi’s, surfacing on her face before she can contain it. Even so, she tries to seem strong, raises her high voice against the dread, and feigns a firmness her body does not yet possess. She is a witness by calling and by fate: she sees what happens, records what changes, and does not forget.

### Relationships

Levi is her brother and her world, the bond that remained when all else was gone, and she watches him with the restless attention of someone who has only that to lose. Among the adults at the chapel, [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]] is the one responsible for the two of them. And it was [[_wiki_en/personagens/talin\|Talin]], the young scout, whom she chose as her protector.
