# Belarbreza Vhaskrith

Belarbreza Vhaskrith, called Torment-Forger, is the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] matriarch of House Vhaskrith in [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]] and one of the six ruling matriarchs of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. She governs the Chambers of Trial, a complex where [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|ophideia]] apprentices are molded by a discipline that does not separate instruction from punishment.

### Appearance

She is as tall as a temple column, with a soft voice that descends to a register low enough to make bones vibrate and with fists calibrated by decades of pain. Her eyes dissect souls before bodies, and her smile is that of a spider lying in wait. The epithet Torment-Forger derives from the Chambers’ method.

### Origin and Role

Belarbreza forges the ophideias she receives. The Chambers of Trial operate under a single principle, that loyalty, technique, and killer instinct are distilled through systematic pain. Initiates remain suspended for days, have their fingers broken repeatedly, and are brought to the limit of survival; those who yield are discarded, and those who endure emerge as instruments of the matriarchs of Xarn’Zareth.

Young [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]]’s training takes place entirely under this jurisdiction. When [[_wiki_en/personagens/nyssira_avaroth\|Nyssira Avaroth]], accompanied by [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], descends to Vor-Ghol to negotiate her daughter’s training, it is Belarbreza who takes the commission and sets the terms. The surface [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]]’s annual descents to Xarn’Zareth all take place under the authority of the Chambers she maintains.

### Method and Voice

Belarbreza appoints [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/zarae\|Zarae]] as the newcomer’s pair of instructors and demands training without concessions from them: everything must be shown, including the arachnid rituals and the forbidden tongue, without softening. The two apprentices bet that the foreigner will beg for her mother in the first week; the matriarch does not correct their presumption and lets the outcome contradict it.

Among those who deal underground, she has a reputation for accepting only barter, favor for favor, and credit with her is worth more than currency, rots more slowly, and cuts deeper.

Her authority is exercised through verdicts. In the duel between apprentices in which Nidzina kills Zarae, Belarbreza watches without interfering, examines the body, and ratifies it as legitimate combat: “Clean. Valid.” The act seals a hierarchy: the foreigner has proven herself true iron in the Chambers she governs, and that carries more weight than the loss of an apprentice.

### Relationships

In the Chambers, the operational rule is that whoever falls has fallen: the matriarch assesses. She accepted Nyssira’s commission and delivered the result without feeling, certifying Nidzina’s temper as a technical matter; what the Shadar does with her is no longer her concern. She validated the death of Zarae, her own apprentice, without hesitation and kept Syndrith under her jurisdiction until she became a full ophideia, because the training makes no provision for mourning, only continuity. The negotiation of Nidzina’s training sealed the alliance between House Vhaskrith and House Avaroth: Nyssira brought the proposal, Orum-Rahru the argument, and Belarbreza supplied the forge.
