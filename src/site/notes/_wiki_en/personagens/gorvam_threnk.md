# Gorvam Threnk

Gorvam Threnk is a councilor on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] with the functional title of the Master of the Forges. He oversees production at the imperial arsenals, a role that gives him direct jurisdiction over [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], the only original dwarven fortress still operating on the continent, and over the subjugated [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]], bound to quotas, who supply it. He assesses the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] in terms of production: burning forges, full arsenals, supply chains.

### Appearance

Gray-toned skin, like cold iron. Stiff fingers, marked by metalworking and stained with permanent soot. At Council sessions he wears decorative ritual armor: reliefs of anvils and hammers on the chest, forge runes on the shoulders, plates that recount the history of iron from ore to war. He retains the accent of the mountains. Recurring habit: drumming his fingers on the table while thinking.

### Function and Jurisdiction

As the Master of the Forges, Gorvam is responsible for the chain that arms the imperial campaigns. While the forges of Thandruk Varn produce steel, the monarch has the means to wage his wars; that production line falls under Gorvam’s authority. He treats the subjugation of the Karashim as a logistical fact before a moral question: he defends the dwarves’ survival because the alternative is empty arsenals. The indispensability he invokes on their behalf functions as management, not alliance.

Consequently, he equates the collapse of the forges with the collapse of the State itself. His position in summary: without steel, there is no Empire.

### Method and Position on the Council

Among the pillars of the Empire, Gorvam occupies the place of matter, and translates politics into logistics. Where [[_wiki_en/personagens/vyrensen\|Malachar Vyrensen]] proposes extreme measures and other councilors operate through veils of power, he argues through calculations of resources.

Faced with a report of sabotage among the dwarven forges and declining production, when Vyrensen proposes exterminating the Karashim leaders, Gorvam blocks the measure: rebels, but irreplaceable, since without them the arsenals fall silent. He defines the stance as pragmatism, not pity (dead dwarves do not forge swords). In the debate over the foundations of the Empire, in opposition to [[_wiki_en/personagens/suriev_molkhiir\|Suriev Molkhiir]], who invokes faith as a pillar, he responds that faith does not boil glass or shape metal, acknowledging the need for both faith and pacts. The formulation is conciliatory on the surface, but measures the Empire by the production line, not the altar.

He also operates by calculating credit: he negotiates recognition before the result, a common practice in a chamber where merit is a bargaining chip.

The jurisdiction he claims has a limit he himself acknowledges. When examining seized weapons of workmanship too refined for an ordinary workshop, he admitted he had never seen a piece like them, even though he sees, or should see, everything that comes out of Thandruk Varn.

### Relationships

**Malachar Vyrensen.** A divergence of method, not ideology. Both serve the Empire and disagree over the acceptable cost of keeping it armed: Vyrensen proposes cutting, Gorvam proposes preserving the productive tool.

**[[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]].** Operational contact via the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]], treated as a political transaction under the appearance of a technical consultation. Gorvam offers institutional validation and weight on the Council in exchange for information from the field and the credit that comes by association; both calculate before speaking.

**[[_wiki_en/personagens/draven-thol\|Draven-Thol]].** The only point in the consultation through the Oculus at which Gorvam’s composure falters. When Nidzina, who still assumes the Justiciar is in service, names him as the man best positioned for the diplomatic front of the forges, Gorvam stalls midway through the name, coughs as though choking on it, and the eyes that were staring at her begin searching for a point out of sight. He follows with brief praise, brings the conversation back to its place, and hastens the end of the connection. Nidzina almost asks what is wrong with him, and the connection closes before she asks.

**Thandruk Varn and Karashim.** A supervised resource. The indispensability he invokes simultaneously protects and imprisons the dwarves whose production sustains the arsenals.
