# Pip

Pip is a [[_wiki_en/racas/hallirin\|Hallirin]], a surface gnome and former trader in [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], where he ran the shop “Pip’s Sundries” before the city’s fall. Captured after the fall, he lives enslaved in the Depths, under the coercive control of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], between [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]].

### Appearance

Short in stature, even for a gnome, he is tiny and quick. In his youth, he slipped between the legs of much larger opponents with the agility of a weasel, and he retains the expansive gestures and rapid speech of someone who fills a small body with a large presence.

### Origin

As a child in Myrgarde, he was a constant presence in the games of [[_wiki_en/conceitos/aroada\|Aroada]] in the city’s squares, where agility made up for size. From the shop “Pip’s Sundries,” he sold candles, ropes, dried herbs, and protective amulets, until the city’s fall scattered everything he knew.

### Role

In the forges of the Depths, the former sundries shopkeeper begins making chains, the instruments of confinement for others. Before captivity, he had an effusive and warm temperament, given to rough, unceremonious affection.

### Relationships

He is a friend of [[_wiki_en/personagens/auren\|Auren]].
