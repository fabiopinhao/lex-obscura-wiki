# Voldriz Vrinn’dal

Voldriz Vrinn’dal is a [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] matriarch of House Vrinn’dal, known by the epithet the Weaver of Chains, and one of the six ruling matriarchs of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. She is based in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]].

### Origin and Role

As matriarch of one of the six ruling Houses of Vor-Ghol, Voldriz shares governance of the Depths with the other weavers. House Vrinn’dal maintains its seat in Ghilanna. Voldriz is also a voice of the doctrine of the Chambers of Trial of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], the initiation regime undergone by children handed over to the matriarchs.

### Method and Voice

The doctrine of the Chambers is conveyed through her voice. Voldriz oversees initiates suspended for days and fingers broken repeatedly, and pronounces the sentence that sums up the regime: spiders know no mercy. For [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith Morghast]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/zarae\|Zarae]], children in the Chambers, Voldriz’s voice was indistinguishable from the voice of the very system that shaped them — it is this phrase that echoes in the initiates’ memory.
