# Koliah

Koliah is a human, the adopted daughter of the priest [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]] and one of the leaders of the resistance in [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]]. A shrewd strategist, she moves skillfully among lies and partial truths, and speaks to act, not to impress.

### Appearance

She wears worked leather that hugs her body, the protection of someone who knows the woods: a huntress’s armor. She wore boots for a long time, in the village and in the woods, until she left them at the edge of the forest, in the hollow of an oak, on the day when, in [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], a sole print became an [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] footprint; since then she goes barefoot, and it is for this trait that [[_wiki_en/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Merari]] belittles her, reducing her to a barefoot woman who smells of sweat and forest.

### Role

She leads among the rebels through tactical clarity: she assesses threats without dramatizing them and sustains the group’s hope as though giving an order. She fights with bow and arrow and short blades. She is [[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]]’s lover, in a bond that survives trauma and imperial pressure.

### Trauma

When she was young, she suffered at the hands of [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]], the chief of the guard of Niedellin. The episode marks her history and underpins much of her strategic hardness.

### Relationships

She and her father, Abramis, share deep respect and have different approaches: she often cuts him off to be more direct. The recognition she offers those around her comes without romanticism, measured by what each chooses to preserve in the face of the threat.
