# Nyssira Avaroth

A [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] of House Avaroth, a member of the imperial nobility, Nyssira Avaroth is the mother of [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]]. She currently waits in [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]]. She is described as power dressed in black silk.

### History

Her decision to send her daughter to [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], persuaded by Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], who argued that Nidzina needed to taste true iron, led to brutal training and the acquisition of spectral magic and the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] language. In [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], she stood alongside Orum-Rahru to negotiate this training with matriarch [[_wiki_en/personagens/belarbreza_vhaskrith\|Belarbreza Vhaskrith]]. The comment by [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]], Nidzina’s uncle, about choices that never cease to surprise suggests a woman whose decisions defy the expectations of the Shadar nobility.

### Relationships

In relation to Nidzina, she is the mother who allowed Drul training to shape her daughter and remains in Khargoth while her daughter operates as a Justiciar. She entrusted her heir’s training to Orum-Rahru, and it was she who led her daughter, still a small child, by the hand to him: among Nidzina’s childhood memories, it is one of the few that remained intact. Vekraal, Nidzina’s uncle, is a relative whose comment hints at familiarity and perhaps veiled disapproval.
