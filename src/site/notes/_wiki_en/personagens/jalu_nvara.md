# Jalú’Nvara

At the time of [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]]’s interrogations, Jalú’Nvara was an old [[_wiki_en/racas/gerut\|Gerut]], a prisoner in the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], and silence was all he had left to offer. He bore the burned seal of [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] on his chest, the house he had served before captivity, and guarded what Orum needed to extract. He would not give it up. The necromancers dug through his memory with spells, the interrogation dragged on day after day, and his mouth would not open in the way they wanted.

### Appearance

Time and imprisonment had diminished him without bringing him down. His skin had the appearance of brittle paper, eroded by the torture and necromancy that interrogated him. His body was thin and wasted by captivity, but his stature remained, sturdy beneath the ruin. Long, graying hair and beard framed a face that no longer had a precise age. He wore only a prisoner’s bare minimum. On his chest, deep in the flesh, the seal of Myrgarde remained as the only insignia he had left, the mark of a life before the cell.

### Origin and Role

He had been a man of Myrgarde, one of the so-called mudfeet, the derogatory nickname those above use for old Geruts. He had survived the fall of the old empire: he had lived in the capital and was on Myrgarde’s walls when they collapsed, and had seen the human banners replaced. It was that time that the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] wanted from him. What remained of that life was a prisoner locked in Ghol’Drokh, with no active affiliation, with nothing to lose except his own word, and that was what made him valuable. Orum wanted him with his tongue intact and his memory reaching as far as it still could, because there were things only Jalú’Nvara knew, and the old man preferred to die with them rather than speak them.

### Method and Voice

Silence was his principal answer. When he spoke, it was to refuse, and refusal was all he consented to voice, meted out with the economy of someone who weighs the price of every sentence. Refusal was his last territory, the only piece of himself that captivity had not managed to take. There was no bravado in it, there was fatigue and stubbornness, the calm of someone who had decided long ago what he would and would not give up.

### Relationships

The relationship that defined him was the one with Orum-Rahru, the interrogator who needed him alive and lucid. Orum brought a bargain instead of iron alone: light, clean water, the silence of the cells in exchange for what the old man guarded, and exploited Jalú’Nvara’s fear of towers and gaps as the first step of the interrogation. Jalú’Nvara listened and remained closed off. Before Orum, he had already passed through [[_wiki_en/personagens/draven-thol\|Draven-Thol]]’s hands in the dungeons, interrogated with a record tablet.
