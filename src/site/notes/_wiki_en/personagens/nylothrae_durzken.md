# Nylothrae Durzken

Nylothrae Durzken is the matriarch of House Durzken and one of the six rulers of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] city of the depths. She goes by the epithet the Threadless Web. She rules from her House-Cathedral in [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], at the end of the thousand-fathom elevator. She does not appear in person on the surface: she makes her presence known through the intermediaries she dispatches and the insignia that circulates in her name.

### Position Among the Rulers

Among the six matriarchs who rule Vor-Ghol, House Durzken occupies a place of its own. It has neither [[_wiki_en/personagens/quavarra_ulvondra\|Quavarra Ulvondra]]’s gold nor [[_wiki_en/personagens/belarbreza_vhaskrith\|Belarbreza Vhaskrith]]’s sting. To the world above, it passes for a safe channel with a clean record: an alliance that demands no explanations.

Those who negotiate with the depths regard her as a safe channel with a clean record. What sustains this position is Nylothrae’s closeness to the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]] and to [[_wiki_en/personagens/jaakurs\|Jaakurs]], greater than that of the other rulers. This bond anchors House Durzken to Vor-Ghol’s religious axis, and not merely its political one.

### Method and Diplomacy

Nylothrae operates through intermediaries and symbols. She maintains a diplomatic channel with [[_wiki_en/locais/xilaverth\|Xilaverth]] and ensures that her designs arrive ahead of her without exposing herself. No words of hers are known.

The insignia of House Durzken marks the matriarch’s ring, an item that distinguishes whoever rules the House and is not lost through carelessness: anyone who found one of these rings in the rubble and returned it to Nylothrae would be making a gesture of good faith.
