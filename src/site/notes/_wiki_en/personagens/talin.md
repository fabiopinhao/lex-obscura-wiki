# Talin

Talin is a young [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriar]] man from [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], a scout for [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/resistencia_de_thal_serin\|the Resistance of Thal’Serin]]. His courage bypasses reason: where others weigh their words, he has already acted, a haste of the heart that makes him useful and exposes him in equal measure.

### Appearance

Thin, with a broad back and quick legs, he has the build of someone made to run ahead and return with news, and the callused hands of a scout. His voice is chatty and soft, spilling out unfiltered, but grows hoarse when fear tightens his throat.

### Origin and Role

An orphan boy from Niedellin, he throws himself into the resistance by the shortest path between indignation and action, and his house without a lock becomes a meeting place for the rebels. When he tried to attack [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]], he was stopped by [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] with a spell of [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], the nameless dread, which saved his life.

### Method and Voice

He is an instinctive scout, quicker with his feet and his tongue than with his judgment. There is no calculation in his bravery, only the raw faith that the right thing must be done now, this very instant, with whatever is at hand.

### Relationships

Devotion to [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] drives him: everything he does that is bravest and most reckless springs from it. [[_wiki_en/personagens/berenice\|Berenice]] chooses him as her protector.
