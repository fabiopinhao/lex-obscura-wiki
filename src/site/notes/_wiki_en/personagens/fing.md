# Fing

Fing is a [[_wiki_en/racas/kobold\|kobold]], a servant of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]]. Yellow-eyed, he lives confined in the monarch’s tower, mutilated and wholly devoted to serving his master.

### Appearance

A small kobold, with scales that feel the cold of the tower’s corridors and yellow eyes always attentive to his master. His tail, which gave him balance and whipped the floor in his haste to serve, had a piece torn off, and the mutilation robbed his movements of their poise.

### Devotion

Fing finds comfort and purpose in servitude. He prepares baths, fetches books, and anticipates his master’s requests before they are spoken. The rare praise he receives comes in terms that reduce him to an object, and after the mutilation, this recognition that once sustained him begins to resonate as a cruel truth: things break, bleed, and are discarded.

### Personal Cosmology

Fing reads the sky as divine language. He interprets the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/noctiluz\|noctiluz]] as a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/manifestacoes_divinas\|manifestation of the gods]]: the pink hue is a rebuke, the cerulean blue is divine weeping, and an eye-shaped tear is a sign that the gods are watching someone.

### Relationships

It was [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]] who tore off a piece of his tail, with the last of his strength and without even seeing him as a person, and kept the piece for himself. With [[_wiki_en/personagens/quaz_har\|Quaz’har]], another servant, he exchanges mutual envy and contempt.
