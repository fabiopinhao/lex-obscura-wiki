# Gaelwyn

Gaelwyn, or Gael, is a veteran Sylvan ranger and druidic channeler, considered the best ranger in [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]]. He fought in the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]] when he was the age the apprentices he trained are today. A devotee of [[_wiki_en/panteao/titania\|Titânia]], he bears the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/marca_das_luas_gemeas\|mark of the twin moons]] and serves [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/resistencia_de_thal_serin\|the Resistance of Thal’Serin]]. He wields the elven bow and druidic magic, and is [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]]’s lover.

### Appearance

He wears light armor, reinforced with living bark leather. His eyes have moss-green irises flecked with golden veins, young emerald in certain light. On his neck he bears the mark of Titânia’s intertwined twin moons, etched with iron and sap, a deliberate scar that glows greenish and that he has sworn never to hide; pulsing seeds, still warm with sap, hang from his belt and necklace.

### Voice

A resigned veteran who still believes in the best, and believes this way because he has already seen the worst. His speech is melancholic, filled with organic imagery (roots, wind, decomposition) and a contempt for imperial “order” that he never expresses openly before the Empire. Among the apprentices, in the refuge, he speaks directly. Nature is the frame for everything he says.

### The Mark of the Forest

Something in Gael’s presence moves the very forest of Thal’Serin: the trees whisper when he passes and the roots draw back around him. It is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] mark that his apprentice [[_wiki_en/personagens/mirin\|Mirin]] noticed even before he acknowledged it, a kind of silent language of the world responding to him.

### Relationships

He is Koliah’s lover and a mentor to the resistance’s new generation of rangers, among them Mirin and [[_wiki_en/personagens/calel\|Calel]], whom he trained. Those he trained owe him their craft, and Mirin, her purpose as well.
