# The Sexton

In a cell in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]] there is an old man whom the jailers call only the Mad Prisoner. He was once the sexton of the [[_wiki_en/locais/jardim_dos_inocentes\|Garden of the Innocents]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], the man who opened the earth for the city’s dead. Today the earth that awaited him is denied him: for nearly three decades he has lived without dying, kept on his feet by a necromancy that prevents him from departing.

### Appearance

Decades of imprisonment without adequate food have reduced him to little more than bones covered in skin. His ribs show beneath his chest, his joints jut out at the corners of his body, his neck narrows between prominent collarbones. His skin clings to his skeleton and still he stands, walks, speaks. It is not the frailty of a sick man near the end. It is the record of a man kept alive against his own flesh, hollowed out by hunger and sustained by a will that is not his own.

### Origin and Role

He dug graves in the Garden of the Innocents when Myrgarde still existed, and saw the city fall in the final siege. Captured, he was taken to Ghol’Drokh and forbidden to die. The necromancy of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]] preserves him as a living archive of Myrgarde before the fall, a memory it uses at its convenience. The old man is at once punishment and archive: the unwilling guardian of a city that no longer exists anywhere except within him, preserved in perpetual delirium. The enchantments that sustain him degrade and need to be renewed from time to time, and magic has wrinkled his memory and, with it, his perception of reality: that he can still form sentences is considered a miracle.

### Method and Voice

His speech is delirium, but not empty madness. It is disordered memory with intact truths, fragments that surface amid the torments and sometimes escape whole, lucid, before sinking back into babbling. Anyone who listens carefully realizes that the old man does not invent: he merely gives back, out of order, things he actually saw. The cycles of his speech always return to the same point of warmth, the son he lost, and from there move on to the rest of the ruined world he carries in his head.

### Relationships

Above all is [[_wiki_en/personagens/ioni\|Ioni]], the dead son whom the old man still sees alive beside him, like a ghost that accompanies him through the cell. He is the only point of light in a world made of torment, and the name to which his voice always returns. To him, the jailers around him are not faces, only shadows of an endless routine; the old man speaks less with them than with the dead whom only he sees.
