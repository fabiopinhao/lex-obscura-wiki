# Borzrun

Borzrun is a [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|Karashim]], leader of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]] caravan, known as the Black Anvil Convoy. With a gray beard tied with copper wire, eyes of washed stone, and a square body hewn for the tunnels, he travels the underground routes around [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] with a miner’s pragmatism and dry humor.

### Role

To avoid the [[_wiki_en/criaturas/dridar\|drídar]] fighting the [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/veio_morto\|the Dead Vein]], Borzrun led his caravan through [[_wiki_en/locais/eixo_de_drenn\|the Axis of Drenn]]. It was during this passage, while crossing paths with [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]]’s group in the tunnels, that [[_wiki_en/personagens/tibaut\|Tibaut]] stole the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/gema-viva\|living gem]] from the caravan with professional precision, under [[_wiki_en/personagens/dribbel_olgund\|Dribbel]]’s watchful eye. Later, he was hired by [[_wiki_en/personagens/nylothrae_durzken\|Nylothrae]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]] for the race to [[_wiki_en/locais/xilaverth\|Xilaverth]].
