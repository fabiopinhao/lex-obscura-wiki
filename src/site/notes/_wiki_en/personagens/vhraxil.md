# Vhraxil

Vhraxil is a young [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] male who guards the Gates of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]], in the service of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/casa_morghast\|House Morghast]], subordinate to Durzken. He carries the delicacy his race imposes on males and a curiosity greater than his post allows. He is [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]]’s only documented emotional touchstone and the male whom [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina]] defended in the Chambers of Trial of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]].

### Appearance

Vhraxil has symmetrical facial features that are unusual among Drul males. When he feels exposed, his ears darken and he straightens his posture. The nickname he bears comes from this appearance: they call him little beetle, in reference to the [[_wiki_en/criaturas/vitraxil\|vitraxil]], a beetle with mosaic elytra that fragment the glow-slime. “Little vitraxil face,” they say of him.

### Origin and Post

Vhraxil comes from the lower forges of Vor-Ghol, which he survived, and today holds a position of trust at the Gates of Ghilanna, the threshold of House Morghast. He obeys the rules of the Drul matrons zealously and is considered dedicated, competent, and attentive. His curiosity, however, slips out in a question: he hesitates, rephrases, and still ends up asking what is not his place to ask.

### Method and Voice

Vhraxil acts through deference. The delicacy he shows is genuine, without calculation, and that is precisely why Syndrith finds it irritating. He serves with zealous care. Once the question is asked and the reprimand received, his ears darken and droop, and he straightens his posture to regain his poise.

### Relationships

Syndrith has known him since childhood and calls him little beetle. Years earlier, when he was still the young male from the forges, the gaze of Nidzina, then an apprentice in the Chambers of Trial, settled on him, and he would reciprocate when he thought no one was watching. There [[_wiki_en/personagens/zarae\|Zarae]] wounded him for sport, driving her dagger into his wrist, between the bones of his hand, and Nidzina stepped in and protested the act. One fáscia later, still on the same descent, Zarae wounded him again: the same young man, now with a pretext (his posture had been wrong during inspection and he had smiled at the wrong moment), and the ceremonial dagger in the same wrist, one finger above the cut that had not yet closed. It was this repeated act, the second wound, that Nidzina called cowardice, in a gesture that challenged Zarae and Vor-Ghol itself, and from this came the duel. The episode was the spark of Nidzina’s break with the Druls. Years later, Vhraxil reappeared alive at the Gates of Ghilanna.
