# Zanzer

Zanzer, known as the Purple Poison, was a councilor on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] before falling into disgrace. He maintained ties with the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]] and was involved in the illegal trafficking of elves. Today he is a prisoner, held in the [[_wiki_en/locais/coracao_oco\|Hollow Heart]] of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]].

### Fall

[[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] captured Zanzer red-handed in the previous [[_wiki_en/conceitos/varnum\|Varnum]]. The operation came at a cost: the others involved in the trafficking escaped, and Zanzer himself nearly dragged her down with him, leaving her with a physical mark she still bears. No councilor had fallen that way before him.

### Captivity

In the cells of the Hollow Heart, [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] recognized him during an inspection of the dungeons and taunted him with formal sarcasm, observing that his accommodations already suited his new position. Nidzina began using him as an example and a threat against other operatives of the Serpent: surrender, or the same fate. She treats him as a living link between the Purple Serpent and the inside of the Council, although the existence and extent of this embedded network remain an open question under investigation.
