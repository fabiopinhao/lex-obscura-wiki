# Mur

Mur is a veteran [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] in [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]]’s guard, the muscle of a troop he sums up in a single formula: Mur breaks, [[_wiki_en/personagens/grub\|Grub]] cuts, [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]] executes. He talks more than Grub, from whom he is inseparable, philosophizes clumsily, and carries a collection of stones that he rummages through, turns between his fingers, gathers wherever he finds them, and stacks when there is nothing to break.

### Appearance

He has a broad torso, covered in scars that tell of old campaigns. He wields a club weighing half a quintal, forged to break walls, and the weapon is almost his signature.

### Role

He serves in Milor’s guard as a shock force, loyal to Grothar and the boss. He worships [[_wiki/panteao/thurgash\|Thûrgash]], the god of orc berserkers, and aspires to one day attain the bond ([[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]]) and the battle frenzy that Grothar already possesses; he is not yet one of the bonded.

### Voice

Mur speaks in short, exclamatory sentences, without flourishes, all full of concrete names and actions. He is enthusiastic, more talkative than Grub, and his clumsy philosophy spills out in direct comments on complicated things that he instinctively simplifies. He addresses everyone as tu, always with the wrong verb conjugation, and calls his superior boss. The stones he collects serve as an anchor: when he lacks a word for what he feels, he turns to a stone, describes the stone, and the feeling stays there, outside him, in something that can be held in the hand.

He likes an audience and telling jokes. He laughs at his own jokes before anyone else does, and many of them are not funny; his humor is directed at whoever is nearby, whether they understand it or not. He seals the joke with a friendly blow to his companion’s arm.

### The Obsession

Mur harbors an obsessive urge concerning elves, especially [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]]: a deep racial hatred that mingles with the desire to humiliate and rape and manifests as a fixation that is both sexualized and violent. It is the trait that distinguishes him from the other orcs in the guard, and also his greatest tactical vulnerability: in the presence of elven prisoners, the obsession can compromise discipline. For this reason, Grothar tolerates him but keeps him under watch.

### Relationships

With Grub he forms a duo with a logic of its own, two who debate kill counts as though checking a bill. Inseparability is the rule: wherever one goes, the other goes. Grothar reins them in without explicit judgment, tolerating Mur’s excesses while keeping them under watch. His authority weighs on Mur without the need for a harsh word.
