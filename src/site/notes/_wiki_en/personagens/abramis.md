# Abramis

Abramis is the priest of [[_wiki/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]] and spiritual leader of the Pindoriares. He lives among those he protects: as a precaution in light of the Justiciar’s arrival, he sleeps in the barn with the peasants and the children. He raised [[_wiki/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] as his daughter. In Niedellin’s resistance, he is the figure of moral leadership.

### Appearance

Older than one expects at first glance. His hair is white as the ashes of burned temples, and his face bears the marks of vigils and losses. He has the dignity of someone who has already lost everything except the right to remain standing.

### Role

He mediates between the people and Lord [[_wiki/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Merari]], maintaining a fragile balance under dark rule. He rarely confronts: he interprets, names, and contextualizes so that others can decide, and he reads the power structure dispassionately, recognizing in a Justiciar such as [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] a judge of the Empire and a direct instrument of the monarch’s will. His leadership is discreet: he credits himself only with lighting the first candle, and the people with choosing not to let it go out.

### Magic

He practices a quiet Alabastrine faith, Sancta reverence with his hands over his heart, and commands the white magic of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ordem_de_azazel\|the Order of Azazel]]. With the Canticle of Restoration, of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]] school, he cleanses corruption and heals, and it was he who healed Milorsevogh Velshard of a dark corruption. In the people’s speech, this same magic is white light, in the register that calls druidic magic green light, and it is in these terms that Koliah offers her father’s healing to the [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], an offer that leads the Justiciar to the chapel. He also performed the Consecration, a rite of territorial exorcism of the [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] school, at a severe physical cost: the effort drained his essence, and he collapsed exhausted, but alive.

### Relationships

In Koliah, his adopted daughter, he has the voice that frequently interrupts him to be more direct, and he accepts this: he recognizes that she sees what he prefers not to see. He sheltered [[_wiki/personagens/levi\|Levi]] and [[_wiki/personagens/berenice\|Berenice]] in the barn, and is the moral origin of both.
