# Noxmoor

Noxmoor, the Strategist of Fortifications, is a Councilor on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], specializing in defenses and military engineering.

### Role on the Council

At the Shadow Council session, Noxmoor rebuts [[_wiki_en/personagens/dessian_broel\|Dessian Broel]]’s proposal to eliminate goblinoids from the fortresses. His argument is tactical and cutting: the [[_wiki_en/racas/goblin\|goblins]] maintain the fortresses’ escape tunnels and traps as sappers, an irreplaceable role; the [[_wiki_en/criaturas/ogro\|ogres]] carry what twenty [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] cannot and move catapults up to the walls. He closes his reply with two blows of disdain: the catapults are moved neither by the numbers on Dessian’s parchments nor by the relics of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]] in his gallery. Noxmoor’s reasoning concerns infrastructure and logistics, not racial purity or raw economics.
