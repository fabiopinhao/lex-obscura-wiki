# Ilúvrae

Ilúvrae is an [[_wiki_en/racas/elarino\|Elarin]] healer captured during the fall of [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]] and held prisoner in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]]. She possesses the healing magic particular to her people and is known among the jailers as “the tearful elf”.

### Appearance

She has wavy hair and an air of fragile dignity. She cries constantly, and the tears keep her elven skin hydrated.

### Origins and Captivity

She was taken during the fall of Elenir, city of the Elarin, and brought to Mor’Severith, where she remains confined. The chains that bind her are loose enough to allow her to move around the cell, but never to escape.

### Magic

She commands Elarin healing, particular to her people, and practices it within the cell.

### Relationships

She shares her confinement with [[_wiki_en/personagens/auren\|Auren]], whom she provides with healing care. The two care for one another under the weight of imprisonment.
