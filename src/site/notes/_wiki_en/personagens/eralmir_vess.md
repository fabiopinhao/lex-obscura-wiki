# Eralmir Vess

Eralmir Vess is the steward and scribe who keeps the books of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]]. He is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] of method before he is a man of any faith: he serves power while power is useful to him, and was spared for that same usefulness when the other Shadar stewards were killed.

### Appearance

He is a Shadar of meticulous appearance, thin, with an upright posture and measured gestures. He wears a black steward’s tunic cut with a tailor’s precision, fitted to his body, with the lesser coat of arms of the Cult embroidered in silver thread on black silk at the left shoulder. His hands are well tended, a scribe’s hands, with clean nails and slender wrists, and silver rings bearing school glyphs gleam on them, a reminder of his years of study in the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]]; on his right ring finger, he wears an iron ring bearing a shadow patron’s inscription. His face is pale. His voice cracks into a high pitch when he finds himself cornered, and it is at that moment that all the composure of his office abandons him.

### Origins and Role

Eralmir learned his place between ink and stone. He studied in the Tower of Khargoth, where he received the silver rings and the discipline that shaped his hand, and came down from those studies to the role of steward, the man who counts, records, and administers on behalf of whoever commands. In Niedellin, he serves under the provincial lord [[_wiki_en/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Merari d’Khunvel]]. The village’s other Shadar stewards were killed; Eralmir remained, and remained because he was useful: a scribe who knows every account and every record of a city is worth more alive than silenced.

### Method and Voice

Eralmir is precision personified. He thinks in records and exact accounts, and is therefore meticulous and bureaucratic in times of order. What he knows how to do, he does with a goldsmith’s rigor; but the image of a servile and harmless functionary is, in part, a mask for survival. Under threat, this facade gives way to a cold pragmatism, capable of quick decisions when survival demands it. When the accounts do not balance and the world demands an answer that does not fit in a book, his composure unravels and his voice rises to a high pitch.

### Relationships

His role binds Eralmir to the two poles of local government: he answers to Lord Merari, in whose name he counts and records, and interacts with [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]], the chief of the guard, on whom the order his books assume depends. Between the two, he does what scribes do: records, keeps silent, and survives.
