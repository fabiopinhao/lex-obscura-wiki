# Khreek Fine-Thread

Khreek Fine-Thread, or simply Khreek, is a small [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derro]] from the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/khelg_dur\|Khelg’Dur]] caravan, one of those who make a living crossing the darkness of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]] and coming back in one piece. Talkative to the point of compulsion, he pours out words like someone who cannot stop breathing, and some suspect that his very name is an invention of the moment, stitched together to escape some trouble.

### Appearance

He is diminutive even for a Derro, a small body that shrinks still further when the light grows strong. His hands are stained gray, dyed by the toll ink that only reveals its glow under the tunnels’ glow-slime, a mark of his trade etched into his very skin. His eyes are restless, always ahead, racing beyond what his mouth can reach; when he is afraid, his eyelids flutter ceaselessly and his speech comes tumbling out, syllable over syllable, never finding an end.

### Origin and Trade

He belongs to Khelg’Dur and knows the Depths as few others do. His work is to read what the underground writes for those who know how to see: the shoring notices, which say where the rock holds and where it gives way, and the traffic markings, which indicate which passage the cargo can travel through safely. He deciphers the signs scratched into the passage walls and the notches in the stone doorframes, reads the toll ink that seals each crossing, and checks the bale count against what the notch declares. In a place where a misread ceiling buries an entire caravan, this knowledge is worth gold, and any convoy descending into Vor-Ghol would do well to bring someone capable of deciphering it.

### Voice

Talkativeness is Khreek’s substance before it is a flaw. He talks because the silence of the dark weighs heavily, talks to avoid hearing what creaks in the rock, talks because his tongue runs ahead of his judgment and never looks back. He is a lesser echo of [[_wiki_en/personagens/dribbel_olgund\|Dribbel]], the same breed of words stumbling over one another, only without the spark of roguishness, more plea than cleverness. When fear tightens its grip, the flow does not cease: it quickens, tangles, overflows, as though each additional sentence were another instant of borrowed life.

### Relationships

[[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith Morghast]] takes him as a hostage and guarantor, a pawn in a negotiation in which he is worth less for what he can read in the passages than for what he guarantees: the delivery of a shipment into the hands of [[_wiki_en/locais/xilaverth\|Xilaverth]]. The toll ink on his hands and his knowledge of the tunnels, which once earned him a place in any caravan, now serve only as collateral until the shipment changes hands.
