# Maraxus

Maraxus is the chief of the guard of [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], a position he holds alongside that of steward to Lord [[_wiki_en/personagens/merari_d_khunvel\|Merari d’Khunvel]]. He is a man of humble origins who rose to command the armed forces of a village in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]] without having any birthright to that position. Under Merari, the village’s terror is concentrated in him, and his actual power grows through accumulation, year after year, beyond what any title would grant him.

### Appearance

He has the tanned skin of someone who has lived under the sun and a physique marked by brute strength. He displays an exotic curved blade as a trophy, and in his quarters he amasses weapons, piece upon piece, in an arsenal that speaks for him before he needs to speak.

### Origin

He comes from [[_wiki_en/locais/karanthia\|Karanthia]], a poor province on the southeastern coast, and therefore bears the derogatory term “Pindoriar” that the Empire reserves for people of his class. His position is irregular: the Cult’s hierarchy reserves armed command for the children of the shadows, and a human of humble birth at the head of the guard is an anomaly that the structure tolerates as long as it suits its purposes. Merari’s accomplice, he has in practice become the instrument that sustains the lord’s dominion over Niedellin: the obedience of the orc guard rests in his hands.

### Method and Voice

He speaks less than his presence suggests. The implicit threat outweighs any statement, and he intimidates with his gaze before resorting to words, with the confidence of someone accustomed to being obeyed even without formal authority. When he opens his mouth, it is to propose an exchange, never to yield: he negotiates even when he loses, and he exacts a price for what he offers. He has a firmly established reputation as a tormentor, and the village takes it seriously.

### Relationships

Merari is the lord to whom he answers on paper, but the balance has reversed in practice: it was by controlling what reached his lord’s ears that Maraxus rose from a mere guard to the de facto power. [[_wiki_en/personagens/koliah\|Koliah]] carries the trauma he inflicted on her when she was still young.
