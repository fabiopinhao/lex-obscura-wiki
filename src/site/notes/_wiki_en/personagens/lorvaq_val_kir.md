# Lorvaq Val’Kir

Lorvaq Val’Kir is a [[_wiki_en/racas/frenim\|Frenim]], a sidereal human from [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]], and a formidable mystic. He is a calculating man who trusts his own calculations, given to plans whose grandeur serves as a substitute for real grandeur.

### Appearance

He is a slender and thin Frenim, with yellowish-green skin beneath whose surface bluish veins pulse. His eyes are horizontal slits of red, liquid flames that move slowly; his face is elongated, and translucent veils float around him without wind.

### Origin and Role

He comes from Neranthis, land of the Frenim, the sidereal humans. A mystic of notable skill, he practices the art of the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal plane]] and of the veil that separates the planes.

### Magic

Lorvaq is a mystic whose art is tied to the ethereal plane and the veil that separates the planes. He is a master of the craft of manipulating this subtle fabric: the same art that keeps hidden what must remain invisible, such as the ethereal veil that conceals his servant.

### Relationships

[[_wiki_en/personagens/babua\|Babua]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidan]] servant, accompanies him hidden beneath the ethereal veil that Lorvaq weaves.
