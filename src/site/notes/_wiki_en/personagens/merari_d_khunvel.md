# Merari d’Khunvel

Merari d’Khunvel is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] and the shadow lord assigned to administer [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]] and its surroundings, including the edges of [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]]. Less sadistic in temperament than other provincial lords of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], he prefers indirect control to open violence and governs as an administrator before governing as an executioner.

### Appearance

He dresses with the formality of the court: imperial black silk with fine purple lines embroidered along the folds, in a complex pattern that reveals the work of a master craftsman, each pleat falling into exactly the right place. His gray hair is tied in an elaborate low knot, in the style of the old court. The wrinkles of middle age only accentuate a dignity cultivated over decades at court.

### Administration

Under his rule, Niedellin maintains an anomalous prosperity for a province under shadow rule. Merari tolerates [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]]’s chapel as a social buffer and allows luminous symbols to survive without destruction, calculating that the Pindoriars yield more under a predictable routine. Of the Shadar stewards themselves, only [[_wiki_en/personagens/eralmir_vess\|Eralmir Vess]] remained, for his usefulness. Part of Merari’s leniency rests on a narrative instilled in him by [[_wiki_en/personagens/maraxus\|Maraxus]], his councilor, on whom he has developed a growing dependence without realizing that the flow of information around him is controlled by other hands.

According to this narrative, an ancestral creature sleeps in the depths of Thal’Serin, and only the druids’ rites keep it asleep; without them, Niedellin would be devoured. Hence Merari’s greatest fear, that “the ritual of Thal’Serin” might cease, and hence the order to end the persecution of the druids and allow the rites in the forest, against the open resistance of the stewards themselves. In Niedellin, the expression refers to the ordinary observance the druids practice among the trees, without identifying any single rite.

Apart from Maraxus, a human in command of the guard, Niedellin’s stewards were all Shadars, and the village saw the Shadar presence dwindle one by one over the years, while Maraxus grew in the space they left vacant.

By Merari’s order, the village children sleep segregated in barns set aside for them, never fewer than fifteen under each roof, on the pretext of strengthening their character, and orcs are quartered beside the children’s barns to keep watch. The communal barn, where the peasants sleep, falls outside this watch: on the night [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]]’s retinue arrived, the adults took the children there, and the orcs stayed close to the small barns.

### Voice

Merari speaks as an administrator. He constructs arguments based on procedure and resorts to legal instruments when direct power eludes him. Beneath the rhetoric of cold reasoning runs the fragility of someone who has always depended on others to sustain his own authority.
