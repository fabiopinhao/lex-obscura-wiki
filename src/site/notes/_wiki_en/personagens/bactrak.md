# Bactrak

Bactrak was a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] and a Templar of [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]]. His death is the oldest moral wound of [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], the man who survives because he fell.

### Origin

When Orum was chosen to serve Vozrador, there was already someone who had entered into a pact before him: Bactrak. The condition imposed was simple: only one of the two would emerge alive and bound by a pact. The two fought in the temple’s basalt halls, and Bactrak fell. His body would belong to the Temple, and his bones would be the reminder of his devotion. Bactrak’s death was the price Orum paid to become the god’s only Templar.

### Memory and Return

Bactrak reappears in two forms. During a meditative descent in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]], he returns as a confessed memory: the duel in which one had to die so that the other could remain. Later, during the invasion of the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_vozrador\|temple of Vozrador]], he returns as a skeleton in Templar armor, reanimated by [[_wiki_en/personagens/qaelun\|Qaelun]]’s still crude necromancy. To Orum, that was the armor of the companion who had died for him: he recognized in it the same weight, angle, and hesitation, a sign that, for him, the temple had never ceased to be inhabited by the dead he had known.
