# Wijmarv

Wijmarv is a [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]] shaman, a tiefling, a scholar of [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconids]], dragons, and [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminars]], and the author of a treatise on the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]]. A fugitive from a Drakhonin clan that he served and betrayed, he is believed to be sheltering in [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]].

### Origin and Role

Before fleeing, Wijmarv served as a sorcerer for a Drakhonin clan, and he is credited with closing, or failing to close, the tunnels in the west of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. When passage underground ceased to be safe, it was his name that [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]] brought up, and Wijmarv fled back to Varnak. [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]] calls him *mraz’kor*, “Heart of Ash”, an archaic Varnakian term for someone who has lost the ability to produce fire: in the warrior culture of the mountain ranges, anyone who bears it is considered a walking corpse, for their existence no longer holds the warmth of honor. Thrax hates him for two distinct reasons: his failure with the clan and the threat posed by the treatise, which delegitimizes the [[_wiki_en/linguas/draconico\|Draconic]] founding myth by asserting that the Fendris wounds Draconids as much as dragons.

The treatise is the work that defines him. In it Wijmarv describes a limitation of the Fendris: the blade recognizes no oath, where there is a pact there is blindness, and it responds only to the hand that owes nothing. The reading is technical and directed against the very caste that produced him as a weapon. The text reached the heart of the Tower when [[_wiki_en/personagens/odran\|Odran]] studied it and read it to [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], bringing Varnakian doctrine to the center of power.

### Relationships

Yzmareth and Thrax were his employers in the clan. Odran is the scholar who brought the treatise into the Tower, and through this channel Wijmarv’s theory reached Arothistar.
