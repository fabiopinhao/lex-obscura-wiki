# Sir Grimald

The most celebrated [[_wiki_en/racas/alvarar\|Álvarar]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]] is Sir Grimald, the Golden Blade: senior paladin of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ordem_de_azazel\|Order of Azazel]], a luminous faith that lives in hiding, and guardian of the Temple’s thresholds, judge of those who ask to enter.

### Appearance

His portrait is entirely golden: golden armor and, in his eyes, the same gleam of heated metal. He is said to have a firm composure before ordinary recruits, which gives way to trembling before certain presences.

### Origin and Role

The Order of Azazel is a devotion that must hide to survive. Grimald’s name is associated with the protection of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/igreja_de_sao_lucian\|Church of Saint Lucian]] in Myrgarde, the judgment of who deserves the mantle of a paladin and who must be sent away, and a maxim: some doors remain closed for a reason.

### Method and Voice

His hallmark is a perception that reaches what is out of sight and, before certain aspirants, the presence of something that should not be there condensed into a single-sentence verdict: you carry something that does not belong here. It is the detection of the supernatural expressed as moral judgment, with an overwhelming authority shot through with poorly concealed fear.

### Relationships

In the Order of Azazel and the Church of Saint Lucian, his is the figure of the guardian and instrument willing to bar the threshold against anything that does not belong.
