# Harlen Vriss

A [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] councilor on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], Harlen Vriss is the economic voice in the deliberations of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. He is thin and crooked-toothed, his appearance a reflection of a sharp and calculating temperament.

### Role on the Council

At the session of the Shadow Council, during the debate on migrations and provisioning, Vriss mocks the cost of maintaining [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]]: feeding an orc, he argues, costs more than feeding three men and more than feeding five elves, and yields half the discipline. As he speaks, his bony fingers drum out the arithmetic on the table, counting how many rations would be saved with each throat cut.
