# Tashkur

Tashkur is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] and [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|justiciar]] with jurisdiction over the eastern border, based in [[_wiki_en/locais/qadesh\|Qadesh]], a vassal city-state nestled in the desert between [[_wiki_en/locais/reinkard\|Reinkard]] and [[_wiki_en/locais/neranthis\|Neranthis]].

### Jurisdiction

Qadesh’s functional autonomy is the very subject of his work: the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] accepts tribute and does not ask what passes through the gates, so the cargo reaches the plateau with an imperial seal and its provenance laundered along the way. The internal end of this business falls to Tashkur. He investigates who owns the lunar steel before it belongs to no one, who in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Cult]] signs the seal, and which officers of the Empire charge to let the cargo go uninspected.

The distance is part of the difficulty: weeks of travel by road separate [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] from the dry port, and his jurisdiction depends on records the city has little interest in opening. The same distance excludes him from any case underground, and the case underway on the border requires him to head to Qadesh.

### Origin

He bears no house name. Among the seven living justiciars, he belongs to the group that rose without the support of a lineage, under the order’s stated standard: no birth guarantees the black mantle. He was trained by [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]].
