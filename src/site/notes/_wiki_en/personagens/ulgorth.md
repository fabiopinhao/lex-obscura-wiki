# Ulgorth

Ulgorth is an elderly [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]], overseer of the orc jailers of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]]. He directs and monitors movement along the fortress’s ramps and stairs, a duty he has performed for decades.

### Appearance

He stands one fathom tall, two meters. Dirty-green skin, yellowed tusks. His body bears scars in overlapping layers, among them a whitish mark on his arm. His breath is strong enough to make those who approach recoil.

### Function

Overseer of Ghol’Drokh’s orc jailers for decades. He works on the fortress’s internal ramps and stairs, where he directs, monitors, and maintains movement. His authority over the corridors derives from his length of service.

### Voice and Method

Nearly mute. He receives [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Justiciar Orum]] with a single grunt of recognition, “Justiciar,” and signals deference through gesture: fist to chest, chin lowered. He resorts to neither ceremonial greeting nor speech. His obedience is physical and trained through decades of service.

### Relationships

Ulgorth’s central relationship is with Ghol’Drokh and the imperial order that the fortress administers. He serves as long as he is useful; he is a disposable servant of a machine that uses and eliminates its own overseers. In Orum’s presence, he maintains the protocol of someone who has learned everyone’s place; the Justiciar keeps his distance.
