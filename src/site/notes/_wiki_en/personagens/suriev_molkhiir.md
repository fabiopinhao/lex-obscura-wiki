# Suriev Molkhiir

Suriev Molkhiir is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] councilor of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], with the functional title of the Servant of Truth. On the Council, he upholds faith and devotion as pillars of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]’s power against the pragmatist councilors, who prioritize material resources over religious doctrine.

### Appearance

He wears the mantle that shifts between black and purple, the colors of the Council. His eyes are black as wells, pure absences, two pits filled with the void between the stars. His fingers leave trails of shadow in the air. His voice is deep and cavernous, and it echoes.

### Role on the Council

At the session of the Shadow Council, Suriev argues that pacts do not fail and that power does not need alchemy, only faith and devotion. He upholds religious doctrine against the pragmatists, for whom material resources carry more weight than belief. The statement “Arothistar guides the faithful” is part of this public conviction, professed before the Council.

### Voice

Suriev’s speech is formal, devout, and institutional. He defends his own convictions through doctrinal argument, not fervor, and treats faith as an instrument of power and not as rapture.
