# Felaren

Felaren is a veteran ranger of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/resistencia_de_thal_serin\|the Resistance of Thal’Serin]] and the only one in the refuge who served in the elven armies before the fall. A [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvan]], he keeps his left sleeve folded and fastened at the shoulder, and carries his longbow on his back. He shoots with his remaining arm: he nocks the arrow, secures the string in a leather catch he holds between his teeth, and draws with his neck, his whole body doing the work of two arms. The wars left him with scars and the habit of not speaking of them.

### Role

He is the refuge’s military memory. He reads the presence of the berserkers as a deliberate message: they are sent when someone higher up wants to make an example, so that fear arrives before they do. It is to him that [[_wiki_en/personagens/peredur\|Peredur]] turns to prepare the thornseeds in the face of the threat.

Before the apprentices, he serves the opposite function from the elder. He appears without being heard approaching and asks aloud why the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]] spare some forests, whether out of cowardice or by agreement. He leaves before the answer, leaving behind the observation that there are hidden truths and that the younger ones know well how this works, enough to justify their distrust. He then joins the young druids at the spring, among whom circulates the suspicion that the elders keep more than they protect.

### Relationships

He lives alongside Peredur without deference: he is the only one who confronts the elder in public without raising his voice, and he does so before the apprentices. For [[_wiki_en/personagens/mirin\|Mirin]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/calel\|Calel]], he represents veteran experience, and Calel cites his judgments as support, counting him among those who ask questions without getting answers.
