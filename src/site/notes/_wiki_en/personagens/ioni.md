# Ioni

Ioni is a human boy from [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], the son of [[_wiki_en/personagens/coveiro-mor\|the Sexton]] of the city. He was born with an intellectual disability and, after his death, was buried in the [[_wiki_en/locais/jardim_dos_inocentes\|Garden of the Innocents]], the section of the cemetery reserved for children.

### Appearance

A boy with eyes that were always slightly misaligned, who rocked from one foot to the other when he became nervous. He stuttered when he spoke, the words stumbling over one another, and a full smile came easily to him.

### Role

He experienced a moment of heroism in a game of [[_wiki_en/conceitos/aroada\|Aroada]], when he scored a decisive goal on a penalty shot. Until the end, he talked about that day when he was a hero. He died of fever in the winter following the game, in a Myrgarde still at peace. Years later, he reappears as a kind of ghost in the delirium of his father, then a prisoner in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_drokh\|Ghol’Drokh]], who summons him and sees him emerge through the crack in the cell. Ioni remains as an echo of a Myrgarde that no longer exists.
