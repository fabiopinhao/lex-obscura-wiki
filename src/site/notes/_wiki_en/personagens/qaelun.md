# Qaelun

Qaelun is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] necromancer of the Ossuary of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], the city’s institution of necromancers, and operated in the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_vozrador\|temple of Vozrador]]. He bore a scar on his throat.

### Appearance

He was thin to the point of transparency and had no eyelids: he paid them as a tithe. His fingers bore traces of verdigris, and he wore black clothes with crimson runes.

### Practices

He used the [[_wiki_en/magia/circulo_de_sutura\|Circle of Suture]], a necromantic heptagram, to animate the dead. His necromancy derived from a pact with [[_wiki_en/panteao/naelkur\|Naelkur]], the devil of [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] who reaps legions of undead. He used the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/luto_de_nyssis\|Mourning of Nyssis]], an antimagic alchemical substance, against the Justiciar investigating him, blowing it through a copper tube; he controlled the Circle with crystal-rings. He was famous for raising many dead at once, few of them any good, and for making up with alchemicals (vitriols, resins, salts) for what he lacked in finesse; he called this a method. He spoke curtly and treated his adversary like a piece on a workbench.

### End

The veteran Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]]’s investigation of the temple ended with Qaelun’s elimination.

### Relationships

He was an employer of [[_wiki_en/personagens/skanivik\|Skanivik]], who set fire to his alchemical supplies during a mission for Orum-Rahru.
