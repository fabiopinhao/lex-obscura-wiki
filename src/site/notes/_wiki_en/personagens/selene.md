# Selene

Selene was a woman of southern origin who lived in [[_wiki_en/locais/valdris\|Valdris]]. Brought from elsewhere by her husband, a fisherman from the city, she was thin and brittle, the kind the angels make to decorate, not to last. She kept her eyes lowered, turned toward the ground and to the sides, and the curls that the wind of the Austral seemed to want to tear out.

### In Valdris

Her husband, a fisherman from Valdris, loved her with an urgency that silenced even her mother-in-law, who saw in her daughter-in-law a woman too fragile for the hard life of the sea. Selene became pregnant during the siege of Valdris, with the gates sealed by the count and her husband called up for war, the promised payment always postponed.

### Death

The pregnancy consumed what the hunger of the siege had spared. Selene could not keep food down: flesh vanished from her cheekbones and shoulders as her belly grew, and the fever would not break. She called for her husband between contractions and survived childbirth, but never recovered: her body seemed drained, feeding her son while she herself dwindled. She faded away before autumn, on a night when the wind was still. Her mother-in-law buried her alone behind the house, where the earth was softer from the damp, without a priest or a gravedigger, because all had become weapons of the count.
