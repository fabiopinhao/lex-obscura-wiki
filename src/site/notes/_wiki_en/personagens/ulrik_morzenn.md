# Ulrik Morzenn

Ulrik Morzenn, the Arcane Dean, is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] councilor of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. He wears blue and crimson robes and displays ten rings of ten colors, one on each phalanx.

He is among the Council’s long-standing members, predating the wave of councilors appointed directly by the monarch. Pragmatic by temperament, he professes allegiance to no faction and cultivates an image of neutrality.

### Stance in the Council

At the session of the Shadow Council, Morzenn defends [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]] against those who want to raze it. He argues that the city is rich in [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] nexuses, the continent’s largest raw source of reagents and a possible key to energy independence from the pacts, while warning that the pacts themselves exact their price. For this reason, he supports sending [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] to Elenir before any irreversible decision, using a technical and measured register, opposed to the military’s destructive impulse.
