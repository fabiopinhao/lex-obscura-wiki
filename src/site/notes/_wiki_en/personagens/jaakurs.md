# Jaakurs

Jaakurs is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] necromancer and ceremonial sorcerer of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Shadow Cult]], a member of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. He wears robes with red runes, wields a staff of bones, and gives off a smell of graveyard earth and old copper. His tone is ceremonial: even politics comes from him with the weight of liturgy.

### Role

He conducted the anointing of [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] in the [[_wiki_en/locais/templo_de_azkaroth\|Temple of Azkaroth]]. At the Shadow Council session, he supported sending [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] to [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]].
