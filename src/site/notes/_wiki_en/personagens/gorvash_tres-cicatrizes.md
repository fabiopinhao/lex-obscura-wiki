# Gorvash Three-Scars

Gorvash Three-Scars is an [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] captain, a veteran of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/koltenmor\|Koltenmor]], serving under General [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] in the Third Eastern Legion of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. Three ragged tracks cross the side of his skull like ancient claw marks and have cost him part of his face.

### Role

He appears at the ceremony marking Vekraal’s promotion to general of the Third Eastern Legion, in the courtyard of the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]]. There he stands beyond the ritual circle, beside [[_wiki_en/personagens/hugur_comedor-de-ferro\|Hugur Iron-Eater]], apart from the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lieutenants. The presence of orc captains in a space of arcane knowledge prompted an objection from [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], for whom “inferior races have no place near the towers of arcane knowledge”. In response to the objection, Gorvash narrows his eyes and fixes them on Orum. Gorvash and Hugur are the concrete reason for this friction, without speaking: they serve as markers of Vekraal’s policy, which places orcs where Shadar tradition excludes them. Whether the general does so out of military pragmatism, to cultivate personal loyalty, or as a deliberate provocation, the record does not say.
