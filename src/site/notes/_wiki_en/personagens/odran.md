# Odran

Odran is a [[_wiki_en/racas/minotauro\|minotaur]] in the service of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_severith\|Mor’Severith]], where he guards the fortress’s library and prisoners. He belongs to a nearly extinct race that most peoples regard as legend. He has decades of experience in the post and describes himself as a civilized administrator.

### Appearance

His horns nearly scrape the doorframes when he passes through. His muscles strain beneath his dark brown fur, and a collar of steel chains encircles his neck. His surprisingly gentle eyes contrast with his build.

### Origin and Role

As the fortress’s administrator, Odran tends to the library and the prisoners in his care, whom he calls involuntary guests. He brings them their meals with meticulous caution and knows in detail the arcane mechanism of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/grilhoes_de_contencao_arcana\|shackles of arcane containment]] that bind them, having already tried to break them out of empirical curiosity. He is also an intellectual by habit: he reads [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakian]] chronicles, campaign accounts, and merchants’ journals, and maintains his erudition as a form of order in an environment of chaos.

### Method and Voice

Odran philosophizes about the nature of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/verbum_arkhon_codex\|Verbum Arkhon]] and defends it as a creed, out of conviction. To him, the Lord of the Tower never goes back on his word, and this inflexibility serves as the foundation of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]. His erudition gives him his arguments, and he uses them with the restraint of someone who prefers order to spectacle.

When the subject touches on his own nearly extinct species, or when someone mentions horns, his hand rises and his fingers brush the curve of his horn, as though confirming his own materiality. The same gesture has a second version, using the whole hand in a caress, reserved for those watching him: in this form it becomes a conversational weapon.

### Relationships

He serves Arothistar out of intellectual loyalty. He tends to the prisoner [[_wiki_en/personagens/thrax\|Thrax]] with meticulous attention, even though Thrax’s draconic philosophy clashes head-on with that of the Verbum. In [[_wiki_en/personagens/fing\|Fing]] he has a fellow in servitude, who seeks him out to inform him of the master’s plans.
