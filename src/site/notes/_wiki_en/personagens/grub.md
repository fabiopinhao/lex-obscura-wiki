# Grub

Grub is a veteran [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] in [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]]’s guard, a dim-witted, gluttonous heavyweight who marches alongside his inseparable companion [[_wiki_en/personagens/mur\|Mur]] and answers to the chief of the guard, [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]]. Where Mur has a tongue and initiative, Grub has flesh, an axe, and silence: he speaks more when someone prods him than of his own accord, and what he says is usually so literal that no one knows whether it is a joke.

### Appearance

He carries the protruding belly of someone who never refuses a meal and the build of someone who has survived many. Of average height for an orc, about one meter ninety centimeters, he falls between Mur’s larger build and Grothar’s smaller one, but is the heaviest of the three: his bulk settles on his abdomen. He wields two matching axes too heavy for human hands, iron that only an orc can lift without trembling. Despite having the full physique of an adult, he is young for his species, only twelve years old. Orcs reach physical maturity at around ten, and the signs of his age are still readily apparent: his white, sharp tusks, his greenish skin with its full, saturated tone. On his chest he bears the scar of the arrow that wounded him in the ambush: the healing closed the muscle and did not erase the mark, which he still scratches.

### Origin and role

A veteran of Milor’s guard, Grub serves less out of conviction than out of habit and simple loyalty. He follows Grothar, follows Milor, follows Mur, and seeks no abstract reasons in this menu of allegiances. He is a soldier of presence, not of command: his worth lies in what he can withstand and what he can bring down. He venerates [[_wiki_en/panteao/thurgash\|Thûrgash]], the god of orc berserkers, and aspires to one day attain the bond ([[_wiki_en/magia/vinculum_daemonis\|Vinculum Daemonis]]) and the combat powers Grothar already possesses; so far he remains without a bond.

### Voice and method

Reserved to the point of seeming mute beside Mur, Grub responds more than he initiates, and all his concerns fit within the concrete: what there is to eat, whether the axes are sharp, where his companion is. He speaks without an emotional filter, just as he sees things. His dim-wittedness is not an act he puts on for others, but is genuine, and that is why no one can predict what he will say. When there is no skull to split, he snores in a duet of saw and stone with Mur, and the two spend hours in cordial dispute over which of them has killed more.

### Combat

In [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]], Grub was given the work no one else wanted: burning the [[_wiki_en/criaturas/troll\|trolls]] that regenerated faster than they could be cut. He did so with alchemical fire, flasks of [[_wiki_en/criaturas/salamandra\|salamander]] blood that Milor put in his hands, and what kept closing up no longer did. His effectiveness coexists with his literal-minded nature: the same orc who incinerates what refuses to die is the one who debates kill tallies as though counting coins.

### Relationships

With Mur he forms a duo in which the two counterbalance each other, one pointing the way and the other hacking a passage open with his axes. He owes Grothar and Milor unconditional obedience. At some point during the march he was healed by [[_wiki_en/personagens/gaelwyn\|Gaelwyn]]’s elven magic, a debt he does not even recognize, because abstract obligation is not something that gets into Grub’s head. The debt he does recognize is a different one: he credits the healing to Milor, who ordered it, and invokes it by beating his own chest, where the scar itches.
