# Corvina

Corvina, known as Mother Corvina, is a human of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/igreja_de_sao_lucian\|Church of Saint Lucian]] in [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]]. An elderly woman bent under the weight of decades, she survived the fall of the city.

### Appearance

An elderly woman whose back is bent under the weight of decades, she wears the habit of the Church of Saint Lucian. Her body bears the mark of time and of survival in a fallen city.

### Voice

Her tone is pastoral, marked by a survivor’s genuine weariness. Her very survival serves as a form of testimony.
