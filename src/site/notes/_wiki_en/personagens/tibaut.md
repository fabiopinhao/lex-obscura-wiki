# Tibaut

Tibaut is a [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worg]] informant from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], and few know the city as he does. The alleys, the shadows, the intervals between one patrol and the next: everything is mapped behind that face that says what is necessary and omits what is dangerous. He sells what he knows to whoever pays well.

### Appearance

Tall and slender, he moves with the economy of someone who has learned not to waste even a gesture, and there is a predator’s force in him. He wears a black leather overcoat with a deep hood, lined with secret pockets where he keeps blades and poisons within reach of his fingers. The hood plunges his face into shadow and conceals his muzzle, so that, from a distance, no one identifies him as a worg, a convenient disguise in a city that bars worgs from entire districts. When the work calls for steel, he wields the blades of [[_wiki_en/locais/qadesh\|Qadesh]]: the crescent (curved and short), the slender one (straight), and the Patient One (long and enchanted).

### Trade

Tibaut’s trade is information. He maintains a network of contacts spread across Morvgrad and knows the city’s alleys and shadows with the intimacy of someone who has already done enough to lose count. When moving through hostile terrain, he reads his surroundings like a map: he identifies patrols by the cadence of boots, detects the changing of sentinels in stagnant sweat and loosened leather, and sniffs out blind spots by the thick dust that no footprint has disturbed. The ban on worgs in certain districts is old, predating the reign of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]], and Tibaut circumvents it by exploiting the precise window between one patrol’s passage and the other’s return.

### Method and Voice

Tibaut is economical, in steel and in speech. His sentences are short, functional, fashioned to deliver exactly what needs to be delivered and nothing more. There is irony, but it is dry, without fanfare. He is an efficient informant precisely because he knows how to measure silence: he says what is necessary, omits what is dangerous, and his physical restraint is mirrored in his verbal restraint. Faced with a risky question, his answers only grow shorter.

### Relationships

Tibaut is a paid informant for [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]], whom he calls “the best payer of my life”. The relationship is one of mutual utility, but he has fought alongside her for enough winters to read in her gait what others fail to notice. When he follows her at a distance through [[_wiki_en/locais/mor_draconis\|Mor’Draconis]], it is for convenience and for another reason, which he does not name.

He introduces [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]] as a “first cousin on the pack’s mother’s side”, and the formula is a bond of honor: worg and [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnoll]] are distinct races, and the kinship is a pack convention. Even so, Tibaut treats him as family and reins in the gnoll’s impulses when noise could prove costly: on the approach to the gnoll’s old den, he reminded him that the mountain has eyes.
