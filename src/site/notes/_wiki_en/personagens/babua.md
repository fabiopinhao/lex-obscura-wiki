# Babua

Babua is a [[_wiki_en/racas/saag\|Saag]] of the [[_wiki_en/racas/ranidano\|Ranidan]] lineage, a watchman in service to a single lord, the mystic [[_wiki_en/personagens/lorvaq_val_kir\|Lorvaq Val’Kir]]. His post is in the western mountains, in the most remote region of the plateau, far from any trail and inhabited by wyverns. Babua’s abilities complement those of his master.

### The Veil and the Rupture

Babua’s strength lies in skin and water. While he meditates and his skin touches a source of water on the material plane, he can remain for moons in the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/plano_etereo\|ethereal veil]], the Aetherium; behind the veil, he is invisible as long as he does not move. This makes him a sentinel who is difficult to detect.

Added to this gift is a second, more dangerous to those who rely on magic: an enchantment capable of breaking magical bonds. Not even the connections of the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]] can withstand it.
