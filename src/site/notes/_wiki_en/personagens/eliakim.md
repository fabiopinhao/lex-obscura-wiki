# Eliakim

Eliakim is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] who holds the highest seat on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], and bears the epithet Architect of Shadow Diplomacy. A veteran councilor whose reputation rests on erudition and rhetoric, he distinguishes himself by his habit of cutting short another’s argument with a laconic, precise phrase, always exact enough to end the discussion.

### Appearance

No one sees him in person. Eliakim manifests through the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/oculus_morvgradi\|Oculus]], the onyx sphere that projects his face into the air before those he summons. His face is pale, with sharp cheekbones and thin lips. His eyes have the glow of embers that have already gone out but retain enough heat to burn, and his smile, when it comes, has the coldness of ancient ice.

### Role

Within the machinery of the Shadow Council, Eliakim holds the seat that sets the tone of the sessions. His authority comes from economy rather than a raised voice: he says little, and what little he says arrives as a verdict. His reputation as a scholar sustains the weight; his sharp rhetoric does the rest.

### Method and Voice

Eliakim’s instrument is the short phrase. Where others offer lengthy justifications, he responds with the sentence that reduces his interlocutor to the essentials. Do not confuse tactical clemency with justice, he warns when someone presents mildness as a virtue, and the distinction hangs in the air like a ruler. The voice is the substance of the councilor: precise, unadorned, trained to separate what seems noble from what is merely convenient.
