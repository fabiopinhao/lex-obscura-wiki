# Kravex Mordaine

Kravex Mordaine was a historical [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] general of the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/guerras_reinkardicas\|Reinkardic Wars]]. He compiled an extensive tactical manual, the Strategicon, with about three thousand folios.

### The Strategicon

The Strategicon catalogs methods of combat against each known race and creature, with tables of optimal weapons, formations, and magical countermeasures. It is considered canonical, but partly outdated since the Shadar adoption of [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] storage crystals.

### Legacy

Kravex Mordaine’s autograph manuscript rests in the tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khar_morthar\|Khar Morthar]], whose courtyard holds his statue. It occupies an isolated niche at the outer edge of the courtyard, with no stone neighbors, and is smaller than other statues; its face, carved from obsidian, retains the half-open mouth of someone who received news they did not expect and died before realizing that death would come. Mordaine neither fought in the field nor subdued enemies by the blade, and his manual served to win wars that mattered until magi crystals made it outdated.

As required by [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], each [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] read the folios of the Strategicon. The manual, which defined how the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] fought during most of the Reinkardic Wars, is no longer taught in Khar Morthar.
