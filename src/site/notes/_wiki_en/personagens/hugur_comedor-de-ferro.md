# Hugur Iron-Eater

Hugur Iron-Eater is an [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]] captain of the Third Eastern Legion, in the service of [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]]. The epithet comes from his habit of chewing nails, or coins when nothing of little value is available, during idle hours.

### Role

He was present at the ceremony promoting Vekraal to general of the Third Eastern Legion, in the courtyard of the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]]. There he positioned himself beyond the ritual circle, beside [[_wiki_en/personagens/gorvash_tres-cicatrizes\|Gorvash Three-Scars]] and apart from the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lieutenants, in an arrangement that made the tension between the hierarchies present visible. He always appears paired with Gorvash and has no lines. At the ceremony, he stopped chewing during Vekraal’s speech about terror and was the one who broke the silence that followed by chewing again; he stopped once more upon the arrival of [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] and spat a nail onto the ground when Orum declared that inferior races have no place near the towers of arcane knowledge.
