# Nidzina Avaroth

Nidzina Avaroth is a [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] from [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] and the youngest of her time to attain the office. She belongs to House Avaroth, of high noble lineage, which [[_wiki_en/personagens/tibaut\|Tibaut]] says descends directly from the First Aegis. She refuses to let the name define her: in her view, she earned the mantle through her own merit, not through her ancestors.

### Appearance

She has golden eyes, an amber that marks pure Shadar blood, and pale skin. She wears the Justiciar’s [[_wiki_en/artefatos/tegmen\|tegmen]], with a black cuirass, and black leather gauntlets with metal plates. An old scar on her temple is a lasting injury from the “[[_wiki_en/personagens/zanzer\|Zanzer]]” case. When she casts spells, the cost of magic surfaces in her body: black veins beneath the skin, obsidian tears, and an exhaustion that seems to age her.

### Origin

She was [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]]’s pupil from childhood. Her mother, [[_wiki_en/personagens/nyssira_avaroth\|Nyssira Avaroth]], arranged brutal training for her in [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], in the Chambers of Trial of the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] matriarchs of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]]. There, beyond discipline, she came to master the Drul language and shadow magic, an uncommon feat for someone of surface blood. At sixteen, in a duel sanctioned by the matriarch, she killed the Drul [[_wiki_en/personagens/zarae\|Zarae]]: it was her first kill, and she climbed back to the light without looking back.

### Method

A legalistic investigator, she operates strictly according to the Lex: chain of custody, jurisdiction, the arrest decree “In the name of the Lex and of [[_wiki_en/personagens/arothistar\|Arothistar]]”. She turns a scene into a map and reads lies during interrogation, though she frequently acts on her own, outside official channels. There is tension in her method: in the eyes of her ally Tibaut, she uses terror as a tool and not as a pleasure, and walks between justice and revenge trying to pretend they are the same thing; she herself fears that the line between justice and massacre may blur. Beneath the coldness, there is constant self-criticism and an icy authority that intimidates effortlessly. This method is [[_wiki_en/conceitos/khemor\|Khemor]] on a personal scale: she wears the mask to produce fear and discards it afterward.

### History

With Tibaut, she investigates a series of methodical thefts that bleed Morvgrad’s Black Market and recognizes the hand of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]] in the pattern. She forcibly recruits the [[_wiki_en/racas/derro\|Derro]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/dribbel_olgund\|Dribbel]] as a guide and descends through Vor-Ghol to trace the shipments to a clandestine arsenal to the west. Along the way, she faces [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Murans]] possessed by red magic and senses a bodiless presence following the group. At the arsenal, she finds an anomalous runic dagger and declares an arrest in the name of the Lex. She destroys the arsenal and executes [[_wiki_en/personagens/svyarnov\|Svyarnov]], who commands the Serpent’s local operation; the [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]], a client of the operation, escapes by flying away. She decides to keep the dagger as bait.

### Weapons and magic

She carries a [[_wiki_en/artefatos/bombarda_ananica\|dwarven bombard]] forged in [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], with dwarven fire, enchantments from [[_wiki_en/locais/elenir\|Elenir]], and Avaroth blood, a rare weapon on the plateau. She also wields a [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] longsword, with a blade infused by interchangeable crystals that respond to a command word, and the dagger [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] artifact that awakens on its own near loose crystal. Her casting focus is the [[_wiki_en/artefatos/spina_lexis\|Spina Lexis]], a moonstone scepter. She casts Drul shadow magic, of the School of [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] through the rite of [[_wiki_en/panteao/ulravaeth\|Ulravaeth]]: webs that seal tunnels, a curse of blindness, and a predatory Eidolon.

### Relationships

Orum-Rahru has been her master since childhood, and she trusts him above the Council itself: the first thing in Morvgrad that did not seem like a lie to her. Once, without context and without repeating it, he told her that [[_wiki_en/personagens/xarn\|Xarn]] was watching her. Tibaut, a [[_wiki_en/racas/worg\|worg]] informant, is a recurring investigative partner, in a relationship that borders on a friendship she does not acknowledge. She treats [[_wiki_en/personagens/fandegarron\|Fandegarrón]], a combat [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnoll]] in her retinue, as a tool, though she feels the weight of it. Her mother, Nyssira Avaroth, arranged the training that forged her; her uncle [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]] ties her to the nobility by blood, treating her intrusively and without affection.
