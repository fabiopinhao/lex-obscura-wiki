# Marta

Marta was a woman from [[_wiki_en/locais/myrgarde\|Myrgarde]], wife of the cabinetmaker Tobias and a devotee of Saint Lucian. She died in [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]] and is buried in the burial ground of [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]]’s family, beneath a headstone that lichen has gnawed away at, nearly erasing the name.

### Life

The faith she practiced was domestic and unostentatious, made up of longing for the vegetable gardens in the temple’s backyard and prayers in chorus. She went north with Tobias in search of the work the new fortresses promised, and the family settled near Niedellin. She was widowed and grew old in the village. She raised her nephew Abramis as a son, who would remain in Niedellin.

### Gestures

She clutched the pendant of Saint Lucian, a silver wing-blade, as a nervous tic, and embroidered in the late afternoon, with her needle suspended when someone came in. She drove rats away from the cloth sacks with sachets of rue. She had supper ready from the moment the bells rang and the chair set for someone who no longer came to eat. The house she kept in the village is remembered for this routine.
