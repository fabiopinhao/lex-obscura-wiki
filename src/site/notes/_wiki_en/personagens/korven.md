# Korven

Korven is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lieutenant in the Bureau of Collections, Councilor [[_wiki_en/personagens/vyrensen\|Malachar Vyrensen]]’s [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/coletoria\|Collectory]], and operates chiefly along the trade routes of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. A coldly efficient tax collector, he does the work no one wants to see done and rarely leaves satisfied witnesses behind.

### Appearance

An old scar cuts across his chin, an old mark of a trade that has always demanded more muscle than words. The others are recent: one on his cheek, another on his throat.

### Origin and Role

In [[_wiki_en/locais/ezrath\|Ezrath]], upon hearing a shopkeeper shout that he had been robbed, Korven takes it upon himself to arrest the [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Muran]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/skinitz\|Skinitz]], pinning the fugitive’s tail beneath his boot. It is [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] who, there, exercises her authority as a Justiciar over him: she stops the beating of the prisoner and decrees the Muran’s fate, the [[_wiki_en/locais/fosso_da_purga\|Pit of the Purge]], to which Korven dispatches him. The work earns him a harsh compliment, the backhanded kind, but he remains what he has always been: still a lieutenant. He has held the rank for more than two decades: he already held it when Nidzina was a child in the courtyards of Morvgrad. As a young lieutenant, he stood in formation in the courtyard of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]] on the day of [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]]’s promotion: when two fellow officers whispered about the clash between the newly promoted general and [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], he cut them off, saying that he had heard nothing and neither had they, if they valued their rank. At the end of the formation, he hovered around the officers in Vekraal’s retinue.

The turning point comes after the confrontation in the cave of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]], where the two of them, she in Justiciar armor, stand on opposite sides of the barrel of her bombard. Korven seeks the dagger [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], avoids open combat when his luck turns, and escapes the cave. Back in Morvgrad, he tells Vyrensen that it was Nidzina who confiscated the relic. The account earns him a promotion to major, paid for in silence, and the place that came with it: the covert machinery of favors and debts that keeps Vyrensen’s Collectory running far from the eyes of the Lex.

### Method and Voice

He is a man who has learned that the truth costs more than it is worth, and that the right omission rises faster than merit. He works with the economy of someone who measures every gesture by its return: a blow, an arrest, or a lie, so long as it balances the books. The next rank drives him.

### Relationships

With Vyrensen he maintains the bond that defines him: the superior who bought his silence with rank insignia and admitted him into the network of covert loyalties, a place where obedience is worth more than honesty and where Korven settles in without ceremony. He is bound to Nidzina Avaroth by the account he gave at her expense: it was by accusing her of keeping the relic that he rose to major, using her name as a shield. To Nidzina, he is the key to the smuggling and the association with the Serpent.

Skinitz’s death earns him his most patient enemy. [[_wiki_en/personagens/skanivik\|Skanivik]] holds on to his grudge and recognizes him in the market as he passes, promoted and bearing new scars. Korven remains alive in Morvgrad, higher in the hierarchy and more surrounded by shadows than when he was merely the lieutenant who followed orders.
