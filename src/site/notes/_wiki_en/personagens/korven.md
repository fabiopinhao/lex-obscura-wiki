# Korven

Korven is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lieutenant in the Bureau of Collections, Councilor [[_wiki_en/personagens/vyrensen\|Malachar Vyrensen]]’s [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/coletoria\|Collectory]], and operates chiefly along the trade routes of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. A coldly efficient tax collector, he does the work no one wants to see done and rarely leaves satisfied witnesses behind.

### Appearance

An old scar cuts across his chin. The others are recent: one on his cheek, another on his throat.

### Origin and Role

In [[_wiki_en/locais/ezrath\|Ezrath]], upon hearing a shopkeeper shout that he had been robbed, Korven took it upon himself to arrest the [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Muran]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/skinitz\|Skinitz]], pinning the fugitive’s tail beneath his boot. It was [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] who, there, exercised her authority as a Justiciar over him: she stopped the beating of the prisoner and decreed the Muran’s fate, the [[_wiki_en/locais/fosso_da_purga\|Pit of the Purge]], to which Korven dispatched him. The work earned him a harsh compliment, the backhanded kind, but he remained what he had always been: still a lieutenant. He had held the rank for more than two decades: he already held it when Nidzina was a child in the courtyards of Morvgrad. As a young lieutenant, he stood in formation in the courtyard of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]] on the day of [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]]’s promotion: when two colleagues whispered about the clash between the newly promoted general and [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]], he cut them off, saying that he had heard nothing and neither had they, if they valued their rank. At the end of the formation, he hovered around the officers in Vekraal’s retinue.

The turning point came after the confrontation in the cave of the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/serpente_purpura\|Purple Serpent]], where he and Nidzina, she in Justiciar armor, stood on opposite sides of the barrel of her bombard. Korven sought the dagger [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], avoided open combat when his luck turned, and escaped the cave. Back in Morvgrad, he told Vyrensen that it was Nidzina who confiscated the relic. The account earned him a promotion to major as payment for his silence, and the place that came with it: the covert machinery of favors and debts that keeps Vyrensen’s Collectory running far from the eyes of the Lex.

### Method and Voice

He learned to keep silent to survive and knows that the right omission rises faster than merit. He works with the economy of someone who measures every gesture by its return: a blow, an arrest, or a lie, so long as it balances the books. The next rank drives him.

### Relationships

With Vyrensen he maintains the bond that defines him: the superior who admitted him into the network of covert loyalties, a place where obedience is worth more than honesty and where Korven settles in without ceremony. He is bound to Nidzina Avaroth by the account he gave at her expense, using her name as a shield. To Nidzina, he is the key to the smuggling and the association with the Serpent.

Skinitz’s death earned him his most patient enemy. [[_wiki_en/personagens/skanivik\|Skanivik]] holds on to his grudge and recognized him in the market as he passed, promoted and bearing new scars. Korven remains alive in Morvgrad.
