# Ravhor

Ravhor is a veteran [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] lieutenant, a comrade-in-arms of [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] from the time when both served in the Tower of [[_wiki_en/locais/khargoth\|Khargoth]]. Narrow face, shaved head, an old rope groove that runs from his neck to his collarbone.

### Profile

A story circulates about him that he escaped the gallows by tearing off his own noose with his teeth, which earned him the nickname gallows veteran. A veteran of [[_wiki_en/locais/valdris\|Valdris]], he bears the marks of that event in his body and memory: he touches the groove in his neck when the memory returns and bites the inside of his cheek when he swallows his words.

### Role

In the military formation at Khargoth, he stands to Milor’s right; during [[_wiki_en/personagens/vekraal\|Vekraal]]’s speech, his hand rises to the groove and his teeth bite his cheek. Between him and Milor there is a closeness never declared, born of shared battles. Later, [[_wiki_en/personagens/valhedras\|Valhedras]] informs Milor that Ravhor has been summoned to Vekraal’s Third Eastern Legion. Milor observes that his companion has always known how to navigate military politics, and that Vekraal needs officers who do not question methods.
