# Fandegarrón

Fandegarrón, also called Garrón, is a [[_wiki_en/criaturas/gnoll\|gnoll]] veteran of the Slaughterhouse of Drakmur and bodyguard for the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/guilda_dos_gnomos\|Gnome Guild]]. The last champion of the Spear of Three Skulls in the [[_wiki_en/locais/arena_de_drakmur\|Arena of Drakmur]], he is a cognitive exception among those of his race: functional enough for personal loyalty and tactical combat.

### Appearance

His silhouette suggests something between an ogre and a bear, muddled by the excess of cloth. His muzzle is wet, black, and broad, nostrils opening and closing like an uncontrolled bellows; his ivory-beige mane tangles into knots resembling rat nests; his yellow, bulging eyes are restless, each looking in a different direction. He wears an ogre cloak far too large for him, which hangs loose at the shoulders, drags on the ground, and catches on everything, and his beige fur stands out against the gray of the rock. The excessive cloth serves a purpose: with his hunched posture and hood, he passes for an ogre at a distance, and gnolls do not move freely through the cities of the Empire. Beneath the disguise, his build runs counter to the racial norm: where the common gnoll is massive and heavily muscled, Garrón is lean and athletic, his body honed by years in the arena.

### Temperament and Combat

He fights with lead flails studded with spikes and takes avowed pleasure in carnage, without concealed shame. He refers to himself in the third person and measures battles by the count of bones he leaves behind. He is extremely foul-smelling, with limited intelligence and brutal ferocity, and a keen sense of smell capable of distinguishing living flesh from dead flesh that still moves. His ethics are those of the mercenary who survives: a good rat comes back alive, a dead rat buys nothing.

### History

He lost his den twice: first to the [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadars]], then to a creature he describes as a toad, against which he holds a lasting grudge: he says the toad pushed the world. For a gnoll, losing the lair is a kind of deferred death, and the loss of his territory pushed him into the wandering life of a mercenary.

### In the Field

He serves chiefly as a tracker. In the tunnels of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]], he sniffed out the possessed [[_wiki_en/racas/murano\|Muran]] sentinels while [[_wiki_en/personagens/dribbel_olgund\|Dribbel Olgund]] negotiated passage, and during the retaking of an old den seized by the Shadars, he advanced ahead of the group, took a bolt in the flank, and kept fighting until he reclaimed the lair.

### Relationships

He developed an unexpected loyalty to [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]] after she helped him; for a gnoll without a lair, personal loyalty replaces lost territory. His cousin [[_wiki_en/personagens/tibaut\|Tibaut]] acts as an intermediary between his brutality and the subtler world, and he serves Dribbel, the Trade-Breaker, with genuine pride in having a boss.
