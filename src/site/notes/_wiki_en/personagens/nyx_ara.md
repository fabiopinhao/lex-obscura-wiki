# Nyx’Ara

Nyx’Ara is a witch of unknown age, possibly hundreds of years old, and an oracle of shifting appearance whose exact nature remains uncertain. She is consulted by shadow lords seeking omens and cultivates deliberately ambiguous speech. She dwells in the depths of [[_wiki_en/locais/ghol_mournak\|Ghol’Mournak]].

### Appearance

Her true form is unknown. Before those who consult her, she rises in a movement that defies mortal joints, and her body transmutes from one appearance to another; none of the forms she displays is considered her true form. The transmutation ends in a sigh that could be either pleasure or pain.

### The Path to Her

The [[_wiki_en/locais/camara_de_othuun\|Chamber of Othuun]] is in the sacred district of [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_aroth\|Xarn’Aroth]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]], where the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/culto_sombrio\|Cult]] maintains total surveillance and the air carries an artificial purity, and is one of the passages into the depths of Ghol’Mournak. Those seeking her omens cross the complex and descend the five hundred whispering steps, each of which is said to keep its own secret, or take the elevator that spares them the penance. She receives visitors at the end of the descent, in the lower galleries, beside the tar pits.

### The Oracle

Her presence is theatrical and devious. She watches from the tar pits with cold amusement and appreciates a good spectacle of cruelty, especially when it involves pathetic creatures begging. When [[_wiki_en/personagens/orum-rahru\|Orum-Rahru]] descends to her, he leaves with what she wanted him to take away: the confirmation that there are eyes on him, and the bitter taste that is usually the result of an audience with the witch. She is consulted by those who hold power and regarded with suspicion by those who understand how much she sees.
