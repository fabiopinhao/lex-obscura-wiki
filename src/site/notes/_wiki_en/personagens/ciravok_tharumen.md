# Ciravok Tharumen

Ciravok Tharumen, the Lord of the Granaries, is a [[_wiki_en/racas/shadar\|Shadar]] councilor on the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]] responsible for provisioning and food logistics in the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]]. He carries a noble scepter that he strikes against the wood to punctuate his remarks, a gesture that combines aristocratic authority and practical impatience.

### Role on the Council

At a session of the Shadow Council, he speaks out against the new migration of savage [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]], comparing insubordinate muscle to a tumor. He presents the objection as a matter of logistics: the Empire does not need more savages from wandering tribes that will not be tamed or trained. He also describes the crisis on the fringes of the domain, where reports of hunger have already reached the Council and [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] have been sent to [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]].
