# Quaz’har

Quaz’har is an [[_wiki_en/criaturas/diabrete\|imp]] with scarlet wings and blood-red skin, a messenger in the service of the Court of [[_wiki_en/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]]. She stutters when she speaks and flies through the city, which she knows better than any messenger before her.

### Appearance

Small, with scarlet wings and blood-colored skin, she wears an expression of permanent terror, as if the lash had pursued her since she was an egg. Perched motionless on the window, she blends in with the gargoyles that decorate the tower’s lower floors. That is what her master calls her, Ornamental Gargoyle, when he loses interest in her.

### Profile

She was born in captivity. She is the fourth imp to hold the post of messenger: the three who preceded her died in horrific ways. She considers her longevity in the post a punishment, not an achievement, and lives with recurring suicidal thoughts. Inwardly, her reasoning is clear, bitter, and lucid; outwardly, her speech comes out broken by her stutter, a constant distance between what she thinks and what she can articulate.

### Relationships

She harbors envy and contempt for [[_wiki_en/personagens/fing\|Fing]]: she envies his status as an earthbound servant and despises him as inferior. The messengers who preceded her hover like a shadow, a recurring reminder of her likely fate. When she sees another servant fall from grace, she watches the scene with a dark pleasure, the relief of not being the one who suffers this time.
