# Varna

Varna is a female dragon who inhabits [[_wiki_en/locais/varnak\|Varnak]], in [[_wiki_en/locais/veinkard\|Veinkard]]. Her history is tied to the [[_wiki_en/locais/dorsal\|Dorsal]], the [[_wiki_en/locais/pico_de_nethys\|Peak of Nethys]], and the changes that transformed the frontier between the continents.

### In the Dorsal

During the [[_wiki_en/conceitos/primeira_era\|First Era]], Varna remained on the Peak of Nethys after the older dragons migrated west. Territorial, she hunted [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orcs]] on the slopes of the Dorsal and held back the tribes’ crossings toward the plateau for generations. She did not protect the peoples of the region: she defended her own territory.

### Migration to Varnak

When dark corruption made the Peak untenable, Varna migrated west. She first tried to settle in the volcano of [[_wiki_en/locais/thandruk_varn\|Thandruk Varn]], but the [[_wiki_en/racas/karashim\|dwarves]] already inhabited the mountain and she found no peace there; the dwarven ancestors remember the terror she inflicted on Thandruk Varn generations ago. She continued farther west, to Varnak. Her departure left the Peak permanently deserted and contributed to the intensification of orc crossings through the Dorsal. In the valleys of Varnak, she was received with reverence or fear.

### Legacy

Varna remained in Varnak when new migrations scattered the great dragon tribes to more distant regions. She became a singular presence in Veinkard and a point of reference for some of the [[_wiki_en/racas/draconideo\|Draconid]] tribes and [[_wiki_en/racas/varnakiano\|Varnakians]].

### The Guardian and the Dagger

Varna guards Varnak, and her presence maintains the region’s balance of power through an individual threat of apocalyptic proportions. [[_wiki_en/artefatos/fendris\|Fendris]], a [[_wiki_en/racas/luminares\|Luminar]] dagger forged to pierce Draconid scales, is the key capable of defeating her: whoever holds it holds that power. The Draconid [[_wiki_en/personagens/yzmareth\|Yzmareth]] seeks Varna’s destruction to unify Varnak, and the blade is currently with [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciar]] [[_wiki_en/personagens/nidzina_avaroth\|Nidzina Avaroth]].
