# Levi

Levi is an orphan boy from [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], a human of the [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriars]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/berenice\|Berenice]]’s brother. He lives in the chapel under the care of [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]]. He is an observer, one of those who see what has no name yet before adults do.

### Appearance

He is a thin boy with a frail chest, his face always pale. His eyes earn him the nickname owl eyes: large, half-open, watchful, fixed on what others prefer not to notice. When he speaks, his voice comes out low, in halting whispers.

### Origin and Role

Under the chapel’s roof, close to Abramis and his sister, his attention sharpened.

### Method and Voice

His defining trait is an uncommon perception of malice. Levi points out what he sees before any proof exists, without arguing, and tells the truth he sees without calculating the political or social weight of his words, in a way only a child dares. He regularly keeps a night vigil, awake when the house sleeps, and that is how he saw [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith Morghast]] pass in the darkness.

### Relationships

Under Abramis’s care, Levi has shelter, food, and a moral boundary; the priest sleeps in the barn with the children and answers for them. His sister, Berenice, is his closest bond, each the only family the other has left. Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] sizes up the boy and recognizes a rare gift in him, expressed in a few words: perceptive, you have a rare gift.
