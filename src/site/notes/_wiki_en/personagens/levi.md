# Levi

Levi is an orphan boy from [[_wiki_en/locais/niedellin\|Niedellin]], a human of the [[_wiki_en/racas/pindoriar\|Pindoriars]] and [[_wiki_en/personagens/berenice\|Berenice]]’s brother. He lives in the chapel under the care of [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]], who sleeps in the barn with the children he protects. He is an observer, one of those who see what has no name yet before adults do.

### Appearance

He is a thin boy with a frail chest, his face always pale. His eyes earn him the nickname owl eyes: large, half-open, watchful, fixed on what others prefer not to notice. When he speaks, his voice comes out low, in halting whispers, like someone keeping a secret that weighs more than his body can bear.

### Origin and Role

He grows up in the streets and corners of Niedellin, an orphan among orphans, quieter than the others. Under the chapel’s roof, he stays close to Abramis and his sister, Berenice, and there his attention sharpens. Where adults see routine, he sees cracks.

### Method and Voice

His defining trait is an uncommon perception of malice. Levi neither argues nor weighs consequences: he points out what he sees, before any proof exists, and tells the truth he sees without calculating the political or social weight of his words, in a way only a child dares. He regularly keeps a night vigil, awake when the house sleeps, and that is how he saw [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith Morghast]] pass in the darkness. Anyone who crosses paths with him notices that those eyes register more than they should.

### Relationships

Under Abramis’s care, Levi has shelter, food, and a moral boundary; Abramis is the man who sleeps in the barn with the children and answers for them. His sister, Berenice, is his closest bond, each the only family the other has left. Justiciar [[_wiki_en/personagens/milorsevogh_velshard\|Milorsevogh Velshard]] sizes up the boy and recognizes a rare gift in him, expressed in a few words: perceptive, you have a rare gift.
