# Ghur

Ghur is an [[_wiki_en/racas/orc\|orc]], brother of [[_wiki_en/personagens/grothar\|Grothar]], who died in [[_wiki_en/locais/lendalas\|Lendalas]]. He died before recent events and survives chiefly in his brother’s memory. Before his death, he recited the [[_wiki_en/personagens/grasnak\|Lays of Grasnak]] at campfires.

### Circumstances of Death

Before the fire in Lendalas, Ghur was among the orcs found hanging by roots at a height of fifteen fathoms, their lungs full of sap. The roots emerged from the ground and coiled around his neck, bringing a slow death through strangulation by vegetation, the work of the [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]]’ druidic roots. To Grothar, elven cruelty surpassed any brutality he witnessed in the service of shadow lords.

### Memory and Legacy

Ghur’s death explains Grothar’s personal and restrained resentment toward elves, the scar of a real loss, and the visceral alarm that elven forests such as [[_wiki_en/locais/thal_serin\|Thal’Serin]] awaken in him.

Ghur was more than a soldier. He recited the Lays of Grasnak and was the first to show his brother that an orc could harbor more than rage: the erudition that Grothar later confesses to [[_wiki_en/personagens/syndrith_morghast\|Syndrith]], the ability to read ancient runes and knowledge of the Lays, originates with him.
