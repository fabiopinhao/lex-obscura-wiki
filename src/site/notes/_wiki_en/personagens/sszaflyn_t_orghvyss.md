# Sszaflyn T’orghvyss

Sszaflyn T’orghvyss is the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Drul]] matriarch of House T’orghvyss, known by the epithet the Mother of Beasts. She rules in [[_wiki_en/locais/ghilanna\|Ghilanna]] and is among the six ruling matriarchs of [[_wiki_en/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]].
