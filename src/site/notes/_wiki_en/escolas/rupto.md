# Rupto

Rupto is the school of pure magic within the domain of destruction and rupture, aligned with the red of the grimoire. It breaks, fragments, and destroys by negating structural cohesion, an arcane force that undoes what holds matter and bonds together. Its key feeling is unease at what is intact, the sense that what has not yet broken has simply not been tested.

### Principle

Rupto neither burns nor melts: it unmakes. This distinction separates it from [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], which consumes through combustion, and from [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]], which dissolves through extreme energy. It creates cracks, shatters objects, forcibly breaks nonphysical bonds, identifies load-bearing points, and collapses structures.

### Practices

The grimoire progresses from cracking to catastrophic rupture. Fissure creates cracks and weakens load-bearing points, preparing the material for the next force, and functions as a debuff across schools, since any subsequent damage encounters reduced resistance. Shatter breaks what is fragile or already compromised; Break bursts whatever binds, such as ropes, chains, locks, and seals. Fragment tears solid objects to pieces by perceiving the material’s lines of tension. Scission separates one material from another in contact with it, such as shattering steel without touching the leather beneath. Abrogation breaks a nonphysical bond, undoing a seal and nullifying a restriction by force, more violent and less precise than [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]], which negates instead of breaking. Demolition finds the critical load-bearing point and initiates a sequential collapse, pillar by pillar. Detonation converts the caster’s physical integrity into a destructive wave radiating from the caster’s own body, at its limit a suicide weapon. Obliteration completely erases a single target, leaving neither fragments nor dust. At the top is Devastation, rupture on a catastrophic scale that shatters terrain, structures, and magical bonds within an area, indiscriminate because allied infrastructure perishes along with enemy infrastructure.

### Costs

The thematic cost is sharp pain in the joints and the sensation of fractures, lasting from two minutes to three hours. The ontological cost is the destructive impulse: building becomes tedious and breaking, satisfying. It begins as a subtle satisfaction at seeing things break and progresses until intact objects cause discomfort and only destruction produces meaning. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners from among the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]], employing them in demolition and sapping.

### Combinations and Opposition

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]], structural degradation brings down decayed walls with a minimal fissure; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], demolition warfare opens armor before the blow; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], the imperial doctrine that makes matter brittle before shattering it, reducing walls to rubble. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], which imposes and binds where Rupto undoes and frees: one declares that something remains, the other that it gives way, mirrors of the same ontological cost at opposite polarities.
