# Anatho

Anatho is the hybrid school of magic dedicated to curses and corruption. It imposes supernatural degradation without an organic cause: the target weakens, falls ill, or comes apart for no visible reason. Its key feeling is the calculated will to degrade, seeing weaknesses as pressure points. It is the school opposed to [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], grace against curse.

### Practices

The school ranges from discomfort to existential inversion. Bad Luck tips probability against the target by narrow margins, each setback feeding the next. Mark identifies the target for future effects and, combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], allows surveillance from a distance. Discomfort imposes persistent physical distress without injury, days of discomfort that break a resistance torture cannot reach. Hex binds the caster to the target in a curse sustained by will, with a choice of misfortune, sabotage of relationships, draining of vitality, or disruption of sleep. Weakening degrades strength, perception, or resistance without the target falling ill. Deterioration sabotages objects from a distance, blades cracking and ropes unraveling fiber by fiber. Scourge is an autonomous curse that parasitizes the victim independently of the caster. Petrification transmutes flesh into stone from the inside out. Damnation marks a soul, lineage, or name. Ruin, its highest level, inverts the target’s existence: strength becomes weakness, loyalty becomes betrayal, achievements collapse as though they had never existed.

### Cursed Sealings

Anatho also produces cursed sealings: patterns of containment and protection that imprison instead of degrading. The towers of [[_wiki/locais/morvgrad\|Morvgrad]] that specialize in the school teach forms of containment considered inviolable, a category distinct from [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]]. Where Abjuro dissipates and negates magic, Anatho binds it to flesh or stone. Sealings anchored in the name of a demonic entity emanate a constant temperature and burn the unprepared intruder. Those anchored in entities of fire, such as [[_wiki/panteao/kaelgrym\|Kaelgrym]], are the hottest, and those of [[_wiki/panteao/warguleth\|Wargûleth]] also burn.

### Trace

A recent invocation of Anatho leaves a trace legible to those who know how to look for it: not a temperature in the air, but the cold that the arcane residue imprints on the nerves of those examining the place. This makes it possible to reconstruct authorship after the act, distinguishing which school operated and by whose hand, even when almost the entire trace has already dispersed. It is not to be confused with the constant heat of sealings anchored in the name of a demonic entity: the heat of the sealings is active containment and remains; the trace is residue and fades.

### Costs

The thematic cost consists of temporary marks of corruption in the caster’s own flesh, and the bond with the target is bidirectional: fragments of the inflicted affliction leak back to the caster. The ontological cost turns every relationship into a vector of harm: it begins with an instinctive distrust of praise and kindness and progresses to an incapacity for genuine intimacy, when all affection seems like a tactic. At the extreme stage, when manipulation turns against superiors within the chain of command itself, the Discard comes through a silent Transfer.

### In the Empire

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners of Anatho as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], Inquisitors, and interrogation [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]], roles that require reading vulnerabilities. Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]], it produces total manipulation; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], it corners the prey, degrading the body while forcing the target to attack even when weakened.
