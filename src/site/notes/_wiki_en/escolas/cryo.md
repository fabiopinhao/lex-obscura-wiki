# Cryo

Cryo is the pure blue school that manipulates cold and ice. Its principle is emotional distance as clarity: the conviction that feeling less means seeing better. Its domain is ice and glaciation, and its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]], where stasis opposes excitation, cold preserving what energy dissolves.

### Principle

Cryo treats cold as an external force, penetrating from the outside in. This is the fundamental distinction from [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]], where cold radiates from the inside out, like death emanating from the practitioner. Cryo’s ice comes from the environment: the surroundings freeze, and heat is torn from the body.

### Practices

The school’s mechanics scale from numbing to absolute glaciation. In its lesser forms, Numbing slows the target’s reflexes, Night Air deposits thin layers of ice on surfaces, and Crystal freezes moisture in the air into sharp formations. Next, Spicule launches shards and spears of ice as projectiles, Mordant immobilizes limbs in ice that tightens the more the victim struggles, and Carapace coats the body in armor that sends cold up the striker’s arm. Coldness drains heat without visible ice, freezing from the inside out through pure thermodynamics, and Brittle weakens matter to the point that metal cracks at a touch and bone turns to glass. At the top of the scale, Blizzard unleashes storms with zero visibility, up to Glaciation, the absolute cold in which air, sound, and movement cease and whatever does not die is preserved, intact and eternally trapped in ice.

### Combinations

Combined with Necro, it produces complete emotional death, the inability to feel. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]], it forms the imperial doctrine of demolition: Brittle weakens matter and Fissure shatters what remains, walls turning to gravel and bones turning to dust. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]], it is the poison that freezes: Mordant immobilizes the target while the venom works without haste.

### Costs

The thematic cost is mild hypothermia and uncontrollable tremors, lasting from two minutes to three hours. The ontological cost is progressive emotional coldness: emotions cool, love becomes distant affection, passion becomes memory. Unlike [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]], which makes one feel too much, Cryo makes one feel too little, an emotional anesthesia. The progression passes through four stages, from the mild dampening of reactions to permanent inner ice, in which feeling becomes an abstract concept. The fourth stage rarely leads to disposal, because coldness is functional in every phase. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits its practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], enforcers, and interrogators, roles in which emotional apathy is an operational advantage.
