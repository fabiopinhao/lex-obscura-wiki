# Necro

Necro is the pure black school that manipulates the energy of death through subtraction: it removes heat, removes will, removes the spark that distinguishes living flesh from dead matter. It is one of the disciplines of black magic, whose domain belongs not to [[_wiki_en/panteao/vozrador\|Vozrador]], but to [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/diabos\|devils]] and black gods bound by separate pacts. Its key feeling is the cold acceptance of death as a natural state. Its opposing counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]].

### Principle

The school operates through subtraction, unlike Vita, which operates through addition. Its thematic cost is a cadaverous cold that radiates from the inside out, a fundamental distinction from [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], where cold penetrates from the outside in: in Necro, it is death emanating from the practitioner, the body cooling because life recedes. It reads residues in the dead, where [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] reads truth in the living.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from the touch that cools to the legion that marches. Rigor drains vital heat through contact and stiffens the target, causing numbness, weakness, and grayish skin. Whisper extracts fragmentary memories from a recent corpse, not dialogue but an echo, with the information degrading over time. Lethargy simulates death in the practitioner’s own body or in a willing target, with the pulse stopping and breathing imperceptible, indistinguishable from a corpse to ordinary observers. Siphon parasitically draws life force from a living being through proximity, the target wasting away while the caster feels a temporary warmth that charges interest in cold when it ceases. Servant reanimates a corpse as a mindless automaton that obeys simple commands, an empty shell dependent on continuous maintenance. Shroud coats the body in necrotic energy as armor that retaliates by draining, leaving the caster’s skin grayish and cold. Revenant reanimates a dead person with fragments of memory and limited will, capable of recognizing faces and wielding weapons, degrading within days without reinforcement. Reap releases a necrotic wave over an area that withers vegetation and drains the vitality of beings, leaving the earth barren for days. Reliquary captures the soul of someone recently deceased before it dissipates and traps it in a prepared receptacle. Legion, the maximum scale, reanimates dozens or hundreds of dead on a field of slaughter, in a fragile bond that the caster sustains until collapsing.

### Costs

The ontological cost operates through progressive desensitization. First, funerals seem like excessive ceremony; then corpses become material, no longer people who once were; next, death itself ceases to be feared or relevant; finally, the distinction between living and dead loses all moral meaning. At this stage, the practitioner is discarded for reanimating without authorization and sent to the Eternal Vigil, in a terminal and structural irony: the necromancer ends up preserved like the dead they manipulated. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] also recruits necromancers such as [[_wiki_en/locais/xarn_zareth\|Xarn’Zareth]], for the maintenance of undead and the custody of catacombs.

### Combinations

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]], it produces zones of total exclusion, killing what lives and preventing anything new from growing. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]], it generates nearly undetectable assassins, whose Shroud drains those it touches while the shroud conceals their approach. With Cryo, it adds the inability to feel to desensitization to death. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], it amplifies parasitism, feeding Siphon with the physiological acceleration of terrified bodies.
