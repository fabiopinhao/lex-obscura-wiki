# Salvo

Salvo is the hybrid school dedicated to healing and restoration. It repairs injuries, stabilizes the wounded, neutralizes toxins and diseases, and regenerates tissue through medico-arcane means. It is reactive by nature, because it responds to damage already done, and this is the distinction that separates it from [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]], which strengthens the body before the blow, and from [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], which works through divine miracle rather than technique.

### Principle

The feeling that drives Salvo is the impulse to restore what is broken, with others’ pain felt as a personal urgency. Where there is a wounded body, the practitioner recognizes less a task than an obligation. Its cycle sums up the school’s calling and danger: those who heal too much lose their own boundaries.

### Practices

Salvo’s mechanics scale from relief to absolute restoration. Balm dulls pain without treating the cause: the agony recedes, replaced by moist warmth, while the wound remains intact beneath the relief. Suture closes cuts and stops bleeding, sealing the broken surface in a pinkish line, while Welding reaches what Suture cannot, realigning fractured bone, reconnecting torn muscle, and sealing punctured organs, with each day of recovery compressed into seconds.

Purification extracts what does not belong from the body, expelling toxins and impurities through sweat, vomit, or urine, without distinguishing poison from medicine. Restoration reverses sensory and functional loss, returning focus to the eyes, sound to the ears, and movement to the numb hand. Remission eliminates established disease, reversing the pathological process rather than treating symptoms, and serves as containment that stabilizes and buys time against magical plagues or infections from [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]]. Reconstitution regenerates tissue that no longer exists, making severed fingers regrow and a destroyed eye re-form in the empty socket, with the new flesh growing from the healer’s own vitality. Catharsis purges what Remission cannot reach, tearing out magical diseases, lesser curses from [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], and resistant plagues layer by layer. Return revokes recent death, when the body is still viable and the spirit remains nearby, a matter of minutes rather than hours, but leaves the lethal wound requiring immediate Welding.

Miracle is the school’s highest level: absolute restoration in which every wound closes, every bone knits, every organ reconstitutes itself, every disease ceases, and every curse dissolves, simultaneously. It restores the living, not the dead, and this sets it apart from Sacro’s Resurrection.

### Combinations

With Vita, Graft drains vitality from a willing source and Welding directs this imported energy into the wound, allowing the healer to work without paying with their own body, although the source pays double. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]], Balm suppresses pain while Lullaby induces sleep, and the wounded person falls asleep without suffering. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]], Stasis freezes the wounded person in the instant before death and Restoration reverses the damage while time is stopped, in a body that neither deteriorates nor bleeds.

### Costs

The thematic cost is the transfer of pain: healing another means feeling the pain being healed, immediately and proportionally. The ontological cost is empathic codependency, in which others’ suffering becomes intolerable and healing becomes addictive. In the first stage, others’ pain is more troubling than normal; in the second, healing produces disproportionate satisfaction; in the third, letting someone suffer seems like active cruelty, personal boundaries disappear, and the practitioner begins to absorb others’ pain compulsively, to the point of sabotaging legitimate violence. The fourth stage is considered an irreversible systemic risk, and the practitioner who reaches it is executed in a Final Consecration, because compulsive empathy sabotages essential functions of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]]. The Empire recruits practitioners as Imperial Healers only up to the second stage, with mandatory disposal before the third.

### Antagonistic Pair

Salvo’s opposite is Patho, which degrades integrity where Salvo restores it. Infection prevents what Welding attempts, Contagion spreads what Catharsis should contain, and the race between healer and plague determines who lives and who rots: active, targeted healing against passive, contagious disease.
