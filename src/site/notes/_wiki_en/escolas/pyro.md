# Pyro

Pyro is the school dedicated to fire and combustion. It creates and manipulates flames, projects jets and balls of fire, and raises fiery barriers, and can boil blood and melt organs. It operates through actual chemical combustion: fire needs fuel and consumes what it touches, a fundamental distinction from [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]], whose extreme energy melts matter without fuel.

### Principle

Its key emotion is anger that demands release, the conviction that containing the fire is worse than unleashing it. Pyro’s fire is literal: it feeds on material and ceases only when there is nothing left to devour.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from spark to conflagration. Ignition kindles flames in flammable materials, the school’s most elementary gesture. Scorching imposes oppressive heat without visible flame, air that suffocates and skin that dries out, leading to heat exhaustion. Cautery burns to seal, closing wounds, marking flesh, and welding metal. Ember envelops weapons and objects in flames that burn without consuming the underlying material, the blade’s edge burning as it cuts. Deflagration launches jets, spheres, and cones of concentrated fire, the school’s standard offensive form.

Pyre raises walls, circles, and cages of fire, blocking passage through the real threat of flame. Consumption is selective fire, devouring only wood, only flesh, or only metal, ignoring the rest. Maelstrom unleashes sentient flames that pursue targets, move around obstacles, cannot be extinguished with water, and cease only when nothing remains. Crucible envelops the caster’s own body in flames, an aura with a lethal touch that burns those who approach. At its greatest scale, Inferno is a devastating conflagration that consumes everything within a radius, a firestorm where air becomes fuel and the caster, at the center, is the last to burn.

### Costs

The thematic cost is internal burns and a temporary high fever, lasting from one minute to two hours. The ontological cost is an incendiary temperament: from growing irritability to anger that arises quickly and dissipates slowly, until serenity becomes impossible and existence is measured only in degrees of fury. In the final stage, the practitioner whose fury turns against allies or superiors is discarded on a Mission of Honor: sent as an incendiary weapon against enemy fortresses, a glory in flames from which no return is expected. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|The Empire]] recruits practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]] through the third stage and as siege weapons.

### Combinations and Opposition

Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], it produces the flaming soldier who advances burning everything while being consumed, a forbidden protocol that desperate sieges nevertheless produce. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]], wind feeds the fire into a cyclone of flames, in which Aero provides rotation and Pyro provides combustion. Through the technique of Cautery, it combines with [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] in field cauterization, in which Abjuro identifies the hemorrhage and Pyro seals it, a resource for military healers without access to heliomancers. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]], where fire devours and water extinguishes and adapts: consuming versus flowing.
