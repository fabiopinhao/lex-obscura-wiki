# Belli

Belli is the hybrid school of magic in the domain of war and combat. Where other schools attack from without, Belli works on the bodies of the caster and those around him: it amplifies martial ability on an increasing scale, from individual enhancement to the amplification of an entire battlefield. Its key feeling is the excitement of confrontation, the perception of conflict as the body’s natural state.

### Principle

The school awakens aggression, reads opponents, marks prey, uses blood as fuel, and projects a presence that anchors allies. It neither creates fire nor alters matter: it operates on instinct, adrenaline, and the cohesion of the band. This distinguishes it from [[_wiki_en/escolas/morpho\|Morpho]], which transforms the body instead of amplifying it, and from [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]], whose Frenzy imposes fury on another while Belli strengthens whoever casts it.

### Practices

Belli’s grimoire scales from provocation to martial transcendence. Provocation awakens aggressive instinct in the target, compelling them to attack the nearest threat; disciplined opponents resist, ordinary soldiers succumb. Fury releases adrenaline into the caster’s own body, dulling pain and accelerating reflexes in a brief burn followed by a crash. Instinct reads the opponent’s body, muscle tension, and direction of gaze, anticipating the blow. Prey designates a target as the group’s priority, making allied attacks converge through pack instinct. Cruor converts spilled blood into fuel, and the more the caster bleeds, the more dangerous he becomes. Clamor is the war cry that instills determination in the band; Immolation abandons all restraint until collapse; Massacre spreads violence in a chain reaction across an entire area, without distinguishing between sides. Standard projects a presence that anchors allies to the field, and if the caster falls, the effect reverses and morale collapses. At the top is Hecatomb, absolute martial power that makes every movement lethal before total physical collapse.

### Costs

The thematic cost is a surge of adrenaline followed by a severe crash and tremors, lasting from five minutes to six hours. The ontological cost is addiction to conflict: peace first bores, then becomes unbearable. In the early stages, an argument energizes instead of draining; further on, prolonged peace generates restlessness and a search for friction; at the extreme, conflict becomes a physiological need and the practitioner provokes fights without realizing it, until war is the only tolerable state. At that point he is discarded on a Mission of Honor, thrown onto a battlefront with no possibility of retreat, dying fighting as he always wanted. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]] on the front line and in the permanent vanguard.

### Combinations and Opposition

Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], Belli produces a flaming front line; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], the crusade of blessed and rallied soldiers; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]], the demolition war that breaks open armor before the blow; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], the cornered prey that attacks even when weakened. The combination with Morpho is forbidden by imperial protocol: it unites a monstrous body with an unstoppable combat state and produces abominations with no verifiable loyalty. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]], which resists, absorbs, and protects where Belli attacks and destroys, with a mirrored ontological cost: one cannot stop attacking, the other cannot stop protecting.
