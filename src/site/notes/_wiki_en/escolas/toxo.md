# Toxo

Toxo is the hybrid school of green and black that poisons, corrodes, and degrades through chemical-arcane means, always as an external agent applied in the form of a substance. This is its fundamental distinction from [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]], where degradation is internal and biological: Toxo applies from outside, Patho rots from within. Its key feeling is fascination with what corrodes and transforms, the knowledge that every substance is a matter of dose. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]].

### Principle

The school corrupts substance where Abjuro purifies, along an axis of opposition defined by chemical integrity. It also differs from [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], whose damage is an arcane curse rather than a chemical poison, and from [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], which prohibits movement instead of dulling the senses.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from subtle poison to lethal cloud. Venom poisons a weapon, object, or food, dulling the senses and inducing torpor without causing injury. Resin secretes an adhesive substance onto a surface, trapping the hand that touches it or the mechanism that moves. Corrosion degrades material through arcane contact, drying out leather, oxidizing metal, and softening wood in a slow, localized effect. Bile projects conjured corrosive liquid that melts organic matter and metal, continuing to corrode until neutralized by solvent, the Purge, or dilution. Bloodletting applies an anticoagulant poison that prevents healing, making superficial cuts bleed like deep ones. Web projects an adhesive organic secretion over an area, with viscous threads that trap and suffocate, removable by fire or solvent. Necrotoxin produces acid that attacks living tissue exclusively, leaving metal and stone intact while flesh dissolves. Exhalation emits a toxic gas cloud that enters through pores and lungs. Intoxication saturates the target’s body with toxins through arcane saturation, causing the organs to fail in sequence. Miasma, the maximum scale, is the area-wide cloud of death where the air poisons and surfaces corrode, static and indiscriminate.

### Costs

The thematic cost is the taste of poison in the mouth, with a numb tongue and excessive salivation, lasting from two minutes to two hours. The ontological cost is a corrupted sense of taste. First, ordinary food loses its flavor and the search for strong seasonings sets in; then only extremes register, and an attraction to bitter and acidic tastes emerges; next, harmful substances become palatable and desirable; finally, poison becomes comfort and purity is tasteless, the body demanding toxins. At this stage, the practitioner is sent on Missions of Honor from which no return is expected, and the impairment from self-poisoning itself limits their use in the field. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|The Empire]] recruits Toxo practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]] for assassination and as poison alchemists.

### Combinations

With Patho, it turns minor wounds into death sentences, simultaneously preventing clotting and healing, a means for Ophideias to bring about deaths that seem natural. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], it immobilizes the target while the poison works without haste. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], it converts the conjured gases into a fiery explosion upon contact with fire. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]], it uses the wind as a vehicle, carrying the toxic cloud toward the target and spreading it over a wide area.
