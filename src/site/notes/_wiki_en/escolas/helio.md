# Helio

Helio is the school of pure magic in the domain of light and radiance. It creates and manipulates light as physical radiation that reveals, burns, and purifies through exposure. Its key feeling is the need to reveal, the conviction that to hide is to corrupt.

### Principle

Helio’s light acts through radiation: it illuminates, dazzles, reveals the invisible and illusions, projects beams that burn, and destroys darkness and corruption through exposure itself. This is the distinction that separates it from [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], which purifies through moral authority and divine ritual while Helio purifies through radiation.

### Practices

The grimoire progresses from illumination to absolute radiance. Flare creates a stable light source that lasts for hours without concentration; Flash releases a brief burst that causes reflexive blindness. Beacon surrounds the target with a luminous outline that shines even behind cover and under an illusion. Blaze concentrates a beam that burns wherever it touches, producing a clean, cauterized burn, and against creatures of darkness it does not burn: it devours. Unveil saturates the area with radiance that dissolves concealment, making the shadows of [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]] recede, undoing invisibility, and making the illusions of [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] lose opacity. Incandescence imbues a weapon or object with concentrated light, and against the undead its touch is agony. Purge releases destructive radiance against corruption, necrotic energy, and possession, dissolving undead like ash beneath the midday sun. Prism splits the beam into vectors that track distinct targets; Zenith sends a pillar of light crashing down from the sky, and only works under an open sky. At the top is Apogee, which transforms the caster into a source of radiance, the body glowing down to the bones beneath translucent skin while a wave of light sweeps over everything.

In the tradition of [[_wiki/panteao/azazel\|Azazel]], Helio is also restoration. [[_wiki_en/personagens/abramis\|Abramis]]’s Canticle of Restoration belongs to the school: light that restores what has been corrupted and returns to the body the life that corruption took away. Healing through transferred pain remains the province of [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]].

### Costs

The thematic cost is temporary blindness and blind spots in vision, lasting from thirty seconds to one hour. The ontological cost is compulsive exposure: secrets become intolerable and transparency becomes an imperative. It begins as discomfort with keeping secrets and progresses until hiding anything seems morally wrong. The most advanced stage is treated as an irreversible systemic risk, and the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] only recruits practitioners for public and ceremonial roles as long as transparency is not compromising; anyone who serves [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]] or the Inquisition and advances too far is disposed of in a Final Consecration before exposing secret operations.

### Combinations and Opposition

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], Helio lights the blades of an entire formation before the advance; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]], it serves field healers in surgical cauterization; against [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]], it provides direct functional opposition, and a heliomancer on a necromantic battlefield is the army’s most valuable resource. Its antagonistic counterpart is Umbra, the most binary axis in the entire system: light exposes what darkness hides, darkness consumes what light reveals, and Eclipse extinguishes what Apogee sets ablaze.
