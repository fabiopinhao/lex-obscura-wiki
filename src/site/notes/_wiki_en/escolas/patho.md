# Patho

Patho is the hybrid school of the colors black and green, dedicated to decay and putrefaction. It causes decomposition, disease, and putrefaction as accelerated biological entropy: it rots organic matter, induces fever, infects wounds, and turns targets into vectors of contagion. It differs from [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]], which corrodes through an external chemical substance, because Patho’s degradation is internal, the body decomposing without a visible agent.

### Principle

Its key feeling is the keen perception of where everything begins to rot, the intuition that decomposition is already underway. The practitioner sees entropy as a permanent process in the world, and this reading colors both their magic and their view of things.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from nausea to environmental pestilence. Nausea emits putrid breath that induces malaise, dizziness, and vomiting, making the body reject itself. Infection contaminates an existing wound, preventing healing and making the flesh suppurate. Atrophy degrades a specific muscle, tendon, or joint, the tissue withering as though aging months in minutes. Fever induces a high temperature, delirium, and tremors, driving the body to consume its reserves against a threat that does not exist. Putrefaction rots nonliving organic matter, softening wood, breaking down leather, and blackening food.

Contagion turns the infected target into a vector: anyone who touches it, breathes nearby, or drinks the same water falls ill with the same symptoms, and each newly infected individual becomes a source. Necrosis rots living tissue, accelerated gangrene that devours the flesh of a conscious body, reversible through [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]] or amputation. Infestation summons natural pests, insects, parasites, and worms that feed on the target or the environment. Decrepitude degrades the internal biological structure, deteriorating bones, skin, and muscles without changing appearance or actual age. At its greatest scale, Pestilence unleashes massive deterioration that spreads across the terrain, each infected body contaminating the soil and all contaminated soil infecting anyone who sets foot on it.

### Costs

The thematic cost is nausea, mild fever, and physical malaise, lasting from five minutes to four hours. The ontological cost is awareness of entropy: from the signs of decay that others ignore to melancholy in the face of all beauty, to the point where everything seems to be actively decomposing and building anything seems absurd. The distinction from [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] is fundamental: Patho makes one feel too much, in active melancholy and depression, while Cryo makes one feel too little, in emotional anesthesia. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners as support necromancers and deterioration assessors.

### Combinations and Opposition

Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]], it produces a total exclusion zone, in which Reap kills what lives and Contagion prevents anything new from growing, a scorched-earth tactic. With Toxo, it combines infection and poison to turn minor wounds into death sentences, a resource of [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]] for assassinations that look like natural deaths. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]], rotted walls give way with a minimal fissure, a doctrine of prolonged siege. Its antagonistic counterpart is Salvo, which restores integrity where Patho degrades it, in a race between healer and plague.
