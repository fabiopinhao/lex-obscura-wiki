# Vita

Vita is the pure school of green, dedicated to manipulating vital energy through addition. Where other schools repair what has already been damaged, Vita acts before the blow: it infuses vigor, accelerates growth, and forces life to multiply where it should not. It is proactive by nature, and this anticipation is what distinguishes it from [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]], which restores after damage. Its domain is life and growth, and its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]].

### Practices

Vita’s mechanics scale from sprout to instant forest. Sprout accelerates plant growth: seeds germinate in seconds, roots grow deeper, branches extend, and the caster feels the sap flowing like their own blood. Vigor infuses vital energy into a target, making fatigue recede and the body respond as if it were not wounded, without healing the wound itself. Affinity perceives nearby living beings and allows rudimentary communication with fauna and flora, sensing life as a frequency. Cortex hardens the body’s surface with an organic coating of bark, scales, or chitin, protection that grows from the flesh itself.

Its more aggressive manifestations treat life as a weapon. Roots commands existing vegetation to grab, restrain, and block passages. Thorns makes vegetation burst from the ground in stakes and piercing points. Graft transfers vitality between living beings, draining a source to strengthen another target. Symbiosis temporarily fuses the caster with a living being, sharing senses, strength, and instincts. Anima infuses temporary consciousness into a plant or animal, which begins to act with limited autonomy. At the top of the scale is Genesis, which creates vast amounts of plant life at once, a forest rising from bare soil, roots shattering stone and a canopy blocking the sky.

### Costs

Every casting of Vita exacts temporary aging: wrinkles and gray hair that last from five minutes to eight hours. Genesis, its maximum scale, exacts years of aging in seconds.

The deeper cost is ontological: a hunger for vitality that makes proximity to youth compulsive. The practitioner progresses through stages. In the first, they feel better near young people and children. In the second, solitude drains them and the search for vital companionship becomes constant. In the third, proximity to life ceases to be a preference and becomes a necessity. In the fourth, isolation is slow death, and the practitioner begins to drain the vitality of others to exist; when their subordinates begin to fall ill in their presence, they are discarded and sent to the Eternal Vigil. The irony is structural: those who cultivated life end up suspended, draining it, without full life, like the dead that Necro manipulates.

### In the Empire

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits Vita practitioners as Imperial Healers, but only up to the second stage of the cost. Combined with Salvo, Vita allows impossible transplants: it supplies the living matter while Salvo integrates it into the recipient’s body, and imperial healers train in both schools to reconstruct amputated limbs in veterans of strategic value. Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]], it transforms rocky terrain into a living trap, with stone cracking and roots forcing their way into the fissures until they bring down walls from below.

### Antagonistic Counterpart

As Necro’s antagonistic counterpart, Vita operates through addition where the other operates through subtraction. An undead being exposed to Vita’s Vigor does not heal: it rots faster, because the life reintroduced into the dead body accelerates the entropy that reanimation was keeping suspended.
