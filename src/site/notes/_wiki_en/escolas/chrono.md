# Chrono

Chrono is the hybrid school of magic that manipulates the flow of time. A combination of blue and red, it governs the domain of time and duration: it slows, accelerates, freezes, ages, and reverses, existing in persistence, where the effect extends and endures. Its underlying feeling is longing for what was or what could have been, the refusal to accept that time only moves forward.

### Principle

Chrono acts on the when, not on the where. This is precisely what distinguishes it from schools that seem related: where [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] fixes objects in space through gravity, Chrono fixes them in time; where [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]] freezes matter through cold and [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]] degrades biological structure, Chrono acts on duration itself, and whatever ages under its influence ages through actual elapsed time, without disease. Those who master the school benefit from preparation, because stopped time does not conduct and nothing flows within it.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from a temporal stumble to the collapse of linearity. Lapse imposes a brief disruption in the target’s flow, an instant out of step sufficient to dodge or strike first; Dilation sustains this slowness, stretching each second until it lasts a subjective eternity. Trace unfolds the caster into temporal echoes, confusing the timing of the blow. Pause freezes a moving object at the exact point, a projectile in the air or a falling glass, later resuming from where it stopped.

On larger scales, Celerity compresses the target’s personal time, accelerating perception and reaction while the world slows down. Interregnum and Stasis paralyze: the former keeps consciousness trapped in an unresponsive body, the latter freezes the target completely, heart, blood, and aging, untouchable and inert, protection or prison depending on the intent. Senescence accelerates the body’s entropy, decades passing in seconds, while Rewind rewinds injuries and wear to an earlier state. At the pinnacle is Catachrony, which fragments time across an extensive area, making past and future collide: stone ages millennia, injuries reverse, and the recently dead inhale before crumbling.

### Combinations

Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], it produces a massacre in slow motion: Dilation slows the target while Cruor amplifies each blow as the target bleeds, a technique used by [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]] in judicial duels, where victory must be spectacular. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]], it serves emergency preservation: Stasis freezes the wounded person at the instant before death and Restoration reverses the damage while time is stopped, the healer working on a body that neither deteriorates nor bleeds. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]], a Clot reinforced by Stasis generates shadow matter frozen in time, which persists without concentration: permanent blades and shackles, an exceedingly rare effect.

### Costs

The thematic cost is personal sluggishness and diminished reflexes, lasting from five minutes to three hours. The ontological cost is temporal desynchronization: the present seems displaced, memory and prediction become confused. In the early stages, the practitioner occasionally mistakes what has already happened for what will happen; in the advanced stages, past, present, and future coexist, and the notion of linear time dissolves. At the final stage, the practitioner is removed from duties that require precise chronology and kept as an isolated temporal oracle, useless for everyday life but valuable for consultation. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|The Empire]] recruits practitioners for arcane research and for this role as oracle.

### Antagonistic Pair

Chrono opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]], the magic of the instant. The opposition is asymmetric: Electro benefits from instantaneous action, because speed resists deceleration, and Chrono benefits from preparation, because within a Stasis no current flows.
