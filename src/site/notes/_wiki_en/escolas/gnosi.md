# Gnosi

Gnosi is the school of pure blue magic, dedicated to Knowledge and Premonition. It knows without invading: it perceives truth as an arcane substance accessible through observation, not force. Unlike [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]], which breaks into another’s mind, Gnosi merely reads the world. Its key emotion is the hunger to understand, the unknown as a discomfort that demands an answer. It is the school opposite to [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]].

### Practices

The school ranges from detection to total understanding. Vigil expands perception, registering presences and hostile intentions as pressure at the nape of the neck. Scrutiny reveals the nature of an object (composition, enchantments, function); Discernment reveals how it works, its internal logic, and its vulnerabilities. Gloss grants the temporary gift of tongues. Clairvoyance projects perception to a distant location while the body remains motionless and vulnerable. Omen glimpses the immediate future, the blow coming from the left, the knife hidden beneath the table. True Sight dissolves illusions of [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] and reveals invisible creatures. Prophecy shows the distant future in symbolic and treacherous fragments. Probe extracts truth without invasion: it perceives the target’s lie, hidden emotion, and true intention, accessing truth as a substance instead of forcing its way into another’s mind. Omniscience, its highest level, answers any question about the present and the past with absolute precision.

### Costs

The thematic cost is an intense headache and profound mental fatigue, lasting from ten minutes to eight hours. The ontological cost is analysis paralysis: seeing too many consequences in every choice until knowledge prevents action. At the most advanced stage, omniscience becomes a prison, and the practitioner is sequestered as an oracle, with no decision-making power.

### In the Empire

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners as Councilors and isolates the most advanced as oracles, a role in which decision paralysis ceases to matter. Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]], it produces diagnosis and arcane surgery against cursed artifacts and ancient seals; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]], anticipatory defense; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]], a seismic sense that reveals the position and number of creatures in contact with the ground.
