# Hypno

Hypno is the hybrid school of sleep and hypnosis magic. A combination of green and blue, it suppresses consciousness without invading it: it lulls the will to sleep instead of subjugating it, and the target yields without knowing it should resist. Its foundational feeling is comfort at the threshold between wakefulness and sleep, the boundary treated as a habitat, not a passage.

### Principle

The school’s central question is “am I awake?”. This separates it from its mental neighbors: [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]] asks “is this real?” and [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]] asks “is this my thought?”. Where Psycho invades consciousness, Hypno suppresses it; it is domination versus lulling to sleep. The school acts on the boundary between sleep and wakefulness, and those who master it learn to inhabit this threshold.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from drowsiness to collective sleep. Languor induces a mental sluggishness that attacks wakefulness, and Lullaby releases a wave of calm that relaxes the muscles and lengthens breathing. Dream plants a message in a sleeper’s sleep, an image or voice that integrates into the ongoing dream. Exhaustion induces a psychosomatic fatigue in which the mind orders the body to stop, and Insomnia reverses the school’s gift, forbidding sleep and keeping the target awake until it hallucinates.

At deeper levels, Nightmare allows the caster to enter a sleeper’s dream as a conscious intruder, interrogating or torturing without leaving a mark, and the target wakes unable to distinguish memory from dream. Coma is bottomless sleep, from which no natural stimulus rouses the sleeper, and within it a dream-prison can be erected where the target lives without knowing it is asleep. Trance makes the target act as a functional sleepwalker, with eyes open and will absent, and Communion weaves a dream network among several minds for wordless coordination. At the apex is Hibernation, a wave of collective sleep that makes every conscious creature within its radius collapse where it stands, while the caster remains awake.

### Costs

The thematic cost is profound exhaustion and irresistible drowsiness, lasting from ten minutes to eight hours. The ontological cost is the erosion of the boundary between sleep and wakefulness: dreams become too vivid and wakefulness clouded. In the advanced stages, the practitioner ceases to distinguish what they dreamed from what they experienced, until reality becomes a continuous dream; at that point they are taken away, their testimony loses legal value, and they begin living in isolation in a chamber of perpetual sleep, awake only for specific missions. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]] for silent neutralization.

### Combinations

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], daytime unease and nighttime nightmares form a self-reinforcing cycle until the target yields, a tool for prolonged interrogations. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], Coma traps the target in a controlled dream while an outside observer extracts information from its reactions, and secrets emerge because, in the dream, the defenses are asleep. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/salvo\|Salvo]], the school serves compassionate sedation, suppressing pain and inducing sleep so that the wounded can be transported without agony.

### Antagonistic Pair

Hypno opposes Psycho, which invades consciousness where Hypno suppresses it: domination versus lulling to sleep, intellectual impulse versus contemplative nature.
