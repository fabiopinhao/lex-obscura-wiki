# Abjuro

Abjuro is the hybrid school dedicated to the negation and dispelling of the arcane. It detects, dispels, blocks, and extinguishes magic: it removes enchantments, suppresses arcane ability, creates antimagic zones, and redirects spells to the original caster. Where [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]] purifies through moral authority, [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]] breaks through force, and [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]] transcends through elevation, Abjuro simply negates, shutting down the enchantment without violence or ritual.

### Principle

Its key feeling is an instinctive distrust of others’ magic, the perception that every enchantment is an imposition to be removed. The abjurer operates through technical negation of the arcane flow: it neither destroys nor purifies, but cuts off what sustains the magic. A seal dispelled by Abjuro is erased, while a seal broken by Rupto shatters.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from perception to eradication. Diagnosis detects active magic nearby, its school, intensity, and general intent, each school having its own texture: [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]] throbs like a wound, [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]] smells of damp earth. The Purge strips minor magical effects from the caster. The Veil conceals the caster or an object from magical detection, causing artifacts to stop radiating a signature. Nullification undoes active enchantments with surgical precision, dissolving [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] barriers, draining Sacro blessings, and peeling off Anatho curses, with effectiveness that decreases against large-scale effects.

Interdiction blocks a casting in progress before it manifests, causing the spell to fail while the caster bears the cost without reaping any result. Suppression temporarily stifles a living target’s arcane ability. Sealing makes a target impenetrable to a specific school, such as preventing Necro reanimation or extinguishing [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]] flames as they cross the boundary. Retribution captures magic directed at the caster and returns it inverted to whoever cast it. The Gap creates an arcane [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/vazio\|void]], an absolute zone where no magic exists, including the caster’s own. At its maximum scale, Extinction permanently eradicates the arcane essence of a target, artifact, location, or creature, and whatever it touches never channels magic again.

### Costs

The thematic cost is temporary magical vulnerability, with resistance reduced for anywhere from two minutes to two hours. The ontological cost is magical denialism, which progresses through stages: from constant distrust of others’ protections to the tendency to dispel before asking, until arcane cooperation becomes impossible and all external magic becomes a threat. In the final stage, when the practitioner begins dispelling even imperial protections and threatens to compromise joint operations, the practitioner is discarded in a silent Transfer. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]] of nullification and defensive countermagic up to the third stage.

### Combinations and opposition

Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], it produces a complete arcane examination: Diagnosis identifies school and intensity while Discernment analyzes structure and mechanism, and imperial nullification mages trained in this pairing are the most sought after against cursed artifacts and ancient seals. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], it results in total silence, combining the blocking of arcane ability with the prevention of verbal communication. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/toxo\|Toxo]]: where Toxo infiltrates slowly, poisoning and corroding, Abjuro reacts quickly, detecting, purifying, and protecting.
