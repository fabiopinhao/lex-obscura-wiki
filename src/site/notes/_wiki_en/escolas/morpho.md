# Morpho

Morpho is the hybrid school of magic in the domain of metamorphosis and transformation. It physically alters the body, either the caster’s own or a target’s, with real and verifiable changes that withstand touch and inspection. Its key feeling is fascination with becoming another, with form taken as a choice and not as destiny.

### Principle

Morpho’s transformation is real, not illusory: what changes holds up under scrutiny. This is what separates it from [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], which deceives perception without altering matter. Morpho truly transforms; Spectro pretends.

### Practices

The grimoire scales from cosmetic changes to a complete biological rewrite. Countenance alters facial features, eyes, hair, skin, and scars, changes that touch confirms. Claws transform fingers, nails, and teeth into weapons, with bones lengthening beneath the skin. Pliability grants the body plasticity, allowing escape from restraints and passage through narrow spaces. Mimicry acquires one functional ability of a natural creature at a time, such as gills, night vision, or scales. Beast-Skin produces a complete transformation into a mundane animal of medium size or smaller, preserving consciousness but eliminating speech. Transmutation deforms the target against their will, reversible only by the original caster or by [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]]. Grotesque partially transforms the caster’s own body into an unnatural creature that retains speech and posture but gains monstrous attributes, and frequent use makes reversal more difficult. Transmorphosis converts the entire target into an animal; Reconstitute rewrites the biological composition of a living being, preserving only consciousness beneath the strange flesh. At the top is Chimera, which rewrites physical form by combining traits of multiple creatures into an anatomy without natural precedent, entirely functional and cohesive.

### Costs

The thematic cost is bodily disorientation and difficulty with coordination, lasting from two minutes to two hours. The ontological cost is fluid identity: the self becomes an unstable category, and the mirror begins to provoke a sense of unfamiliarity. It begins as occasional unfamiliarity and progresses until any form will do and the original form is lost. At that point, when no one knows their original form and loyalty becomes unverifiable, the practitioner is discarded in a silent Transfer. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|The Empire]] recruits practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]] for long-term infiltration, because an unanchored identity makes the bond impossible to verify.

### Combinations and Opposition

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/vita\|Vita]], metamorphosis is cultivated instead of forced, slower but more stable and less costly, a specialty of druids and [[_wiki_en/racas/sylvano\|Sylvans]]; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/patho\|Patho]], transformation degenerates the target into something wrong instead of functional. The combination with [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]] is forbidden by imperial protocol. Its antagonistic counterpart is Spectro, the distinction between becoming and seeming: Morpho deals with doubt about identity, Spectro with doubt about perception.
