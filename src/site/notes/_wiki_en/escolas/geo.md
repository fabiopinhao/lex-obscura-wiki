# Geo

Geo is the pure green school that manipulates earth, stone, and soil as real matter, with weight and permanence. Its domain requires contact with the ground: it becomes useless over water or in the air. Its key feeling is obstinacy, the refusal to yield felt in the body as the conviction that remaining is already resisting. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]], the school of movement.

### Principle

The school treats earth as both medium and matter, controlling where earth is more than what it is. This distinguishes it from its neighbors: from [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]], which moves through gravity and space; from [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], which works with ice; from [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]], which destroys through structural analysis; from [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]], which travels through shadow. The stone Geo raises remains after the caster stops, unlike the force barrier of [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]], which dissolves when the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] ceases.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from vibration to the reshaping of geography. Tremor releases localized vibration in the soil that disrupts balance and interrupts concentration, affecting only those touching the ground. Mold manipulates loose earth, clay, sand, or mud, excavating and sculpting simple shapes. Sediment alters surface cohesion, compacting loose soil into rock or making rock friable. Stake tears stone from the soil and launches it as spikes and columns that erupt to impale. Wall raises a barrier of earth or stone from the ground itself, with weight and mass that persist. Sinkhole makes the ground give way beneath the target, opening fissures and trapping through real earth, in an effect reversible by the geomancer. Crushing closes stone walls around the target with slow, inexorable pressure. Immersion merges the geomancer with rock and allows him to travel within it, sinking and emerging at another point without leaving a trace. Earthquake unleashes seismic upheaval over a wide area, with radial fissures and structures that collapse when their foundations move in opposite directions. Cataclysm, the maximum scale, makes the crust obey: mountains erupt where there was a plain, valleys open where there was a road, rivers change course.

### Costs

The thematic cost is increased weight and muscles stiffened like stone, lasting from one minute to two hours. The ontological cost is psychological immobility, in which change becomes aversive and routines calcify. It progresses in stages: first a preference for routines; then active resistance to novelty; next the perception of any alteration as a threat to stability; finally, the stage in which movement seems like violence and only immobility seems safe. At that point the practitioner is kept in permanent defensive positions, where the refusal to relocate ceases to be a flaw and becomes a function. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|The Empire]] recruits geomancers as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]], guards at fixed positions, and defenders of fortifications.

### Combinations

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]], it produces mud, quicksand, and directed erosion, saturating the soil Geo controls. With Rupto, it performs assisted demolition, Geo identifying the structure and Rupto breaking the critical point. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], it generates a seismic sense in which vibrations reveal the position and weight of creatures in contact with the soil. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]], it melts the surface of the raised wall into an impermeable vitrified crust.
