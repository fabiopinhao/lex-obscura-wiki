# Aegis

Aegis is the hybrid school of protection and barriers, a combination of white and blue. It creates shields, barriers, and protective auras, deflects blows, absorbs damage, reflects attacks, and manifests sentinels of force, for personal or group protection. Its key feeling is protective vigilance: the perception that something needs to be defended, now.

### Principle

Aegis protects through pure arcane force, and this is what distinguishes it from neighboring schools: it blocks damage, not matter, not bonds, not flow. It differs from [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]], which protects with real stone, from [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], which restricts through arcane law, and from [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]], which negates magic. Its mechanics scale from the defensive reflex to the impenetrable sphere.

### Practices

At the simplest level, Deflection diverts a single blow at the instant the air hardens, and dissipates after impact. Shield creates a disk of force fixed in space that absorbs successive blows until it cracks and collapses. Ward filters arcane effects before they reach the flesh, diluting curses and mitigating magical poisons. Barrier raises a semitransparent wall of force that blocks a corridor or protects a formation, impenetrable to projectiles and partially permeable to high-powered magic, and disappears when the [[_wiki_en/cosmologia/magi\|magi]] ceases.

Above these come linked and reactive protections. Custody links the protector to an ally, redirecting some of the damage the protected ally receives to the caster, in the form of exhaustion and pain. Retribution returns the blow suffered by the caster through arcane force. Cuirass covers the entire body in armor of force that follows its movements. Aegis projects a radial field that covers nearby allies, weakening with each blow absorbed. Guardian manifests a semiautonomous sentinel of force that physically interposes itself before threats. At the pinnacle, Dome creates a sphere of absolute force where nothing enters and nothing leaves, neither projectile, nor blade, nor magic, nor sound, nor air.

### Combinations

Aegis intertwines with other schools. With the Consecration of [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], it forms a sacred fortress, in which the holy perimeter repels profane creatures even before the barrier does. With Geo’s Wall, it produces layered fortification, real stone reinforced by a force field. With the Cruor of [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], the protector bleeds for the protected. With the Vigil of [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], it achieves anticipatory defense, deflecting the blow it senses before seeing it. With the Static of [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]], it becomes reactive defense, punishing whoever gets past the deflection with a discharge.

### Costs

The thematic cost is emotional vulnerability, anxiety, and amplified fear, lasting from five minutes to four hours. The ontological cost is hypervigilance: threats begin to appear everywhere, relaxing becomes impossible, and protection becomes obsession. The progression goes from heightened vigilance to the inability to lower one’s guard, to a stage where rest seems like neglect and carelessness seems like betrayal. At this extreme, a Dome sustained for too long needs to be broken from the outside, because the caster cannot release it. Unlike with other schools, a practitioner at an advanced stage is rarely discarded: permanent vigilance is useful for protecting a location or a single person. In the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Shadow Empire]], these practitioners are recruited as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]], imperial healers, and personal guards for dignitaries.

### Antagonistic Pair

Aegis has Belli as its antagonistic counterpart, on the axis of order and chaos. Where Aegis resists, absorbs, and protects, Belli attacks, amplifies, and destroys. The ontological cost is mirrored: Belli’s worst-case scenario is being unable to stop attacking; Aegis’s, being unable to stop protecting.
