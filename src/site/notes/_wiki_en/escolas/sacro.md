# Sacro

Sacro is the school of pure white magic, dedicated to Grace and Holiness. It blesses, purifies, consecrates, and protects through moral authority and divine intervention, requiring genuine faith as fuel: cynicism weakens its power. Unlike [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]], which purifies through physical radiance, Sacro purifies through ritual, divine law, and consecration. It is the school opposed to [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]].

### Practices

The school ranges from favorable luck to the miracle of resurrection. Fortune tilts chance in the target’s favor by narrow margins. Courage dispels fear, despair, and paralysis through touch or a word of faith. Premonition grants directional certainty of the presence of corruption, desecration, and malignancy. Blessing amplifies a specific capacity with sanctified vigor. Safeguard prevents or removes curses and mind control. Consecration declares a perimeter to be sacred ground, where profane energy weakens. Exorcism expels entities with wills of their own in spiritual combat of faith. Pardon inscribes in advance an enchantment that intercepts death at the exact instant it occurs, automatic and requiring no intervention. Sanctuary creates an inviolable area where no hostile intent manifests. Resurrection, its highest scale, revokes death already consummated through direct divine intervention: the caster kneels, invokes, and the god decides.

### Costs

The thematic cost is spiritual weight and a sense of unworthiness and impurity, from ten minutes to eight hours. The ontological cost is spiritual perfectionism: impurity, one’s own and others’, becomes visible and intolerable, and moral judgment becomes an automatic reflex that makes forgiveness difficult.

### In the Empire

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners as Inquisitors and ritual purifiers. Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]], it erects sacred fortresses, with a consecrated perimeter and a force barrier over its boundaries; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], it sustains the crusade, soldiers blessed and called upon fighting beyond human limits; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], it sacralizes oaths that wound on two layers, arcane damage and spiritual corruption.
