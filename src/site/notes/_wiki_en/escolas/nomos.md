# Nomos

Nomos is the hybrid school of white and black that imposes restrictions, bonds, and prohibitions as laws inscribed in reality, an arcane force that maintains cohesion and obedience where the target’s will would attempt to resist. Its key feeling is the certainty that the imposed bond is just, the legitimate possession of what has been sealed. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]], the school that undoes structure and liberates.

### Principle

The school governs through authority, not brute force: law revokes law. This distinguishes it from its neighbors. Restricting is not protecting, as [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] does; binding is not nullifying, as [[_wiki_en/escolas/abjuro\|Abjuro]] does; confining through arcane law is not the physical gravity of [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]]; prohibiting an action is not suppressing consciousness, as [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]] does.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from a lock to a cosmic edict. Seal locks a door, safe, or drawer by touch, and the material refuses to open even under brute force. Injunction compresses a command into word and will that compel organic obedience, with the body obeying before refusal. Enfranchisement unlocks a lock, seal, or arcane bond through authority. Gag imposes silence on a mouth or an area, with the voice dying in the throat without pain. Shackle inscribes a specific prohibition on the target, in which the first violation causes pain, the second knocks the target down, and the third causes actual harm. Sigil engraves a rule on a surface that awaits a violation and triggers without the caster’s presence. Oubliette pulls the target into a sealed dimensional pocket, a finite chamber where time passes normally and the prisoner ages at a human rate. Oath inscribes a promise in flesh and soul, and does not check intention, only action: anyone who swears never to raise a weapon pays the same price for reflex or betrayal. Confinement is absolute imprisonment, with the body completely motionless and the mind completely present, the target existing conscious and immobilized. Edict, the maximum scale, declares a law and reality obeys, but the cost is defining: the caster internalizes the law they imposed, and whoever decrees that no one lies becomes incapable of lying.

### Costs

The thematic cost is mental rigidity and difficulty questioning or improvising, lasting from five minutes to four hours. The ontological cost is pathological literalism. First comes discomfort with ambiguity and a preference for clear rules; then jokes and metaphors genuinely confuse; next, exceptions seem like threats to order; finally, reality exists only where there is law, and chaos becomes literally invisible. At this stage the practitioner is withdrawn from field duties, but retained in legal and administrative duties, where absolute rigidity is desirable. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|The Empire]] recruits practitioners of Nomos as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]], Magistrates, Councilors, and arbiters of last resort.

### Combinations

With Cosmo, it produces absolute imprisonment, multiplying weight while binding the body to its position. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], it sanctifies Oath, which begins to operate on two layers. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], it generates the paralysis of knowing everything and deciding nothing, a combination that reinforces the ontological costs of both schools.
