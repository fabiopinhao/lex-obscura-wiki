# Cosmo

Cosmo is the hybrid school of blue and black whose domain is space and gravity. It manipulates gravity and space as tools of physical control, trapping, compressing, and displacing matter by altering the laws that govern it. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]].

### Principle

The feeling that drives the practitioner is vertigo before the immense and the impulse to respond to vastness instead of surrendering to it. Those who have looked too long into the abyss can no longer feel that they belong anywhere.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from a shove to orbital impact. Pulse releases a brief, imprecise gravitational wave that pushes or pulls. Attraction pulls small objects toward the caster, such as keys, daggers, and flasks that slide toward them. Lightening reduces gravity on a target, and leaps cover improbable distances while heavy armor becomes light clothing. Burden multiplies the gravitational weight on the target, buckling knees and bending spines, scaling from light pressure to lethal compression. Suspension removes gravity within an area, and those affected float without direction or support. Telekinesis positions, rotates, and guides objects at a distance with fine, sustained precision. Rending applies opposing gravitational forces to the same target, tearing apart limbs and joints silently and surgically. [[_wiki/feiticos/singularidade\|Singularity]] fixes a point of intense attraction in space, and everything within a radius is pulled toward the center. Portal tears the fabric of space between two known points, a circular frame of distorted air that allows instantaneous passage. Meteor, its maximum scale, tears rock from orbit or the ground, melted by speed, and sends it down in a straight line onto the target, the crater forming even before contact.

### Costs

The thematic cost is an increase in the practitioner’s own weight and difficulty moving, lasting from thirty seconds to thirty minutes. The ontological cost is spatial dissociation. At Stage I, there is an occasional feeling of being out of place; at II, physical proximity is uncomfortable and embraces feel like an invasion; at III, the body itself seems to occupy the wrong space; at IV, dissociation is permanent and physical presence becomes an abstract concept, and the practitioner is taken away, rarely discarded, kept in a chamber of altered gravity for experiments. The fundamental difference from Urano is one of direction: Cosmo traps the body in space, Urano frees the spirit from matter.

The invocation known as the Black Vacuum, *Vacuum aeternum*, opens a Major-scale Singularity and exacts spatial dissociation proportional to its duration; there is no proscription against it. The invocation requires a consecrated focus, and the cost takes an extreme form when the rite breaks midway: once the focus is lost, the caster begins sustaining the singularity with their own body, and the void tries to be born through their chest.

### Combinations

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners for arcane research and gravitational experiments, roles in which dissociation compromises coordination but not obedience. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], it produces absolute imprisonment, a resource used by [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] in high-risk apprehensions. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], it generates the invisible ambush, erasing the attacker’s presence while the target floats disoriented. Its antagonistic counterpart is Urano, which releases and elevates where Cosmo traps and compresses.
