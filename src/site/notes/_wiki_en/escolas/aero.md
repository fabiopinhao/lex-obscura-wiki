# Aero

Aero is the hybrid school whose domain is wind and storm. It creates and controls air currents as a real physical force, limited in confined spaces but devastating under the open sky, manipulating the medium every living being needs to breathe. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]].

### Principle

The feeling that drives the Aero practitioner is the need for open space and the sense that rootedness suffocates. Those who need the open sky to breathe cannot tolerate any permanence.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from a breeze to a regional squall. Breeze manipulates light air currents, ventilates spaces, and generates a pocket of breathable air. Gust releases a single directional blast that pushes light objects, disperses smoke, and extinguishes flames. Lift generates updrafts that cushion falls and allow brief gliding. Gale is a strong, continuous wind that knocks down and unbalances targets within an area, dragging small creatures and deflecting projectiles. Vortex traps targets in a localized whirlwind, with debris that obscures vision and cuts exposed skin. Slash projects an invisible, silent blade of compressed air, the wound appearing before the target understands. Resonance controls the pressure waves carried by the air: in combat, it pulses concussive force that ruptures eardrums and shatters fragile surfaces; in espionage, it carries whispers over absurd distances. Cyclone raises a column of wind that tears off roofs, topples trees, and hurls creatures and rubble in an upward spiral. Suffocation extracts the air from the lungs and creates a pocket of vacuum around the target’s head. Squall, its maximum scale, unleashes regional winds that tear structures from their foundations, with the horizon disappearing into a rotating wall of darkness and, in the eye of the storm, absolute silence.

### Costs

The thematic cost is shortness of breath and hyperventilation, lasting from thirty seconds to one hour. The ontological cost is an existential claustrophobia in which commitments become prisons and freedom becomes a pathological need. At Stage I, enclosed spaces cause mild discomfort; at II, long commitments provoke anxiety; at III, any permanence seems like imprisonment and the practitioner is discarded; at IV, freedom is the only need and bonds are chains, a stage treated as an irreversible systemic risk, in which the practitioner is executed in a Final Consecration before they can escape the system. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|The Empire]] recruits practitioners only as scouts and messengers through Stage II, in short-term roles without permanent ties.

### Combinations

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], it produces the fire cyclone, a siege specialty. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]], it generates freezing winds, directed blizzards, and surfaces frozen by the wind. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/electro\|Electro]], it forms the true storm, in which the wind carries an electric charge and lightning is redirected. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], Resonance amplifies auditory illusions with physical fidelity. Its antagonistic counterpart is Geo: movement against permanence, air against stone, passage against the root.
