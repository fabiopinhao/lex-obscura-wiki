# Phobos

Phobos is the hybrid school of red and black that turns fear into a weapon, one of the disciplines of black magic. It operates on what the victim feels, not on what they think, and its key feeling is intimacy with horror: the perception that others’ fear has texture, form, and utility.

### Practices

Its mechanics escalate from diffuse discomfort to the annihilation of the soul. In its mild forms, it sows unease without an object, the sensation of eyes on one’s back, or discharges pure alarm into the nervous system. In its intermediate forms, it imposes dread that compels flight, corrodes trust to the point of paranoia that devastates a chain of command, or freezes the body in terror while the mind remains conscious. In its extreme forms, it inscribes permanent phobias in the psyche, contaminates perception with the target’s worst fears, or strikes the soul with the visceral certainty of annihilation, from which the weak die and the strong survive broken.

### Cost

The thematic cost is symmetrical: the caster inherits echoes of what they inflict, apparitions that haunt them for ten minutes to twelve hours. The ontological cost is familiarity with horror, and it progresses in stages. First, others’ fears become readable as open text; then one’s own fear atrophies and danger ceases to sound the alarm; finally, empathy disappears, pleas become boring, and nothing frightens or moves them anymore. The practitioner who reaches the final stage is disposed of in unproductive massacres, sent on a Mission of Honor from which no return is expected.

### In the Empire

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners of Phobos as Inquisitors and [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]] and employs them in tactical terror operations until the third stage, when the absence of empathy is still operational. It combines with other schools in recognizable effects: with [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], it produces a terror that can be seen, an illusion that cannot be disbelieved because fear demands that it be real; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]], a nocturnal erosion that turns every night into torment, a tool for prolonged interrogations; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/psycho\|Psycho]], a surgical interrogation that exposes the target’s dominant fear and amplifies it; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/umbra\|Umbra]], darkness becomes a field of amplification, since without visual reference points terror escalates on its own; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]], an aggravated parasitism, the terrified bodies pumping more blood and breathing faster while the draining feeds on this acceleration.

Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]]: knowledge clarifies the judgment that fear clouds, and thus learned targets resist terror better while the superstitious resist truth better.
