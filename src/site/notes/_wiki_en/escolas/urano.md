# Urano

Urano is the hybrid school of magic of transcendence and elevation. A combination of blue and white, it frees the spirit from matter: it instills peace, dissolves bonds, and raises the body above the laws that govern the physical plane. Its underlying feeling is the perception that the mundane is small, the attraction to what transcends the immediate.

### Principle

Where other schools bind, compress, and displace, Urano releases, elevates, and dissolves. This is the axis that separates it from [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]], its opposite: Cosmo manipulates matter through space using gravity as a weapon, and Urano transcends it through will. The school neither negates the magic of others nor forces anything: it lets the bond continue to exist while the target rises above it.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from stillness to total transcendence. Stillness silences mental noise without inducing sleep, quieting what prevented clarity, and Feather makes the body respond to the spirit before gravity, softening jumps and falls. Breath makes air enter the lungs as though the obstruction did not exist, making smoke or water breathable for brief moments, and Elevation raises the caster off the ground without propulsion, suspended contemplation in which the ground ceases to matter. Flight is full aerial movement, silent and indifferent to the weather, and responds better the more detached its caster is from earthly anchors.

In matters of bonds, Freedom loosens ropes, shackles, and arcane seals, declaring that the target is not bound, and Absolution undoes bonds imposed by others, forced oaths, and curses, without destroying the surrounding prison. Serenity imposes peace on a creature or area, and the desire to hurt becomes alien without the target losing lucidity. Armistice declares a zone in which all violence becomes impossible, including defensive violence. At the apex is Ascension, which transcends the physical plane for brief moments, the caster dissolving into light as pure consciousness, immune to the mundane but also unable to affect it, and the return is violent because the solid body seems like a prison.

### Costs

The thematic cost is intense vertigo, loss of balance, and nausea, lasting from two minutes to two hours. The ontological cost is contempt for the mundane: earthly matters shrink and transcendence becomes the only goal. In the advanced stages, the practitioner treats earthly bonds as anchors to be cut and abandons their duties. The final stage is considered an irreversible systemic risk, and the practitioner is executed before abandoning the material plane, because there is no return from vertical ascension. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners only for advanced arcane research and short-term roles, with no permanent bond.

### Combinations

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/nomos\|Nomos]], the relationship is asymmetrical: Urano undoes bonds that Nomos imposes, but Nomos can prevent transcendence if it acts first, and the contest always comes down to who acts first. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]], Stillness clears the noise that clouds the mind and produces clearer visions, and oracles who master both are rare and vied for by the [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/conselho_sombrio\|Shadow Council]]. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/rupto\|Rupto]], Freedom loosens the arcane bond while Break tears apart the physical one, a rescue protocol for infiltrations that fail. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/sacro\|Sacro]], Consecration repels malign influences while Armistice prevents a negotiation from degenerating into violence, training for hierophants and imperial diplomats.

### Antagonistic Pair

Urano opposes Cosmo: Burden crushes what Elevation raises, and Singularity traps what Absolution frees. It is spatial dissociation against contempt for the mundane, the body that does not seem to be here against the world that does not seem to matter.
