# Plasma

Plasma is the hybrid school of red and white whose domain is energy and fusion. It manipulates extreme energy, in which matter melts, ionizes, and superheats, with a self-contained bluish-white glow that needs no fuel. This autonomy distinguishes it from [[_wiki_en/escolas/pyro\|Pyro]], whose chemical combustion consumes matter and depends on it to burn. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/cryo\|Cryo]].

### Principle

The feeling that drives the practitioner is impatience with the solidity of things and the impulse to reduce everything to its essence. Those who see only essence forget that they have a body.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from charge to volcanic eruption. Ionization saturates the air or matter with energy until the surroundings crackle, with static snaking between metal surfaces and the smell of ozone. Spark fires brief plasma discharges that leap from the fingers and leave vitrified marks. Incandescence forces matter to absorb energy until it glows, with glowing metal and red rock radiating heat from an internal excess. Fulgurite fires an orb or beam of concentrated plasma that tears through the air in a bluish-white line, vitrifying surfaces and fusing flesh into a smooth crust. Gladius condenses plasma into the shape of a weightless blade, with an edge that cuts without resistance and emits a continuous hum. Liquefaction converts a solid surface into a pool of molten matter, turning stone into lava and metal into incandescent mercury. Bolide tears rock from the ground, superheats it to white incandescence, and hurls it, exploding into vitrified shards and a heat wave that ignores armor. Disintegration converts matter into pure energy, and the target is not destroyed but dissolved, its edges glowing until no substance remains. Corona surrounds the caster’s body in an aura of incandescent plasma, and anyone who approaches feels their skin dry out before any contact. Eruption, its maximum scale, liquefies the ground within a devastating radius, with columns of magma bursting forth in geysers and the air ionizing until it glows purple.

### Costs

The thematic cost is a burn and intense tingling in the skin, lasting from one minute to two hours. The ontological cost is a progressive material detachment in which the body seems disposable. In Stage I, material possessions lose importance; in II, the body itself seems temporary; in III, matter is an illusion and only energy is real; in IV, physical existence seems an accident and dissolution seems a return, and the practitioner is discarded through Final Consecration when they abandon their body on a mission. Unlike [[_wiki_en/escolas/urano\|Urano]], which ascends vertically by transcending the mundane, Plasma dissolves horizontally, discarding matter. The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners for arcane research and energy manipulation.

### Combinations

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/geo\|Geo]], it produces fused fortification, raising stone and fusing its surface into an impermeable vitrified crust. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], it generates a blade that feeds on blood: the more the swordsman bleeds, the whiter the edge becomes and the deeper the cut, a resource used by duelist [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]] who refuse a shield. Its antagonistic counterpart is Cryo, whose ice paralyzes what plasma transforms, preservation against dissolution: extreme cold weakens Corona as a direct counter.
