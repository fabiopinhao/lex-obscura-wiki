# Psycho

Psycho is the hybrid school of red and blue magic, dedicated to the Mind and the Psyche. It invades, reads, destroys, and rewrites the minds of others through intrusive force: where [[_wiki_en/escolas/gnosi\|Gnosi]] knows passively, Psycho breaks the door open and enters. Its key feeling is the impulse to enter, another’s mind as a door that can, and must, be opened. It is the school opposite to [[_wiki_en/escolas/hypno\|Hypno]].

### Practices

The school scales from perception to absolute control. Watch scans an area for conscious minds, each presence registering as a point of heat. Influx projects a thought into the mind of an awake target. Vertigo pulses disorientation capable of interrupting spellcasting. Intrusion forces its way into the mind and reads what floats on the surface, the immediate intention, the newly formulated lie. Torment inflicts pure mental pain with no physical correlate, an interrogation tool that leaves no marks. Dulling crushes cognitive capacity. Oblivion rips out memories with surgical precision. Frenzy injects homicidal rage that suppresses recognition of ally and enemy. Fabulation implants false memories indistinguishable from true ones, a political weapon par excellence. Possession, its highest level, is absolute mind control: the caster inhabits the target’s mind, suppresses its will, and the body obeys as though it were the caster’s own.

### Costs

The thematic cost is dizziness, mental confusion, and difficulty concentrating, lasting from two minutes to four hours. The ontological cost is the porosity of mental boundaries: others’ thoughts begin to seem like one’s own until individuality dissolves. The school’s central question is “is this my thought?”.

### In the Empire

The [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|Empire]] recruits practitioners as Inquisitors of mental extraction and as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/ophideias\|Ophideias]]. Combined with [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], it produces surgical interrogation, a technique of high-level [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]]; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], it fabricates testimony, making the target remember seeing what never existed; with [[_wiki_en/escolas/anatho\|Anatho]], total manipulation, a dangerous synergistic combination that accelerates both ontological costs.
