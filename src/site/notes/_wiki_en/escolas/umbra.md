# Umbra

Umbra is the pure black school whose domain is darkness and obscurity. It manipulates actual darkness, not illusion or death, but the matter of darkness as a physical substance with weight and a cutting edge, and is one of the disciplines of black magic. Its antagonistic counterpart is [[_wiki_en/escolas/helio\|Helio]].

### Principle

The feeling that drives the practitioner is the desire to hide and the conviction that darkness protects. Those who hide too much forget how to exist in the light.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from penumbra to eclipse. Penumbra darkens a limited area and smothers small flames, reducing light sources to trembling ripples. Shape animates existing shadows, lengthening them and separating them from the body that casts them. Shroud envelops the caster in clinging darkness that absorbs outlines and muffles sound, turning the caster into a blot that the eye dismisses. Blindness covers the target’s eyes with darkness that clings like pitch, darkness within rather than around them. Clot solidifies shadow into dense, temporary matter, such as a blade, shield, shackle, or stake, which crumbles into black dust when concentration ceases. Threshold sinks the caster into their own shadow to emerge from another connected to it, passing through the shadow dimension where the cold and silence of the in-between reign. Blackness creates a zone of impenetrable darkness in which no light survives and only the caster can see. Gully projects extensions of semisolid shadow that grab, constrict, and drag, causing the skin to necrotize where they tighten. Eidolon detaches the caster’s own shadow from their body and animates it with a fragment of will, sliding across surfaces as an extension of the senses or, with more [[_wiki/cosmologia/magi\|magi]], gaining volume and the ability to hunt; it is vulnerable to direct natural light and returns as a physical rupture in the caster if destroyed. Eclipse, its maximum scale, actively consumes light across a vast area, with the sun, stars, and torches going out, darkness pressing against the chest and causing plants to wither and animals to enter torpor.

### Costs

The thematic cost is the distortion of the practitioner’s own vision, a temporary partial blindness that lasts from thirty seconds to thirty minutes. The ontological cost is progressive psychological photophobia. At Stage I, there is discomfort with bright light and a preference for penumbra; at II, the practitioner avoids windows, chooses dark corners, and wakes at night; at III, sunlight causes physical and psychological irritation; at IV, brightness is an assault and the practitioner functions fully only in darkness. Stage IV rarely results in disposal, because [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|the Empire]] recruits Umbra practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/justiciares\|Justiciars]] and for nighttime operations, underground surveillance, and dungeon garrisons, roles in which photophobia is an advantage.

### Combinations

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/necro\|Necro]], it produces nearly undetectable assassins, concealing their approach while their touch drains life. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/phobos\|Phobos]], Blackness amplifies fear spells, since without visual reference points, terror escalates. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/spectro\|Spectro]], it animates shadows and projects soldiers or monsters for tactical disinformation, a technique favored by the [[_wiki_en/racas/drul\|Druls]] of [[_wiki/locais/vor-ghol\|Vor-Ghol]]. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]], it produces shadow matter frozen in time, with permanent blades and shackles, something exceedingly rare. Its antagonistic counterpart is Helio, the most binary axis of the system, in which light exposes what darkness hides.
