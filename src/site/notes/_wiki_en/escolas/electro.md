# Electro

Electro is the hybrid school of magic that generates and controls electricity and magnetic fields. It governs the current that flows between conductors, amplified by metal and water, and exists in the instant, because lightning strikes now. Its underlying feeling is a restlessness that needs discharge, the sensation of being too charged to stand still.

### Principle

The school belongs to the instant: the effect occurs at the exact moment of casting and is exhausted within it. It differs from neighboring schools in the nature of what it moves. Where [[_wiki_en/escolas/plasma\|Plasma]] melts through heat, Electro shocks nerves through electricity; where [[_wiki_en/escolas/cosmo\|Cosmo]] controls any matter through gravity, Electro affects only metal, but with superior precision within that specialty. Its power grows in the presence of conductors and plummets where there are none: in a forge or an arsenal it is apocalyptic, in a forest without metal it can be useless.

### Practices

The mechanics scale from a spark to devastation in a chain reaction. Spark releases a localized static discharge, a shock that hurts and interrupts concentration; Lightning fires a direct arc at a single target, causing a burn and a muscle spasm; Voltaic Bolt jumps between conductive targets, preferentially striking those who are wet or wearing metal armor. Induction infuses bodies, weapons, and surfaces with a steady current, and Static accumulates charge in the caster as a defensive deterrent.

In the magnetic domain, Magnetize attracts and repels small objects, Magnetization raises a defensive field that deflects projectiles, and Magnetarch controls metal over a wide radius, tearing weapons from hands and immobilizing warriors inside their own armor. Convulsion releases a wave that overloads the nervous system, and the target knows what they want to do but their body refuses to obey. At the pinnacle is Fulmination, a catastrophic discharge in which lightning strikes in succession, the air ionizes, and the magnetic field crushes armor. Below it, *Fulgur caelestis* is an invocation of a single lightning bolt; like every Electro invocation, it exacts the thematic cost from the body of whoever casts it.

### Costs

The thematic cost is muscle spasms and involuntary tremors, lasting from thirty seconds to one hour. The ontological cost is a chronic restlessness: calm is agitating and rest becomes impossible. In the early stages, difficulty staying still, fragmented sleep, and racing thoughts emerge; in the advanced stages, silence becomes unbearable and the practitioner exists only in motion. At that extreme he is discarded on a Mission of Honor, sent on lightning-fast infiltrations at suicidal speed. [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/imperio_sombrio\|The Empire]] recruits practitioners as [[_wiki_en/organizacoes/templarios\|Templars]], urgent messengers, and agents for lightning operations, in which speed is the function itself.

### Combinations

With [[_wiki_en/escolas/hydro\|Hydro]], the current travels through any volume of water connected to the target, multiplying damage and area. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/aero\|Aero]], the two schools produce a true storm, each creating half of the phenomenon. With [[_wiki_en/escolas/belli\|Belli]], Induction charges allied blades with current, and with [[_wiki_en/escolas/aegis\|Aegis]] it forms a reactive defense in which whoever touches the caster receives the discharge.

### Antagonistic Pair

Electro opposes [[_wiki_en/escolas/chrono\|Chrono]], the magic of duration. The opposition is rhythmic and asymmetric: Electro negates Chrono because a target under current resists being slowed, and Chrono negates Electro because stopped time does not conduct and within a Stasis no current flows.
